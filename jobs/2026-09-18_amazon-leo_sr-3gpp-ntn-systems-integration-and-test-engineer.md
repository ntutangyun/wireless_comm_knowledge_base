# Sr. 3GPP NTN Systems Integration and Test Engineer, Amazon Leo

**Amazon (Project Kuiper / Amazon Leo)** · Redmond, WA · req `10552215`

This role focuses on hands-on integration, validation, troubleshooting, and qualification of end-to-end satellite communications systems for Amazon's LEO satellite constellation project. The team leads protocol validation for Release 17 NTN features across RAN and Core systems, converting 3GPP specifications into executable integration, regression, conformance, and interoperability test cases. Work is described as lab-centered and system-focused rather than software development, though the ability to use Linux, Python, protocol traces, packet captures, and automation to debug complex systems is highly valued.

## Responsibilities

- Lead protocol validation for Release 17 NTN features across RAN and Core systems.
- Convert 3GPP specifications and standards requirements into executable integration, regression, conformance, and interoperability test cases.
- Analyze protocol logs and packet captures to identify root causes.
- Partner with multiple technical teams to close compliance gaps.
- Produce test coverage documentation and risk assessments.

## Basic Qualifications

- Associate's degree or above.
- 7+ years in LTE/5G protocol validation or systems integration.
- Deep knowledge of 3GPP Release 17 with NTN understanding.
- Strong protocol knowledge (RRC, NAS, NGAP, F1AP, etc.).
- Open RAN architecture experience.
- VoNR and IMS protocol expertise.
- Experience analyzing protocol logs and packet captures for root cause analysis.
- Lab test design experience.
- Linux proficiency.

## Preferred Qualifications

- LEO or satellite communications experience.
- Release 18 NTN or standards body participation.
- MOCN/MORAN network experience.
- Emergency services or roaming validation.
- Tool expertise (Keysight, Anritsu, etc.).
- Python scripting for protocol analysis.
- Mentoring capability.
- Trace-based defect narrative expertise.

Salary range: $159,100 - $215,300 USD annually (Redmond, WA).
