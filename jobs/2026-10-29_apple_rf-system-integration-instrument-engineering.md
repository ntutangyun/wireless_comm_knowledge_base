# RF System Integration Engineer (Instrument Engineering)

**Apple** - Cupertino - posted 2026-10-28

### Summary

Join our Wireless team and build the technology that makes every Apple radio possible. We're looking for engineers with strong fundamentals in RF, communication, software and signal processing to join the growing Instrument Engineering team at Apple. This small team of extraordinary engineers designs and produces precision RF and analog test equipment used to calibrate and validate cellular (5G/4G and legacy), Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mmWave, UWB, and NFC radios across millions of iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch units. You'll collaborate closely with RF, software, and signal processing engineers, and work directly with the internal customers who rely on this equipment, to take internal products from concept to mass production, each one built to Apple's exacting standards. If you love chasing an elusive RF problem down to root cause and then writing the automation that makes sure it never comes back, this is your team.

### Description

This is a hands-on RF role that blends bench debugging, RF applications support, customer engagement, and test automation. You'll characterize and troubleshoot RF measurement systems on the bench, develop and maintain automated test and calibration software, and partner across hardware, software, and customer teams to keep our test equipment accurate and reliable at production scale. You'll work across the RF bench, test-automation software, and direct collaboration with internal customers.

### Minimum qualifications

BS in Electrical Engineering or a related field; MS preferred.
3–4 years of hands-on experience in RF, wireless, or test/measurement engineering.
Strong RF fundamentals and demonstrated ability to debug RF problems on the bench using standard RF test equipment (signal generators, analyzers, VNAs, power sensors).
Proven experience developing automated test software in Python and/or C++ from the ground up.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with the ability to work directly with customers and present technical findings to engineering and cross-functional audiences.
Willingness to travel internationally up to 15%.

### Preferred qualifications

Experience with RF system calibration methods, procedures, and traceability.
Recent experience analyzing RF parameters in Python or MATLAB (power, return loss, S-parameters, EVM, ACLR, IQ mismatch, statistics).
Working knowledge of DSP and communications theory.
Familiarity with cellular (5G/4G), Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mmWave, UWB, or NFC radio test and measurement.
Familiarity with RF test equipment vendors (Keysight, Rohde & Schwarz, NI, LitePoint, Anritsu).
CI/CD experience and a track record of integrating automated tests into a pipeline.
Hands-on experience using AI assistants and agentic coding workflows to design, write, and debug production-level engineering software.
Experience applying AI/ML techniques to RF data analysis, modeling, or anomaly detection.
