Job Description:
Senior WIFI Application Engineer critical for FiFEM integration/design win support.
Senior Application Engineer WiFi
Broadcom Inc.
Position Overview
Broadcom is seeking an experienced Senior WiFi Application Engineer to join the Wireless Semiconductor Division organization supporting Broadcom’s industry-leading Wi-Fi connectivity solutions. This customer-facing role works closely with strategic OEMs, ODMs, chipset architects, software engineering, systems engineering, marketing, and field sales to enable successful integration and optimization of Broadcom Wi-Fi chipsets across consumer and enterprise, platforms.
The ideal candidate possesses expertise in IEEE 802.11 technologies, RF systems, wireless protocol analysis,  with a proven ability to solve complex customer issues from product development through mass production.
⸻
Responsibilities
- Serve as the primary technical interface for strategic customers integrating Broadcom Wi-Fi chipsets.
- Support design-in activities from concept through production for Broadcom wireless connectivity solutions.
- Debug complex hardware, firmware, driver, and system-level issues involving Wi-Fi performance and interoperability.
- Analyze customer logs, protocol traces, and RF measurements to identify root causes and recommend solutions.
- Work closely with chipset design, firmware, software, validation, and RF engineering teams to resolve technical issues.
- Optimize Wi-Fi throughput, latency, roaming performance, coexistence, and power consumption.
- Provide technical guidance on:
- IEEE 802.11ax (Wi-Fi 6)
- IEEE 802.11be (Wi-Fi 7)
- Support RF performance characterization including:
- EVM
- Spectral Mask
- ACLR
- Output Power
- Receiver Sensitivity
- PER testing
- Assist customers with board bring-up, software integration, driver configuration, and manufacturing support.
- Create technical documentation, application notes, troubleshooting guides, and customer presentations.
- Train customers and internal engineering teams on Broadcom wireless solutions.
- Drive issue resolution across cross-functional engineering teams while managing multiple customer priorities.
⸻
