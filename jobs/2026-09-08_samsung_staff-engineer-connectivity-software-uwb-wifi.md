# Samsung Semiconductor — Staff Engineer, Connectivity Software, UWB/WIFI

Location: Daily onsite presence at San Jose, CA headquarters (Flexible Work policy)

## Summary

The Samsung Semiconductor Inc., Wireless Connectivity Software Engineering team is seeking highly motivated and enthusiastic Software Engineers to join our team in developing software infrastructure, framework, and protocol stack for 802.11 based WLAN chipsets. We are looking for engineers with expertise in MAC Layer concepts and firmware development. As a member of the Wireless Software team, you will design and develop MAC and physical layer software modules—covering both data and control paths—for various WLAN and UWB chipsets.

## What You'll Do

- Work with 802.11 WLAN chipsets, devices and protocols, including other networking devices
- Collaborate with wider teams for end to end feature deliverables, build and maintain production quality Firmware
- Design and develop modules in embedded firmware for Samsung's Wireless Connectivity solutions
- Major focus will be implementing, unit testing and fixing issues on various features of 802.11 MAC

## What You Bring

- Bachelor's Degree in Engineering or Computer Science Discipline with 10+ years of relevant industry experience, or Master's with 8+ years or PhD with 5+ years in field of wireless communication technologies
- Excellent coding skills in C in embedded space for firmware development is critical
- Ability to debug issues with low-level embedded software, custom/proprietary code and enhancements
- WLAN standards, domain knowledge of Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11) MAC protocols, WFA protocols and coexistence with other wireless technologies such as BT, UWB and cellular
- Knowledge of OFDM, OFDMA principles, beam forming and RF concepts will be additional advantage
- Understanding of QoS concepts and data path optimization algorithms
- Understanding of the Linux Operating System
