# Modem System Test Engineer - Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems

**Apple** · Sunnyvale, California · req `200684982`

Join the Wireless Technologies and Eco-Systems (WTE) organization and
drive innovation that matters! We build and seamlessly integrate
technologies that enrich peoples' lives and deliver the best
user-experience.

## Description

We expect you to be hands-on relying upon diagnostics, analytics, crowd
sourced information, lab/field experimentations as well as code/
architecture review to exploit the opportunities for improvement and work
multi-functionally with platform architecture, standardization,
firmware/protocol development, system test & carrier engineering teams to
drive the enhancement proposals and baseband optimizations. You will be
required to develop advanced system evaluation and debugging capabilities.
Our members enjoy the flexibility to be involved in all aspects of
Protocol software - PHY, RF, SIM, NAS, AS, IP, TCP/UDP and iOS interfaces,
to ensure the most robust and customer friendly software. As part of this
team you'll be impacting iPhone user experience in 100+ countries by
engaging in continuous process improvements for a well tested and robust
software.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Education: Master's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer
  Engineering, Telecommunications, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Protocol & Systems Knowledge: Deep understanding of 3GPP LTE and 5G NR
  specifications across the protocol stack (PHY, MAC, RLC, PDCP, SDAP,
  RRC, NAS) and end-to-end cellular architecture.
- Hands-on Implementation or Validation: Hands-on experience implementing,
  debugging, or validating cellular protocol stacks or physical-layer
  modules using commercial test equipment or open-source wireless
  testbeds.
- Scripting & Automation: Strong proficiency in Python for test
  automation, data processing, and log parsing.
- Physical Layer Fundamentals: Solid grasp of digital communication
  principles, modulation, OFDM, and channel coding schemes.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Applied AI/ML in Wireless: Experience applying machine learning
  frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow) to wireless communications problems
  (e.g., intelligent scheduling, link adaptation, neural decoders, or
  automated log anomaly detection).
- Standards & Pre-Standard Research: Familiarity with 3GPP standardization
  workflows (RAN1, RAN2, RAN4 or RAN5) or research contributions in
  beyond-5G/6G physical and MAC layer architectures.
- Lab Instrumentation: Exposure to wireless test equipment (call boxes,
  network emulators, spectrum analyzers) or RF interface protocols.
