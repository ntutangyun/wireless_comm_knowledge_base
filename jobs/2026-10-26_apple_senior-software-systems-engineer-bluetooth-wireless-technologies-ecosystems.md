SENIOR SOFTWARE SYSTEMS ENGINEER, WIRELESS TECHNOLOGIES & ECOSYSTEMS

Summary:
Join our Bluetooth Wireless team and be part of our innovation engine that drives the future! You will be part of the team where the focus is on enriching people’s lives by designing efficient and useful algorithms and driving well established technologies like Bluetooth and its coexistence with other radios. In this role you will take a systems approach to enable the best performance of wireless radios. This work requires strong understanding of wireless communications, signal processing and software engineering knowledge. If you think you are a great match for this role and are passionate about building awesome Bluetooth solutions, reach out to us.


Description:
In this role, you'll be working on the design of BT systems, focusing on wireless aspects. They will be involved in the early design phase and have the opportunity to influence the software design and provide hardware design recommendations. The candidate will communicate with cross-functional partners, including hardware, software, firmware, marketing, and performance teams, across various radio technologies such as Wi-Fi, cellular, Thread, and satellite. Their responsibilities will include specifying requirements for design improvements and identifying new areas for enhancement.

Responsibilities:
Design and implementation of new features.
Defining, planning, and designing the overall system architectures and protocols for our products.
Work cross-functionally with other software teams.
Debugging failures during field, interoperability, and type-approval testing.

Minimum Qualifications:
5+ years of industry experience.
Strong experience with Python
Broad experience in overall system design and implementation
Solid understanding of multi-threaded programming and synchronization.
Strong problem-solving and debugging skills.
Master’s or PhD degree in Computer Science (or an equivalent qualification) is required.

Preferred Qualifications:
Demonstrated experience mentoring other team members while leading projects.
Excellent communication and collaboration skills, with the ability to work independently or in small teams.
Comfortable with juggling projects across different domains.
Excellent debugging skills in wireless communications environments
