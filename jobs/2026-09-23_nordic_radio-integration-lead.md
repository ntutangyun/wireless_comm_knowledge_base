# Radio Integration Lead

**Nordic Semiconductor** · Trondheim, Norway | Oslo, Norway · req `8350371`

At Nordic Semiconductor we are looking for a Radio Integration Lead to own
how our short-range radio IP gets designed into Nordic products. Our radio
IP is reused across many SoCs and generations, and every integration brings
its own mix of floorplan, supply, clocking, interference and verification
challenges. This role makes sure those integrations go right the first
time.

You will be the technical bridge between the radio IP team and the product
design groups that consume the IP — setting the methodology for how
integration is done and verified, and turning hard-won project experience
into methodologies, guidelines, and documentation that scale beyond a
single chip. You will work across RF and analog design, digital
implementation, verification, systems and validation.

This is a technical leadership role, leading through expertise, methodology
and cross-team influence rather than formal line management, while acting
as the go-to authority on radio IP integration within Nordic.

## Key responsibilities

- Support integration of the radio IP into Nordic products — work
  alongside product design teams from early planning through tape-out,
  resolving integration issues on floorplan, supplies, clocking,
  interfaces, isolation and interference.
- Define and drive the integration and verification methodology —
  establish how integrations are planned, executed, checked and signed
  off, and improve the flows, checklists and automation that make that
  repeatable across projects.
- Own the integration documentation and tools — define what the IP
  deliverables and integration guidelines must contain, drive their
  development and maintenance.
- Coordinate support to the design groups using the radio IP.
- Participate in review of test results — assess verification and silicon
  measurement data from integrated products, judge whether the radio meets
  expectations in context, and drive root-cause work when it does not.
- Keep integration status and risk visible.

## Key Qualifications

- Bachelor, MSc or PhD in Electronics, Telecommunications, Physics or
  equivalent — or equivalent practical experience.
- Several years of experience with IC design or integration, including
  hands-on work on RF/analog IP or mixed-signal SoCs.
- Practical knowledge of integration-critical topics: power supply and
  regulation, clocking and reference distribution, substrate and supply
  noise coupling, isolation, ESD and floorplan constraints.
- Familiarity with mixed-signal implementation and verification flows, and
  with the deliverables an IP hand-off consists of.
