# Entry Level: Test & Systems Engineer - ISAC Over-the-Air Testing & Field Systems

**Ericsson** - Lund, Skåne, Sweden - posted 2026-11-24

About this opportunity
Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) turns the mobile network into a sensor: the same radio that carries traffic can also detect and track objects such as drones. We are now taking ISAC from the lab out into the air and into the field, and we are looking for two engineers who want to own how it is tested and how the systems behind it are built and kept running.
You will be part of the team that runs ISAC testing in the Lund Test Network (LTN), our live over-the-air test network in Lund, and that builds and operates our ambulating ISAC systems: self-contained, mobile sensing units that we take to field sites and that are now being replicated for deployments together with partners around the world. When ISAC developers need to see how their features behave in the real world, or a partner needs their system up and running, you are the one who makes it happen.
You will work side by side with the ISAC developers, as part of the same team. There is no hand-over between "development" and "test": you are involved from the moment a feature takes shape, developers join you in the field, and what you find goes straight back into the next iteration. This seamless cooperation is what lets us go from idea to over-the-air result in days rather than months.
What you will do
Testing
Take ownership of ISAC over-the-air testing in the Lund Test Network: keeping the test setup available, documented and ready to use.
Handle test requests from ISAC developers, from understanding what they need to verify to planning and executing the test.
Configure and operate test scenarios with stationary LTN radio nodes as well as moving systems, including drone flights and mobile setups.
Take part in field measurement campaigns outside Lund.
Collect and analyze measurement data, logs and traces, and work with developers to explain deviations and find defects.

Field systems
Build, integrate and maintain the ambulating ISAC systems: hardware assembly, servers, power and communication cabling, and firmware updates.
Help scale the systems from a handful of units to many, by making the build repeatable: build instructions, checklists and acceptance tests for every new unit.
Maintain and run the test scheme that keeps the system firmware reliable from release to release.
Operate the backend services our partners depend on, with a focus on stability, availability and monitoring.
Support partners and internal collaborators who run the systems: troubleshooting, onboarding and adapting interfaces to their setups.

Across both
Work as part of the development team: join planning, give early feedback on testability, and debug issues together with the developers.
Develop and automate test and deployment tooling, so that tests and new systems become faster to set up, easier to repeat and easier to analyze.
The skills you bring
MSc in Electrical Engineering, Engineering Physics, Computer Science or equivalent.
Programming skills in Python, C, or C++
Interest in radio, RF measurements and hands-on lab and field work.
You enjoy building, cabling and getting a physical system to work.
Comfortable with Linux and basic networking.
Ownership: you take responsibility for a test environment, for the systems you build and for your own development.
A positive, can-do attitude and enjoyment of working closely with developers and partners.
Curiosity about new technology, including using AI to amplify your work.
Meriting:
Experience with drones; an EU drone pilot certificate (A1/A3 or A2) is a plus.
Pre-offer checks including references and relevant background screening will be conducted for final candidates.
