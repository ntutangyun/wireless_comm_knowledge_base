We are looking for (junior) System Validation Engineer who recently graduated from university. In this role you will be responsible for the verification of NXP products and their features from a customer system point of view. 
This role will allow you to broaden your technical scope and gain extensive product know-how, especially in the area of NFC (near field communication) and UWB (ultra-wide band) systems.
 
Your Responsibilities:
- Create and verify system test cases according to a given test-plan

- Analyze, optimize and maintain test cases

- Run system tests both automated and manually

- Document test cases, test results and problem reports

- Support first level issue debugging

- Maintain and extend NFC/UWB system test environment

- Automation of test execution and result reporting

Your Profile:
- Master's degree in Information and Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Telematics, Computer Engineering, Biomedical Engineering or similar

- Good knowledge of Python and/or other scripting languages

- Knowledge of C programming language

- Knowledge in test automation & management is an advantage

- Experience with laboratory equipment like oscilloscope, logic analyzer, etc. is an advantage

- Strong analytic skills

- Accurate and well-structured way of working

- Reliable and autonomous working style

- Proficient in spoken and written English

- Team player, working in a diverse and international team

Please note: The successful candidate may/will be responsible for security related tasks. The assignment may/will be in scope of security certifications, therefore a conscious and reliable way of working is necessary.
