# RF Engineer FSS

**Ericsson** - Rijen, Noord Brabant, Netherlands - posted 2026-11-16

About the Role

Join Ericsson's antenna technology team to develop advanced electromagnetic structures for next-generation mobile communication systems.

We are seeking an RF engineer with hands-on experience in Frequency Selective Surfaces (FSS), sub-10-GHz electromagnetic design, and electromagnetic transparent structures. You will develop and optimise structures that control electromagnetic wave propagation while meeting the demanding performance requirements of modern antenna systems.

We welcome applications from both graduates with relevant research or demonstrator experience and experienced engineers with industrial development expertise. Technical capability and demonstrated practical experience matter more than years of service alone.

What You Will Do

- Design and develop wideband electromagnetic structures for applications across cellular frequency bands between 600 MHz and 10 GHz.

- Simulate and optimise electromagnetic performance using 3D simulation tools, investigating bandwidth, transmission, reflection, insertion loss, angular stability and polarisation effects.

- Evaluate electromagnetic transparency and the interaction of passive structures with radiating elements, radomes and antenna arrays.

- Investigate innovative concepts, including three-dimensional geometries, multilayer arrangements and other advanced periodic structures.

- Validate designs through prototype development, RF measurements and simulation-to-measurement correlation.

- Solve complex engineering challenges and collaborate with antenna, RF, mechanical and materials specialists to translate concepts into practical solutions.

- Communicate technical results through engineering reports, design reviews and knowledge sharing.

What You Will Bring

- A degree in Electrical Engineering, Communications Engineering, Applied Physics or a related discipline.

- Demonstrated hands-on experience with Frequency Selective Surfaces (FSS) through industrial development, academic research, a thesis, laboratory work or a demonstrator.

- Practical experience with RF or electromagnetic structures operating below 10 GHz, within the 600 MHz–10 GHz range.

- Understanding of electromagnetic wave propagation, periodic structures, transmission and reflection behaviour, and electromagnetic transparency.

- Practical experience with 3D electromagnetic simulation software, preferably Ansys HFSS or CST Studio Suite.

- Analytical problem-solving skills and proficiency in English.

Nice to Have

- Experience with wideband or multiband designs, three-dimensional structures, multilayer configurations, radomes or electromagnetic bandgap structures.

- RF measurement experience, including vector network analysers, S-parameter characterisation and prototype validation.

- Knowledge of Floquet modes, periodic boundary conditions, oblique incidence and equivalent-circuit modelling.

- Experience with Python, MATLAB, simulation automation or AI-assisted electromagnetic design.

- Familiarity with base-station antennas, passive arrays or Active Antenna Systems

The role requires relocation to Rosenheim, Germany.
