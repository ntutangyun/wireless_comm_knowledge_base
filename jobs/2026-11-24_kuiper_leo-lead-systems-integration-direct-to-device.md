# Lead Systems Integration, Direct-to-Device, Amazon Leo

**Amazon (Project Kuiper / Leo)** - US, TX, Austin - posted 2026-11-23

### Summary (page text condensed by the fetch summarizer)

Amazon LEO is building a Low Earth Orbit satellite constellation to provide connectivity worldwide. The team needs a technical professional with hands-on experience across the direct-to-device (D2D) satellite and cellular ecosystem, spanning satellite systems, ground infrastructure, cellular networks, commercial devices, and external partners. The ideal candidate has already worked on D2D, NTN, or satellite-to-cellular systems, understands how the pieces of an end-to-end service fit together, and can translate between satellite, network, device, and customer teams.

### Key responsibilities

- Develop capabilities, processes, and tools to scale D2D services across satellite, network, device, and partner environments.
- Lead cross-functional technical programs, coordinating engineering teams and external partners.
- Work with mobile network operators, device OEMs, RAN vendors, and technology partners to define requirements and resolve technical challenges.
- Map the full D2D service chain (satellite and ground systems, RAN, core networks, devices) to identify dependencies.
- Support system-level technical decisions using working knowledge of satellite, ground, and cellular architectures.
- Build scalable approaches for partner integration, device and system validation, automation, data collection, and operational readiness.
- Take part in lab, integration, and field work when hands-on involvement is needed.

### Basic qualifications

- 5+ years of professional D2D experience, including NTN or direct satellite-to-cellular connectivity.
- Experience across both satellite/ground systems and cellular RAN environments.
- Experience delivering end-to-end D2D programs involving multiple systems and stakeholders.
- Experience working directly with mobile network operators and technology partners.
- Experience with commercial mobile-device ecosystems (e.g., Apple, Samsung, Google) in D2D contexts.

### Preferred qualifications

- Experience building or leading technical tools, automation, or operational capabilities for D2D programs.
