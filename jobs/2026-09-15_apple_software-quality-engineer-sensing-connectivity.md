# Software Quality Engineer, Sensing & Connectivity

**Apple** · San Diego · req `200679705`

## Summary

Join the Sensing and Connectivity team to work on Location Technologies that power critical experiences in Apple products. We build highly innovative and deeply impactful location features based on the core wireless technologies such as GNSS, Ultra Wide Band (UWB), WiFi and Bluetooth. These are used across many of the our products and is the foundation of Maps and Fitness applications, Proximity sensing features such as the ground breaking AirTags UWB Precise finding and Indoor Location/iBeacons - among many more. As a member of our dynamic group, you will have the rare and rewarding opportunity to craft upcoming products that will delight and inspire our customers. We’re looking for an individual to help us build automation infrastructure, automated tests, and tools to enhance our QA processes and deliver the best location experience for our customers. Are you highly technical, a strong communicator, collaborative, and have great programming skills? If so, we’d love to hear from you!

## Description

The Location Software Quality team undertakes special projects for the Sensing and Connectivity groups at Apple. These projects have ranged from developing data crowdsourcing systems to prototyping apps & features for future OS releases. We are looking for an engineer with deep understanding of wireless technologies and the possibilities such technologies hold for improved positioning. You will work closely with developers and other cross functional stake holders to understand the features and use data-driven test results to influence and craft the end product. You will use your programming skills to automate test processes, data analysis, and reporting.

## Key Qualifications / Responsibilities

Automating E2E use cases and building data processing pipelines
Designing test plans around engineering requirements and collaborating with cross functional teams
Driving product / feature development to completion to ship high quality customer experiences
Supporting R&D and other investigative experiments

## Minimum Qualifications

Bachelors Degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent experience.
3+ years of experience writing Python code
Experience with or designed automated build & test systems
Excellent knowledge of software development lifecycle, testing methodologies, and testing tools
Ability to drive cross-functional projects and communication, understanding dependencies while ensuring tight timelines are met with high quality deliverables

## Preferred Qualifications

Laser-focused on the smallest details that are meaningful to our customers.
You have worked in fast-paced, agile environment, and can pivot easily to support new user requirements
Experience with wireless technologies: GNSS, Ultra Wide Band (UWB), WiFi and Bluetooth
