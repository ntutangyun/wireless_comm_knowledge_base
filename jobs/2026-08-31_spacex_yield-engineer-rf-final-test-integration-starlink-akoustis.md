# Yield Engineer, RF Final Test Integration (Starlink/Akoustis)

**SpaceX (Starlink)** — Bastrop, TX

RESPONSIBILITIES: 

- Develop and implement RF test cases and calibration procedures for BAW filter devices operating in the 2 GHz to >10 GHz range

- Establish strong linkages between wafer probe data and final RF test performance, driving root cause analysis of yield detractors

- Use statistical tools such as SPC and DOE to monitor, control, and improve yield across the manufacturing flow

- Develop and maintain Cost of Ownership (CoO) and risk models to evaluate probe test strategies and ensure effective test coverage

- Sustain and optimize workflow and throughput in a high-mix RF test work center, driving continuous improvements in cycle time and cost

- Collaborate with design teams to provide feedback on DFM (Design for Manufacturability) and yield optimization opportunities

- Lead and mentor test operators, ensuring proper execution of test plans and maintenance of clean, organized workflows

- Support installation and qualification of new test tools, including both capability enhancements and capacity expansions

- Work closely with operation team and tool vendors to resolve equipment and process-related issues

- Create and maintain Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), engineering instructions, and test documentation

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS: 

- Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, physics, materials science, or other STEM discipline

- 1+ years of experience in RF testing, yield engineering, or semiconductor manufacturing(internship or professional)

PREFERRED QUALIFICATIONS: 

- 2+ years of experience in RF test development or semiconductor yield engineering

- Working knowledge of RF measurement principles, including S-parameters, insertion loss, return loss, and calibration

- Familiarity with RF BAW filter technology and associated test challenges

- Experience using automated probe stations, VNAs, and RF measurement tools

- Strong analytical skills with demonstrated ability to perform data analysis, root cause analysis, and yield correlation

- Proficiency in Statistical Process Control (SPC), DOE, and related quality improvement methodologies

- Familiarity with scripting and automation (e.g., Python, LabVIEW, MATLAB) for test or data analysis is a plus

- Ability to work independently and cross-functionally in a high-mix, fast-paced production environment

- Excellent verbal and written communication skills for collaboration across engineering, operations, and design teams
