# ASIC Design Engineer, Satellite Communication

**Amazon (Project Kuiper)** · US, CA, San Diego · req `10543752`

Amazon Leo seeks an ASIC Digital Design Engineer to develop digital chip SOCs for satellite communications. The role involves implementing wireless system architecture in silicon from system specification to chip specification to RTL, and optimizing for performance, power, and cost while meeting customer requirements for throughput and latency.

## Basic Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or related field
- Experience troubleshooting/debugging technical systems with strong analytical skills and attention to detail
- Experience verifying at multiple levels of logic from IP blocks to SoCs to full system testing

## Preferred Qualifications

- Master's degree in Electrical or Communications Engineering
- Formal verification techniques experience
- Background with RF standards (4G/5G, WiMAX, 802.11ad, DVB-S2/DVB-C)
- Industry standard tools and scripting languages (Python or Perl)
- Experience with ARM, DSP ISAs, DMAs, NoC

Salary range: $136,000–$184,000 USD annually. U.S. citizenship or permanent residency required (export control).
