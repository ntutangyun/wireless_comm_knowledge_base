# Satellite Terminal System Engineer, Customer Terminal

Amazon Leo is an initiative to launch a constellation of Low Earth Orbit satellites that will provide low latency, high-speed broadband connectivity to unserved and underserved communities around the world. Come work at Amazon!Key job responsibilitiesSatellite Terminal System Engineer, you will be responsible for Customer Terminal (CT) integration, firmware/software development, implementation, test and validation of various CT configurations. You will be working in a collaborative environment with a multi-disciplinary team. In this role, your responsibilities include:• Define CT systems architectures/sub-systems and specifications• Design tradeoffs of various CT architectures including RF and performance simulation to support tradeoff decisions• Develop integration & test Software & Firmware for lab verification measurements.• Develop, maintain, and support customer terminal validation hardware• Proactive engagement with internal and external development partners on Systems’ hardware and software architectures and implementation• Ensure delivered work meets technical, functional, performance, regulatory and business requirements• Perform in-field test and validation of various CT architectures• Expect up to 20% of travel depending on the phases of the projectsExport Control Requirement:Due to applicable export control laws and regulations, candidates must be a U.S. citizen or national, U.S. permanent resident (i.e., current Green Card holder), or lawfully admitted into the U.S. as a refugee or granted asylum.A day in the lifeAs a Satellite Terminal System Engineer on the Leo Customer Terminal Team, you will work daily with hardware, RF, antenna, silicon, product design engineers, and the Operations team in a collaborative environment. Your focus will be on architecting, integrating, and verifying customer terminal functional performance to ensure they delight our customers.About the teamThe Leo Customer Terminal team is responsible for developing both outdoor & indoor devices, enabling customers internet service via the Leo satellite network. We own the entire process from early prototypes through mass production including requirements documents, architecture definition, hardware development, and all integration/verification testing.
