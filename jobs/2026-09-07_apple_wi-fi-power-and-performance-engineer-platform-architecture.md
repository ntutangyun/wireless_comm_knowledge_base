# Apple — Wi-Fi Power and Performance Engineer - Platform Architecture

## Summary

Apple's Platform Architecture group is looking for a motivated engineer with a strong background in Wi-Fi and Bluetooth measurements and analysis. We are seeking a team member who is self-driven with initiative and passion for Wi-Fi connectivity on consumer devices.

You will work with a team to provide power and performance data on shipping Wi-Fi/"WLAN" and BT combo solutions and inform future direction. This work is a vital component of the quality and efficiency of Apple's future products.

## Responsibilities

- Operate industry standard and in-house wireless test equipment.
- Gather Wi-Fi power timelines using our in-house DAQ.
- Work in a lab environment with other wireless power/performance engineers.

## Minimum Qualifications

- BS degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field.
- Experience operating wireless test equipment such as Octoscope/Spirent or Keysight Wi-Fi UXM.
- Experience gathering power time series traces for Wi-Fi power dashboards, common Wi-Fi power and/or radio performance use cases (scan, transmit, receive, RvR).
- Experience with modern smartphone and laptop/portable wireless architecture, from AP to Antenna, for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, UWB, NFC.
- Experience with black box testing on consumer samples.

## Preferred Qualifications

- MS or Ph.D. degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or related field.
- 10+ years relevant industry experience.
- Experience with modern standards including 802.11a/b/g/n/ax/be, BT Classic, BT LE, GNSS, LTE, 5G NR.
- Strong passion for fast connectivity, Wi-Fi speed and coverage, and consumer electronics.
- Familiarity with Wi-Fi baseband marketplace, market players, discrete vs integrated markets, and trends.
- Understanding of foundational RF concepts which drive Wi-Fi and BT power in the real world on live networks and devices.
- Proficiency in a data processing language/library such as Python3, Pandas, Scipy, Numpy, Matplotlib, Matlab or similar, and basic scripting (Bash).
- Common end user benchmarking applications and tools (iPerf3, IxChariot, Keysight TestApp, etc).
