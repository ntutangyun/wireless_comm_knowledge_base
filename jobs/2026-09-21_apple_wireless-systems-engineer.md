# Wireless Systems Engineer

**Apple** · Cupertino, California · req `200682520`

Join Apple's System RF organization and drive innovation that matters! We're
looking for engineers who will re-imagine the way wireless performance of a
device is analyzed and work cross-functionally with teams to deliver the best
in class wireless performance for our customers. Our engineers help
architect, research, and design Apple's world-class wireless products.

## Description

The job role will entail wireless performance research focused primarily on
understanding how various factors like antenna performance, modem
performance, SAR, RF algorithms, user interaction with device, channel
conditions, co-ex and interference will impact wireless performance and in
turn the end user experience. You will use these findings to work with
cross-functional teams to help drive antenna, wireless algorithms and system
level design and architecture changes.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Masters degree in Electrical or Telecommunications Engineering or
  equivalent.
- Strong understanding of cellular, WiFi or GNSS technologies.
- Test automation experience. Python preferred and fluency with AI tools.

## Preferred Qualifications

- 3+ years industry experience in RF and/or wireless systems.
- Experience working on physical layer wireless algorithms.
- Strong background in wireless communications.
- Capturing and analyzing baseband logs to root cause performance issues.
