# Applied R&D Engineer 3 - IVT

**Nokia** - India - posted 2026-10-27

The Applied R&D Engineer conducts target-oriented research to directly apply findings to the specification, design, further development, and incremental improvement of products, services, systems, tools, processes, etc. Integrates, verifies, tests, and modifies SW / HW / system components and capitalises on innovative solutions to meet particular requirements and specifications.

- Strong understanding on “IEEE 802.11 standards (a/b/g/n/ac/ax/be/etc) , Voip, SIP and related network protocols (TCP/IP, DHCP, DNS) embedded systems (drivers, firmware on Linux/RTOS(OpenWRT/PrplOS)),   performance , statistics and Management protocols: OMCI, TR069/TR098/TR181, USP.
- Good Understanding Qualcom/Mediatek/RealTek WiFi chipsets/solutions
- Good understanding on new Wi-Fi System-on-Chips (SoCs) , drivers, integrate with hardware.
- Well versed with debugging tools like ASAN,KSAN, Valgrind Memory Profiling Tool, GDB.
- Understanding different SoC Architecture including 32 bit, 64bit and respective tool chains and able to collaborate with different Vendors to make the solution up and running.

You have:
-  Bachelor or above, telecommunication, electronic engineering, computer science, or related major preferred with 5 to 10 years strong development & debugging understanding on C / C++ in multi process/ threaded environment with following skills.
- Strong understanding on “IEEE 802.11 standards (a/b/g/n/ac/ax/be/etc) , Voip, SIP and related network protocols (TCP/IP, DHCP, DNS) embedded systems (drivers, firmware on Linux/RTOS(OpenWRT/PrplOS)),   performance , statistics and Management protocols: OMCI, TR069/TR098/TR181, USP.
- Good Understanding Qualcom/Mediatek/RealTek WiFi chipsets/solutions
- Good understanding on new Wi-Fi System-on-Chips (SoCs) , drivers, integrate with hardware. 

It would be nice if you also had:
- Strong understanding on Linux , IPC techniques, user space and kernel space deep understanding and good understanding on network architecture is preferred
- Understanding of IPV4/IPV6 addressing and TCP/IP and basic understanding of L2/L3 routing, Multicast/IPTV protocols
- Working on GPON technologies is added advantage
