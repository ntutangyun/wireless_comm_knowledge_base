# Cisco — Senior Embedded Wireless Software Engineer

## Meet the Team

The Cisco Wireless Feature & System Engineering team builds end-to-end wireless capabilities across access points, controllers, and cloud-managed platforms. We design features that span device software, control planes, and management systems to deliver seamless, secure, and high-performance enterprise Wi-Fi.

## Your Impact

As a Senior Embedded Software Engineer, you will lead development of key software components and subsystems for wireless access points. You will drive design decisions, ensure high-quality implementation, and collaborate across teams to deliver scalable and reliable systems.

In this role:

- Lead design and development of software subsystems and features
- Drive architectural decisions within your area of ownership
- Debug and resolve complex issues across multiple layers (networking, OS, system)
- Collaborate with platform, radio, QA, and cloud teams for system integration
- Optimize performance, scalability, and reliability of the system
- Mentor junior engineers and provide technical guidance
- Leverage and promote AI-assisted development practices within the team

## Minimum Qualifications

- 7+ years of experience in embedded or systems software development
- Strong expertise in C/C++ and software design
- Deep understanding of Linux systems and networking protocols (TCP/IP, L2/L3)
- Experience designing and owning subsystems or large features
- Strong debugging skills across system layers
- Ability to influence design and execution across teams
- Interest in system-level optimization and scalability

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with wireless systems and Wi-Fi features
- Experience with performance tuning and large-scale deployments
- Familiarity with distributed or cloud-managed systems
- Experience using AI tools to improve development workflows
