# Junior Radio Engineer - Fixed Term

**Nokia** · Ukraine · req `39326`

The team is responsible for the research, development, testing, and optimization of advanced Radio Access Network (RAN) technologies. By combining expertise in RF engineering, network performance, and wireless communications, the team contributes to the design and validation of innovative solutions that support the evolution of mobile networks. Working in a collaborative laboratory environment, team members are involved in RF measurements, troubleshooting, performance analysis, and the continuous improvement of next-generation connectivity technologies.

This is a 12-month fixed-term opportunity with possibility of extension.

## Responsibilities

- Support the design and planning of radio network elements and RF links.
- Perform RF measurements and analyze results using laboratory measurement equipment.
- Assist in the configuration, testing, and troubleshooting of radio equipment.
- Document test procedures, measurement results, and technical findings.
- Collaborate with transport and core network engineers on end-to-end system integration.
- Assist in the design, implementation, and testing of RAN features for 4G/5G networks.
- Participate in the analysis and optimization of radio network performance.
- Support the integration and verification of new RAN software and hardware components.

## Qualifications

- Currently pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Telecommunications, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field.
- Basic understanding of wireless communication principles (e.g., OFDM, MIMO, cellular concepts). Knowledge of radio network design principles and RF planning fundamentals.
- Experience working with measurement equipment (spectrum analyzers, signal generators, or similar).
- Familiarity with 4G (LTE) and/or 5G (NR) architecture and protocols.
- Basic understanding of radio propagation and antenna theory.

**Nice to have:** experience with network simulation tools (NS-3, MATLAB), basic Python/C++, Linux familiarity, prior hands-on RF lab work.
