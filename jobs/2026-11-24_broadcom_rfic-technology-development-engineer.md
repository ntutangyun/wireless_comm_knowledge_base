# RFIC Technology Development Engineer

**Broadcom** - San Jose, California, US - posted 2026-11-18

Senior RF IC technology integrator supporting RF IC technologies for cellular front-end module development. Specializing in RF power amplifier technologies e.g. GaAs HBT, GaN HEMT. Part of a global team that develops RF semiconductor device (GaAs HBT, RF SOI, RF CMOS, GaN HEMT) and MMIC (power amplifiers, low-noise amplifiers, RF switches, antenna tuners) technology for the wireless communications industry. Collaborate with external foundry R&D and integration teams on new technology qualification and applications support. Support internal design, modeling, and quality teams to define and execute evaluation and qualification plans and provide design applications support. Take a leading role in RF IC process technology development, providing design enablement for key RF product lines.

The successful candidate will have hands-on experience in RF semiconductor device fabrication and operation, IC foundry process control, MMIC process integration, new technology qualification, process design kit (PDK) development, RF circuit design and modeling, and RF test development and execution. Has experience with design of experiments, parametric/yield data analysis, and can drive new developments and existing technology improvements at external foundries. Is capable of independent project management from concept/planning phase through execution of transfer to high-volume manufacturing. Facilitates clear communication with external foundry collaborators and internal design and validation teams. Can perform risk assessment and manage technology risk mitigation activities for multiple projects in parallel. Typically possesses a graduate degree in electrical engineering and has multiple years of experience in the field. 

Key Qualifications
·     5+ years of experience in semiconductor process technology and RF applications
·     Broad knowledge of semiconductor technologies for RF/microwave applications
·     Experience as technical lead for device and/or IC technology development
·     Experience with layout and circuit simulation tools (Cadence Virtuoso, Keysight ADS)
·     Experience with device model development and validation, PDK integration
·     Experience layout design rule development and validation, PDK integration
·     Experience with parametric and yield data analysis. Proficiency with statistical data analysis tools
·     Experience with yield failure mechanisms and tools for root cause finding (EFA, PFA)
·     Familiarity with device reliability degradation mechanisms, test methods, and lifetime modeling
·     Experience with device and circuit DC and RF electrical characterization techniques and equipment
·     Exceptional data analysis, problem solving, and interpersonal skills

Education/Experience
Bachelor's degree in Engineering/Computer Science and 12+ years of related experience or Masters degree in Engineering/Computer Science and 10+ years of related experience
