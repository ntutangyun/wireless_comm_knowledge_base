## Summary

Join our Connectivity, Cellular, and Satellite team to pioneer the next generation of Multi-Radio Coexistence (Coex). In this role, you will architect and optimize the intelligent real-time scheduling algorithms that allow concurrent wireless technologies—Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Satellite, Thread , Cellular and next-gen protocols—to operate seamlessly in tight shared spectrum without missing a beat.

If you thrive at the intersection of complex radio architectures, concurrent resource arbitration, and world-class user experience, come build connectivity that enriches lives.

## Description

As a Multi-Radio Coex Software  Engineer, you will take an active, influential role in shaping wireless software and systems from concept to release. You will partner closely with development and systems teams across Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Cellular, Thread, and Satellite to tackle critical coexistence challenges, prioritize concurrent traffic, and eliminate mutual RF interference.

You will drive architectural enhancements, identify coverage gaps, and design next-generation workflows—including developing and maintaining AI-based automation frameworks across complex & real-world multi-radio scenarios.

## Responsibilities

Early Design & Architecture Influence: Engage in early design phases to evaluate wireless coexistence architectures, review software/firmware specifications, and provide cross-functional hardware design recommendations.
Coex Strategy: Develop end-to-end qualification strategies focusing on multi-radio arbitration, real-time priority scheduling, latency control, and throughput optimization.
AI-Driven Automation: Identify high-impact automation opportunities; develop and maintain intelligent, AI-based test automation frameworks to qualify complex concurrent radio states.
Cross-Functional Leadership: Collaborate closely with SW, FW, HW, Systems, and Performance teams across Cellular, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Thread, and Satellite domains to drive root-cause analysis, and specify design improvements.
Quality & Coverage Defense: Identify gaps in test coverage, eliminate testing redundancies across teams, and establish rigorous regression suites to safeguard highest-quality software release.

## Minimum Qualifications

Master's or Ph.D. degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent practical experience.
3 to 5 years of experience in a Wireless Systems Engineering role
In-depth understanding of Multi-Radio Coexistence (Coex) scheduling, real-time priority arbitration, and concurrent wireless behavior across technologies such as Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Cellular, Thread, and Satellite.
Excellent communication skills with the ability to articulate technical concepts to diverse audiences across hardware, software, firmware, and performance teams.
Hands-on experience for Systems engineering across multi-radio technologies under concurrent traffic and high-interference conditions.
Proficiency in deep dive analysis, with a strong willingness to engage in both manual exploration and automation.
Proven track record designing, building, and maintaining robust test automation frameworks.
Demonstrated ability to investigate complex multi-radio failures, prioritize by customer impact, and propose clear technical resolutions.
Proven ability to work under tight schedules and deliver reliable, high-quality results on time.
