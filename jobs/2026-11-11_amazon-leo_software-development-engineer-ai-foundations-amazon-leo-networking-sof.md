Amazon Leo is Amazon's Low Earth Orbit satellite network, built to deliver fast, reliable internet to customers beyond the reach of existing networks. The AI Foundations team builds AI agents that turn Leo's best troubleshooting expertise into diagnoses in minutes instead of hours, helping keep the network available for every customer.

As a Software Development Engineer on AI Foundations, you will design and build production agentic systems on AWS that reason over real satellite, ground, and network data. You will work directly with principal engineers and network operators, and your work will shape how Leo is operated at scale.



Key job responsibilities
- Design, build, and operate AI agents and services that diagnose network and connectivity issues using telemetry, logs, and engineering knowledge.
- Turn proven engineering tools and data sources into reusable agent tools so teams across Leo can build on them.
- Build evaluation harnesses that measure agent accuracy, coverage, and latency, and use them to gate launches.
- Own your services in production: on-call, operational metrics, root-cause analysis, and security reviews.
- Partner with principal engineers, operators, and program managers to turn ambiguous problems into shipped features.
- Write clear design docs, give thoughtful code reviews, and help teammates adopt agentic development practices.

A day in the life
You might start the morning reviewing overnight evaluation results and spotting a class of network failures your agent misdiagnosed. You trace the gap to a missing data source, then sit down with the principal engineer who owns that subsystem to agree on how to expose their tool to the agent. After lunch you build the integration and add new test cases, then review a teammate's pull request. Late afternoon, you join an operations review where an operator walks through a real incident your agent helped resolve, and you leave with a clear next priority.

About the team
AI Foundations is a new team in Amazon Leo's networking software organization. Our mission is to help Leo reach and sustain its network availability goals by turning the organization's best troubleshooting expertise into AI agents anyone can use. We build the shared foundations (agent tooling, evaluation, and integrations) that let engineering and operations teams across Leo ship their own agents safely. Because the team is early, you will help shape its architecture, engineering bar, and culture. We value builders who are curious about how satellite networks work, measure what they ship, and prefer simple systems that operators trust.
