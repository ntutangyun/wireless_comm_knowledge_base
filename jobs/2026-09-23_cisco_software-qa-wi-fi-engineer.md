# Software QA Wi-Fi Engineer

**Cisco** · Bangalore, India · req `2022866`

## Meet the Team

You will join the MR Firmware Engineering team that is fully embedded
within Cisco's Wireless Software Engineering Org. The Quality team builds
and runs any and all software and hardware tests to ensure that new
releases meet our quality and reliability standards. With the large
footprint of over 12 million wireless access points deployed worldwide,
quality is our highest priority.

## Your Impact

- Design and scale wireless testbed operations to test products and
  software before market launch.
- Design and execute test plans to support next-generation Wi-Fi products
  and features.
- Support firmware engineers in executing test cases, understanding test
  results, reproducing and investigating bugs.
- Collaborate with Functional and Release engineers to plan release
  timelines for upcoming products and software releases.
- Collaborate with Automation engineers to scale, improve and maintain
  testbed operations.
- Collaborate with Platform Engineering, Hardware and Product Management
  teams to develop reliable and scalable testing processes for existing
  and upcoming products and software features.
- Collaborate with Wi-Fi chipset vendors on a regular basis to
  troubleshoot and solve low-level Wi-Fi issues that might block releases.
- Own test topologies and testbeds from topology-diagram inception, to
  proof-of-concept testbeds that scale well, all the way to a production
  test in our release cycle.

## Minimum Qualifications

- BS/MS in Computer Networking, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science
  or equivalent experience.
- 3+ years of experience in Wi-Fi QA and testing technologies/tools.
- Strong foundation in the fundamentals of computer wired and wireless
  networking (VLANs, DHCP, TCP, VoIP).
- Strong foundation of 802.11 wireless standards, RF, channels, and specs
  on 2.4GHz, 5GHz and 6GHz.
- Proficiency in writing quality code with one of the programming or
  scripting languages in Ruby or Python.
- Hands-on experience leveraging AI assistants (e.g., OpenAI, Gemini,
  Claude) to streamline QA workflows, draft Wi-Fi test plans, and parse
  test results.
- Experience with UI automation tools like Selenium or Cypress.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience in product and software qualification testing.
- Experience in automation framework programming.
- Experience in configuring wireless routers or access points.
- Experience in working with Gitlab, Jenkins and Jira.
