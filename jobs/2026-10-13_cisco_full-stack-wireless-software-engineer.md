Meet the team
The Cisco Wireless Feature & System Engineering team builds end-to-end wireless capabilities across access points, controllers, and cloud-managed platforms. We design features that span device software, control planes, and management systems to deliver seamless, secure, and high-performance enterprise Wi-Fi.

Your Impact
As a Full-Stack Wireless Software Engineer, you will contribute to building and validating wireless features across the stack—from access points to controllers and management systems. This is a full development and test role, where you will own features from implementation through validation and automation.
In This Role, You Will
- Develop and integrate wireless features across AP, controller, and management layers
- Write and maintain software in C/C++ and scripting languages (Python)
- Design and execute test plans, and develop automation for feature validation
- Analyze logs, packet traces, and system behavior to debug issues
- Reproduce issues and validate fixes across system components
- Set up and use lab environments for testing wireless scenarios
- Collaborate with platform, radio, QA, and cloud teams
- Use AI-assisted tools to improve development, testing, and debugging efficiency

Required Skills
- MS + ~3 years or BS + ~5 years of experience
- Strong coding skills in C/C++ and Python
- Good understanding of networking fundamentals and Wi-Fi basics (802.11)
- Experience or interest in testing, automation, and system validation
- Strong debugging and analytical skills
- Interest in working across development and test domains

Preferred Skills
- Familiarity with WPA2/WPA3 security basics
- Exposure to controllers or cloud-managed systems
- Experience with Wireshark or similar tools
