# Antenna & Wireless Engineering Internship

**Apple** · Madrid, Spain · req `200684586`

Join our Antenna & Wireless engineering team and drive innovation that
matters! We're looking for engineers with strong fundamentals in RF and
antenna/wireless system design who want to work at the front of the product
cycle, where the constraints are loosest and the impact is largest. These
teams of world-class engineers help architect and design the best wireless
products at Apple. We take the earliest industrial design concepts and
answer the hard questions: what antennas can fit, what performance is
achievable, what the radio architecture should be, and what we are willing
to commit to. Our decisions set the RF targets that every downstream
wireless team designs against.

## Description

Imagine what you could do here. At Apple, new ideas have a way of becoming
products, services, and customer experiences very quickly. Do you want to
impact billions of users by developing an extraordinary product with a
prime focus on achieving the best wireless performance? You will become
part of a hands-on development team that furthers engineering perfection,
creativity and innovation.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Currently pursuing a BS, MS, or Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering, Physics,
  or a related field.
- Strong understanding of RF fundamentals, antenna theory, and
  communication theory.
- Experience with antenna radiation patterns, including handling,
  visualizing, and analyzing pattern and efficiency data.
- Ability to write efficient, scalable code in MATLAB, Python, C, or C++
  for modeling and data analysis.
- Experience with EM simulation tools such as CST, XFdtd, or HFSS.
- Hands-on experience with RF lab equipment such as spectrum analyzers,
  vector signal generators, network analyzers, power sensors, and
  oscilloscopes.
- Energy and drive to thrive in an ambiguous, fast-moving, startup-like
  environment.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Understanding of RF channel modeling, including knowledge of ray tracing
  techniques for wireless propagation analysis.
- Antenna design and integration experience in space-constrained consumer
  products.
- Testing experience in anechoic chambers and reverberation chambers.
- Familiarity with RF calibration methods and the RF metrics used to judge
  product performance.
- Experience with wireless link budget, sensitivity, and system-level
  performance modeling.
- Basic understanding of wireless standards such as NR, LTE, UWB, Wi-Fi,
  Bluetooth, and GNSS.
