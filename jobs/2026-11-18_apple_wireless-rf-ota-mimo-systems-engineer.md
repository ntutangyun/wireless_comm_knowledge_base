# Wireless RF OTA MIMO Systems Engineer

## Summary
At Apple, we work every single day to craft products that enrich people’s lives. Do you love working on challenges that no one has solved yet? Do you like changing the game? As a Wireless RF OTA MIMO Systems Engineer, you will have the unique and rewarding opportunity to shape upcoming products that will delight and inspire millions of Apple’s customers every day. Apple's Wireless Systems team is looking for an engineer to develop the test systems and methodologies that characterize over-the-air performance across different wireless technologies for Apple's wireless products.

## Description
In this role, you will own the methodology behind our radiated MIMO measurements. You will design and verify OTA test systems that reproduce real-world propagation environments in the lab, develop test strategies for new wireless features as they come to market, and turn measurement data into clear insights about device performance. You will partner closely with cross-functional teams to find and resolve system-level issues early in the product cycle, and your work will directly influence design decisions across Apple’s wireless products.

## Minimum qualifications
Bachelor’s Degree in EE or equivalent with 10+ years of relevant work experience.
Strong technical background in wireless communication, including wireless protocols, antennas, and radio propagation.
Working experience with MIMO characteristics (at a system level), antenna design and requirements for modern wireless communication systems such as LTE, 5G NR (FR1/FR2) and Wi-Fi.
Strong problem solving and analytical skills.
Strong programming skills (MATLAB/Python).

## Preferred qualifications
Master’s Degree in EE or equivalent with 10+ years of relevant work experience.
Working experience with different lab equipment: network analyzers, signal generators, signal analyzers, call boxes, channel emulators.
Experience with OTA test systems such as multi-probe anechoic chambers (MPAC), radiated two-stage (RTS), or reverberation chambers.
Familiarity with regulatory requirements and certification process for CTIA and 3GPP, including MIMO OTA test methodologies.
Experience with post-processing techniques and algorithms for extracting MIMO channel characterization metrics and other key performance indicators.
Knowledge of spatial channel models and their implementation in channel emulators.
Experience building test automation and data analysis pipelines.
Excellent communication skills and the ability to work across hardware, software, and program teams.
