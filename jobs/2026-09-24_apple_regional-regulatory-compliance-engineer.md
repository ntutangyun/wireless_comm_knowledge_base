# Regional Regulatory Compliance Engineer

**Apple** · Taipei, Taiwan · req `200684766`

## Job Summary

The Regulatory Compliance team ensures every product meets global
regulatory requirements while maintaining excellent performance.

In this role, you will be responsible for all Apple product regulatory
compliance activities in Taiwan and Asia Pacific (APAC) region. You will
also be influencing Taiwan and APAC countries' standards/regulations and
working out the certification plans for new technologies/new products to
allow Apple products to be launched in Taiwan and APAC region.

## Description

You will work together with a team of highly skilled project managers,
test engineers and developers, both local and remote.

You need to preempt all upcoming standards/regulations that impact Apple
products and have strong connections with Taiwan and APAC regulators and
be able to influence countries' standards and regulations by direct
advocacy and/or through industry associations.

You also need to work out the certification requirements and test plans
for new technologies and new products. The candidate needs to have strong
RF test/certification experience in BT/WLAN/NFC/UWB/WPT/GSM/UMTS/LTE/5G/MSS.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree EE/Communication Engineering required.
- Deep understanding of wireless technology and RF test experience in
  BT/WLAN/NFC/UWB/WPT/GSM/UMTS/LTE/5G/MSS.
- Familiar with RF test instruments.
- Fluent in English, Mandarin and any other APAC local languages.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Strong relationships with Taiwan and APAC region's regulators, like NCC,
  BSMI etc.
- Experience in influencing regulations/standards changes in Taiwan and
  APAC region.
- 5+ years of Regulatory Compliance Engineer experience.
- Familiar with WW and regional regulatory compliance requirements.
- Master's degree EE/Communication Engineering.
- Ability to work collaboratively with external test labs and
  certification bodies.

## Responsibilities

- Manage/engage external ministries/regulators/certification bodies/agents
  to be updated on the latest/upcoming standards/regulations in Taiwan and
  APAC region, including label, manual, RF/Safety/EMC/SAR standards etc.
- Attend RF/Safety/EMC/SAR technical standards meetings.
- Communicate standards/regulations changes effectively to internal
  cross-function team, assess impact for Apple products and lead
  cross-function team to ensure compliance.
- Influence and advocate ministries/regulators to favour Apple's design.
- Generate test and certification plan for new technologies and new
  products.
- Perform RF/Safety/EMC/SAR certification testing and debugging in country.
- Optimize and obtain regulatory compliance certifications in region on
  time.
- Manage the market surveillance and field escalation.
- Collaborate effectively between standard engineering, test engineering,
  software engineering, hardware engineering internally.
