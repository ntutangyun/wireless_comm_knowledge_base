About the job
As a ASIC RTL Design Engineer at Nordic Semiconductor, you will play an important role in developing the next generation of cutting-edge Wi-Fi SoCs that power connected devices around the world. You will be working on digital IC design, from architecture and RTL implementation through verification, integration, and tape-out, using advanced technologies that push the boundaries of low-power wireless innovation. Collaborating closely with talented experts across architecture, software, verification, and implementation, you will help deliver high-quality silicon solutions that combine outstanding performance, reliability, and energy efficiency. This is an exciting opportunity for a motivated engineer who thrives on solving complex technical challenges, enjoys technical ownership, and is eager to make a tangible impact on products used by millions of people worldwide.

Key Responsibilities
- Front-end design and development of digital ICs for Wi-Fi SoCs, from requirements and architecture through RTL implementation and tape-out.

- Design, integrate, and verify IP blocks, subsystems, and SoCs, ensuring functionality, performance, and quality targets are met.

- Develop and maintain high-quality RTL code and verification environments to deliver robust and reliable silicon solutions.

- Drive SoC integration activities, working closely with architecture, verification, software, DFT, and implementation teams to ensure seamless product development.

- Implement and optimize low-power design techniques, contributing to industry-leading energy efficiency and performance.

- Support ASIC and FPGA synthesis, create and maintain technical specifications, and provide technical guidance and mentoring to fellow engineers.

Key Qualifications
- BSc/MSc in Electronics Engineering or similar

- Experience in digital IC design, verification, synthesis, static timing analysis or pre-silicon prototyping

- Solid understanding of RTL design and verification principles, SoC implementation, synthesis and STA.

- Knowledge of RTL implementation flows and tools, and of downstream constraints: synthesis, timing, clock-domain crossing, low-power/UPF and DFT.

- Strong proficiency in hardware description languages, including SystemVerilog, Verilog and VHDL, with a solid understanding of the end-to-end digital design flow.

- Proficiency in running/debugging LEC and VCLP violations as a design quality sign-off requirement

- Familiarity with functional verification and coverage-driven sign-off.

- Familiarity with scripting (Tcl, Python) and a Linux working environment; industry EDA toolchains

- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English

Personal Skills
The ideal candidate will thrive with solving complex technical challenges and enjoy taking ownership from concept to silicon. With a structured and analytical mindset, you are able to navigate intricate digital designs while keeping quality, performance, and deadlines in focus. You combine strong attention to detail with a practical, results-oriented approach, enabling you to deliver reliable and robust solutions in a fast-paced development environment. As a natural collaborator, you build strong relationships across disciplines and enjoy working closely with architects, verification engineers, software developers, and implementation teams to bring cutting-edge products to life. You are proactive in sharing knowledge, supporting colleagues, and contributing to a culture of continuous learning and technical excellence. Most importantly, you are motivated by innovation and excited by the opportunity to shape the next generation of low-power wireless technology used by millions of devices worldwide.
