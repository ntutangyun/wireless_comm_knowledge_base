## Description

Amazon's Leo Silicon Team is building next-generation modem SoCs for broadband
communication, a constellation of low Earth orbit satellites designed to deliver fast, affordable broadband to unserved and underserved communities around the world. Our Design Verification team is seeking a highly skilled and motivated Senior Formal Verification Engineer to drive formal verification efforts across our complex modem SoC designs.

In this role, you will apply rigorous mathematical methods to prove design correctness and
uncover corner-case bugs that escape simulation-based verification. You will serve as a subject matter expert in formal verification methodologies, mentor engineers across the team, and collaborate closely with RTL designers, simulation-based DV engineers, emulation engineers, and architects to shift verification left and improve overall silicon quality. This is a high-impact role on a team that is scaling verification capabilities with each successive tape-
out and actively integrating AI-centric verification methodologies.


Due to applicable export control laws and regulations, candidates must be a U.S. citizen or national, U.S. permanent resident (i.e., current Green Card holder), or lawfully admitted into the U.S. as a refugee or granted asylum.

Key job responsibilities
● Develop and execute formal verification strategies using JasperGold and/or Questa
Formal for property checking (FPV), connectivity checking (CC), register verification,
and sequential equivalence checking (SEQ)
● Write, review, and debug SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) — including assumptions,
assertions, and cover properties — to verify complex modem SoC design behaviors
across NOC, interconnect, modem IPs and control logic
● Drive convergence on formal proofs by applying abstraction techniques, complexity
reduction strategies, and assume-guarantee reasoning
● Collaborate with RTL designers, architects, and simulation-based DV engineers to
define verification plans that incorporate formal methods alongside UVM-based
simulation and emulation approaches
● Champion a "formal-first" verification strategy by identifying and pursuing
opportunities to apply formal verification to new design blocks — building on our
established formal verification checklist deployed across SoC and NoC partitions
● Develop reusable formal verification infrastructure, including constraint libraries,
parameterized property templates, and automated regression flows integrated into our
CI/CD verification pipeline
● Contribute to coverage closure strategies by leveraging formal coverage analysis to
complement simulation-driven functional coverage
● Mentor and guide DV engineers in formal verification techniques, SVA best practices,
and proof convergence strategies
● Partner with EDA vendors to evaluate new formal verification capabilities and drive
tool improvements that benefit the team

## Basic qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, or equivalent
- 8+ years of experience in ASIC/SoC design verification with a strong focus on formal verification
- Experience with JasperGold and/or Questa Formal
- Strong proficiency in SystemVerilog Assertions (SVA) and formal property specification
- Experience with formal verification applications: FPV, connectivity checking, register verification, equivalence checking, and coverage analysis
- Demonstrated ability to drive proof convergence on complex designs using abstraction, case-splitting, and assume-guarantee techniques
- Proficiency in SystemVerilog and UVM concepts
- Proficiency in leveraging AI/LLM tools to accelerate formal verification workflows including AI-assisted SVA generation, property debugging, proof exploration, and automated constraint refinement

## Preferred qualifications

- Master's or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related field with a focus on formal methods
- Experience verifying complex SoC designs including NoC/interconnect fabrics, memory subsystems, or communication protocol logic
- Familiarity with modem/PHY/DSP architectures or satellite communications systems
- Experience with scripting languages (Python, TCL, Perl) for flow automation and regression management
- Track record of finding critical silicon bugs using formal methods that escaped simulation
- Experience integrating formal verification into continuous integration (CI) workflows
- Familiarity with emulation-based verification and how formal methods complement emulation strategies
- Strong written and verbal communication skills with the ability to influence cross- functional teams
