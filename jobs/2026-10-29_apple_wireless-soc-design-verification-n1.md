# Wireless SOC Design Verification Engineer

**Apple** - Irvine, San Diego, Sunnyvale - posted 2026-10-27

### Summary

Apple’s N1 wireless chip powers connectivity across every new iPhone — and its successor is already taking shape. As a Wireless SOC Verification Engineer, you’ll own your verification scope end-to-end — starting with a well-defined block or feature and growing in scope and complexity as you do. Over time, you’ll build depth in the IPs you know best and breadth across others, from block-level verification up toward subsystem and SOC integration.

### Description

You’ll own verification for your scope — with a mentor’s guidance as you build toward full ownership — on a chip that ships in every new iPhone, starting with a block or feature sized to where you are and expanding from there. You’ll work across the Power Management Unit, the CPU subsystem, on-chip debug infrastructure, PCIe, bus fabric, boot and secure-boot sequencing, and peripherals like SPI and UART — primarily through simulation, with exposure to formal verification as you grow. Low-power is part of the picture too: you’ll get hands-on with power intent verification using UPF within your scope. AI is reshaping how the team works, and you’ll help apply AI-assisted tools for test generation, debug, or verification closure as part of your day-to-day.

### Minimum qualifications

Minimum requirement of a bachelors degree.
Strong academic record (GPA).
Proficiency in SystemVerilog.
Strong analytical thinking and problem-solving skills.

### Preferred qualifications

Familiarity with UVM methodology.
Exposure to PCIe, Bus Fabric, NOC, AHB, AXI-based bus architectures in a UVM environment.
Experience verifying IP or top-level integration for a WiFi or Bluetooth SOC.
Experience with Formal Verification.
In-depth knowledge and experience working with low power design, UPF integration, boot-up, power-cycling, HW/FW interaction verification.
Should be a great teammate with excellent communication skills and the desire to seek diverse challenges.
