# Apple — Wireless RF Systems Validation Engineer (Connectivity)

## Job Summary

At Apple, we work every single day to craft products that enrich people's lives. Do you love working on challenges that no one has solved yet? Do you like changing the game? As a member of our dynamic group, you will have the unique and rewarding opportunity to shape upcoming products that will delight and inspire millions of Apple's customers every day.

Apple's RF System Engineering team is looking for a passionate, self-motivated individual to work on integration of connectivity (WLAN, BT, UWB, Thread) technologies into Apple's wireless products.

## Description

You will validate & characterize wireless system performance across wireless technologies towards industry conformance, regulatory compliance, & use case requirements. You will perform debug and failure analysis of issues with cross-functional engineering teams and drive issues to closure. You will be responsible to complete certification testing. Examples of activities you may be involved in are:

- Executing validation and characterization test plans to meet industry, emissions and performance requirements.
- Bringing-up device-under-test, help define test requirements and implement test systems setups for validation readiness.
- Tracking validation status, analyze results, communicate issues & insights from data to cross functional forum.
- Performing failure analysis to understand low margin or failures making use of experimental SW/FW and modified development hardware.
- Collaborating with vendors, technology suppliers and cross-functional staff to optimize radio performance of the device.
- Supporting manufacturing test and Certification of Apple products.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree.
- Practical knowledge of at least one Connectivity Wireless technology including WLAN, BT, UWB, Thread.
- Experience with bench level wireless testing, debug at the module, reference board or system level.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Master's degree.
- Practical knowledge of at least one of connectivity industry standards including 802.11, Bluetooth SIG, 802.15 requirements, compliance requirements and required testing methodologies.
- Expertise in configuring and operating complex RF test setups for hardware validation. Proven experience in characterizing and validating devices against industry and global regulatory RF emission standards using laboratory equipment such as call boxes, power meters, network analyzers, spectrum analyzers, and signal generators.
- Good understanding of system-level RF emissions and their sources, including RF component characteristics and behavior.
- Experience with device certification processes, including industry certifications (e.g., Bluetooth SIG, FiRa) and regulatory compliance (e.g., FCC, EU, China).
- Debug level experience on connectivity radio systems at the conducted level.
- Strong written and verbal communication skills.
