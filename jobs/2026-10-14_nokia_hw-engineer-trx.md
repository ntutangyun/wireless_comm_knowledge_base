Be part of an innovative and open-minded culture at Nokia, where we develop mainstream radio technology products. Your role will focus on designing, simulating, and testing RF circuits as part of a larger TRX design team, with opportunities to grow into more senior roles.

Responsibilities
-  Design RF circuits for cellular base stations, including ADC, power supplies, transmitters, receivers, and more.
-  Simulate and test RF circuits, ensuring they meet performance and reliability specifications.
-  Document and write technical reports on design processes and outcomes.
-  Participate in design reviews and technical information exchange sessions.
-  Support subcontractors and collaborate with other design engineers and specialists.
-  Stay updated with RF simulation and electromagnetic tools, such as HP ADS, CST, and HFSS.
-  Utilize analytical simulation tools like Matlab and Python for advanced analysis.
-  Work in a team environment with a positive attitude, fostering collaboration.
-  Plan and organize tasks efficiently, ensuring timely project delivery.

Qualifications
-  BSEE with 3 years or MS with 2 years of experience in Electrical Engineering or Physics.
-  Coursework or job experience in RF or Microwave technology is preferred.
-  Knowledge of RF circuit design and signal processing techniques.
-  Good understanding of basic RF engineering principles, including digital modulation/demodulation.
-  Familiarity with RF performance specifications and test methodology.
-  Experience with RF simulation tools is a strong advantage.
-  Proficiency in analytical simulation tools like Matlab and Python.
-  Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written.
-  Technical leadership potential and problem-solving abilities.
-  Ability to travel internationally occasionally, with less than 10% travel expected.
