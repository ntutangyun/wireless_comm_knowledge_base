# Senior NFC/UWB System Validation Engineer (m/f/d)

**NXP** - Gratkorn - posted 2026-10-28

As a Senior NFC/UWB System Validation Engineer, you will play a key role in ensuring the quality, reliability, and performance of NXP’s NFC and UWB solutions from a customer system perspective.
 
You will take ownership of system-level validation activities, drive complex technical investigations, and contribute to the continuous improvement of our validation methods and environments. Working in an international, cross-functional team, you will have the opportunity to deepen your expertise in NFC, UWB and embedded systems, while sharing your experience with other engineers.

Your Responsibilities:
- Develop system test plans based on product and system requirements.
- Analyze, optimize, and maintain system test cases.
- Create and verify system test cases.
- Drive and execute system tests, both automated and manual.
- Document test cases, test results, and problem reports.
- Drive issue analysis and debugging within international, cross-disciplinary project teams.
- Drive continuous improvement and innovation in our daily ways of working.
- Define improvements for the NFC/UWB system test environment and drive their implementation.
- Support and guide junior team members, sharing technical expertise and best practices.

Your Profile:
- Master’s degree in Computer Science, Information and Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or a related field.
- 5+ years of experience in system validation, preferably in the semiconductor industry.
- Good skills in Python and/or other scripting languages.
- Good knowledge of C programming and microcontroller-based systems.
- Experience with test automation and test management.
- Hands-on experience with laboratory equipment such as oscilloscopes and logic analysers.
- Experience with standard interfaces such as SPI, I²C, and UART, as well as RF interfaces in NFC and/or UWB.
- Experience with version control tools.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
- Accurate and well-structured way of working.
- Reliable, self-driven, and autonomous working style.
- Proficient in spoken and written English.
- Team player who enjoys working in a diverse and international environment.

Please note: The successful candidate may/will be responsible for security related tasks. The assignment may/will be in scope of security certifications, therefore a conscious and reliable way of working is necessary.
