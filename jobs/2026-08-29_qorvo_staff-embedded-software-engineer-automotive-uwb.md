**Vision:**

You will be part of the growing automotive software team which manage all our UWB product software development. Following the last embedded software development rules defined by the automotive world this team is implanted cross around the world.  

You will deliver robust, secure, and compliant embedded software for Qorvo’s automotive and industrial platforms (UWB/RTLS, RF connectivity, power management). Embed coding excellence, traceability, and secure-by-design principles into every layer—from MCAL drivers to edge SDKs—ensuring first-pass integration success and audit readiness. 

As a particular motivation factor, your work will directly be used by the major car companies and tier 1 in the world.  

**Description:**

Sr Embedded Software Engineer will be responsible for developing software on automotive. In this role you will be responsible for all firmware development aspects life cycle from the software detailed design & implementation until the integration tests.  

Sr Embedded Software Engineer will engage and work with internal cross-functional engineering teams across the globe as well as collaborate with internal and external technical partners. In this role you will need to be comfortable, experienced and adopt working across time zones, geographical and organizational boundaries as well as ambiguity.  

Sr Embedded Software Engineer will leverage a structured and systematic approach, applying and verifying best practices and Lean Product Development principles such as Front Loading, Risk Detection & Countermeasures. In this role will participate to quote software project development needs to respect the On-Time, On-Quality, On-Cost approach in relation with the software project manager. In this role will be responsible to spread good practices and specific skills to other engineers from the team.  

You will be the technical authority for embedded software design and implementation. 

**Key Responsibilities: **

- All aspects of the software development life cycle:  

- Ensure the link between the SW architecture team and the SW development team  

- Technical study and detailed design definition  

- Software implementation   

- Unit and Integration test  

- Core team member of software project team to develop new products/processes using DFSS tools & techniques and software metrics  

- Work closely with software project management, software architects and quality teams.  

- Be a strong technical partner and provide support to software product development team.  

- Perform firmware safety-oriented analysis (ISO 26262)  

- Leverage structured new product development process and participate to stage gate frameworks  

- Anticipate and alleviate bottlenecks, identify and mitigate risks, provide escalation management, anticipate and make tradeoffs and be responsible to ensure every aspect of the project is properly and promptly executed.  

- Constantly assess what is working, what is not working, identify areas for improvement and consistently drive continuous improvement.  

- Work with Hardware on boot modes, power sequencing, and timing sync. 

- Partner with Systems/Architecture on interface contracts and NFR budgets (latency, memory, power, reliability). 

- Assist FAEs/Customer Support with complex integration issues and field escalations. 

- Secure Software Lifecycle by implementing secure boot, image signing, and OTA update mechanisms with rollback protection and by applying secure coding practices: input validation, memory safety, least privilege, and crypto integration. 

- Ensure backward compatibility, versioning discipline.
