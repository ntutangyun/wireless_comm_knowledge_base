# Senior Modem Engineer, Satellite Communication

**Amazon (Project Kuiper / Amazon Leo)** - San Diego, CA - posted 2026-10-29

### Description

Amazon Leo is an initiative to launch a constellation of Low Earth Orbit satellites that will provide low-latency, high-speed broadband connectivity to unserved and underserved communities around the world. In this role you will: . Engage with modem system teams to translate system-level requirements into hardware specifications and RTL implementation. · Collaborate with system architects on SoC subsystem integration, including IP block configuration, instantiation, and interface validation. · Support synthesis, static timing analysis (STA), and timing closure activities to meet performance and power targets. · Contribute to low-power design implementation and verification, including power domain definition and UPF/CPF development. · Work with verification, physical design, and firmware teams to ensure design quality and first-time-right silicon. · Develop and maintain comprehensive gate-level simulation test plans for verifying ASIC functionality and timing. Analyze simulation results, identify and debug logic errors, and propose solutions. . Work closely with design and verification engineers to validate fixes and ensure design closure. · Develop AI-assisted Design Automation/Debug skills for various aspects of SoC development. Export Control Requirement: Due to applicable export control laws and regulations, candidates must be a U.S. citizen or national, U.S. permanent resident (i.e., current Green Card holder), or lawfully admitted into the U.S. as a refugee or granted asylum. Key job responsibilities . Work closely with the system architects to develop world-class SOC and IP blocks, which meet power, area and performance targets. . Define, configure and integration SoC Subsystems . Own one or more Modem IP blocks. . Contribute to the SoC floor planning effort . Define and develop any necessary support logic . Configure, instantiate and integrate 3rd party IP blocks . Understand low power design & the impact of DFT on the blocks . Perform initial synthesis & timing analysis . Assist verification team in unit verification including test plan development . Assist with debug and bring-up About the team Be part of Leo's sub-team responsible for defining and implementing the digital chip SOCs for communications via Low Earth Orbit satellites and Amazon gateways. This is a unique opportunity to define a state-of-the-art wireless solution with few legacy constraints. The team works with customer requirements and wireless system teams to define modems, high-speed interfaces, embedded processors, and custom solutions in latest CMOS generation technologies.

### Basic Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical/Communications Engineering, or experience working in electrical engineering field - 7+ years of experience in digital design, preferably in SoC design and implementation. - Experience with Modem SoC design and development.

### Preferred Qualifications

- 7+ years of digital design in communication systems experience - Master's or Ph.D degree in Electrical / Communications Engineering. - Deep understanding of modem SoC architecture, including PHY/MAC subsystems and system-level integration. - Familiarity with UVM. - Ability to write assertions and exposure to Formal verification.
