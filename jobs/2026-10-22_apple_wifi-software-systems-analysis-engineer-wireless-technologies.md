WIFI SOFTWARE SYSTEMS ANALYSIS ENGINEER, WIRELESS TECHNOLOGIES & ECOSYSTEMS

Summary:
Join the Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems (WTE) organization and be part of a best-in-class team of engineers, working with multi-functional teams to drive innovation and develop products used by millions worldwide. We are looking for a highly reciprocal individual who can excel in a high-paced environment. Be ready to make something extraordinary when you come here. Dynamic, encouraging people and innovative, industry-defining technologies are the norm at Apple. The people who work here have reinvented and defined entire industries with our products and services. The same real passion for innovation also applies to our business practices - strengthening our dedication to leave the world better than we found it. You should join Apple if you want to help deliver the next amazing Apple product.

Description:
Apple’s Cellular Systems Analysis team is seeking talented, highly motivated and disciplined engineers to work across layers on groundbreaking wireless technologies. You will be responsible for analyzing WiFi/BT functional, performance issues and driving architectural improvements for future Apple product lines with emphasis on improving the overall user experience in terms of performance. You will be working cross functionally with platform architecture, standardization, firmware/host development, system test and QA teams to drive the enhancement proposals from concept to commercialization. Development of advanced system evaluation and debugging capabilities are required on an as needed basis. As part of this team you will impact iPhone, iPad & Mac user experience worldwide.

Minimum qualifications:
Bachelors or Masters in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent experience.
In-depth understanding and strong hands-on experience on 802.11 MAC/PHY protocols, WiFi standards (WiFi-7) & systems debugging

Preferred qualifications:
Hands-on experience working with wireless sniffers and protocol analyzers to debug and root cause system level issues
Hands-on experience with AI/ML tools, expertise on building agents to streamline data analysis and issue triage
Proficient understanding of TCP/IP suite of protocols
Excellent analytical and debugging skills
Effective written and oral communication skills, particularly with technical content
Highly organized, creative, motivated, and passionate about achieving results
