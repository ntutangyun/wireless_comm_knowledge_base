# Senior Software Engineer, Fixed Wireless Access

**Nokia** · Athens, Greece · req `40790`

The Fixed Wireless Access (FWA) Business Line plays a crucial role in
developing global broadband access device products, with focus on FWA and
home connectivity solutions. Based in Athens, Greece, the FWA team designs
and tests new features for 5G FWA products, delivering high-quality
solutions to customers worldwide. Working in this dynamic team, you will
have the opportunity to hone your skills in telecommunications,
contributing to the evolution of last-mile 5G networks and collaborating
with stakeholders across the globe.

The organization is evolving. While no final decisions have been taken,
Nokia is planning to divest the FWA CPE unit towards end of 2026 and this
role is planned to be moving to Inseego Corp. together with the rest of
the FWA CPE unit.

## Responsibilities

- Analyze, design, and implement new features following FWA SW
  architecture guidelines and quality standards.
- Write clean, efficient, and maintainable code while implementing
  unit/module tests.
- Contribute to documentation and internal error analysis and resolution.
- Resolve static and dynamic code analysis issues to maintain high-quality
  standards.
- Resolve internal/customer error analysis and resolution in a timely and
  qualitative manner.
- Develop new methodologies and tools to improve overall software quality
  and efficiency.
- Collaborate with cross-functional teams to ensure feature development
  aligns with customer requirements.
- Support the deployment and maintenance of software solutions globally.
