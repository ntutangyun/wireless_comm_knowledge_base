# Embedded Software Engineer

**Nordic Semiconductor** - Oslo, Norway - posted 2026-11-09

About the job
Nordic Semiconductor is looking for an Embedded Software Engineer to join our Long Range & Cloud Radio Software team in Oslo. This role is a strong fit for an engineer who enjoys writing embedded radio code at the PHY-HW interface and building reliable tests and infrastructure.
You will help develop and maintain embedded software and test functionality for Nordic devices and supporting platforms. The work includes hands-on development in C and Python, test automation, hardware debugging, and close collaboration with our hardware teams. You’ll also help define a robust pipeline to help customers understand the quality and functionality available from the different protocols and modulations supported by our radio.

Key responsibilities
- Develop and maintain embedded software and test functionality for Nordic devices and internal platforms.

- Design and implement tests for radio software - at both the PHY and lower-MAC levels.

- Develop and maintain supporting tools and scripts, primarily in Python, to simplify testing, developer experience and velocity, and failure analysis.

- Investigate failures, identify root causes, and help drive corrective actions.

- Improve continuous integration workflows, test coverage, and result reliability.

Key Qualifications
At a minimum, you need:
- Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Electronics, Cybernetics, Software Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a similar field.

- Strong knowledge and development skills in embedded C.

- Good English communication skills.

We think you will do well in this role if you bring a solid radio engineering foundation and enjoy both development and test-oriented work, which might include experience or interest in:
- Experience with embedded debugging on hardware.

- Radio operation, RF testing, or wireless protocol knowledge.

- Python for scripting, test automation, and tooling.

- Test automation frameworks and structured validation methods.

- Experience with continuous integration systems and automated test environments.

- Knowledge of developer tools such as Git, Jenkins, Zephyr, or GCC

Personal Skills
We are looking for a team player with strong collaboration skills and the ability to work independently.
- You should be comfortable taking ownership of tasks and following them through.

- You communicate clearly, ask good questions, and proactively share knowledge.

- You enjoy solving technical problems, especially when they cross between firmware, tooling, hardware, and test infrastructure.

- You are structured in how you work, but flexible when priorities change.

- You care about quality and want to improve both the product and the way the team works.

We also value curiosity, sound judgment, and a genuine interest in learning. This role is a good fit for someone who likes finding root causes and helping others succeed.
