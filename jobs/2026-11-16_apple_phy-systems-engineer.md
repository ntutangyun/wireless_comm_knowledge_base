# PHY Systems Engineer

We are searching for a talented engineer to join our cellular systems engineering team of innovators. In this role, you will be a key member of modem systems engineering team with a broad view of the end-to-end baseband modem.

## Description

Design and simulate state-of-the-art physical layer wireless communication algorithms, realizable with power and area efficient implementation.
Implement system models and perform detailed performance investigations to allow performance sign-off and enable RTL development.
Collaborate with hardware digital designers and firmware developers to bring-up and productize whole modem designs.

## Minimum qualifications

Bachelors Degree in EE or equivalent.
Knowledge of digital signal processing and design flow methodology.
Experience in Matlab, C/C++ and/or Python for algorithm development, modeling (floating-point and fixed-point) and link-level simulation.
Analytical skills (linear algebra, random processes, detection and estimation, etc.)

## Preferred qualifications

Deep-knowledge of communication system theory (MIMO detection, estimation, mmWave, FEC), machine learning, signal processing and hardware design.
Knowledge of 3GPP cellular standards (LTE and 5G) with a good visibility on the 6G activities.
Experience with using AI/ML tools (e.g. Claude) for assisting in code design and algorithm development.
Strong background in AI/ML and familiarity with tools such as PyTorch and TensorFlow.
