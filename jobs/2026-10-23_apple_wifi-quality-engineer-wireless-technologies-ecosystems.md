WI-FI QUALITY ENGINEER, WIRELESS TECHNOLOGIES & ECOSYSTEMS

Summary:
Join Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems (WTE) and drive innovation that matters! Our Wi-Fi QE team is looking for a highly motivated individual with excellent problem solving and communication skills. We build and seamlessly integrate technologies that enrich people’s lives and deliver the best user-experience. If you're passionate about quality and innovation, we may have the job for you.

Description:
Our Wi-Fi Quality Engineering team is looking for a highly motivated individual with excellent problem-solving and communication skills. You'll take an active role in shaping the quality of our software. This will demand deep integration with our development engineers, creating test plans, bug reporting and triaging, identifying automation opportunities and working on developing/maintaining AI based Automation framework/solutions, and supporting design reviews. You'll help us identify holes in coverage, actively sync with multi-functional teams to reduce duplication, foster better testing, and resist regressions to deliver high-quality releases. You will join a dynamic team responsible for qualifying the CoEx features on Apple’s innovative devices.

Minimum Qualifications:
You have a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience
You have 3+ years of experience in Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, wireless networking, mobile platforms, or wireless validation/testing
You have experience with Python or another scripting language for test automation and data analysis
You have a strong understanding of 802.11 Wi-Fi fundamentals, including 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz, 6 GHz, channel/bandwidth configuration, throughput, latency, packet loss, and roaming
You have hands-on experience testing Wi-Fi and Bluetooth coexistence, including concurrent Wi-Fi + Bluetooth traffic and performance scenarios
You have experience with Wi-Fi/Bluetooth log analysis and debugging, with the ability to isolate issues across software, firmware, driver, and RF layers
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to document reproduction steps, logs, performance data, and root-cause findings
You have experience with AI and real world problem solving

Preferred Qualifications:
You're highly motivated, self-driven, and passionate about Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and wireless technologies, with the ability to work effectively both independently and as part of a team
You have exceptional problem-solving, debugging, and root-cause analysis skills, with experience investigating complex Wi-Fi/Bluetooth coexistence and performance issues
You have strong knowledge of 802.11 Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, wireless performance, and CoEx, with hands-on experience in OTA, RvR, mobility, and system-level validation
You have experience developing and maintaining automation and testing tools, including Python-based frameworks and integration with AQS, ATP, CI, or Jenkins environments
You're passionate about innovation and continuous improvement, with experience leveraging Generative AI or agentic tools to improve test coverage, log analysis, root-cause analysis, and wireless validation
