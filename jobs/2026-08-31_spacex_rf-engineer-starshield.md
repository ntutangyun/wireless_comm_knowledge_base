# RF Engineer (Starshield)

**SpaceX (Starlink)** — Hawthorne, CA

RESPONSIBILITIES: 

- Drive program-level decisions on RF system designs

- Research novel solutions for unique large-scale satellite RF systems

- Work in close collaboration with hardware, FPGA, DSP, and software engineers to design and validate RF systems

- Responsible for development and full life cycle support of RF hardware spanning PCBA board testing and defect detection, full functional testing of an assembled unit, and resolving on-orbit vehicle anomalies

- Create electromagnetic simulation models of hardware (antennas, filters, PCB layout) to design and evaluate performance

- Modify electrical schematics and mechanical drawings for flight applications as they relate to reliability and manufacturability

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS: 

- Bachelor's degree in computer engineering, or electrical engineering

- 1+ years of experience RF engineering:

- Experience with modeling/analysis tools/software such as HFSS, CST, ADS, MATLAB, C/C++, and/or Python

- Experience with RF test equipment such as VNA, spectrum analyzer, etc.

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE: 

- Master's degree in electrical engineering with emphasis in microwave/RF engineering

- 1+ years of professional experience in a relevant field

- Experience modeling RF and channel impairments such as multipath, phase noise, group delay, fading and nonlinear distortion

- Experience designing RF systems including radio astronomy, SIGINT, ELINT, radar, or wireless communications

- Experience with basic principles of antenna theory

- Experience with software-defined radios and frameworks, such as GNU Radio

- Understanding of link budgets and analysis related to communications and remote sensing systems

- Ability to obtain and maintain a Top Secret SCI clearance
