# Applied R&D Engineer 2 - IVT

**Nokia** · France · req `38982`

## Job Description

At Nokia Networks France, we are seeking a talented Applied R&D Engineer -
IVT to join our cutting-edge team. With a strong 5G portfolio and expertise
in open and virtualized radio access networks, we are looking for an
individual who can contribute to our continued success. In this role, you
will play a crucial part in testing and validating new radio systems,
ensuring they meet our rigorous standards. Your work will directly impact
the performance and reliability of our wireless networks, making a
significant contribution to our overall success.

## Responsibilities

- Develop comprehensive test strategies for new radio systems, ensuring
  coverage of all critical aspects and potential edge cases.
- Set up and maintain a diverse range of test equipment, including but not
  limited to spectrum analyzers, synthesizers, and thermal chambers.
- Conduct both automated and manual tests to identify technical issues and
  ensure optimal system performance.
- Analyze test results with a critical eye, identifying problems and
  estimating their potential impact on the system and our valued
  customers.
- Collaborate closely with development teams to propose and validate
  solutions, ensuring a swift and effective resolution to any issues.
- Write detailed test reports in English, documenting findings,
  recommendations, and any notable observations.
- Validate pre-production and industrial processes, ensuring they meet our
  stringent quality standards.
- Provide technical support and guidance to the industrial team.
- Stay abreast of the latest industry trends and technologies,
  particularly in RF Transceiver architecture, to ensure our testing
  practices remain cutting-edge.

## Qualifications

- Master's degree in Engineering.
- A strong understanding of RF Transceiver architecture and testing
  methods is essential for success in this role.
- Experience with microwave test equipment, including spectrum analyzers
  and synthesizers, is a non-negotiable requirement.
- A minimum of 2 years of experience in a validation team is preferred.
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills in English.
- Knowledge of modems and their seamless integration with radio systems is
  a significant advantage.
- Experience with automated testing tools and methodologies is desirable.
- Familiarity with O-RAN and vRAN technologies is a plus, as it
  demonstrates an understanding of the latest industry advancements.
- A passion for wireless communications and a desire to contribute to
  cutting-edge technology are essential for this role.
