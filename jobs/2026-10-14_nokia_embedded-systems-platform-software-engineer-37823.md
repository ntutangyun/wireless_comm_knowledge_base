Join US20 - Nokia of America Corp. as an Embedded Software Developer, where you'll play a crucial role in creating robust and high-performance embedded software for cutting-edge communication systems. Collaborate with diverse teams to integrate and optimize wireless communication modules, ensuring seamless operation across all subsystems.

Responsibilities
-  Develop firmware and low-level embedded software using C/C++.
-  Integrate and control WiFi and 5G/LTE modules for real-time, deterministic tasks.
-  Work closely with hardware teams for board bring-up and low-level validation.
-  Optimize system performance, including CPU, DSP, FPGA, and power efficiency.
-  Support secure firmware updates, including OTA mechanisms.
-  Maintain detailed technical documentation and participate in code reviews.
-  Collaborate with cross-functional teams for seamless hardware-software integration.
-  Debug and troubleshoot hardware-software interactions using advanced tools.
-  Ensure robust and high-performance operation across all subsystems.

Qualifications
-  Bachelor's or Master's degree in Electrical/Computer Engineering or related field.
-  5+ years of embedded firmware/software development experience.
-  Proficiency in C and C++ programming.
-  Experience with ARM processors, MCUs, DSPs, and FPGA-based systems.
-  Strong understanding of Linux Kernel architecture and secure boot process.
-  Familiarity with real-time execution and deterministic scheduling.
-  Experience with embedded OS and RTOS environments, including FreeRTOS and Linux with PREEMPT_RT.
-  Proficiency in scripting languages (Python, Bash) for automation and development.
-  Ability to read and interpret hardware schematics and data sheets.
-  Excellent communication skills and a collaborative mindset.
