Youll work across fields to transform improved hardware elements into a single, coordinated design. By collaborating with other product development groups across Apple, youll push the industry boundaries of what wireless systems can do and improve the product experience for our customers worldwide.

The wireless RFIC team architects, designs, and validates radio transceivers integrated into sophisticated wireless SoCs. Our transceivers have powered wireless connectivity solutions for Apple wearable products, enabling the smallest form factor at the lowest power envelope. You will have the opportunity to do innovative development in transceiver design while working closely with systems and product teams at Apple! In this role, you will be part of an RFIC design team and perform the tests, debugging, and measurements for RF SOC IP. Would you like to join us and become part of this diverse team?

Run bench test and debug of RF SoC IP, including transmitters, receivers, PLL, power amplifiers, ADC/DAC, crystal oscillators, and PMU.\n\nDebug chip level or board level de-sense and EMI issues.\n\nWrite scripts using a buildup environment to automate measurements.\n\nPerform RF system characterization measuring EVM, spectral mask, phase noise, sensitivity, and other radio parameters.\n\nDesign external matching networks to optimize Tx/Rx radio performance.\n\nGenerate RF design/verification/test report.\n\nCollaborate with RF systems and integration teams on RF board layout, test interface methodology, and programming sequence and RF calibration programming.\n\nSynchronize radio register settings between the simulation environment and test environment.

Minimum Qualifications
BS and 10+ years of relevant industry experience.\nExperience in RF test development, debugging, and reporting.\nScripting and debugging knowledge in any of the following: Python, Perl, TCL, C++, or equivalent.\nSolid understanding of RF test equipment (network analyzer, spectrum analyzer, vector signal analyzer, vector signal generator, etc.).\nGood knowledge of RF, PLL, and general wireless systems measurements (i.e., IP3, NF, ACPR, phase noise, lock time, etc.).

Preferred Qualifications
MS and 10+ years of relevant industry experience.\nStrong RF background and familiarity with RF systems fundamentals.\nSome experience in RF PCB board design, debugging, and development.\nExposure to RFIC design and simulation/verification.\nExposure to SOC design and simulation/verification.
