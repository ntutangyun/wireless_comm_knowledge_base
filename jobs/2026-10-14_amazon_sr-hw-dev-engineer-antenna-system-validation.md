Amazon Leo is an initiative to launch a constellation of Low Earth Orbit satellites that will provide low-latency, high-speed broadband connectivity to unserved and underserved communities around the world.

As a Senior Phased Array Architect, you will lead critical efforts to design and deliver next-generation antenna systems that will redefine how people connect to the internet. you will:

Key job responsibilities
- Define and lead the architectural vision for advanced phased array antenna systems, ensuring they align with performance, scalability, and reliability goals.
- Serve as the technical authority on phased array systems, providing strategic input on program decisions and long-term technology roadmaps.
- Drive system-level design and optimization, creating blueprints that guide RF, digital, and hardware teams across the organization.
- Oversee system integration, ensuring seamless interaction between phased array systems, RF front-ends, and digital beamforming architectures.
- Guide cross-functional teams—including RF, software, and systems engineers—to implement designs that meet stringent performance, power, and thermal targets.
- Collaborate with product management, supply chain, and operations to ensure architectural decisions align with business goals and production realities.
- Set architectural direction for manufacturability and testability, ensuring designs are robust, scalable, and ready for high-volume production.
- Mentor and develop junior engineers, cultivating a team culture of technical excellence and innovation.

Export Control Requirement
Due to applicable export control laws and regulations, candidates must be a U.S. citizen or national, U.S. permanent resident (i.e., current Green Card holder), or lawfully admitted into the U.S. as a refugee or granted asylum.

Basic Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree or above in electrical engineering, computer engineering, or equivalent
- Experience leading cross-functional teams to deliver products and projects on tight deadlines
- Experience in phased array design principles, including beamforming, steering, and calibration
- Experience with RF front-end design, high-speed digital systems, and signal integrity.
- Experience with system modeling tools and architectural design methodologies.

Preferred Qualifications
- Master's degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, or equivalent
- Experience in Space Vehicle/Satellite Operations, Space Communications or related industry
- Experience in wireless system design and architecture, system integration, and RF system analysis
- Experience in high-volume manufacturing environments
- Familiarity with EM simulation tools (e.g., CST, HFSS, ADS) and system-level modeling platforms.
- Knowledge of at least one modern programming or scripting language such as C, C++, Python, or MATLAB
