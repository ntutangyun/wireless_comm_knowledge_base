# Experienced Cellular RF Receiver Engineer (m/f/d)

**Apple** · Munich, Germany · req `200684799`

Apple's RF Systems Engineering team is seeking a motivated and talented RF
engineer to develop and optimize Cellular RF Receiver architectures. In
this role, you will architect and deliver wireless solutions for Apple
products, working on today's 5G New Radio and pioneering the path to 6G.

## Description

As a key member of our industry-leading team, you will drive the
optimization of our RF Receiver architectures. You will influence and
collaborate with RF circuit design, platform architecture, and software
engineering teams to implement performance-enhancing concepts that deliver
uncompromising performance and quality.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Several years of experience in Radio Receiver design and optimization.
- Demonstrated ability to influence and drive innovation and execution
  across an organization.
- Proven System Engineering experience with complex Cellular RF systems,
  with expertise in state-of-the-art receiver architectures.
- Good understanding of RF line-up analysis for modern receiver
  architectures, including modeling techniques in time and frequency
  domains.
- Theoretical and practical understanding of RF system performance metrics
  and their direct impact on overall system performance (noise, linearity,
  memory, cross-talk, etc.).
- Understanding of 3GPP standards with a focus on RF requirements and
  physical layer procedures.
- Proficiency in using MATLAB or Python for RF system modeling and
  simulations.
- Experience with lab bring-up, system calibration, and debugging RF
  hardware.

## Preferred Qualifications

- An MSc. or PhD in Electrical Engineering or a related field, paired with
  several years of relevant industry work experience.
- Understanding of cellular system use cases, transmit/receive
  interactions, and coexistence challenges in complex consumer
  electronics.
- Familiarity with modern digital modem architectures.
- Familiarity with RFIC design and layout considerations.
