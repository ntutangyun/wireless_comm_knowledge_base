# RF System Modeling Engineer

**Nordic Semiconductor** · Turku, Finland | Oulu, Finland | Trondheim, Norway | Oslo, Norway · req `8350613`

Join our Bluetooth Radio R&D team and help shape the next generation of
wireless technology. As our RF and EMI Simulation Expert, you will work at
the forefront of semiconductor innovation, using advanced electromagnetic
simulation techniques to solve complex challenges and ensure world-class
product performance.

Collaborating with leading experts across IC, package, PCB, and system
design, you will influence critical design decisions, drive cutting-edge
EM/EMI methodologies, and help deliver robust, high-performance solutions
used in millions of connected devices worldwide.

## Key responsibilities

- Perform advanced electromagnetic (EM) simulations at both integrated
  circuit (chip) and printed circuit board (PCB) levels using
  industry-standard tools (e.g., Ansys HFSS, Cadence Sigrity, CST Studio
  Suite, Keysight RFPro).
- Assess and optimize chip- and package-level power distribution networks
  (PDNs) for noise, integrity, and EMI compliance.
- Simulate and analyze EMI radiation to ensure designs follow international
  standards.
- Collaborate closely with IC designers, PCB layout engineers, and system
  architects to provide EM/EMI guidance throughout the product development
  lifecycle.
- Propose and validate effective mitigation solutions for EMI/EMC issues at
  both layout and circuit design levels.
- Prepare and document simulation results, analysis reports, and
  recommendations for design improvements.
- Drive development of EM simulation methods, EMI/EMC standards, and
  relevant EDA tools.
- Support laboratory EMI/EMC testing and debug activities as needed,
  correlating measurements with simulations.

## Key Qualifications

- Master's or PhD in Electrical Engineering, Electronics, Physics, or
  related technical field.
- 5+ years of experience in RF/microwave engineering, EM simulations, or
  EMI/EMC analysis.
- Expertise with EM simulation software (e.g., Ansys HFSS, CST, Cadence
  Sigrity), and familiarity with SPICE-based simulation tools.
- Deep knowledge of chip and PCB power distribution networks, grounding,
  decoupling, and relevant design practices.
- Solid understanding of EMI/EMC theory, design, and compliance processes.
