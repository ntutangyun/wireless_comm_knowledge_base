# Post Silicon Engineer, Amazon LEO

**Amazon (Project Kuiper / Amazon Leo)** - Austin, TX - posted 2026-10-29

### Description

Amazon LEO is expanding global broadband access through a constellation of low Earth orbit satellites. You will be part of a team that works on cutting edge silicon engineered to operate in the harsh environment of space. As a Post Silicon Engineer, you will be a critical member of a high-performance team responsible for bringing up, validating, and characterizing cutting-edge MODEM SOC. You will drive functional validation and characterization of high-speed interfaces and ensure successful integration of the MODEM SOC in Amazon LEO products. Export Control Requirement: Due to applicable export control laws and regulations, candidates must be a U.S. citizen or national, U.S. permanent resident (i.e., current Green Card holder), or lawfully admitted into the U.S. as a refugee or granted asylum. Key job responsibilities Lead comprehensive validation and characterization planning for MODEM SOC and high-speed peripherals (LPDDR4, SerDes) Design and develop SOC test platforms with robust architectural integrity Develop and maintain advanced test automation and comprehensive testbenches for high-speed interfaces and peripheral (I3C, QSPI, Ethernet) Conduct system-level debugs in collaboration with cross-functional teams (Design, DV, Architecture, Manufacturing) Work with FPGA, Emulators and DV Environment to ensure successful Bring Up.

### Basic Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or a related field - 5+ years of work experience in Bring Up, Validation and characterization of SOC or ASIC - Proficient in characterizing PLL performance through metrics like jitter, phase noise - Familiarity with protocols such as I2C, SPI, I3C and eMMC - Experience using Bench top instruments such as Power Supply, DMM, High Speed Oscilloscope, Signal generator, Spectrum Analyzer, BERT, RF Switches and DAQs - Working knowledge of programming languages such as Python and Embedded C

### Preferred Qualifications

- 3+ Years of experience Validating and Characterizing High speed peripherals – LPDDR4 (or Equivalent) and SerDes - Knowledge of Power Analysis and Characterization, Signal-Integrity and Power-Integrity - Experience using FPGA or Emulator for Pre-Silicon Validation and Bring-Up Prep. - Working knowledge of a MODEM is a plus - High sense of ownership, urgency, and drive; resourceful, and able to deliver results with minimal direction Amazon is an equal opportunity employer and does not discriminate on the basis of protected veteran status, disability, or other legally protected status.
