# Cellular Field Design Engineer

**Apple** - Cupertino - posted 2026-11-19

**Summary**
At Apple, new ideas have a way of becoming products, services, and customer experiences very quickly! Do you want to impact millions of users by developing an extra-ordinary product with a prime focus on accuracy, understandability and performance of the product? 

We are the Apple Field Design Engineering cellular team specializing on wireless performance over cellular networks. We are looking for a highly motivated and technical individual to work on testing unreleased Apple products. Come join our team and become part of a hands-on group that fosters engineering excellence, creativity and innovation. We are a team of multifaceted, encouraging people and innovative technologies are the norm here.

You should be proactive and very detail oriented, have great teamwork skills, and a strong passion for Apple. You must be able to collaborate with team members in a fast-paced dynamic environment and still feel comfortable working alone to complete individual responsibilities.

**Description**
You will be responsible for evaluating the latest iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch hardware systems in the field from early prototype to final products. This will be on different network and wireless technologies, including performance metrics creation/evolution, post processing, and analysis of device logs from the field. 

We are looking for someone who will work with the HW/SW development teams and other multi-functional teams on identifying issues impacting the Cellular functionality and performance. You will perform HW and SW cellular testing on Apple products along with analysis into internal logs to understand/identify root cause.

**Responsibilities**
Design, develop, and execute comprehensive field test plans covering voice (VoLTE, VoNR, EPSFB), data performance/throughput (UDP/TCP, DL/UL, CA, MIMO), mobility (handover, redirection, cell reselection), and end-user experience scenarios.
Validate device performance across diverse radio access technologies—including 5G NR (SA / NSA), LTE, and legacy systems—over various network vendor infrastructures (e.g., Ericsson, Nokia, Samsung, Huawei) and frequency bands (FR1 sub-6GHz and FR2 mmWave).
Travel to test locations for executing field evaluations in stationary, pedestrian and mobility conditions
Root-cause critical field failures such as dropped calls, call setup failures, throughput degradation, radio link failures (RLF) and out-of-service (OOS) conditions
Compile and evaluate key performance indicators (KPIs) against baseline/golden units or competitor reference devices to quantify deltas and ensure quality targets are met.
Partner closely with Baseband Firmware, Cellular Systems, Protocol Stack, RF Hardware, Antenna Design, and QA/Product teams to drive issues from initial reproduction through verification of fixes.
Provide actionable field insights and network anomaly data to systems architecture and algorithm teams to guide modem tunables and next-generation designs.
Strong technical communication and presentation skills to convey complex field issues to upper management.

**Minimum qualifications**
Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering or equivalent major with 2-5 years relevant work experience 
Hands-on experience with end-to-end testing of cellular technologies that includes test plan preparation, field testing, log analysis & debugging issues
Working knowledge in at least one of the wireless technology- 5G NR, LTE/LTE-A
Excellent verbal and written communication skills, detailed oriented, analytical, able to work independently
Availability to travel for work up to 10% of the year (domestic and sometimes internationally) without restrictions

**Preferred qualifications**
Master’s degree in Electrical Engineering or equivalent major
Deep understanding of cellular protocol stack from LTE PHY/MAC to higher layers, call processing. 
Experience with being responsible for a complete functional area of an application or product with clear communication across multiple levels in the organization. 
Knowledge of Antenna/ RF design
Basic scripting experience (Shell/Perl/Python/Matlab)
