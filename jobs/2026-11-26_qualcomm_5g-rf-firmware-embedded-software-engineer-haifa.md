# Embedded Software Engineer – 5G RF Firmware - Haifa

**Qualcomm** - Haifa, Israel - posted 2026-11-25

Company:
Qualcomm Israel Ltd.
Job Area:
Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Software Engineering
General Summary:

Embedded Software Engineer – 5G RF Firmware

We are looking for a talented and driven Embedded Software Engineer with strong C/C++ expertise to join our cutting‑edge 5G software development team.
In this role, you will design, implement, test, and maintain RF firmware components that power key 5G features in a high‑performance, real‑time environment.

Responsibilities

- Develop embedded software in C/C++ for hard real‑time systems.
- Design, implement, and maintain hardware - software interfaces for seamless interaction with dedicated hardware components.
- Implement 5G feature functionality at the firmware level.
- Perform rigorous unit and integration testing to ensure reliability, performance, and robustness.
- Participate in debugging, optimization, and continuous improvement of firmware modules.

Requirements

- BSc or MSc in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related discipline.
- 1-3 years of hands‑on experience in embedded real‑time software development.
- Experience with driver development on RTOS or other embedded operating systems.
- Strong proficiency in C/C++ programming.

Advantages
- Experience developing HAL drivers, embedded systems, and low‑level firmware.
- Knowledge of memory‑constrained systems and optimization of code and data paths.
- Solid understanding of operating system concepts and system architecture.
- Familiarity with 5G protocols and wireless communication concepts.

Minimum Qualifications:
•    Bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering, Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, or related field.
