# Satellite Payload Operations Engineer, Leo Networking Software

**Amazon (Project Kuiper / Amazon Leo)** - Redmond, Washington, USA - posted 2026-11-25

We are seeking an experienced Satellite Payload Operations Engineer to join our Amazon Leo team. In this critical role, you will be responsible for the day-to-day monitoring, control, and optimization of the satellite payload systems.

The Amazon Leo Satellite Payload Operations Engineer owns the operational response to in-orbit payload anomalies: detect, diagnose to the level the established flow requires, act, escalate, and verify.

Export Control Requirement:
Due to applicable export control laws and regulations, candidates must be a U.S. citizen or national, U.S. permanent resident (i.e., current Green Card holder), or lawfully admitted into the U.S. as a refugee or granted asylum. 

Key job responsibilities
Your primary duties will include:
• Continuously monitoring payload subsystem health to detect defects proactively, analyzing telemetry to isolate the affected subsystem, and performing pre-authorized commanding and configuration changes to restore service and shorten downtime
• Contributing to the root cause of procedure failures: determining why an automated procedure is not progressing, which checklist item is blocked, and which remediation path applies
• Working established process flows end to end: branching on measured data, taking the prescribed actions (configuration, component health state, checklist bypass), consulting the responsible subject matter expert where the judgment call is theirs, and carrying the issue to a conclusion
• Remediating or escalating identified defects to shorten downtime, providing a comprehensive set of evidence for each unresolved defect before handing it to the owning team for next steps, and escalating again when a handoff is not accepted or not acted on
• Verifying the fix after action, and restoring component health status once the underlying repair completes
• Coordinating with the multiple Flight, software, and ground control teams to ensure seamless integration between the payload and other systems
• Updating operational procedures, checklists, and documentation and specifying and pushing for improved automation of the flows you run

A day in the life
This is an operations role, so you must be able to work a rotating shift schedule, including overnight and weekend on-call rotations.

About the team
The Network Operations Center (NOC) team within Leo Networking Software is responsible for tier 1 support for software defined network services at Amazon Leo. You'll join a growing team of network operations engineers supporting Amazon Leo's global satellite network. Our services operate at scale and support workloads critical to customer network performance and availability, so a passion and discipline around operational excellence is key.

**Basic qualifications**
- 4+ years of software development, or 4+ years of technical support experience
- Experience in a 24x7 operational services or support environment
- Bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering, aerospace engineering, software engineering, or a related technical field, or related technical expertise
- Ability to work a documented decision tree accurately, and to recognize when a case falls outside it and needs escalation
- Excellent communication and teamwork abilities, including written work that other teams act on
- Working proficiency with SQL, Grafana, and Python for telemetry analysis

**Preferred qualifications**
- 2+ years of experience in satellite payload or spacecraft operations
- Experience with AWS services and CloudWatch
- Experience of satellite payload subsystems and their interdependencies
- Proficiency with satellite telemetry and commanding, data analysis tools, and fault management processes
