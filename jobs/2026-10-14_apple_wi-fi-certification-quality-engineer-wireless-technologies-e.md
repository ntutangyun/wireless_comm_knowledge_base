Join Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems (WTE) and drive innovation that matters! Our Wi-Fi QE team is looking for a highly motivated individual with excellent problem solving and communication skills. We build and seamlessly integrate technologies that enrich people’s lives and deliver the best user-experience. If you're passionate about quality and innovation, we may have the job for you.

You will join a dynamic quality assurance team responsible for interoperability and performance validation through WFA Certification Programs on iOS, watchOS, and other exciting products. You will deliver a flawless connectivity experience to our customers. Responsibilities include crafting test strategies using various test methodologies, building test setups, automating test frameworks, triaging issues with development engineers, and executing WFA Certification Program testing to ensure the best Wi-Fi quality across our product line.

Responsibilities
Execute Wi-Fi Alliance (WFA) Certification Program testing (Wi-Fi PHY/MAC, Security, and Application programs) on iOS, watchOS, and other Apple products, and support certification submissions.
Develop a deep understanding of WFA Certification Program test plans, and execute certification tests based on ratified test plans. Provide feedback and report issues to WFA task groups and the CMG to improve the test plans.
Build and maintain up-to-date certification test setups and test beds, including test-bed APs, STAs, sniffers, and other test-bed devices.
Analyze Wi-Fi, system, and packet-capture logs, and work with firmware, driver, and system teams to isolate, root-cause, and resolve interoperability and certification failures.
Develop and maintain automated test frameworks and suites for certification and regression testing, including AI-assisted automation where it helps. This covers device configuration, result analysis, and reporting.
Participate in field interoperability test events with ecosystem partners, and follow up on the results.

Minimum Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience
You have 3+ years of experience with 802.11/Wi-Fi protocols, including packet-level analysis
You have 3+ years of experience in software quality assurance (SQA) methodologies and practices
You have experience developing test strategies and test plans, and executing them through automated or manual testing
You have experience applying AI to automation or testing workflows, and solving real-world problems

Preferred Qualifications
Experience with Wi-Fi Alliance (WFA) Certification Programs, including building and maintaining test setups or test beds
Experience using tools such as Wi-Fi and Bluetooth packet sniffers and network protocol analyzers to debug issues, identify root causes, and provide findings to engineering teams
Collaborative, and comfortable working with cross-functional teams
Strong attention to detail, with a focus on the smallest details that matter to our customers
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including the ability to document reproduction steps, logs, performance data, and root-cause findings
