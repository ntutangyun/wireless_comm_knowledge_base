## Description

WTE PST team is looking for a motivated, highly-technical Bluetooth/Wi-Fi QA Engineer with excellent problem solving and communication skills. You will join a dynamic team responsible for qualifying the latest iPhone/iPad/Watch. Your focus will be testing the Wi-Fi, Bluetooth features in Apple’s innovative new products. The successful candidate will complete the functional testing and identify connectivity issues to ensure high quality releases.

## Minimum qualifications

Bachelor’s degree or above in Electrical Engineering/Computer Science
Hands-on experience in validating and testing products with domain expertise in one of these areas: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, Wireless RF protocols
Experience on defining test plans and creating test cases based on technical specifications and feature requirements

## Preferred qualifications

IOT experience on Wi-Fi, Bluetooth
Experience with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth performance testing
Coding & Automation scripting skill with Python, Shell, etc
Details-oriented, experience in root cause analysis for complex problems
Excellent communication & interpersonal skills, both in Chinese & English
Highly motivated, dedicated, quick learner with ability to work individually and as a team
Able to work independently with minimal direction
