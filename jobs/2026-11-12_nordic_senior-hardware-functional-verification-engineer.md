About the job
We are looking for an experienced engineer to join our Wi-Fi team in Stockholm. This is a software role at the hardware-software boundary. Tasks include designing and writing the firmware that exercises our hardware's functions the way a customer's application would, using the documented registers and APIs to uncover issues and weaknesses.
You will develop firmware for pre-silicon FPGAs and prototype ASICs. Your work will ensure that every internal module meets the expectations of the customers who build on it. You will work with other teams in the Wi-Fi BU throughout the entire device lifecycle, from specification and prototype design to product release and beyond.
In this role you will also help build the team's verification infrastructure, including the software framework, libraries, and the automated build and run systems. You will propose and implement improvements across these areas.
You will plan your own work to meet team goals, and your results will directly influence the quality of our products.
The scope of our work ranges from register MMI semantics to the whole device(s) behavior in a wide variety of configurations, resulting in a deep and wide understanding of our devices over time.
Key Qualifications
- Several years of low-level, bare-metal embedded experience (C, Rust etc.)
- Solid Git/jj, build systems, GNU binutils, GDB and the GCC toolchain
- Daily Linux command-line use
- Knowledge within Python, Shell scripting, Docker, SCons, Jenkins, VCS tooling
- Hands-on experience with embedded debugging, electronics, communications protocols
- Excellent skills in English
