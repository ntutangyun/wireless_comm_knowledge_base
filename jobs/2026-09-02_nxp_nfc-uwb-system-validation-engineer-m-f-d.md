The System Validation Engineer is responsible to verify the product and it’s features from a customer system point of view. 

This role allows you to broaden your technical scope and gain extensive product know-how, especially in the area of NFC (near field communication).

Your Responsibilities:

- Contribute to the definition of a testplan based on product/system requirements

- Create and verify system test cases according a testplan

- Analyze, optimize and maintain existing test cases                                                                         
 

- Drive and run system tests both automated and manually

- Document test cases, test results and problem reports                                                                                   
 

- drive issue debugging in an international, cross discipline project team

- Drive further innovating of our daily way of working                                                                             
 

- Maintain and extend NFC/UWB system test environment

Your Profile:

- Master's degree in Computer Science, Information and Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent

- At least 3 years of experience in systen validation, preferably in the semiconductor industry

- Good skills in Python and/or other scripting languages

- Good knowledge of C programming language and Microcontroller

- Experience with test automation&management

- Experience with laboratory equipment like oscilloscope, logic analyzer, …

- Experience with standard interface like SPI, I2C, UART…

- Experience with version control tools

- Strong analytic skills

- Accurate and well-structured way of working

- Reliable and autonomous working style

- Proficient in spoken and written English

- Team player, working in a diverse and international team

Please note: The successful candidate may/will be responsible for security related tasks. The assignment may/will be in scope of security certifications, therefore a conscious and reliable way of working is necessary.
