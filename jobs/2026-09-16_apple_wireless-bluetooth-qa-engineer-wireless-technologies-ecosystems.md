# Wireless Bluetooth QA Engineer, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

**Apple** · Cupertino · req `200678728`

## Summary

As a Bluetooth Quality Engineer, you will own the quality of Bluetooth-enabled features across Apple's product lineup — driving testing, validation, and engineering partnerships to deliver experiences customers love.

## Description

Join our Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems team and drive innovation that shapes how people experience technology every day. The Bluetooth Quality team is looking for a highly technical, motivated engineer who thrives on solving complex problems and brings strong collaborative skills to every challenge. In this role, you will ensure the quality and reliability of Bluetooth features across Apple's latest products, directly impacting the experience of millions of customers worldwide.

## Responsibilities

- Own end-to-end quality for cutting-edge wireless features through both manual and automated testing
- Champion product quality by identifying root causes, driving bug fixes, and partnering closely with engineering teams to ship exceptional experiences
- Partner with cross-functional teams to shape requirements and design comprehensive test plans that reflect technical depth and real-world customer impact
- Proactively triage discrepancies, monitor field issues, and drive resolution of customer-reported problems
- Execute structured and exploratory testing to ensure high-quality, reliable releases
- Build, maintain, and continuously improve test setups, automation scripts, and infrastructure

## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent experience
- 1+ years of hands-on experience validating and testing products, with domain expertise in one or more of the following: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC, Audio, or Networking
- 2+ years of experience defining test plans and creating test cases based on technical specifications and feature requirements
- 1+ years of experience developing test automation scripts using Python, Perl, or similar programming languages

## Preferred Qualifications

- Proficiency in C++, C, Objective-C, or Swift
- Exceptional problem-solving, debugging, and critical thinking skills
- Experience with Continuous Integration tools such as Jenkins
