# Experienced UWB/RF System Validation Engineer (m/f/d)

**NXP** - Gratkorn - posted 2026-10-23

System Validation develops and validates end-to-end solutions based on Ultra-Wideband (UWB) technologies for Automotive, Mobile, and Industrial applications. The team designs proof-of-concept (POC) implementations to demonstrate customer use cases and validate solution feasibility in real-world environments. It performs use case level product validation, interoperability testing, and Performance KPI validation across hardware, firmware, software, and security components. Close collaboration with product development, architecture, and design teams ensures that solutions meet customer expectations and market requirements. By identifying issues early and validating complete customer scenarios, the department helps deliver robust, secure, and high-quality products.

You will be integrated into a team of software and hardware experts and work together with them on testing our products. 

Your responsibilities will be:

- Definition and implementation of HW Setups and Lab Environments
- Definition and implementation of new features of test frameworks
- Definition and implementation of system test cases, test firmware and test drivers
- Execution and automation of system test cases. Support of hardware and software experts while debugging identified issues.
- Debugging of system issues; root causing malfunctions; impact analysis
- Creating effort estimates for your own deliverables
- Contribution in risk-assessment based selection of test strategies
- Documenting and presenting own results
- Developing proof of concepts together with technical and non-technical experts

Your Profile:

- 3-5 years professional experience in validation and testing
- Strong interest in testing complex systems
- Embedded firmware development (of test-applications) in C
- Willingness to work with lab and measurement instruments, evaluation boards and related hardware equipment
- Experience in C# and/or python
- Highly independent working
- Good English writing and speaking skills
- Good communication skills to align with peers and architects
- Curiosity and enjoying thinking outside the box

Please note: The successful candidate may/will be responsible for security related tasks. The assignment may/will be in scope of security certifications, therefore a conscious and reliable way of working is necessary.
