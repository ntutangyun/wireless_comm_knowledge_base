# HW RF Board Designer - Haifa

**Qualcomm** - Haifa, Israel - posted 2026-11-26

Company:
Qualcomm Israel Ltd.
Job Area:
Engineering Group, Engineering Group > Systems Engineering
General Summary:

HW RF Board Designer

Location: Haifa, Israel

Job Overview

Qualcomm is a company of inventors that unlocked 5G—ushering in an era of rapid acceleration in connectivity and new possibilities that transform industries, create jobs, and enrich lives. This is only the beginning. As we pioneer the path toward 6G, we are looking for inventive minds with diverse skills and backgrounds to help turn breakthrough ideas into world‑changing technologies.

In this role, you will lead the design, bring-up, debug, and characterization of advanced reference RF hardware platforms across Mobile, Access Point, Computing, Automotive, Drone, Infrastructure and Satellite domains.

Key Responsibilities

- Own end-to-end RF hardware board design, including system architecture definition, functional specifications, part selection, schematic capture, layout guidance, and technical documentation.
- Design and develop complex RF, digital, and power circuitry from concept through production including system diagrams & test procedures.
- Lead and support PCB layout, and stack-up definition, ensuring compliance with signal, power, and RF integrity & isolation requirements.
- Perform hands-on board bring-up, debug, and characterization using RF, digital, and power analysis equipment.
- Review and provide feedback on customer and partner designs (schematic and layout), and support system integration and issue resolution.
- Collaborate closely with SoC (RF, Analog, Baseband), System, and Firmware teams to ensure robust end-to-end platform integration.

Required Qualifications

- Proven experience delivering Digital/RF board designs end-to-end, from concept through production.
- Extensive background in complex wireless transceivers ICs operating from UHF to mmWave frequencies, including RF, digital, clocking and power circuits. Strong background in Tx & Rx RF line-up design including component selection & characterization, complex impedance match tuning.
- Deep understanding of RF layout techniques and cross-talk, noise, and coupling mechanisms impacting system performance.
- Solid knowledge of power architecture design, including DC/DC converters, LDOs, and PDN optimization.
- Proficiency with schematic capture and PCB layout tools (Mentor Xpedition, Cadence Allegro).
- Hands-on lab experience using vector signal & network analyzers, spectrum analyzers and digital oscilloscopes.
- Self-driven, detail-oriented, and effective in a fast-paced, multi-tasking environment.
- Excellent English written and verbal communication skills.

Preferred Qualifications

- Experience designing very high-density, multi-layer PCBs.
- Experience designing Cellular Infrastructure Systems.
- Familiarity with 3GPP 4G/5G and IEEE 802.11 (Wi‑Fi) wireless standards.
- Experience with high-speed interfaces such as PCIe, USB, GbE, DDR, MIPI RFFE, SPI, and similar protocols.
- Hands-on experience with RF & Signal Integrity simulation tools (e.g., ADS, HFSS, AWR, SIPro).

Education
- B.Sc. in Electrical Engineering with 8+ years of hands-on hardware board design experience.
