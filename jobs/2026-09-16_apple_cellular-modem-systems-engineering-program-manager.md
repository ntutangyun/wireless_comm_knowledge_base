# Cellular Modem Systems Engineering Program Manager

**Apple** · San Diego · req `200683129`

## Summary

Are you a cellular modem system engineering professional who is looking to make big things happen and can you influence, connect, get results, and communicate effectively? Come to Apple and work with us to deliver a broad range of wireless technologies.

## Description

Apple seeks a technical leader to program manage groundbreaking cellular baseband modems to meet performance, quality and schedule requirements for Apple products. You will be responsible for multi-functional team coordination, communication and representation across the systems & silicon engineering ecosystem from concept to production. Technical background in cellular systems engineering will be key to success in this role.

## Responsibilities

- Drive cellular modem systems development across several projects simultaneously and meet execution targets
- Cover all aspects of modem development including PHY, MAC/RLC/RRC, multi-sim, and concurrent RAT operation
- Track project schedules across all development phases: architecture, design, prototyping, system bring-up, validation, integration, field test, interoperability testing, and production ramp
- Drive resolution of technical issues between functional teams including Modem systems, VLSI, firmware, RF systems, Technology, IP owners, design, digital verification, characterization, and test teams

## Minimum Qualifications

- BS in engineering discipline and 10+ years relevant industry experience
- Experience working in the development of wireless baseband modem systems, algorithms, hardware and/or firmware

## Preferred Qualifications

- Good knowledge of wireless communications (OFDM/MIMO, etc.)
- Experience in wireless standards a significant plus (5G-NR/4G LTE/UMTS/GSM/CDMA)
- Good understanding of modern cellular PHY building blocks, RF and Baseband integration strongly preferred
