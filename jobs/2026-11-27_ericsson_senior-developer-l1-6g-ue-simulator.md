# Senior Developer L1 in 6G simulator

**Ericsson** - Krakow, Poland - posted 2026-11-26

Join our Team

About this opportunity
We are now looking for  engaged Layer 1   Developer to join our team based in Poland! The team will work on how to prepare for development of a powerful UE Simulator meeting the needs of 6G. The team will later be the core team for actual development of the 6G UE simulator.
The Simulation Services (SIMS) unit is responsible for providing Simulators to product development within Business Area Networks. These Simulators are used in product verification instead of real mobile phones and real core networks. The SIMS responsibility includes development of internal solutions for Simulators, including our main simulator CSIM FsUE NR and sourcing of Simulators from external vendors.

What you will do
• Be part of an agile team that contributes to Layer 1 development of a UE Simulator 
•  Work in an exciting technical area, supporting Ericsson internal users with an important product for 6G
• Have the possibility to drive innovation and make new insights reality
• Get the opportunity to work with everything needed in a team: analysis, system design, pre-development and implementation in small increments and ensuring short feedback loops

You will bring
- Strong software engineering skills with ability to write qualitative code (must have: C or Golang, nice to have: Python and Java).
- Familiarity with the standards and protocols used in LTE or 5G networks, including the physical layer (Layer 1)specifications.
- Knowledge of embedded systems and how software interacts with hardware components.
- Skills in developing test cases to validate the simulator’s accuracy and reliability.
- Knowledge of Multi-core/multithreading processor programming and parallel processing to simulate multiple data link connections efficiently.
- A spirit of positiveness and creativeness with a can-do-attitude.
- The skills to be ready to explore new opportunities as well as continuously learn and improve together with the team and really enjoy coding.
- MSc or BSc level in Telecommunications, Electronics or Computer Science
- Excellent speaking and writing skills in English
