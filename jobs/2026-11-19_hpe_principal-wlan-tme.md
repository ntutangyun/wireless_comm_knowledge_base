# Principal WLAN TME

Principal WLAN TME
This role has been designed as 'Hybrid' with a requirement that you will work on average 2 days per week from an HPE office.
Who We Are:
Hewlett Packard Enterprise is the global edge-to-cloud company advancing the way people live and work. We help companies connect, protect, analyze, and act on their data and applications wherever they live, from edge to cloud, so they can turn insights into outcomes at the speed required to thrive in today’s complex world. Our culture thrives on finding new and better ways to accelerate what’s next. We know varied backgrounds are valued and succeed here. We have the flexibility to manage our work and personal needs. We make bold moves, together, and are a force for good. If you are looking to stretch and grow your career our culture will embrace you. Open up opportunities with HPE.
Job Description:
Summary
To meet growing customer support and sales enablement demands, we are seeking an experienced Principal Technical Marketing Engineer who can help customers achieve successful technical and business outcomes.
The Principal WLAN TME thrives in a fast-paced environment where customer success drives every priority. In this role, you will create technical documentation that helps customers and the field adopt HPE Networking’s WLAN portfolio, AI capabilities, and location and analytics technologies.
Internal collaboration
As a WLAN TME, you will partner with Product Management, Product Engineering, Product QA, and Product Marketing from the earliest stages of a product’s life. You will help shape ideas, support development and testing, and plan how products go to market.
Field, partner, and customer engagement
You will work closely with sales engineers, field engineers, partners, and major customers. You will train them on new products and provide design advice, best-practice configurations, implementation support, and help with major issues, including for solutions already deployed. Field
experience, ability to troubleshoot issues and technical skills are essential to making customers successful.
The successful candidate will be highly technical, with broad experience in networking technologies; highly effective in pre-sales engagements; experienced in product development and solution architecture; equipped with very strong verbal and written communication skills; and able to articulate the business benefits of technical solutions effectively.
Responsibilities:
- The Principal TME will be a critical part of the team and play an important role in mentoring and supporting our more junior team members.
- Be a part of HPE Networking NOC team for major events.
- Delivery of detailed technical presentations for employees, partners, and customers.
- Be involved in product development and testing.
Required Qualifications:
- Bachelor's degree in computer science or engineering, or equivalent experience
- Minimum of ten (10) years of combined experience in networking, technical sales, and solutions architecture roles.
- Aruba Certification (ACMX, ACDX, ACEX) or Cisco Certification (CCIE Wireless and/or Routing & Switching), or equivalent experience.
- Domain expertise in Wireless LAN, LAN, WAN, network management, network access control, network security, and IoT.
- Strong routing and switching background, theoretical and practical level knowledge.
- One or more areas of deep technical specialization from the above listed domains.
- In depth knowledge of networking industry standards, RFCs, and common solutions and architectures.
- Strong ability to troubleshoot wired and wireless networks
- Industry knowledge in one or more verticals (retail, healthcare, hospitality, K-12, higher education).
- Must be viewed as a technical leader by management, peers, customers, and partners.
- Strong ability to present technical topics either in a customer meeting or to employees or partners from a stage.
- Thorough competitive knowledge (in area of specialization) including solutions, technology, and product offerings.
- Able to work with Product & Marketing to develop and maintain technical design documents, validated architecture designs, deployment guides, white papers, and enablement content for pre-sales for Aruba field sales and partner organizations.
- Strong technical writing and editing skills, with the ability to quickly turn ideas into clear, polished content.
- Familiar with the usage of AI in the context of networking
- Able to tie technical solutions to business value and identify high impact features.
- Position technical content for both business and technical decision makers so that the HPE Networking value proposition is clearly articulated to both audiences.
- Able to ramp up on new technologies quickly and understand how new products and features can integrate into existing solutions.
- Strong presentation skills, comfortable presenting to small groups of very technical people and large groups with varying technical skills and business interests.
- Able to measure the impact of content with analytics to enable data-driven decision making.
- Ability to develop strong relationships with key stakeholders and build a network of advisors across all parts of the company.
- Continually gather product and solution requirements and submit findings to BUs and LOBs to drive corporate business requirements.
- Ability to train others and share knowledge across teams.
- Ability to understand complex technical and selling situations and solve the problem or solicit the required resources.
- Able to ensure all content is on-brand, consistent in terms of style, quality, voice, and able to be adapted easily to multiple communication channels.
- Able to effectively scope work, commit to deadlines, and deliver work products on time.
Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with HPE Aruba Networking solutions.
Location:
- Sunnyvale, CA or Remote / Home-Based
