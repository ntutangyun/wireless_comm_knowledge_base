PHOTONICS ENGINEER - FIBER AMPLIFIERS, SATELLITES (STARLINK)

Starlink is the world's largest satellite constellation and the leading global satellite internet provider. The laser communications team develops the high-power optical links that interconnect Starlink satellites enabling a high-throughput, low-latency global network. This role focuses on the fiber amplifier systems that are central to those links: high-performance, high-reliability amplifiers that must operate in the extreme thermal, radiation, and vacuum environment of space.

RESPONSIBILITIES:
- Own the development of fiber amplifier systems for laser communications, including requirements definition, system design and analysis, and design validation.
- Conduct validation and verification testing, including environmental qualification (thermal, TVAC, and radiation considerations), performance characterization, reliability testing, and fault injection testing
- Work closely with manufacturing and supply chain teams to ensure final designs can be manufactured in accordance with overall project goals
- Represent the photonic system in program-level, cross-disciplinary trades and issue resolution
- Lead optical architecture definition through internal trade studies, concept of operations definition, and risk reduction testing
- Lead anomaly investigations through to resolution.
- Maintain link budgets, design specifications, and system capabilities data.
- Collaborate with software and automation teams to develop tools and protocols for automated tested of fiber amplifier systems.
- Represent laser communications systems for reliability and risk discussions with internal reliability teams, management, customers, and vendors.

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:
- Bachelor's degree in physics, optics, electrical engineering, or other engineering/STEM discipline
- 2+ years of hands-on laboratory or work experience in fiber-based optical systems/networks, integrated photonics chips, or fiber laser technology
- 1+ years of experience in a computer programming language (e.g. C++, MATLAB, and/or Python)

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:
- Master's degree or PhD in physics or an engineering discipline
- Minimum of 5 years of experience in engineering capacity in the field of fiber amplifiers, laser systems, or opto-electronics.
- Hands-on experience in fiber amplifier design, integration, and test
- Experience with high-power fiber system design, mitigation of non-linearities, and thermal management
- Hands-on experience with optical pump lasers, fiber combiners, filters, and large-mode-area (LMA) fiber, electro-optic modulators.
- Competence with fiber optics processing and handling (cleaving, stripping, splicing, recoating, glass processing, etc.)
- Experience with characterizing fiber laser systems using optical test equipment (power meters, spectral analyzers, high-speed photodiodes, oscilloscopes, laser sources, beam profilers)
