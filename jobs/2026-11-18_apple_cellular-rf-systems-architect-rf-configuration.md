# Cellular RF Systems Architect - RF Configuration

## Summary
At Apple, our work is dedicated to creating products that connect and enrich people's lives. The Cellular RF Systems Engineering team sits at the very core of that mission, defining the wireless technologies that enable seamless experiences for hundreds of millions of users worldwide.
We are looking for a cellular RF engineer to translate complex 5G and 6G use cases into optimized RF configurations that bring our products to life. Configurations define how the transceiver and front-end are set up for each band, band combination, and use case, including signal routing, port mapping, gain and linearity modes and front-end control settings. If you enjoy structuring complex systems, building tools that make work scale, and connecting RF hardware and software teams, this role is for you.

## Description
In this role, you will be responsible for the optimal interaction between our RF transceivers and RF front-end modules. You will turn advanced cellular capabilities, such as carrier aggregation, MIMO, SRS antenna switching and Multi-SIM into concrete transceiver and front-end configurations. You will work closely with transmitter and receiver teams to define and refine these configurations, and partner with hardware, software and validation teams to bring them into our products. You will develop tooling and automation that keep configurations consistent across programs and contribute to the system design documentation that guides implementation.

## Minimum qualifications
Minimum requirement of a bachelors degree
Solid understanding of 3GPP 5G NR and LTE physical layer and RF requirements, including band combinations, carrier aggregation, MIMO and SRS.
Working knowledge of RF front-end architectures and the system-level impact of components like PAs, LNAs, filters, and switches.
Proficiency in Python, MATLAB, or C/C++ for data analysis, system modeling, and algorithm development.
Ability to critically analyze complex trade-offs, such as routing flexibility versus insertion loss or throughput versus battery life.
Strong communication skills with a proven ability to work across diverse, cross-functional teams to solve highly complex, multi-disciplinary problems.

## Preferred qualifications
MS or PhD in Electrical Engineering with a focus on advanced cellular communication topics.
5+ years of experience in RF systems engineering or 10+ years in communications circuit design.
Experience translating system requirements into hardware configurations, control settings or structured system descriptions.
Familiarity with RF configuration frameworks is a plus.
Enjoy structuring complex information with a strong focus on precision and consistency.
Experience applying AI/ML to solve system-level optimization problems.
History of successfully balancing cutting-edge hardware innovation with the practical constraints of high-volume manufacturing.
