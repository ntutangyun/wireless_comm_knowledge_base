SR RF/MIXED SIGNAL TEST ENGINEER

Job Responsibilities:
- Develop production, qualification and characterization test programs for WLAN, BT, and UWB system on a chip (SOC) devices on ATE (Automatic Test Equipment)
- Advantest 93K/Exascale, Teradyne UFLEX/UFLEX+ ATE experience preferred
- Test development from design kick-off to production release
- Debug design and process related yield and performance problems
- Develop new DFT (Design for Test) concepts and
test techniques to improve test efficiency and reduce cost of test
- Collaborate closely with Design, Systems, and Software teams to drive innovative new RF/analog test methods and accompanying device design implementation (Built in self-test, firmware-based testing, etc.)
- Work across product lines and other teams to drive new DFT and Test Engineering approaches within the group and across the company. 
Job Requirements:
- Masters or Bachelor of Science degree in Computer or Electrical Engineering with 10+ years of experience
- Strong communication and problem solving skills
- Self-driven, able to take ownership and initiative of all project aspects while working independently and with the entire team
- Strong programming background (C++, Java , Visual Basic)
- RF and mmWave testing experience a big plus
- Fluent in English
