# Principal Technical Program Manager, NTN Satellite Payload and Devices Integration

**Amazon (Project Kuiper / Amazon Leo)** · Redmond, Washington, USA · req `10557151`

Amazon Leo is an initiative to increase global broadband access through a
constellation of 3,236 satellites in low Earth orbit (LEO). This role leads
technical program management for satellite payload and device integration
for the Leo direct-to-device (D2D) system, owning end-to-end technical
program strategy from planning to deployment.

## Key job responsibilities

- Lead technical program management of DTD payload and device integration
  through architecture, design, integration and testing phases.
- Develop processes connecting multi-disciplinary teams including payload
  engineering, business, product management, device vendors and partners.
- Align teams across organizations, remove obstacles, and drive complex
  technical programs with speed, clarity, and accountability.
- Own program planning, milestones, and risk management across D2D payload
  and device feature integration.
- Partner with test and validation teams to demonstrate system performance
  and service launch readiness.
- Help scale from early development into a large-scale device ecosystem.

## Basic Qualifications

- 5+ years of technical product or program management experience.
- 7+ years of wireless communications systems and implementation
  experience.
- Bachelor's degree or above in electrical engineering, computer
  engineering, or equivalent.
- Experience leading cross-functional teams to deliver products on tight
  deadlines.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience leading engineering discussions on technology decisions and
  strategy.
- Experience handling ambiguity and delivering results in agile
  environments.
- Master's degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, or
  equivalent.
- Experience leading large-scale verification, validation, and
  certification programs for wireless networks.
- Experience with handset and IoT device development on 3GPP mobile
  networks.
- Experience delivering cross-functional programs using data and metrics.
