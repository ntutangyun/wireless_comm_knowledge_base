# Wireless System Integration Engineer

## Summary
At Apple, we work every single day to craft products that enrich people’s lives. Do you love working on challenges that no one has solved yet? Do you like changing the game? We have an opportunity for a forward-thinking and unusually hardworking Wireless System Integration Engineer. As a member of our multi-faceted group, you will have the rare and extraordinary opportunity to craft upcoming products that will delight and encourage millions of Apple’s customers every single day.
In this technical role, you will be part of a silicon design group responsible for Phy and radio system integration, characterization, and NPI Support for state-of-the-art wireless SoC products from early silicon to production.

## Description
Tune, debug, validate phy/radio system performance on silicon platforms to achieve optimal performance.
Characterize, tune, and debug on and off-chip calibration algorithms with HW/SW constraints for factory tuning and real time operation.
Develop and maintain Python validation and debug environments, including bench and handlers.
Work alongside PHY, MAC, FW, RFIC and digital circuit designers on efficient implementation of DSP algorithms.
Provide onsite factory support for the phy/radio system design as the SOC’s move from prototype through NPI.
Develop and implement test plans, collect and analyze test results, identify and debug issues.

## Minimum qualifications
BS and a minimum of 6 years relevant industry experience
Hands-on experience with chip (SoC) debug and/or tuning of transmitter and receiver chain performance, and familiarity with calibration algorithms.
Knowledge of wireless communication protocols like 802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax/bc, 802.11ad/ay or LTE.
Strong understanding of communication theory, OFDM systems and radio systems, radio impairments and their measurement techniques.
Experience with Python, Shell scripting and/or C/C++ implementation.
Hands-on experience using wireless test lab equipment (spectrum analyzer, signal generator, network analyzer, power meter), along with a solid understanding of the underlying theory for communication system debugging.

## Preferred qualifications
Good understanding of communication theory and signal processing algorithms (such as timing recovery, signal estimation and detection, automatic gain control, RF impairment estimation and correction, channel estimation, equalization, coexistence, interference mitigation etc.)
Experience debugging and/or tuning algorithms for calibration, pre-equalization, cancellation, and/or digital pre-distortion techniques.
Excellent analytical and debug skills, with an ability to debug hardware issues within emulation environment.
Experience building test automation and verification flow development with scripting languages such as Python
