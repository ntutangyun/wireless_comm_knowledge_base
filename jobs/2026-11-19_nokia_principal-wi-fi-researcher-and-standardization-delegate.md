# Principal Wi-Fi Researcher and Standardization Delegate

As a principal researcher and delegate in the Wi-Fi team, you will propose and define our research and standardization strategy and agenda for Wi-Fi 9 and beyond
You will develop innovations with other members of the Wi-Fi Center of Excellence, draft and drive contributions in IEEE 802.11.
You will be a leading member of Nokia's IEEE 802.11 standardization delegation.
You contribute to the development of our industry leading system and link simulation platforms.
You use your broad experience in IEEE 802.11 as well as your experience in academia and/or adjacent standardization organizations like 3GPP to innovate and to adapt promising ideas for IEEE 802.11 and Wi-Fi Alliance.
You will be a lead researcher and standardization delegate in Nokia's IEEE 802.11 PHY or MAC team and define the research agenda and standardization strategy.
You will develop winning concepts, evaluate them and collaborate with other members in IEEE to bring them to live in Wi-Fi 9.
PhD in EE, CS, Physics, or similar or MS degree and 5+ years of experience as a lead Wi-Fi standardization delegate, and a track record of successful contributions to IEEE 802.11. You fulfill multiple of these requirements:
- You are enthusiastic about Wi-Fi technology and you are open minded and curious
- You have an outstanding track record of innovations, drafting impactful contributions and have contributed successfully to IEEE 802.11
- You can define and drive a research and standardization strategy and agenda with a team
- You have excellent communications skills and track record of working effectively in IEEE 802.11
- You have expert knowledge in IEEE 802.11 especially of the mainstream generation projects 802.11bn, 802.11be and 802.11ax.
- You have a deep understanding of a state-of-the-art Wi-Fi device implementation
- You are highly efficient, develop and apply (agentic) AI workflows
- You are an experienced SW developer and have done extensive link simulations using Matlab and/or extensive wireless system simulations with expert C++ knowledge, used modern SW development processes and and have a good command of AI tools
- You have deep knowledge of how to apply AI/ML in PHY and MAC layer and understand the comparison with traditional signal processing methods.
