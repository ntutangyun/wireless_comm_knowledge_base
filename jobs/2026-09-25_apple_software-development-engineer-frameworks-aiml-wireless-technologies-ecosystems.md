# Software Development Engineer - Frameworks & AI/ML, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

**Apple** · Cupertino, California, United States · req `200646833`

## Job Summary

Are you obsessed with seamless connectivity? We are looking for an
outstanding Software Development Engineer to join our team and revolutionize
how the world connects. This isn't just about writing code; it's about
blending wireless technologies with cutting-edge AI to create intelligent
connected experiences.

## Description

- Ownership: Develop features across software stacks (Frameworks and API,
  UI, OS services, and drivers) - from concept to shipping and ensure they
  perform flawlessly for years.
- Integrate AI: Don't just build it, make it smarter. Teach our Wi-Fi to
  think for itself. Create smart internal systems that use AI to fix bugs
  faster, improve code quality and make day-to-day life more productive.
- Design & Implement: Build next-level Wi-Fi features across Apple products
  including iPhone, Mac, Apple Watch and Apple TV.
- Collaborate: Work alongside engineers from design, performance, security
  and privacy teams to make intelligent compromises.

## Minimum Qualifications

- BS in Computer Science or equivalent field.
- Strong knowledge of data structures and algorithms.
- Experience developing software using Objective-C/Swift, or C/C++.
- Deep understanding of operating systems including process, filesystems and
  memory management.
- Has used AI to turn good features into amazing ones.
- Strong understanding of networking and TCP/IP suite of protocols.
- Hands-on development and troubleshooting expertise - e.g., GDB, shell
  scripting.
- Excellent communication skills and can clearly explain complex technical
  concepts to any audience.

## Preferred Qualifications

- MS or PhD in Computer Science or equivalent field.
- Experience developing libraries for inter-process communication.
- Knowledge of IEEE 802.11/Wi-Fi protocols and standards
  (802.11a/b/g/n/ac/ax).
- Experience developing on Apple platforms (Xcode).
- Experience with secure coding practices.
