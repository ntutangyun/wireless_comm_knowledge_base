# Wi-Fi /BT/Thread Stability QA Engineer, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

**Apple** - San Diego - posted 2026-11-03

### Summary

Join our Software Engineering team to design, develop, and patent groundbreaking wireless connectivity technologies like Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and Thread. Collaborate cross-functionally to craft wireless solutions that elevate the customer experience with Apple products and services. The Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems team is looking for a software engineer to help ensure quality and stability for Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Networking technologies for all iOS, macOS, tvOS, visionOS and watchOS platforms. If you're motivated, highly qualified, and passionate about quality, problem-solving, and are a wireless technologies enthusiast, we may have the job for you.

### Description

As a Wireless Stability Test Engineer, you will join a dynamic team responsible for the stability of WiFi, Bluetooth and Thread software on Apple products. The successful candidate will be involved in stress and stability testing for WiFi/BT technologies and its features for iPhones/iPads, Apple Watches, Apple TV’s, and Mac product lines.

### Minimum Qualifications

Experience in testing Wireless technologies - WiFi , especially Bluetooth - Functional or Performance or Stability Testing
Preferable to have automation experience in any of the programming languages - C/C++ or scripting skills in python/shell.
Experience using AI tools in testing or automation work, and analyzing test or log data to draw actionable insights on quality and stability.
Deep understanding of SQA methodologies & practices
Good Understanding and working knowledge of WiFi and BT protocols preferred, CWNA preferred
Good understanding of Operating System concepts
Experience in developing test strategies and test methodologies
Experience in developing automated test cases
Can clearly communicate and drive projects across many teams
Bachelors degree in Computer Science / Electronics / Telecommunications Engineering

### Preferred Qualifications

Masters degree in Computer Science / Electronics / Telecommunications Engineering 
Thrive in a collaborative environment and comfortable working with cross-functional teams
Experience building AI-assisted workflows, test cases and dashboards that turn test data into insights.
Laser-focused on the smallest details that are meaningful to our customers
Passionate about learning
