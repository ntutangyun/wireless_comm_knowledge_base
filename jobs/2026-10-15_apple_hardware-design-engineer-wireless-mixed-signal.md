We have built the best RF hardware design team on the planet! We’re looking for a dedicated individual to join our team. We support Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, UWB, NFC, and emerging technologies. As a Mixed Signal Hardware Design Engineer, you will be responsible for the design, implementation and verification of digital and power portions of wireless designs across all Apple products. The applicant should be familiar with computer system architecture, digital design and power distribution networks. The candidate will work proactively with various cross-functional engineering groups responsible for other blocks in the system. May require traveling internationally.

Description
In this role you will drive end-to-end hardware design for cutting-edge wireless systems, from schematic and layout implementation to bring-up and validation of digital interfaces and power supplies. Collaborate cross-functionally with hardware, software, and silicon teams as well as external chipset vendors to deliver robust, high-performance mixed signal solutions.

Responsibilities
Closely work with internal hardware, software and silicon teams to define system interconnects and power supplies.
Implement in-product schematics and layout
Work with internal hardware and silicon teams to bring-up, characterize (performance/power) and validate new digital interfaces + protocols
Work with power supply application circuit design and validation, including load step response, ripple and accuracy, stability
Work with external chipset vendors to develop successful integration and verification plans
Be required to travel internationally as needed (~15%)

Minimum Qualifications
BS and 10+ years of relevant industry experience
Good understanding of protocol specifications for communication interfaces like PCIe, MIPI, I2C, SPI, UART, etc.
Experience with power supply/power management and power distribution networks
Hands on debug experience with usage of modern lab equipment (DSOs, Logic Analyzers, Protocol Analyzers)

Preferred Qualifications
