# Apple — Bluetooth Firmware Engineer, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

## Summary

Join the Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems organization and be part of an outstanding team of engineers driving innovation and developing products used by millions worldwide. Our team seeks a highly motivated embedded software engineer to design and develop Bluetooth Firmware for Apple's next-generation products.

## Description

At Apple, creative thinking and thoughtful insights quickly transform into extraordinary products. As a firmware engineer in the Bluetooth Controller Team, you will be responsible for the design, development, testing of firmware, and bringup of innovative, fresh wireless silicon for various Apple products — the iPhone, Mac and more!

## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science, or a related field, with 10+ years of hands-on industry experience in embedded systems or related disciplines.
- Deep expertise in Bluetooth technologies, including protocol stack internals, wireless communication standards, and experience optimizing for power, performance, and reliability.
- Strong proficiency in C and C++ programming, with the ability to write clean, efficient, and maintainable code for resource-constrained environments.
- Solid foundation in embedded hardware platforms, including microcontrollers, SoCs, and embedded processors, with demonstrated experience in low-level hardware interaction such as register-level programming, peripheral interfaces (UART, SPI, I2C), and hardware bring-up.

## Preferred Qualifications

- You're able to communicate clearly and thrive in a collaborative, demanding environment.
- You're passionate about solving engineering challenges.
- You strive for perfection in all tasks and initiatives.
- You can navigate ambiguity with clarity, flexibility, and effective decisions.
- You focus on actions that drive the most significant impact.
