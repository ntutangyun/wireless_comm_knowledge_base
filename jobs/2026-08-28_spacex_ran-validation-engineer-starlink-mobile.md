# RAN Validation Engineer (Starlink Mobile)

SpaceX was founded under the belief that a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. Today SpaceX is actively developing the technologies to make this possible, with the ultimate goal of&nbsp;enabling human life on Mars.

RAN VALIDATION ENGINEER (STARLINK MOBILE)

SpaceX is developing a satellite-to-mobile network to deliver ubiquitous cellular connectivity worldwide through the Starlink constellation. We are partnering with mobile network operators globally to provide direct-to-cell service, extending coverage to billions of existing cellular devices.&nbsp;Starlink Mobile was designed to connect the unconnected, and this new product will greatly expand that mission by extending coverage to billions of existing cellular devices. Applications include connecting hikers in national parks, emergency responders, rural communities, remote sensors, and people in emergency situations.&nbsp;

As a RAN Validation Engineer on the Starlink Mobile program, you will&nbsp;be responsible for&nbsp;validating&nbsp;and&nbsp;optimizing&nbsp;the 4G/5G RAN software protocol stack. This includes developing automated test cases for feature validation, performing lab and field testing,&nbsp;analyzing&nbsp;and optimizing network performance, and collaborating with developers to resolve complex issues.&nbsp;

RESPONSIBILITIES:&nbsp;

- Develop and&nbsp;maintain&nbsp;automated test cases and frameworks for&nbsp;eNB/gNB&nbsp;feature validation, including functional, performance, interoperability, and regression testing&nbsp;

- Analyze UE and&nbsp;eNB/gNB&nbsp;logs to debug and root-cause RAN issues, and collaborate with developers to drive fixes&nbsp;

- Lead UE log collection and field testing to support&nbsp;eNB/gNB&nbsp;feature&nbsp;development&nbsp;

- Build software tools and database queries to analyze RAN performance and tune&nbsp;eNB/gNB&nbsp;parameters to optimize KPIs&nbsp;

- Manage and prioritize network issues and work with developers to resolve them&nbsp;

- Troubleshoot and&nbsp;maintain&nbsp;RAN lab infrastructure in collaboration with partner teams&nbsp;

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:&nbsp;

- Bachelor’s degree in a STEM discipline&nbsp;

- Experience with RAN parameter configuration, optimization, and performance analysis&nbsp;

- Experience with Python for test automation and data analysis, along with experience in Linux and code repositories (Git)

- Experience developing, executing, and managing LTE/5G-NR test cases (including test planning and results tracking)&nbsp;

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:&nbsp;

- 2+ years of experience with&nbsp;eNB/gNB&nbsp;and/or modem validation&nbsp;

- 2+ years of lab and/or field experience in new product introduction or product certification&nbsp;

- Strong understanding of LTE and 5G-NR protocols with hands-on experience debugging issues at the PHY, MAC, and RRC layers&nbsp;

- Experience with UE log analysis and associated software tools&nbsp;(QXDM/XCAL)&nbsp;

- Experience with RAN lab equipment, including testbed setup and RF troubleshooting&nbsp;

- In-depth knowledge of 3GPP specifications and R17+ RAN features&nbsp;

- Experience with RAN lab equipment, including testbed setup and RF troubleshooting&nbsp;

- Strong skills in debugging and performance optimization; exceptional analytical skills and engineering fundamentals&nbsp;

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:&nbsp;

- Willing to work extended hours and weekends when needed
