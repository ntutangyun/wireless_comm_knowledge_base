# Sr. Software Development Engineer, Amazon Leo Government, ALG Software

**Amazon (Project Kuiper / Leo)** - US, CA, Sunnyvale - posted 2026-11-20

Build brand-new, mission-critical embedded software, with no legacy code, that connects U.S. and allied government customers to Amazon Leo's low Earth orbit satellite network. As a Sr. SDE on the Amazon Leo Government (ALG) Software team, you'll design high-assurance systems for RF, optical and networking device endpoints that connect government customers to the Leo network in mission-critical environments."

Amazon Leo is building a satellite constellation to bring fast, affordable connectivity to customers worldwide. ALG extends Leo to U.S. and international government customers, including NASA, DoD and other national security agencies, through mission-critical space and ground systems. The ALG Software org builds embedded software for ALG-designed RF, optical and networking devices, web applications, APIs and cloud services that connect with Leo's ground, network and business support systems.

You'll work in a fast-paced, high-impact environment on hard problems in secure satellite communications. You'll partner with hardware, security, systems and product teams to deliver scalable, real-time solutions. The role needs deep expertise in fault-tolerant, high-assurance software and in modern languages such as Rust.

Onsite work is required 5 days a week at one of the listed locations. U.S. relocation benefits are available.

Export Control: This position requires that the candidate selected be a U.S. Citizen in order to comply with U.S. government-imposed requirements related to the nature of the work and/or where it will be performed.

Key job responsibilities
• Design, develop, and optimize embedded software for ALG's satellite and ground terminal systems, focusing on fault-tolerant, real-time platforms.
• Use modern application frameworks for Embedded Linux and RTOS platforms to develop scalable, modular, and high-performance software in languages such as Rust.
• Debug and resolve complex hardware-software integration issues using tools such as logic analyzers and oscilloscopes.
• Collaborate with hardware, security, systems, and product teams to ensure reliable hardware-software interaction.
• Own the architecture and design of key components, and make sound tradeoffs between performance, reliability and security.
• Operate the systems you build in production, including monitoring, handling incidents, and making ongoing improvements.
• Mentor engineers, review designs and code, and raise the team's engineering standards.

A day in the life
Your day might start with a design review for a new embedded component on an ALG terminal, weighing performance, reliability and security tradeoffs with hardware and systems engineers. Next, you could be in the lab with a peer hardware/FPGA engineer, tracking down a timing issue between firmware and hardware. In the afternoon, you might write and review Rust code for real-time platforms, then meet with systems and product partners to refine requirements for government customers. You'll also mentor teammates, monitor systems in production, and help shape the architecture of a platform with zero legacy constraints.

About the team
The ALG Software team builds the embedded and services software that extends Amazon Leo to U.S. and allied government customers. We're a new team building a new platform: no legacy code, and real ownership from day one. Our engineers work closely with hardware, FPGA, RF, security and systems experts to deliver high-assurance software for mission-critical environments. We value deep technical ownership, rigorous design and engineering quality, and we invest in mentorship so every engineer grows. If you want your code to run on hardware that supports national security missions, you'll fit right in.

Basic qualifications
- 5+ years of non-internship professional software development experience
- 5+ years of programming with at least one software programming language experience
- 5+ years of leading design or architecture (design patterns, reliability and scaling) of new and existing systems experience
- Experience as a mentor, tech lead or leading an engineering team

Preferred qualifications
- 5+ years of full software development life cycle, including coding standards, code reviews, source control management, build processes, testing, and operations experience
- Bachelor's degree in computer science or equivalent
