## Summary

Join the Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems organization and be part of an outstanding team of engineers driving innovation and developing products used by millions worldwide. Our team seeks a highly motivated embedded software engineer to design and develop Bluetooth for Apple’s next-generation products.

## Description

Be a vital member of the wireless connectivity team at Apple. You will design and develop embedded software for Bluetooth at the MAC layer and be involved in our products from beginning to end. In developing for the Apple ecosystem, you will have the opportunity to tackle complex problems, utilize technology, and work with other exceptional engineers across a wide range of teams to create unique products.

## Minimum qualifications

You have 5+ years of embedded software development experience
You have strong coding skills in C
You're experienced in the design & development of embedded software for real-time operation, especially on ARM processors
You have a thorough understanding of Bluetooth
You have a Bachelor's degree in computer science, computer engineering, electrical engineering, or equivalent experience

## Preferred qualifications

You have excellent analytical and debugging skills
You're able to communicate clearly and thrive in a collaborative, demanding environment
You're passionate about solving engineering challenges
You strive for perfection in all tasks and initiatives
You can navigate ambiguity with clarity, flexibility, and effective decisions
You focus on actions that drive the most significant impact
