# Bluetooth/Thread Embedded Software Engineer, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

Join Apple's wireless connectivity software engineering organization and help develop the Bluetooth MAC layer and Thread software that runs on custom silicon like Apple N1 and ships in nearly every Apple product. We're looking for a highly motivated embedded software engineer to help turn new ideas into wireless features that make Apple products work better together.

Our team develops the Bluetooth MAC layer and Thread software that runs on custom silicon like Apple N1, H2, and S11 and ultimately powers wireless connectivity across nearly every Apple product. We own development end-to-end, from early feature design through chip and platform bring-up, development, and debug. Apple's vertical integration and ecosystem scale let us build wireless features that ship to hundreds of millions of devices and shape the connectivity experience our customers rely on every day.

You will develop Bluetooth MAC layer and Thread features from concept through production, working closely with PHY layer firmware engineers, Bluetooth/Thread stack software teams, hardware design teams, and other cross-functional partners across Apple.

## Responsibilities
Designing, implementing, and debugging Bluetooth MAC layer and Thread software
   Bringing up new chips and platforms
   Debugging complex issues across hardware and software boundaries
   Collaborating with cross-functional teams to define and deliver connectivity features
Contributing to the architecture of next-generation wireless technologie

## Minimum qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent experience
 3+ years of embedded software development experience
 Strong proficiency in C programming
 Experience designing and developing embedded software for real-time operation on ARM processors
 Working knowledge of Bluetooth/Thread protocols and standard

## Preferred qualifications
Excellent analytical and debugging skills, including experience with debuggers and protocol analyzers
   Experience with chip/platform bring-up
   Proven track record of shipping consumer products
   Strong communication and collaboration skills in cross-functional team environments
   Passion for solving complex engineering challenges and delivering exceptional user experience
