# Apple — Wireless Systems Validation Eng

## Description

APPLE INC has the following available in Cupertino, California and various unanticipated locations throughout the USA. Validating RF Exposure test cases for different regulatory domains in certification test setups. Translating WW regulatory requirements into test cases. Coordinating with regulatory compliance teams to ensure product validation covers all certification tests. Defining/executing validation plan for RF Exposure facing SW features. Debugging marginal/failure tests with cross-functional teams like RF HW / Antenna / Product Design / EE / etc and driving design changes to address issues. Maintaining test setups via periodic checks and calibration and helping Regulatory Compliance teams in planning/executing on final certification testing at 3rd party test labs. 40 hours/week.

## Minimum Qualifications

Master's Degree or foreign equivalent in Electrical and Computer Engineering or related field.

Experience and/or education must include:

- Performing Specific Absorption Rate (SAR) measurements for wireless communication devices using standardized RF exposure testing methodologies to evaluate compliance with international regulatory limits under head, body-worn, and extremity operating conditions.
- Conducting spatial peak SAR evaluations using both 1-gram and 10-gram mass averaging techniques in accordance with internationally recognized RF exposure standards to determine compliance margins for consumer wireless products operating across multiple frequency bands.
- Preparing and validating tissue-equivalent liquid mixtures by measuring dielectric properties such as conductivity and permittivity to ensure RF absorption characteristics remain within regulatory tolerance ranges during SAR testing.
- Performing SAR system validation procedures including probe verification, measurement drift assessments, and calibration checks to maintain traceable measurement accuracy and regulatory compliance of RF exposure test systems.
- Analyzing SAR measurement data to evaluate spatial peak RF energy absorption patterns and determine compliance margins relative to regulatory exposure limits under multiple device configurations and transmission conditions.
- Applying RF power scaling and extrapolation techniques to evaluate SAR levels under maximum transmitter output power conditions when testing is performed below maximum rated power.
- Executing automated scanning procedures to map electric field distributions within standardized anthropomorphic phantoms and determining localized RF energy absorption within simulated biological tissue models.
- Developing automated data processing or workflow optimization tools to improve SAR measurement efficiency, reduce testing cycle time, and ensure consistent analysis of RF exposure compliance results.
- Develop and validate cellular power tables for mobile communication devices by defining evaluation methodologies, validating compliance under multiple operating conditions, and aligning testing strategies with cross-functional engineering teams to ensure RF exposure requirements are met.
