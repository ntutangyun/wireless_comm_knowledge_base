MANAGER, PAYLOAD AND TERMINALS LAB, LEO ANTENNAS AND CT

Amazon Leo is an initiative to launch a constellation of Low Earth Orbit satellites that will provide low-latency, high-speed broadband connectivity to unserved and underserved communities around the world.

Key job responsibilities
As Manager of the Payload and Terminals Lab team, you will build and lead a team of hardware and test experts who prototype and validate spaceborne antennas and customer terminals across Research and Development (R&D) and production phases.

You will direct the following capabilities:

 - Over-the-air (OTA) test infrastructure — Design and build anechoic chambers and near-field scanners to support antenna characterization and validation.
- Hardware (HW) prototyping capability — Stand up and maintain prototyping equipment, software (SW) tools, rework stations, and inspection cameras to enable rapid design iteration.
- HW test benches — Configure and operate Network Analyzers, Signal Generators, and Signal Analyzers for RF performance testing.
- R&D prototype build and test — Build functional prototypes and execute test campaigns that directly support R&D development efforts.

- Spaceborne payload and customer terminal verification — Lead test and verification activities at each stage of the product lifecycle; generate test reports and deliver analysis that informs development and production decisions.

- Antenna test and calibration hardware — Design and develop custom hardware solutions that enable antenna test and calibration at scale.
