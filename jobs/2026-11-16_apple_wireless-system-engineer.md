Join our wireless organisation and drive innovation that matters! Weʼre looking for engineers with good fundamentals in RF, Antenna Design, System Integration, wireless communications, signal processing or scientific software to join any of our Wireless teams in Apple's Product RF Organisation.

Imagine what you could do here. At Apple, new ideas have a way of becoming products, services, and customer experiences very quickly. Every single day, people do amazing things at Apple. Do you want to impact billions of users by developing an extraordinary product with a prime focus on achieving the best wireless performance? ## Minimum qualifications

Bachelors degree in Electrical Engineering or Telecommunication Engineering is needed and we highly value Master degrees and PhD/Doctorate.
Excellent understanding of RF fundamentals and communication theory
Hands-on experience with RF lab equipment like spectrum analyzers, oscilloscopes, vector signal generators, power sensors, etc.
Solid understanding of Wi-Fi or Bluetooth wireless standards
Ability to write efficient, scalable Matlab, Python, C or C++ code

## Preferred qualifications

Experience in the development or characterisation of Wi-Fi systems
Strong energy and drive to thrive in a start-up like environment
Knowledgeable in radiation performance, antenna, and wave propagation theory
Experience in testing in anechoic chambers, and with RF test equipment
Experience handling, visualising and analysing antenna radiation patterns
Familiar with RF calibrations and RF metrics
Ability to debug system level electromagnetic problems and characterise nature of aggressor/victim.
Basic understanding of DSP concepts
