# Qorvo — Senior Principal Analog/RF Design Engineer (m/f/d)

## About the Role

Qorvo (Nasdaq: QRVO) supplies innovative semiconductor solutions that make a better world possible. We combine product and technology leadership, systems-level expertise and global manufacturing scale to quickly solve our customers' most complex technical challenges. Qorvo serves multiple high-growth segments of large global markets, including consumer electronics, smart home/IoT, automotive, EVs, battery-powered appliances, network infrastructure, healthcare and aerospace/defense.

Join our innovative team as a Senior Principal Analog/RF Design Engineer (m/f/d), where you will play a crucial role in delivering the next generation of wireless transceivers for world-leading UWB products. This position offers the opportunity to work on cutting-edge technology in Automotive, Mobile, Industrial, IoT, and Consumer applications.

## Key Responsibilities

- Create and develop high-quality Analog/RF systems and sub-systems using industry best practices for design, verification, and testing.
- Write verification plans and develop automated test benches.
- Create, document, and verify behavioral models of analog and analog-mixed-signal blocks (e.g., VerilogA/AMS, SystemVerilog-RNM, Wreal) to ensure they are optimized and up-to-date.
- Design and verify Analog/RF blocks and sub-blocks (e.g., Transmitter, PA, LNA, Filters, ADC, etc) to at least 20GHz using industry-standard tools and design flows.
- Collaborate with IC Layout Design Engineers to optimize design and layout.
- Ensure designs follow industry best practices, including Design-For-Test (DFT), isolation, and power optimization.
- Host design and verification reviews.
- Support lab evaluation, characterization, and testing.
- Collaborate with mixed signal, digital/systems, software, applications, and test teams to ensure products meet specifications on time and within budget.
- Drive continuous productivity improvements through enhanced work methodologies, efficient tool use, and thorough documentation.
- Technical leadership; mentor and guide employees and interns.

## Required Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering or a related field.
- 15+ years of Analog/RF design experience (or an advanced degree with equivalent experience).
- Experience working on circuit design of Transceivers.
- Excellent working knowledge of the Cadence Virtuoso design environment.
- Detailed design experience with analog/mixed-signal circuits on advanced CMOS nodes.
- Demonstrate strong teamwork and interpersonal skills while being able to work independently on complex tasks.
- Clear and concise communication and presentation skills.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Master's degree in Electronic Engineering or a related field.
- Experience with high-frequency RF designs.
- Experienced in getting chip designs to product release.
- Working knowledge of EM field solvers such as Momentum, EMX, HFSS.
