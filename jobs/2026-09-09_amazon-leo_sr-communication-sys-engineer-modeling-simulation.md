# Amazon (Project Kuiper / Amazon Leo) — Sr. Communication Sys Engineer, Modeling and Simulation

## Position Overview

This role supports Amazon Leo's government satellite constellation mission, focusing on system modeling and analysis for government use cases. The engineer will assess how Leo's capabilities meet government customer requirements across coverage, capacity, latency, and availability metrics.

## Key Responsibilities

The position encompasses system-level modeling, simulation, and analysis for government mission scenarios and requires translating customer objectives into measurable outcomes. Core duties include orbital mechanics analysis, resource scheduling design, RF/optical link evaluation, and simulation model development primarily in MATLAB, C++, and Python.

## Basic Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Mechanical, Aerospace, or Manufacturing Engineering.
- 8+ years satellite or network system design experience.
- Strong orbital mechanics and constellation geometry knowledge.
- 8+ years programming experience (MATLAB, C, C++, or Python).
- RF or optical link budget expertise.
- Experience with resource scheduling, allocation, or optimization.
- Strong communication abilities for technical presentations.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Advanced degree (Master's/PhD) in Aerospace, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or Physics.
- Satellite constellation design background.
- RF/communications system design experience.
- Optical inter-satellite link expertise.
- Graph theory and network topology knowledge.
- Government or national security program experience.
- Mission engineering and design reference missions development background.

## Security Requirement

Active TS/SCI clearance (polygraph preferred) and U.S. citizenship required.
