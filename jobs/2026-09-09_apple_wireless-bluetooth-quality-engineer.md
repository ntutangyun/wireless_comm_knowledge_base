# Apple — Wireless Bluetooth Quality Engineer, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

## Job Summary

As a Bluetooth Quality Engineer, you will own the quality of Bluetooth-enabled features across Apple's product lineup — driving testing, validation, and engineering partnerships to deliver experiences customers love.

## Description

Join our Bluetooth Quality Engineering team and help shape the future of wireless technology! We ensure that the Bluetooth experiences powering audio, gaming, fitness, location, and so much more work flawlessly for millions of people around the world. If you are a passionate engineer who loves building smart, scalable automation solutions and pushing the boundaries of test innovation, this role is for you. Our team is looking for a driven engineer to design, develop, and maintain cutting-edge test automation frameworks, including robotics-based systems, that validate next-generation Bluetooth connectivity on the latest Apple products. Your work will directly impact the quality and reliability of technology that touches people's everyday lives.

## Minimum Qualifications

- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, or equivalent experience.
- 3+ years of hands-on experience validating and testing products, with domain expertise in one or more of the following: Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, NFC, Audio, or Networking.
- 3+ years of experience developing test automation scripts using Python, Perl, or similar programming languages.
- 1+ year of using GenAI tools and LLMs.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Highly motivated, dedicated, quick learner with ability to work individually and as a team.
- Exceptional problem solving and debugging skills.
- Excellent interpersonal, communication and documentation skills.
- Efficient time management skills, ability to handle multiple tasks and thrive in a fast paced environment.
- Laser-focused on the smallest details that are meaningful to customers.
