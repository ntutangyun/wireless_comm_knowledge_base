# Antenna Systems Engineer, Antenna Calibration, Amazon Leo

**Amazon (Project Kuiper)** · US, WA, Redmond · req `10544030`

This position focuses on phased array antenna system design and optimization for Amazon Leo, a low Earth orbit satellite broadband network. The role involves calibration systems, performance analysis, modeling, lab measurement, and cross-functional collaboration with antenna and RF teams.

## Basic Qualifications

- Experience programming with at least one modern language such as Python, Ruby, Golang, Java, C++, C#, Rust
- Experience with Linux/Unix
- BS in Electrical Engineering with relevant coursework in digital communication theory, digital signal processing, RF/Microwave Engineering, Antennas and Electromagnetic theory
- Experience in DSP techniques, digital modulation, radio architecture, line-up analysis, RF system design
- Experience with lab test equipment like VSAs, VSGs, VNAs and Oscilloscopes
- Coding and scripting skills with demonstrated experience in Matlab/Python/C++
- Experience with simulation tools such as SystemVue, ADS or LabView

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with CI/CD pipelines build processes
- MS in Electrical Engineering with 3+ years of work experience or PhD with 2+ years of experience
- Experience working with phased arrays, calibration and antenna measurement
- Experience with one of the mainstream antenna simulation tools (CST/HFSS)
- Familiarity with modern wireless technologies and probability/estimation theory
