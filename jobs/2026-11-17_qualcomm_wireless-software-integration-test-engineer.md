# #Wireless Software Integration & Test Engineer

Company: Qualcomm Technologies, Inc.
Job Area: Engineering Group > Cellular Technologies Software

General Summary:
Wireless R&D - Qualcomm Research (QR) division drives cutting edge research in enabling the next generation (6G) of wireless. It involves development of research-grade prototypes and early-stage platforms to enable advances in RF, baseband, communication protocols and algorithms; User Experience (UX) technologies that enable transformative experiences across XR, AI-powered context-aware communications, agentic AI, and robotics; multi-modal sensing using RF, vision and maps; system architectures to both meet and drive industry standards. These prototypes/platforms are end-to-end systems that enable the characterization and demonstration of advanced technologies, improved application-performance and user experience, usage of Qualcomm's compute platforms, leverage of open-source libraries wherever applicable, and pre-commercial inter-operability trials and showcases with industry-partners. It requires advanced SW development across a multitude of domains, layers, and platforms.

We are seeking a Wireless Software Integration & Test Engineer to manage end-to-end feature validation, test-framework development, continuous integration, and process automation for large-scale software subsystems supporting advanced 5G and 6G wireless research. This role works closely with system architects, software developers, and test engineers, with opportunities to contribute to software development.

Principal Duties and Responsibilities
- Integrate, test, and maintain embedded and system software across the development lifecycle to support research prototypes, demonstrations, and industry showcases.
- Develop feature test plans and validate features end to end across large-scale software subsystems and advanced wireless research platforms.
- [portion of the duties list not captured]
- Plan, configure, and maintain software, hardware, device, and network environments for integration, testing, and demonstrations.
- Use standard embedded and Linux debugging tools and techniques to develop and debug software on and off target.
- Troubleshoot and resolve complex software, hardware/software integration, networking, functionality, performance, timing, and system stability issues.
- Develop and apply network diagnostics, logging, data analysis, visualization, and performance tools to accelerate debugging and root-cause analysis.
- Collaborate with software development, system architecture, baseband, RF, and test teams to streamline subsystem integration and software releases.
- Use advanced compute, device, hardware, networking, and software platforms to develop and validate next-generation wireless systems.
- Continuously identify and adopt tools and processes, including AI-assisted development and automation, to improve engineering productivity, software quality, and development speed.

Preferred Qualifications
Understanding of wireless systems, networking, QoS frameworks, and protocols including 5G NR, LTE, and IEEE 802.11.
Experience with end-to-end software and hardware integration in networked or distributed environments.
Familiarity with virtualization, containerization, and orchestration technologies, including Docker, Kubernetes, and Helm.
