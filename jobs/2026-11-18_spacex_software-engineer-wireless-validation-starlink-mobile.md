# Software Engineer, Wireless Validation (Starlink Mobile)

SpaceX was founded under the belief that a future where humanity is out exploring the stars is fundamentally more exciting than one where we are not. Today SpaceX is actively developing the technologies to make this possible, with the ultimate goal of enabling human life on Mars. SOFTWARE ENGINEER, WIRELESS VALIDATION (STARLINK MOBILE)
The Starlink Mobile network will expand Starlink’s vision by providing ubiquitous connectivity and seamless access to text, voice, and data direct to devices across the globe. We are partnering with mobile network operators globally to provide direct-to-cell service, extending coverage to billions of existing cellular devices. As the global leader in rocket and satellite launch and manufacturing, SpaceX is uniquely positioned to rapidly scale our Starlink Mobile network and will rapidly launch a constellation of hundreds of satellites.
As a Wireless Validation Software Engineer on the Starlink Mobile program, you will build the software and lab setups that test the cellular software running the network. You will build continuous integration and deployment (CI/CD) frameworks, write automated tests that stress the network, build hardware-in-the-loop testbeds (HIL) that connect real satellite and cellular hardware to that software, and work with developers to resolve complex issues.
RESPONSIBILITIES:

Develop CI/CD frameworks and automated tests for cellular features, network performance, and stress testing
Automate cell phones and wireless test equipment to simulate conditions found across the Starlink Mobile network
Design, integrate, and operate satellite and cellular hardware-in-the-loop testbeds used for development and regression testing
Root-cause failures across software, RF, and hardware with cross-functional teams
Improve test coverage, cycle time, and testbed reliability
Participate in and lead architecture, design, and code reviews

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS:

Bachelor’s degree in computer science, engineering, math, or a science discipline; OR 2+ years of professional software development experience in lieu of a degree
Development experience in Python, C, or C++

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

Shipped software on real hardware or in production (test systems, robotics, automotive, manufacturing, or similar)
Built test frameworks, CI/CD pipelines, and hardware-in-the-loop or production test systems
Created software that drives test instruments or hardware, and have used oscilloscopes, multimeters, and spectrum analyzers to debug issues
Experience with Linux, IP networking, containers, and databases
Cellular or RF familiarity is helpful, not required
Creative approach to problem solving, exceptional analytical skills, and engineering fundamentals
Ability to work effectively in a dynamic environment with changing needs and requirements
