**About the job**
We are recruiting talented and experienced embedded software engineers to join the Software Platform Group in R&D to further develop Nordic nRF Connect SDK. Most of this software is publicly available on GitHub and you will take part in open-source software development with partners world-wide. You will work in a smaller scrum team, as a part of a large group of experienced and competent embedded SW engineers. We strive every day to improve our ways of working and exchange best practices.
We are looking for embedded software engineers for our team in Kraków. This offer is for an experienced engineer and a good team player, who will develop software powering our world leading ultra-low power chips. Together with a group of talented people, you will be working on reference designs, applications and samples integrating a wide set of functionalities offered by Nordic’s nRF Connect SDK. Your code has a chance to be used by millions of people globally.

**Key responsibilities**
- Development of applications based on Nordic Semiconductor technologies

- Creating examples presenting our products’ functionality and performance

- Ensuring high code quality by providing tests and documentation of developed code

- Provide support to Nordic’s Technical Support and Field Application Engineers

**Key Qualifications**
- B. Sc. or higher in Computer Science, Electrical Engineering or similar

- At least 5 years of experience in embedded software design, implementation and testing

- Very good knowledge of C

- Experience working with ARM CPUs RTOS or Linux kernel

- Good written and spoken Polish and English communication skills

**Personal Skills**
We’re looking for someone with strong problem‑solving abilities and a structured, analytical approach to development, paired with excellent communication skills that make it easy to collaborate with technical teams and share knowledge effectively. You thrive on learning new technologies, stay curious about how things work, and apply a high level of attention to detail to ensure clean, reliable code and clear documentation. With a customer‑focused mindset and a strong sense of ownership, you take responsibility for delivering high‑quality solutions that make our products easy to understand, use, and support.
