# Cellular RF System Architect

**Apple** - San Diego, Sunnyvale - posted 2026-11-04

### Summary

At Apple, our work is dedicated to creating products that connect and enrich people's lives. The Cellular RF Systems Engineering team sits at the very core of that mission, defining the wireless technologies that enable seamless experiences for hundreds of millions of users worldwide. 

We are looking for a motivated and dedicated engineer to join our team and contribute to our Cellular RF architectures. In this role, you will be at the center of RF innovation, helping to drive the evolution of our RF architecture by optimizing current systems, defining next-generation transceiver and front-end solutions and setting critical system requirements.

### Description

In your role as the Cellular RF System Architect you will contribute and be part of an elite Cellular RF Systems Engineering Team. We are tasked with driving cellular radio technologies addressing GSM, UMTS, LTE, 5G NR and upcoming 6G standards. You will work closely with Platform Architecture, RFIC and PMU design teams, and suppliers to assess, scope, and ultimately drive Cellular RF technology innovation to meet the challenges of next generation wireless products. Occasional travel including international may be required.

### Responsibilities

Understand and translate complex use cases and derive corresponding implementation requirements for RF algorithms, RFICs and RFFW for optimum user experience. 
Analyze different RF transceiver architecture options for optimum KPIs such as power consumption and area.
Analyze RF simulation & measurement results to identify trade-offs and propose system level improvements.

### Minimum Qualifications

BS and a minimum of 10 years relevant industry experience
You have experience in deriving implementation requirements based on high level system requirements. 
You have strong analytical, interpersonal and communication skills with a proven ability to work across diverse, cross-functional teams to solve highly complex, multi-disciplinary problems.
You have strong Technical Writing Skills.

### Preferred Qualifications

We value your 5+ years of relevant experience in RF including direct expertise in transmitter and receiver architectures. You should never be satisfied with your knowledge so continuous study, research and collaboration is needed from you to grow.
You have in-depth knowledge of cellular radio standards (GSM, UMTS, LTE, 5GNR and upcoming 6G), regulatory, and carrier requirements including their implications on overall radio architectures and RF system design.
You have experience in RF performance criteria and concepts such as EIRP, TRP, noise figure, sensitivity/desense, phase noise, EVM, spectral mask, spurious, harmonics, intermodulation, s-parameters, Smith charts, and tuning networks.
You have experience in RF line-up analysis, AGC and TPC concepts, calibration and compensation methods.
You have experience in understanding complex system use cases and derive corresponding implementation requirements for optimum user experience. 
You have understanding of antenna array theory and associated measurement methods.
