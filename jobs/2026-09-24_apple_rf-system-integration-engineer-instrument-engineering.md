# RF System Integration Engineer (Instrument Engineering)

**Apple** · Cupertino, California, United States · req `200685129`

## Job Summary

Join our Wireless team and build the technology that makes every Apple radio
possible. We're looking for engineers with strong fundamentals in RF,
communication, software and signal processing to join the growing
Instrument Engineering team at Apple.

This small team of extraordinary engineers designs and produces precision
RF and analog test equipment used to calibrate and validate cellular
(5G/4G and legacy), Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mmWave, UWB, and NFC radios across
millions of iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Watch units. You'll collaborate
closely with RF, software, and signal processing engineers, and work
directly with the internal customers who rely on this equipment, to take
internal products from concept to mass production, each one built to
Apple's exacting standards.

## Description

This is a hands-on RF role that blends bench debugging, RF applications
support, customer engagement, and test automation. You'll characterize and
troubleshoot RF measurement systems on the bench, develop and maintain
automated test and calibration software, and partner across hardware,
software, and customer teams to keep our test equipment accurate and
reliable at production scale.

## Minimum Qualifications

- BS in Electrical Engineering, Physics, or a related field; MS preferred.
- 3-4 years of hands-on experience in RF, wireless, or test/measurement engineering.
- Strong RF fundamentals and demonstrated ability to debug RF problems on the
  bench using standard RF test equipment (signal generators, analyzers, VNAs,
  power sensors).
- Proven experience developing automated test software in Python and/or C++
  from the ground up.
- Willingness to travel internationally up to 15%.

## Preferred Qualifications

- Experience with RF system calibration methods, procedures, and traceability.
- Recent experience analyzing RF parameters in Python or MATLAB (power,
  return loss, S-parameters, EVM, ACLR, IQ mismatch, statistics).
- Working knowledge of DSP and communications theory.
- Familiarity with cellular (5G/4G), Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mmWave, UWB, or NFC
  radio test and measurement.
- Familiarity with RF test equipment vendors (Keysight, Rohde & Schwarz, NI,
  LitePoint, Anritsu).
- CI/CD experience and a track record of integrating automated tests into a
  pipeline.
- Experience applying AI/ML techniques to RF data analysis, modeling, or
  anomaly detection.

## Responsibilities

- Debug RF and analog measurement problems on the bench, isolating root
  cause across hardware, firmware, and software.
- Develop, maintain, and extend automated test and calibration software for
  RF instrumentation.
- Characterize RF systems and instruments using signal generators,
  signal/spectrum analyzers, vector network analyzers, and power sensors.
- Support RF applications and bring-up, partnering with hardware and
  software engineers from prototype through mass production.
- Analyze RF measurement data and present clear, actionable findings to
  technical and cross-functional audiences.
- Improve test coverage, repeatability, and throughput by automating manual
  workflows and integrating them into CI/CD.
- Contribute to calibration methods and procedures that keep test equipment
  accurate and traceable.
