# RF Hardware Engineer

**Broadcom** · San Jose, California · req `R027076`

The successful candidate will work closely with the design, integration,
test and qualification engineers to develop cutting edge technologies for
high performance RF Switches and Filters used in wireless front-end
modules. We are seeking expertise in supporting engineering activities
related to development of test setups, design, modeling and
characterization of SOI and FBAR/SAW devices.

## Description

Your daily responsibilities include development of new RF characterization
setups and automation tools to evaluate RF switches, resonators, filters
and front-end modules for high-power, nonlinear, and noise performance
characterization. As part of this role, you will be involved in the design
and synthesis of RF filters using acoustic FBAR technologies. Your
responsibilities will include developing models for high-Q resonators for
various design environments, physical die layout, and module PCB design.
Additionally, you will perform 3D EM co-simulation as well as yield and
tolerance analysis to ensure design robustness for high-volume
manufacturing. As a highly skilled RF engineer, you will be participating
in R&D activities to implement characterization and qualification plans,
reviewing test results, generating reports, and providing feedback to our
global design, quality and processing teams.

## Required Skills

- Master's or PhD in Engineering with 6+ years of related experience in RF
  front end module building blocks, filters, switches, and semiconductor
  devices; PhD degree is preferred.
- In-depth proficiency in RF/EM engineering and semiconductor physics.
- Expertise with RF modeling tools such as ADS, HFSS, Cadence and Allegro
  SiP.
- Experience with finite element modeling: piezoelectric, acoustics,
  thermo-mechanical, and RF domains.
- Filter, switch, PCB, die layout design and modeling experience is
  desired.
- Strong understanding of small-signal linear, nonlinear, harmonics,
  intermodulation, high-power RF, and noise measurement concepts.
- Knowledge and hands-on experience with RF test equipment: spectrum
  analyzers, network analyzers, signal generators, function generators,
  power meters, DC supplies, oscilloscope, multi-meters, load-pull
  systems, and probe stations, working with RF components such as
  couplers, filters, duplexers, amplifiers, and isolators.
- Programming skills using Python, MATLAB is required.
