Summary: system integration and test for next-generation Amazon Leo satellites, high-throughput wireless systems that must meet 3GPP standards.

Responsibilities: bring up, integrate, tune and debug advanced PHY/MAC and beamforming radio systems; design, document and run 3GPP-based test plans for satellite-to-ground networks and work with vendors on conformance testing; collaborate with PHY, RF, digital beamforming, antenna, MAC and firmware teams; study Doppler pre/post-compensation and CFO/SFO effects in LEO systems and test them in the lab; trade link budgets and verify RF line-ups for payloads in RF and IF configurations; debug power, clock, RF, air-interface and network issues; write bring-up and test automation (Linux, Python, bash); verify time and frequency requirements with synchronization, signal generators and spectrum analyzers; participate in FPGA verification and integration.

Basic qualifications: BS in EE or related; 5+ years in L1/L2 or beamforming; RF lab equipment, bring-up, test and integration; FPGA verification and integration.

Preferred: MS/PhD in wireless communication systems; MATLAB and/or C/C++, test automation; end-to-end network architecture from RF/PHY to application; 3GPP NTN and O-RAN conformance testing; LTE, 5G/6G, RLC, PDCP, RRC and RAN protocols; IP/UDP/TCP/HTTP and Wireshark; wireless synchronization, ranging/positioning or low-power design; channel emulators, spectrum analyzers, signal/traffic generators.
