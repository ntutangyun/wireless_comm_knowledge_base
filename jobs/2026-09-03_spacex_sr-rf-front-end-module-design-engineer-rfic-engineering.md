# SpaceX — Sr. RF Front-End Module Design Engineer (RFIC Engineering)

SR. RF FRONT-END MODULE DESIGN ENGINEER (RFIC ENGINEERING) 

At SpaceX we’re leveraging our experience in building rockets and spacecraft to deploy Starlink, the world’s most advanced broadband internet system. Starlink is the world’s largest satellite constellation and is providing fast, reliable internet to millions of users worldwide. We design, build, test, and operate all parts of the system – thousands of satellites, consumer receivers that allow users to connect within minutes of unboxing, and the software that brings it all together. We’ve only begun to scratch the surface of Starlink’s potential global impact and are looking for best-in-class engineers to help maximize Starlink’s utility for communities and businesses around the globe. 

We are seeking a motivated and technically excellent Sr. RF Front-End Module Design Engineer to develop cutting-edge RF modules for deployment into the Starlink Mobile network. You would be responsible for the specification, material-selection, design, simulation, integration, fabrication, production, measurement, debug, and optimization of compact, multi-chip modules (MCMs) containing custom RF front-end components. Come join us and contribute to RF systems that will expand the performance and capabilities of Starlink Mobile.

RESPONSIBILITIES: 

Design and develop RF front-end MCMs for 5G NR, LTE, and Wi-Fi applications
Integrate RFICs (PAs, LNAs, switches), power management ICs, BAW/SAW filters, printed passives, and SMDs into compact MCMs
Design custom passives (inductors, couplers, etc.) printed into module laminates
Specify RFIC and BAW filter performances; define pin-to-pin lineups, linearity performances, and coupling specifications of RF modules
Work closely with BAW filter designers, RFIC designers, and package/layout engineers in module floor-planning
Perform detailed circuit and EM simulations, and co-simulation of the full module with sub-components
Conduct lab bring-up, characterization, and debugging of module prototypes; drive learnings into improving and optimizing subsequent prototype iterations
Manage laminate and assembly suppliers
Develop ATE test plans and coordinate with OSAT teams on implementation
Support the integration of RF modules into production PCBAs
Perform yield analysis and implement design improvements for high-volume production

BASIC QUALIFICATIONS: 

Bachelor's degree in electrical engineering, computer engineering, or computer science 
5+ years of research and/or industry experience in RF/Microwave engineering or RFIC engineering

PREFERRED SKILLS AND EXPERIENCE:

Master’s degree or PhD in an engineering discipline with emphasis in RF/microwave circuits/systems, BAW/SAW resonators, advanced semiconductor packaging, or electromagnetic theory
Experience in designing multi-chip RF modules for cellular or Wi-Fi applications
Familiar with MCM substrate/packaging technologies (e.g. double-sided BGA), and manufacturing processes
BAW filter design experience
Thorough understanding of RF passive components and EM principles
Experience in the design of linear and non-linear circuits such as power amplifiers, low noise amplifiers, mixers, filters and PLLs
Module/RF/microwave design using ADS, HFSS, AWR (or similar tools)
Experience in MATLAB, Python or equivalent tools for modeling and analyzing RF systems
Familiar with radio related test equipment such as spectrum analyzers, vector signal analyzers, vector signal generator, network analyzers
Experience characterizing radio performance for cellular and Wi-Fi systems
Python programming experience for test automation

ADDITIONAL REQUIREMENTS:  

Ability to work long hours and weekends as necessary to meet critical project milestones
