# Senior Product Manager, Tech - Emergency Services , Amazon Leo DTD

## Description
Help build the future of global emergency communications.
What if the product you built could help someone reach emergency services from virtually anywhere in the world?
Amazon’s Global Emergency Services team is building next-generation capabilities that connect customers with emergency assistance through Amazon’s Direct-to-Device (D2D) technology. As a Senior Product Manager – Technical, you will define and deliver products that have the potential to improve safety for hundreds of millions of customers worldwide.
You will create resilient, mission-critical services that operate across satellite networks, wireless infrastructure, public safety aggregation points, and Amazon technologies. The work demands a product leader who understands networking, telecom and systems deeply enough to shape technical requirements, including the server and API infrastructure these services depend on.
Key job responsibilities
1. Own the long-term technical roadmap for a defined area of Global Emergency Services capabilities, including the networking and systems architecture decisions that underpin it.
2. Apply a deep working knowledge of the satellite, wireless, and cloud networks that carry emergency traffic to define product requirements.
3. Define server, compute, and capacity requirements for emergency services, partnering with engineering to size, scale, and harden the supporting infrastructure for high reliability.
4. Define customer needs and translate them into scalable technical product requirements across networking, systems, and server infrastructure.
5. Define network health monitoring and service level agreement (SLA) requirements, establishing the availability, latency, and reliability metrics used to measure and report service performance.
6. Define and deliver technical integrations between external partners and Amazon systems, specifying the optimal integration method and delivering integrations through testing, launch, and production operation.
8. Launch globally scalable services while continuously improving customer experience, system resilience, and operational excellence.
