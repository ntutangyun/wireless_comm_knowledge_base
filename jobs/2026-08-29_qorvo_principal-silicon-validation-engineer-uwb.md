Qorvo (Nasdaq: QRVO) supplies innovative semiconductor solutions that make a better world possible. We combine product and technology leadership, systems-level expertise and global manufacturing scale to quickly solve our customers' most complex technical challenges. Qorvo serves multiple high-growth segments of large global markets, including consumer electronics, smart home/IoT, automotive, EVs, battery-powered appliances, network infrastructure, healthcare and aerospace/defense. Visit www.qorvo.com to learn how our innovative team is helping connect, protect and power our planet.

Qorvo is a leader in Ultra-Wideband (UWB) technology, enabling precise location, secure ranging and wireless connectivity across Mobile, Consumer, Automotive and Industrial markets. Based in our Dublin design centre, this role will help validate next-generation UWB semiconductor products used in millions of devices worldwide.

As a Principal Silicon Systems Validation Engineer, you will define and drive validation strategy from architecture engagement and silicon bring-up through production release. This role combines hands-on technical execution with principal-level ownership of validation methodology, complex system-level debug, cross-functional technical leadership and mentoring across silicon, firmware, hardware, RF, software and system domains.

**Key Responsibilities**

- Define and lead validation and evaluation strategy, plans, methodologies and coverage for next-generation UWB SoCs, subsystems and semiconductor products.

- Provide technical leadership for silicon bring-up, functional validation, performance characterization, production readiness and complex system-level debug.

- Architect, develop and improve automated validation, characterization, data analysis and test frameworks to increase coverage, efficiency, scalability and quality.

- Develop embedded and host-side software to exercise silicon functionality, accelerate debug and support advanced validation activities.

- Lead root-cause analysis of complex silicon, firmware, hardware, RF, software and system-level issues, translating findings into actionable engineering recommendations.

- Partner with IC design, architecture, firmware, software, systems, applications, product engineering, test, customer engineering and operations teams to resolve technical issues, improve yield, productivity, quality and product robustness.

- Influence architecture and design decisions by identifying validation risks, design implementation gaps, coverage needs and system-level trade-offs early in the product lifecycle.

- Mentor engineers, lead technical reviews, communicate risks and recommendations to stakeholders, and drive continuous improvement in validation infrastructure, documentation, methodology and engineering processes.

**Required Qualifications**

- Bachelor's in Electronic Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science or a related discipline.

- 12+ years of experience, or an equivalent combination of advanced education and experience, leading silicon validation and evaluation, SoC bring-up, semiconductor characterization, embedded/firmware validation, RF or system-level debug, product engineering, or related semiconductor engineering experience.

- Demonstrated ability to define validation strategies, develop test plans, improve methodologies and lead complex technical execution across the full product lifecycle.

- Proven experience leading debug and root-cause analysis across silicon, firmware, hardware, RF, software and system-level domains, with strong analytical and data interpretation skills.

- Excellent communication and technical leadership skills, with the ability to influence cross-functional teams, present findings to stakeholders and mentor engineers.

**Preferred Qualifications:**

- Prior ownership of semiconductor, SoC validation or complex system validation programs.

- Experience with wireless technologies such as UWB, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, GNSS, cellular or other RF systems.

- Strong background in advanced test automation, data analysis, validation infrastructure development and tools such as Python, LabVIEW or similar frameworks.

- Experience with RF measurements, mixed-signal, digital or analog fundamentals, PCB design and bring-up, hardware debugging or board-level validation.

- Familiarity with statistical analysis, characterization techniques, production correlation, FPGA, emulation or pre-silicon validation environments.
