# Product System Test Engineer, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

**Apple** · San Diego · req `200675469`

## Summary

Join our Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems (WTE) Org and drive innovation that matters! We build and seamlessly integrate technologies that enrich people's lives and deliver the best user-experience. The WTE team is looking for a highly qualified software test engineer to help ensure quality for any of the Wireless Communication Technologies - like Cellular/Non Terrestrial Networks/Satellite/Telephony for Apple platforms.

## Description

Product System Test team is looking for a highly technical and motivated QA/Test Engineer in Telephony/Satellite/Cellular domain with excellent problem solving and communication skills. You will be collaborating on cutting edge technologies with experts and delivering a top quality product that is proven in lab simulations and over the air campaigns that you would have designed and executed hands on. Your focus will be to ensure highest level of product quality and that connectivity features are ready for Apple's innovative products serving millions of customers.

## Minimum Qualifications

- 3+ years of experience in Telephony/Cellular/Satellite Functional or Performance Testing
- Experience in developing test strategies and executing it with automation or manual testing
- Solid understanding of existing cellular specifications and protocols
- Support development in debugging and reproducing issues
- Can clearly communicate and driving multiple projects across many teams

## Preferred Qualifications

- 6+ years of experience in Telephony/Cellular/Satellite Functional or Performance Testing
- Proficiency in Python and/or C/C++. Previous automation experience required
- Independently develop test suites, scripts and automate campaigns.
- Knowledge using and understanding of lab equipment: cellular simulators, signal analyzer, signal generator, spectrum analyzer, sniffer tools etc
