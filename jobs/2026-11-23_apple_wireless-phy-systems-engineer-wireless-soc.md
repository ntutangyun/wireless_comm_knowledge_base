# Wireless PHY Systems Engineer

**Apple** - Sunnyvale - posted 2026-11-20

### Summary

Join Apple's Wireless System-on-Chip (SoC) design team to define next-generation modem architectures and develop cutting-edge physical-layer algorithms for products like AirPods, iPhone, and Apple Watch. You'll work on system modeling, design and simulation, collaborating with RF, antenna, firmware, and software engineers to transform wireless innovations from concept to reality.

### Description

Your responsibilities include wireless system design, simulation, and pre-/post silicon bring-up in the lab by collaborating with Radio/RTL/FW team as well as cross-functional teams to deliver the products that enhance end user experience.

### Minimum qualifications

BS and minimum 10 years relevant industry experience. 
Mastery of complex wireless systems with deep understanding of component dependencies and protocol layer interactions.
Expertise in DSP, wireless communications, and wireless chip development. 
Strong software programming skills for RF/PHY modeling and MAC protocol simulation.
Proficiency in Matlab, C, and C++.

### Preferred qualifications

MSEE/Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering with Digital Communications & Signal Processing focus and minimum 10 years relevant industry experience. 
Wireless standards organization or industry consortia participation.
Experience with large-scale field and simulation dataset processing.
Publications or patents in wireless communications or signal processing.
Fixed-point design and RTL vector generation experience.
Knowledge of analog/RF impairments in wireless systems.
Industry standards experience (e.g., Bluetooth, Ultra-Wideband, GPS).
Hands-on lab FPGA/silicon bring-up and PHY/RF/MAC testing experience.
