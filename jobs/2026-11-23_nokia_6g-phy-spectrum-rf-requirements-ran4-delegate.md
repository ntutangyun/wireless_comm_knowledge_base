# Senior Standards researcher/delegate, 6G Physical Layer

**Nokia** - Finland - posted 2026-11-20

### Description

Research & Innovation develops concepts that advance mobile networks and drive their adoption in international standards. We are seeking an ambitious and highly engaged physical layer Radio Research / Delegation Specialist with focus on spectrum utilization and RF requirements to join our Physical Layer Standardization & Research team. In this role, you will drive 6G research and standardization, lead innovation for 6G radio interface and access technologies, and help shape the future of mobile communications—ensuring Nokia’s continued leadership in wireless research.

### Responsibilities

- Lead end-to-end 6G radio research with focus on spectrum utilization and RF requirements, integrating MIMO, initial access, RF performance, AI native PHY design, advanced Layer 1 control, and coexistence considerations to enable scalable, intelligent, and regulation compliant 6G systems. 
- Represent and champion Nokia’s technical leadership in 3GPP and external research forums, advocating next generation PHY/L1 architectures. 
- As delegation specialist actively participating in 3GPP RAN4 promoting Nokia view and research into standardization.
- Validate novel radio access concepts and algorithms through link-level and system-level simulations, including performance evaluation, benchmarking, and design optimization. 
- Shape Nokia’s 6G technology and standards strategy, develop pioneering 6G intellectual property and contributing to long term research roadmaps aligned with business and ecosystem priorities. 
- Embed AI first principles into 6G standards research, defining robust evaluation methodologies for AI enabled PHY validation aligned with regulatory and compliance requirements. 
- Collaborate closely with RAN Protocols, RF, regulatory, and system teams to ensure coherent evolution from research concepts to product relevant technologies.

### Qualifications

- Master’s degree or Ph.D. in electrical engineering, communications, signal processing, or a related field. 
- Strong system level expertise in 5G and/or emerging 6G systems with focus on spectrum utilization and RF requirements. 
- Strong understanding of 3GPP standardization processes, including active participation, contribution preparation and review, specification analysis, change request (CR) handling, and cross-working-group collaboration. 
- Proven capability in developing, enhancing, and using link-level and/or system-level simulators for wireless research and standardization activities. 
- Strong expertise in radio transmitter / receiver design, implementation, and performance optimization, including baseband signal processing, channel estimation, impairment handling, architecture, simulation setup and evaluation, feature optimization, and troubleshooting across a wide range of configurations and deployment scenarios. 
- Deep understanding of radio access systems and associated technology challenges. 
- Solid grasp of radio product portfolios and wireless business strategy. 
- Familiarity with AI driven research methodologies, including data driven and agentic workflows. 
- Prior 3GPP standardization experience is preferred.
- Ability to bridge topics across 3GPP RAN1 and RAN4 will be a plus.
IDEAL BACKGROUND
- 5+ years of research experience in 6G relevant radio topics.
- Proven track record in concept development, performance evaluation, and technical leadership in complex research programs and/or Standards Development Organization such as e.g. 3GPP, ATIS.
- Experience working in multinational and cross cultural environments.
- Strong ability to operate in highly complex, innovation driven organizations. 
- Proactive in scouting, assessing, and applying state of the art research results. 
- Fluent in English, both spoken and written. 
PERSONAL ATTRIBUTES
- Strong technical depth combined with influencing and communication skills. 
- Excellent networking and collaboration abilities across organizational boundaries. 
- Open minded, adaptable, and resilient in fast evolving research and standardization environments. 
- Analytical and structured, with the ability to cut through complexity and prioritize effectively.
- Passion for continuous learning and professional growth. 
- Strong results orientation and willingness to take on new challenges.
