# Wireless Intelligence Software Engineer, Wireless Technologies & Ecosystems

**Apple** - San Diego - posted 2026-11-20

### Summary

Are you excited by the idea of wireless devices that can sense, learn and adapt on their own? Do you enjoy problems where real-time embedded systems meet machine learning? In the Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems (WTE) organization, you'll help bring smart, reliable connectivity to the world through cellular, satellite and Wi-Fi. Our team builds the intelligence behind that connectivity: the scheduling control software that runs on various Apple products, and ML-driven schedulers and models that power wireless intelligence features. You'll design, develop and deliver the next generation of these technologies so that connections stay fast, robust and power-efficient.

Join WTE and be part of a world-class team of engineers working with cross-functional partners to drive innovation in products used by millions of people worldwide. We are looking for talented, highly motivated, self-directed engineers who can work across layers, from embedded scheduling and control software to the ML models that guide it. Our work starts on Apple Watch and extends to iPhone, iPad, Mac and other wireless products, solving everyday connectivity problems at scale. You'll take ideas from research and prototype through to shipping features, and your work will directly shape how Apple products connect.

### Description

We are looking for a highly motivated software engineer to join an exceptional development team building wireless intelligence for Apple's next-generation products. In this role, you will develop wireless software solutions used across Apple’s wireless products, combining embedded software development with applied artificial intelligence and machine learning. Your work will include designing ML-driven smart scheduling algorithms for 5G/6G, satellite and Wi-Fi, and integrating them into real-time, resource-constrained embedded systems. You'll take ownership of features end to end, from prototyping and evaluation through production integration, validation and field performance analysis. The role needs exceptional problem-solving and communication skills, strong programming ability, careful attention to detail and a passion for building great products. This position demands exceptional problem-solving and communication abilities, robust programming proficiency, a meticulous attention to detail, and a passionate commitment to creating superior products. You will do well in a dynamic, fast-paced environment, and your contributions will make a clear difference to future Apple products.

### Minimum qualifications

MSc in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science or a related field.
Strong foundation in wireless communication systems, such as 5G NR/LTE, Wi-Fi (802.11) or satellite communications.
Hands-on experience developing and applying machine learning models to engineering problems.
Proficiency in C/C++ and Python.
Experience using AI coding tools as part of your regular workflow, with the ability to critically review, verify and debug AI-generated code.

### Preferred qualifications

PhD in Electrical Engineering, Computer Science or a related field.
Experience with embedded or real-time software development on resource-constrained devices.
Knowledge of MAC/PHY scheduling, radio resource management, or multi-radio coexistence.
Experience deploying ML models on-device, model quantization and optimization.
Experience analyzing wireless logs and over-the-air performance data.
Track record of taking research concepts or prototypes through to product-quality implementations.
Ability to communicate complex technical work clearly to cross-functional partners and senior engineers.
Comfortable navigating ambiguity and adapting quickly as priorities change.
