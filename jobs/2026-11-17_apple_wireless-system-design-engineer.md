# Wireless System Design Engineer

Our manufacturing wireless footprint grew 200% between 2024 and 2026, and we forecast
another 50% growth each year. Every new deployment adds clients, access points, and tighter
latency budgets to factory RF environments that are already crowded.

We are hiring a Wireless System Design Engineer to architect, secure, and debug these networks
at scale. You will own end-to-end wireless design on the factory floor:

• Spectrum and channel planning for dense deployments
• Low-latency design for time-critical test traffic
• Secure onboarding of thousands of device

When something breaks, you will lead root-cause analysis across client, AP, controller, and host
logs. You will also build the AI-driven analysis that makes this correlation fast, repeatable, and
increasingly automatic. You will partner with local operations, test, and network teams during new production line launches, so issues are resolved before they block production.

## Responsibilities
Wireless Architecture and RF Design
Architect factory Wi-Fi on enterprise platforms: AP placement, controller topology, and
roaming design.
Plan spectrum and channels for dense deployments, balancing width, power, and
interference, and use spectrum analysis to characterize the RF environment.
Define QoS policies and metrics (latency, jitter, loss, airtime utilization, retry rate) that
support time-critical test and automation traffic.
Design for resilience against interference, high client counts, and AP or controller
failures. Validate against defined targets before production ramp.
Security and Identity
Own secure device onboarding and access, including certificate-based authentication,
certificate lifecycle management, and zero-touch provisioning for APs and clients.Root-Cause Analysis and AI-Driven Tooling
Lead root-cause analysis of connectivity, performance, and security issues by correlating
client, AP, controller, and host logs from radio to application.
Apply LLMs, agents, and ML to fleet-wide telemetry to catch emerging roaming,
authentication, addressing, and interference failures early. Maintain production-quality,
documented, tested code.
Factory Support - Serve as wireless technical lead during new production line launches. Travel to factory sites for hands-on support, or lead remote debugging with local teams, to resolve issues that block production.

## Minimum qualifications
10+ years of experience in wireless and RF engineering, including hands-on design and
deployment on at least one enterprise Wi-Fi platform (e.g., HPE Aruba, Cisco
Catalyst/Meraki, Juniper Mist, Extreme, Ruckus).
Working knowledge of network access security and device identity: certificate-based
authentication, RADIUS, PKI, and zero-touch provisioning.
Wireless system design experience: spectrum and channel allocation in dense networks,
RF planning and site surveys, quality-of-service design, and low-latency tuning.
Solid networking fundamentals, including IP addressing and services, VLANs, and
routing.
Demonstrated use of AI to automate debugging, such as LLM- or ML-based workflows
that correlate logs, detect anomalies, or propose root causes, with results you can
describe and measure.
 Python and Bash development skills: building and maintaining tools others rely on, with
version control, code review, and testing.

## Preferred qualifications
Wireless deployments in manufacturing, industrial IoT, or other high-density, mission-
critical environments.
Familiarity with RF simulation and predictive planning tools, network security policy and
segmentation for device fleets, and Bluetooth or other short-range wireless coexistence
with Wi-Fi.
Industry certifications such as CWNE, CWNA, CWDP, CWAP, or vendor equivalents
(ACMX, CCNP Wireless)
