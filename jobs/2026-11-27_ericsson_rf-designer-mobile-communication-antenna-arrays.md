# RF Designer (m/f/d)

**Ericsson** - Rosenheim, Bavaria, Germany - posted 2026-11-23

Grow with us

About this opportunity:

We are seeking a RF Designer to join our R&D department specializing in mobile communication antennas. In this role, you will design, develop, and optimize advanced RF array components, ensuring high-efficiency, performance, and reliability across multiple frequency bands. You will take part in the end-to-end process — from requirement analysis, simulation, and design, to hardware integration, testing, and validation. As a technical designer, you will influence design decisions, drive innovation, and ensure compliance with industry standards, contributing significantly to the evolution of next-generation antenna systems.

What you will do:

- Design and optimize RF array components, feeding systems, RF printed circuit boards (PCBs), FSS structures and calibration boards.
- Translate product and feature requirements into practical RF hardware design solutions.
- Build, test and validate hardware designs; analyze results and use them to improve the design.
- Take ownership of self-directed work packages within an agile development team and coordinate work with relevant team members.
- Contribute to design reviews, technical documentation and integration activities to support high-quality product development.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and contribute to continuous improvements in products, processes and development practices.
- Share technical knowledge and collaborate openly with colleagues in a multidisciplinary environment.

The skills you bring:

- A degree in electrical engineering, communications engineering or a related discipline, or equivalent practical experience.
- Background in radio frequency (RF) development, with a focus on passive hardware.
- Practical knowledge of RF PCB design.
- Experience translating technical requirements into hardware designs and validating designs through hardware testing.
- Ability to work through technical problems methodically and communicate design decisions and test findings clearly.
- Strong English communication skills, both written and spoken.
- A collaborative, open-minded approach and the ability to take initiative and manage your own work packages in an agile team.
- Nice to have: antenna design knowledge.
