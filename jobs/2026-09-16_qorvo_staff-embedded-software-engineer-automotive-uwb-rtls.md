# Staff Embedded Software Engineer (Automotive UWB/RTLS)

**Qorvo** · Barcelona, Spain · req `1422137000`

## Vision

You will be part of the growing automotive software team which manages all of Qorvo's UWB product software development. Following the latest embedded software development rules defined by the automotive world, this team is implanted cross around the world. You will deliver robust, secure, and compliant embedded software for Qorvo's automotive and industrial platforms (UWB/RTLS, RF connectivity, power management). Embed coding excellence, traceability, and secure-by-design principles into every layer — from MCAL drivers to edge SDKs — ensuring first-pass integration success and audit readiness. Your work will directly be used by major car companies and Tier 1 suppliers worldwide.

## Description

Sr Embedded Software Engineer will be responsible for developing software for automotive platforms. In this role you will be responsible for all firmware development aspects of the lifecycle, from software detailed design and implementation until integration tests. You will engage and work with internal cross-functional engineering teams across the globe as well as collaborate with internal and external technical partners, working across time zones, geographical and organizational boundaries. You will leverage a structured and systematic approach, applying and verifying best practices and Lean Product Development principles such as Front Loading and Risk Detection & Countermeasures. You will be the technical authority for embedded software design and implementation.

## Key Responsibilities

- All aspects of the software development life cycle: link between the SW architecture team and the SW development team
- Technical study and detailed design definition
- Software implementation, unit and integration test
- Core team member of software project team to develop new products/processes using DFSS tools and techniques and software metrics
- Work closely with software project management, software architects and quality teams
- Provide support to the software product development team as a strong technical partner
