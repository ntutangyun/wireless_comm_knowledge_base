This role requires critical expertise in multiple layers of cellular protocols. Experience with UE and/or network protocol development for 4G/5G technologies is essential. The candidate must demonstrate a solid understanding of relevant protocol procedures and network interactions. The duties of this position will include designing, developing, and maintaining network simulations. The candidate will need to understand the 4G/5G protocol functionality in the respective 3GPP releases and convert that knowledge into corresponding protocol implementations and interfaces in the cellular network simulation system domain.

Minimum Qualifications
Bachelors or graduate degree in Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering, Computer Science or equivalent degree.\nExcellent knowledge of 4G/5G protocol stack and procedures, especially in RRC, MAC, and PHY layers.\nWorking knowledge of implementing test basestation and RAN/CN network emulators (Keysight, R&S, Anritsu, Anite, etc.).\nExcellent software development and debugging skills, including design, programming (C, C++, scripting). \n

Preferred Qualifications
Strong experience in shell scripting and Python.\nStrong experience in cellular protocol stack host test or simulation environment development is key.\nExperience in test case scripting and debugging regression test suites conforming to relevant ETSI/3GPP protocol technical specifications is a big plus.\nGenAI design and coding experience is a plus.\nStandard software development & tool environments is a plus.\nHighly motivated and self-directed, fast learner, a good team player.\nExcellent verbal and written communication skills.
