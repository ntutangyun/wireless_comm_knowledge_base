# Wireless Analytics & Intelligence Software Engineer

**Apple** · SF Bay Area · req `200679300`

## Summary

Join our Wireless Technologies and Ecosystems (WTE) organization to be part of an outstanding team of engineers focused on driving innovation and enriching experiences for millions of customers worldwide. Wireless Software team is looking for an exceptional software engineer to develop advanced telemetry & diagnostics frameworks to drive analytics for wireless products. Telemetry frameworks you build enable analytics, diagnostics, product experimentation, modem performance & usage characterization and anomaly identification / mitigation techniques to track, analyze and improve wireless experience of Apple products.

## Description

The position demands expertise in Wireless Systems with an emphasis on Cellular systems and requires close collaboration with cross functional hardware and software teams. Passionate, highly motivated and self-directed engineer to work across the L1/ PHY layer of groundbreaking wireless technologies. Such engineers with cellular systems and/or firmware development experience will be part of an innovative team that work on L1/PHY layer of 4G/5G modem that go into a range of Apple products. The position involves identifying and/or developing core wireless/cellular technologies that solve everyday problems for customers using Apple wireless product lines including wearables. This includes having an eye to identifying use-case based innovative solutions that enhance user experience. The ability to work with cross functional teams in the modem stack is a must-have skill and the desire to vertically integrate with new use cases and applications is expected as a second nature! Finally, working on perfectly delivering the implementation along with supporting the complete system validation of such solutions is a requirement.

## Key Qualifications / Responsibilities

Deploy and maintain Machine Learning models on-device using tools like Core AI needed for Wireless Connectivity and Voice Call features. 
Build scalable model evaluation pipelines for validating model performance. 
Design and develop Analytics module running on-device that aggregates and correlates information across the Wireless Technology software stack impacting WiFi, Bluetooth, Cellular etc. 
Design Wireless telemetry probes on device that will be used for performance & functionality monitoring, modem optimizations and identify various software / hardware anomalies. 
Build scalable solutions to unleash the potential of telemetry for data mining and anomaly & trend detection algorithms. 
Develop on device and crowd sourced learning frameworks to improve Wireless product experience. 
Use product experimentation and A/B testing frameworks to validate machine learning model deployments.

## Minimum Qualifications

B.S or M.S in Computer/Electrical Engineering, Computer Science or equivalent

## Preferred Qualifications

Strong object-oriented programming experience in C++, Objective C or Swift
Strong understanding of machine learning fundamentals
Proficiency in GenerativeAI with experience building Agentic AI workflows for machine learning model training, deployment and evaluation
Experience with iOS ML frameworks such as Foundation Models, Core AI
Understanding of and passion for data analytics & product quality
Ability to thrive with a highly collaborative team that loves learning
Experience with scalable & cross layer analytics system design is a plus
Excellent communication skills
