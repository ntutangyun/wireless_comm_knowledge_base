CELLULAR RF TRANSMITTER SYSTEMS ENGINEER

Are you interested in doing work that challenges you, expands your thinking, provides innovative solutions for future problems while driving your growth as recognized technical leader in the cellular industry? Join our team of leading experts in cellular RF industry and deliver differentiating transmit architectures and solutions for Apple’s wireless products that create amazing value for our customers!

Description
In this role, you will be part of an industrial-leading RF Transmitter Systems Engineering team. You will work closely with other architecture and systems engineering teams, RF design teams and SW teams to assess, specify and drive transmitter concepts with focus on leading-edge performance and uncompromised quality as baseline for differentiating products. Your daily work includes the following tasks

Responsibilities
Translate cellular system-level and performance requirements into requirements for the TX line-up and features of RF front-end components, transceiver, and system firmware.
Understand and specify RF algorithms (e.g. power control, calibration) and define the RF control infrastructure, including compute resources, interfaces, memory and timing constraints.
Lead hardware/software partitioning of RF algorithms, ensuring optimal trade-offs between flexibility, latency, area and power.
Develop  models and run simulations (e.g., MATLAB, Python, C) for functional verification, performance analysis, and measurement correlation.
Identify and analyze critical requirements and provide assessments of system performance impact, timelines and risks.
Drive the alignment of requirements, design targets and trade-offs with other teams such as hardware design and software engineering.

Minimum Qualifications
BS and a minimum of 10 years relevant industry experience
5+ years of relevant experience in RF, including cellular transmit architectures, or PhD degree with previous work on cellular topics.
Knowledge of cellular radio/3GPP standards (GSM, UMTS, LTE, LTE-A, 5G NR), including RF aspects as well as system use cases and TX waveforms.
Familiarity with RF systems, front-end (power amplifier, filters, switches, power management), and transceiver architecture, including lineup design and trade-offs (e.g. gain, linearity, noise, power, thermal).
Experience with hardware and software co-design and clear understanding of trade-offs between analog and digital hardware and firmware implementations.
Strong analytical skills, proactive user of AI tools, and ability to collaborate and communicate across multicultural, global teams.

Preferred Qualifications
Prefer a Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering or equivalent.
Knowledge of how to translate top-level RF requirements into specifications for RF front-end and transceiver architecture and building blocks
Initial expertise in RF transmitter system-level concepts, control and data translation into modulations, and control procedures like power control, digital predistortion, and envelope tracking.
RF front-end and RFIC system modeling and simulation.
Expertise in RF front-end architectures, technologies, and design.
Knowledge of digital signal processing and mixed-signal circuit design.
Understanding of SoC integration constraints and multi-chip RF architectures.
