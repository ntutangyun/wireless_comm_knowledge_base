## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-21/22, the 2026 China Radio Conference ("无线创新 谱绘未来") was held in Xiong'an, Hebei, drawing 1,200+ attendees. On the morning of 09-22, the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) hosted a dedicated seminar, "SparkLink Secure Smart Connection — Industry Collaboration Takes Flight" (星闪安全智联·行业聚力启航), chaired by Hu Zhiyuan (胡之源), director of the National Radio Monitoring Center's testing center, with an opening address by Wang Wenjian (王文俭), president of the Institute for Radio Spectrum Development and Technology Innovation. Attendees included Beijing Municipal Bureau of Economy and Information Technology deputy director Tang Jianguo (唐建国), China Radio Association deputy chairman/secretary-general Kan Runtian (阚润田), and iSLA chairman Shi Jinglin (石晶林) and vice-chairman Wan Lei (万蕾, Huawei Fellow).

Shi Jinglin's keynote, "'Agent' Communication and SparkLink" (智能体"通信"与星闪), framed agent applications as requiring "five highs and three lows" (超高带宽/密度/精度/安全/高精同步, 超低时延/功耗/抖动) that legacy short-range technologies cannot meet, positioning SparkLink's low-latency, high-reliability, high-security, multi-concurrency profile as the industrial-grade answer. Zhang Mingyuan (张明远), director of the wireless technology division of the National Radio Monitoring Center's testing center and head of iSLA's spectrum group, stated in his talk that **15 SparkLink group standards have been systematically converted into national standards in 2026**, and that the alliance plans to introduce security-testing capability into its standard test system, building out a SparkLink Secure Wireless LAN (SWL) test system, plus a combined HarmonyOS+SparkLink one-stop test offering.

The seminar held a product-roadmap launch ceremony for **SparkLink Secure Wireless LAN (SWL)**, unveiling 9 product roadmap items witnessed by 10 guests including Shi Jinglin, Wan Lei, and representatives from Beijing Zhongke Jingshang (北京中科晶上科技股份有限公司) and Wuhan Pansheng Dingcheng (武汉攀升鼎承科技有限公司): SWL secure communication chip, SWL encryption chip, SWL secure AP, SWL dedicated-network card, SparkLink secure office terminals (PC, camera, printer, tablet), SparkLink power-communication gateway, and SparkLink power-communication terminal. Follow-up talks covered: Xuji Electric (许继电气) sharing a SparkLink-enabled power near-field communication practice; Huawei's campus-network senior architect describing a "SparkLink secure dedicated-network system" for industrial/financial/government wireless deployment (data collection, swarm AGV, industrial control, mobile robotics); Jiangnan Xin'an (江南信安) presenting a four-layer defense-in-depth security architecture; Beijing Peony Electronics Group (北京牡丹电子集团) describing what it calls the first domestic "SparkLink secure dedicated network × government secure office" demonstration project (at the National IP Operation Public Service Platform), covering general office, classified/secure meetings, and collaborative low-latency/high-concurrency office scenarios based on SLB; and Ailing Network (艾灵网络) on industrial-manufacturing deployments spanning automotive manufacturing to concentrating solar power plants, describing a "5G for wide-area, SparkLink for near-field" combined coverage pattern.

A separate talk by Gao Jing (高静), project manager at the Beijing Machinery Industry Automation Research Institute / National Technical Committee for Standardization of Robots, introduced a **"Humanoid Robot Short-range Interaction Communication Technical Specification" currently in drafting**, using SparkLink as its core short-range technology, targeting interoperability, interface matching and security for robot-to-robot, robot-to-edge-device, and robot-to-human short-range communication. A dedicated exhibition zone at the conference showed ~40 products from 10+ partners (Peony Group, Banglian Technology, Beijing Institute of Technology, Huawei, Jiangnan Xin'an, Yinji Technology, Yuanfeng Technology, etc.) spanning government office, interactive screen-casting, industrial sites, emergency response, smart-home terminals and digital keys.

## Summary (ZH)

2026年9月21日至22日，以"无线创新 谱绘未来"为主题的2026中国无线电大会在河北雄安新区举行，吸引1200余人参会。9月22日上午，国际星闪联盟（iSLA）承办专题研讨会"星闪安全智联·行业聚力启航"，由国家无线电监测中心检测中心主任胡之源主持，无线电频谱开发利用和技术创新研究院院长王文俭致辞。出席嘉宾包括北京市经济和信息化局副局长唐建国、中国无线电协会副理事长兼秘书长阚润田，以及国际星闪联盟理事长石晶林、副理事长万蕾（华为Fellow）。

石晶林在题为《智能体"通信"与星闪》的主旨演讲中，将智能体应用的通信需求概括为"五高三低"（超高带宽/密度/精度/安全/高精同步，超低时延/功耗/抖动），认为传统短距通信技术难以承载，星闪的低时延、高可靠、高安全、多并发特性可提供工业级保障。国家无线电监测中心检测中心无线技术事业部主任、国际星闪联盟频谱组组长张明远在演讲中表示，**2026年15项星闪团体标准成体系转化为国家标准**，联盟计划在标准测试体系中引入安全测试能力，逐步构建星闪安全无线局域网（SWL）测试体系，并打造鸿蒙+星闪一站式测试。

研讨会现场举行了**星闪安全无线局域网（SWL）产品路标发布仪式**，在石晶林、万蕾及北京中科晶上科技股份有限公司、武汉攀升鼎承科技有限公司等10位嘉宾见证下，发布9款产品路标：星闪SWL安全通信芯片、星闪SWL加密芯片、星闪SWL安全AP、星闪SWL专网网卡、星闪安全办公终端（PC、摄像头、打印机、平板）、星闪电力通信网关、星闪电力通信终端。随后的分享包括：许继电气分享面向电力近场通信的星闪解决方案实践；华为园区网络高级架构师介绍面向工业/金融/政务无线化场景（数采、集群AGV、工业控制、移动机器人）的"星闪安全专网系统"；江南信安介绍四层纵深防御安全架构；北京牡丹电子集团介绍其所称的国内首个"星闪安全专网×政务安全办公"示范项目（落地于国家知识产权运营公共服务平台），基于星闪SLB覆盖通用办公、涉密会议与协同低时延高并发办公场景；艾灵网络分享从汽车制造到光热电站的工业制造部署实践，提出"5G主广域、星闪主近场"的组合覆盖模式。

另有北京机械工业自动化研究所有限公司/全国机器人标准化技术委员会项目经理高静介绍了**正在编制的《人形机器人短距交互通信技术规范》**，以星闪为核心短距技术，解决机器人之间、机器人与边缘设备、机器人与人之间短距通信的互联互通、接口匹配与安全问题。大会同期展区展示了牡丹集团、邦联科技、北京理工大学、华为、江南信安、银基科技、远峰科技等10余家伙伴的近40款产品，覆盖政务办公、交互投屏、工业现场、应急救援、终端家居与数字钥匙。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the first appearance in this KB of "SparkLink Secure Wireless LAN (SWL)" as a named product category/sub-brand, distinct from the SLB/SLE access-mode naming this KB has tracked to date — it bundles chips, encryption, APs, dedicated-network cards and office/power terminals under a security-oriented umbrella, with a dedicated SWL test-system plan layered onto the existing certification scheme (2026-08-17_nearlink-isla-certification-scheme). The government-secure-office and power-near-field case studies add two named deployer-level examples (Beijing Peony Group's government secure-office project; a Xuji Electric talk on power near-field communication practice, related to 2026-09-09_nearlink-power-grid-switchgear-xuji). The humanoid-robot short-range communication spec in drafting is a new, concrete standards-track item distinct from the existing 2026-08-31_chuangyao-ethercat-nearlink-humanoid-robot-comms entry (a single vendor's chip-level implementation) — this is alliance/standards-committee-level work defining the interaction protocol itself, not a vendor product.
