## Summary (EN)

SatNews (2026-11-18) reports that the FCC adopted the schedule and bidding procedures for Auction 115, which begins on 2027-04-27 and offers 160 MHz of Upper C-band spectrum at 3.98-4.14 GHz across the contiguous United States for terrestrial wireless use. Under Public Notice DA 26-1219 the FCC will offer 3,248 flexible-use licenses. The article gives these pre-auction dates: short-form application window 2027-01-06 (12:00 p.m. ET) to 2027-01-20 (6:00 p.m. ET), an application tutorial due by 2026-12-11, upfront payments due 2027-03-18, a mock auction on 2027-04-21 and live bidding from 2027-04-27. It says the schedule implements the FCC's July 2026 Upper C-band Report and Order, which allocated $5.6 billion in gross incentive payments to SES and $504 million to Eutelsat to vacate the 160 MHz of satellite capacity; with 280 MHz of lower C-band cleared earlier, the article describes a contiguous 440 MHz mid-band block. It states winning licensees are expected to activate commercial mobile networks from December 2030 in the top 75 U.S. markets, and that incumbent satellite operators must compress remaining media feeds into the protected upper-band frequencies, reconfigure head-ends and install ground-station filtering. Clearing deadlines are given as December 2030 and 2031, tied to incentive-payment eligibility. The article presents hybrid IP delivery, terrestrial fiber and Ku-band backup as options for broadcasters.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Auction:** Auction 115, Upper C-band, 160 MHz at 3.98-4.14 GHz, 3,248 flexible-use licenses (Public Notice DA 26-1219); live bidding from 2027-04-27.
- **Calendar:** tutorial by 2026-12-11; short-form applications 2027-01-06 to 2027-01-20; upfront payments 2027-03-18; mock auction 2027-04-21.
- **Satellite-side clearing:** incentive payments of $5.6B (SES) and $504M (Eutelsat); remaining media feeds compressed into protected upper-band frequencies; head-end reconfiguration and ground-station filters; deadlines December 2030 and 2031 per the article.
- **Adjacent-band protection:** technical rules developed with the FAA to protect adjacent-band aviation safety systems (per the article).
- **Deployment expectation:** commercial mobile networks from December 2030 in the top 75 U.S. markets (per the article).
- **Not in the article:** the July 2026 order's docket number.
