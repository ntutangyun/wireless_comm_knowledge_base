## Summary (EN)
At IFA 2026 in Berlin (announced 2026-08-31), TP-Link unveiled what it calls the industry's first complete consumer Wi-Fi 8 product lineup, moving from the roadmap it published in May 2026 (KB: 2026-05-30_tplink-wifi8-archer8-roadmap) to concrete, specced hardware. The **Archer 8 Ultra** (model BN19000 / Archer BN800) is a tri-band Wi-Fi 8 router rated up to 19 Gbps aggregate wireless throughput with 10 Gbps wired connectivity and 18 internal antennas; it opens for regional pre-order on 2026-09-30. The **Deco 8 Ultra** (model BN22000 / Deco BN85) is a tri-band Wi-Fi 8 mesh system rated up to 22 Gbps aggregate throughput, also with 10 Gbps wired backhaul, with a three-node kit rated for up to 9,800 sq ft of coverage and 200+ connected devices; it is scheduled for Q1 2027. A Roam 8 travel router plus range extenders and adapters follow in Q2 2027.

TP-Link brands its 802.11bn feature set "Wi-Fi 8 StabilityEngine," and names the specific UHR mechanisms it implements: Enhanced/Extended Long Range (ELR), Distributed Resource Units (DRU), Non-Primary Channel Access (NPCA), Unequal Modulation (UEQM), and new Modulation and Coding Schemes (MCS) — the same core 802.11bn mechanism set this KB has tracked through Mentor contributions and the CUHK/Huawei verification paper (2026-07-24_arxiv-wifi8-uhr-mechanisms-verification). No pricing was disclosed.

## Summary (ZH)
在柏林 IFA 2026 展会上（2026-08-31 宣布），TP-Link 发布了其所称的业界首个完整消费级 Wi-Fi 8 产品线，从 2026 年 5 月发布的路线图（KB：2026-05-30_tplink-wifi8-archer8-roadmap）走向了具体规格的硬件。**Archer 8 Ultra**（型号 BN19000 / Archer BN800）是一款三频 Wi-Fi 8 路由器，无线聚合速率最高 19 Gbps，有线连接 10 Gbps，内置 18 根天线；将于 2026-09-30 在部分地区开放预订。**Deco 8 Ultra**（型号 BN22000 / Deco BN85）是一款三频 Wi-Fi 8 Mesh 系统，无线聚合速率最高 22 Gbps，同样支持 10 Gbps 有线回程，三件套覆盖面积最高 9,800 平方英尺，可支持 200 台以上联网设备；计划 2027 年第一季度上市。旅行路由器 Roam 8 及扩展器、适配器等产品将于 2027 年第二季度跟进。

## Key technical points (EN)
- Archer 8 Ultra (BN19000): tri-band, ~19 Gbps aggregate, 10 Gbps wired, 18 antennas; pre-order opens 2026-09-30, select regions.
- Deco 8 Ultra (BN22000): tri-band mesh, ~22 Gbps aggregate, 10 Gbps wired backhaul, 3-node kit up to 9,800 sq ft / 200+ devices; ships Q1 2027.
- Roam 8 travel router + extenders/adapters follow Q2 2027.
- Named UHR mechanisms: ELR, DRU, NPCA, UEQM, new MCS — branded together as "Wi-Fi 8 StabilityEngine."
- No pricing disclosed; this is a pre-standard product (802.11bn ratification is not expected until 2028), consistent with the "draft-spec hardware ships ahead of ratification" pattern already seen with Wi-Fi 7.
