## Summary (EN)

This is a Japanese-language area report titled "Moves to promote the short-range wireless standard SparkLink (China)", dated 24 December 2025 and written by Koki Ono of JETRO's Guangzhou Office (Japan External Trade Organization). It organises the technology's characteristics, how the standard came about, and the state of adoption and standardization. The report uses the name "SparkLink" and notes in a footnote that NearLink is another English name, with media mixing the two.

On the technology, the report describes a layered protocol with two access-layer modes: SLB, which it glosses as Synchronous Low Latency Broadband (low latency, high concurrency, high-precision synchronization), and SLE, glossed as Synchronous Low Energy (low cost, low power). It cites the alliance as listing further characteristics: low latency, high throughput, many simultaneous connections, high reliability, strong interference resistance and high-precision positioning. On origins, the report first gives a technical motivation, that a single short-range technology cannot serve diverse task needs, and adds that US-China decoupling appears to be one further factor: citing multiple press reports, it says the Wi-Fi Alliance and other bodies restricted Huawei's activities after its 2019 US Entity List designation, that the restriction was soon lifted, and that the episode may have accelerated Huawei's own-standard effort. The report says the alliance was founded in 2020 by CAICT and Huawei.

On applications, the report says Huawei launched its first SparkLink product, a stylus, in September 2023, and that more than 200 products had adopted the technology by June 2025, mainly home appliances, PC peripherals, car electronics and production equipment. Low-latency examples are esports mice, keyboards and gamepads; in cars, SparkLink digital keys are fitted in mass-production vehicles. For automotive use the report says active noise cancellation, in-vehicle infotainment and battery management systems are under consideration, and that some voices suggest SparkLink wireless links could replace the wiring harness for in-vehicle electronic devices. The report's photographs are credited to JETRO.

On standardization, the report classifies SparkLink today as a "forum standard" developed by volunteer companies. As of June 2025 the alliance had more than 1,200 members, including Lenovo, China Mobile, Beijing New Energy Vehicle, ZTE, Haier and Midea; foreign participation is described as limited so far, and SparkLink Japan, the alliance's Japanese branch, was established in August 2025. Two Chinese industry standards that incorporate SparkLink ("Technical requirements and test methods for commercial-vehicle surround-view systems" and "Technical requirements and measurement methods for set-top-box universal remote controls") are described as already recognised, and national-standard applications for commercial-vehicle surround view and for vehicle digital keys are said to be in progress. The report also says the Chinese delegation has proposed SparkLink standardization in an ITU working group. It adds that SparkLink and HarmonyOS could be packaged together, and concludes that SparkLink is still consolidating a base within China and that overseas expansion could accelerate as Chinese manufacturers expand abroad.

## Summary (ZH)

这是一篇日文分析报告，题为《短距离无线通信规格“SparkLink”的普及动向（中国）》，日期为2025年12月24日，作者为日本贸易振兴机构（JETRO）广州事务所的小野好树。报告整理了该技术的特点、标准的产生背景，以及普及和标准化现状。报告使用“SparkLink”一名，并在脚注中说明 NearLink 是另一个英文名称，媒体中两者混用。

技术方面，报告描述了一种分层协议，其接入层有两种模式：SLB（报告将其解释为 Synchronous Low Latency Broadband，特点为低时延、高并发、高精度同步）和 SLE（解释为 Synchronous Low Energy，特点为低成本、低功耗）。报告引用联盟列出的其他特性：低时延、高吞吐、高并发连接、高可靠、强抗干扰和高精度定位。关于起源，报告先给出技术上的动机——单一短距离技术无法满足多样化的任务需求，并称中美脱钩似乎也是因素之一：报告援引多家媒体报道称，2019年华为被列入美国实体清单后，Wi-Fi 联盟等组织曾限制其活动，限制不久后解除，这一事件可能加快了华为自主推出标准的努力。报告称联盟由中国信通院（CAICT）与华为于2020年发起成立。

应用方面，报告称华为于2023年9月推出首款星闪产品——手写笔，截至2025年6月累计已有200多款产品采用，主要领域为家电、PC 外设、车载电子和生产设备。低时延的例子包括电竞鼠标、键盘和游戏手柄；车载方面，星闪数字钥匙已装配于量产车。报告称汽车领域正在考虑将其用于主动降噪、车载信息娱乐和电池管理系统，并有声音认为星闪无线通信可能取代车载电子设备间的线束连接。报告中的照片署名为 JETRO 拍摄。

标准化方面，报告将星闪目前归为由志愿企业制定的“论坛标准”。截至2025年6月，联盟成员超过1,200家，包括联想、中国移动、北京新能源汽车、中兴通讯、海尔和美的等；外资参与目前有限，联盟于2025年8月成立日本分支 SparkLink Japan。报告称两项纳入星闪的中国行业标准（《商用车全景环视系统技术要求及试验方法》和《机顶盒通用遥控器技术要求及测量方法》）已获认定，商用车环视系统和车辆数字钥匙的国家标准申请流程正在推进。报告还称中国代表团已在 ITU 的一个工作组中提出星闪标准化提案。报告另指出星闪与 HarmonyOS 可能打包推广，并总结称星闪目前仍处于在中国国内巩固基础的阶段，随着中国厂商出海，海外普及有可能加速。
