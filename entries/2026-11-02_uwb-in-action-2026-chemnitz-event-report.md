## Summary (EN)

Wi-Fi NOW Global published an event report on 30 October 2026 by its CEO and Chairman, Claus Hetting, about "UWB in ACTION 2026", held on 29 October 2026 in Chemnitz, Germany. The conference was organised by the German UWB specialist Pinpoint together with Wi-Fi NOW; the organiser's own event page places it at the University Library Chemnitz and lists an English-language programme running from 08:30 to a 19:00 dinner. The report states the event was fully booked and combined talks with hands-on demos and workshops on mobility, entertainment, retail and industrial tracking.

According to the report, participants included silicon vendors (Qorvo, NXP, STMicroelectronics, Infineon), HPE and SICK, plus standards bodies, analysts and end users. Among end users, Deutsche Bahn presented a vision for passenger navigation and Schwarz IT (the technology arm of Schwarz Group) discussed positioning in retail and smart spaces. The report names Qorvo's Alexis Bizalion as a speaker, says NXP addressed the move "from precise location to intelligent motion", HPE covered UWB infrastructure for enterprises, SICK and the omlox ecosystem covered interoperable positioning for industrial material flows, and STMicroelectronics highlighted IEEE 802.15.4ab as the next-generation UWB standard. The organiser's agenda adds an ABI Research market-outlook slot, a Flowcate session on UWB and "physical AI", and an Infineon session on UWB opportunities.

The technical claims in the report are: centimetre-level positioning accuracy linked to industrial and "physical AI" uses; secure proximity detection, for example keeping robots and human workers safe around each other; and indoor wayfinding that protects privacy because the phone knows the user's location while the network does not. The report also notes that Apple and Google have opened UWB capabilities to third-party developers, which it says could speed adoption through smartphones. Looking ahead, it says the next challenge is turning the technology into scalable deployments with clear business cases. The report frames UWB alongside Wi-Fi: Wi-Fi supplies enterprise connectivity and UWB adds precise spatial awareness for buildings, factories, hospitals and venues, while the closing paragraph adds that UWB may in many cases supersede Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and other technologies for accurate location.

Source note: Wi-Fi NOW co-organised the event, and the report is written by its CEO. Descriptions such as "proven" applications and the privacy benefit reflect the organisers' perspective and are not independently verified in the text; no measured results or product announcements are given.
