## Summary (EN)

SatNews reports that on October 8, 2026, Lithuanian optical technology firm Astrolight, the National and Kapodistrian University of Athens (NKUA) and Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTh) announced the commissioning of an end-to-end space-to-ground laser communication link. The link connected Astrolight's ATLAS-1 laser communication terminal aboard the ERMIS-3 nanosatellite to the upgraded OGS-2.5 optical ground station in Holomondas, Greece, operated remotely from Astrolight's facility in Lithuania.

Reported link metrics: first light during the initial orbital pass and routine 1 Gbps downlinks within one week, with link budget capacity supporting up to 10 Gbps; two pre-scheduled passes daily across daytime and nighttime conditions; closed-loop tracking maintained below 10 degrees elevation; link availability above 85% for passes above 15 degrees; and, in accordance with Space Development Agency (SDA) standards, re-acquisition of interrupted optical signals in under 10 milliseconds without a beam-scanning step.

The article states the ERMIS-3 and PeakSat missions were funded via the European Recovery Fund "Greece 2.0" and the Hellenic Ministry of Digital Governance, with oversight from ESA and the Hellenic Space Center, and that the spacecraft was deployed on SpaceX's Transporter-16 rideshare. Astrolight's CTO is quoted saying the company is "among the first companies to autonomously operate an end-to-end laser communication link using its own systems in orbit and on the ground, attended purely remotely from the moment of installation." The article also lists an Arctic optical ground station in Greenland in cooperation with ESA, qualification of the ATLAS-2 and ATLAS-X-MINI terminals (up to 2.5 Gbps), and ATLAS-X for commissioning aboard ESA's HydRON program in 2028-2029.

## Summary (ZH)

SatNews 报道，2026 年 10 月 8 日，立陶宛光学技术公司 Astrolight、雅典国立卡波迪斯特里安大学（NKUA）和塞萨洛尼基亚里士多德大学（AUTh）宣布完成端到端空地激光通信链路的调试。该链路连接 ERMIS-3 纳卫星上的 Astrolight ATLAS-1 激光通信终端与希腊 Holomondas 升级后的 OGS-2.5 光学地面站，由 Astrolight 位于立陶宛的设施远程操作。

报告的链路指标：首个轨道过境即实现首次光信号（first light），一周内转入常规 1 Gbps 下行，链路预算支持最高 10 Gbps；每天两次预先安排的过境，涵盖白天和夜间条件；在仰角低于 10 度时保持闭环跟踪；仰角高于 15 度的过境链路可用率超过 85%；并按照太空发展局（SDA）标准，在无需波束扫描步骤的情况下于 10 毫秒内重新捕获中断的光信号。

文章称，ERMIS-3 与 PeakSat 任务由欧洲复苏基金“Greece 2.0”和希腊数字治理部资助，并受 ESA 和希腊航天中心监督，航天器通过 SpaceX Transporter-16 拼车发射部署。Astrolight 首席技术官称公司“是最早之一使用自有在轨和地面系统自主运行端到端激光通信链路、且自安装起完全远程值守的公司”。文章还列出：与 ESA 合作在格陵兰建设北极光学地面站，ATLAS-2 与 ATLAS-X-MINI 终端（最高 2.5 Gbps）正在鉴定，ATLAS-X 将于 2028-2029 年在 ESA HydRON 计划上调试。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Link**: ATLAS-1 terminal on the ERMIS-3 nanosatellite to the OGS-2.5 ground station in Holomondas, Greece; remotely operated from Lithuania.
- **Throughput**: routine 1 Gbps downlinks within one week of first light; link budget supports up to 10 Gbps.
- **Tracking and availability**: closed-loop tracking below 10 degrees elevation; availability above 85% for passes above 15 degrees.
- **Re-acquisition**: under 10 ms without a beam-scanning step, per SDA standards as stated in the article.
- **Roadmap**: ATLAS-2 and ATLAS-X-MINI (up to 2.5 Gbps); ATLAS-X aboard ESA HydRON in 2028-2029.
