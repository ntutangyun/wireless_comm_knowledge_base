## Summary (EN)
Telesat Government Solutions and Cailabs US announced on 1 September 2026 a strategic collaboration agreement to explore integrating the optical inter-satellite links (ISL) planned for Telesat's **Lightspeed** LEO constellation with Cailabs' production-ready optical ground systems, for secure, resilient, high-capacity optical space-to-ground connectivity aimed at U.S. Government and Allied missions. Telesat Lightspeed's space-based mesh architecture is built around **900 optical ISL terminals** that route traffic in orbit to form a secure space-based backbone, and both companies will integrate around the **TESAT SCOT80** terminal family — the same terminals selected for Lightspeed — on the space side. On the ground side, Cailabs contributes proprietary **atmospheric-turbulence-compensation** technology, already used to establish routine optical space-to-ground links with satellites carrying SCOT80-family terminals; the release states Cailabs is targeting production capacity of up to **50 optical ground stations per year** as it scales. The companies will pursue joint studies and demonstrations of the combined space+ground optical capability for U.S. Government opportunities, including space data relay services. Lightspeed is described as operating in both commercial Ka-band and Mil-Ka spectrum for flexible, mission-critical defense use. No specific timeline for the studies, demonstrations, or a first operational deployment was given.
