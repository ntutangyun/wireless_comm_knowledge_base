## Summary (EN)
MikroTik announced the KNOT Gateway HL9 on 5 November 2026 (product code L14G-2ax-BT5-HL9&EG800Q; suggested price $139), as relayed in Wi-Fi NOW's 9 Nov roundup, which names the Morse Micro MM6108 as the HaLow chipset and lists industrial IoT, agricultural monitoring and smart infrastructure as target uses. The product page describes an IP66-rated outdoor gateway combining 915 MHz Wi-Fi HaLow, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi 6, BLE 5.1, an LTE Cat 1 bis modem and multi-GNSS.

Listed specifications: IPQ-5010 dual-core ARM64 at 800 MHz, 256 MB RAM and 128 MB NAND; 2.4 GHz 802.11b/g/n/ax with one chain, up to 287 Mbit/s and 3.9 dBi antenna gain; LTE Cat 1 bis (10 Mbps down / 5 Mbps up, bands 2/4/5/12/13/66) with nano-SIM and eSIM; one Gigabit Ethernet port with PoE-in (802.3af/at, 18-57 V) and 12-57 V DC input; maximum 7 W, passive cooling; tested ambient temperature -40 to 50 C. Integrated antennas cover BLE, Wi-Fi, LTE, sub-GHz and GNSS, with external connectors for BLE and sub-GHz. The page does not list HaLow channel widths, data rate or range.
