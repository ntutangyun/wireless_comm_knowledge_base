## Summary (EN)

In a release dated 12 November 2026 (Sydney) and posted on the Wireless Broadband Alliance site on 17 November, Morse Micro announced a collaboration with CamThink, a Milesight sub-brand that sells open edge-vision-AI hardware (NeoEyes cameras, NeoEdge computing products, the NeoMind edge AI platform). Wi-Fi HaLow is now integrated across CamThink's compact camera lineup: the NeoEyes NE101, described as the first camera launched through the partnership and as a modular sensing camera for large areas such as agricultural fields; the NE301, a new HaLow option combining on-device AI with long-range connectivity for unattended monitoring at remote sites; and the NE302, the newest model, available with embedded Wi-Fi HaLow for developers and OEM teams integrating vision AI into their products.

The release says the HaLow versions of all three are available now through camthink.ai/store. It describes Morse Micro's first-generation MM6108 and second-generation MM8108 HaLow chips and repeats the company's claims of 10x the range and 100x the area of traditional Wi-Fi and 1,000x the data rate of traditional LPWAN technologies. The release frames HaLow as filling the range and power-efficiency gap of conventional Wi-Fi for battery-powered devices in remote locations; De Nil's quote (Morse Micro co-founder and CEO Michael De Nil) cites range and power efficiency, and Shen's (CamThink co-founder Ming Shen) cites long-range, low-power connectivity for remote monitoring and embedded products. The release does not state which chip each camera uses, link throughput, or on-device model details.
