## Summary (EN)

MediaTek announced on 2026-11-02 (Hsinchu) that, together with Ericsson, it completed what it calls the first successful multi-vendor pre-6G over-the-air field trial, hosted by T-Mobile US at its 6G Innovation Hub in Bellevue, Washington. The trial itself ran in September 2026. Ericsson supplied the pre-6G radio infrastructure and MediaTek supplied a pre-6G user-equipment proof-of-concept platform, connected over the air on T-Mobile's network.

The release reports a 400 MHz downlink channel and a 200 MHz uplink channel, potential peak speeds above 5 Gbps downlink and above 2 Gbps uplink, and end-to-end latency as low as 4 ms. It names no 3GPP release, saying only that the device platform aligns with the 3GPP RAN plenary direction on 6G device capability. The item was found through a 6G search on X on 2026-11-06 where it was being relayed.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Setup: Ericsson pre-6G radio infrastructure plus MediaTek pre-6G UE proof-of-concept platform, over the air, at T-Mobile's Bellevue 6G Innovation Hub; trial conducted September 2026, announced 2026-11-02.
- Channel configuration reported: 400 MHz contiguous downlink, 200 MHz uplink. The release does not state the frequency band; trade coverage (Light Reading, https://www.lightreading.com/6g/t-mobile-tests-6g-with-ericsson-prototypes) reports an FCC experimental licence for T-Mobile to test Ericsson and MediaTek prototype gear around Bellevue in 6425-6825 MHz (not confirmed in the release).
- Performance claims are potential peaks (above 5 Gbps downlink, above 2 Gbps uplink) and a best-case latency of 4 ms; the release does not describe the test conditions, antenna configuration or sustained throughput.
