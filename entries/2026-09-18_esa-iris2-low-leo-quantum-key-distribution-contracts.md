## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-17, the European Space Agency (ESA), under its Connectivity and Secure Communications directorate, signed 18 contracts engaging an industrial consortium of 80 space companies, research institutes, and technology SMEs across European ESA member states, per SatNews. The contracts open the next technology-development phase for the EU's IRIS² (Infrastructure for Resilience, Interconnection and Security by Satellite) multi-orbit constellation, and focus on evaluating and maturing Low-Low Earth Orbit (Low-LEO) concepts — orbital altitudes below the standard 600-1,200 km LEO range, intended to reduce transmission latency — alongside resilient communications links and quantum-resistant cryptographic payloads.

The contract streams target several engineering domains beyond the baseline IRIS² architecture already tracked in this KB: atmospheric-drag mitigation and high-efficiency propulsion needed to sustain Low-LEO orbits, optical inter-satellite links, advanced spatial frequency reuse, space-based Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) and post-quantum cryptographic protocols for securing government communications traffic, and software-defined payloads intended to support interoperable handoffs between GEO, MEO, and Low-LEO segments. According to the article, operating at lower LEO altitudes is intended to reduce the link-budget power required for ground-to-space communication, which the article states would enable smaller user terminals for mobile defense personnel and uncrewed vehicles. The article gives no specific budget figures, satellite counts, or deployment dates for this phase; the 18 industrial teams will run system design reviews and payload feasibility assessments over the coming months, with findings feeding into the European Commission's procurement specifications for future IRIS² constellation-expansion tranches.

## Summary (ZH)

据 SatNews 报道，2026 年 9 月 17 日，欧洲空间局（ESA）通信与安全通信理事会（Connectivity and Secure Communications directorate）与来自欧洲各 ESA 成员国的 80 家航天企业、研究机构与科技类中小企业组成的产业联合体签署了 18 份合同。这些合同开启了欧盟 IRIS²（弹性、互联与安全卫星基础设施）多轨道星座的下一技术研发阶段，重点评估并推进超低地球轨道（Low-LEO，即低于常规 600-1,200 公里 LEO 高度范围的轨道）概念的技术成熟，同时涵盖弹性通信链路与抗量子密码载荷技术。

这批合同所涉及的工程领域超出了知识库此前已收录的 IRIS² 基线架构，包括：维持超低轨运行所需的大气阻力抑制与高效推进技术、光学星间链路、先进的空间频率复用技术、用于保护政府通信流量的星载量子密钥分发（QKD）与后量子密码协议，以及旨在支持 GEO、MEO 与 Low-LEO 各轨道段之间可互操作切换的软件定义载荷。文章指出，在更低的 LEO 高度运行有助于降低地面-太空通信所需的链路预算功率，从而使移动国防人员与无人系统能够使用更小型的用户终端。文章未披露本阶段的具体预算、卫星数量或部署日期；18 个产业团队将在未来数月内开展系统设计评审与载荷可行性评估，评估结果将纳入欧盟委员会针对 IRIS² 星座未来扩展批次的采购规范。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Contract scope**: 18 contracts, 80 industrial/research/SME partners across ESA member states, under ESA's Connectivity and Secure Communications directorate.
- **Orbital regime focus**: "Low-LEO" concepts operating below the standard 600-1,200 km LEO altitude band, aimed at reducing transmission latency versus IRIS²'s baseline architecture.
- **Engineering domains funded**: atmospheric-drag mitigation, high-efficiency propulsion, optical inter-satellite links, advanced spatial frequency reuse.
- **Security payloads**: space-based Quantum Key Distribution (QKD) and post-quantum cryptographic protocols for government communications.
- **Interoperability target**: software-defined payload designs to support seamless handoffs between GEO, MEO, and Low-LEO segments.
- **Stated rationale**: lower-altitude operation reduces required link-budget power for ground-to-space links, enabling smaller user terminals for mobile defense and uncrewed-vehicle users (per the article; no independent verification of this claim was found).
- **No disclosed figures**: article provides no budget total, satellite counts, or deployment dates for this development phase.
