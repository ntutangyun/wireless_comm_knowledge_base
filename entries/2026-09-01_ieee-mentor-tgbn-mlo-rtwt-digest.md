## Summary (EN)
TGbn uploaded two new contributions this run, plus a late first-upload of a third that had been assigned a document number earlier but only surfaced today's scrape. DCN 11-26-1577r0 (Hang Yang, Ruijie) is titled "Discussion on ST Preparation for Multiple Target AP MLDs" — an early-stage discussion document on how a non-AP multi-link device should prepare state when a link switch or reassociation has more than one candidate target AP MLD to consider. DCN 11-26-1572r0 (Junpeng Lou, Ruijie) is "Further Considerations on Co-RTWT" — a continuation of the coordinated restricted target wake time (Co-RTWT) discussion thread across multiple APs; it was first uploaded 2026-08-31 even though its document number sits below documents already logged in a prior run, an artifact of the reservation-then-late-upload pattern this Mentor listing periodically shows.
