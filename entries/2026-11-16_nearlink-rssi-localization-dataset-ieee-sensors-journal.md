## Summary (EN)

"Unveiling NearLink's Localization Potential: First Public RSSI Dataset and Cross-Environment Evaluation With Wi-Fi and BLE" (Song Xie, Yixue Guo, Fangming Guo, Xianlei Long, Jianguo Zhou, Yan Li, Leilei Li, Fuqiang Gu) is in IEEE Sensors Journal vol. 26, no. 17, pp. 26863-26875, 1 September 2026 (DOI 10.1109/JSEN.2026.3710363). The paper evaluates NearLink alone and combined with Wi-Fi and BLE in a classroom, a parking lot and a helipad, using RSSI data collected by an autonomous robot. The authors release what they describe as the first publicly available NearLink RSSI fingerprint dataset and benchmark six localization methods (MLT, KNN, MLP, LSTM-RNN, HADNN, GConvLoc). They report that NearLink in SLE mode reaches submeter accuracy (0.81 m in classrooms), robust anti-interference behaviour, up to 725 m communication range and 2.39 mW power consumption, and that fusion with BLE further improves stability.

## Summary (ZH)

《Unveiling NearLink's Localization Potential: First Public RSSI Dataset and Cross-Environment Evaluation With Wi-Fi and BLE》（Song Xie、Yixue Guo、Fangming Guo、Xianlei Long、Jianguo Zhou、Yan Li、Leilei Li、Fuqiang Gu）发表于 IEEE Sensors Journal 第26卷第17期，第26863-26875页，2026年9月1日（DOI 10.1109/JSEN.2026.3710363）。论文在教室、停车场和停机坪三类场景中，使用自主机器人采集的 RSSI 数据，评估星闪单独使用以及与 Wi-Fi、BLE 组合的定位表现。作者发布了其称为首个公开的星闪 RSSI 指纹数据集，并对六种定位方法（MLT、KNN、MLP、LSTM-RNN、HADNN、GConvLoc）进行了基准测试。报告称星闪 SLE 模式可达亚米级精度（教室 0.81 米）、抗干扰性能稳健、通信距离最远 725 米、功耗 2.39 mW，与 BLE 融合可进一步提升稳定性。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Scenarios**: classroom, parking lot, helipad (indoor and outdoor).
- **Data collection**: high-fidelity RSSI gathered with an autonomous robotic platform.
- **Dataset**: described by the authors as the first publicly available NearLink RSSI fingerprint dataset.
- **Benchmarked methods**: MLT, KNN, MLP, LSTM-RNN, HADNN, GConvLoc.
- **Reported results** (authors' own): 0.81 m accuracy in classrooms in SLE mode; up to 725 m range; 2.39 mW in SLE mode; BLE fusion improves stability and robustness.
- **Limits of this record**: abstract only; per-method error tables and test conditions for the 725 m figure are not covered.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **场景**：教室、停车场、停机坪（室内外）。
- **数据采集**：使用自主机器人平台采集高保真 RSSI 数据。
