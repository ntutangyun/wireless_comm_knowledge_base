## Summary (EN)

A 2026-11-03 article on the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) news feed describes how Guodong Qinda (Shanghai) Intelligent Technology (国东勤达), a core member of iSLA and of the E-Paper Industry Alliance (EPIA) and a core drafter of the "SparkLink E-Paper Requirements White Paper", exhibited at CEIC2026 and the iSLA industry summit. It showed a terminal matrix for six scenarios (healthcare, office, universities, industry, hotels, airports) that combines SparkLink communication with e-paper displays. The article is a vendor recap; no radio or battery figures are given.

## Summary (ZH)

国际星闪联盟新闻栏目2026年11月3日刊发文章，介绍国东勤达（上海）智能科技有限公司（国际星闪联盟及电子纸产业联盟核心成员、《星闪电子纸需求白皮书》核心起草单位）在CEIC2026暨国际星闪联盟产业峰会上的展示：面向医疗、办公、高校、工业、酒店、机场六大场景的星闪电子纸终端矩阵，将星闪通信与电子纸显示结合。文章为厂商回顾，未给出射频或电池方面的数据。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Concept**: SparkLink is credited with real-time control and large-scale networking of terminals; e-paper with near-zero static power and no backlight; the article calls the combination a "sense-communicate-display" integrated end-point.
- **Healthcare**: nurse badges, ward bedside terminals and ICU boom/tower-mounted terminals; the vendor says deployments include Shanghai Ruijin Hospital, Beijing Tongren Hospital and Hong Kong Union Hospital, with batch doctor-order updates in seconds (vendor claim). The badge combines identity, area permission and message reception.
- **Office and campus**: meeting-table and desk signs updated in batch, with a single base station said to cover a standard meeting room; wiring-free timetable and wayfinding signs for universities (China Agricultural University and Duke University named as recognising the solution).
- **Industry**: badges for sites where phones are not permitted (e.g. restricted or clean workshops) and fixed-asset labels updated remotely.
- **Hotel and airport**: guest-room information signs at Sanya Sofitel and a Hangzhou Xiaoshan Airport project for centralised batch updates of dispersed points.
- **Source limits**: no update latency, battery life, base-station capacity or SparkLink mode (SLE/SLB) is stated.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **理念**：文章称星闪赋予终端实时管控与大规模组网能力，电子纸具备静态近零功耗、无背光等特性，二者结合为“感通显”一体化的末端入口。
- **医疗**：医护胸卡、病房床头终端、ICU吊塔吊桥终端；厂商称已部署于上海瑞金医院、北京同仁医院、香港仁安医院等，可批量医嘱秒级更新（厂商表述）；胸卡集身份标识、区域权限、信息接收于一体。
- **办公与高校**：会议桌牌、工位牌批量更新，称单基站即可覆盖整间标准会场；高校课表牌、导视牌免布线部署（文中称获中国农业大学、美国杜克大学等认可）。
- **工业**：面向不允许携带手机的场所（如涉密、洁净车间）的胸卡，以及可远程更新的固定资产标签。
- **酒店与机场**：与三亚索菲特酒店合作的客房信息牌；杭州萧山机场项目，实现分散点位信息集中管控、批量更新。
- **来源局限**：未给出更新时延、电池寿命、基站容量，也未说明所用星闪模式（SLE/SLB）。
