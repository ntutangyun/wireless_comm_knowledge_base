## Summary (EN)
On **2026-08-26** the International SparkLink Alliance published a member profile of **Shenzhen WaveConn Intelligent Technology (深圳市问沧智能技术有限公司)** describing what is, so far as this knowledge base records, the **first commercially deployed NearLink MESH network operating at thousand-node self-organising scale**. Every NearLink deployment previously captured here — car keys, earbuds, keyboards/mice, routers/gateways, the OneConnect smart-home bus — is a *star* topology: peripherals associating with one gateway or host over SLE. WaveConn's claim is topological rather than merely commercial: **"千量级 MESH 自组网" (thousand-order mesh self-organising networking), where a single SparkLink network covers an entire building, an entire underground car park, or a whole campus.**

The concrete deployment cited is a university campus in Shenzhen where the network spans **above-ground floors 1 through 18 plus an underground car park of more than 10,000 m²**, with the car park **covered by a single SparkLink smart gateway**. Third-party testing is claimed to show **energy savings above 85 % in the underground-car-park scenario and above 90 % for public-area lighting**. The company says the same stack is already deployed in hospitals, commercial plazas and industrial parks, with **road/street-lighting pilots progressing**.

WaveConn positions itself as a **full-stack supplier — modules, gateways and cloud platform — for NearLink MESH plus AIoT**, targeting smart lighting and industrial data acquisition. Its own website confirms four product lines: **SparkLink Mesh communication modules, SparkLink gateways (星闪 MESH 通信网关), SparkLink smart dimming drivers, and a SparkLink sensor family** (temperature/humidity sensors, 4-key scene panels, air-conditioner IR controllers), built on a **"OpenHarmony + SparkLink dual ecosystem"** with an OpenHarmony ecosystem product-compatibility certificate, and lists **HiSilicon as a partner** (implying the HiSilicon WS63-class SLE silicon that the rest of this ecosystem uses). Ecosystem credentials cited: national champion of the alliance's **"星闪杯" (SparkLink Cup) application challenge**, "Shanghai HiSilicon SparkLink Excellence Ambassador", a **"SparkLink Most Commercially Valuable" award**, and an invitation to **HDC 2026**.

**Sourcing caveat:** this is an alliance-published member showcase — vendor-supplied claims. The node count, the coverage figure and the energy-savings percentages are not independently verified here; the third-party testing body is not named, and the university customer is unnamed. The company's own site corroborates the product line and the OpenHarmony/HiSilicon basis but publishes no node-count or coverage specification. Treat the numbers as vendor claims pending an independent measurement.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
Multi-hop mesh has been the missing topology in this knowledge base's NearLink coverage. HiSilicon's 2026 roadmap material listed **Mesh 组网** as a theme (noted and declined on 2026-08-03 as undated marketing), and 2026-08-28_oneconnect-smart-home-unified-interconnect-nearlink tags **SLE-Mesh** as part of the OneConnect hardware bus, but until now there was no dated, deployment-backed data point showing SparkLink actually running as a large multi-hop network rather than as a fast star link to a gateway. This item supplies one — with a node order of magnitude, a coverage figure, an energy-savings number and named verticals — and it comes from the alliance's own channel rather than the news wires, which have carried nothing about it.

The comparison that makes it interesting is direct: 2026-08-28_bluetooth-nlc-hvac-integration-mesh-energy-savings documents Bluetooth NLC (networked lighting control) as the incumbent mesh-lighting standard pursuing exactly this application with exactly this energy-savings argument. NearLink's pitch against BLE mesh in lighting is the same one the ACM TIoT measurement study quantified at the access layer (2026-08-19_acm-tiot-sle-vs-ble-comparative-measurement: ~4× rate, ~3.84× lower latency, ~+10 dB sensitivity vs Bluetooth) — the +10 dB sensitivity margin is precisely what buys hop-count reduction and single-gateway car-park coverage. A commercial mesh deployment is therefore the first place where that access-layer advantage should show up as a *network-level* result, and it also sharpens the EE Times Asia thesis (2026-08-09) that NearLink's 2027 shift is toward consumer-IoT peripherals: industrial/commercial lighting infrastructure is a different, higher-node-count market than earbuds and car keys.

What to watch next: whether an independent measurement or a named test report substantiates the thousand-node and >10,000 m² claims; whether SLE Mesh gets a normative home in the 《星闪无线通信系统》 GB standard family (2026-08-23 entry — Part 301 covers basic-service-layer device discovery and transport control, the natural anchor for a mesh profile); whether the alliance's certification scheme (2026-08-17 entry) adds a mesh/networking conformance tier beyond RF + protocol + interop; and whether any of these mesh vendors migrate onto the open-sourced `communication_nearlink_service` stack, which as of the last successful check still shipped **SLE low-power access mode only**.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
多跳 Mesh 一直是本知识库星闪覆盖中缺失的拓扑。海思 2026 路线图材料曾把 **Mesh 组网** 列为主题（2026-08-03 记录并因"无日期营销材料"而弃用），2026-08-28_oneconnect-smart-home-unified-interconnect-nearlink 也把 **SLE-Mesh** 标注为 OneConnect 硬件总线的一部分，但此前始终没有一个有日期、有落地支撑的数据点，能证明星闪确实作为大规模多跳网络运行，而不仅是通往网关的高速星型链路。本条目提供了这样一个数据点——含节点数量级、覆盖面积、节能数字与具名行业场景——且来自联盟自有渠道，新闻媒体对此毫无报道。

真正有意思的是这组直接对比：2026-08-28_bluetooth-nlc-hvac-integration-mesh-energy-savings 记录了蓝牙 NLC（网络化照明控制）作为在位的 Mesh 照明标准，正以完全相同的节能论证争夺完全相同的应用。星闪在照明场景对 BLE Mesh 的卖点，与 ACM TIoT 实测研究在接入层量化的优势同源（2026-08-19_acm-tiot-sle-vs-ble-comparative-measurement：相对蓝牙约 4 倍速率、约 3.84 倍更低时延、约 +10 dB 灵敏度）——其中 +10 dB 灵敏度余量正是换取跳数减少与单网关覆盖整个车库的关键。因此，商用 Mesh 部署是这一接入层优势首次可能体现为*网络层*结果的场合；它同时也让 EE Times Asia 的判断（2026-08-09：星闪 2027 年将转向消费 IoT 外设）更加细化：工商业照明基础设施是与耳机、车钥匙截然不同、节点数高得多的市场。

后续关注：是否出现独立实测或具名检测报告以证实千量级节点与超 1 万平方米覆盖的主张；SLE Mesh 是否会在《星闪无线通信系统》国标族中获得规范性归属（见 2026-08-23 条目——第 301 部分覆盖基础服务层的设备发现与传输控制，是 Mesh Profile 的天然锚点）；联盟认证方案（2026-08-17 条目）是否会在射频+协议+互操作之外增设 Mesh/组网一致性层级；以及这些 Mesh 厂商是否会迁移到已开源的 `communication_nearlink_service` 协议栈——截至最近一次成功检查，该栈仍仅支持 **SLE 低功耗接入模式**。
