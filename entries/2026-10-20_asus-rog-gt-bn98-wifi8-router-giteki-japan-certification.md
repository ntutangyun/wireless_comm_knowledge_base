## Summary (EN)

INTERNET Watch reported on 2026-10-20 (07:30 JST) that ASUS's Wi-Fi 8 gaming router ROG Rapture GT-BN98 has obtained 技適 (giteki), Japan's radio-equipment technical conformity certification. According to the article, the approval date is 12 September, and the certification cites three provisions of the relevant ministerial ordinance (Article 2, item 19; item 19-3; item 80), which it says correspond to the 2.4 GHz, 5 GHz and 6 GHz bands respectively. The article describes the router as quad-band with a stated total of 25,000 Mbps and notes that Wi-Fi 8 (IEEE 802.11bn) is still being standardised.

The article does not give the certification number, a launch date, a price or availability in Japan; it reads the certification as a sign that preparations for a domestic release are under way.
