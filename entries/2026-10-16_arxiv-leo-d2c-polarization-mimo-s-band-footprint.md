## Summary (EN)

Bachl, Ferro, Peng and Nabeel (arXiv 2610.20296, submitted 14 October 2026) note that a single LEO satellite offers almost no spatial degrees of freedom, but its two polarizations could carry a second layer to a handset, which 5G NR non-terrestrial networks do not use yet. The paper maps the downlink spectral-efficiency (SE) gain of this second layer over the footprint of a satellite at 330 km altitude above Munich transmitting at 2 GHz.

The channel is modelled as a cascade of Jones factors covering the phased-array payload, Faraday rotation, a dual-polarized land mobile satellite (LMS) channel with line of sight, and a standardized handset antenna element at three attitudes. For a zenith-pointing handset, ideal precoding selects rank 2 with probability of at least 0.95 within about 340 km of the nadir point at a reference SNR of 6 dB; with closed-loop precoding, a handset on the nadir track is in rank 2 for 99% of a pass. At 13 dB, closed-loop rank selection raises mean SE over the footprint by 49%, and upright handsets gain about 31%, limited to positions where they face the satellite. At 3 dB the gains fall to 15% and 7%.

## Summary (ZH)

Bachl、Ferro、Peng 和 Nabeel（arXiv 2610.20296，2026 年 10 月 14 日提交）指出，单颗 LEO 卫星几乎没有空间自由度，但其两个极化可为手机承载第二层数据，而 5G NR 非地面网络目前尚未使用这一点。论文在位于慕尼黑上空、高度 330 km、工作于 2 GHz 的卫星覆盖区内，绘制了这一第二层带来的下行频谱效率（SE）增益分布。

信道建模为琼斯因子级联，包括相控阵载荷、法拉第旋转、带视距的双极化陆地移动卫星（LMS）信道，以及三种姿态下的标准化手机天线单元。对朝向天顶的手机，在参考信噪比 6 dB 时，理想预编码在距星下点约 340 km 范围内以至少 0.95 的概率选择 rank 2；采用闭环预编码时，位于星下轨迹上的手机在一次过境中有 99% 的时间处于 rank 2。在 13 dB 时，闭环秩选择使覆盖区平均 SE 提高 49%，直立手机提高约 31%，且仅限于手机正对卫星的位置。在 3 dB 时，增益降至 15% 和 7%。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Scenario**: LEO at 330 km above Munich, 2 GHz, downlink to handsets.
- **Channel**: Jones-factor cascade (phased-array payload, Faraday rotation, dual-polarized LMS channel with LOS, handset antenna at three attitudes).
- **Rank-2 probability**: at least 0.95 within about 340 km of nadir at 6 dB SNR (ideal precoding, zenith-pointing handset).
- **Closed-loop precoding**: nadir-track handset in rank 2 for 99% of a pass.
- **SE gain**: +49% mean over footprint at 13 dB (about +31% for upright handsets); +15% and +7% at 3 dB.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
