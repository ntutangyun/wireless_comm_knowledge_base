## Summary (EN)

On 23 September 2026, HID's Sewio business (acquired by HID Global in August 2024) launched **Zevon**, a new UWB real-time location system (RTLS) product line aimed at industrial asset tracking, with people- and vehicle-tracking use cases also named on HID's Zevon product page. Zevon's headline design choice is operating in the **8 GHz UWB band** rather than around 6 GHz, where the source says conventional UWB systems often operate and growing wireless activity raises interference risk; per HID's Zevon product page, HID calls 8 GHz a "cleaner," less congested band with lower interference risk in dense industrial RF environments. The platform is built on the **IEEE 802.15.4z** standard and is positioned as reducing dependence on proprietary hardware, with 802.15.4z-compliant infrastructure working with compatible tags from other manufacturers.

The hardware line includes two anchor form factors — the **Zevon Omni Anchor** (360° omnidirectional coverage) for open halls and the **Zevon Direct Anchor** (120° focused field of view) for corridors and aisles — plus a **Zevon Asset Tag** with built-in motion detection, a customizable button and LED for alerts, and a multi-year battery life. Both anchors are rated IP65 and specified for -30°C to +60°C operation, targeting harsh factory-floor conditions. The cited article reports that daisy-chain anchor connectivity reduces cabling requirements by 50 to 80%; per HID's Zevon product page, this also cuts installation costs by up to 80% versus a conventionally cabled anchor grid, with accuracy quoted at up to 30 cm (1 ft) under optimal conditions and roughly 0.5 m typical in real industrial deployments. The system integrates with HID's RTLS Studio software and is pitched for production-facility material flow automation, warehouse tracking and AI-driven process optimization, with ERP/MES integration. Lubomir Mraz, Co-Founder of HID's Sewio and UWB Evangelist, is quoted framing Zevon as addressing "the three biggest challenges facing industrial RTLS users: reliability, infrastructure costs and vendor lock-in." No pricing or general-availability date was disclosed in the source article.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Launch date 23 September 2026 (per ThinkWIoT/wiot-group.com); HID Sewio is the UWB RTLS unit HID Global acquired in August 2024.
- Operates in the **8 GHz UWB band** rather than around 6 GHz, where conventional UWB systems more often operate per the source; HID calls 8 GHz "less congested." Standard: **IEEE 802.15.4z**.
- Two anchor types: Zevon Omni Anchor (360°, open-hall coverage) and Zevon Direct Anchor (120° focused FoV, corridors/aisles); both IP65, -30°C to +60°C.
- Zevon Asset Tag: motion detection, configurable button/LED, multi-year battery.
- Claimed cabling reduction of 50–80% (up to 80%) via daisy-chain anchor connectivity; the up-to-80% installation-cost figure is per HID's product page.
- Accuracy: up to 30 cm (1 ft) optimal, ~0.5 m typical in industrial deployments (per HID's Zevon product page).
- Software: RTLS Studio, with ERP/MES integration; target use cases are production-facility material flow, warehouse tracking, AI-driven process optimization.
- No pricing or general-availability date disclosed.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The KB's existing UWB RTLS coverage is anchor-vendor-centric but band-agnostic: NXP's omlox Starter Kit (2026-08-15), Murata's factory tracking system (2026-08-05) and Qorvo's UWB-in-enterprise-AP program (2026-08-19_qorvo-uwb-rtls-enterprise-wifi-access-points) all describe deployment models and integration points without highlighting a specific UWB sub-band as a differentiator. Zevon is the first entry in this bin where a vendor makes the **choice of UWB frequency band itself** (8 GHz vs. the more contested channels other RTLS stacks use) the headline reliability argument, alongside the now-familiar "reduce anchor cabling cost" and "avoid vendor lock-in via IEEE 802.15.4z" pitches already seen from NXP and Qorvo. It also adds a second dedicated-anchor industrial RTLS data point (after Murata) with named accuracy figures (30 cm best-case / 0.5 m typical) that can be compared directly against Qorvo's enterprise-AP framing (10-30 cm, sub-100 ms).

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

KB 此前的 UWB RTLS 相关条目以锚点厂商为中心，但均未强调具体频段：NXP omlox 入门套件（2026-08-15）、Murata 工厂追踪系统（2026-08-05）以及 Qorvo 的企业级 AP 内嵌 UWB 项目（2026-08-19_qorvo-uwb-rtls-enterprise-wifi-access-points）都描述了部署模式与集成方式，但未把某个具体 UWB 子频段作为差异化卖点。Zevon 是该主题下首条把**UWB 频段选择本身**（8 GHz，而非其他RTLS系统常用、更拥挤的6 GHz附近频段）作为可靠性核心卖点的条目，同时延续了 NXP、Qorvo 已出现过的"降低锚点布线成本"与"通过 IEEE 802.15.4z 避免厂商锁定"的宣传角度。它还为专用锚点工业 RTLS 补充了第二个具体精度数据点（继 Murata 之后），给出了最优 30 厘米／典型 0.5 米的精度数字，可与 Qorvo 企业 AP 方案（10-30 厘米、亚 100 毫秒）直接对比。
