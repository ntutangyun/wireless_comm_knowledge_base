## Summary (EN)

The alliance's recap of the 2026 SparkLink Application Forum (afternoon of 23 October 2026, Shenzhen, theme "SparkLink AI - connecting everything"; four tracks: AI foundations and embodied intelligence, industries, people-vehicle-home-campus, and chip foundations) lists the talks in order.

Alliance secretary-general Zeng Guosong said SparkLink is no longer a "to be verified" technology but at the turning point from standards to scale deployment. Vice-chairman Wan Lei (Huawei Fellow) presented SparkLink's passive SLZ mode, integrated communication-positioning-sensing, the AI development kit and all-optical SparkLink. A researcher from the Institute of Computing Technology (CAS) described untethered intra-body communication for embodied agents and named three challenges: high-performance receiver design, system resource efficiency and channel characterisation in complex environments. The Dora-rs community said determinism must come from several layers - SparkLink for wireless transmission, DORA for real-time scheduling and execution, Octos for safety constraints on multi-agent orchestration. Huawei Cloud's embodied-intelligence platform team described a cloud-edge-device architecture, saying SparkLink is stable under strong electromagnetic fields, at height and in multi-robot clusters, addressing Wi-Fi instability and costly 5G uplink. A Tsinghua speaker presented the SparkLink AI multimodal information encapsulation and transmission standard (AIEP), part of SparkLink 3.0, aimed at unified multimodal encapsulation, native agent interaction and an AI quality-of-service framework; the standard is being drafted, with a draft expected by end of 2026 and formal submission for approval in 2027.

China Mobile Research Institute reported pilots of a 5G-plus-SparkLink all-wireless industrial-control approach (oilfield wireless data collection, robot cooperative handling, retrofit of automotive-factory robot arms), with 5G as the base and SparkLink covering the last hop, while saying broad short-range deployment "still has distance to go". A State Grid vehicle-grid specialist said SparkLink could fill the car-charger-grid communication gap without changing existing hardware interfaces and replace cabling at charging sites. The National New-Type Energy Storage Innovation Center said it formed, with Huawei, CSG Technology, Zhongke Jingshang and Xuji, a power-operations SparkLink SIG, piloting personal-safety protection, distribution maintenance, switchgear monitoring and transmission-line bird-damage prevention; it cautioned that replacing Bluetooth, Wi-Fi and some wired links is a long-term process, with differentiated high-value routes the key.

Other talks: the China Audio Industry Association said the industry's output passed 400 billion yuan for the first time this year and that wireless is being driven by SparkLink; a Huawei terminal scientist said the commercially mature protocol-stack code is open-sourced to OpenHarmony and announced a "Xingji" positioning brand; Midea reported progress on the SparkLink smart-appliance (Shanghai) innovation lab and said SparkLink Mesh is deployed in hotels and smart door locks, with human-presence sensing used for elder and child care; Zhongke Jingshang said it will launch a SparkLink 2.0 broadband chip (frequency coverage, range, reliability, anti-interference, full-stack security); Maxscend said its automotive-grade SparkLink/Bluetooth dual-mode chip MXD2680 passed SLE 2.0 RF and protocol conformance tests at the telecom-terminal lab and will enter volume production at year end; and the China Academy of ICT's terminal lab said the alliance has authorised three test laboratories and added integrated SparkLink-plus-HarmonyOS testing, with a "one horizontal, several vertical" scheme covering peripherals, car keys and audio and rules to avoid repeated testing after variant changes. A closing roundtable involved Huawei, Fraunhofer IIS, STMicroelectronics, Xuji, NXP and DEKRA.

## Summary (ZH)

联盟对2026星闪应用论坛（2026年10月23日下午，深圳，主题“星闪AI·智合万物”，设“AI筑基·具身领航”“千行百业·智赋未来”“人车家园·智享生活”“芯火燎原·硬核底座”四个议题）的回顾稿依次列出各场发言。

联盟秘书长曾国松表示，星闪已不再是“待验证”的新技术，而是站在从标准建设迈向规模商用的关键拐点。副理事长、华为Fellow万蕾介绍了星闪无源SLZ、通定感一体、星闪AI开发套件与全光星闪。中科院计算所的研究者讲述具身智能体域无缆化通信，指出高性能接收机设计、系统资源效率提升、复杂环境信道表征三大挑战。Dora-rs社区称确定性需多层共同实现：星闪提供无线传输确定性，DORA提供实时调度与执行确定性，Octos提供多智能体编排的安全约束。华为云具身智能平台介绍云边端架构，称星闪在强电磁、高空、多机集群工况下稳定可靠，补齐WiFi不稳、5G上行昂贵的短板。清华大学讲者介绍《星闪AI多模态信息封装传输标准》（AIEP），属星闪3.0的组成部分，目标是统一多模态封装、构建原生智能体交互、梳理AI服务质量体系；标准正在编制，预计年底形成草案、明年正式报批。

中国移动研究院介绍5G+星闪全无线工控试点（油田无线数采、机器人协同搬运、汽车制造机械臂改造），5G打底、星闪补齐末梢，同时坦言短距规模推广仍有距离。国网智慧车联网的专家称，星闪可在不改造原有硬件接口的前提下打通车、桩、网之间通信链路，并以无线替代场站布线。国家新型储能创新中心称已联合华为、南网科技、中科晶上、许继电气组建电力能源智慧运维星闪SIG，试点人身安全防护、配电运维、开关柜监测、输电鸟害防治等场景，并强调星闪替代蓝牙、WiFi及部分有线是长期过程，关键是走差异化高价值路线。

其他发言：中国电子音响行业协会称今年行业产值首次站上4000亿元，无线化正得到星闪赋能；华为终端科学家称成熟商用的协议栈代码已开源至OpenHarmony，并推出“星迹定位”品牌；美的汇报星闪智能家电（上海）创新实验室建设进展，并称星闪Mesh组网已在酒店、智能门锁等场景落地，依托人体感知可为老人儿童提供看护；中科晶上称将推出星闪2.0宽带芯片（频段覆盖、距离、可靠性、抗干扰、全栈安全）；卓胜微称其车规级星闪蓝牙双模芯片MXD2680已在泰尔实验室通过SLE 2.0射频与协议一致性测试，年底量产；中国信通院泰尔终端实验室称联盟已授权三家测试实验室，新增“星闪+鸿蒙”一体化测试能力，构建“一横多纵”体系覆盖外设、车钥匙、音频，并通过优化改型规则避免重复测试。圆桌嘉宾来自华为、弗劳恩霍夫集成电路研究所、意法半导体、许继电气、恩智浦和德凯。

## Key technical points (EN)

- AIEP: part of SparkLink 3.0; scope is multimodal encapsulation, native agent interaction and an AI QoS framework; draft by end of 2026, formal approval submission in 2027 (speaker's statement).
- Three-layer determinism framing: SparkLink (wireless transmission), DORA (real-time scheduling/execution), Octos (safety constraints on multi-agent orchestration).
- Industrial control: 5G as the backbone with SparkLink at the last hop; pilots in oilfield data collection, robot handling, robot-arm retrofit; the speaker noted the scale gap.
- Power sector: SIG covering personal-safety protection, distribution operations, switchgear monitoring and line bird-damage prevention; vehicle-grid communication without altering hardware interfaces.
- Chips: MXD2680 (automotive SLE 2.0/Bluetooth dual-mode) passed SLE 2.0 RF and protocol conformance at the lab, volume production at year end (stated); a Zhongke Jingshang SparkLink 2.0 broadband chip announced as coming.
- Testing: three authorised labs; SparkLink-plus-HarmonyOS integrated testing added; variant-change rules to avoid retesting.
- No quantitative performance figures are given in this recap.
