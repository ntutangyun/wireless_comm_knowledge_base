## Summary (EN)

On 22 September 2026 LEWITT launched the **LW6**, a closed-back wireless studio headphone for recording, mixing, mastering and monitoring, and SPARK Microsystems announced that its **LE-UWB** (Low-Energy Ultra-Wideband) wireless chipset provides the headphone's wireless link. SPARK frames this as its **first commercially shipping pro-audio design win** for LE-UWB — the technology had previously appeared in the KB only as a WWC/CES pitch for the SR1120 silicon (2026-05-24) and as a FiRa Consortium membership announcement (2026-07-03), neither of which was an actual shipping product.

The LW6 delivers **uncompressed, bit-perfect audio at under 6 ms latency**, a figure LEWITT and SPARK both position as matching wired-headphone performance without the cable — the headline pitch for studio engineers who need wireless freedom of movement without the latency and compression artifacts of Bluetooth audio codecs. Battery life is rated at up to 21 hours per charge, and the headphones ship at EUR 599 / USD 699, available from the announcement date. LEWITT CEO Roman Perschon frames the pitch as "freedom of movement and freedom from cables with no compression and no compromises in audio quality"; audioXpress's launch article frames the alternative as Bluetooth audio, with its latency and lossy codecs. SPARK CEO Fares Mubarak calls it proof LE-UWB "eliminates the tradeoff between wireless freedom and wired performance."

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 9 月 22 日，LEWITT 推出 **LW6**——一款面向录音、混音、母带处理与监听场景的闭背式无线录音室耳机，SPARK Microsystems 同日宣布其 **LE-UWB**（低能耗超宽带）无线芯片为该耳机提供无线连接。SPARK 将其定位为 LE-UWB **首个实际商用出货的专业音频产品**——此前该技术在本知识库中仅以 WWC/CES 上的 SR1120 芯片推介（2026-05-24）和加入 FiRa 联盟公告（2026-07-03）形式出现，均非实际出货产品。

LW6 提供**无压缩、位精准（bit-perfect）音频，时延低于 6 毫秒**，LEWITT 与 SPARK 均将此定位为在无需连线的情况下媲美有线耳机性能——面向需要无线移动自由、同时避免蓝牙音频编解码器带来的时延与压缩伪影的录音室工程师。电池续航额定单次充电最长 21 小时，耳机定价为 599 欧元／699 美元，自公告发布之日起发售。LEWITT CEO Roman Perschon 将其宣传为"移动自由与摆脱线缆的自由，且无压缩、音质零妥协"；audioXpress 的发布报道将其对比对象描述为蓝牙音频，指出其时延与有损编解码问题。SPARK CEO Fares Mubarak 则称这证明 LE-UWB"消除了无线自由与有线性能之间的取舍"。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Announcement/availability date: 22 September 2026; per SPARK, the first commercially available pro-audio application using its LE-UWB.
- Latency: under 6 ms, uncompressed, "bit-perfect" audio quality.
- Battery life: up to 21 hours per charge.
- Price: EUR 599 / USD 699.
- Wireless layer: SPARK's LE-UWB impulse-radio UWB chipset (pulses ≤5 ns); the specific SPARK part used in the LW6 is not stated. LEWITT brands the link "RealSound."
- Positioning: LEWITT's CEO contrasts it with "legacy connectivity"; audioXpress's launch article frames the alternative as Bluetooth audio, with its latency and lossy codecs, rather than with FiRa/802.15.4z ranging use cases.
- Quotes: Roman Perschon (LEWITT CEO), Fares Mubarak (SPARK CEO).

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The KB's two prior SPARK entries were both forward-looking: the 2026-05-24 entry captured SPARK's SR1120 pitch (40.96 Mbps, sub-200 µs latency, 1 nJ/bit) as a positioning claim at WWC/CES, and the 2026-07-03 entry captured SPARK joining FiRa as an ecosystem-alignment move. This is the first entry documenting a **commercially available product** built on LE-UWB; SPARK says it confirms the technology's potential for high-quality, low-latency wireless audio. It's also notable that the design win landed in pro audio rather than the AI-wearable, presence-detection or gaming uses SPARK emphasised at WWC/CES.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

KB 此前两条 SPARK 相关条目均为前瞻性：2026-05-24 条目记录了 SPARK 在 WWC/CES 上对 SR1120 的定位宣传（40.96 Mbps、亚 200 微秒时延、1 nJ/比特）；2026-07-03 条目记录了 SPARK 加入 FiRa 联盟的生态布局动作。本条目是首条记录基于 LE-UWB 的**商用产品**的条目；SPARK 称这证明了该技术在高品质、低时延无线音频方面的潜力。值得注意的是，此次设计导入落地在专业音频领域，而非 SPARK 在 WWC/CES 上强调的 AI 可穿戴、存在检测或游戏场景。
