## Summary (EN)

Between October 13 and 17, 2026 the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) published three countdown posters on its own news feed giving the programmes for its events at CEIC2026 (Consumer Electronics Innovation Congress) at the Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center (Futian), under the theme "SparkLink AI · Intelligent Integration For All Scenarios" (星闪AI·智合万物). An overview post dated October 12 describes the wider line-up: the industry summit, the finals of the third "SparkLink HarmonyOS Cup" innovation-application challenge (25 teams), an industry capital-matching session, an application forum, and a promotion-group meeting, plus an ecosystem exhibition (October 22-24, nearly 50 companies, 100+ exhibits) covering chip modules, test instruments and dev boards, vertical solutions (power, mining, smart manufacturing) and people-car-home scenarios.

The industry summit (October 22, 13:30-18:30, Plum Blossom Hall, 5F) is organised in an opening plus four chapters. After remarks, a keynote and an alliance summary/outlook, Chapter 1 ("Technology innovation, standards leading") lists a SparkLink standards-and-evaluation release ceremony, a release of "SparkLink globalisation results", a release of an advantage-experience evaluation, and a release of an embodied-intelligence special report. Chapter 2 covers new ecosystem products (SparkLink-scenario product launches and ecosystem results). Chapter 3 covers a "SparkLink + HarmonyOS" industry-ecosystem release, a power-sector special report, a smart-manufacturing special report, and a ceremony establishing a new SIG. Chapter 4 covers an MOU signing, the HarmonyOS Cup finals awards, annual acknowledgements and a wrap-up.

The application forum (October 23, 13:00-17:10 per the poster; sessions start 13:30) has four modules. Module 1 ("AI foundation, embodied-intelligence navigation") opens with a keynote by Wan Lei, the alliance's vice chair and a Huawei Fellow, followed by embodied-intelligence talks from the Institute of Computing Technology (CAS), Dora-RS, Huawei Cloud Computing and Tsinghua University. Module 2 covers industry talks from China Mobile's research institute, State Grid Smart Internet of Vehicles Technology Co. and a national new-energy-storage innovation centre. Module 3 covers smart-terminal, automotive and smart-home application talks from Huawei Terminal, the China Electronic Audio Industry Association and Midea Group. Module 4 has a panel on SparkLink's value and role in the AI era, followed by chip-industry talks from Beijing Zhongke Jingshang, Jiangsu Zhuosheng Micro, Chuangyuan (Suzhou) and the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology. The capital-matching session (October 23, 09:00-11:50, Lotus Hall 2, 5F) consists of an industry introduction by Pingshan District, a standards-and-ecosystem progress update from iSLA, a roundtable on "high-quality wireless connectivity: the key leap for AI agents", project roadshows and judges' comments; the overview post says it is the first such session the alliance has organised and quotes 1,260+ member units and 380 million+ cumulative chip shipments.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 10 月 13 日至 17 日，国际星闪无线短距通信联盟（iSLA）在其官方动态栏目连续发布三张倒计时海报，公布其在 CEIC2026（消费电子创新大会）深圳会展中心（福田）系列活动的议程，主题为"星闪AI·智合万物"（SparkLink AI · Intelligent Integration For All Scenarios）。10 月 12 日的全景前瞻文章介绍了整体安排：产业峰会、第三届"星闪鸿蒙杯"创新应用挑战赛全国总决赛（25 支战队）、星闪产业资本对接会、星闪应用论坛、产业推广组会议，以及 10 月 22-24 日的星闪生态创新展（近 50 家企业、100+ 件展品，涵盖芯片模组、测试仪器与开发板，电力、矿山、智能制造等垂直方案，以及人车家园场景）。

产业峰会（10 月 22 日 13:30-18:30，深圳会展中心福田 5 楼梅花厅）分为序幕和四个篇章。序幕包含领导致辞、主旨报告与联盟总结展望；第一篇"技术创新·标准领航"安排星闪标准与评测发布仪式、星闪全球化成果发布、星闪优势体验测评发布和星闪具身智能专题发布；第二篇为生态新品与生态成果发布；第三篇"千行百业·场景创新"包含"星闪+鸿蒙"产业生态发布、星闪电力专题发布、星闪智能制造专题发布以及星闪新 SIG 成立仪式；第四篇为 MOU 签约、星闪鸿蒙杯总决赛颁奖、年度致谢与总结。

应用论坛（10 月 23 日 13:00-17:10，13:30 起正式议程）分四个模块。模块一"AI 筑基·具身领航"由星闪联盟副理事长、华为 Fellow 万蕾作主旨报告，随后是中国科学院计算技术研究所、Dora-RS、华为云计算技术有限公司和清华大学的具身智能演讲；模块二为中国移动研究院、国网智慧车联网技术有限公司和国家新型储能创新中心的行业应用演讲；模块三为华为终端、中国电子音响行业协会和美的集团的智能终端、汽车、家居创新应用演讲；模块四“巅峰对话·智见未来”先设“星闪在 AI 时代的价值和作用”圆桌对话，随后为芯片产业演讲，嘉宾来自北京中科晶上、江苏卓胜微、创耀（苏州）通信科技和中国信息通信研究院。产业资本对接会（10 月 23 日 09:00-11:50，5 楼荷花 2 厅）包括坪山区产业推介、国际星闪联盟标准成果与产业生态进展、"高质量无线连接：智能体的关键一跃"圆桌研讨、项目路演与评委点评；前瞻文章称这是联盟首次举办该类活动，并提到 1260+ 成员单位与 3.8 亿+ 芯片出货量。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is a pre-event programme, not an outcome: the actual releases, the new SIG's scope and any chipmaker announcements are not yet public. At the CEIC2026 press conference on 22 September (iSLA news item 948), organisers said five major chipmakers would release 'SparkLink 2.0' chips at the event; none of the three posters uses the term '2.0' (the summit's Chapter 2 is titled 'ecosystem foundation · chip product renewal'), so what '2.0' denotes remains unverified. What the agendas do add is the set of verticals the alliance chose to foreground (embodied intelligence, power grid, smart manufacturing) plus a new-SIG establishment ceremony and a capital-matching format (which the alliance itself calls its first). The follow-up is to read the October 22-23 outcome recaps once the alliance posts them.
