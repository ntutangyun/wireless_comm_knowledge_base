## Summary (EN)

Wi-Fi NOW (Claus Hetting, 22 October 2026) reports a new ABI Research study, authored by Principal Analyst Andrew Spivey, on the market for Wi-Fi HaLow, the sub-1 GHz long-range, low-power Wi-Fi variant. According to the article, ABI Research puts the total addressable market (TAM) at nearly 10 billion annual device shipments in 2026, rising to 14.3 billion by 2031. The serviceable addressable market (SAM) is forecast to grow from 27 million devices this year to 297 million in 2031, a compound annual growth rate of about 62% (the article also says "more than 60% annually").

The article names three major segments for the opportunity: IoT applications, smart home devices, and networking equipment. It quotes the report's argument that once Wi-Fi HaLow infrastructure is embedded in an environment for an initial application, the additional cost of enabling further applications is marginal, so ISPs and enterprises will be incentivised to expand it to further applications. ABI Research expects the diversity of HaLow-enabled smart-home devices to increase significantly after 2028, with security cameras, smart doorbells and smart lighting expected to take a substantial share, and adoption could broaden to door locks, garage door sensors and environmental sensors. Smart metering is called another growth opportunity, while industrial automation and HaLow-enabled smartphones and smartphone adaptors are expected to remain comparatively small segments.

The article credits Morse Micro as a principal architect of the HaLow ecosystem and mentions Newracom as a chipmaker that has also contributed. Wi-Fi NOW states that it will post a link to the full ABI Research report when available and announces a 3 November webinar on Wi-Fi HaLow for Europe. The KB entry is based on the Wi-Fi NOW article; the ABI Research report itself was not available.

## Summary (ZH)

Wi-Fi NOW（Claus Hetting，2026 年 10 月 22 日）报道了 ABI Research 首席分析师 Andrew Spivey 撰写的一份关于 Wi-Fi HaLow（Sub-1 GHz 长距离、低功耗 Wi-Fi 变体）市场的新研究。据该文章，ABI Research 估计 2026 年的总可寻址市场（TAM）接近每年 100 亿台设备出货，到 2031 年增至 143 亿台。可服务可寻址市场（SAM）预计从今年的 2700 万台增长到 2031 年的 2.97 亿台，复合年增长率约 62%（文章另有"年增长超过 60%"的表述）。

文章指出机会集中在三大领域：物联网应用、智能家居设备和网络设备。文章引述报告的观点：一旦 Wi-Fi HaLow 基础设施因某个初始应用而部署到某一环境中，启用更多应用的边际成本很低，因此 ISP 与企业会有动力把它扩展到更多应用。ABI Research 预计 2028 年之后支持 HaLow 的智能家居设备种类将显著增加，其中安防摄像头、智能门铃和智能照明预计占较大份额，并可能扩展到门锁、车库门传感器和环境传感器等。智能电表被称为另一个增长机会，而工业自动化以及支持 HaLow 的智能手机与手机适配器预计仍是相对较小的细分市场。

文章称 Morse Micro 是 HaLow 生态的主要构建者之一，并提到芯片厂商 Newracom 也做出了贡献。Wi-Fi NOW 表示一旦完整的 ABI Research 报告可用将发布链接，并预告了 11 月 3 日面向欧洲的 Wi-Fi HaLow 网络研讨会。本条目依据 Wi-Fi NOW 的文章撰写，未能获得 ABI Research 报告原文。
