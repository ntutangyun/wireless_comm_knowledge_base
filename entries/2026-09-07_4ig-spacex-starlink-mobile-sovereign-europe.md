## Summary (EN)
4iG Group (Hungary) and SpaceX announced on 2 September 2026 two agreements signed in Bastrop, Texas by 4iG chairman/CEO Gellért Jászai and SpaceX president/COO Gwynne Shotwell, described as Europe's first combined commercial satellite-to-mobile and sovereign-solutions partnership. The first agreement deploys **Starlink Mobile**, providing direct LTE-compatible connectivity to unmodified mobile phones — "without requiring dedicated satellite equipment" — to complement 4iG's terrestrial mobile networks in areas of limited or no coverage, to launch first in Hungary and Albania; the release describes the technology as improving availability particularly in mountainous areas and coastal waters, and states that 4iG is set to become one of Europe's front-runners in the deployment of "Starlink Mobile services". A follow-on generation, **Starlink Mobile V2**, is planned for Hungary, Albania, Montenegro, and North Macedonia, using SpaceX's global **2GHz priority rights** to enable **5G non-terrestrial network (NTN)** capabilities across those four markets. The second agreement, **Sovereign Solutions**, is scheduled to launch next year (2027) and covers secure satellite networks built for national-security use, delivering broadband connectivity plus "end-to-end secured data communications" infrastructure for sovereign operations.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
This KB's starlink and sat-direct-device bins have tracked Starlink Mobile mostly through US earnings-call disclosures and FCC spectrum filings. This is the first entry documenting a Starlink Mobile *market-launch agreement* with a named European carrier group, and the first to name a concrete spectrum mechanism — SpaceX's "global 2GHz priority rights" — as the basis for a planned 5G NTN upgrade path (Starlink Mobile V2), giving the D2D thread a forward-looking technical detail beyond the current LTE-compatible service. The paired Sovereign Solutions agreement also extends the KB's national-security-satellite-network thread (alongside the UK Open Cosmos TacSys item logged the same week) to a second, differently-structured European sovereign-connectivity commitment — this one from the world's largest direct-to-cell operator rather than a dedicated LEO-IoT specialist.
