## Summary (EN)

ABI Research published "As the Short-Range Wireless Evolution Continues, What Role Does SparkLink/NearLink Have in the Future of Connectivity?" on 28 August 2026 (author Andrew Zignani, Senior Research Director; ABI Insight IN-8238). It opens by placing SparkLink among other short-range developments: Wi-Fi moving to Wi-Fi 7 and 8, with Wi-Fi sensing and HaLow gaining traction; Bluetooth adding features such as ULL HID, HDT and IPv6; and UWB and IEEE 802.15.4ab advancing.

The note says SparkLink was initially developed by Huawei in the aftermath of U.S. sanctions and expulsions from standards bodies in 2019. It describes SparkLink Basic (SLB) as a Wi-Fi alternative with 20 microsecond latency and sub-microsecond synchronisation, and SparkLink Low Energy (SLE) as a low-power option with 125 microsecond latency, 12 Mbps (E1.0) or 16 Mbps (E2.0) throughput and sub-metre ranging, operating mainly at 2.4 GHz. It lists Synchronous Link Positioning (SLP) using UWB for centimetre-level positioning. Its timeline: alliance formed September 2020 and officially registered as the International SparkLink Wireless Short-range Communication Alliance (iSLA) in June 2023; NearLink 1.0 in November 2022, 2.0 in March 2025, and 3.0 started June 2025 and expected 2027. It states membership grew from 80 founding members to over 1,200, that the ITU added the technology to its global wireless access standard in May 2025, and that SparkLink Japan launched in August 2025.

On the market it names HiSilicon as the leading chipset vendor (Kirin A2, Hi3863V100) along with Chengdu Aich, Triductor, Beijing Sylincom, SIGCHIP, Bestechnic and MacroGiga, notes most SLE chips are combined with Wi-Fi or Bluetooth, and says NearLink chipset technology remains mostly restricted to Chinese vendors. It says the device market has been driven primarily by Huawei smart devices, that several vehicle models (AITO, Luxeed, Maextro, Stelato, Shangjie), most relying on HarmonyOS, have integrated NearLink for digital keys, and that cumulative shipments exceed 100 million, nearly all using SLE. Its challenges: competing technologies closing performance gaps; the technology being seen as Huawei- and China-centric, which may deter non-Huawei ecosystem players; an immature standard with few chipmakers and an unfinished roadmap; and a lack of SLB deployments limiting wider reach.

## Summary (ZH)

ABI Research 于2026年8月28日发布《As the Short-Range Wireless Evolution Continues, What Role Does SparkLink/NearLink Have in the Future of Connectivity?》（作者 Andrew Zignani，高级研究总监；ABI Insight 编号 IN-8238）。文章开篇把星闪置于其他短距技术的发展背景中：Wi-Fi 向 Wi-Fi 7 和 8 演进，Wi-Fi 感知与 HaLow 受到关注；蓝牙新增 ULL HID、HDT 和 IPv6 等特性；UWB 与 IEEE 802.15.4ab 也在推进。

文章称星闪最初由华为在2019年遭美国制裁并被标准组织除名之后研发。它把星闪基础接入（SLB）描述为 Wi-Fi 的替代方案，时延20微秒、同步精度亚微秒；把星闪低功耗接入（SLE）描述为低功耗方案，时延125微秒，吞吐 12 Mbps（E1.0）或 16 Mbps（E2.0），具备亚米级测距，主要工作在 2.4 GHz。文中提到同步链路定位（SLP）借助 UWB 实现厘米级定位。时间线：联盟于2020年9月成立，2023年6月正式注册为国际星闪无线短距通信联盟（iSLA）；NearLink 1.0 于2022年11月发布，2.0 于2025年3月发布，3.0 于2025年6月启动、预计2027年完成。文章称会员从80家创始成员增至1,200余家，2025年5月ITU将该技术纳入全球无线接入标准，2025年8月星闪日本成立。

市场方面，文章称海思为领先芯片供应商（Kirin A2、Hi3863V100），其他包括Chengdu Aich、Triductor、Beijing Sylincom、SIGCHIP、Bestechnic 和 MacroGiga；多数 SLE 芯片与 Wi-Fi 或蓝牙组合，星闪芯片技术仍主要局限于中国厂商。文章称终端市场主要由华为智能设备推动，多款车型（AITO、Luxeed、Maextro、Stelato、Shangjie，多数基于鸿蒙）已集成星闪用于数字车钥匙，累计出货量超过1亿，几乎全部使用 SLE。文章提出的挑战包括：竞争技术正在缩小性能差距；该技术被视为以华为和中国为中心，可能让非华为生态参与者却步；标准尚不成熟、芯片厂商少、路线图未完成；缺少 SLB 部署，限制了更广泛的影响力。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **SLB (per ABI)**: 20 microsecond latency, sub-microsecond synchronisation; Wi-Fi alternative targeting phones, PCs, XR, robotics and industrial uses.
- **SLE (per ABI)**: 125 microsecond latency; 12 Mbps (E1.0) or 16 Mbps (E2.0); sub-metre ranging; mainly 2.4 GHz; audio, wearables, keys, sensors and healthcare.
- **Standards timeline (per ABI)**: 1.0 November 2022; 2.0 March 2025 (audio/video, HID, positioning, digital key); 3.0 started June 2025, expected 2027 (positioning, sensing, IoT, UWB/passive applications).
- **Chipsets (per ABI)**: HiSilicon leads; most SLE chips combined with Wi-Fi or Bluetooth; chipset vendors mostly Chinese.
- **Shipments (per ABI)**: cumulative above 100 million, nearly all SLE.
