## Summary (EN)

Adnan Aijaz (arXiv v1, 1 Sep 2026; accepted for IEEE Next Generation Communications, NextGCom 2026) poses the paper's central question directly: "will there be a 7G, and if so, what would justify it?" The stated motivation is to resist treating a seventh mobile generation as an inevitable successor to 6G by default because six generations came before it, and instead ask whether any future capability set represents a genuine system-level discontinuity that cannot be absorbed into 6G-Advanced, WiFi, private networks or satellite systems.

The paper first establishes a 6G baseline — grounded in the ITU-R IMT-2030 framework and 3GPP's Release 21 timeline for the first 6G specifications, expected to freeze in the 2027-2029 window — and then proposes a six-dimension readiness framework for judging any 7G claim: (1) demand-led need, requiring evidence that stakeholders actually require capabilities unavailable from 6G evolution or adjacent systems, not just researcher aspiration; (2) system-level discontinuity, requiring new architectures, PHY assumptions, security models or interfaces rather than incremental enhancement; (3) coordination value, asking whether a new generation label better synchronizes investment cycles, device roadmaps, spectrum policy and security baselines than continuous releases would; (4) sustainability and circularity, evaluated on absolute energy consumption, embodied carbon and circular procurement rather than efficiency-per-bit alone; (5) trust and quantum-era security, distinguishing post-quantum cryptographic migration (treated as 6G's task) from deeper quantum-enabled interworking (a candidate 7G domain); and (6) geopolitical viability, requiring that standards survive divergent regional spectrum policy, AI governance and supply-chain-security requirements.

Against that framework, the paper examines seven candidate areas where a future generation might differentiate itself: agentic network operation (autonomous agents handling planning, policy and spectrum negotiation, requiring new intent interfaces and safety guardrails); RF-native computing (RF front-ends performing analog inference rather than only transporting data); quantum-enabled interworking (QKD, quantum repeaters and sovereign quantum infrastructure integration, beyond post-quantum cryptography); policy-aware spectrum governance (dynamic sharing across fragmented bands via real-time governance APIs, rather than seeking new clean spectrum); grid-interactive infrastructure (base stations, batteries, renewables and edge workloads coordinated as flexible energy assets through standardized interfaces); outcome-assured services (moving service models beyond connectivity/slices toward measurable task completion, deadline compliance and productivity metrics); and regionalized standards (deliberately accommodating divergent regional spectrum, AI-governance and supply-chain requirements rather than one uniform global standard). The paper also discusses business-model and stakeholder implications, and closes with a standardization-gaps table mapping each of the seven candidate areas to the standards work and technical artifacts it would require.

The paper's conclusion is conditional rather than declarative: a 7G deserves to exist only if it becomes a coordination framework for a genuinely wider post-6G fabric spanning radio, sensing, compute, trust, energy, automation and policy; otherwise, the capabilities it might contain should simply be absorbed into 6G-Advanced or adjacent ecosystems.

## Summary (ZH)

Adnan Aijaz（arXiv v1，2026年9月1日；已被IEEE Next Generation Communications，NextGCom 2026 接受）在论文中直接提出核心问题："会有7G吗？如果有，其正当性何在？"论文表明的动机是：反对将第七代移动通信标准视为6G之后仅因为已经有六代在先就理所当然到来的下一代，转而追问未来的能力集合是否真正构成6G-Advanced、WiFi、专网或卫星系统所无法吸纳的系统级断层。

论文首先确立6G基线——立足于ITU-R IMT-2030框架，以及3GPP Release 21关于首批6G规范、预计在2027至2029年间冻结的时间表——随后提出一个六维就绪度框架，用以评判任何关于7G的主张：(1)需求驱动的必要性，要求有证据表明利益相关方确实需要6G演进或相邻系统所无法提供的能力，而非仅是研究者的愿景；(2)系统级断层，要求出现新的架构、物理层假设、安全模型或接口，而非渐进式增强；(3)协调价值，即新一代标签是否比持续演进的版本更能同步投资周期、终端路线图、频谱政策与安全基线；(4)可持续性与循环性，以绝对能耗、隐含碳排放与循环采购为评判标准，而非单纯的单比特能效；(5)信任与量子时代安全，将后量子密码迁移（视为6G的任务）与更深层的量子赋能互联（作为7G的候选领域）加以区分；以及(6)地缘政治可行性，要求标准能够在不同地区分化的频谱政策、AI治理与供应链安全要求下存续。

在该框架下，论文考察了未来一代可能借以实现差异化的七个候选领域：智能体化网络运营（由自主智能体处理规划、策略与频谱协商，需要新的意图接口与安全护栏）；RF 原生计算（射频前端执行模拟推理而不仅是承载数据传输）；量子使能互通（量子密钥分发、量子中继器与主权量子基础设施集成，超越后量子密码本身）；政策感知的频谱治理（通过实时治理API在碎片化频段上实现策略感知的动态共享，而非寻求新的洁净频谱）；电网互动型基础设施（基站、电池、可再生能源与边缘工作负载通过标准化接口作为灵活能源资产被协同调度）；结果保障型服务（服务模式从连接/切片进一步转向可测量的任务完成度、截止时限合规性与生产力指标）；以及区域化标准（有意兼容不同地区分化的频谱政策、AI治理与供应链安全要求，而非单一的全球统一标准）。论文同时讨论了商业模式与利益相关方影响，并以一张标准化缺口表收尾，将上述七个候选领域分别映射到其各自所需的标准化工作与技术产物。

论文的结论是有条件的，而非断言式的：唯有当7G真正成为一个覆盖无线、感知、算力、信任、能源、自动化与政策的、更广泛的后6G架构的协调框架时，它才有存在的正当性；否则，其可能包含的能力理应直接被6G-Advanced或相邻生态系统所吸纳。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
This knowledge base's 6g-vision coverage includes several capability-specific position papers (e.g. Soldani's guaranteed-digital-services framing, 2026-07-28) and technical-mechanism entries, but none so far has stepped back to ask the standardization-process question of whether a next mobile generation is even justified, or proposed a reusable readiness framework for answering that question. It is the first entry here that treats "should there be a next generation" as the object of study rather than assuming the answer and evaluating a specific technology within it.
