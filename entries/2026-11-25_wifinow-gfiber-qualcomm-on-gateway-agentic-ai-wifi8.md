## Summary (EN)
A Wi-Fi NOW article dated 10 November 2026 (by Claus Hetting) reports that gFiber (formerly Google Fiber) and Qualcomm are teaming up on what the article calls one of the world's first commercial deployments of an AI-native residential gateway. The gateway is built on Qualcomm's Dragonwing NPro A8 Elite platform, supports 20 Gbps fibre service, is Wi-Fi 8-capable, and is scheduled to roll out in the first half of 2027. Subscribers will be able to query the system in natural language; the article's examples are activating parental controls ("block my kids' traffic starting from 9 pm"), setting up guest Wi-Fi access, and seeing which traffic types (such as YouTube or Instagram) are in use together with usage patterns. Inference runs on the SoC's integrated NPU rather than in the cloud.

Quoted in the article, André Rosete (CPE Architect, gFiber) says the team wanted to use the chipset's native AI capability to keep processing local and offer subscribers simpler controls first, with better network optimisation to follow later. On Wi-Fi 8 he says it resolves some issues gFiber has had with its mesh extender: with Wi-Fi 7 there are limitations in airtime sharing between the extender and the clients, and Wi-Fi 8 addresses much of that through built-in bandwidth-sharing features. He says all Wi-Fi 8 features, including improved roaming, will be active on the gateway. The article adds that gFiber will likely be one of the first operators to deploy a live Wi-Fi 8 network next year, ahead of the Wi-Fi Alliance's formal Wi-Fi 8 certification programme, which it says is due to start in early 2028.

The article's own commentary says edge AI in a gateway could bring privacy benefits, more responsive services and potentially lower cloud-inference and customer-support costs, but that it is "still early days" and it is "not clear what new upsell or revenue opportunities exist". It also remarks that connectivity chipset vendors such as Qualcomm are incorporating dedicated NPUs into their platforms, and that centralised and edge AI architectures will likely coexist.

## Key technical points (EN)
- Platform: Qualcomm Dragonwing NPro A8 Elite SoC with integrated NPU; gateway for 20 Gbps fibre service; Wi-Fi 8-capable; roll-out scheduled for H1 2027.
- Functions named: natural-language control (parental controls, guest access), traffic-type identification and visibility into usage and usage patterns; inference is local on the gateway.
- Stated design intent (gFiber): keep processing local, start with simpler subscriber controls and expand to better network optimisation later.
- Wi-Fi 8 rationale (gFiber): airtime-sharing limits between mesh extender and clients under Wi-Fi 7 are addressed by Wi-Fi 8 bandwidth-sharing features; all Wi-Fi 8 features including improved roaming to be activated.
- Timing statements in the article: a live Wi-Fi 8 network from gFiber next year, ahead of the Wi-Fi Alliance Wi-Fi 8 certification programme expected in early 2028.
