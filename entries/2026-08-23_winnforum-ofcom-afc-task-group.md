## Summary (EN)
The Wireless Innovation Forum (WinnForum) announced on 18 August 2026 the formation of an Ofcom AFC Task Group — a collaborative industry body dedicated to supporting the implementation of Automated Frequency Coordination (AFC) systems for standard-power operation in the UK's 6 GHz band. The group brings together AFC providers, device manufacturers, network operators, technology suppliers, regulators and industry experts to develop a common framework aligned with Ofcom's recently published AFC requirements, covering interference-protected spectrum access across the full 5925–7125 MHz band.

The concrete regulatory timeline behind it: in July 2026 Ofcom published its decision on the registration conditions and process for AFC service providers (and the licence-exempt conditions for standard-power devices), and Ofcom intends to invite applications from prospective AFC service providers from 1 September 2026. The task group launch is the industry-side mobilization to be ready for that window.

WinnForum is the natural venue — it played the equivalent role for the US 6 GHz AFC ecosystem (test vectors, system interoperability specs, certification support for the FCC's AFC framework), so its UK task group signals that the operational machinery behind Ofcom's full-band decision (adopted 21 July 2026; see 2026-07-22_uk-ofcom-full-band-6ghz-adoption) is now being built rather than merely announced.

## Summary (ZH)
无线创新论坛（WinnForum）于 2026 年 8 月 18 日宣布成立 Ofcom AFC 任务组——一个致力于支持英国 6 GHz 频段标准功率运行的自动频率协调（AFC）系统落地的行业协作机构。该任务组汇集 AFC 提供商、设备厂商、网络运营商、技术供应商、监管机构与行业专家，围绕 Ofcom 最新发布的 AFC 要求制定通用框架，覆盖 5925–7125 MHz 全频段的抗干扰频谱接入。

其背后的具体监管时间线：2026 年 7 月，Ofcom 发布了 AFC 服务提供商注册条件与流程（以及标准功率设备的免许可条件）的决定，并计划自 2026 年 9 月 1 日起邀请潜在 AFC 服务提供商提交申请。任务组的成立正是行业侧为该窗口做准备的动员。

WinnForum 是顺理成章的载体——它曾为美国 6 GHz AFC 生态承担同等角色（测试向量、系统互操作规范、FCC AFC 框架的认证支持），因此其英国任务组表明：Ofcom 全频段决定（2026 年 7 月 21 日通过；见 2026-07-22_uk-ofcom-full-band-6ghz-adoption）背后的运行机制已进入实际搭建阶段，而非停留在宣布层面。

## Key technical points (EN)
- Ofcom AFC Task Group launched 18 August 2026 under the Wireless Innovation Forum; membership spans AFC providers, device makers, operators, technology suppliers, regulators and experts.
- Goal: a common industry framework supporting Ofcom's published AFC requirements for standard-power 6 GHz operation across the full 5925–7125 MHz band.
- Ofcom published its AFC service-provider registration conditions/process decision in July 2026 and will invite AFC provider applications from 1 September 2026.
- AFC function: automatically determines spectrum availability and permitted power for standard-power devices from device location and parameters plus the protected-incumbent database — enabling higher-power indoor and outdoor Wi-Fi while protecting incumbents.
- Mirrors WinnForum's role in the US FCC AFC ecosystem (interoperability specs, test vectors, certification support).

- 对标 WinnForum 在美国 FCC AFC 生态中的角色（互操作规范、测试向量、认证支持）。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The KB recorded the UK's full-band 6 GHz adoption on 2026-07-22 (2026-07-22_uk-ofcom-full-band-6ghz-adoption); this entry records the first operational follow-through: a dated application window for AFC operators (1 September 2026) and the industry coordination body that will standardize how UK AFC systems interoperate. For the spectrum-policy thread, the UK is now on the same institutional trajectory the US followed (regulation → WinnForum framework → certified AFC operators → standard-power devices), and also connects to the South Africa OpenAFC datapoint (2026-07-10_south-africa-standard-power-6ghz-afc) — AFC is consolidating as the global template for standard-power 6 GHz Wi-Fi.
