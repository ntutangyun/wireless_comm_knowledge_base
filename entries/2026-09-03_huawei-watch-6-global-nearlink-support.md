## Summary (EN)

Huawei unveiled the Watch 6 and Watch 6 Pro at IFA 2026 in Berlin on 2 September 2026 (per Tech Advisor's same-day coverage, https://www.techadvisor.com/article/3225480/huawei-watch-6-official-as-pixel-watch-5-rival.html; UK availability from 23 September at £399.99 for the Watch 6 and £599.99 for the Watch 6 Pro). The company's **global (en) specification pages** for the two models — Watch 6 Pro at https://consumer.huawei.com/en/wearables/watch-6-pro/specs/ and Watch 6 at https://consumer.huawei.com/en/wearables/watch-6/specs/ — list the following wireless interfaces: **"NearLink – Supported"**, **"BT5.2 supported"**, Wi-Fi **"2.4 GHz + 5 GHz"**, **"NFC – Supported"**, and multi-constellation GNSS (GPS L1+L5 dual-band, GLONASS, BeiDou B1I+B1C+B2a tri-band, Galileo E1+E5a, QZSS L1+L5). Phone compatibility is stated as Android 9.0 or later and iOS 13.0 or later. The Watch 6 is offered in 42 mm (White, Gold, Black) and 46 mm (Blue, Black) variants; the Watch 6 Pro is a separate size line — 43 mm (White, Ceramic Edition) and 46 mm (Brown, Silver, Space Edition). Tech Advisor also reports eSIM support "for connectivity without a companion phone".

This entry records what the spec sheets say and no more: the pages do not state which NearLink profile (SLE or SLB) is implemented, which peripherals or phones the watch's NearLink link is used with, or whether NearLink behaves differently by region. A widely shared social post from a HarmonyOS enthusiast account claims this is the first Huawei watch to ship NearLink "out of the box" globally; that claim is the poster's, not Huawei's, and is not verified here.
