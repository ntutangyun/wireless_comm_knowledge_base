## Summary (EN)

D-Link announced on 19 August 2026 an expansion of its DAP-E3620 line into a three-model family of BE3600-class dual-band Wi-Fi 7 enterprise access points: the ceiling-mount DAP-E3620, the wall-plate DAP-E3620W, and the outdoor-rated DAP-E3620OU. All three carry the same radio configuration and the same 802.11be feature set — Multi-Link Operation, 4K-QAM and Multi-RU — and are positioned for hospitality, education, general enterprise and multi-dwelling-unit deployments.

The notable detail is the class rather than the features: BE3600 is a **dual-band** part, covering 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz only, with no 6 GHz radio. That places these APs in the cost-optimised tier of the Wi-Fi 7 enterprise market, where the buyer gets the 802.11be MAC/PHY improvements — MLO across 2.4/5 GHz for link redundancy and failover, 4K-QAM for peak-rate headroom at high SNR, Multi-RU for finer OFDMA resource assignment — without paying for a 6 GHz radio, an AFC dependency, or the client-side 6 GHz support that much of the installed base still lacks.

Management is through D-Link's Nuclias stack in both deployment shapes: an on-premises hardware or software controller, or Nuclias Unity cloud management. The release pairs the APs with D-Link's DMS-1250 series 2.5 Gbps multi-gigabit PoE switches (10-, 12-, 18- and 28-port variants: DMS-1250-10SP, DMS-1250-12TP, DMS-1250-18P, DMS-1250-28P) to give a single-vendor uplink path that does not bottleneck the wireless side at 1 GbE. Fast roaming, enterprise authentication and network segmentation are cited but not quantified.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The `wifi7-deployment` bin already tracks the premium end of the Wi-Fi 7 enterprise wave — `2026-07-09_zyxel-outdoor-wifi7-wbe665s` for outdoor, `2026-08-03_extreme-multibeam-matsing-launch` for high-density venues. This entry marks a different movement in the same market: the arrival of a **dual-band, 6 GHz-less** BE3600 tier as a distinct enterprise product category rather than a consumer compromise, offered simultaneously in indoor, wall-plate and outdoor form factors from one radio design.

That matters for how the 802.11be feature set actually reaches deployment. MLO is usually discussed as a 5+6 GHz story; on a BE3600 part it becomes a 2.4+5 GHz story, where the second link is a slow, congested, long-range band rather than a fast clean one — so MLO here buys reliability and seamless failover far more than it buys throughput. Similarly, 4K-QAM on 5 GHz in a hospitality or MDU environment will rarely be reachable at the SNRs those deployments see. The practical value concentrates in Multi-RU and MLO robustness, which is a different value proposition from the 6 GHz-centric Wi-Fi 7 marketing.

The 2.5 GbE PoE pairing is the other signal worth noting: even in the 6 GHz-less tier, vendors now assume 1 GbE uplink is the bottleneck and sell the switch refresh alongside the AP refresh. As a routine product launch this is low-novelty on its own, but it is a useful datapoint on where the volume of Wi-Fi 7 enterprise shipments is actually landing while Wi-Fi 8 silicon dominates the headlines.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

`wifi7-deployment` 分区已在跟踪 Wi-Fi 7 企业浪潮的高端一侧——室外有 `2026-07-09_zyxel-outdoor-wifi7-wbe665s`，高密场馆有 `2026-08-03_extreme-multibeam-matsing-launch`。本条目标记的是同一市场中的另一股动向：**双频、无 6 GHz** 的 BE3600 档位作为一个独立的企业产品类别出现，而非消费级的妥协产物，并以同一射频设计同时提供室内、面板与室外三种形态。

这对 802.11be 特性集如何真正落地部署很关键。MLO 通常被当作 5+6 GHz 的故事；在 BE3600 器件上它变成 2.4+5 GHz 的故事，其中第二条链路是慢速、拥挤、远距离的频段而非高速干净的频段——因此此处的 MLO 买到的是可靠性与无缝故障切换，远多于吞吐。同理，酒店或 MDU 环境中 5 GHz 上的 4K-QAM 在实际信噪比下很少能真正用上。实用价值集中在 Multi-RU 与 MLO 的鲁棒性，这与以 6 GHz 为中心的 Wi-Fi 7 营销叙事是不同的价值主张。

2.5 GbE PoE 配套是另一个值得注意的信号：即便在无 6 GHz 的档位，厂商现在也默认 1 GbE 上行是瓶颈，并把交换机换代与 AP 换代一起售卖。作为一次常规产品发布，它本身新意有限，但在 Wi-Fi 8 芯片占据头条之际，它是关于 Wi-Fi 7 企业出货量究竟落在哪个档位的有用数据点。
