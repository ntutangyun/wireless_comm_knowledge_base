## Summary (EN)

AST SpaceMobile disclosed in a July 15, 2026 regulatory filing (8-K) that **commercial direct-to-device service slips from late 2026 to early 2027**. The trigger chain: a late-June 2026 Blue Origin **New Glenn upper-stage explosion during a ground test** sidelined the heavy-lift vehicle that was to loft the 45 Block-2 BlueBird satellites needed for initial continuous coverage — compounding the earlier wrong-orbit New Glenn deployment already logged in the KB's Q1-readout entry (~$155–160M write-off).

The company's mitigation is a **strategic pivot to SpaceX Falcon 9** for intermediate batches: BlueBirds 11–13 have completed fabrication and are manifested on a Falcon 9 in the first half of August 2026. AST also announced plans to raise **up to $1 billion in new financing** alongside the revised schedule. Commentary (NextBigFuture) notes early service will be *intermittent* D2D coverage, with continuous service dependent on constellation build-out through 2027–2028.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Commercial D2D launch: late 2026 → early 2027 (8-K filed July 15, 2026).
- Cause: New Glenn upper-stage ground-test explosion (late June 2026) on top of the earlier wrong-orbit deployment.
- Mitigation: Falcon 9 pivot; BlueBirds 11–13 fabricated, launching 1H August 2026.
- Financing: up to $1B new raise disclosed with the schedule change.
- Early service will be intermittent; continuous coverage still requires the 45-satellite Block-2 build-out.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Updates the KB's AST thread (Block-2 first launch, Q1-2026 readout with the 45-satellite year-end target, FCC full-constellation clearance): the year-end-2026 commercial target that survived the wrong-orbit incident has now formally slipped, and AST becomes launch-dependent on its D2D rival's rocket (Falcon 9). Combined with Amazon's Globalstar consolidation (2026-07-22_amazon-globalstar-acquisition-apple-satellite), the D2D field's competitive timing just re-shuffled: Starlink's T-Mobile service operational, AST early-2027 intermittent, Amazon next-gen D2D 2028.
