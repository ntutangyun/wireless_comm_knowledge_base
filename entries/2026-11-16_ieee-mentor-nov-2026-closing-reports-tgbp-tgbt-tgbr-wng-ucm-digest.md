## Summary (EN)

Five closing reports uploaded on 11-12 November 2026 give the status of task groups and committees at the end of the Bangkok plenary. This digest follows the documents' own content; the groups' earlier history is not assessed here.

TGbp (Ambient Power, Chair Bo Sun, Sanechips; [DCN 11-26-2293r1](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2293&is_year=2026)) held two meetings, discussed six technical contributions and performed a demo of potential 11bp applications. It states that the initial WG ballot for D1.0 (LB298) closes on 30 November and that four teleconferences (1, 8 and 15 December and 5 January) will process the collected comments. The timeline lists D2.0 WG recirculation in March 2027, SA-ballot pool formation 1-31 March 2027, initial SA ballot (D4.0) in August 2027, final WG approval in January 2028 and RevCom/SASB approval in May 2028. TGbt (Stephen Orr, Cisco; [DCN 11-26-2358r1](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2358&is_year=2026)) met four times, continued LB297 comment resolution (32 CIDs resolved this week), ran three motions and handled the WBA/Wi-Fi liaison; five teleconferences are planned between 30 November and 17 December and five slots requested for the January interim; its timeline lists a D2.0 letter ballot in January 2027, D3.0 in May 2027, initial SA ballot (D4.0) in September 2027 and RevCom/SASB approval in September 2028.

TGbr (Enhanced Light Communications, Nikola Serafimovski, FTH; [DCN 11-26-2353r0](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2353&is_year=2026)) considered comments against D0.1; D0.2 is to be available in the 802.11 Members Area by 10 December, and the key technical issues named for the January and March 2027 meetings are capability exchange, common mode and MLO. Its timeline lists completing MAC work (D0.2) in January 2027, starting WG comment collection in July 2027, a D1.0 WG letter ballot in November 2027, SA ballot from May 2028 and RevCom submission in November 2028. The WNG SC (Chair Jim Lansford, DeepSig; [DCN 11-26-2352r0](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2352&is_year=2026)) lists its November presentations: evaluation of DFS in the presence of emulated solid-state weather radar signals (Carleton University), "WLAN Sensing 2" (Huawei), an ns-3-ai update on multi-agent workflow and tutorial (University of Washington), insider attack defenses (KU Leuven), enhanced channel estimation in MIMO networks with multi-band fusion (Northeastern University), and a proposal for IEEE 802.11 as an IMT-2030 candidate technology (HPE); it plans a call for presentations before January, with no motions or straw polls in the SC. The UCM TIG (Volker Jungnickel, Fraunhofer HHI; [DCN 11-26-2361r1](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2361&is_year=2026)) reports unified text related to the 3GPP channel model and added performance-evaluation text; it resolved 3/3 general, 64/98 technical (21 awaiting further input) and 83/83 editorial comments, 124 of 183 in total (about 67 percent), passed a motion to update the draft report with the resolved comments, and noted that efficient ray-tracing tools should be made available as open source for research and standardization. The report is planned to be available for WG comment collection in mid-December, with teleconferences on 3 and 16 December.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 11 月 11-12 日上传的五份闭幕报告给出了曼谷全会结束时各任务组与委员会的状态。本摘要仅依据各文档自身内容，不评判这些小组此前的历史。

TGbp（环境供电，主席 Bo Sun，Sanechips；[DCN 11-26-2293r1](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2293&is_year=2026)）开了两次会议，讨论 6 份技术贡献，并演示了 11bp 潜在应用。报告称 D1.0 的首轮 WG 投票（LB298）于 11 月 30 日截止，并将在 12 月 1、8、15 日及 1 月 5 日召开四次电话会议处理收集到的意见；时间线为 2027 年 3 月 D2.0 WG 再循环、2027 年 3 月 1-31 日组建 SA 投票池、2027 年 8 月首轮 SA 投票（D4.0）、2028 年 1 月 WG 最终批准、2028 年 5 月 RevCom/SASB 批准。TGbt（Stephen Orr，思科；[DCN 11-26-2358r1](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2358&is_year=2026)）开了四次会议，继续处理 LB297 意见（本周解决 32 个 CID），进行三项动议，并处理 WBA/Wi-Fi 联络；11 月 30 日至 12 月 17 日计划五次电话会议，1 月临时会议申请五个时段；时间线为 2027 年 1 月 D2.0 LB、2027 年 5 月 D3.0、2027 年 9 月首轮 SA 投票（D4.0）、2028 年 9 月 RevCom/SASB 批准。

TGbr（增强光通信，Nikola Serafimovski，FTH；[DCN 11-26-2353r0](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2353&is_year=2026)）审议了针对 D0.1 的意见；D0.2 预计 12 月 10 日前在 802.11 会员区提供，2027 年 1 月与 3 月会议要解决的关键技术问题为能力交换、公共模式（common mode）与 MLO。其时间线为 2027 年 1 月完成 MAC 部分（D0.2）、2027 年 7 月开始 WG 意见征集、2027 年 11 月 D1.0 WG Letter Ballot、2028 年 5 月起 SA 投票、2028 年 11 月提交 RevCom。WNG SC（主席 Jim Lansford，DeepSig；[DCN 11-26-2352r0](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2352&is_year=2026)）列出本月报告：模拟固态气象雷达信号下的 DFS 评估（卡尔顿大学）、"WLAN Sensing 2"（华为）、ns-3-ai 多智能体工作流与教程更新（华盛顿大学）、内部攻击防御（鲁汶大学）、多频带融合的 MIMO 增强信道估计（东北大学）、以及把 IEEE 802.11 作为 IMT-2030 候选技术的提案（HPE）；计划 1 月前征集报告，SC 内不设动议和 Straw Poll。UCM TIG（Volker Jungnickel，弗劳恩霍夫 HHI；[DCN 11-26-2361r1](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2361&is_year=2026)）报告了与 3GPP 信道模型相关的统一文本和新增的性能评估文本；已解决 3/3 条一般意见、64/98 条技术意见（21 条待进一步输入）和 83/83 条编辑意见，合计 183 条中的 124 条（约 67%），通过了用已解决意见更新草案报告的动议，并指出应以开源方式提供高效的射线追踪工具以支持研究与标准化。报告计划 12 月中旬供 WG 征集意见，12 月 3 日与 16 日举行电话会议。
