## Summary (EN)

SatNews (29 October 2026, Nick David) reports that Space42, Viasat and Terrestar Solutions signed a memorandum of understanding on an open, standards-based interoperability framework linking Equatys, the D2D venture co-founded by Space42 and Viasat, with CaStar, Terrestar's planned Canadian LEO constellation for the country's far north. Equatys follows a "sovereign shell" model pairing a global commercial system with constellations built for specific countries or regions.

Terrestar's stated contributions are 40 MHz of mobile-satellite spectrum, a stake in the EchoStar T1 satellite that carries its Canadian service, and the CaStar constellation, which is still under construction. The article quotes Terrestar's Jason Trembley (VP commercial solutions) on why a national constellation cannot go alone.

## Summary (ZH)

Terrestar 声明的贡献包括 40 MHz 移动卫星频谱、承载其加拿大服务的 EchoStar T1 卫星的权益，以及仍在建设中的 CaStar 星座。文章引述 Terrestar 的 Jason Trembley（商业解决方案副总裁）解释国家星座为何无法单独运营。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Economics quoted**: a Canadian constellation spends about 2% of its time over Canada; Terrestar's Jason Trembley likened it to "renting out an apartment and it only being available 2% of the time."
- **Spectrum**: Terrestar's 40 MHz sits on the Canadian side of the same band SpaceX has agreed to buy from EchoStar; the article does not name the band.
- **Context**: Iridium CTO Greg Pelton, paraphrased in the article from the same Silicon Valley Space Week panel, said competing at scale needs launch, manufacturing, spectrum and a high-value service. Terrestar says it can partner with other networks at the same time.
- No technical interface specification is given for the interoperability framework. Single-outlet report.
