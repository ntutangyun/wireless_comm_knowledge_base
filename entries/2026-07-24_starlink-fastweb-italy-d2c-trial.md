## Summary (EN)

On July 16, 2026, Fastweb+Vodafone (the merged Italian operator) and SpaceX's Starlink announced Italy's first direct-to-cell trial. The pilot runs in the Apennine mountains of central Italy — chosen precisely because terrestrial coverage is thin — and is restricted to industry professionals: a maximum of 3,000 participants, authorized until September 30, 2026. Standard, unmodified 4G smartphones connect directly to Starlink's direct-to-cell LEO satellites.

Notably, the trial includes data services from the start — WhatsApp and Google Maps are cited alongside SMS/MMS — rather than following the SMS-first sequencing most national D2C launches have used. Commercial launch is explicitly deferred until after the trial concludes and regulators approve; Fastweb+Vodafone frames the service as rural coverage-fill, not a terrestrial substitute.
