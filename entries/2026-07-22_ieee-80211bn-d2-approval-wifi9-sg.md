## Summary (EN)

At the July 12–17, 2026 IEEE 802 plenary in Montreal, the 802.11 working group approved **802.11bn Draft 2.0** — the milestone the task group's timeline had predicted for July 2026. TGbn ran ten sessions during the week and resolved on the order of 1,200 comments from comment collection CC50 en route to the new draft. Industry coverage (Wi-Fi NOW, July 20) frames the approval as clearing the way for faster Wi-Fi 8 product development, with "only minor remaining standard changes expected" before the ratification target of 2028. D2.0 now proceeds to its working-group letter ballot, following D1.0's bruising 61%-approve LB291 (August–October 2025) and two heavy comment-resolution cycles (~1,800 comments in March, ~670 in May).

In the same week the working group **established a study group for the next generation beyond 802.11bn** — informally "Wi-Fi 9" — with a stated focus on throughput, latency predictability, and adaptation to emerging application requirements such as AI-generated traffic. This formalizes the post-UHR exploratory work that had been circulating in WNG SC through the spring, and lands alongside the separately-tracked draft P802.11bu PAR for AI offload (DCN 11-26/1477, uploaded on the plenary's closing day).

The official ieee802.org status pages lag the event: tgbn_update.htm still shows D1.5/Antwerp state, and the LetterBallots page lists nothing newer than LB294. The Session 218 report (11-26-1140-02) is posted on the Reports page; the ballot announcement for D2.0 should appear in the coming weeks.

## Summary (ZH)

ieee802.org 官方状态页面滞后于该事件：tgbn_update.htm 仍显示 D1.5/安特卫普状态，LetterBallots 页面最新仍为 LB294。Session 218 报告（11-26-1140-02）已发布在 Reports 页面；D2.0 的投票公告预计数周内出现。

## Key technical points (EN)

- 802.11bn Draft 2.0 approved during the July 2026 Montreal plenary (Session 218, July 12–17), on schedule with the TGbn timeline's "D2.0 WG Letter Ballot: July 2026" prediction.
- ~1,200 CC50 comments resolved across ten TGbn sessions during the plenary week.
- Prior ballot history: D1.0 initial letter ballot LB291 passed at 61% approve (308/198/8) — the two comment-resolution cycles since (~1,800 + ~670 comments) fed D1.5 and now D2.0.
- New study group established for post-802.11bn work ("Wi-Fi 9"): throughput, latency predictability, and AI-traffic adaptation named as focus areas.
- Remaining TGbn milestones per the published timeline: D3.0 letter ballot January 2027, final WG approval March 2028, IEEE completion May 2028.
- Official status pages (tgbn_update.htm, LetterBallots.shtml) not yet updated as of July 22; Session 218 report posted as DCN 11-26-1140-02.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the single most-watched standards event of the summer for this KB: the D2.0 approval converts months of "expected July" status-entries (see 2026-07-17_ieee-80211bi-conditional-approval-milestone for the parallel TGbi milestone the same week) into a confirmed fact, and resets the Wi-Fi 8 drumbeat toward the D3.0/January-2027 checkpoint. The Wi-Fi 9 study group formation is the more forward-looking signal: together with the P802.11bu draft PAR (2026-07-19_ieee-p802-11bu-draft-par-ai-offload), it shows the working group formally splitting "next-gen throughput/latency" and "AI offload" into separate post-bn tracks, with AI traffic named in both scopes.
