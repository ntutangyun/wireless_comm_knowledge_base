## Summary (EN)

SatNews reports that on Monday 19 October 2026 Deutsche Telekom, Orange, Telefónica and Vodafone announced a Letter of Intent (LOI) to explore a joint bid for upcoming Mobile Satellite Services (MSS) spectrum across Europe. The target is the upcoming European allocation of 2 GHz MSS frequencies.

The four operators plan a cross-border, operator-led framework for direct-to-device (D2D) satellite connectivity. The stated aim is a unified, European-governed non-terrestrial network (NTN) layer serving commercial mobile subscribers and emergency services without fragmenting national markets, with open, non-discriminatory wholesale access for all mobile network operators in Europe. The stated aim also includes countering single-vendor mega-constellations, and the article describes the initiative as complementary to ESA's multi-orbit IRIS² constellation. The operators say a joint bid will not replace their existing commercial satellite arrangements with NTN partners.

On architecture, the consortium will not build its own constellation: it will select an independent satellite operator for the space segment and invest in ground-segment routing nodes, core network interfaces and European data-monitoring facilities, using standardized 3GPP NTN protocols so standard smartphones can connect directly in remote areas. Over the coming months the operators will formalize operational specifications, engage regulators and run a competitive evaluation to choose a satellite partner ahead of the European Commission's spectrum allocation process.

The article does not state the LOI's terms or binding status, financial figures, satellite counts, the allocation timeline, the selected satellite partner or executive quotes. A separate SatNews article of 19 October reports that the U.S. Ambassador to the EU, Andrew Puzder, told the Financial Times (interview published 18 October) that Brussels would face "severe American retaliation" if it restricts upcoming satellite spectrum exclusively to European operators; per that article, the European Commission proposes reserving portions of the 2 GHz band for D2D connectivity and is preparing formal allocation guidelines.

## Summary (ZH)

SatNews 报道，2026 年 10 月 19 日（周一），德国电信、Orange、Telefónica 和沃达丰宣布签署意向书（LOI），探讨联合竞标欧洲即将分配的移动卫星业务（MSS）频谱，目标为欧洲即将进行的 2 GHz MSS 频段分配。

四家运营商计划建立跨境、由运营商主导的直连终端（D2D）卫星连接框架，其宣称目标是建设统一的、由欧洲治理的非地面网络（NTN）层，服务商用移动用户和应急服务而不割裂各国市场，并向欧洲所有移动网络运营商提供开放、无歧视的批发接入。其宣称目标还包括制衡单一供应商巨型星座；文章称该举措与 ESA 的多轨道 IRIS² 星座互补。运营商表示联合竞标不会取代其与现有 NTN 合作伙伴的商业卫星安排。

架构方面，该联盟不自建星座：将选择一家独立卫星运营商提供空间段，并把资本投入地面段路由节点、核心网接口和欧洲数据监控设施，采用标准化的 3GPP NTN 协议，使普通智能手机可在偏远地区直连。未来数月内，运营商将细化运营规范、与监管机构沟通，并在欧盟委员会频谱分配流程之前通过竞争性评估选定卫星合作伙伴。

文章未说明意向书的条款或约束力、财务数字、卫星数量、分配时间表、所选卫星合作伙伴或高管表态。SatNews 当日另一篇文章报道，美国驻欧盟大使 Andrew Puzder 在接受《金融时报》采访（10 月 18 日发布）时称，若布鲁塞尔将即将分配的卫星频谱仅限于欧洲运营商，将面临“严厉的美国报复”；据该文，欧盟委员会提议为 D2D 连接预留部分 2 GHz 频段，并正在准备正式的分配指南。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Spectrum**: upcoming European 2 GHz MSS allocation; the Commission is reported to propose reserving portions for D2D.
- **Parties**: Deutsche Telekom, Orange, Telefónica, Vodafone (LOI to explore a joint bid).
- **Space segment**: independent satellite operator to be selected via competitive evaluation; no own constellation.
- **Operator investment**: ground-segment routing nodes, core network interfaces, European data-monitoring facilities.
- **Air interface**: standardized 3GPP NTN protocols; standard smartphones, no specialized hardware.
- **Access model**: open, non-discriminatory wholesale to all European mobile network operators.
