## Summary (EN)

At Samsung's IFA 2026 stand in Berlin, SmartThings demonstrated combined UWB and Wi-Fi ranging to track a Galaxy phone's location room-to-room inside a home, rather than merely knowing which devices are connected to the network. Samsung frames it as a "localized form of intelligence" within a broader "ambient intelligence" vision: SmartThings determining which room a user is physically in and triggering automations accordingly — for example, adjusting lighting or activating an appliance as someone enters a room, without a manually configured routine. Samsung frames this as part of a broader "Spatial AI" vision connecting Galaxy phones and connected home devices. As shown, the capability remains a demonstration rather than a shipping SmartThings feature. This item reached the KB via a social-media echo (X live search, UWB search term) rather than a UWB-scout primary source; multiple posters (independent tech accounts) covered the same demo on 2026-09-03/04, and it is confirmed by Sammy Fans' 2026-09-04 write-up.
