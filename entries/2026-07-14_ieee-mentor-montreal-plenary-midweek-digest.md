## Summary (EN)

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
Mid-plenary the signal is breadth, not a single-group feature drop: the WNG SC "Beamforming Feedback Information and Privacy" contribution (1438) is the one to watch — it revisits whether explicit BF feedback leaks identifying/location information, a live thread as sensing and privacy (TGbi) mature, and worth a dedicated entry if it develops into a study-group proposal. TGbr's "MLO with ELC PHY links" (1445) is an early sign of light-communications being framed as just another MLO link type. The rest is liaison and editorial cleanup typical of a plenary week; D2.0 ballot progress for TGbn is expected to surface later in the session and will be captured when substantive drafts post.
