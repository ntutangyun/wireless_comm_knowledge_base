## Summary (EN)

On 31 August 2026 (Suwanee, Georgia), YFORE Technology — a global Tier-1 automotive electronics supplier partnering with over 30 OEMs across North America, Europe and Asia — announced a multi-scenario UWB radar sensing portfolio spanning in-cabin safety, vehicle access and automated parking, all reusing the same UWB radar hardware already present in a vehicle for ranging/keyless-access purposes.

The in-cabin safety set includes Child Presence Detection (CPD) using high-penetration pulse signals to detect subtle micro-breathing, independent of optical line-of-sight — the release claims a life-detection rate exceeding 99%, signal leakage through windows kept under 20 cm, and a response time under 13 seconds to meet Euro NCAP requirements. Related applications built on the same radar layer include an Occupant Detection System that reports exact passenger counts (for accurate eCall dispatch) and an Occupant & Driver Monitoring System (ODMS) that fuses radar with smart vision mirrors to eliminate blind spots behind seats.

On the access and security side, Kick-Sensing Access enables hands-free liftgate opening with a claimed >98% activation rate and under 0.5% false-trigger rate in rain, car washes, or from animal movement; an Intrusion Detection function monitors side windows and liftgates for unauthorized entry; and a Low-Power Sentry Mode uses proximity radar to wake power-hungry cameras only on demand, claimed to save up to 2 kWh per night. A Parking Assistance System rounds out the portfolio, identifying parallel, perpendicular and angled spaces while detecting low-profile obstacles and filtering ground clutter, with the radar packaged invisibly behind bumper panels.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 8 月 31 日（美国佐治亚州萨旺尼），全球一级汽车电子供应商 YFORE（在北美、欧洲及亚洲与逾 30 家整车厂合作）宣布推出面向车内安全、车辆进入与自动泊车的多场景 UWB 雷达感知产品组合，全部复用车辆中原本用于测距/无钥匙进入的同一套 UWB 雷达硬件。

车内安全部分包括儿童遗留检测（CPD），利用高穿透脉冲信号检测细微的呼吸起伏，不依赖光学视线；官方称其生命检测率超过 99%，透过车窗的信号泄漏被控制在 20 厘米以内，响应时间低于 13 秒以满足 Euro NCAP 要求。基于同一雷达层构建的相关应用还包括可报告精确乘员数量（用于准确的 eCall 呼救调度）的乘员检测系统，以及将雷达与智能后视镜视觉方案融合、消除座椅后方视觉盲区的乘员与驾驶员监测系统（ODMS）。

在进入与安防方面，脚踢感应式尾门开启支持免提操作，宣称激活率超过 98%，在雨天、洗车或动物走动时误触发率低于 0.5%；入侵检测功能监控侧窗与尾门以发现未授权进入；低功耗哨兵模式则利用接近感应雷达仅在需要时唤醒高耗电摄像头，宣称每晚可节省最多 2 千瓦时电量。产品组合还包括一套泊车辅助系统，可识别平行、垂直与斜列车位，检测低矮障碍物并过滤地面杂波，雷达则隐蔽封装于保险杠面板之后。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The KB already has automotive UWB-radar coverage from silicon vendors (Infineon AIROC TSL100, 2026-06-27) and V2X ranging (Hyundai/Kia Vision Pulse, 2026-08-18), but this is the first Tier-1 systems-integrator entry: YFORE packages CPD, occupant monitoring, kick-sensing access, intrusion detection, sentry-mode power management, and parking assistance as one product family running on shared UWB radar hardware, rather than as a single-purpose chip or standalone feature. It illustrates how 802.15.4z-class UWB radar is being commercialized at the systems-integration layer — one radar doing double (or sextuple) duty across safety, access, and convenience functions — ahead of any 802.15.4ab-specific claim in the release.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
KB 此前已有来自芯片厂商的汽车 UWB 雷达报道（英飞凌 AIROC TSL100，2026-06-27）以及 V2X 测距应用（现代/起亚 Vision Pulse，2026-08-18），而本条目是首个来自一级系统集成商的条目：YFORE 将儿童遗留检测、乘员监测、脚踢感应进入、入侵检测、哨兵模式功耗管理与泊车辅助整合为运行在同一套 UWB 雷达硬件上的一个产品系列，而非单一功能芯片或独立功能。这体现了 802.15.4z 级 UWB 雷达正在系统集成层实现商业化——一套雷达同时承担安全、进入与便利等多重功能——且新闻稿中并未特别提及 802.15.4ab。
