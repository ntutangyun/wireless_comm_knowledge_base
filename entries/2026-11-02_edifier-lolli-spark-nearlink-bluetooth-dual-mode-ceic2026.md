## Summary (EN)

A vendor-supplied item published on Sina Tech on 2026-10-28 reports that Edifier (漫步者) showed two SparkLink earphones side by side at CEIC2026 (held 22-24 October in Shenzhen): the Lolli Spark in-ear noise-cancelling true-wireless earbuds and the Comfo Clip Q2 open-ear clip, shown alongside it. The company presents them as the first two members of its SparkLink product family. The Lolli Spark had earlier appeared only as a certification and alliance test-listing entry (see the 2026-10-13 entry); this item gives vendor-stated specifications.

Claimed specifications: a new-generation HiSilicon Tingting (谛听) audio chip; the L2HC codec carrying 48 kHz/24-bit lossless audio at 2.3 Mbps, with HWA Lossless Gold certification; active noise cancellation up to 50 dB plus AI call noise reduction; Bluetooth 6.0 and SparkLink dual mode with automatic switching; wear detection, IP55 and fast charging. No price or release date is given; the page says supported phones and connection methods are subject to official product information, and the company has opened a crowd-testing recruitment ('先闪为快') offering pre-launch units to users who publish reviews. The page notes that playback devices must also support SparkLink audio.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Platform**: new-generation HiSilicon Tingting audio chip (the earlier listing showed Hi3372 on OpenHarmony 5.1.0; the page says the product is built on OpenHarmony but names neither the version nor the chip part number).
- **Audio**: L2HC, 48 kHz/24-bit, 2.3 Mbps, HWA Lossless Gold certification (vendor claim).
- **Radio**: Bluetooth V6.0 + SparkLink dual mode with automatic switching.
- **Vendor-quoted SparkLink background figures**: SLE up to 16 Mbps (six times BLE bandwidth), a 125 microsecond slot versus 625 microseconds for Classic Bluetooth, and a 5.5 dB receive-sensitivity gain over BLE at 3 Mbps. These are the vendor's claims, not independent measurements.
- **Other**: ANC up to 50 dB, wear detection, IP55, AI translation and AI Q&A.
- **Not stated**: price, release date, battery life, which phones are supported.
