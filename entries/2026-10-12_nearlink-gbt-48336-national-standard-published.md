## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-28, China's State Administration for Market Regulation (National Standardization Administration Committee) published its latest batch of national-standard announcements, among which it approved the **《星闪无线通信系统》("SparkLink Wireless Communication System") GB/T 48336—2026 series** of recommended national standards (GB/T 48336.101—2026 and others). Per the International SparkLink Alliance's own announcement, this marks the technology's step up from group standard to recommended national standard (推荐性国家标准), and the series takes effect **2027-04-01**. SAMR's notice dated 2026-09-28 approves "466 recommended national standards and 4 amendments"; its attached standards list includes GB/T 48336.101—2026 through GB/T 48336.406—2026 (15 parts), all effective 2027-04-01.

Per the alliance's announcement, the published GB/T 48336 series spans four tiers: **overall architecture** — Part 101 (architecture and general requirements) and Part 102 (media-access-layer identifiers) jointly establish the overall technical framework; **access layer** — Part 201 specifies the SLB (Synchronous Low-latency Broadband) air interface; Part 202 the SLB device requirements and test methods; Part 203 the SLE (Synchronous Low-power) device requirements and test methods; **basic service layer** — Parts 301–304 specify device discovery, quality of service, and multi-domain coordination, with **Part 304 specifically establishing the specification for NearLink's convergence/coordination with 5G cellular networks**; **basic application layer** — Parts 401–406 cover mainstream application scenarios including audio streaming, video streaming, media control, positioning, and IP bearer.

The alliance's announcement frames the publication as providing authoritative technical endorsement for SparkLink's market-facing products and as guidance for upstream/downstream product development, quality control and market access across the SparkLink supply chain, intended to accelerate scaled application deployment. Separately, Xinhua (2026-09-23), reporting from the 2026 China Radio Conference, cited industry-scale figures: International SparkLink Alliance membership at 1,264 units, cumulative chip shipments exceeding 380 million units (3.8亿), a 2026 shipment projection of 200 million units, a 2030 shipment target of at least 1 billion units, 91 group standards released by the alliance to date, and 15 of the alliance's group standards among the first batch of 76 group standards nationally recognized for adoption into national standards. These are industry-context figures from a separate press account, not line items of the GB/T 48336 announcement itself.

## Summary (ZH)

2026年9月28日，国家市场监督管理总局（国家标准化管理委员会）发布最新一批国家标准公告，其中批准发布《星闪无线通信系统》**GB/T 48336—2026系列**推荐性国家标准（GB/T 48336.101—2026等）。据国际星闪联盟自身公告，这标志着星闪技术完成了从团体标准向推荐性国家标准的跃升，该系列标准将于**2027年4月1日**起正式实施。国家市场监督管理总局2026年9月28日公告批准"466项推荐性国家标准和4项推荐性国家标准修改单"，其附件清单列出GB/T 48336.101—2026至GB/T 48336.406—2026共15个部分，实施日期均为2027-04-01。

据联盟公告，本次发布的GB/T 48336系列覆盖四个层级：**总体架构**——第101部分（架构和通用要求）与第102部分（媒体接入层标识）共同确立总体技术框架；**接入层**——第201部分规定SLB（同步低时延宽带）空中接口技术要求，第202部分规定SLB设备技术要求和测试方法，第203部分规定SLE（同步低功耗）设备技术要求和测试方法；**基础服务层**——第301—304部分规范设备发现、服务质量、多域协同等机制，其中**第304部分专门确立星闪与5G蜂窝网络协同融合的规范**；**基础应用层**——第401—406部分覆盖音频流、视频流、媒体控制、定位、IP承载等主流应用场景。

联盟公告将此次发布定位为：为星闪产品市场化提供权威技术背书，为产业链上下游的产品开发、质量管控及市场准入提供指导，加速星闪规模化应用场景落地。另据新华网2026年9月23日对2026中国无线电大会的报道：国际星闪联盟成员单位达1264家，芯片累计出货超3.8亿颗，2026年出货量预计2亿颗，2030年出货目标至少10亿颗，联盟已发布91项团体标准，其中15项入选国家首批76项团体标准采信名单。上述为另一篇报道中的产业背景数据，并非GB/T 48336公告本身的条目内容。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Standard**: 《星闪无线通信系统》("SparkLink Wireless Communication System") GB/T 48336—2026 series, recommended national standard (推荐性国家标准).
- **Approval/publication date**: SAMR notice dated 2026-09-28, part of a batch of 466 recommended national standards + 4 amendments; the alliance's own page lists release_time 2026-10-09.
- **Effective date**: 2027-04-01.
- **Architecture tier**: Part 101 (architecture & general requirements), Part 102 (media-access-layer identifiers).
- **Access-layer tier**: Part 201 — SLB air interface; Part 202 — SLB device requirements/test methods; Part 203 — SLE device requirements/test methods.
- **Basic-service-layer tier**: Parts 301–304 — device discovery, QoS, multi-domain coordination; **Part 304 = NearLink/5G cellular convergence specification**.
- **Basic-application-layer tier**: Parts 401–406 — audio stream, video stream, media control, positioning, IP bearer.
- **Industry-context figures (Xinhua, 2026-09-23, 2026 China Radio Conference)**: 1,264 alliance member units; 380M+ (3.8亿) cumulative chip shipments; 2026 projection 200M units; 2030 target ≥1B units; 91 group standards released; 15 of the first 76 nationally-recognized group standards are SparkLink's.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This resolves the watch item opened by **2026-08-23_nearlink-slb-gb-standard-family-in-approval**, which had verified Part 101 (plan 20260704-T-469) and Part 202 (plan 20260705-T-469) at the **正在批准 (under approval)** stage on std.samr.gov.cn and flagged "watch next: formal publication with GB/T numbers and dates" as the open question. That question is now answered: the family has been formally published as GB/T 48336—2026, with a confirmed effective date (2027-04-01) — a full systematic numbering (101/102 architecture, 201–203 access, 301–304 basic service, 401–406 basic application) rather than the partial view available in August. This is also an institutional escalation distinct from GB/T 46803.1/.2-2025 (published 2025-12, in force 2026-07-01), which standardized only the SLE PHY/data-link layer under a generic ("低功耗无线通信网络") description without the SparkLink brand name — unlike GB/T 46803, GB/T 48336 carries the SparkLink name and spans SLB, SLE and the basic service/application layers. The new Part 304 (NearLink–5G convergence) is a concrete new data point not previously documented in this KB's standards-track entries. Still open: the published standard text itself (parameter values, conformance details).
