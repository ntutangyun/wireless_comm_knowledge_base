## Summary (EN)
On 23 June 2026 the FCC announced the close of **Auction 113**, the first US spectrum auction in roughly four years and the first since Congress restored the Commission's auction authority (the auction-authority lapse ran from March 2023 until the 2025 Working Families Tax Cut Act, which also mandated an 800 MHz spectrum pipeline by 2034 and an Upper C-band sale of ≥100 MHz by July 2027). The inventory was the **200 AWS-3 licenses in 1695-1710 MHz (unpaired uplink), 1755-1780 MHz and 2155-2180 MHz (paired)** that had sat unused since the 2014 AWS-3 auction (Auction 97) because of bid defaults/withdrawals by the DISH-affiliated designated entities whose bidding credits were later denied. Bidding ran 2–23 June over 72 rounds with 17 qualified bidders; **gross winning bids exceeded $3.5 billion**, which the FCC characterised as beating expectations "with significant per-unit prices". Up to **$3.3 billion** of proceeds is earmarked to repay the amounts borrowed to fund the "rip and replace" (Secure and Trusted Communications Networks Reimbursement) programme and other Commerce Department programmes. The auction also debuted the FCC's new auction application system.

The winning-bidder public notice (DA 26-633, 26 June 2026; payments due 13 and 27 July) shows a lopsided outcome reported by Light Reading, Fierce and Inside Towers: **Verizon (Cellco Partnership) — 82 licenses for $3.16 billion**, concentrated in major metros (New York, Chicago, Boston) to add mid-band capacity; **T-Mobile — 102 licenses for $277.8 million**, the largest count, aimed at rural mid-band build-out; **AT&T — 10 licenses for $120.8 million**; **SpaceX (Space Exploration Holdings) — 2 licenses for $8.5 million**, notably *uplink-only* AWS-3 blocks that fill a gap in its Band-70 direct-to-device holdings acquired from EchoStar; **EchoStar (as Conundrum Wireless) — 1–2 licenses for $1.2 million**; plus Blue Ridge Wireless and Citizens Band License Company with one small-market license each (25 % bidding credit). Analysts note that the total cleared the ~$2.9 billion threshold that shielded EchoStar from a residual liability tied to the original 2014 default. Chairman Carr framed the result as proof that "FCC auctions are finally back" and as a dress rehearsal for the Upper C-band auction.

## Summary (ZH)
2026 年 6 月 23 日，FCC 宣布**第 113 号拍卖**收官——这是美国约四年来的首次频谱拍卖，也是国会恢复委员会拍卖授权后的首次（拍卖授权自 2023 年 3 月失效，直至 2025 年《工薪家庭减税法案》恢复，该法案同时要求到 2034 年释放 800 MHz 频谱管线并在 2027 年 7 月前拍卖 ≥100 MHz 高段 C 频段）。标的为 **1695-1710 MHz（非成对上行）、1755-1780 MHz 与 2155-2180 MHz（成对）的 200 张 AWS-3 牌照**——自 2014 年 AWS-3 拍卖（第 97 号）以来因 DISH 关联指定实体的竞标违约/撤标（其竞标抵免随后被否）而闲置。竞标于 6 月 2–23 日进行、共 72 轮、17 家合格竞标者；**总中标价超过 35 亿美元**，FCC 称其“单位价格显著”、超出预期。最多 **33 亿美元**收入将用于偿还为“拆除与替换”（安全可信通信网络补偿）计划及商务部其他项目所借款项。此次拍卖还首次启用了 FCC 的新版拍卖申请系统。

中标者公告（DA 26-633，2026 年 6 月 26 日；付款截止 7 月 13 日与 27 日）显示结果高度不均衡，Light Reading、Fierce 与 Inside Towers 均有报道：**Verizon（Cellco Partnership）——82 张牌照、31.6 亿美元**，集中于纽约、芝加哥、波士顿等大城市以补充中频段容量；**T-Mobile——102 张、2.778 亿美元**，数量最多，面向农村中频段建设；**AT&T——10 张、1.208 亿美元**；**SpaceX（Space Exploration Holdings）——2 张、850 万美元**，且是*仅上行*的 AWS-3 频块，用于填补其从 EchoStar 收购的 Band-70 直连终端频谱缺口；**EchoStar（以 Conundrum Wireless 名义）——1–2 张、120 万美元**；另有 Blue Ridge Wireless 与 Citizens Band License Company 各获一张小市场牌照（25 % 竞标抵免）。分析人士指出总额越过了约 29 亿美元的门槛，使 EchoStar 免于因 2014 年违约而承担的剩余责任。主席 Carr 将结果定性为“FCC 拍卖终于回来了”，并视其为高段 C 频段拍卖的预演。

## Key technical points (EN)
- Bands: 1695-1710 MHz (15 MHz unpaired uplink, shared with federal meteorological-satellite operations under coordination), 1755-1780 MHz uplink paired with 2155-2180 MHz downlink (3GPP Band 66 / AWS-3 portion; 1695-1710 also underlies Band 70 uplink) — mid-band FDD spectrum usable by existing LTE/NR device ecosystems.
- Inventory: 200 licenses of various sizes and geographies left in FCC inventory after 2014 defaults; re-offered as-is.
- Process: 2–23 June 2026, 72 rounds, 17 qualified bidders, 7 winners; new FCC auction application system debuted.
- Proceeds: >$3.5 B gross; up to $3.3 B to repay rip-and-replace borrowing.
- Winners: Verizon 82 licenses / $3.16 B (NY, Chicago, Boston); T-Mobile 102 / $277.8 M (rural); AT&T 10 / $120.8 M; SpaceX 2 / $8.5 M (uplink-only, Band-70 gap-fill for D2D); EchoStar/Conundrum 1–2 / $1.2 M; Blue Ridge Wireless and Citizens Band License Co. one each with 25 % credit.
- Deadlines: 20 % down payment 13 Jul 2026; final payment 27 Jul 2026.
- Policy context: first auction after authority restoration; 800 MHz pipeline by 2034; Upper C-band (3.98-4.14 GHz) auction by July 2027 (see 2026-08-05_fcc-upper-cband-auction-rules-adopted).

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
Deliberate catch-up entry (event 23–26 June 2026, ~8 weeks old): the KB's cellular-spectrum bin records the Upper C-band rules (2026-08-05_fcc-upper-cband-auction-rules-adopted), the NTIA federal-band studies (2026-08-15_ntia-4-4ghz-study-cleared-four-band-6g-pipeline) and the coexistence research (2026-08-15_arxiv-coordinated-federal-spectrum-coexistence-oran), and mentions AWS-3 only in passing inside satellite/Part-100 entries — the outcome of the first post-hiatus US auction was missing. Three technical takeaways: (i) the market priced *legacy-band-compatible mid-band* (Band 66/70) at levels the FCC called above expectations, so mid-band FDD that works with today's devices still commands a premium even in the 6G-planning era; (ii) SpaceX's uplink-only purchase is a concrete example of a satellite direct-to-device operator assembling terrestrial band fragments (Band 70) rather than relying on MSS spectrum alone — a data point for the D2D entries in the satellite bin; (iii) the FCC's read-across is explicit — Auction 113 was the systems test for the Upper C-band auction, which the KB is tracking toward its July-2027 deadline.

刻意补录（事件发生于 2026 年 6 月 23–26 日，约 8 周前）：知识库 cellular-spectrum 主题已记录高段 C 频段规则（2026-08-05_fcc-upper-cband-auction-rules-adopted）、NTIA 联邦频段研究（2026-08-15_ntia-4-4ghz-study-cleared-four-band-6g-pipeline）与共存研究（2026-08-15_arxiv-coordinated-federal-spectrum-coexistence-oran），AWS-3 仅在卫星/Part-100 条目中顺带提及——美国拍卖授权恢复后首次拍卖的结果此前缺失。三点技术启示：(i) 市场对*与既有频段兼容的中频段*（Band 66/70）给出的价格被 FCC 称为超出预期，说明即使在 6G 规划时代，能与现有终端配合的中频段 FDD 仍有溢价；(ii) SpaceX 只买上行频块，是卫星直连终端运营商拼凑地面频段碎片（Band 70）而非仅依赖 MSS 频谱的具体实例——为卫星主题的 D2D 条目提供了数据点；(iii) FCC 的对照意图明确——第 113 号拍卖是高段 C 频段拍卖的系统演练，知识库正跟踪后者的 2027 年 7 月截止期。
