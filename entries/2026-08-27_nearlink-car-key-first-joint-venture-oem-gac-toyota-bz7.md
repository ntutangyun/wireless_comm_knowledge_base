## Summary (EN)
The **GAC-Toyota bZ7 (广汽丰田铂智7)** went on sale in China on **2026-03-29** and is, by the account of its key supplier and of the SparkLink alliance ecosystem, the **first joint-venture-brand vehicle to mass-produce a NearLink (星闪) digital car key**. Until now every NearLink car-key deployment recorded in this knowledge base has been a Chinese domestic brand — the Huawei-aligned marques (AITO/问界, Luxeed/智界, Maextro/尊界, Stelato/享界, Onvo-class 尚界) plus Dongfeng, BAIC, SAIC, JAC, Chery, Geely and GAC's own-brand lines. The bZ7 is a Toyota-badged product from the GAC–Toyota joint venture, which makes it the first crossing of the technology out of the domestic-brand ecosystem and into an international OEM's nameplate.

The vehicle is a battery-electric flagship sedan launched in five trims across 600 km and 700 km battery options, at a guide price of **¥169,800–229,800** (limited-time subsidised pricing ¥147,800–199,800). Notably the NearLink key is **not** reserved for the halo trim: Chinese launch coverage lists the "尊界同款星闪钥匙" (the same SparkLink key as the Maextro S800) as **standard equipment on the entry-level 600Pro trim**, alongside a HarmonyOS cockpit built on the MoLA architecture and Huawei's 智擎 powertrain integration. The digital-key solution itself was built as a Tier-1 package by **银基科技 (Yinji Technology)**, a council member of the International SparkLink Alliance, using a **phase-difference plus time-of-flight (TOF) ranging** combination for positioning and interference resistance, and supporting **multi-brand wallet keys** across phone vendors.

The programme has a visible announcement trail: 银基 first stated the bZ7 would be the first joint-venture mass-production model at the **2025 International SparkLink Japan Industry Forum (2025-08-26/27, Nagoya)**, when the project was described as being in final testing; a supplier-side write-up followed in October 2025, the vehicle launched in March 2026, and further ecosystem coverage appeared in May 2026.

Caveats worth carrying: the "first joint-venture brand" claim originates with the supplier and alliance-side coverage rather than an independent audit of every OEM programme; and the frequently quoted ranging figures (see below) are **technology-level claims published by HiSilicon for the ICCE-NearLink car-key profile in April 2024, not measurements taken on this vehicle**. The approach-unlock and walk-away-lock distances are likewise vendor-stated user-experience figures.

## Summary (ZH)
**广汽丰田铂智 7** 于 **2026-03-29** 在中国正式上市。据其钥匙方案供应商与星闪联盟生态方的说法，这是**首款量产星闪数字车钥匙的合资品牌车型**。在此之前，本知识库收录的所有星闪车钥匙落地案例均为自主品牌——华为系品牌（问界、智界、尊界、享界、尚界）以及东风、北汽、上汽、江淮、奇瑞、吉利与广汽自主线。铂智 7 是广汽丰田合资公司挂丰田标的产品，因而是该技术首次走出自主品牌生态、进入国际车企品牌序列。

该车为纯电旗舰轿车，共 5 款配置，提供 600 km 与 700 km 两种续航版本，官方指导价 **16.98–22.98 万元**（限时补贴权益价 14.78–19.98 万元）。值得注意的是，星闪钥匙**并非**高配专属：中文上市报道明确将"尊界同款星闪钥匙"列为**入门款 600Pro 的标配**，同时标配基于 MoLA 架构的鸿蒙座舱与华为智擎动力集成。钥匙方案本身由国际星闪联盟理事单位 **银基科技** 以 Tier-1 身份打造，采用**相位差 + 飞行时间（TOF）测距**组合实现定位与抗干扰，并支持跨手机厂商的**多品牌钱包钥匙**。

该项目有清晰的公开时间线：银基科技最早在 **2025 国际星闪日本产业论坛（2025-08-26/27，名古屋）** 上宣布铂智 7 将成为合资车企中首款量产星闪数字钥匙的车型，当时项目处于最后测试阶段；2025 年 10 月出现供应商侧报道，车辆于 2026 年 3 月上市，2026 年 5 月又有生态侧报道跟进。

需要保留的限定：「首个合资品牌」的说法来自供应商与联盟侧报道，而非对所有车企项目的独立核查；常被引用的测距指标（见下）是 **海思于 2024 年 4 月针对 ICCE 星闪车钥匙规范发布的技术级宣称，并非在本车上的实测值**。靠近解锁与离车落锁距离同样是厂商给出的体验值。

- **Fitment**: NearLink key is **standard on the entry 600Pro trim**, marketed as the "same SparkLink key as the Maextro S800 (尊界同款星闪钥匙)" — i.e. not a halo-trim-only feature.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The knowledge base already tracks NearLink car keys as a scaling story — 260M+ chips shipped, ~800k vehicles keyed in the past year with 1M+ projected for 2026, 43 automakers on Huawei Wallet, ~50M handsets enabled by a HarmonyOS 6.0 OTA, and an 88-standard alliance corpus with a national car-key standard in approval. What none of those entries record is a **structural boundary being crossed**: every named model was a Chinese domestic brand. A Toyota-badged joint-venture product adopting NearLink — and standard-fitting it from the cheapest trim rather than gating it behind a halo variant — is a different kind of signal from another domestic design win, because joint ventures answer to a foreign parent's own connectivity and security roadmaps (Toyota is otherwise aligned with the CCC Digital Key ecosystem built on BLE/UWB). It is the closest thing yet to evidence for or against the "China-centric without international standardisation" thesis that the April 2026 market analysis in this KB set as the yardstick.

Two caveats keep this a novelty-2 rather than a headline item. First, a joint venture localising a China-market-only nameplate is not the same as Toyota adopting NearLink globally; the bZ7 is a China-specific BEV built on a heavily Huawei-integrated stack (HarmonyOS cockpit, Huawei powertrain), so the key may travel with the cockpit rather than representing an independent OEM decision. Second, the "first joint-venture brand" claim is supplier-sourced. Worth watching next: whether a second joint-venture or a wholly foreign-owned OEM programme appears; whether any NearLink key ships in a vehicle sold outside China; whether the SparkLink Japan and Europe forum tracks convert into non-China design wins; and whether the pending brand-named GB national-standard family and the iSLA certification scheme become the normative anchor such programmes cite.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
本知识库已将星闪车钥匙作为一条"规模化"线索持续跟踪——芯片累计出货 2.6 亿+、过去一年约 80 万辆装车（2026 年预计破 100 万）、华为钱包接入车企 43 家、HarmonyOS 6.0 OTA 覆盖约 5000 万台手机、联盟标准累计 88 项且国家车钥匙标准进入报批阶段。但这些条目都没有记录一件事：**一条结构性边界被跨越**——此前出现的所有车型均为中国自主品牌。一款挂丰田标的合资产品采用星闪，并且是从最低配就标配、而非作为高配专属功能，这与又一个自主品牌定点是不同性质的信号，因为合资公司需要对外方母公司自身的连接与安全路线负责（丰田在其他市场与基于 BLE/UWB 的 CCC 数字钥匙生态保持一致）。这也是迄今最接近于验证或证伪本库 2026 年 4 月市场分析所设标尺——"以中国为中心、缺乏国际标准化"这一论断——的证据。

有两点限定使其保持在 novelty-2 而非头条级别。其一，合资公司为一款仅在中国销售的车型做本地化，并不等同于丰田在全球范围采用星闪；铂智 7 是深度整合华为方案（鸿蒙座舱、华为动力）的中国专属纯电车型，因此星闪钥匙可能是随座舱方案一同引入的，而非车企独立的技术选型。其二，「首个合资品牌」的说法来自供应商侧。后续值得关注：是否出现第二个合资项目或外商独资车企项目；是否有星闪钥匙随车型销往中国以外市场；星闪日本与星闪欧洲论坛这两条线是否转化为非中国市场的定点；以及待发布的以品牌命名的国标族与 iSLA 认证方案是否成为此类项目引用的规范锚点。
