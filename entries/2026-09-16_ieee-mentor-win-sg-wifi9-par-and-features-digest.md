## Summary (EN)

On day 3 of the 2026-09-13/18 Waikoloa interim, the WLAN Intelligent Networking Study Group (WIN SG — the study group this KB has tracked since its 2026-08-27 naming, chartered to produce the PAR for what would become the next 802.11 amendment after 802.11bn) uploaded the first document this KB has seen that is specifically about drafting **PAR language**: a proposed project timeline (SG creates the PAR and CSD to allow the Task Group to start in May 2027, targeting first WG approval in January 2027 and final approval in March 2027) and a tentative attempt at quantifying the amendment's two headline requirements — throughput and latency predictability. Alongside it, three further WIN SG contributions this run fill in what those requirements might look like in practice: a candidate-feature list from OPPO spanning coordinated OFDMA, low-latency uplink and reliability mechanisms; a device-experience-centred vision from Samsung under the informal "11bX" label, organised around device-to-device P2P, deterministic latency, and legacy-feature enhancement; and an empirical tail-latency measurement study from Nokia characterising four distinct causes of non-preemptive channel blocking in existing 802.11 networks.

## Summary (ZH)

在 2026-09-13 至 18 日 Waikoloa 临时会议第 3 天，WLAN 智能组网研究组（WIN SG —— 本知识库自 2026-08-27 其获得命名起便持续跟踪的研究组，负责为 802.11bn 之后的下一个 802.11 修订案起草 PAR）上传了本知识库所见的第一份专门讨论 **PAR 文本**的文档：一份拟议的项目时间表（研究组在 2027 年 5 月前完成 PAR 与 CSD 以启动任务组，目标是 2027 年 1 月获得工作组初步批准、2027 年 3 月获得最终批准），以及对修订案两项核心需求——吞吐量与时延可预测性——的一次初步量化尝试。与之相伴，本次运行中的另外三份 WIN SG 贡献勾勒出这些需求在实践中可能的样貌：OPPO 提出的候选特性清单，涵盖协同 OFDMA、低时延上行与可靠性机制；三星以非正式的"11bX"名称提出的以设备体验为中心的愿景，围绕设备间 P2P、确定性时延与既有特性增强三条主线；以及 Nokia 的一项实证尾时延测量研究，刻画了现有 802.11 网络中四类不可抢占信道阻塞的成因。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **DCN 11-26-1859r0 · WIN SG** — "WIN PAR Discussion". Laurent Cariou (Intel), uploaded 15-Sep-2026. Proposes the project schedule (SG produces PAR + CSD to start the Task Group in May 2027; first WG approval targeted January 2027, final approval March 2027) and raises open questions for the PAR text itself: whether to quantify the two headline requirements at all (the amendment's working name lacks "High"/"Ultra High", which the author suggests may mean quantification isn't required, "but needs confirmation"); a tentative throughput requirement framed as throughput-at-range rather than peak throughput (as in UHR/802.11bn), plus "in presence of over-the-air or in-device interference" to admit more PHY/MAC candidate features; a tentative latency requirement of "at least one mode of operation capable of reducing latency by 25% for the 95th percentile of the latency distribution of at least one access category compared to the Ultra High Reliability MAC/PHY operation"; whether a third requirement on "adaptation to emerging application requirements such as from AI traffic or services" should be folded into throughput/latency or captured as either a new WIN capability element or a standalone third requirement; and a band scope of 1-7.25 GHz with mandatory backward compatibility and coexistence with legacy 802.11 devices in the 2.4/5/6 GHz unlicensed bands. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1859&is_year=2026) (An identical deck was also cross-posted under DCN 11-26-1858r0 in TGbn.)
- **DCN 11-26-1695r1 · WIN SG** — "Candidate Features For Wi-Fi 9". Chaoming Luo (OPPO), uploaded 15-Sep-2026 (revision). Proposes nine candidate feature areas: DL/UL **Co-OFDMA** for mid-duration (100 microseconds-10 s) low-latency traffic shared between co-channel APs, usable together with Distributed Resource Units; combining **Co-RTWT with Co-BF/Co-SR** so a Co-RTWT coordinating AP can signal that Co-SR/Co-BF transmission is expected during a coordinated R-TWT service period; **IDC-mitigation** signalling to capture directional (Rx/Tx) unavailability that current DUO feedback lacks; **mobile-AP power save** (citing a survey in which 37% of employees and 45% of people overall reported using a phone as a hotspot) via per-TWT-SP parameter flexibility and MLD-level EMLSR-style enable/disable; a third **preemption** case (Case 3: a non-AP STA preempting another non-AP STA's UL TXOP, motivated by smart-factory humanoid-robot traffic) beyond the two cases already extensively discussed in UHR (Case 1, an AP preempting a UL TXOP; Case 2, a non-AP STA preempting a DL TXOP); **UL enhancements** for AI-driven traffic growth (candidate ideas include NOMA, control-link-signalled reservation of a separate data link, massive OFDMA via smaller 13-/6-tone RUs, and UL MU-MIMO); **reliability enhancements** via duplicate transmission and PPDU-level retransmission; **interference measurement and suppression (IMS)** extended beyond today's full-bandwidth-SU-only IM pilots to UL-DL and UL-UL interference, adding suppression/coordination on top of measurement; and **low-latency buffer status reporting** so a receiver can learn an MSDU's expiration time and prioritise/schedule accordingly. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1695&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1588r2 · WIN SG** — "View on 11bX: Device Experience Revolution for Next Generation Wi-Fi". Jack Lee et al. (Samsung Electronics), uploaded 15-Sep-2026 (revision). Frames the informally-named "11bX" amendment around three themes: **device-centric intelligent P2P** — relaxing the same-AP-association requirement for TDLS, adding multi-link P2P (e.g. P2P EMLSR), traffic-aware QoS/scheduling for groups of P2P devices, and P2P spatial reuse; **low latency** aimed at device-level *deterministic* latency (not just lower average latency) for XR/gaming/AI-era services, via low-overhead signalling and a new aggregation framework allowing multi-source/multi-destination packets in a single transmission; and **legacy-feature enhancement** — a mobile-AP DUO mechanism sharing dynamic RF-unavailability information with associated STAs in real time, link-to-MLD-level inter-link signalling building on 802.11bq's inter-link coordination, and improved power-save plus multicast/broadcast efficiency for low-capability devices such as wearables. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1588&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1653r1 · WIN SG** — "Channel-Blocking Events and Tail Latency in 802.11 Networks". Behnam Dezfouli et al. (Nokia), uploaded 15-Sep-2026 (revision). An empirical frame-capture and controlled-experiment study arguing that average channel utilization alone does not predict tail latency, because a given amount of channel occupancy produces much worse tail latency when concentrated into long, contiguous blocking intervals than when spread across shorter ones. It characterises four non-preemptive blocking sources: **long TXOPs** (both full-protection and incremental-protection variants can still create a single contiguous blocking event; example trace shows an 11.2 ms incremental TXOP, and file-download/video-streaming activity by ordinary stations produced multi-millisecond blocking events even without a heavy workload); **BSRP buffer-status polling** (polling duration rises in RU-allocation steps as more STAs are polled, with the P99 polling-event rate above 70/second across measured deployments, i.e. one poll or shorter roughly every 14 ms; across six environments the group's own data shows a consistent BSRP rate of 12 Mbps and response MCS of 3 -- lower rate/MCS choices would increase the overhead further); **channel sounding** (NDPA/NDP/polling/feedback exchanges scale with sounded-STA count, LTF count, bandwidth, and BF-report size; P99 sounding-event frequency was 34/second in the measured single-slow-STA case); and **beacon transmission** (a roughly 490-byte beacon PSDU sent as a 20 MHz non-HT OFDM PPDU at 6 Mb/s occupies about 680 microseconds; with beacons this length, aggregate beacon airtime rises from 0.66% at 1 overlapping AP to 6.64% at 10 overlapping APs). The paper's own recommendation is that delay-aware channel-access mechanisms should limit or coordinate contiguous channel-unavailability intervals rather than only budgeting for average utilization. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1653&is_year=2026)
- Also uploaded in WIN SG / TGbn / TGbq / UCM TIG / TGbt / 802.11 WG this run, named here but not analysed individually: DCN 1860r0 (TGbq, "channel-access-for-BE", Xiangxin Gu/Spreadtrum), 1861r0 (UCM TIG, "template for comments", Volker Jungnickel/Fraunhofer HHI), 1867r0 (TGbn, LB296 CR for PAR Verification / Roaming, Akira Kishida/NTT — continuation of the LB296 comment-resolution wave this KB has covered on 2026-09-14/15), 1868r0 (802.11 WG, September 2026 Editors' closing report, Edward Au/Huawei), 1870r0/r1 (TGbt, transcript and PTK-derivation comments on P802.11bt/D1.0, Jouni Malinen/Qualcomm), and 1871r0 (802.11 WG, Wi-Fi Alliance liaison report, Juan-Carlos Zuniga/Cisco).

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **DCN 11-26-1859r0 · WIN SG** — "WIN PAR 讨论"。Laurent Cariou（英特尔），2026-09-15 上传。提出项目时间表（研究组在 2027 年 5 月前完成 PAR 与 CSD 以启动任务组；目标 2027 年 1 月获工作组初步批准、2027 年 3 月获最终批准），并针对 PAR 文本本身提出待解决的问题：是否需要量化两项核心需求（该修订案的工作名称中不含"High"/"Ultra High"字样，作者提出这也许意味着无需量化，"但需确认"）；一项初步吞吐量需求，以"特定距离处的吞吐量"而非峰值吞吐量表述（沿用 UHR/802.11bn 的做法），并加入"存在空口或设备内干扰情况下"的表述，以容纳更多 PHY/MAC 候选特性；一项初步时延需求："至少一种工作模式，能够将至少一个接入类别的时延分布第 95 百分位相较于超高可靠性 MAC/PHY 操作降低 25%"；是否将"适应新兴应用需求（如 AI 流量或服务）"这一第三项需求并入吞吐量/时延需求，或作为新的 WIN 能力元素、或作为独立的第三项需求单列；以及 1-7.25 GHz 的频段范围，并要求与 2.4/5/6 GHz 免许可频段中现有 802.11 设备保持向后兼容与共存。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1859&is_year=2026)（同一份文稿也以 DCN 11-26-1858r0 的形式交叉上传至 TGbn。）
- **DCN 11-26-1695r1 · WIN SG** — "Wi-Fi 9 候选特性"。Chaoming Luo（OPPO），2026-09-15 上传（修订版）。提出九个候选特性方向：面向 100 微秒至 10 秒中等时长、由同信道 AP 共享的低时延流量的 DL/UL **协同 OFDMA（Co-OFDMA）**，可与分布式资源单元（DRU）配合使用；**Co-RTWT 与 Co-BF/Co-SR 的组合**，使发起 Co-RTWT 协调的 AP 能够在协调 R-TWT 服务周期内提前告知预期发生 Co-SR/Co-BF 传输；**IDC 缓解**信令，用以捕捉当前 DUO 反馈所缺失的方向性（Rx/Tx）不可用信息；**移动 AP 省电**（引用一项调查：37% 的受访员工、总体 45% 的人曾将手机用作热点），通过按 TWT SP 灵活调整参数以及 MLD 级别的类 EMLSR 使能/禁用实现；在 UHR 已充分讨论的两种抢占场景（场景 1：AP 抢占 UL TXOP；场景 2：非 AP STA 抢占 DL TXOP）之外新增第三种**抢占**场景（场景 3：非 AP STA 抢占另一非 AP STA 的 UL TXOP，动机来自智能工厂人形机器人流量）；面向 AI 驱动流量增长的 **UL 增强**（候选思路包括 NOMA、通过控制链路预约独立数据链路、采用更小的 13/6 音调 RU 实现大规模 OFDMA，以及 UL MU-MIMO）；通过重复传输与 PPDU 级重传实现的**可靠性增强**；将**干扰测量与抑制（IMS）**由当前仅支持全带宽单用户（SU）的 IM 导频扩展至 UL-DL 与 UL-UL 干扰，在测量之上增加抑制/协调能力；以及**低时延缓冲状态上报**，使接收方能够获知 MSDU 的到期时间并据此优先处理/调度。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1695&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1588r2 · WIN SG** — "关于 11bX 的看法：面向下一代 Wi-Fi 的设备体验革新"。Jack Lee 等（三星电子），2026-09-15 上传（修订版）。围绕非正式命名的"11bX"修订案提出三大主题：**以设备为中心的智能 P2P**——放宽 TDLS 的同 AP 关联要求、引入多链路 P2P（如 P2P EMLSR）、面向 P2P 设备群组的流量感知 QoS/调度，以及 P2P 空间复用；**低时延**，目标是设备级*确定性*时延（而非仅仅降低平均时延），服务于 XR/游戏/AI 时代应用，通过低开销信令与一种新的聚合框架（允许单次传输中包含多源/多目的地数据包）实现；**既有特性增强**——移动 AP DUO 机制，实时向关联 STA 共享动态射频不可用信息；建立在 802.11bq 链路间协调基础上的链路到 MLD 级别信令；以及针对可穿戴设备等低能力设备改进的省电与组播/广播效率。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1588&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1653r1 · WIN SG** — "802.11 网络中的信道占用事件与尾时延"。Behnam Dezfouli 等（诺基亚），2026-09-15 上传（修订版）。一项基于帧捕获与受控实验的实证研究，主张仅凭平均信道利用率无法预测尾时延，因为同样数量的信道占用时间，若集中在长而连续的占用区间中，会比分散在较短区间中产生严重得多的尾时延。文中刻画了四种非抢占式占用来源：**长 TXOP**（完全保护与增量保护两种方式均可能形成单一连续占用事件；示例轨迹显示一次 11.2 毫秒的增量保护 TXOP，普通站点的文件下载/视频流活动即便在非高负载条件下也会产生多毫秒级的占用事件）；**BSRP 缓冲状态轮询**（轮询时长随被轮询 STA 数量按 RU 分配级别台阶式上升，各测量部署中轮询事件频率的 P99 超过每秒 70 次，即大约每 14 毫秒或更短即出现一次；六个环境中该团队自身数据显示 BSRP 速率一致为 12 Mbps、响应 MCS 为 3——更低的速率/MCS 选择将进一步增加开销）；**信道探测（sounding）**（NDPA/NDP/轮询/反馈交换随被探测 STA 数量、LTF 数量、带宽及 BF 报告大小而增长；在所测的单一慢速 STA 场景中，探测事件频率的 P99 为每秒 34 次）；以及**信标传输**（约 490 字节的信标 PSDU 以 20 MHz 非 HT OFDM PPDU、6 Mb/s 速率发送约占用 680 微秒；以该信标长度计，聚合信标占用时间在 1 个重叠 AP 时为 0.66%，在 10 个重叠 AP 时升至 6.64%）。论文自身的建议是，时延感知的信道接入机制应当限制或协调连续的信道不可用区间，而不应仅按平均利用率预算。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1653&is_year=2026)

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the first document this KB has seen that discusses concrete **PAR language** for the amendment WIN SG is chartered to produce, including a project schedule and tentative quantified requirements (25% 95th-percentile latency reduction vs UHR MAC/PHY on at least one access category; throughput-at-range framing). The KB's 2026-08-27 entry recorded WIN SG's formation and naming but not this level of procedural or quantitative detail. The three companion contributions (candidate-feature list, "11bX" device-experience vision, and the Nokia tail-latency study) all bear directly on the PAR discussion's open latency question: the Nokia study in particular supplies the kind of empirical, cause-by-cause channel-blocking data (TXOPs, BSRP polling, sounding, beacons) that a 95th-percentile latency target would need to be checked against.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

这是本知识库首次看到就 WIN SG 所负责修订案的具体 **PAR 文本**展开讨论的文档，其中包括项目时间表与初步量化需求（相较于 UHR MAC/PHY，在至少一个接入类别上将第 95 百分位时延降低 25%；以"特定距离处吞吐量"表述吞吐量需求）。本知识库 2026-08-27 的条目记录了 WIN SG 的成立与命名，但未涉及这一层级的流程或量化细节。
