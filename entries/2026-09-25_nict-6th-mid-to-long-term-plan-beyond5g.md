## Summary (EN)

Japan's National Institute of Information and Communications Technology (NICT) published the English-language announcement of its 6th Mid- to Long-Term Plan (English press release dated July 31, 2026; the Japanese-language original was issued April 20, 2026), covering the five-year period April 1, 2026 - March 31, 2031. The plan frames ICT as "increasingly vital both as a foundational technology supporting national competitiveness and security and as a key driver of Japan's growth strategy," citing rising energy consumption, natural disasters, cyber risk, and geopolitical tension as the pressures behind the strategy, and names "technological autonomy and strategic indispensability" in critical digital infrastructure as an explicit national-security framing.

The plan organizes NICT's R&D around four strategic technology fields: AI and Communication, Beyond 5G, Quantum ICT, and Cybersecurity. Separately, the plan lists six foundational R&D themes supporting innovation, three of which are Beyond-5G-related: Beyond 5G Architecture Technologies, Terahertz-wave ICT Platform Technologies, and Testbed Construction Technologies for the Beyond 5G Era (the others: Resilient ICT, Global Quantum-secure Networks, Advanced ICT Device Development Platforms). The announcement is strategic-direction framing rather than a technical result — it names no numerical targets, funding figures, or milestone dates for the Beyond 5G theme.

## Summary (ZH)

日本国立研究开发法人情报通信研究机构（NICT）发布了其第六期中长期计划的英文版公告（英文新闻稿日期为 2026 年 7 月 31 日；日文原文早在 2026 年 4 月 20 日已发布），覆盖 2026 年 4 月 1 日至 2031 年 3 月 31 日这一五年期间。该计划将信息通信技术（ICT）定位为"作为支撑国家竞争力与安全保障的基础技术，同时也是日本增长战略的关键驱动力，其重要性正日益凸显"，并将能源消耗上升、自然灾害、网络安全风险及地缘政治紧张列为推动该战略的压力因素，明确以关键数字基础设施领域的"技术自主性与战略不可替代性"作为国家安全层面的表述框架。

该计划将 NICT 的研发工作围绕四大战略技术领域展开：AI 与通信、Beyond 5G、量子 ICT，以及网络安全。此外，计划另列六项支撑创新的基础研发主题，其中三项与 Beyond 5G 相关："Beyond 5G 架构技术"、"太赫兹波 ICT 平台技术"以及"面向 Beyond 5G 时代的测试床建设技术"（其余三项为韧性 ICT、全球量子安全网络、先进 ICT 器件开发平台技术）。该公告属于战略方向层面的表述，而非技术成果——针对 Beyond 5G 主题未给出任何具体数值目标、资金规模或里程碑日期。
