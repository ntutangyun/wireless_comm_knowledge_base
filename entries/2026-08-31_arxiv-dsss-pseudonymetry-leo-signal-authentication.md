## Summary (EN)

Passive LEO-PNT — using broadband satellite downlinks as signals of opportunity for positioning without a dedicated navigation payload — has a problem that accuracy work tends to skip past: **attribution**. A receive-only, low-complexity receiver can extract a Doppler or timing observable from a beacon or pilot component, but in a dense constellation several satellites are visible at once and their reference components land in nearby time-frequency regions. The receiver may see a feature without being able to say which satellite produced it, and the navigation solution needs that answer. Nisanur Camuzcu and Alireza Vahid (Rochester Institute of Technology, NSF award 2348589, accepted to **MILCOM 2026**) also note the practical trigger: reported Starlink pilot-tone observability has **degraded since 2023**, weakening the earlier pilot-tone-based approaches for low-gain passive receivers.

Their proposal is a transmitter-side **DSSS watermark superimposed on the existing downlink** rather than allocated as a separate DSSS channel. The watermark frame has three fields: a synchronisation field (48 symbols), a **public pseudonym / code-index field** (12 symbols) that names the satellite, and an **HMAC-SHA256 keyed authentication field** (68 symbols) that lets an authorised receiver verify the pseudonym was generated by a transmitter holding the key. Each symbol is spread over a 511-chip code at 400 kchips/s, giving a 163.5 ms frame. The receiver is *motion-aware*: it first removes the dominant LEO Doppler and Doppler-rate using TLE-derived range rate and range acceleration, then uses the beacon/pilot for a residual CFO estimate, then searches DSSS delay/frequency hypotheses only in a small neighbourhood — correlating coherently within short chip blocks and summing block magnitudes noncoherently to survive residual Doppler drift.

The evaluation is a conservative multi-satellite simulation, not a hardware capture: real Starlink TLEs via Skyfield give slant range, FSPL, delay, Doppler and Doppler drift at 11.325 GHz; a **Sionna RT scene of the RIT campus** supplies local multipath and blockage via a proxy transmitter placed along the satellite's TLE-derived azimuth/elevation. Eight satellites are co-observed in a 1 MHz band; the closest interferer sits about 77 microseconds (roughly 31 chips) from the candidate, so the test is asynchronous same-band overlap rather than a chip-aligned worst case. Crucially the watermark is **not** placed in a protected quiet subband — passive primary-waveform suppression is applied before DSSS acquisition.

Results establish a clear operating threshold. Below about **-37.5 dB** relative to the primary downlink, blind acquisition and keyed verification are unreliable; around the selected **-35 dB** point both rise sharply to near-unity, with low wrong-ID probability that is sustained as co-observed satellites are added. The spoofing sweep is the security headline: replayed/old-authentication, random-authentication and wrong-ID attacks produce **no spoof acceptance across the tested power range**, though a sufficiently strong spoofer can still deny acquisition of the legitimate watermark, and replay protection depends on validating the expected frame counter. Compatibility with legacy observables is quantified rather than asserted: at -35 dB the beacon amplitude bias is about **0.5 dB** and phase bias about **2 degrees**, while the projected distortion on the primary waveform is **-46.61 dB**, implying 0.00009 dB equivalent SNR loss and a 0.0013 dB total power change.

## Summary (ZH)

被动式低轨 PNT——把宽带卫星下行当作机会信号用于定位，而不依赖专用导航载荷——存在一个精度研究常常绕开的问题：**归属判定**。仅接收、低复杂度的接收机可以从信标或导频分量中提取多普勒或定时观测量，但在密集星座中同时有多颗卫星可见，其参考分量落在相邻的时频区域。接收机可能"看到"了某个特征，却说不清是哪颗卫星产生的，而导航解算恰恰需要这个答案。Nisanur Camuzcu 与 Alireza Vahid（罗切斯特理工学院，NSF 2348589 项目，已被 **MILCOM 2026** 录用）还指出了现实诱因：已有报道显示 Starlink 的导频音可观测性**自 2023 年起明显下降**，削弱了此前基于导频音的方法对低增益被动接收机的适用性。

他们的方案是在发射侧把 **DSSS 水印叠加在既有下行信号之上**，而非另辟一条独立的直扩信道。水印帧包含三个字段：同步字段（48 个符号）、命名卫星的**公开化名 / 码索引字段**（12 个符号），以及 **HMAC-SHA256 密钥认证字段**（68 个符号），使授权接收机能验证该化名确由持有密钥的发射机生成。每个符号以 400 kchips/s 的速率扩至 511 chip，整帧时长 163.5 ms。接收机是*运动感知*的：先用 TLE 推得的距离变化率与距离加速度消除主导的低轨多普勒及其变化率，再用信标/导频估计残余载频偏，随后只在小邻域内搜索直扩的时延/频率假设——在短 chip 块内做相干相关、块间做非相干幅度累加，以抵抗残余多普勒漂移。

评估是保守的多星仿真而非实测：通过 Skyfield 处理真实 Starlink TLE，给出 11.325 GHz 上的斜距、自由空间损耗、时延、多普勒与多普勒变化率；**Sionna RT 构建的 RIT 校园场景**沿卫星 TLE 方位/仰角方向放置代理发射机，提供本地多径与遮挡。8 颗卫星在 1 MHz 带内共视；最近的干扰源与目标星相距约 77 微秒（约 31 个 chip），因此该测试代表异步同频重叠而非 chip 对齐的最坏情形。关键在于水印**没有**被放进受保护的安静子带——直扩捕获前先做被动主波形抑制。

结果给出了清晰的工作门限。相对主下行低于约 **-37.5 dB** 时，盲捕获与密钥验证均不可靠；在选定的 **-35 dB** 工作点附近，两者迅速趋近于 1，且随共视卫星数增加仍保持低误识别概率。抗欺骗扫描是安全性方面的主要结论：重放/过期认证、随机认证与错误 ID 三类攻击在整个测试功率范围内**均未产生一次欺骗接受**；但足够强的欺骗信号仍可阻断合法水印的捕获，且重放防护依赖对预期帧计数器的校验。与既有观测量的兼容性是量化而非断言的：-35 dB 下信标幅度偏差约 **0.5 dB**、相位偏差约 **2 度**，而投射到主波形上的失真为 **-46.61 dB**，对应 0.00009 dB 的等效信噪比损失与 0.0013 dB 的总功率变化。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Concept**: "pseudonymetry" = embedding a recoverable, replaceable satellite identifier into the transmitted signal for passive attribution; here realised as a physical-layer DSSS watermark for LEO downlinks.
- **Frame structure**: N_sync = 48, N_ID = 12, N_auth = 68 (N_sym = 128 symbols); spreading L_c = 511 chips, chip rate 400 kchips/s, 6 samples/chip -> T_pkt = 163.5 ms. Watermark offset from the primary reference: 65.9 kHz (chosen to avoid the beacon/pilot comb).
- **Two-field split**: public DSSS field for open identification, HMAC-SHA256 field over {pseudonym, frame counter} for keyed verification by authorised receivers only. An attacker with the public codebook can imitate a pseudonym but cannot forge a valid tag.
- **Motion-aware receiver chain**: TLE-derived Doppler + Doppler-rate de-rotation -> beacon/pilot residual CFO estimate -> narrow DSSS delay/frequency search; coherent correlation within chip blocks, noncoherent magnitude accumulation across blocks.
- **Simulation setup**: Python 3.14; Starlink TLEs via Skyfield (slant range, FSPL, delay, Doppler, Doppler drift); Sionna RT campus scene for local LoS/NLoS multipath; carrier 11.325 GHz, 1 MHz observed band, Rx gain 35 dBi, NF 3 dB, 8 satellites; beacon at -25 dB and watermark at -35 dB relative to the primary downlink component.
- **Operating threshold**: unreliable below about -37.5 dB; near-unity acquisition and keyed verification at the -35 dB design point; wrong-ID probability stays low as the co-observed satellite count grows.
- **Attribution behaviour**: performance is governed by post-despreading candidate reliability — both candidate C/N0 and the candidate-to-other-watermark ratio drive acquisition, authentication-bit recovery and wrong-ID rate; an ID-margin metric (strongest vs second-strongest public-ID hypothesis) diagnoses the attribution.
- **Security envelope**: zero spoof acceptance for replayed/old-auth, random-auth and wrong-ID attacks across the tested spoof-to-reference power range; **but** strong interference or spoofing can still deny acquisition (no DoS protection), and replay protection requires frame-counter validation.
- **Legacy compatibility (quantified)**: beacon amplitude bias ~0.5 dB, phase bias ~2 degrees; projected primary-waveform distortion -46.61 dB => 0.00009 dB equivalent SNR loss, 0.0013 dB total power change. Transparent to receivers that never despread the watermark.
- **Scope honesty**: explicitly a transmitter-side enhancement for *future* LEO systems requiring operator participation — it is not part of current Starlink transmissions. Future work: shorter frames, smaller-aperture and bare-LNB receivers, ephemeris uncertainty, hardware-calibrated measurements.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **概念**："化名标记"（pseudonymetry）= 把可恢复、可更换的卫星标识嵌入发射信号以支持被动归属判定；此处实现为面向低轨下行的物理层直扩水印。
- **帧结构**：N_sync = 48、N_ID = 12、N_auth = 68（N_sym = 128 个符号）；扩频码长 L_c = 511 chip、码速率 400 kchips/s、每 chip 6 个采样点 -> 帧长 T_pkt = 163.5 ms。水印相对主参考的频偏为 65.9 kHz（避开信标/导频梳状谱）。
- **双字段划分**：公开直扩字段用于开放识别，HMAC-SHA256 字段对 {化名, 帧计数} 计算，仅授权接收机可做密钥验证。掌握公开码本的攻击者能模仿化名，却无法伪造有效认证标签。
- **运动感知接收链**：TLE 推算的多普勒与多普勒变化率去旋 -> 信标/导频残余载频偏估计 -> 窄范围直扩时延/频率搜索；块内相干相关，块间非相干幅度累加。
- **仿真设置**：Python 3.14；Skyfield 处理 Starlink TLE（斜距、自由空间损耗、时延、多普勒、多普勒变化率）；Sionna RT 校园场景提供本地 LoS/NLoS 多径；载频 11.325 GHz、观测带宽 1 MHz、接收增益 35 dBi、噪声系数 3 dB、8 颗卫星；信标与水印相对主下行分量分别为 -25 dB 与 -35 dB。
- **工作门限**：低于约 -37.5 dB 时不可靠；在 -35 dB 设计点上捕获与密钥验证概率接近 1；随共视卫星数增加，误识别概率仍保持低位。
- **归属行为**：性能由解扩后目标信号的可靠性主导——目标 C/N0 与"目标对其他水印"功率比共同决定捕获率、认证比特恢复率与误识别率；ID 裕量指标（最强与次强公开 ID 假设之差）可用于诊断归属过程。
- **安全边界**：在测试的欺骗功率范围内，重放/过期认证、随机认证与错误 ID 攻击的欺骗接受率均为零；**但**强干扰或强欺骗仍可阻断合法水印的捕获（不提供抗拒绝服务能力），且重放防护需要校验帧计数器。
- **与既有观测量的兼容性（量化）**：信标幅度偏差约 0.5 dB、相位偏差约 2 度；投射到主波形的失真 -46.61 dB，对应 0.00009 dB 等效信噪比损失与 0.0013 dB 总功率变化。对不解扩水印的接收机完全透明。
- **范围诚实性**：论文明确说明这是面向*未来*低轨系统的发射侧增强，需要运营商配合——当前 Starlink 发射中并不存在。后续工作：缩短帧长、评估小口径与裸 LNB 接收机、考虑星历不确定性、开展硬件标定实测。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The satellite bin's security thread has so far been about *detecting* attacks after the fact — GNSS spoofing detection (2026-07-28 VLM cross-check, 2026-08-20 SLM narratives), integrity monitoring over CORS networks (2026-08-12), RF fingerprint benchmarks (2026-08-20 IRISig), quantum SAR authentication (2026-08-21). This paper takes the other route: **change the transmitted signal so attribution becomes cheap and authentication becomes possible at all**, for a receiver that never demodulates the payload. Combined with the same-day fused-PNT beam-scheduling entry, it also draws a sharper picture of where LEO-PNT is heading — one paper argues the ranging signal should be budgeted as a first-class beam resource, the other argues it should carry a keyed identity.

Three details are worth remembering when judging later work in this thread. First, the **-35 dB / -37.5 dB cliff** is a concrete design number: the framework only works in a narrow band between "too weak to despread" and "loud enough to perturb the beacon". Second, the paper does not hide the residual: zero spoof *acceptance* is not zero *vulnerability*, because a strong spoofer still denies acquisition, and replay defence lives entirely in frame-counter discipline rather than in the physical layer. Third, the compatibility cost is reported as a measured number (-46.61 dB projected distortion, ~0.5 dB beacon amplitude bias), which is exactly the currency an operator would need to evaluate whether to add such a watermark — and the honest framing that current Starlink does not transmit one makes this a proposal to operators, not a claim about a deployed capability.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

卫星条目中的安全主线至今主要围绕*事后检测*：GNSS 欺骗检测（2026-07-28 视觉语言模型交叉校验、2026-08-20 语言模型驾驶叙事）、基于 CORS 网络的完好性监测（2026-08-12）、射频指纹基准（2026-08-20 IRISig）、量子 SAR 认证（2026-08-21）。本文走的是另一条路：**改造发射信号本身，使归属判定变得廉价、使认证成为可能**——而且面向的是从不解调载荷的接收机。与同日的融合 PNT 波束调度条目合看，低轨 PNT 的走向也更清晰了：一篇主张测距信号应作为一等波束资源纳入预算，另一篇主张它应当携带带密钥的身份。

在评判该主线的后续工作时，有三个细节值得记住。其一，**-35 dB / -37.5 dB 的悬崖**是一个具体的设计数字：整个方案只在"弱到无法解扩"与"强到扰动信标"之间的狭窄区间内成立。其二，论文并未掩饰残余风险：零欺骗*接受*不等于零*脆弱性*，因为强欺骗方仍可阻断捕获，而抗重放完全依赖帧计数器纪律而非物理层。其三，兼容性代价是以实测数字给出的（投射失真 -46.61 dB、信标幅度偏差约 0.5 dB），这恰是运营商评估是否值得加入此类水印所需的度量单位——而作者坦承当前 Starlink 并不发送这种水印，使本文成为面向运营商的提案，而非对既有能力的宣称。
