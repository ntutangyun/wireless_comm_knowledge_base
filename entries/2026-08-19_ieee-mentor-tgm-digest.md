## Summary (EN)
The only new 802.11 Mentor document past the previous watermark (11-26-1547) is DCN 11-26-1549-00, "TGmf-October-Adhoc-Meeting-Information", uploaded on 18 August 2026 by TGm chair Nikola Serafimovski. It announces an out-of-session ad hoc for the REVmf maintenance revision in October 2026 — the slot between the September interim and the November plenary — which fits the D3.0 timeline: the July plenary approved a recirculation letter ballot on REVmf D3.0 and started planning the mandatory draft review, and the September session is scheduled to begin resolving D3.0 comments (Timelines page, still stamped 2026-08-06, lists REVmf at D3.00 with the D2.0 ballot at 94 %). Separately, the TGbp AMP receive-procedure PDT (11-26-1547) received a third revision (r2) on 18 August from Lumin Liu (Huawei), continuing the pre-ballot consolidation noted in 2026-08-18_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest. No other task group (TGbn, TGbi, TGbq, TGbt, TGbr) posted a new DCN; the range 1550–1586 was empty at scan time.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
Purely a schedule signal: REVmf is on course to consolidate 802.11-2024 plus the be/bf/bk-era amendments, and an October ad hoc means the group expects a large D3.0 comment load and wants to reach SA-ballot formation before year-end (Timelines predicts SA formation around October). For UHR watchers, the REVmf baseline is what TGbn's D2.0 text is edited against, so its cadence matters indirectly. The between-sessions lull ahead of the September interim otherwise continues — TGbp remains the only group producing normative text.
