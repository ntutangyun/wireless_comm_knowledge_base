## Summary (EN)

At Cisco Live US 2026, Cisco announced a refresh of its campus portfolio — Catalyst 9550 400G core switches (6.4 Tbit/s, up to 96×50G + 4×400G ports), Catalyst 9350 multi-gigabit access switches with up to 4,320 W PoE budget, new 8100/8200/8300/8600 router series arriving September 2026 — and, on the wireless side, the model 9177 outdoor access point (reported by heise on June 4, 2026).

The 9177 is a Wi-Fi 7 outdoor AP in a 4×4 antenna configuration, offered with integrated omnidirectional or directional antennas or an external antenna connector. Its distinguishing feature is support for Ultra-Reliable Wireless Backhaul (URWB) — Cisco's fixed-wireless mesh technology (from the Fluidmesh acquisition) targeting low-latency, low-packet-loss links for critical outdoor systems such as industrial sites, transport, and large venues. Cisco's Campus Gateway scales the architecture to 200 APs and 64,000 clients.

Notably, no Wi-Fi 8 hardware was announced — Cisco's enterprise line stays on Wi-Fi 7 while consumer and silicon vendors seed early 802.11bn products. The article also notes a regional constraint: 6 GHz operation remains unavailable outdoors in Germany, so the 9177's 6 GHz radio is region-dependent — a live example of the outdoor 6 GHz regulatory split.
