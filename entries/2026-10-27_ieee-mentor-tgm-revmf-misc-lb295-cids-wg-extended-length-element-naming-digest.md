## Summary (EN)

Two documents with new content were uploaded on 26 October 2026 outside the TGbn comment-resolution stream. DCN 11-26-1975r1, "Proposed resolution for miscellaneous REVmf LB295 comments" (Edward Au, Huawei; TGm), proposes resolutions for 17 LB295 comment IDs on P802.11REVmf, with the first three (CIDs 2448, 2449, 2327) based on D3.0 and the rest on D3.1. R1 removes seven CIDs that were in R0 (2622, 2181, 2175, 2176, 2173, 2572, 2646). DCN 11-26-1928r1, "The naming of extended length element" (Jay Yang, ZTE; filed under the 802.11 WG group), is a seven-slide presentation (five content slides) on how to name the element type that the 11bt draft defines to carry larger fields such as the PQC parameter.

In 1975r1 the resolutions are mostly editorial or wording items, and the document records each as Accepted, Revised, Rejected or TBD. Examples: CIDs 2448 and 2449 (quotation-mark and punctuation placement in the MSCS text, subclause 11.25.3) are Revised to replace the quoted "Add"/"Change" phrasing with "the Request Type field indicating Add or Change"; CID 2327 (blank pages) is Revised to remove unnecessary blank pages globally and delete lines 36 to 38 of page 3690; CID 2380 (35.3.16) is Accepted, correcting "Operation Update Type subfield" to "Reconfiguration Operation Type subfield" in three places; CID 2313 (12.2.4) replaces "seven ways" with "the following ways" because the list has eight items; CID 2317 changes "per MLD setup" to "per ML setup"; CID 2180 (11.21.6.4.2) is reworded to "Only a successful FTM retransmission shall be counted towards the total number of FTMs per burst value"; CIDs 2167 to 2169 (Vendor Specific element naming and hyphenation) are Revised for the heading and Rejected for the body text on the stated ground that "Vendor Specific" is the element's name; CID 2563 ("HE sounding NDP" to "HE Sounding NDP", about 94 instances in D3.1) is Accepted; CIDs 2606 and 2656 are Rejected because the comments lack sufficient detail; CIDs 2170 and 2177 (Pseudo-Static capitalisation) are Revised; and CID 2683 (placement of KDK derivation) is marked TBD.

DCN 1928r1 states in its background slide that the 11bt draft defines "extended length element" as a new element type, with the PQC Parameter element as one example, that at least one comment was received during the initial WG ballot, and that the contribution aims at consensus in the Editors meeting. It then asks three questions, each with options: whether to hyphenate "extended-length" (Opt1 hyphen as a compound adjective; Opt2 reduce hyphen use because hyphens cause keyword-search problems in the draft), supported by the Editorial Style Guide (11-09/1034) examples "fixed-length" and "variable-length"; what form to use when the words are part of a field name ("Extended-Length", "Extended-length" or "Extended Length"); and whether the element type is written "Extended-Length element" or "extended-length element".
