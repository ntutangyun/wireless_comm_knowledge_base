## Summary (EN)

Between the 2026-09-05 watermark (DCN 11-26-1629) and 2026-09-07, three IEEE 802.11 groups uploaded new-DCN contributions: TGbp (Ambient Power Communication) posted one PDT on AMP service-period configuration carrying uplink access information; TGbq (Integrated Millimeter Wave) posted two PDTs — a MAC clause on BSS operation and an introduction subclause for IMMW beamforming; and TGm (REVmf maintenance) posted a comment-resolution document for eight miscellaneous CIDs, listed here for completeness but not treated as new technical content. A third row surfaced by the below-watermark late-upload check — DCN 11-26-1218r1 (TGbn, VESTEL) — is a revision of a feasibility-based MAPC AP group-formation proposal this KB already recorded at r0 in `2026-07-09_ieee-mentor-tgbn-preplenary-digest`; it carries no separately stated new content and is not counted as new.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

TGbp's contribution continues the AMP service-period thread this KB has tracked since `2026-07-28_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest` (HaiLa's DCN 1503 AMP Service Period PDT), alongside the WPT sub-thread Huawei opened with the End-Time PDT in the same digest and ZTE extended with the 2026-08-25 WPT setup/energizer-control pair; per the title it now carries uplink access information. The two TGbq documents are baseline clause text — a BSS-operation MAC clause and a beamforming introduction subclause — rather than the link-management behaviour this KB recorded earlier (link switching, P2P operation, cross-link Co-TWT, state transitions in `2026-08-13_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest`; a channel-access simulation revision in `2026-09-01_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest`). TGbq has not yet reached a numbered draft per the IEEE 802.11 Timelines page, so baseline-clause contributions of this kind are part of the group's earliest drafting activity.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

TGbp 的这份贡献延续了本知识库自 `2026-07-28_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest`（HaiLa 的 DCN 1503 AMP 服务周期 PDT）以来持续跟踪的 AMP 服务周期主线，与之并行的是华为在同一期摘要中以 End-Time PDT 开启、中兴于 2026-08-25 以 WPT 设置/激励器控制文档对扩展的 WPT 子线程；按标题所述，本文档将服务周期配置扩展为携带上行接入信息。TGbq 的两份文档均为基线条款文本——一份 BSS 操作 MAC 条款、一份波束成形引言子条款——而非本知识库此前记录的链路管理行为（`2026-08-13_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest` 中的链路切换、P2P 操作、跨链路 Co-TWT、状态转换；`2026-09-01_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest` 中的信道接入仿真修订）。根据 IEEE 802.11 Timelines 页面，TGbq 尚未形成编号草案，因此此类基线条款贡献属于该组最早期的起草活动。
