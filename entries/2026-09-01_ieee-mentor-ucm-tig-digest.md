## Summary (EN)
A group designation not previously seen in this KB's Mentor tracking — "UCM TIG" — uploaded its first document today: DCN 11-26-1578r0, "Accuracy Assessment and Validation of Sionna-Based Ray Tracing for Indoor Environments" (Murat Uysal, NYUAD Wireless Center). The document evaluates the accuracy of NVIDIA Sionna-based ray-tracing simulation against measured indoor propagation, positioning it as channel-modeling / simulation-validation work rather than a specific amendment's normative text. "UCM" most likely stands for a use-case-and-channel-modeling topic interest group; the exact charter is not yet confirmed from the public listing.
