## Summary (EN)

SatNews (4 Nov 2026), citing aerospace analyst Chris Forrester, reports that Russia has filed ITU documents for a new non-geostationary network named Rassvet-4. Per the ITU documentation as described, the network has 374 satellites in 17 orbital planes of 22 satellites each, in circular orbits at 800 km, with a multi-beam payload of "12 discrete transmission beams" in SatNews's wording (which conflicts with MediaSat's 7 transmit / 5 receive split, below) and reconfigurable regional coverage of Russian and Eurasian territory. SatNews describes the filing as advance-publication material, a required first step towards NGSO frequency registrations and coordination priority.

A second outlet, MediaSat (3 Nov 2026), gives more detail on the filing: it was submitted on 29 October by Russia's communications administration and appears in the ITU's "As Received" registry as an advance publication notice (RUS2026-85633) and a coordination request (RUS2026-85634). It says each satellite has 12 beams, seven for transmission and five for reception, and that the operating frequencies were updated but not stated in the article. Neither article lists frequency bands.

Bureau 1440 has not officially announced Rassvet-4 or scheduled launches. SatNews recounts that Bureau 1440 launched 32 Rassvet-3 satellites in two Soyuz missions (16 each, on 23 March and 19 July); two suffered uncontrolled altitude loss and re-entered, and the remaining 30 had not reached the intended 800 km altitude. Unnamed observers quoted there view Rassvet-4 as an upgraded design addressing the earlier propulsion and orbit-raising problems; MediaSat quotes Defense Express experts who read the filing as pointing to Rassvet-4 tests alongside Rassvet-3 as a single system. Both are interpretations, not official statements.

## Summary (ZH)

SatNews（2026 年 11 月 4 日）援引航天分析师 Chris Forrester 报道，俄罗斯已就一个名为 Rassvet-4 的新非静止轨道网络向 ITU 提交文件。按所述 ITU 文件，该网络共 374 颗卫星，分布于 17 个轨道面、每面 22 颗，为 800 km 圆轨道，采用多波束载荷（SatNews 称为“12 个离散发射波束”，与下文 MediaSat 的 7 发 5 收划分相冲突），区域覆盖可重构，覆盖俄罗斯与欧亚地区。SatNews 称该文件属于提前公布（advance publication）资料，是取得 NGSO 频率登记与协调优先权的必要第一步。

另一家媒体 MediaSat（2026 年 11 月 3 日）给出更多细节：文件由俄罗斯通信主管部门于 10 月 29 日提交，在 ITU“已收到”（As Received）登记中显示为提前公布通知（RUS2026-85633）和协调请求（RUS2026-85634）。该文称每颗卫星有 12 个波束，其中 7 个用于发射、5 个用于接收，工作频率已更新但文中未给出。两篇文章均未列出频段。

Bureau 1440 尚未正式宣布 Rassvet-4，也未安排发射。SatNews 回顾称，Bureau 1440 通过两次联盟号任务发射了 32 颗 Rassvet-3 卫星（3 月 23 日与 7 月 19 日各 16 颗）；其中两颗发生不受控的高度损失并再入，其余 30 颗尚未到达预定的 800 km 高度。该文引述的匿名观察人士认为 Rassvet-4 是针对此前推进与轨道抬升问题的升级设计；MediaSat 引述 Defense Express 专家，认为该文件更像是 Rassvet-4 将与 Rassvet-3 作为单一系统开展测试的信号。二者均为解读，并非官方表态。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Constellation: 374 satellites, 17 planes x 22, circular 800 km (SatNews; MediaSat gives the same plane and altitude figures).
- Payload: SatNews says "12 discrete transmission beams", which conflicts with MediaSat's 12 beams per satellite split 7 transmit / 5 receive; reconfigurable regional coverage over Russia and Eurasia.
- Filing: submitted 29 Oct 2026 (MediaSat); advance publication notice RUS2026-85633 and coordination request RUS2026-85634; shown in the ITU "As Received" registry, i.e. before review and official publication.
- Not given in either article: frequency bands, ITU publication date, launch schedule.
- Status: not officially announced by Bureau 1440; interpretation of purpose (upgrade of Rassvet-3) comes from unnamed observers and Defense Express experts.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
