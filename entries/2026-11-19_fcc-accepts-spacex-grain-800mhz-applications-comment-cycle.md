## Summary (EN)

Total Telecom (2026-11-11) reports that the FCC has accepted as valid, and opened for public comment, SpaceX's application to acquire Grain Management's 800 MHz spectrum, which SpaceX agreed to buy in October 2026 (see 2026-10-13_spacex-grain-800mhz-definitive-agreement). The article says the portfolio comprises 546 paired spectrum licenses covering roughly 100% of the U.S. population, valued at roughly $8 billion based on estimates it cites. Acceptance is not approval: comments and petitions to deny are due 2026-12-01, oppositions 2026-12-08, and the FCC decides afterwards. The article lists deployment obligations that SpaceX would inherit from Grain: a terrestrial option with LTE coverage targets in 2029 and 2034, and a satellite option, the route it says Starlink would use, with quality, uplink-throughput and spectral-efficiency benchmarks in 2029, 2031 and 2036. It describes 800 MHz as lower-frequency than Starlink's existing 2 GHz mid-band spectrum, with better propagation and potential indoor penetration, and notes that actual direct-to-device performance depends on satellite power, antenna characteristics and link budget. A web search additionally returned reports citing the bureau's public notice as DA 26-1218 in WT Docket 26-313 and a notice date of 2026-11-10; those identifiers are not in the Total Telecom article and are from secondary reports.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Application:** transfer of 546 paired 800 MHz licenses (about 100% of U.S. population) from Grain Management to SpaceX; accepted for filing, not approved.
- **Calendar:** comments and petitions to deny 2026-12-01; oppositions 2026-12-08; FCC decision after that.
- **Inherited obligations (per the article):** terrestrial option, LTE coverage targets 2029 and 2034; satellite option, quality / uplink-throughput / spectral-efficiency benchmarks 2029, 2031, 2036.
- **Technical framing (per the article):** 800 MHz offers better propagation and potential indoor penetration than the 2 GHz band; D2D performance depends on satellite power, antenna and link budget.
- **Identifiers from secondary reports only:** DA 26-1218, WT Docket 26-313.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The earlier entry recorded the definitive agreement; this one records the first formal FCC step on it, the acceptance for filing and the opening of the comment cycle. The article also supplies the inherited-obligation structure (terrestrial LTE targets versus satellite performance benchmarks), which the agreement entry did not carry. Approval, and the separate pending request for satellite use of these frequencies mentioned in secondary reports, remain open.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

此前条目记录的是最终协议；本条目记录 FCC 对此的首个正式程序步骤，即受理申请并开启评论期。文章还给出继承义务的结构（地面 LTE 目标与卫星性能基准），这在协议条目中没有。批准结果，以及二手报道提到的、另行待决的这些频率的卫星使用申请，仍未定。
