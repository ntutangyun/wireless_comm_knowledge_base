## Summary (EN)

Korea's Electronics and Telecommunications Research Institute (ETRI) — the national research institute that carries most of the country's 6G programme under the Ministry of Science and ICT (MSIT) — announced on 6 January 2026 that it had completed an AI-based Radio Access Network (AI-RAN) technology stack intended as the foundation platform for 6G, in which artificial intelligence autonomously controls and optimises the radio system rather than assisting a conventional one.

The headline component is a *neural receiver*: instead of the classical chain of channel estimation, equalisation and demodulation built on statistical models, a learned receiver restores the wireless signal directly from the received samples and detects errors immediately. ETRI reports roughly 18% better data-recovery accuracy, about 15% better channel-prediction accuracy, and a 30% reduction in data-loss rate against its conventional baselines, with the overall platform claimed to reach up to ten times the transmission efficiency of 5G. The stated scope is deliberately end-to-end — AI applied across transmission, network control and edge computing, rather than a single learned block dropped into an otherwise conventional PHY.

ETRI frames the work as much as a standards-position play as a technology result. Alongside the platform it reports 119 domestic and international IP filings, 17 SCI journal papers, and 68 technical contributions submitted to 3GPP of which 12 were adopted, with claimed leadership positions in the AI/ML-based wireless interface and AI-based mobility management work items. That contribution count is the part of the announcement that is externally checkable over time, and it is the mechanism by which a national research institute — as opposed to a vendor — converts a lab platform into standardised behaviour.

## Summary (ZH)

韩国电子通信研究院（ETRI）——在科学技术信息通信部（MSIT）之下承担该国大部分 6G 计划的国家级研究机构——于 2026 年 1 月 6 日宣布，已完成拟作为 6G 基础平台的 AI-RAN（基于人工智能的无线接入网）技术栈：由人工智能自主控制并优化无线系统，而非仅为传统系统提供辅助。

核心组件是**神经接收机**：不再采用基于统计模型的信道估计—均衡—解调经典链路，而由学习型接收机直接从接收样本恢复无线信号并即时检测错误。ETRI 报告称，相对其常规基线，数据恢复准确率提升约 18%、信道预测准确率提升约 15%、数据丢失率降低 30%，整体平台宣称可达 5G 的最高十倍传输效率。其声明的范围刻意覆盖端到端——AI 贯穿传输、网络控制与边缘计算，而非在传统 PHY 中嵌入单一学习模块。

ETRI 把这项工作同时定位为标准话语权的布局，而不只是技术成果。除平台外，其报告了国内外知识产权申请 119 项、SCI 论文 17 篇、向 3GPP 提交技术提案 68 篇（其中 12 篇被采纳），并宣称在"基于 AI/ML 的无线接口"与"基于 AI 的移动性管理"课题上取得主导地位。提案数量是该公告中随时间可外部核验的部分，也是国家研究机构（区别于厂商）把实验室平台转化为标准化行为的机制。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The cellular-ai bin in this KB has grown mostly from academic and vendor material: foundation-model surveys, RAN-control agents, CSI-feedback learnware, the AI-RAN platform launches from Nokia and Ericsson, and the two-sided-model testbed work that argues over what may cross the UE-gNB boundary. Missing from all of it was the national-research-institute layer, which behaves differently from both. ETRI does not have a product to ship or a paper's narrow claim to defend; its output is a platform plus a contribution count, and the contribution count is what actually moves 3GPP. Reading "68 submitted, 12 adopted" next to the vendor launches gives a rough sense of how much of the AI air-interface agenda is being written from Daejeon rather than from Espoo or Stockholm.

The neural-receiver framing is also worth registering on its own. Most of the KB's AI air-interface material concerns the *two-sided* problem — paired UE and gNB models, and the multi-vendor question of who trains what — because that is where 3GPP's hardest disagreements sit. A neural receiver is the one-sided case: it lives entirely at the receiver, needs no cross-vendor model exchange, and therefore can be deployed without settling any of the collaboration-type arguments. That makes it the most likely first AI air-interface feature to reach real equipment, and ETRI's numbers give a first public reference point for what the one-sided case buys.

Caveats are substantial. Every figure is self-reported with no disclosed measurement setup, no band or bandwidth, and no stated baseline beyond "conventional"; the "10x transmission efficiency of 5G" platform claim in particular is unverifiable as stated and should be treated as a programme aspiration rather than a measurement. Watch for the corresponding 3GPP contributions to surface in Release 21 AI/ML air-interface documentation, for Korea's announced 2026 6G pilot to produce field figures, and for whether the neural receiver appears in any commercial Samsung or Korean-operator trial.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

本库 cellular-ai 栏目主要由学术与厂商材料构成：基础模型综述、RAN 控制智能体、CSI 反馈 learnware、诺基亚与爱立信的 AI-RAN 平台发布，以及争论"UE-gNB 边界上可传递什么"的双侧模型测试床工作。其中一直缺失的是国家研究机构这一层——它与前两者的行为逻辑都不同。ETRI 既无产品要出货，也无单篇论文的狭窄论点要辩护；其产出是"平台 + 提案数"，而真正推动 3GPP 的正是提案数。把"提交 68、采纳 12"与厂商发布并读，可粗略感知 AI 空口议程中有多少是从大田而非埃斯波或斯德哥尔摩写出来的。

神经接收机这一定位本身也值得记录。本库多数 AI 空口材料涉及**双侧**问题——UE 与 gNB 成对模型，以及"谁训练什么"的多厂商难题——因为 3GPP 最难的分歧就在那里。神经接收机则是单侧情形：完全位于接收端，无需跨厂商模型交换，因此无需先解决任何协作类型之争即可部署。这使它最有可能成为首个进入真实设备的 AI 空口特性，而 ETRI 的数字给出了单侧方案收益的首个公开参考点。

保留意见相当大。所有数字均为自报，未披露测量配置、频段带宽，基线仅称"常规"；尤其"5G 的 10 倍传输效率"这一平台级宣称按其表述无法核验，应视为计划愿景而非实测。关注对应的 3GPP 提案是否出现在 Release 21 AI/ML 空口文档中、韩国既定的 2026 年 6G 试点是否产出外场数据，以及神经接收机是否出现在三星或韩国运营商的商用试验中。
