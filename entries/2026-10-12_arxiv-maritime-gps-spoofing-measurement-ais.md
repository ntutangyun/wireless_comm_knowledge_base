## Summary (EN)

Raymaker, Von Brock, Pickren, Chhotaray, Li, Zonouz and Beyah present what the paper describes as "the first large-scale measurement study of maritime GPS spoofing," using global Automatic Identification System (AIS) vessel-tracking data, submitted 2026-09-29 and accepted at IEEE S&P 2027. Analyzing vessel data from late November 2024 through early February 2025, the authors identify 31 persistent anomalous location hotspots across high-traffic maritime regions worldwide, of which at least 22 show strong evidence of GPS spoofing. Among the findings: the authors detected spoofing activity in the Red Sea months before the container ship MSC Antonia grounded there, and found persistent spoofing activity in the Strait of Hormuz. The paper's framing is that GPS spoofing is a widespread, recurring, and measurable threat to global maritime navigation.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Method**: large-scale measurement using global AIS vessel-tracking data from over 367,000 vessels.
- **Window analyzed**: late November 2024 through early February 2025.
- **Scale of findings**: 31 persistent anomalous hotspots across high-traffic maritime regions globally; ≥22 with strong spoofing evidence.
- **Named incidents**: Red Sea spoofing activity detected months before the MSC Antonia grounding; persistent spoofing in the Strait of Hormuz.
- **Paper's own claim**: "first large-scale measurement study of maritime GPS spoofing" using AIS data — relayed as the authors' claim.
- **Venue**: accepted at IEEE S&P 2027.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **方法**：基于逾 367,000 艘船舶的全球 AIS 船舶跟踪数据的大规模测量。
- **分析时间窗口**：2024 年 11 月下旬至 2025 年 2 月初。
- **发现规模**：全球高流量海运区域中 31 个持续性异常热点；其中至少 22 个具有明确的欺骗证据。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This KB's GNSS/GPS-spoofing entries have so far covered detection mechanisms (often UAV-, driving-, or receiver-focused) rather than a ground-truth measurement of how often and where spoofing actually occurs at scale. This paper supplies the empirical, AIS-measured counterpart — real-world hotspot locations and an incident (MSC Antonia) tied to detected spoofing activity — rather than a new detection algorithm, giving this bin its first entry on spoofing prevalence/impact rather than countermeasure design.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

本知识库此前的 GNSS/GPS 欺骗相关条目主要关注检测机制（多聚焦于无人机、驾驶场景或接收机侧），而非对欺骗在实际规模下发生的频率与地点进行真实测量。本论文提供了基于 AIS 数据的实证测量结果——真实世界的热点位置，以及与已检测欺骗活动相关联的具体事件（MSC Antonia）——而非提出新的检测算法，为该主题分组首次带来了关于欺骗普遍性/影响本身的条目，而不是对策设计。
