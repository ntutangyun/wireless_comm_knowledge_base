## Summary (EN)

Eutelsat awarded Airbus Defence and Space a contract for 340 additional OneWeb low-Earth-orbit satellites (announced January 15, 2026), on top of the 100-satellite batch ordered in December 2024 — 440 next-generation units in total, taking the expanded OneWeb constellation to roughly 1,040 satellites. The satellites will be manufactured in Toulouse, France, with deliveries starting at the end of 2026, replacing earlier tranches progressively to preserve service continuity. Eutelsat separately secured an ~€1bn export-credit-agency-backed financing agreement supporting the LEO procurement.

The next-generation satellites carry advanced digital channelizers for onboard processing, and the replacement generation is slated to add 5G integration, higher throughput, and compatibility with the EU's IRIS² sovereign-connectivity programme. Eutelsat is also evaluating hosted-payload opportunities on the new platform. OneWeb currently operates 600+ satellites in 12 synchronized orbital planes at ~1,200 km — one of only two fully operational European-managed LEO networks — and positions itself on "sovereign-grade connectivity" for government customers.

Strategically, this is Europe's answer to the scale race: OneWeb's Gen-1 constellation was capacity-limited by design (bent-pipe, ~600 satellites), and the 1,040-satellite next-gen fleet with onboard processing and IRIS² alignment is what keeps a European LEO option credible against Starlink (10,000+ satellites) and Amazon Leo. The OneWeb business grew ~80% in 2025, while Eutelsat's legacy GEO business declines — the LEO segment is now the group's growth engine.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the KB's first OneWeb/Eutelsat entry, anchoring the European side of the LEO constellation race that the KB has so far tracked through Starlink and Amazon Leo. The technically meaningful shift is architectural: Gen-1 OneWeb is a bent-pipe design, and the next-gen order brings onboard processing (digital channelizers), 5G/NTN alignment, and IRIS² hooks — the same direction of travel as Starlink Gen2 and 3GPP NTN. The end-2026 delivery start makes this a thread to watch through 2027–2028 deployment.
