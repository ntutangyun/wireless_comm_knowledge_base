## Summary (EN)

The IEEE 802.11 Working Group's official Timelines page carries a fresh stamp for the first time since 2026-08-06, and records that TGbn's current Working Group Letter Ballot on draft D2.0 stands at **77% approval as of 2026-09-02**, up from the 61% recorded on the initial D1.0 letter ballot (2025-10-06). The page still lists TGbn's Sponsor Ballot as not yet initiated. Corroborating activity on IEEE Mentor: DCN 11-26-1613, "IEEE 802.11bn LB296 comments on D2.0," uploaded 2026-09-03 by Ross Jian Yu (Huawei), is a comment spreadsheet against this same letter ballot (numbered LB296), indicating the group is now in the comment-submission phase of this recirculation round. The Timelines page's other task-group entries are otherwise unchanged from the prior stamp: TGbi's D6.0 Standards Association ballot remains recorded at 94% (2026-08-22), and TGmf's D3.0 Working Group Letter Ballot is recorded at 95% (2026-08-31), a one-point increase over the 94% figure carried in this KB's most recent REVmf entry.
