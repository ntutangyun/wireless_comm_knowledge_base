## Summary (EN)
Cambium Networks announced on 18 August 2026 that its ePMP 4000 series of point-to-multipoint (PtMP) fixed-wireless radios has passed two shipment milestones: more than 220,000 radios shipped to service providers globally since the product line launched, and — the headline for the 6 GHz story — more than 50,000 6 GHz radios shipped to service providers in North America. The ePMP 4000 platform is built on Wi-Fi 6-generation silicon adapted with Cambium's proprietary PtMP scheduling for WISP (wireless ISP) access networks, and its 6 GHz variants operate under the FCC's standard-power unlicensed regime with AFC coordination.

The announcement follows the release of ePMP Software 5.12, a free field upgrade that improves performance of already-deployed radios and, per Cambium, gives operators a lower-cost approach to expanding coverage in low-density areas.

For the KB's spectrum-policy thread, the number is the point: 50,000 standard-power 6 GHz radios in North American WISP networks is one of the first concrete, vendor-verified deployment-scale figures for the AFC-coordinated 6 GHz regime — evidence that the framework whose regulatory construction the KB has tracked across the US, UK, South Africa and Costa Rica is carrying commercial traffic at volume, not just certification tests.
