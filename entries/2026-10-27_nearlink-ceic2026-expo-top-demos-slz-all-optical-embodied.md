## Summary (EN)

The International SparkLink Alliance's own recap of the SparkLink ecosystem expo held with the 2026 industry summit (closed 24 October 2026, Shenzhen) says more than 30 partners showed hundreds of exhibits under the theme "SparkLink AI - connecting everything". The article organises the show in four parts; its first part lists six "Top" exhibits, each with measured or claimed figures.

(1) SparkLink positioning on Huawei Petal Maps: positioning anchors were deployed across all booths so that Huawei phone users could scan a code and get navigation from the entrance to a chosen booth; the article gives 1-2 m accuracy and says it exceeds Bluetooth/Wi-Fi indoor positioning, with an unlimited number of access devices and maintenance-free deployment. (2) Four embodied-AI zones: an unmanned-retail zone in which three robots share one "brain" through SparkLink to a compute server, with trailing latency under 30 ms under multi-device concurrency; a household-task zone where a unified world model (WALL-B) runs on an edge workstation, with a stated task success rate above 90%; an immersive teleoperation zone with VR-headset video and millisecond-level motion sync; and a smart robot factory in which robots talk to an edge Ascend compute node, with end-to-end inference-plus-transmission latency held at a stable 100 ms and a "brain/cerebellum separation" architecture. (3) All-optical SparkLink, which combines the fibre-side deterministic time-division mechanism with SparkLink wireless determinism; with the SLB 2.0 air interface it reports, in the show-floor interference environment, 99%-probability trailing latency below 10 ms across node handover ("zero-interruption handover"). (4) The SparkLink 3.0 passive SLZ mode, shown with a passive temperature-sensing tag from Zongjiyuan: no battery or capacitor, microwave-powered, system response under 1 ms (about 100 ms for conventional RFID per the article), 30 m sensing range, +/-0.1 degC multi-tag accuracy and up to 3000 tags/s; already deployed in power-grid and generator settings. (5) A 4K live-video demo with partner KuVision's codec at 20 ms end-to-end, with inter-camera synchronisation for multi-angle capture. (6) A distributed multi-channel PA for the awards ceremony of the third SparkLink-HarmonyOS Cup: the article states 90% less cabling and 70% less deployment time, multiple lossless audio channels per access point, and an 8-element microphone array with more than 5 m pickup distance and more than 20 dB directivity suppression.

## Summary (ZH)

国际星闪联盟自家新闻稿回顾了随2026年产业峰会同期举办的星闪生态创新展（10月24日落幕，深圳），称30余家生态伙伴参展、数百件展品，主题“星闪AI · 智合万物”。文章分四部分，第一部分列出六大“Top级”展项，并给出实测或宣称数据。

（1）星闪定位花瓣导航：在华为花瓣地图上叠加星闪室内高精定位，展会所有展位部署定位锚点，华为手机用户扫码即可导航到目标展位；文章给出定位精度1～2米，称较传统蓝牙、WiFi室内定位大幅提升，并具备不限接入数量、部署快速、免维护等特点。（2）具身智能四个展区：无人智慧零售展区三台机器人共用一个“大脑”，经星闪连接算力服务器，多设备并发下拖尾时延小于30ms；具身家政展区将统一世界模型WALL-B部署在边缘工作站，任务成功率超过90%；机器人沉浸式遥操作展区实现VR眼镜视频回传与毫秒级动作同步；智慧机器人工厂展区中机器人与边缘昇腾计算节点经星闪通信，端到端推理+传输时延稳定在100ms，采用“大脑小脑分离”架构。（3）全光星闪：结合光纤有线的时分确定性机制与星闪无线确定性，配合星闪宽带2.0空口，在展会复杂干扰环境下，跨节点切换时99%概率拖尾传输时延低于10毫秒，称实现切换零中断。（4）星闪3.0无源SLZ：以纵激元的纯无源温度传感标签展示——无需电池与电容、微波供能，系统响应时间小于1毫秒（文中称传统RFID约100毫秒），传感覆盖30米，多标签温度精度±0.1℃，速率最高3000PCS/秒，已落地电网、发电机等能源场景。（5）与合作伙伴KuVision的编解码技术结合的4K直播，端到端时延低至20ms，并支持多机位相机间高精同步。（6）第三届“星闪鸿蒙杯”总决赛颁奖会场的分布式多通道扩声系统：文章称布线量减少90%、部署时间缩短70%，单个接入点支持多路无损音频通道，8单元麦克风阵列拾音距离大于5米、指向性抑制增益超过20dB。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Positioning: 1-2 m accuracy via anchors at every booth, layered on Huawei Petal Maps; claimed unlimited number of connected devices.
- Embodied AI: shared-compute robots over SparkLink, trailing latency under 30 ms (multi-node concurrency); edge-hosted world model with success rate above 90%; 100 ms end-to-end inference-plus-transmission at the edge-compute factory demo.
- All-optical SparkLink: fibre time-division determinism plus SLB 2.0 air interface; 99%-probability trailing latency under 10 ms across node handover on the show floor.
- Passive SLZ (SparkLink 3.0): batteryless microwave-powered tags, under 1 ms response, 30 m range, +/-0.1 degC, up to 3000 tags/s, resistant to metal and industrial interference (exhibitor figures).
- Live video: 4K at 20 ms end-to-end with a partner codec; synchronised multi-camera capture.
- Audio PA: lossless multi-channel audio from one access point; cabling reduced 90%, deployment time 70% (as stated).
- All figures are from the organiser's recap; test conditions and baselines are not given.
