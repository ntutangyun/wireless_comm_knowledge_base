## Summary (EN)

SatNews (23 November 2026) reports that Elveo Mobile, a direct-to-device (D2D) satellite operator, and Smart Communications, a subsidiary of PLDT Inc., announced completion of trials of live D2D satellite connectivity along the maritime corridor between Batangas Port and Calapan City in Oriental Mindoro, Philippines. The tests ran on sea cargo vessels crossing a busy inter-island route in Southern Luzon.

Per the article, standard LTE smartphones with no external antennas or special terminal hardware connected directly to Elveo Mobile's low Earth orbit satellites. Participants sent text messages from the vessels to contacts in Manila, devices received emergency broadcast alerts, and users loaded official Smart and TNT websites over mobile data.
