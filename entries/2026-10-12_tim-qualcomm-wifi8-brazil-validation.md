## Summary (EN)

On 30 September 2026, Brazilian operator TIM and Qualcomm announced a joint demonstration of Wi-Fi 8 (based on the still-under-development IEEE 802.11bn specification) at TIM Lab, the operator's laboratory in Rio de Janeiro. Teletime's coverage of the announcement frames Wi-Fi 8 as emphasizing Ultra High Reliability rather than the raw-speed increases that characterized earlier generational transitions, and describes the tested capabilities as covering interference management, in-home coverage improvements, and spectrum efficiency. A second source, minhaoperadora.com.br (2026-10-01), adds that the demonstration used a home gateway unit (HGU) built on Qualcomm's Dragonwing F8 or Dragonwing FiberPro A8 Elite platforms, combining the 802.11bn draft standard with XGS-PON fiber access, AI-assisted network operations, an added cybersecurity layer, and Dynamic Subband Operation (DSO, plus Qualcomm's proprietary "DSO Pro" variant) for resource allocation, and that TIM characterized the event as a technology-validation exercise rather than a commercial launch, with Qualcomm and TIM stating they are evaluating next steps toward validating the technology in real residential environments to analyze the performance of the new features across different home scenarios.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Venue/date**: TIM Lab, Rio de Janeiro; announced 30 September 2026.
- **Hardware** (per minhaoperadora.com.br, 2026-10-01, not stated in the cited Teletime article): home gateway unit on Qualcomm Dragonwing F8 or Dragonwing FiberPro A8 Elite platforms.
