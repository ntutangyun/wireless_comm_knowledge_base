## Summary (EN)

Shenzhen News Network reported on 23 September 2026 on the press conference held on 22 September for CEIC2026 (Consumer Electronics Innovation Congress), which opens at the Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center (Futian) on 22 October. Zeng Guosong, Secretary-General of the International SparkLink Alliance, said that as AI agents and embodied-intelligence devices grow, the underlying wireless connectivity needs upgrading for consumer electronics to become smarter.

According to the report, at the 2026 SparkLink industry summit and ecosystem innovation expo the alliance will publicly present, for the first time, its core technology plan for "SparkLink 3.0 for the AI era", and will launch a SparkLink connectivity kit for AI agents and embodied-intelligence devices, described as offering low latency and integrated communication, positioning and sensing. The summit will also present the full results of SparkLink and OpenHarmony cross-ecosystem integration, again described as a first.

Yang Tao, Deputy Director of the CEIC Organizing Committee, said five mainstream chipmakers will release SparkLink 2.0 chips together at the event, and that a plaque will be awarded to the Beijing Chip IP OpenLab. The third "SparkLink HarmonyOS Cup" application challenge finals will be held at the expo as a special event.
