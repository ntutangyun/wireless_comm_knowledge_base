## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-17, the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) held a "SparkLink Smart Healthcare Seminar" at Huawei's Beijing Research Institute, reported on the alliance's own news feed (navId=33, item id 945, published 2026-09-22). Chinese Academy of Engineering academician Wu Hequan (邬贺铨) and other industry experts attended. The meeting was chaired in two halves by Li Man (李曼), director of the Cloud/Big-Data Institute at CAICT (中国信通院云大所), and Hong Lei (洪磊), executive deputy director of the Medical & Wellness Robotics branch of the China Mechatronics Technology Application Association (中国机电一体化技术应用协会).

The headline outcome is the announcement that iSLA is beginning preparatory work on two new organizational bodies: a **SparkLink Smart Healthcare Special Interest Group (星闪智慧医疗SIG)**, to be led by CAICT and formally announced in October 2026, and a **SparkLink Medical Care & Wellness Expert Subcommittee (星闪医疗康养专家分委会)**, to sit under the alliance's existing expert committee. The SIG's planned scope covers wireless access for medical terminals, interconnection of medical devices/systems, and medical-space sensing, with a work programme spanning regulatory support, industry research, standards development, test/validation and application promotion.

Presenters gave several data points and use-case requirements. Huawei's SparkLink project manager Yang Changqing (杨常青) stated that iSLA membership has passed 1,250 organizations, with 150+ developer partners, cumulative chip shipments over 380 million units, and 880 products test-listed as of end-August 2026, across the alliance's SLB/SLE/SLP/SLZ multi-mode technology stack. CAICT's Dr. Wang Dan (王丹) framed the opportunity around China's ongoing medical-equipment renewal and primary-healthcare-strengthening policy programmes. Wang Meng (王萌), general manager of Guodong Qinda Intelligent Technology (国东勤达), said SparkLink e-paper bedside cards, footboard cards and door cards are already in mass production (extending the Hong Kong hospital deployment covered in this KB's 2026-09-11 entry) and that SLE testing shows faster downlink, longer range and higher reliability than BLE. Weigao Huasheng Medical's Zou Lianghui (邹粮徽) listed four target wireless-medical scenarios: operating rooms/endoscopy centers, ICU/life-support equipment, smart wards with wearable monitoring, and medical-gas monitoring. Xie Le (谢叻), deputy director of Shanghai Jiao Tong University's Medical Robotics Institute, said medical robots need fast transmission and high positioning accuracy — centimeter-level for rehabilitation exoskeletons and lower-limb walking aids, and millimeter/sub-millimeter-level for surgical navigation and vascular-suturing robots.

A closing roundtable brought together officials from the National Health Commission's Science & Technology Research Institute, the National Rehabilitation Aids Research Center, Shanghai Jiao Tong's robotics institute, and Huawei's medical business unit, alongside representatives from device, chip-module, industrial-network, e-paper and test/certification vendors. Tang Wenbin (NHC S&T Research Institute) urged standards-first development compatible with existing hospital interoperability and rating standards; Li Zengyong (National Rehabilitation Aids Research Center) proposed prioritising elder-care vital-sign monitoring networks, then home/community rehabilitation devices; Xie Le proposed prioritising validation of positioning and multi-device coexistence in operating rooms and offered a surgical-robot platform for SparkLink trials. The one point of stated consensus was that SparkLink's technical advantages align well with smart-healthcare needs. iSLA vice-chairman and Huawei Fellow Wan Lei (万蕾) gave closing remarks alongside academician Wu Hequan, who stressed that healthcare imposes stricter bandwidth/latency/synchronization/reliability/privacy requirements than other verticals, and that hospital validation cycles are long because patient safety is paramount.

## Summary (ZH)

2026年9月17日，国际星闪联盟（iSLA）在华为北京研究所举行了"星闪智慧医疗研讨会"，相关报道刊登于联盟自有资讯栏目（navId=33，条目 id 945，发布于2026-09-22）。中国工程院院士邬贺铨及多位业界专家出席。会议分设上下半场，分别由中国信通院云大所主任李曼、中国机电一体化技术应用协会医疗与康养机器人分会常务副主任委员洪磊主持。

本次会议的核心成果是宣布iSLA将启动两个新组织机构的筹建：由中国信通院牵头、拟于2026年10月正式宣布成立的**星闪智慧医疗特别兴趣组（SIG）**，以及拟在联盟专家委下设立的**星闪医疗康养专家分委会**。SIG的规划范围涵盖医疗终端无线接入、医疗设备/系统互联、医疗空间感知等专题，工作内容包括监管支撑、产业研究、标准研制、测试验证和应用推广。

多位发言人给出了具体数据与场景需求。华为星闪项目经理杨常青介绍，国际星闪联盟成员单位已突破1250家，开发者伙伴150余家，芯片累计出货量超3.8亿片，截至2026年8月底已有880款产品通过测试列名，技术体系覆盖SLB、SLE、SLP、SLZ等多模。中国信通院云大所王丹博士从政策角度分析，随着医疗卫生领域大规模设备更新、医疗卫生强基工程等政策推进，无线短距通信正在加速融入专业医疗设备与智慧医院。国东勤达总经理王萌表示，星闪电子纸床头卡、床尾卡、房门卡已实现量产（延续了本知识库2026-09-11条目所记录的香港医院部署），测试数据显示SLE较BLE下行更快、覆盖更远、可靠性更高。威高化生医疗的邹粮徽列出四大医疗无线化典型场景：手术室与内镜中心、ICU与生命支持设备、智慧病房与穿戴监测、医用气体监控与运维。上海交通大学医疗机器人研究院副院长谢叻指出，医疗机器人对星闪的核心诉求是传输快、定位准：康复机器人下肢行走需要厘米级定位，手术导航、血管缝合等场景需要毫米级/亚毫米级精度。

会议闭幕圆桌汇聚了国家卫健委科学技术研究所、国家康复辅助器具研究中心、上海交大医疗机器人研究院、华为医疗军团的代表，以及来自设备、芯片模组、工业网络、电子纸、测试认证等企业的与会者。国家卫健委科学技术研究所唐文斌建议标准先行，须与现有医院互联互通/评级标准兼容；国家康复辅助器具研究中心李增勇建议优先攻克养老机构生命体征监测网络，再推进居家/社区康复设备；谢叻建议优先攻克手术室内定位与多设备共存的验证，并表示愿提供手术机器人平台供星闪联合试验。与会者达成的共识仅在于：星闪的技术优势与智慧医疗需求高度契合。国际星闪联盟副理事长、华为Fellow万蕾在总结发言中致辞；邬贺铨院士强调，医疗对通信提出的带宽、时延、同步、可靠性与隐私要求比其他行业更严格，且医院验证周期长，须以患者安全为最高准则。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Event**: SparkLink Smart Healthcare Seminar, 2026-09-17, Huawei Beijing Research Institute; attended by CAE academician Wu Hequan and industry experts.
- **New bodies planned**: SparkLink Smart Healthcare SIG (led by CAICT, formal launch targeted October 2026) and a SparkLink Medical Care & Wellness Expert Subcommittee under iSLA's expert committee.
- **iSLA ecosystem stats (as stated by Huawei's Yang Changqing)**: 1,250+ member organizations, 150+ developer partners, 380M+ cumulative chip shipments, 880 test-listed products as of end-August 2026, spanning the SLB/SLE/SLP/SLZ multi-mode stack.
- **Existing product line cited**: SparkLink e-paper bedside/footboard/door cards already in mass production (Guodong Qinda), extending the 2026-09-11 Hong Kong hospital e-paper deployment already in this KB.
- **Four target wireless-medical scenarios** (Weigao Huasheng Medical): operating room/endoscopy center, ICU/life-support, smart ward/wearable monitoring, medical-gas monitoring.
- **Medical-robotics positioning requirements** (SJTU Medical Robotics Institute): centimeter-level for rehabilitation/lower-limb walking aids; millimeter/sub-millimeter-level for surgical navigation and vascular suturing.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the first entry in this KB documenting a formal organizational step — a dedicated SIG plus an expert subcommittee — aimed specifically at the healthcare vertical, distinct from the 2026-09-11 e-paper deployment entry (`2026-09-11_nearlink-epaper-smart-healthcare-hongkong`), which covered a single product deployment rather than alliance-level standards/governance planning. It also gives the first medical-robotics-specific positioning-accuracy requirements (centimeter for rehabilitation, millimeter/sub-millimeter for surgical navigation) recorded against SparkLink in this KB, and the first named four-scenario wireless-medical taxonomy (OR/endoscopy, ICU, smart ward, medical gas) from an equipment vendor. The iSLA ecosystem figures (1,250+ members, 380M+ chips shipped, 880 test-listed products) update the alliance's previously reported scale figures with a newer end-August-2026 snapshot.
