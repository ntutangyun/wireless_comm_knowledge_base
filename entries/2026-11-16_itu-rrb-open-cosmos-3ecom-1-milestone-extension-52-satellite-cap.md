## Summary (EN)

NewSpace Economy (4 Nov 2026) reports that the ITU, in decisions published 3 November, extended the deployment milestone for the 3ECOM-1 satellite filing to 30 June 2027 and capped the modified total at 52 satellites, while declining an extension for the sister filing 3ECOM-3. Both systems were filed by Liechtenstein's regulator on behalf of Open Cosmos. Advanced Television (5 Nov) adds that the original plan was 96 satellites, i.e. a roughly 50% cut, with the milestone being half the constellation (48 satellites) in orbit in 2026.

The Board accepted the launch-disruption circumstances behind 3ECOM-1; Open Cosmos had cancelled a launch plan after an Indian rocket failure earlier in 2026. For 3ECOM-3, Liechtenstein had proposed counting the same 24 satellites toward the second milestone of both systems at once; the Board said its rules do not allow the same satellites to meet milestone requirements for two or more non-GSO systems simultaneously and that it was "not in a position to accede to the request."

## Summary (ZH)

NewSpace Economy（2026 年 11 月 4 日）报道，ITU 于 11 月 3 日公布的决定将 3ECOM-1 卫星网络申报的部署里程碑延长至 2027 年 6 月 30 日，并将修改后的总数上限定为 52 颗卫星，同时拒绝了姊妹申报 3ECOM-3 的延期。两个系统均由列支敦士登监管机构代 Open Cosmos 申报。Advanced Television（11 月 5 日）补充：原计划为 96 颗卫星，即削减约 50%，里程碑要求 2026 年内在轨 48 颗（一半）。

委员会接受了 3ECOM-1 延期所依据的发射受阻情形；Open Cosmos 在今年早些时候一枚印度火箭失利后取消了原发射计划。对于 3ECOM-3，列支敦士登曾提议将同样的 24 颗卫星同时计入两个系统的第二个里程碑；委员会表示规则不允许同一批卫星同时满足两个或多个非静止轨道系统的里程碑要求，并称"无法同意该请求"。

## Key technical points (EN)

- 3ECOM-1: milestone moved to 30 June 2027; modified total "not to be greater than 52 satellites," counting satellites already deployed (the Board's wording as quoted by [Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/?p=295283)).
- 3ECOM-3: extension declined; the Board ruled that counting the same 24 satellites toward two systems' milestones simultaneously is not permitted.
- Principle drawn by NewSpace Economy: milestones attach to specific satellite-system filings, and an operator's fleet is not treated as one undifferentiated constellation.
- Deployment status: two placeholder satellites were launched on a Rocket Lab Electron in January 2026 ([Advanced Television](https://www.advanced-television.com/?p=295283)).
- Open Cosmos's ConnectedCosmos plan (announced 5 October, per NewSpace Economy): 192-satellite core constellation, more than 96 satellites by end of 2028, global deployment in 2030, optical inter-satellite links.
- Open Cosmos's CEO welcomed the decision, per Advanced Television.
- The sources do not give the specific Radio Regulations provisions applied.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The decision shows how the ITU Radio Regulations Board applies NGSO milestone rules to a single operator's parallel filings: a launch-disruption extension was granted with a satellite-count cap, while double-counting one batch of satellites across two filings was refused. It complements the KB's earlier Open Cosmos coverage (September 2026) with a regulatory outcome.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

该决定展示了 ITU 无线电规则委员会如何将非静止轨道里程碑规则应用于同一运营商的并行申报：因发射受阻给予延期并附带卫星数量上限，而同一批卫星在两份申报间重复计数被拒绝。它为 KB 此前（2026 年 9 月）有关 Open Cosmos 的报道补充了监管结果。
