## Summary (EN)

An IEEE ComSoc Technology Blog analysis (Alan Weissberger, posted September 19, 2026) argues that the FCC's accelerating "6G" spectrum-auction agenda is running ahead of the international standards process that is supposed to make auctioned spectrum globally usable. FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said the agency's planned wireless-spectrum auctions could generate more than $100 billion in proceeds over the next several years. Following the July 2026 vote to hold a 2027 auction of 160 MHz in the Upper C-Band, Carr said the FCC is preparing three additional auctions, targeting completion of all four by the end of 2028. Carr framed the auctions as broadening participation beyond AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile and noted a prior auction had funded the replacement of Chinese-made network gear over national-security concerns. The piece also cites CTIA data: U.S. consumers used 159.3 trillion MB of mobile data in 2025 (up 20% from 2024), with AI-related traffic growing roughly three times faster than conventional wireless traffic and projected to reach nearly one-third of all broadband traffic by 2034.

The article's central argument is that frequency bands for 6G — formally IMT-2030 at the ITU — will not even be identified internationally until the ITU-R World Radiocommunication Conference 2027 (WRC-27, Shanghai, October 18-November 12, 2027), and that ITU-R Working Party 5D will only begin developing the actual IMT-2030 frequency arrangements (duplexing mode, uplink/downlink allocation, duplex direction and separation, center gap, band segmentation, channel/carrier placement, bandwidth scalability, TDD framework, coexistence support) starting at WP 5D meeting #57 in January/February 2028. The article details what a "frequency arrangement" recommendation actually specifies (a table of eleven distinct functions, from duplexing mode to harmonization reference) and argues that a band being "identified" for IMT does not by itself make it a harmonized, deployable, interoperable 6G band plan.

As precedent, the article points to a roughly two-year-and-ten-month gap (the article's own section header reads "2.5 Year Gap", while its body gives "over two and 10 months") between the two co-requisite IMT-2020 (5G) ITU-R recommendations: the radio-interface specification M.2150-0 was approved February 1, 2021, while the frequency-arrangements recommendation M.1036-7 was not approved until December 13, 2023. The article attributes several downstream 5G problems to that gap: stagnation of mmWave 5G deployment (24.25-27.5 GHz and 37-43.5 GHz bands) outside early adopters like the US, due in part to a geopolitical impasse over M.1036 consensus; industry bypassing the ITU by treating 3GPP Release 16 as the de facto authority; fragmented, region-specific RF front-end architectures in early 5G handsets that slowed cost declines; and cross-border interference-coordination friction in Europe, Africa, and parts of Asia absent an agreed guard-band/TDD-synchronization baseline. The article's bottom line: the ITU-R IMT-2030 frequency-arrangements recommendation is expected only in late 2030 or early 2031, so spectrum auctioned by the FCC in 2028 under a "6G" label would lack internationally harmonized band plans at auction time, risking reduced device economies of scale, higher RF complexity, and weaker cross-border roaming — even though a domestic US auction remains feasible as a policy matter independent of that international timeline.

## Summary (ZH)

IEEE ComSoc 技术博客的一篇分析文章（作者 Alan Weissberger，发布于 2026 年 9 月 19 日）指出，FCC 正在加速推进的"6G"频谱拍卖议程，其节奏已经跑在了本应使拍卖频谱具备全球可用性的国际标准化进程之前。FCC 主席 Brendan Carr 表示，该机构规划中的一系列无线频谱拍卖未来数年内有望带来超过 1000 亿美元的收益。继 2026 年 7 月投票决定在 2027 年拍卖高段 C 频段 160 MHz 频谱之后，Carr 表示 FCC 正在筹备另外三场拍卖，目标是在 2028 年底前完成全部四场。Carr 将这些拍卖定位为让参与主体不再局限于 AT&T、Verizon 与 T-Mobile 三家，并指出此前一场拍卖曾为替换出于国家安全考虑而被替换的中国产网络设备筹集了资金。文章还引用 CTIA 的数据：2025 年美国消费者使用的移动数据量达 159.3 万亿 MB，较 2024 年增长 20%；与 AI 相关的流量增速约为传统无线流量的三倍，预计到 2034 年将占全部宽带流量的近三分之一。

文章的核心论点是：正式名称为 IMT-2030 的 6G 频段，要到 ITU-R 2027 年世界无线电通信大会（WRC-27，2027 年 10 月 18 日至 11 月 12 日于中国上海举行）才会在国际层面被识别；而 ITU-R 第 5 工作组（WP 5D）要到 2028 年 1/2 月的第 57 次会议才会真正开始制定 IMT-2030 的频率安排（包括双工模式、上下行分配、双工方向与间隔、中心保护带、频段划分、信道/载波配置、带宽可扩展性、TDD 框架、共存支持等）。文章详细列出了"频率安排"建议书实际规定的内容（一张涵盖十一项不同功能的表格，从双工模式到协调统一基准），并指出某个频段被 IMT"识别"本身并不等同于形成了协调一致、可部署、可互操作的 6G 频段方案。

作为先例，文章指出 IMT-2020（5G）两份互为前提的 ITU-R 建议书之间存在约两年十个月的间隔（文章自身的小标题写作"2.5 Year Gap"，而正文给出的是"两年零十个月以上"）：无线接口规范 M.2150-0 于 2021 年 2 月 1 日获批，而频率安排建议书 M.1036-7 直到 2023 年 12 月 13 日才获批。文章将若干下游 5G 问题归因于这一间隔：毫米波 5G（24.25-27.5 GHz 与 37-43.5 GHz 频段）部署停滞不前（美国等早期采用者除外），部分原因是围绕 M.1036 共识的地缘政治僵局；产业界绕开 ITU，转而将 3GPP Release 16 当作事实标准；早期 5G 手机因此采用碎片化、区域专属的射频前端架构，拖慢了成本下降；以及在欧洲、非洲及亚洲部分地区，由于缺乏统一的保护频带/TDD 同步基准而产生跨境干扰协调摩擦。文章的结论是：ITU-R IMT-2030 频率安排建议书预计要到 2030 年底或 2031 年初才会获批，因此 FCC 若在 2028 年以"6G"名义拍卖频谱，届时将缺乏国际协调一致的频段方案，可能导致设备规模经济下降、射频复杂度上升、跨境漫游能力减弱——尽管作为国内政策行动，美国本土拍卖本身在技术上仍是可行的，与国际时间表无关。

## Key technical points (EN)

- FCC plans four spectrum auctions by end of 2028, starting with the 2027 Upper C-Band auction (160 MHz), targeting $100B+ in proceeds (Carr).
- CTIA: US mobile data usage hit 159.3 trillion MB in 2025 (+20% YoY); AI traffic growing ~3x faster than conventional traffic, projected near one-third of broadband traffic by 2034.
- WRC-27 (Shanghai, Oct 18-Nov 12, 2027) is the earliest point IMT-2030 (6G) bands get identified internationally; ITU-R WP 5D only starts drafting the actual frequency-arrangements recommendation at meeting #57 (Jan/Feb 2028).
- A "frequency arrangement" recommendation covers 11 distinct functions: duplexing mode, uplink/downlink allocation, duplex direction, duplex separation, center gap, band segmentation, channel/carrier placement, bandwidth scalability, TDD operating framework, coexistence support, and harmonization reference.
- IMT-2020 precedent cited: radio-interface recommendation M.2150-0 approved Feb 1, 2021 vs. frequency-arrangements recommendation M.1036-7 approved Dec 13, 2023 — a ~2 year 10 month gap blamed for mmWave 5G stagnation, 3GPP-vs-ITU fragmentation, non-scaled RF front ends, and cross-border coordination friction.
- IMT-2030 frequency-arrangements recommendation is expected only in late 2030/early 2031 — after the FCC's planned 2028 "6G" auctions.
