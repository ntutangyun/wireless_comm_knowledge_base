## Summary (EN)

SatNews (2 November 2026) reports that MDA Space announced on 30 October 2026 an authorization to proceed from prime contractor Thales Alenia Space, with an initial value above C$100 million (about US$72 million), covering engineering and production work for the first 66 LEO satellites of the IRIS2 multi-orbit network. A definitive contract for the full industrial scope remains subject to final documentation, and MDA expects to book the initial award in its fiscal Q4 2026 backlog.

MDA will supply digital phased-array antennas and digital antenna controllers derived from its modular AURORA platform; the article says the beamforming system shares its core architecture with the Telesat Lightspeed constellation, enabling dynamic in-orbit beam steering and capacity allocation across early-delivery orbital planes.
