## Summary (EN)

SpaceX's spectrum subsidiary **Starlink Spectrum Holdings** has applied to the FCC to take control of **three unpaired AWS-3 licences in 1695-1710 MHz** held by **Tristar License Group (TLG)**. The structure is a membership-interest purchase agreement under which Starlink acquires all outstanding membership interests in TLG, making it a Starlink subsidiary and an indirect SpaceX subsidiary. The application was filed in **mid-July 2026** and accepted by the Commission; the comment cycle closed at the end of July with **reply comments due 10 September 2026**. Alongside the transfer, SpaceX asked for licence renewal, an extension of the build requirements through **July 2027**, and a **permanent waiver of the terrestrial coverage requirements** attached to the licences.

The three licences are 5 MHz each and are designated **supplemental uplink only** — device-to-network transmission, with no matching downlink. They cover roughly **4% of the US population across eight states**. TLG is not using them for service: it had acquired them (for a reported ~$6 million) intending to support EchoStar's Dish 5G network, a plan that became unworkable once EchoStar sold its spectrum position to SpaceX.

The strategic point is the **completion of a band**. SpaceX already holds unpaired AWS-3 spectrum from two prior routes recorded in this knowledge base: the **15 MHz of unpaired AWS-3** inside the FCC-approved $17 billion EchoStar acquisition (40 MHz AWS-4 + 15 MHz unpaired AWS-3 + 10 MHz H-Block, transfer completing around November 2027), and **two uplink-only AWS-3 licences won for $8.5 million in Auction 113**, whose results the FCC announced on 23 June 2026. Adding the Tristar licences gives SpaceX what Light Reading describes as near-total nationwide coverage in this band, and takes its reported AWS-3 holdings to roughly **75 MHz**.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The knowledge base has tracked four constellation-scale direct-to-device entrants whose defining difference is **spectrum posture** rather than antenna technology — Starlink leasing terrestrial operators' bands, AST going per-operator, Amazon Leo filing on Globalstar spectrum, Equatys licensing MSS multi-market. This filing shows how far the Starlink posture has travelled: from borrowing a partner's PCS spectrum for first-generation direct-to-cell, to buying an exclusive mid-band position outright, to now **sweeping up the residual secondary-market licences that complete a nationwide footprint in one uplink band**. Buying 5 MHz slivers covering 4% of the population makes little sense as a coverage play on its own; it makes sense only as the last pieces of a band-completion strategy.

The waiver request is the part worth watching. AWS-3 licences carry **terrestrial coverage requirements** — obligations to build out and serve. Asking for a *permanent* waiver of those duties, together with a build-requirement extension, is an argument that a satellite operator should be able to hold terrestrial mid-band spectrum for **satellite** device-to-network uplink without ever constructing the terrestrial network the licence contemplates. Whichever way the FCC rules, the outcome sets a reference point for how terrestrial spectrum obligations apply to satellite direct-to-device systems — a question that also sits behind the unlicensed-D2D rulemaking (ET 26-169) and the 800 MHz spectrum contest this knowledge base has been tracking. Reply comments were due 10 September 2026, so the disposition is a concrete follow-up to watch rather than a settled result.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

本知识库追踪的四家星座级直连终端参与者，其真正的差别在于**频谱姿态**而非天线技术——Starlink 租用地面运营商频段、AST 逐运营商合作、Amazon Leo 基于 Globalstar 频谱申报、Equatys 以 MSS 多市场牌照切入。这份申请显示 Starlink 的姿态已经走了多远：从第一代直连手机借用合作方的 PCS 频谱，到直接买断独占的中频段资产，再到如今**把补齐某个上行频段全国版图所需的二级市场零散牌照逐一收拢**。单看覆盖，收购覆盖 4% 人口的 5 MHz 碎片并不划算；只有作为频段补齐战略的最后拼图，它才说得通。

真正值得关注的是那份豁免申请。AWS-3 牌照附带**地面覆盖要求**——即建设并提供服务的义务。请求*永久*豁免这些义务、同时延长建设期限，实质上是在主张：卫星运营商应当可以持有地面中频段频谱、用于**卫星**方向的终端到网络上行，而永远不必建设牌照所设想的地面网络。无论 FCC 如何裁决，其结果都会为"地面频谱义务如何适用于卫星直连终端系统"树立一个参照点——这个问题同样潜藏在本知识库追踪的免许可直连终端规则制定（ET 26-169）与 800 MHz 频谱争夺之后。答复评论截止于 2026 年 9 月 10 日，因此其处置结果是一个明确的后续观察点，而非已成定局的结论。
