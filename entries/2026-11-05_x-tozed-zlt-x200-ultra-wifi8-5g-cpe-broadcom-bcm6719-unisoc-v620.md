## Summary (EN)

On 2026-10-13, at Network X in Vienna, Tozed Kangwei Tech announced the ZLT X200 ULTRA, which it calls a Wi-Fi 8 5G CPE (fixed wireless access terminal). According to the Tozed press release, the device is built on UNISOC's V620 5G SoC platform with Broadcom's BCM6719 Wi-Fi 8 chip and was designed in-house by Tozed. The release states that the cellular side supports 3GPP Release 16, NR carrier aggregation (NRCA) and LTE 5-carrier aggregation (5CC), and describes the wireless side as dual-band 4x4 MIMO Wi-Fi (the release names the part BN7200). Broadcom's wireless and broadband marketing vice president and a UNISOC IoT product-line general manager are quoted in the release as partners in the development. Target use cases listed include home broadband, mobile office, enterprise networking and industrial IoT. The release gives no price, shipping date, Wi-Fi 8 feature list or throughput figure.

The device came to this sweep through an X post on 2026-11-04 by a Wi-Fi engineer who linked the Tozed page and Broadcom's product page and speculated about default channel widths; that speculation is not a sourced fact and is not recorded as one here.
