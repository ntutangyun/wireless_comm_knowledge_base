## Summary (EN)

SatNews (Sep-24) reports remarks from Mario Maniewicz, outgoing Director of the ITU Radiocommunication Bureau, and Barbara Pareglio, GSMA Senior Technical Director and NTN lead, made at World Space Business Week, Paris, 16 Sep, on the outlook for satellite direct-to-device (D2D) spectrum at the next World Radiocommunication Conference, WRC-27, scheduled for 2027-10-18 through 2027-11-12 in Shanghai. Maniewicz said he expects the conference to "end up with a regulatory framework for these D2D services in IMT identified bands at least on some of them," and separately projected that roughly 85% of the WRC-27 agenda will address space-related items. He cited a fourfold increase in narrowband satellite spectrum filings over the past four years, with the number of satellites named in those filings growing sixteenfold over the same period. Pareglio cited GSMA figures that 151 mobile operators worldwide now have satellite services "live, in testing, or planned," representing roughly six billion mobile connections, of which she estimated around 50 operators are currently live; she also noted that commercial arrangements between mobile and satellite operators remain unsettled. The article identifies agenda item 1.13 (direct connectivity between space stations and IMT user equipment, 694/698 MHz-2.7 GHz) as the D2D item, and separately notes agenda item 1.10, which sets power flux-density and e.i.r.p. limits for satellite services in the E-band (71-76 GHz and 81-86 GHz). It reports two technical approaches to D2D under consideration — mobile-satellite-service (MSS) spectrum, already in use, and reuse of terrestrial IMT bands — both of which Maniewicz said WRC-27 will consider.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Conference details**: WRC-27, 2027-10-18 to 2027-11-12, Shanghai; Maniewicz projects ~85% of the agenda will address space-related items.
- **Filing growth**: narrowband satellite spectrum filings up fourfold over four years; satellites named in those filings up sixteenfold over the same period.
- **Operator adoption (GSMA)**: 151 mobile operators worldwide with satellite services live, in testing, or planned, representing ~6 billion mobile connections; ~50 operators estimated currently live.
- **D2D band**: agenda item 1.13 (694/698 MHz-2.7 GHz); agenda item 1.10 is a separate item setting power flux-density and e.i.r.p. limits for satellite services in the E-band (71-76 GHz and 81-86 GHz), not a D2D item.
- **Two D2D approaches (both on the agenda)**: dedicated mobile-satellite-service (MSS) spectrum, already in use, vs. reuse of terrestrial IMT bands — both of which Maniewicz said WRC-27 will consider.
- **Open commercial question**: Pareglio notes business-model arrangements between mobile and satellite operators remain unsettled even as technical/regulatory groundwork proceeds.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This KB's ngso-regulation coverage has so far centered on specific national/regional regulatory actions (FCC spectrum-abundance orders, ITU NGSO coordination items) and on individual operators' D2D rollouts (AST SpaceMobile, Apple/Globalstar, Amazon Leo). This is the first entry to capture the ITU's own forward-looking framing of WRC-27 as the venue expected to formalize a D2D regulatory framework across IMT-identified bands, with quantified adoption data (151 operators, fourfold/sixteenfold filing growth) from named officials rather than a single operator's announcement — giving this bin its first direct look at the multilateral process whose decisions bind ITU member states through the Radio Regulations.
