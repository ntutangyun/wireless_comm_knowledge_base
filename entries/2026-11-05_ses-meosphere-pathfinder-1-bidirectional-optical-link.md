## Summary (EN)

SES announced on 4 Nov 2026 that its meoSphere Pathfinder 1 mission demonstrated a bidirectional optical communications link between the ground and space, transmitting video messages from CEO Adel Al-Saleh over the link. The ground end was Cailabs' optical ground station in Rennes, France. The space end was an SES payload flying on K2's GRAVITAS mission, hosted on K2's Mega-class satellite platform and carrying a Mynaric CONDOR optical terminal.

SES says it designed, developed and operated the optical mission and provided automation software for the demonstration, with Thales Alenia Space supporting system assembly, integration, testing and operations. Carmel Ortiz, SVP of MEO Programs at SES, said the result validates SES's "vertically integrated, iterative approach" and that the team has "successfully and repeatedly transferred data between space and the ground". The Pathfinder 1 team plans further flight tests to extend link duration and to evaluate link speed (and, per secondary coverage from SatNews and Converge rather than the SES release, pointing stability), with results feeding future pathfinder missions and the optical networking planned for the meoSphere constellation.

The release and the SatNews write-up (4 Nov 2026) do not give data rates, link distances, wavelength, the satellite's orbital altitude, demonstration dates or a meoSphere service timeline.

## Summary (ZH)

SES 于 2026 年 11 月 4 日宣布，其 meoSphere Pathfinder 1 任务演示了地面与太空之间的双向光通信链路，并经该链路传送了 CEO Adel Al-Saleh 的视频讯息。地面端为 Cailabs 位于法国雷恩的光学地面站；空间端为搭载于 K2 的 GRAVITAS 任务上的 SES 载荷，由 K2 的 Mega 级卫星平台承载，并配备 Mynaric CONDOR 光学终端。

发布稿与 SatNews 报道（2026 年 11 月 4 日）均未给出数据速率、链路距离、波长、卫星轨道高度、演示日期或 meoSphere 的服务时间表。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Link: bidirectional optical, ground station (Cailabs, Rennes) to the SES payload on K2's GRAVITAS satellite.
- Space terminal: Mynaric CONDOR; host platform: K2 Mega-class bus.
- Roles: SES designed and operated the mission and provided automation software for the demonstration; Thales Alenia Space supported AIT and operations.
- Next steps stated: longer link duration, higher link speed, pointing-stability benchmarking (pointing stability per SatNews/Converge coverage, not the SES release).
- Not disclosed: data rate, range, wavelength, orbit parameters, test dates.
