## Summary (EN)

SatNews reports that on 29 October 2026 the FCC voted at its open meeting to advance a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking modernising the regulatory structure for direct-to-device satellite connectivity in licensed flexible-use bands, docketed as GN Docket Nos. 26-281 and 23-65. It extends the Supplemental Coverage from Space (SCS) framework first introduced in 2024. This is the meeting outcome of the item previewed in the KB's 23 October agenda entry.

The article lists the proposals: authorising up to 482 MHz of additional spectrum for commercial satellite-to-cellular operation, applying a license-by-rule mechanism to consumer earth stations, dropping individualised equipment certification for standard terrestrial handsets, and baseline interference limits with questions on aligning SCS with legacy Mobile Satellite Service standards. It is a proposal, not a final rule.

## Summary (ZH)

SatNews 报道，2026 年 10 月 29 日 FCC 在公开会议上表决通过一项立法建议通知（NPRM），对授权灵活使用频段内的卫星直连终端（D2D）监管框架进行现代化，案卷号为 GN Docket 26-281 与 23-65。该通知扩展了 2024 年首次提出的“太空补充覆盖”（SCS）框架，是本知识库 10 月 23 日议程条目所预告事项的会议结果。

文章列出的提议包括：授权最多 482 MHz 额外频谱用于商用卫星对蜂窝运行；对消费级地球站适用“规则授权”机制；取消标准地面手机的单独设备认证要求；设定基线干扰限值，并就 SCS 与传统移动卫星业务（MSS）标准的衔接提出技术问题。这是提议而非最终规则。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Spectrum**: up to 482 MHz of additional licensed wireless spectrum under review for satellite-to-cellular use.
- **Licensing**: license-by-rule for consumer earth stations that communicate with space systems; no individualised equipment certification for standard terrestrial handsets.
- **Interference**: baseline interference limits proposed, with technical questions on aligning SCS operation with legacy MSS standards; aggregate interference modelling and carrier coordination rules are the expected comment focus.
- **Opposition**: per the article, AT&T, T-Mobile and Verizon petitioned earlier that week to shield 290 MHz across 2.5 GHz and 3.45 GHz from orbital operations, citing ground-noise rise and TDD frame interference at cell sites.
- **Process**: a 60-day comment period opens after Federal Register publication; the article gives no date. Power and out-of-band limits, vote tally and commissioner statements are not reported. Single-outlet report.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **频谱**：正在审议授权最多 482 MHz 额外授权无线频谱用于卫星对蜂窝。
- **许可方式**：对与空间系统通信的消费级地球站采用规则授权；标准地面手机不再需要单独设备认证。
- **干扰**：提出基线干扰限值，并就 SCS 与传统 MSS 标准的衔接提出技术问题；预期评议重点为聚合干扰建模与运营商协调规则。
- **反对意见**：据文章，AT&T、T-Mobile 与 Verizon 当周早些时候请愿，要求保护 2.5 GHz 与 3.45 GHz 间 290 MHz 免受轨道运行影响，理由是地面噪声抬升及基站 TDD 帧干扰。
- **流程**：联邦公报公布后开启 60 天评议期，文章未给出日期；未报道功率与带外限值、票数及委员发言。仅单一媒体来源。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The vote moves the SCS framework from the 2024 introduction toward a broader band set and a handset-side licensing model that removes per-device certification. Its practical weight depends on the unpublished text and on how the carrier interference objections are resolved; the article reports the proposals but not their technical parameters.
