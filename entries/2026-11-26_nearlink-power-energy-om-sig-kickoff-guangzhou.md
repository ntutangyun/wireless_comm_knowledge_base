## Summary (EN)

A post on the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) news feed, dated 2026-11-23, reports that the alliance's Power and Energy Smart Operation and Maintenance special interest group (SIG) held its kickoff meeting on 17 November at the National Energy Storage Building in Baiyun District, Guangzhou. According to the post, more than twenty expert representatives attended, from the alliance's staff, the group's lead organisation (the National New Energy Storage Innovation Center, abbreviated "国储中心" in the post), four deputy-lead organisations (Huawei Technologies, China Southern Power Grid Power Technology, Beijing Zhongke Jingshang Technology, XJ Electric) and a first batch of member units including Telink Semiconductor (Shanghai), South China University of Technology's School of Electronic and Information Engineering, Beijing Kaihong Digital Technology, Guangzhou Robustel and others. Before the meeting the delegates visited energy-storage application scenes in the lead centre's exhibition hall.

The post quotes alliance industry-promotion department deputy director Zhang Hanfeng as saying that, with large-scale grid connection of wind and solar and mass access of distributed generation and storage, the power system's "terminal nerves" are becoming denser, and that connection pain points in the "last mile" (mass terminal access, substation equipment interconnection, and sensing of working personnel) are where SparkLink can be applied. Jiang Ruichun, deputy general manager of the lead centre's new-type power system institute, is quoted as saying the centre will provide platform, scenario and test-and-verification capabilities as organisational and resource support for the SIG. Member representatives, speaking from the chip-module, power-equipment and system-solution perspectives, are described as presenting SparkLink technology evolution, customised adaptation and grid-intelligence application practice, which the post says built consensus on the SIG's overall construction plan and action plan.

The post says the SIG will focus on power-terminal access, equipment interconnection and spatial sensing, and will advance industry research, standard development, test verification and application demonstration, aiming over time at a set of replicable typical application cases. It does not give a schedule, performance figures or named pilot sites.

## Summary (ZH)

国际星闪联盟官网动态（2026年11月23日发布）报道，联盟电力能源智慧运维专项兴趣小组（SIG）于11月17日在广州市白云区国家储能大厦召开启动会。帖文称，联盟相关负责人，以及来自组长单位国家新型储能创新中心（帖文简称“国储中心”）、四家副组长单位（华为技术有限公司、南方电网电力科技股份有限公司、北京中科晶上科技股份有限公司、许继电气股份有限公司）和首批成员单位（包括泰凌微电子（上海）股份有限公司、华南理工大学电信学院、北京开鸿数字科技发展有限公司、广州鲁邦通物联网科技股份有限公司等）的二十余位专家代表出席。会前，代表们实地考察了国储中心展厅的储能现场应用实景。

帖文引述联盟产业推广部副部长张瀚峰的致辞：随着风电、光伏大规模并网及分布式电源与储能海量接入，电力系统的“末端神经”日益密集，海量终端接入、厂站设备互联与作业人员感知等“最后一公里”连接痛点正是星闪的用武之地。国储中心新型电力系统研究所副总经理蒋瑞春表示，国储中心将发挥平台、场景与检测验证能力优势，为SIG提供组织保障和资源支撑。成员单位代表从芯片模组、电力装备到系统解决方案的角度介绍了星闪技术演进、定制化适配及电网智能化应用实践，帖文称这为明确SIG总体建设方案与行动计划凝聚了共识。

帖文表示，SIG将围绕电力终端接入、设备互联与空间感知等方向，推进产业研究、标准研制、测试验证与应用示范，逐步形成可复制、可推广的典型应用案例。帖文未给出时间表、性能数据或具体试点地点。
