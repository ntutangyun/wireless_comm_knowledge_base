## Summary (EN)

On August 6, 2026 the IEEE PES Electric Vehicle Technical Committee (China) and the Charging & Swapping Branch of the China Association of Automobile Manufacturers held the "Vehicle-Grid Interaction Development Forum" in Beijing. Shi Lei, deputy GM of the HaiHe business unit at HopeRun Software (江苏润和软件, an iSLA standing-council member) gave a keynote titled "NearLink wireless technology empowering vehicle-grid interaction applications", published by the International SparkLink Alliance on August 7. The talk positions NearLink's integrated sensing-and-communication (通感一体) capability as the vehicle-charger-grid link for large-scale V2G: NearLink SLE narrowband communication provides low-latency, high-reliability wireless sync of battery capacity, charge/discharge power and vehicle state, supporting unified control of multi-vehicle clusters in large car parks; the sensing side localises vehicles and identifies remaining charge so that charging resources become "observable, measurable, controllable and adjustable" for the grid, including in underground garages and high-density charging stations with difficult RF conditions. On the user side, NearLink auto-pairing establishes the link automatically when a vehicle enters a V2G bay, after which the system schedules charge/discharge against peak-valley tariffs and the driver's travel plan without manual action.

The alliance post also discloses that HopeRun, Luruan Technology (鲁软科技, the State Grid Shandong software arm) and Chery Automobile have completed a joint R&D pilot of NearLink-enabled V2G, building a "vehicle + charger + NearLink" integration path intended as a replicable template for large V2G stations and home bidirectional charging, and that iSLA will pursue NearLink V2G industry-standard work covering home V2H, campus V2B and city virtual-power-plant scenarios. Context figures cited: BYD, Changan, Seres and Chery now list bidirectional discharge as a standard smart feature; V2G is projected to deliver "over a billion tonnes" of annual CO2 reduction by 2035.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Link layer: NearLink SLE narrowband used as the vehicle-charger-grid control channel; real-time sync of battery capacity, charge/discharge power and vehicle status; multi-vehicle cluster control in large car parks.
- Sensing side ("通感一体"): NearLink ranging/positioning to localise the vehicle in the bay and associate it with remaining charge — grid gets per-bay observability/controllability.
- Deployment environments named: underground garages and high-density charging stations (complex electromagnetic conditions) — the interference-resistance claim rests on NearLink's Polar coding and short-frame design already documented in this KB.
- UX: automatic NearLink pairing on entering a V2G bay; scheduling against peak-valley tariffs and user travel plans.
- Pilot: HopeRun + Luruan (State Grid Shandong) + Chery joint R&D pilot completed; "vehicle + charger + NearLink" three-in-one path; targets large V2G stations and home bidirectional chargers.
- Standards: iSLA to drive NearLink V2G industry-standard work; scenarios V2H (home), V2B (campus), city virtual power plant.
- Event: 2026-08-06, Beijing; IEEE PES EV Technical Committee (China) + CAAM Charging & Swapping Branch; alliance publication 2026-08-07.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the first NearLink automotive application in this KB that is *not* the digital car key: a grid-side (V2G) control link, with a completed three-party pilot involving a State Grid subsidiary and a volume OEM (Chery), and an explicit alliance intention to standardise it. Two things are new. First, the role assigned to NearLink here — a short-range, interference-hardened control plane between EV, charger and grid aggregator, plus positioning to bind a vehicle to a bay — is a distinct use of the sensing-and-communication capability that the GB/T 46803 SLE standard entries in this KB describe only abstractly; V2G gives it a concrete, regulator-relevant scenario (China's V2G scale-up pilots have been running since 2025). Second, it extends the NearLink automotive story from the in-vehicle/keyless domain into energy infrastructure, where the competing wireless options (Wi-Fi, BLE, PLC over the charging cable per ISO 15118) each have known weaknesses in dense underground car parks. Caveats: the alliance post is a keynote write-up — no latency, ranging-accuracy or field-trial figures are given, and the "pilot completed" claim is not independently dated; treat this as an application-direction signal rather than a measured deployment. Watch items: publication of an iSLA V2G standard project, a dated Chery / State Grid Shandong field deployment, and whether the wireless-BMS (无线电池管理) thread this KB has tracked converges with the same SLE narrowband link.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

这是本知识库中首个**非数字车钥匙**的星闪汽车应用：面向电网侧（V2G）的控制链路，已完成含国网子公司与量产主机厂（奇瑞）在内的三方试点，且联盟明确表达标准化意图。两点新意。其一，星闪在此承担的角色——车、桩、电网聚合器之间的短距、抗干扰控制面，加上把车辆绑定到具体车位的定位能力——是本库中 GB/T 46803 SLE 标准条目仅抽象描述的通感一体能力的一种具体用法；V2G 为其提供了具体且与监管相关的场景（中国车网互动规模化试点自 2025 年起推进）。其二，它把星闪汽车叙事从车内/无钥匙领域延伸到能源基础设施，而在密集地下停车场中，竞争性无线方案（Wi-Fi、BLE、依 ISO 15118 的充电线缆 PLC）各有已知短板。注意事项：联盟稿件为演讲纪要——未给出时延、测距精度或现场试验数据，"试点已完成"亦无独立日期；应视为应用方向信号而非实测部署。关注点：iSLA V2G 标准项目立项；奇瑞/国网山东有明确日期的现场部署；本库跟踪的无线电池管理（无线 BMS）线索是否与同一 SLE 窄带链路汇合。
