## Summary (EN)

The alliance news feed (dated 2026-11-14) reports that the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) held its Q4 "development problem debugging" activity in Shanghai. The SparkLink Smart Appliance (Shanghai) Innovation Lab ("Smart Appliance OpenLab"), as one of the co-hosts, completed a four-day centralised debugging session together with the alliance's Testing and Certification Group and vendor experts. The article states that the lab passed acceptance on 29 September and became iSLA's 4th authorised OpenLab, the first aimed at smart appliances, and is now in operation.

It lists the capabilities it attributes to SparkLink in appliances (data transmission, sensing, positioning, flexible networking) and examples of new scenarios: SparkLink pointing remote controls around TVs, SparkLink communication-sensing integration for white goods, and a "wide-and-narrow single network" around routers. It names three obstacles for the appliance industry: few applications, key technologies still to be broken through, and insufficient supply-chain coordination. The lab is built and operated by the Shanghai Qingpu Xinglian SparkLink Promotion Center, initiated by nine units (Midea, Shanghai HiSilicon, Hangzhou Yongxie, Shanghai National Engineering Research Center of Digital Television, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Hefei Hengxinji, Shanghai Yangtze Delta Baoye, Shanghai Bilu Electronics and the Yangtze River Delta AI+ Joint Innovation Center).

Details not in earlier coverage: governance bodies (council, supervisory board, strategic advisory committee, secretariat); a team of 10+ part-time technical staff from member units across three business lines (solution development, test lab, ecosystem development), with seven full-time staff planned by end-2026; and a three-phase roadmap: an "ice-breaking" phase to end-2026 (organisation and rules, capability building, incubating solutions with partners), a "platform" phase 2027-2028 (an integrated platform for technology-application services, testing capability and industry promotion, pushing applications and industrial policy in East China), and a "sustainable" phase from 2029 (a stable R&D service team and project incubation). Four stated positionings: industry solution base, test service centre, innovation and results-transfer platform, ecosystem booster.
