## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-15, Space42 (the UAE-based AI-and-space firm formed from the Bayanat-Yahsat merger) and Viasat announced the formal establishment of Equatys, the direct-to-device (D2D) joint venture the two companies had previously advanced into a satellite-procurement phase (covered in this KB's 2026-08-14 entry). The formal establishment is backed by a binding agreement (signed and announced by the two companies on 2026-09-14 at World Space Business Week in Paris; reported by SatNews on 2026-09-15) committing up to $1 billion (about €0.87 billion) in combined initial equity: each company contributes $400 million initially, with a further $200 million from Space42 in connection with a future equity funding round open to third-party investors. The companies' own release states that the formation of the venture, the constellation procurement and the corresponding definitive agreements remain "subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory approvals and the appointment of Viasat as the prime technology contractor."

The venture's architecture and business model are unchanged from the August disclosure: a neutral-host "Space TowerCo" structure in which mobile network operators lease non-terrestrial-network (NTN) capacity and Mobile Satellite Services (MSS) from a shared constellation — rather than each operator building or leasing its own satellites — while retaining their own spectrum licenses, branding, and subscriber relationships. The constellation is designed to scale to up to 2,800 satellites across 60 orbital planes and three altitude layers. (For KB context, not the source's own framing: this KB's 2026-08-14 entry already tracks Starlink's direct-to-cell fleet, AST SpaceMobile and Amazon Leo as the other constellation-scale D2D entrants.)

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 9 月 15 日，Space42（由 Bayanat 与 Yahsat 合并组成的阿联酋 AI 与航天公司）与 Viasat 宣布正式成立 Equatys——此前双方已推动该手机直连（D2D）合资公司进入卫星采购阶段（相关进展见本知识库 2026-08-14 条目）。此次正式成立由一份具约束力的协议支撑（该协议由两家公司于 2026 年 9 月 14 日在巴黎世界航天商业周签署并宣布，SatNews 于 9 月 15 日报道），双方合计初始股权承诺最高达 10 亿美元（约合 8.7 亿欧元）：两家公司初始各出资 4 亿美元，另有 2 亿美元由 Space42 在未来一轮向第三方投资者开放的股权融资中投入。两家公司的官方新闻稿指出，合资公司的设立、星座采购及相应的最终协议仍“须满足惯常的交割条件，包括监管批准以及指定 Viasat 为技术总承包方”。

该合资公司的架构与商业模式与 8 月披露的内容一致：采用中立主机的"太空铁塔公司（Space TowerCo）"模式，移动运营商从共享星座租用非地面网络（NTN）容量及移动卫星服务（MSS），而非各自自建或租赁卫星，同时保留自身的频谱许可、品牌及用户关系。星座设计规模最高可达 2,800 颗卫星、60 个轨道面、三个高度层。（以下为本知识库自身的交叉参照，并非消息源本身的表述：本知识库 2026-08-14 条目已跟踪 Starlink 直连星座、AST SpaceMobile 与 Amazon Leo 等其他星座级手机直连玩家。）

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Event:** binding agreement to co-found Equatys (Space42 + Viasat joint venture) signed and announced 2026-09-14 at World Space Business Week, reported by SatNews 2026-09-15 — a progression from the 2026-08-10 "procurement phase" milestone already in this KB.
- **Financing:** binding agreement for up to $1B combined initial equity; $400M initially from each founding partner, plus a further $200M from Space42 in connection with a future round open to third-party investors. Formation, procurement and definitive agreements remain subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory approvals and Viasat's appointment as prime technology contractor.
- **Model (unchanged from prior coverage):** neutral-host "Space TowerCo" — MNOs lease shared NTN/MSS capacity rather than building fleets; Space42 and Viasat are anchor tenants.
- **Constellation (unchanged from prior coverage):** up to 2,800 satellites, 60 orbital planes, three altitude layers.
- **Also in the release:** access to over 100 MHz of globally coordinated MSS spectrum and combined distribution agreements spanning more than 400 mobile network operators worldwide, serving unmodified standard smartphones.
