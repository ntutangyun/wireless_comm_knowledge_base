## Summary (EN)

Four straggler contributions landed across three groups on July 9, the eve of the Montreal plenary. In TGbn, ZTE's Zhanjing Bao uploaded **DCN 1395 — LB291: CR for AP PUO and MAPC** — a comment-resolution tying AP power-up operation (PUO) behaviour into the multi-AP coordination framework ahead of the D1.50 recirculation. TGm (REVmf maintenance, now at D2.20) received two LB294 comment resolutions: **DCN 1387** (Bo Sun, Sanechips, CID 1222) and **DCN 1397** (Xiaofei Wang, InterDigital, CID 1005). The Coexistence Standing Committee received **DCN 1396 — ETSI TC BRAN July 2026 update** (Guido Hiertz, Ericsson), the recurring liaison readout that keeps 802.11 aligned with European regulatory/coexistence work — noteworthy this cycle given the June 19 Wi-Fi Alliance / Bluetooth SIG 6 GHz coexistence announcement (2026-07-10_wfa-bluetooth-sig-6ghz-coexistence).

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Individually minor, these four documents mark the close of the pre-Montreal upload window: after the July 8-9 wave (134 DCNs, see the 2026-07-09 digests) the stream thinned to stragglers, and the next Mentor activity will be plenary-session documents themselves. The AP-PUO/MAPC coupling in 1395 is a small but real design surface — how a freshly-powered AP joins an existing multi-AP coordination set — that the KB's mapc-cosr bin hasn't seen isolated before.
