## Summary (EN)
Japanese trade site Business Network (businessnetwork.jp, article dated 18 November 2026, headline "モースマイクロが850MHz帯の技適取得、Wi-Fi HaLowで利用可能に") reports that Morse Micro's module MM8108-MF15457, built on its Wi-Fi HaLow SoC MM8108, received Japan's technical conformity certification (技適; the article calls it a 工事設計認証). The certification targets the high-power 850 MHz band that Japan newly opened for Wi-Fi HaLow, which the article describes as Japan's second HaLow band after the 920 MHz band (2022). One certification covers both the 850 MHz and 920 MHz bands.

Figures given in the article: maximum transmit power of 200 mW (23 dBm), which it states is ten times the 20 mW allowed in the 920 MHz band; the 920 MHz band limits each device to 360 seconds of transmission per hour, whereas the 850 MHz band has no transmit-time limit and allows continuous transmission; and the 920 MHz band is shared with LoRa, Wi-SUN and RFID systems whereas the 850 MHz band is dedicated to Wi-Fi HaLow. The article does not state the channel bandwidth and gives no certification date other than the announcement date.

A Wi-Fi NOW news roundup published 24 November 2026 (WiFi NOW, "Roundup: Ofcom's 88 areas for mobile priority, Morse Micro high power Wi-Fi HaLow in Japan, ...") carries the same item, dated November 18, with the same 200 mW versus 20 mW and no-360-seconds-per-hour points. In addition, that roundup says Japan allocated a total of 8 MHz of new 850 MHz spectrum to HaLow with full availability of the band scheduled for June 2029, and lists video surveillance, industrial monitoring and edge AI as applications that the removal of the duty limit could enable; those two points are Wi-Fi NOW's and are not in the Business Network article.

## Summary (ZH)
日本行业网站 Business Network（businessnetwork.jp，文章日期 2026 年 11 月 18 日，标题《モースマイクロが850MHz帯の技適取得、Wi-Fi HaLowで利用可能に》）报道，基于 Morse Micro Wi-Fi HaLow SoC MM8108 的模块 MM8108-MF15457 获得了日本技术基准符合认证（技適；文章称之为工事设计认证）。认证面向日本为 Wi-Fi HaLow 新开放的高功率 850 MHz 频段，文章称这是继 2022 年 920 MHz 频段之后日本的第二个 HaLow 频段。一份认证同时覆盖 850 MHz 与 920 MHz 两个频段。

文章给出的数据：最大发射功率 200 mW（23 dBm），文章称这是 920 MHz 频段 20 mW 的十倍；920 MHz 频段限制每台设备每小时发射 360 秒，而 850 MHz 频段无发射时间限制，可连续发射；920 MHz 频段与 LoRa、Wi-SUN、RFID 系统共用，而 850 MHz 频段专用于 Wi-Fi HaLow。文章未给出信道带宽，除发布日期外也未给出认证日期。

Wi-Fi NOW 于 2026 年 11 月 24 日发布的新闻汇总（《Roundup: Ofcom's 88 areas for mobile priority, Morse Micro high power Wi-Fi HaLow in Japan, ...》）也报道了同一事项，日期同为 11 月 18 日，并给出同样的 200 mW 对 20 mW 以及不再受每小时 360 秒限制的要点。此外，该汇总称日本为 HaLow 共分配了 8 MHz 的新 850 MHz 频谱，整个频段预计于 2029 年 6 月全面可用，并列举视频监控、工业监测与边缘 AI 为取消占空限制后可能受益的应用；这两点来自 Wi-Fi NOW，不在 Business Network 的文章中。

## Key technical points (EN)
- Module: Morse Micro MM8108-MF15457 (Wi-Fi HaLow SoC MM8108); Japan technical conformity certification (技適) covering both 850 MHz and 920 MHz.
- 850 MHz band: up to 200 mW (23 dBm) versus 20 mW at 920 MHz; no transmit-time restriction versus 360 s per hour at 920 MHz; band dedicated to Wi-Fi HaLow versus 920 MHz shared with LoRa, Wi-SUN and RFID.
- Per Wi-Fi NOW only: 8 MHz of new 850 MHz spectrum in total, full availability scheduled for June 2029.
