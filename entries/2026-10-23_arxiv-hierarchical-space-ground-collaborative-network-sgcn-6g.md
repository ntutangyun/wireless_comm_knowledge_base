## Summary (EN)

Geng, Feng, Hao, Guo, Wang, Ding, Zheng, Wei, Jiang and Feng (arXiv 2610.28459, eess.SP, 10 pages, 4 figures, 1 table, IEEE Network; v1 20 October 2026, v2 21 October adding an author) address space-ground integrated networks (SGINs) for 6G. They argue that independent management approaches struggle with the time-varying nature of low-Earth-orbit (LEO) channels and with diverse service demands.

The paper reviews SGIN trends and application scenarios, then proposes a hierarchical space-ground collaborative network (SGCN) architecture aimed at greater network capability, continuous coverage and better spectral efficiency. The architecture is evaluated across three scenarios against progressive baselines, and a service-aware deployment strategy is introduced to support engineering feasibility. This entry is based on the arXiv abstract page; the full text was not retrievable, so layer definitions, scenario parameters and quantitative results are not recorded here.
