## Summary (EN)

ProPakistani (12 Oct 2026) reports that Qualcomm CEO Cristiano Amon, in an interview with Fireside Alpha (a clip was posted on X by @firesidealpha on 10 Oct 2026; OODA Loop links the full interview at sources.news), said AI companies want smartphones that can continuously run models of 100 billion parameters by 2028, and that some AI companies are already developing smartphones capable of running 100B models. He did not name the companies. The report says he did not give a detailed roadmap for how memory, storage, power and thermal limits would be overcome.

The article adds its own arithmetic and context, not Amon's: a 100B-parameter model at 4-bit needs about 50GB for the weights alone (about 25GB at 2-bit), while current DRAM supply makes phones unlikely to move beyond the 16GB range soon, though Amon is said to expect manufacturers to add significantly more memory. It lists possible routes, namely more aggressive quantization, sparse mixture-of-experts with expert offloading to flash, and smaller distilled models of roughly 20-30 billion parameters. A separate brief (OODA Loop) summarises the same interview, adding that Amon expects an always-on agentic shift and calls AI the next upgrade driver for phones.
