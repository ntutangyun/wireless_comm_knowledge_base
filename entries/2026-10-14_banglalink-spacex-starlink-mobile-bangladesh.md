## Summary (EN)

SatNews (2026-10-12) reports that Banglalink, a VEON subsidiary and the third-largest mobile operator in Bangladesh, announced on 2026-10-09 the launch of Starlink Mobile direct-to-device connectivity in Bangladesh, under an agreement with SpaceX first announced by VEON on 2026-04-22. The service is described as working with unmodified 4G smartphones and standard Banglalink SIM cards, with no special terminals or chipsets, and with automated failover between terrestrial 4G towers and LEO satellites. The Bangladesh Telecommunication Regulatory Commission (BTRC) granted formal authorization in September 2026, which the article says established a framework for NTN spectrum use and satellite-to-cellular interconnection.

VEON's release states this makes Bangladesh "the first country in South Asia to introduce the service"; SatNews repeats the claim in its own words. Frequency bands, voice support and data speeds are not stated.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Device side**: unmodified 4G handsets, standard SIM; article mentions only 4G, not 5G.
- **Offer**: five free satellite-routed SMS per subscriber per month; one complimentary month of satellite data at onboarding.
- **Regulatory**: BTRC authorization, September 2026.
- **Context cited**: VEON sister companies Kyivstar (Ukraine; SatNews reports 3.0 million users, while VEON's April 2026 release cites more than 5 million unique customers connected at least once) and Beeline Kazakhstan also work with Starlink Direct to Cell.
- **Stated goal**: emergency communications during severe weather in flood-prone areas.
- **Not stated**: bands, voice, speeds.
