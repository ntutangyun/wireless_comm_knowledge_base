## Summary (EN)

Boldyn Networks has connected an environmental monitoring device from the agritech firm Pneumonitor to one of its private 5G networks in West Sussex using 5G Reduced Capability (RedCap, the 3GPP Rel-17 device class also marketed as NR-Light). The sensor sits inside livestock buildings and reports pen conditions back over the private network, giving farmers a near-real-time view of the indoor environment instead of the periodic manual checks the application previously depended on. Boldyn's own framing of the milestone is deliberately modest: connecting Pneumonitor through RedCap "shows how private 5G can support practical agritech".

The deployment sits inside the Growing Sussex 5G Innovation Region, a programme run with West Sussex County Council that won roughly GBP 3.8 million from the UK Department for Science, Innovation and Technology, under which Boldyn designed and built four private 5G networks across agricultural growing sites and colleges in the county, with the first networks going live in Q1 2025. The RedCap connection is a device-class milestone within that existing infrastructure rather than a new network build: the private 5G coverage was already there, and what is new is the class of endpoint now riding on it.

The stated benefits are operational rather than radio-technical — earlier warning of conditions that affect animal health and welfare, and faster response to weather-driven problems such as high temperatures inside buildings. No spectrum band, throughput, latency, battery-life or device-count figures are published, and the article presents the work as a capability demonstration rather than a completed project with measured outcomes. That limitation should be read plainly: this is an existence proof of RedCap in a real commercial private network serving a real customer application, not a performance result.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Device class**: 5G RedCap (3GPP Rel-17 Reduced Capability / NR-Light) — narrower bandwidth, fewer antennas and half-duplex operation relative to full NR, targeted at mid-tier IoT between LPWAN and full 5G.
- **Endpoint**: Pneumonitor environmental monitoring device deployed inside livestock buildings, reporting pen conditions continuously rather than by periodic manual inspection.
- **Network**: an existing Boldyn private 5G network, not a new build; the RedCap connection is an endpoint-class addition to already-live infrastructure.
- **Programme context**: Growing Sussex 5G Innovation Region with West Sussex County Council, ~GBP 3.8M from the UK DSIT, four private 5G networks across agricultural sites and colleges, first live Q1 2025.
- **Published metrics**: none. No band, bandwidth, throughput, latency, power or device-count figures appear in the announcement; benefits are stated qualitatively (animal health and welfare, response to high temperatures).
- **Article datestamp verified**: 27 August 2026 on the carrying article itself, distinct from the 2024/2025 Growing Sussex programme announcements that dominate search results for the same partners.
