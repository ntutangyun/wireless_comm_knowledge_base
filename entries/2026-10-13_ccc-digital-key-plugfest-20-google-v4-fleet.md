## Summary (EN)

On 12 October 2026 the Car Connectivity Consortium (CCC) announced, via Business Wire and its own press page, the 20th CCC Digital Key End-to-End Plugfest, hosted by Google in San Jose, California, on 12-16 October 2026. Listed participants are Apple, BMW Group, DEKRA, Ellisys, Ford Motor Company, Google, Motorola Mobility LLC, Rivian, Volkswagen Group Technologies LLC, Samsung and TTA. The release states that members are testing the newest set of Digital Key Version 4 capabilities for the first time; these extend digital key access into broader mobility use cases, namely fleets, rentals and service-based vehicle access. Listed test areas are new Version 4 test cases (Server-Based Owner Device, Server-Based Fleet Device, Fleet Management Server, Service Provider Server and other fleet and service-based access capabilities), Version 4 versus Version 3 cross-version interoperability to identify candidates for the Version 4 Interoperability Device List, Bluetooth LE sniffing validation, updated NFC test cases, and continued validation of remote keyless entry and passive entry. CCC President Alysia Johnson and Bahar Sadeghi (Senior Director, Technical Strategy and Advisory) are quoted. The release also announces a CCC Members Meeting in Vancouver on 20-22 October 2026 with a Fleet Forum open to members and non-members.

## Key technical points (EN)
