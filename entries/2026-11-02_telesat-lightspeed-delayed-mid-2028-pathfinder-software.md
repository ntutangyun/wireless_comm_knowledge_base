## Summary (EN)

SatNews (31 October 2026) reports that Telesat, on an earnings call on 29 October 2026, delayed commercial rollout of its Lightspeed LEO constellation by three months, now targeting full global service by the end of Q2 2028. The cause given is assembly and software development problems at prime contractor MDA Space, which delayed the first two pathfinder satellites.

The pathfinders move from a December launch to June 2027 on a SpaceX rideshare, followed by a 60-day in-orbit test campaign. Dedicated deployment launches begin in September 2027; the article gives no earlier full-service date.

## Summary (ZH)

SatNews（2026 年 10 月 31 日）报道，Telesat 在 2026 年 10 月 29 日的财报电话会上宣布，Lightspeed LEO 星座商用推出推迟三个月，目标为 2028 年第二季度末实现全球服务。原因是主承包商 MDA Space 的组装与软件开发问题导致前两颗试验星延误。

试验星发射从 12 月推迟至 2027 年 6 月，搭乘 SpaceX 拼车发射，之后进行 60 天在轨测试。专用部署发射自 2027 年 9 月开始；文章未给出此前的全面服务日期。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Pathfinders**: first two satellites slip from a December launch (year not stated) to June 2027 on a rideshare; Telesat chose not to buy a full Falcon 9 for two test satellites; 60-day in-orbit test campaign follows.
- **Deployment**: dedicated missions begin September 2027; 14 Falcon 9 launches contracted with SpaceX and a 15th planned.
- **Scale**: about 96 satellites for baseline global coverage; all 225 projected operational by end of 2028. A Space Intel Report projection cited in the article suggests 15 launches over 45 weeks could allow initial commercial service as early as March 2028.
- **Context**: CEO Dan Goldberg said Starlink disruptions in Ukraine and geopolitical tension have raised customer interest in secure sovereign communications. No financial figures reported. Single-outlet report.
