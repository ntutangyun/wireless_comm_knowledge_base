## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-20, China Mobile and Qualcomm Technologies announced they had completed an end-to-end interoperability test between a 6G prototype base station and a 6G prototype terminal in the U6G band, which the companies describe as compliant with the latest 3GPP definitions for the band. The test ran on China Mobile's "6G Open Co-Creation Testbed" (6G开放众创试验装置) at its Collaborative Innovation Base, with China Mobile supplying the base-station side (a 128-channel massive-MIMO array supporting 400 MHz of downlink bandwidth) and Qualcomm supplying the terminal-prototype platform (supporting 200 MHz of uplink bandwidth in a 4T8R configuration), using 30 kHz subcarrier spacing. The companies describe it as the world's first interoperability test between a 6G prototype base station and a 6G prototype terminal in the U6G band compliant with the latest 3GPP definitions. The figures above follow the announcement as carried by Sina Finance's relay of the same-day IT之家 report; c114pro.com carried a secondary report of the same event.

The U6G band referenced is the upper-6 GHz range (approximately 6425-7125 MHz) that multiple national and regional bodies have been considering for future mobile/6G use, distinct from the lower 6 GHz (5925-6425 MHz), which is licence-exempt Wi-Fi spectrum in the US and several other regions. The announcement frames the test as evidence that 6G's large-bandwidth, high-throughput link technologies are moving from standalone research into cross-vendor system-level validation ahead of formal 3GPP 6G standardization.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Date: 2026-09-20. Parties: China Mobile (operator, base-station side) and Qualcomm Technologies (chipset vendor, terminal-prototype side).
- Band: U6G (upper-6 GHz range, ~6425-7125 MHz), described as aligned with the latest 3GPP definition for the band.
- Bandwidth: 400 MHz downlink (base-station side) and 200 MHz uplink (terminal side); 30 kHz subcarrier spacing.
- Base station: 128-channel massive MIMO array, supplied/operated by China Mobile.
- Terminal: Qualcomm's 6G terminal-prototype platform, supporting 200 MHz uplink bandwidth in a 4T8R configuration.
- Venue: China Mobile's "6G Open Co-Creation Testbed" (6G开放众创试验装置) at its Collaborative Innovation Base.
- Claimed scope: an end-to-end (base station <-> terminal) interoperability/link test, not a standalone single-side demo; the companies describe it as the world's first interoperability test between a 6G prototype base station and a 6G prototype terminal in the U6G band compliant with the latest 3GPP definitions.
