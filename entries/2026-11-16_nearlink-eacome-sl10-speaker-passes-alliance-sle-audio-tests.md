## Summary (EN)

An article dated 2026-11-10 (IT之家 reporting, carried by ifeng Tech) says a speaker with model number SL10, belonging to Shenzhen EACOME Communication Co., Ltd. (EACOME brand), has appeared on the SparkLink Alliance website's list of tested products. The listing shows three conformance tests passed: SLE radio-frequency conformance, basic service layer protocol conformance, and high-definition audio protocol conformance. From this the article concludes the speaker "will support NearLink E2.0" (NearLink audio) and headlines it as the first NearLink speaker to come. The article gives no SL10-specific specifications, price or release date; it speculates the product targets office or conference use, given EACOME's smart-conferencing and education business.
