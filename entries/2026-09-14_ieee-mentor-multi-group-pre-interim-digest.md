## Summary (EN)

The most substantive item in this cluster is a technical proposal, not housekeeping: in TGm (REVmf, the 802.11 maintenance revision), Ericsson proposes normative text recommending that access points confine themselves to the three non-overlapping 2.4 GHz channels — "An AP should not operate on the 2.4 GHz channels 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12 and 13" — and answers five objections raised since the July 2026 TGmf meeting. Alongside it, TGm uploaded two genuine Letter Ballot 295 comment resolutions (InterDigital on CIDs 2416/2418/2419, Intel on "ED1" comments), continuing the LB295 work this KB has tracked since 2026-09-02. The remaining uploads are administrative: a September 2026 Editors' meeting agenda (Huawei) and the 802.18 (Radio Regulatory) liaison report (HPE) at Working Group level, an ARC SC September agenda (Ruckus), an AI Offload SG agenda revision (Qualcomm), and — from the Unified Channel Model (UCM) Topic Interest Group — revised July minutes and a September status snapshot (Fraunhofer HHI) whose subject matter is ray tracing, optical channel models and tri-band sub-7 GHz / mmWave / optical channel measurements. A substantial set of further uploads in the same groups and window is named at the end of the key points and not covered individually.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **DCN 11-26-1803r0 · TGm** — "Follow-up on 11-26/1274". Guido R. Hiertz (Ericsson), uploaded 11-Sep-2026. **Not a comment resolution**: it is a technical proposal. Building on 11-26/1274, in which the authors explained why they recommend APs confine themselves to channels 1, 6 and 11, this deck proposes the normative sentence "An AP should not operate on the 2.4 GHz channels 2, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12 and 13" and addresses comments received since the July 2026 TGmf meeting. Its five answers: channel 14 is omitted because Japan restricts it to DSSS and 802.11 has not improved DSSS since 1999; the "should not" form is a recommendation, so 40 MHz operation in 2.4 GHz is not prohibited (though the authors note industry best practice discourages it); already-shipped products stay compliant with the standard current at their market introduction, so the change is not retroactive; non-AP STAs are unaffected and may keep associating on other channels; and the authors offer to add a further "should" recommending that non-AP STAs scan channels 1, 6 and 11 first. ## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The headline item is DCN 1803. It asks the 802.11 maintenance revision to write into the standard what has been deployment folklore for two decades: that APs should stay on 2.4 GHz channels 1, 6 and 11. That is a normative change to AP channel selection in the most crowded band 802.11 operates in, and the deck's five answers show it is being contested on exactly the practical grounds one would expect — Japan's channel 14, 40 MHz operation, the status of already-shipped products, and the effect on clients. Because it is framed as a "should", it would guide rather than constrain implementations, and the authors explicitly confirm it is not retroactive. The two genuine LB295 comment resolutions (1709, 1804) continue the maintenance work this KB has tracked since 2026-09-02 (`2026-09-02_ieee-mentor-tgm-revmf-lb295-digest`), and the remaining WG-level, ARC SC, AI Offload SG and UCM TIG uploads are agendas, liaison reports and status snapshots of the kind that cluster ahead of an interim. The UCM TIG snapshot is worth a second look on its own terms: the group's subject is channel modelling — ray tracing, optical channel models and same-scenario tri-band measurements — and it is pushing for open-source ray-tracing tooling usable in standardization. The novelty score is raised from the administrative default because 1803 proposes normative text, not housekeeping. Readers should also note the long list of further uploads named above; the window carried substantive contributions in WIN SG, AI Offload SG and TGbt that this entry names but does not analyse.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

本组最值得关注的是 DCN 1803。它要求 802.11 维护性修订把二十年来只存在于部署经验中的做法写进标准：AP 应当固定在 2.4 GHz 的 1、6、11 号信道上。这是针对 802.11 所使用的最拥挤频段中 AP 信道选择的一项规范性修改，而文稿中的五点回应显示，反对意见恰恰集中在可以预期的实务层面——日本的 14 号信道、40 MHz 工作、已上市产品的合规状态，以及对客户端的影响。由于采用"宜（should）"的表述，它只起引导作用而非强制约束，作者也明确确认该修改不具追溯效力。两份真正的 LB295 意见处理文档（1709、1804）延续了本知识库自 2026-09-02 起跟踪的维护性工作（`2026-09-02_ieee-mentor-tgm-revmf-lb295-digest`）；其余工作组层面、ARC SC、AI Offload SG 与 UCM TIG 的上传，则属于临时会议前照例集中出现的议程、联络报告与状态快照。UCM TIG 的快照本身也值得再看一眼：该组的主题是信道建模——射线追踪、光信道模型与同场景三频段测量——并正在推动可用于标准化工作的开源射线追踪工具。新颖度评分之所以高于行政类默认值，是因为 1803 提出的是规范性文本，而非事务性材料。读者还应注意上文列出的大量其他上传：本窗口内在 WIN SG、AI Offload SG 与 TGbt 中都有实质性技术贡献，本条目仅列名而未加分析。
