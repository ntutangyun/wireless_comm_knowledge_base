## Summary (EN)

SatNews reports (22 October 2026) that Korea Testing Laboratory (KTL), South Korea's national certification body, has selected Keysight Technologies' New Radio Non-Terrestrial Network (NR-NTN) test solutions. The tools give KTL radio-frequency (RF) and radio resource management (RRM) validation for acceptance testing of satellite-enabled devices and networks.

The article describes equipment vendors shifting from early LTE-based direct-to-device services to 3GPP NR-NTN; it says earlier proprietary systems supported basic emergency messaging, while NR-NTN supports broad device categories and higher throughput. Device makers, chipset vendors and operators must validate RF performance and interoperability through standardised acceptance testing before commercial launch.

KTL will integrate Keysight's Wireless Network Emulation Solutions for pre-conformance and formal certification workflows. The emulation covers simulated orbital dynamics, including high Doppler shifts and variable propagation delays. The article says Keysight's portfolio includes expanded test-case coverage recently approved by the Global Certification Forum (GCF), and that the benches target 3GPP Release 18 and Release 19 chipsets. It builds on an earlier Keysight-Samsung validation of Release 19 NR-NTN over S-band. KTL's Song Tae-seung and Keysight's Muthu Kumaran are quoted on independent validation reducing deployment risk. The article ties the pre-conformance pipeline to satellite-enabled devices entering commercial operation through 2027, and gives no contract terms, frequency bands for the KTL bench or test-case counts.
