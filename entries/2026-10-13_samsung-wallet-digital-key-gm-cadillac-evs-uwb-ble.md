## Summary (EN)

Samsung Electronics America announced on 7 October 2026 that Samsung Wallet's Digital Key now supports select GM vehicles in the United States and Canada, beginning with select 2026 and 2027 Cadillac EVs, including the 2025-2027 LYRIQ, 2026-2027 OPTIQ and 2027 VISTIQ; additional models are expected by the end of the year. With a compatible Galaxy device and Digital Key enabled, users can unlock, lock and start the vehicle without a physical key. The release states that, using UWB and Bluetooth LE, the car unlocks automatically as the user approaches without touching the phone, and locks automatically once the user walks away. Owners can share Digital Key access, set permissions and revoke access remotely in Samsung Wallet, and can suspend or delete a key for a lost or stolen device remotely through Samsung Find. Keys are stored in the embedded secure element, Samsung Wallet is protected by Samsung Knox, and the release states that Digital Keys meet EAL6+ certification standards. A footnote lists UWB-capable Galaxy devices from the Note 20 Ultra and S21 series through the S26 series, and Z Fold2 through Z Fold8 and Z TriFold. Woncheol Chai (EVP and Head of the Digital Wallet Team, Samsung MX Business) is quoted.
