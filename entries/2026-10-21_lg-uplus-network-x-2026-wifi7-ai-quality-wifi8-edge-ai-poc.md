## Summary (EN)

A bylined news article (Hyun-Seon Park, published 2026-10-19), reproduced on the WBA site on 2026-10-20, reports that LG Uplus exhibited next-generation Wi-Fi technology at Network X 2026, held 12-15 October at Messe Wien in Vienna. The article gives three items from the booth: a commercial Wi-Fi 7 deployment supporting the 6 GHz band (a router using the 2.4, 5 and 6 GHz bands), AI-based quality management technology, and proof-of-concept (PoC) results for Wi-Fi 8-based edge AI.

According to the article, the quality management technology analyses network performance data collected from access points to identify causes of service degradation and alert customers to potential problems before they experience disruptions. The edge AI item is described as routers equipped with neural processing units (NPUs) that analyse and optimise network conditions locally, without relying on cloud servers. The article says LG Uplus plans to continue advancing the technology so that AI can independently identify the causes of network failures and automatically restore service.

The page does not give PoC metrics, the router or chipset vendor, the NPU specification, or the Wi-Fi 8 features used; the Wi-Fi 8 reference is stated only as "Wi-Fi 8-based edge AI".

## Summary (ZH)

页面未给出 PoC 指标、路由器或芯片厂商、NPU 规格以及所用的 Wi-Fi 8 特性；对 Wi-Fi 8 的提法仅为“基于 Wi-Fi 8 的边缘 AI”。
