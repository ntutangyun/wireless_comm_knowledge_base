## Summary (EN)

Eutelsat announced on 2026-10-02 that the first 32 satellites of its OneWeb constellation renewal program have completed production at Airbus Defence and Space's facility in Toulouse, France, and are now being prepared for shipment ahead of an upcoming SpaceX launch. The release calls this "a major milestone in Eutelsat's programme to renew and enhance the OneWeb constellation", and says these are the first in Eutelsat's planned programme of 669 new OneWeb satellites (per this KB's 2026-09-11 entry: 440 previously ordered plus 229 ordered 2026-09-10). Eutelsat CEO Jean-François Fallacher called it "an exciting moment for Eutelsat as our first new OneWeb satellites leave the production line and head towards launch," while Airbus Defence and Space's Head of Space Systems, Alain Fauré, described the milestone as both "a symbol of our industrial relocalisation" and "confirmation of our high volume, high cadence satellite delivery capability." The release states the new satellites will assure continuity of service for customers and enhance the network's capabilities, progressively replacing early-generation satellites as they reach end of operational life.
