## Summary (EN)
Ngo Hoang Tu, Huy T. Nguyen, Mao V. Ngo, Tran Thien Thanh, Vo Nguyen Quoc Bao, and Tony Q. S. Quek (Van Lang University; Ho Chi Minh City University of Transport; Singapore University of Technology and Design) present a synthesis of agreed 3GPP material across all three Technical Specification Groups (TSG RAN, SA, CT) as of a fixed snapshot date, 30 June 2026, following the June 2026 TSG plenary meetings and the associated 6G workshop in Singapore. Rather than offering another forward-looking 6G vision paper, the authors track the study-to-specification machinery: 3GPP has concluded two releases are needed for 6G — Release 20 (6G study phase, running alongside continued 5G-Advanced normative work) and Release 21 (first normative 6G specifications and the IMT-2030 submission, delivered as a single drop with a unified code freeze; its agreed schedule places the Stage-1 freeze in March 2027, Stage-2 in June 2028, Stage-3 in December 2028, and the interface-definition freeze in March 2029). Every cited item is tagged by status at the snapshot date — A (approved, under change control), D (draft, agreed at working-group level), P (individual proposal, not agreed), or T (vendor/operator trial, feasibility evidence only) — to avoid overstating what is settled.

On the most mature track, Stage 1: SA1's 6G use-case and service-requirements study (TR 22.870) was approved at the March 2026 TSG#111 plenary; by June 2026 the normative TS 22.270 had passed 20% completion with 163 consolidated potential requirements across eight clusters (system/operational aspects, AI, ISAC, ubiquitous connectivity, immersive communication, massive communication, industry/verticals, compute-centric networking). The paper highlights concrete new KPIs: 50 ms burst latency for a 400 KB generative-AI image-generation burst, 400 ms for a 20 MB video burst, 64-400 Mbps uplink for edge-rendered AI, satellite positioning accuracy of 1-5 m, and gigabit holographic telepresence at 99.999% reliability. On architecture, SA2's study (TR 23.801-01) has identified 24 key issues on an evolved Service-Based Architecture baseline that integrates terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks "from day one," targeting completion by March 2027; SA3 security work (TR 33.801-01) proceeds in parallel across seven areas. On radio, RAN approved a study on 6G scenarios and requirements (TR 38.914) at TSG RAN#112 (8-12 June 2026), with a WG-level radio-interface study due June 2027 and the normative Work Item phase scheduled March 2027-December 2028. From reading these three workstreams together, the authors offer seven cross-cutting lessons (presented as their own synthesis, not 3GPP positions), including that AI has moved from a feature to a cross-stage primitive, non-terrestrial access has crossed from overlay to substrate, connectivity is now one objective among three alongside computing and sensing, the industry favors deployability over clean-slate redesign (spectrum access and site reuse, rather than waveform innovation, appear to be the tighter constraint), and standardization is targeting AI workflow interoperability rather than specific AI models, with testability across vendors recurring as a decisive criterion for which techniques survive.

## Summary (ZH)
Ngo Hoang Tu、Huy T. Nguyen、Mao V. Ngo、Tran Thien Thanh、Vo Nguyen Quoc Bao 与 Tony Q. S. Quek（文郎大学；胡志明市交通大学；新加坡科技设计大学）对截至固定快照日期（2026 年 6 月 30 日）、3GPP 三个技术规范组（TSG RAN、SA、CT）已达成一致的材料进行了综合梳理，该时间点紧随 2026 年 6 月 TSG 全会及新加坡配套 6G 研讨会。论文并未再提出一份前瞻性的 6G 愿景文章，而是追踪"从研究项到规范"的标准化机制：3GPP 已确定需要两个版本（Release）来完成 6G 标准化——Release 20（6G 研究阶段，与持续推进的 5G-Advanced 规范工作并行）与 Release 21（首批正式 6G 规范及 IMT-2030 提交，以单一版本、统一代码冻结的方式交付；已商定的时间表为：第一阶段冻结 2027 年 3 月、第二阶段 2028 年 6 月、第三阶段 2028 年 12 月，接口定义冻结 2029 年 3 月）。文中引用的每一项内容均标注其在快照日期的状态——A（已批准，纳入变更控制）、D（草案，工作组层面已达成一致）、P（个人提案，尚未达成一致）或 T（厂商/运营商试验，仅作为可行性证据）——以避免夸大已确定的内容。

在最成熟的第一阶段轨道上：SA1 关于 6G 用例与服务需求的研究（TR 22.870）已于 2026 年 3 月 TSG#111 全会获批；截至 2026 年 6 月，正式规范 TS 22.270 已完成 20%，在八大需求集群（系统与运营方面、AI、ISAC、泛在连接、沉浸式通信、海量通信、行业与垂直应用、以计算为中心的网络）下整合了 163 项潜在需求。论文列出了具体的新关键性能指标：面向生成式 AI 的 400 KB 图像生成突发流量时延为 50 毫秒，20 MB 视频突发流量时延为 400 毫秒，边缘渲染 AI 所需上行速率为 64–400 Mbps，卫星定位精度为 1–5 米，全息远程呈现需达到千兆比特级速率且可靠性为 99.999%。在架构方面，SA2 的研究（TR 23.801-01）已识别出 24 个关键问题，基于"从第一天起"即整合地面与非地面网络的演进型服务化架构（SBA）基线，目标在 2027 年 3 月前完成；SA3 安全工作（TR 33.801-01）在七个领域并行推进。在无线接入方面，RAN 于 2026 年 6 月 8–12 日的 TSG RAN#112 全会批准了关于 6G 场景与需求的研究（TR 38.914），工作组层面的无线接口研究预计于 2027 年 6 月完成，正式工作项阶段计划在 2027 年 3 月至 2028 年 12 月间进行。通过将三条工作线一并解读，作者提出了七条跨领域的归纳性结论（明确说明是作者自身的综合判断，而非 3GPP 的正式立场），包括：AI 已从一项功能演变为贯穿各阶段的基础原语；非地面接入已从"叠加层"转变为"基础层"；连接性如今与计算、感知并列，成为三大目标之一；产业界更倾向于可部署性而非从零重设（频谱接入与站点复用（而非波形创新）似乎才是更紧的约束）；标准化的目标是 AI 工作流的互操作性而非特定 AI 模型，跨厂商可测试性反复成为决定哪些技术得以保留的一项关键标准。

## Key technical points (EN)
- Snapshot-dated (30 June 2026) synthesis across 3GPP TSG RAN, SA, and CT, with every cited item tagged A/D/P/T by approval status to avoid overstating consensus.
- 6G requires two releases: Release 20 (study phase, parallel to 5G-Advanced) and Release 21 (first normative 6G specs + IMT-2030 submission; Stage-1/2/3 freezes March 2027/June 2028/December 2028, interface freeze March 2029).
- SA1's TR 22.870 (6G use cases) approved March 2026; TS 22.270 at 20% completion with 163 requirements across 8 clusters by June 2026.
- New concrete KPIs cited: 50 ms / 400 ms burst latency for 400 KB / 20 MB generative-AI bursts, 64-400 Mbps uplink for edge-rendered AI, 1-5 m satellite positioning accuracy, 99.999%-reliable gigabit holographic telepresence.
- SA2 architecture study (TR 23.801-01) has 24 key issues, targets terrestrial/non-terrestrial integration "from day one," completion targeted March 2027; RAN approved TR 38.914 (6G scenarios/requirements) at TSG RAN#112, June 2026.
- Seven cross-cutting lessons (authors' synthesis): AI as a cross-stage primitive, NTN as substrate not overlay, connectivity/computing/sensing as co-equal objectives, deployability over redesign, spectrum access and site reuse appearing to be the tighter constraint, AI-workflow (not model) standardization with cross-vendor testability as the survival criterion.
