## Summary (EN)

A Bluetooth SIG blog post by Henry Wong (22 October 2026) states that all of the "big five" hearing aid manufacturers - Sonova, Demant, GN, WS Audiology and Starkey - now offer hearing aids that support Auracast broadcast audio. The post says these companies together hold about 90 percent of the global hearing aid market, calls this "a significant milestone" and says Auracast is "no longer a future promise". Citing the World Health Organization, it says nearly 2.5 billion people are projected to have some degree of hearing loss by 2050, and more than 700 million are expected to need hearing rehabilitation services.

The post says an Auracast broadcast can be received by hearing aids, earbuds, headphones and assistive listening receivers without venue-specific hardware, and that it works through consumer devices such as smartphones, speakers and TVs. Named deployments are Stadium Taranaki (a 21,000-seat outdoor venue) and Isaac Theatre Royal (Christchurch) in New Zealand, the MOVIX cinema chain operated by Shochiku Multiplex Theaters in Japan, the hearing care provider SONEO Hörgeräte (Saarland, Germany), and Resonate Health (New Zealand). It says deployments span clinics, houses of worship, stadiums, theatres, entertainment venues and universities. This is SIG-authored promotional content; the market-share and manufacturer-support claims are the post's own and were not independently verified.
