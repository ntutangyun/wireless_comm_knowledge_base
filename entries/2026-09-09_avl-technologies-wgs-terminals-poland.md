## Summary (EN)
AvL Technologies (Asheville, NC) was awarded a $31 million contract with Polish defense supplier GISS Sp. z o.o. to supply Wideband Global SATCOM (WGS)-certified satellite terminals to Poland's Ministry of National Defence. The terminal is a 2.4-meter trailer-mounted driveaway antenna with a high-rigidity carbon-fiber reflector and automated antenna-positioning system, certified for simultaneous X-band (chosen for weather penetration and atmospheric-interference resistance) and military Ka-band (for high-throughput bandwidth, video surveillance, and secure voice) operation. WGS certification means the terminals meet the same standards as the US Department of Defense's Wideband Global SATCOM constellation, allowing Polish defence forces to securely interface with U.S. and allied space assets during multinational operations. Production occurs at AvL's Asheville facility, with field integration handled by GISS in Poland; initial deliveries are scheduled across Poland's ongoing force-modernization phases. The procurement is funded under the EU's Security Action for Europe (SAFE) programme, under which Poland -- SAFE's largest recipient -- was allocated EUR 43.7 billion of the programme's EUR 150 billion loan envelope.

## Key technical points (EN)
- **Terminal:** 2.4 m trailer-mounted driveaway antenna; high-rigidity carbon-fiber reflector; automated antenna-positioning system for rapid signal acquisition in field conditions.
- **Bands:** simultaneous X-band (weather/atmospheric-interference resistance) and military Ka-band (high-throughput bandwidth, video surveillance, secure voice) dual-band support.
- **Certification:** WGS-certified, i.e. compliant with US DoD Wideband Global SATCOM constellation standards, allowing Polish defence forces to securely interface with U.S. and allied space assets during multinational operations.
- **Contract:** $31M, AvL Technologies (manufacturer, Asheville NC) with GISS Sp. z o.o. (Polish integrator); funded under the EU SAFE programme (Poland, the programme's largest recipient, was allocated EUR 43.7B of its EUR 150B loan envelope).
- **Deployment:** production in Asheville, field integration in Poland, deliveries phased across Poland's force-modernization program.
