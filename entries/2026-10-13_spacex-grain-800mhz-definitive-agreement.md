## Summary (EN)

SatNews (article dated 2026-10-11) reports that SpaceX announced on 2026-10-08 a definitive agreement to acquire Grain Management's nationwide 800 MHz low-band spectrum portfolio: up to 14 MHz of paired spectrum in the 800 MHz band, formerly part of Sprint's 800 MHz SMR band, which passed to T-Mobile after the Sprint merger and which Grain acquired from T-Mobile in August 2026 through an asset swap involving cash and Grain's 600 MHz holdings. Financial media reported about $8 billion in cash; the article says official filings did not disclose financial terms and it names no specific press release or filing as its source. According to the article, the spectrum would be used with SpaceX's newly authorized 15,000-satellite very-low-Earth-orbit constellation to deliver indoor 5G service to unmodified smartphones, and after regulatory approval SpaceX plans to integrate the 800 MHz band into its next-generation Starlink satellite production lines, combining it with 2 GHz mid-band capacity and onboard digital beamforming compute. The deal is subject to customary closing conditions and FCC and U.S. Department of Justice review. The article quotes Elon Musk: "This is the last critical piece of the spectrum puzzle needed for SpaceX to provide complete phone coverage in America."

- **Price**: about $8 billion cash per financial media; official filings reportedly did not disclose terms.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Per the article, owning low-band spectrum moves SpaceX from leasing partner-operator spectrum (such as T-Mobile's PCS allocation) toward direct control of its direct-to-cell service; the deal awaits FCC and DOJ review.
