## Summary (EN)

MDA Space announced on 2026-11-10 that all eight Globalstar 2-R replenishment satellites launched on 2026-08-15 have completed initial on-orbit functional tests. The eight are part of a 17-satellite order for Globalstar's existing low-Earth-orbit (HIBLEO) constellation, which the release says supports direct-to-device services on select mobile phones and IoT applications. Payload health checks are under way. Electric orbit raising has begun for the first two satellites, and orbit raising for the first set will continue with Rocket Lab support (Rocket Lab designed, built and commissioned the satellite platforms, per the release). The satellites will climb to a final testing altitude before being manoeuvred into their final orbit, while Globalstar and MDA conduct in-orbit payload and bus testing as the orbital planes align for the remaining six. Final integration of the other nine satellites was recently completed at MDA's Montreal facility. MDA's CEO describes MDA as prime contractor and says its focus remains on delivery to the customer. Other reporting cites a $143 million Rocket Lab bus agreement and a prime contract of roughly C$415 million (about US$327 million); neither figure is in the MDA release.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Status:** 8 of 17 satellites launched 2026-08-15; all eight passed initial on-orbit functional tests (announced 2026-11-10); payload health checks continuing.
