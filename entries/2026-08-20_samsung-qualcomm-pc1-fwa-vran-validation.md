## Summary (EN)
Samsung and Qualcomm announced (Samsung Newsroom, May 6, 2026) the industry's first validation of **Power Class 1 (PC1)** operation for 5G **Fixed Wireless Access (FWA)** on a fully virtualized RAN. PC1 is the highest-power 3GPP UE power class (lower class number = higher transmit power); putting it in an FWA terminal attacks the structural weak side of FWA economics — the **uplink** — where cell-edge subscribers otherwise drag down capacity or fall off coverage. The network side ran Samsung's vRAN software with Samsung 3.7 GHz massive-MIMO radios; the terminal side used the **Qualcomm Dragonwing FWA Gen 4 platform** with the **X85 Modem-RF** chipset. Validation started in Samsung's R&D lab and progressed to field tests on a U.S. Tier 1 operator's network. Headline numbers versus a PC1.5 device: **up to 10x uplink throughput at the cell edge** and **up to 40% range extension (1.4x coverage expansion)**. Commercial availability of PC1 FWA devices is expected in **2027**. Samsung frames the result as vRAN feature-parity proof — the highest-power UE class working against software RAN plus mMIMO, not just classic purpose-built basebands; Qualcomm positions PC1 as expanding "both the performance and reach of FWA devices."

## Summary (ZH)
三星与高通宣布（三星新闻编辑室，2026 年 5 月 6 日）完成业界首个在全虚拟化 RAN 上运行 5G **固定无线接入（FWA）** **Power Class 1（PC1）** 的验证。PC1 是 3GPP 最高功率的 UE 功率等级（等级数字越小、发射功率越高）；将其用于 FWA 终端直击 FWA 经济性的结构性短板——**上行链路**——否则小区边缘用户要么拉低容量、要么掉出覆盖。网络侧运行三星 vRAN 软件配三星 3.7 GHz massive-MIMO 射频；终端侧采用**高通 Dragonwing FWA Gen 4 平台**与 **X85 Modem-RF** 芯片组。验证始于三星研发实验室，随后在美国一家 Tier 1 运营商网络上完成现场测试。相对 PC1.5 终端的标志性数据：**小区边缘上行吞吐最高 10 倍**、**覆盖半径最高扩展 40%（1.4 倍覆盖面积）**。PC1 FWA 终端预计 **2027 年**商用。三星将此定位为 vRAN 特性对齐的证明——最高功率 UE 等级在软件 RAN + mMIMO 上跑通，而非仅限传统专用基带；高通则称 PC1 "同时扩展 FWA 设备的性能与触达范围"。

## Key technical points (EN)
- First PC1 (highest 3GPP UE power class) validation for 5G FWA on virtualized RAN; baseline comparison is PC1.5.
- Network: Samsung vRAN + Samsung 3.7 GHz massive-MIMO radios; device: Qualcomm Dragonwing FWA Gen 4 platform + X85 Modem-RF.
- Gains vs PC1.5: up to 10x cell-edge uplink throughput; up to 40% range extension / 1.4x coverage expansion.
- Validation path: Samsung R&D lab → field tests on a U.S. Tier 1 operator network.
- Commercial PC1 FWA devices expected 2027.
- Strategic read: uplink power is the FWA coverage currency — higher-power CPE lets operators serve more subscribers per site before densifying.
