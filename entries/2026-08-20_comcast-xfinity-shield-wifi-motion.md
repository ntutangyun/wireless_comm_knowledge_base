## Summary (EN)
On August 18, 2026 Comcast launched Xfinity Shield, a bundled cyber- and home-protection platform for Xfinity Internet customers, and with it began the widest consumer rollout of Wi-Fi sensing to date: the opt-in WiFi Motion feature turns the customer's existing Xfinity Gateway into a motion detector, with rollout to eligible customers starting the week of August 19. Shield comes in two tiers — WiFi Shield, included at no extra cost with Xfinity Internet (cybersecurity protections, parental controls, and WiFi Motion), and Shield Select at $15/month, which adds hardware, AI-enhanced monitoring, and access to emergency services. Comcast's press materials frame the sensing plainly: "An opt-in feature, WiFi Motion uses the WiFi signal from a customer's Xfinity Gateway to sense movement in their home — no cameras needed or recordings captured."

Mechanically, WiFi Motion monitors disturbances in the Wi-Fi signal paths between the gateway and up to three customer-selected stationary Wi-Fi devices (smart plugs, printers, speakers, etc.), which act as fixed reflect/receive anchors. The Xfinity app exposes three monitoring modes — Home Watch (residents present), Away Watch (absence), and Dark Watch (overnight) — and lets customers configure sensing zones. Compatible hardware spans the XB7, XB8, and XB10 gateways plus XE2 Wi-Fi Extenders; detection quality depends on gateway placement, signal strength, home layout, and building materials. Comcast (and the Wi-Fi NOW coverage) explicitly pushes back on media claims that the system can image or identify individuals — it is presence/motion detection, not through-wall imaging.

The launch drew immediate privacy scrutiny alongside the product coverage. Reporting around the launch (The Register, TechTimes, Gizmodo, 9to5Mac) highlighted that enabling WiFi Motion creates a timestamped household movement record held by Comcast, and that Comcast's support documentation states such records may be disclosed to law enforcement or in litigation without an obligation to notify the subscriber. Because the sensing rides on leased ISP gateways already installed in tens of millions of US homes, the feature converts an installed base into a sensing network at the flip of a software switch — a scale no dedicated Wi-Fi-sensing product (or the still-unfinished 802.11bf certification ecosystem) has approached.

## Summary (ZH)
2026 年 8 月 18 日，康卡斯特（Comcast）发布 Xfinity Shield——面向 Xfinity 宽带用户的网络安全与家庭防护打包平台，并借此启动了迄今规模最大的消费级 Wi-Fi 感知部署：选择性开启（opt-in）的 WiFi Motion 功能将用户现有的 Xfinity 网关变成运动探测器，8 月 19 日当周开始向符合条件的用户推送。Shield 分两档：WiFi Shield 随 Xfinity 宽带免费提供（网络安全防护、家长控制与 WiFi Motion）；Shield Select 每月 15 美元，增加硬件、AI 增强监控与紧急服务接入。康卡斯特的官方表述直白：“WiFi Motion 是一项可选功能，利用用户 Xfinity 网关的 WiFi 信号感知家中的运动——无需摄像头，也不录制任何影像。”

发布同时引发了隐私层面的密切关注。围绕本次发布的报道（The Register、TechTimes、Gizmodo、9to5Mac）指出：开启 WiFi Motion 会在康卡斯特侧生成带时间戳的家庭活动记录，且康卡斯特的支持文档写明此类记录可依执法要求或诉讼程序披露，无义务通知订户。由于该感知功能运行在已安装于数千万美国家庭的租赁 ISP 网关上，一次软件开关即可把存量设备变成感知网络——这一规模是任何专用 Wi-Fi 感知产品（乃至尚未完成的 802.11bf 认证生态）都未曾接近的。

## Key technical points (EN)
- Xfinity Shield launched 2026-08-18; two tiers: WiFi Shield (free with Xfinity Internet: cybersecurity, parental controls, WiFi Motion) and Shield Select ($15/month: hardware, AI-enhanced monitoring, emergency services).
- WiFi Motion is opt-in and senses movement by monitoring disturbances in Wi-Fi signal paths between the gateway and up to 3 stationary customer Wi-Fi devices; no cameras, no recordings.
- Three app-configurable monitoring modes: Home Watch, Away Watch, Dark Watch; user-defined sensing zones.
- Compatible hardware: Xfinity XB7 / XB8 / XB10 gateways and XE2 Wi-Fi Extenders — i.e., the sensing runs entirely on the existing leased CPE installed base (tens of millions of homes).
- Performance depends on gateway placement, signal strength, home layout, and building materials; Comcast states the system cannot image or identify individuals.
- Privacy dimension raised by launch coverage: enabling the feature creates a timestamped household movement log that Comcast documentation says can be disclosed to law enforcement or in litigation without subscriber notification.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Comcast reaches production presence-detection at tens-of-millions-of-gateways scale years before 802.11bf-certified silicon, using only signal-disturbance measurement between existing devices — evidence that the commercially valuable sensing tier is coarse motion detection, not the fine-grained imaging the research bin studies. The subpoena-able movement-log angle lands directly on the sensing-privacy debate the KB has been tracking: the threat model shifts from over-the-air CSI eavesdroppers to the service provider as a lawful data custodian — a dimension none of the technical-defense literature in the bin addresses.
