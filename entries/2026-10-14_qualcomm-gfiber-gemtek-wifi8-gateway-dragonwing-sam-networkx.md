## Summary (EN)

Wi-Fi NOW's coverage of Qualcomm's Network X 2026 announcements (Vienna, article dated 13 October 2026) lists three items. First, gFiber (formerly Google Fiber), Qualcomm and Gemtek unveiled a residential gateway for gFiber's symmetrical 20 Gbps service, described as gFiber's flagship 20 Gbps router. It is built on the Qualcomm Dragonwing NPro A8 Elite platform with concurrent quad-band Wi-Fi 8 (802.11bn), a dedicated Hexagon NPU and a penta-core CPU. The article says it supports natural-language control of parental controls, guest access, smart-home integration and connectivity diagnostics, and has an interactive touchscreen. Existing gFiber 20 Gig customers get the equipment first, after certification, and gFiber plans a residential 50G PON trial in spring 2027. Qualcomm calls it "the local platform for agentic AI at the edge" and argues on-device AI keeps sensitive home-network data local. The article notes that its two gateway images are artist's renditions.

Second, Qualcomm formally launched Dragonwing SAM (Security and Management), a platform combining on-gateway intelligence with cloud capabilities for device identification, network monitoring, troubleshooting, security management and broadband-experience optimisation. It works with Qualcomm and third-party silicon and supports RDK-B, prplOS and OpenWrt. Qualcomm states that SAM agents are already deployed on more than 10 million home gateways, including Tier-1 operators in North America, Europe and Latin America.

Third, Qualcomm announced Dragonwing FWA Gen 6 Elite for fixed wireless access. Per the Wi-Fi NOW article it combines 5G Advanced (X105 modem-RF, 14.8 Gbps peak download and 4.2 Gbps peak upload, 3GPP Release 19-ready), tri-band Wi-Fi 8 with a 5x5 radio architecture, NR-NTN and NB-NTN satellite connectivity, and a fifth-generation 5G AI processor for congestion detection, traffic prioritisation, antenna management and interruption recovery. A separate CNX Software report adds platform details: on the Wi-Fi side, Wi-Fi 8/7/6E/6/5/4, up to 12 spatial streams, up to 4K QAM and AES-CCMP/GCMP; plus an integrated NPU of up to 10 TOPS, OpenWrt/prplOS support with the RDK-B SDK, and 2x 10GbE. That report itself notes that details are still light at this stage.

## Summary (ZH)

Wi-Fi NOW 对高通 Network X 2026（维也纳，文章日期 2026 年 10 月 13 日）发布内容的报道列出三项。其一，gFiber（原 Google Fiber）、高通与 Gemtek 发布面向 gFiber 对称 20 Gbps 宽带服务的家庭网关，文章称其为 gFiber 的旗舰 20 Gbps 路由器。它基于高通 Dragonwing NPro A8 Elite 平台，具备并发四频 Wi-Fi 8（802.11bn）、专用 Hexagon NPU 和五核 CPU。文章称其支持以自然语言控制家长控制、访客接入、智能家居集成与连接诊断，并配有交互式触摸屏。现有 gFiber 20 Gig 用户在认证后将首批获得设备，gFiber 计划于 2027 年春季开展住宅 50G PON 试验。高通称其为“边缘智能体 AI 的本地平台”，并认为设备端 AI 能让敏感的家庭网络数据留在本地。文章注明其中两张网关图片为示意渲染图。

其二，高通正式推出 Dragonwing SAM（安全与管理）平台，将网关端智能与云端能力结合，用于设备识别、网络监控、故障排查、安全管理和宽带体验优化。该平台可配合高通及第三方芯片，支持 RDK-B、prplOS 和 OpenWrt。高通表示 SAM 代理已部署在超过 1000 万台家庭网关上，涉及北美、欧洲和拉美的一级运营商。

其三，高通发布面向固定无线接入的 Dragonwing FWA Gen 6 Elite。据 Wi-Fi NOW 文章，它整合 5G Advanced（X105 调制解调器-射频系统，峰值下行 14.8 Gbps、上行 4.2 Gbps，支持 3GPP Release 19）、5×5 无线架构的三频 Wi-Fi 8、NR-NTN 与 NB-NTN 卫星连接，以及第五代 5G AI 处理器（用于拥塞检测、流量优先级、天线管理和中断恢复）。CNX Software 的另一篇报道补充了平台细节：Wi-Fi 方面支持 Wi-Fi 8/7/6E/6/5/4、最多 12 条空间流、最高 4K QAM 及 AES-CCMP/GCMP；此外集成最高 10 TOPS 的 NPU，支持 OpenWrt/prplOS 及 RDK-B SDK，配 2 个 10GbE 接口。该报道自身指出目前细节有限。

## Key technical points (EN)

- gFiber/Gemtek gateway: Dragonwing NPro A8 Elite, concurrent quad-band Wi-Fi 8 (802.11bn), Hexagon NPU, penta-core CPU, natural-language home-network control, touchscreen; existing gFiber 20 Gig customers first after certification.
- Dragonwing SAM: on-gateway plus cloud intelligence for device identification, monitoring, troubleshooting, security and experience optimisation; RDK-B, prplOS and OpenWrt; Qualcomm states deployment on more than 10 million gateways.
- Dragonwing FWA Gen 6 Elite (reported by Wi-Fi NOW and CNX Software): tri-band Wi-Fi 8 with 5x5 radios and up to 12 spatial streams, X105 5G modem-RF with NR-NTN/NB-NTN, integrated NPU up to 10 TOPS; the specification figures are vendor-supplied.
