## Summary (EN)

Three new contributions appeared on IEEE 802.11 Mentor in the 25–26 August 2026 window, past the previously tracked document number 11-26-1560. Two are TGbn (Wi-Fi 8 / UHR) technical submissions from Ruijie Networks, and one is the **first meeting agenda posted by the WIN SG** — the WLAN Intelligent Networking Study Group, which is the study group chartered to develop the PAR and criteria for what will become Wi-Fi 9. Its September 2026 agenda was uploaded by Laurent Cariou (Intel).

The TGbn pair sits on the multi-AP coordination thread. **11-26-1563** proposes MLME SAP support for MAPC traffic indication — i.e. exposing multi-AP-coordination traffic indication at the management-service-interface boundary rather than leaving it purely internal, which is the layer at which an implementation and a standard actually have to agree. **11-26-1562** offers further considerations on the "IM extension"; the acronym is not expanded in the public title and the document body is account-gated, so the precise feature is not determinable from the listing alone.

Two additional rows are noted but not counted as new technical content: **11-26-1521r04** is a revision of HaiLa Technologies' PDT on the AMP Group Ack frame format (a TGbp document already covered by earlier digests, revised 26 Aug), and **11-26-1551r00** is the TGbi CRG August telecon minutes from Cox — an administrative document whose document number sits *below* the tracking watermark but whose first upload only happened on 26 August.

As always for Mentor digests, only the title, author, affiliation and upload time are public; the `.docx` bodies return 418/403 to anonymous downloaders, so no claim here is made about document content beyond what the titles state.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Two things make this small batch worth recording. First, it puts a **name** on the study group whose formation this knowledge base captured in `2026-07-22_ieee-80211bn-d2-approval-wifi9-sg`: that entry recorded "a Wi-Fi 9 study group established" at the Montreal plenary but not its designation. The group is the **WIN SG — WLAN Intelligent Networking** — and its first public artefact is a September 2026 meeting agenda, chaired-side from Intel. That is the entity to watch for the Wi-Fi 9 PAR, and it sits alongside the separate AI-offload project tracked in `2026-07-19_ieee-p802-11bu-draft-par-ai-offload`.

Second, the MAPC thread is moving from mechanism design toward **interface definition**. A contribution on MLME SAP support for MAPC traffic indication is a sign that TGbn is now arguing about what has to be visible at the management-service boundary, not just what the coordinating APs do over the air — a later-stage question than the Co-SR/Co-BF comment resolutions that dominated the July and August Mentor traffic (see `2026-08-03_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest` and the TGbn rows in the preceding digests). Ruijie appearing twice in one evening on TGbn is also a small contributor-mix datapoint in a task group whose recent traffic has skewed toward Huawei, ZTE, Intel, Qualcomm and Hikvision.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
