## Summary (EN)
On Friday 14 August 2026 the FCC granted AST SpaceMobile a **30-day experimental special temporary authority (STA)** to test its satellite-to-smartphone (supplemental coverage from space) service in the **800 MHz band**: non-commercial testing with **up to 100 commercially available handsets**, using 817–824 MHz for the earth-to-space uplink and 862–869 MHz for the space-to-earth downlink (Broadband Breakfast, 17 Aug 2026; multiple outlets 14–17 Aug). The authorization carries the usual guardrails — controlled, non-commercial operation, coordination with the relevant spectrum holders, and immediate shutdown on harmful interference.

The spectrum angle is what makes this more than a routine test grant. The 817–824/862–869 MHz pair sits in the 800 MHz portfolio that **T-Mobile transferred to Grain Management in a ~$2.9 billion transaction** completed the same week — a deal AST SpaceMobile had publicly supported before the FCC, and which gives AST a path to nationwide low-band spectrum for its D2D service that is distinct from its existing per-carrier arrangements (AT&T/Verizon low-band in the US, plus FirstNet coordination). Low-band spectrum matters disproportionately for direct-to-device: propagation and building/foliage penetration at 800 MHz relax link budgets for the satellite-to-handset downlink relative to the 850 MHz cellular allocations AST has borrowed so far. Context from the same coverage: AST counts ~9 active commercial/test satellites in orbit as of early August 2026 and targets ~45 by early 2027; the FCC has authorized its 248-satellite constellation with buildout milestones of 124 satellites by August 2030; Starlink's competing direct-to-cell fleet exceeds 650 satellites.

## Summary (ZH)
2026 年 8 月 14 日（周五），FCC 授予 AST SpaceMobile 为期 **30 天的实验性临时特别授权（STA）**，在 **800 MHz 频段**测试其卫星直连智能手机（天基补充覆盖，SCS）服务：非商用测试，**最多 100 部商用现货手机**，上行（地对空）使用 817–824 MHz、下行（空对地）使用 862–869 MHz（Broadband Breakfast，2026 年 8 月 17 日；多家媒体 8 月 14–17 日报道）。授权附常规约束——受控、非商用运行，与相关频谱持有方协调，出现有害干扰须立即停止。

频谱来源使此事超出例行测试授权的意义：817–824/862–869 MHz 频对属于 **T-Mobile 以约 29 亿美元转让给 Grain Management 的 800 MHz 频谱组合**，交易于同一周完成——AST SpaceMobile 此前曾在 FCC 公开支持该交易，它为 AST 的手机直连服务提供了一条有别于现有按运营商合作模式（美国境内 AT&T/Verizon 低频段，另有 FirstNet 协调）的全国性低频段频谱路径。低频段对手机直连的意义尤为突出：800 MHz 的传播与建筑/植被穿透特性使星对手机下行链路预算比 AST 此前借用的 850 MHz 蜂窝频段更宽松。同期报道的背景：截至 2026 年 8 月初 AST 约有 9 颗在轨商用/测试卫星，目标 2027 年初约 45 颗；FCC 已授权其 248 星星座，建设里程碑为 2030 年 8 月前 124 颗；Starlink 竞争性直连蜂窝星座已超 650 颗。

## Key technical points (EN)
- **Grant:** FCC experimental special temporary authority, granted Friday 14 Aug 2026; 30-day term; non-commercial testing only.
- **Bands:** 817–824 MHz earth-to-space uplink, 862–869 MHz space-to-earth downlink (expanded-800 MHz block, ex-SMR heritage).
- **Devices:** up to 100 commercially available (unmodified) handsets — standard-phone testing, not test terminals.
- **Spectrum provenance:** part of the 800 MHz portfolio T-Mobile transferred to Grain Management for ~$2.9 B (completed the week of 11–14 Aug 2026); AST had supported the transaction before the FCC.
- **Conditions:** coordination with spectrum holders (reports name AT&T, Verizon and FirstNet among coordinating licensees), stay within existing agreements, cease on harmful interference.
- **Fleet status (per coverage):** ~9 active AST satellites in early Aug 2026, ~45 expected early 2027; FCC-authorized 248-sat constellation with 124-sat buildout milestone by Aug 2030, completion by Aug 2033.
- **Competitive frame:** AT&T and Verizon back AST for satellite supplement rather than Starlink (which partners with T-Mobile; >650 direct-to-cell satellites).

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The KB's AST thread has tracked hardware and business milestones — BlueBird Block-2 launches (2026-06-18, 2026-08-07), the commercial-service deferral to early 2027 (2026-07-22), and the Q2 beta build-out with 3,000 activated digital cells (2026-08-15). This entry adds the **spectrum-strategy** dimension: it is the first KB event where AST tests on low-band spectrum sourced through **Grain Management's newly acquired ex-T-Mobile 800 MHz portfolio**, rather than through per-carrier leases from AT&T/Verizon. If the testing matures into a long-term arrangement, AST would hold a nationwide 2×7 MHz low-band path that no single carrier controls — a fifth distinct D2D spectrum posture alongside the four the KB already tracks (Starlink's leased EchoStar terrestrial spectrum, AST's per-operator model to date, Amazon Leo's filed constellation, Equatys' multi-market MSS licenses; cf. 2026-08-14). The 100-commercial-handset scale also signals the pre-beta phase is moving from lab terminals to population-representative devices, consistent with the 3,000-digital-cell beta preparation. Watch items: whether an STA extension or a full experimental license follows the 30-day window; whether Grain formalizes a spectrum agreement with AST; and the note that discrepant satellite counts circulate (this coverage says ~9 active; AST's own Q2 update claimed 13 BlueBirds) — count claims should be keyed to AST filings, not press summaries.

知识库的 AST 脉络此前追踪硬件与商业里程碑——BlueBird Block-2 发射（2026-06-18、2026-08-07）、商用服务推迟至 2027 年初（2026-07-22）、以及 Q2 激活 3,000 个数字小区的测试网建设（2026-08-15）。本条目补上**频谱战略**维度：这是知识库中首个 AST 通过 **Grain Management 新收购的前 T-Mobile 800 MHz 组合**（而非向 AT&T/Verizon 逐家租借）获得低频段进行测试的事件。若测试发展为长期安排，AST 将握有一条不受任何单一运营商控制的全国性 2×7 MHz 低频段路径——成为知识库已追踪的四种 D2D 频谱姿态（Starlink 租用 EchoStar 地面频谱、AST 迄今的按运营商模式、Amazon Leo 的在批星座申请、Equatys 的多市场 MSS 牌照；参见 2026-08-14）之外的第五种。100 部商用手机的规模也表明预测试阶段正从实验终端转向具人群代表性的设备，与 3,000 数字小区的 beta 准备一致。观察项：30 天窗口后是否跟进 STA 延期或正式实验牌照；Grain 是否与 AST 正式签订频谱协议；另注意流传的卫星数目不一致（本报道称约 9 颗在轨；AST 自己的 Q2 通报称 13 颗 BlueBird）——卫星数应以 AST 官方文件为准，而非媒体摘要。
