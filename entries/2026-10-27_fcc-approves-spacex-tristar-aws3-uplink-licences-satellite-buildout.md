## Summary (EN)

This entry records the outcome of the licence transfer described in the KB's 2026-08-31 entry on SpaceX's application for Tristar's unpaired AWS-3 licences. According to trade-press reports of the order (Tesla North, 21 October 2026; Drive Tesla Canada, 22 October 2026), the FCC Wireless Telecommunications Bureau adopted and released a Memorandum Opinion and Order on 21 October 2026 approving the transfer of control of TLG Unpaired Holdings, a unit of Tristar License Group, to Starlink Spectrum Holdings, a SpaceX subsidiary. The order text was not available for checking; the FCC public notice that opened the proceeding (DA 26-747, released 17 July 2026, WT Docket No. 26-187) was read directly and confirms the application details.

The three licences are unpaired AWS-3 authorisations at 1695-1710 MHz (5 MHz each, uplink only) in three Economic Areas, covering 62 counties in eight states and about 4% of the US population (per the public notice, 28 Cellular Market Areas in all or part). The public notice records that, after the deal, SpaceX would be attributed with 75 MHz of spectrum in each relevant area, and that the applicants sought renewal plus waivers or extensions of the terrestrial build-out and service obligations. The trade-press reports say Tristar never built on the spectrum and the licences, due to expire on 5 July 2026, remained active through timely renewal filings.

As reported, the bureau waived the old tower-based construction requirements, the licence renewal standard and the rule that cancels licences after long periods without service. In exchange SpaceX must meet the same satellite build-out targets attached to the EchoStar spectrum: uplink speed and spectral-efficiency measures at two, four and nine years after 30 November 2027; reports say failing the final target means losing the licences. The reports state that no petition to deny was filed (one general comment about SpaceX was found not to address the transaction), that the competition review found the risk of harm low, that the decision took effect immediately with petitions for reconsideration allowed within 30 days, and that the price and closing date were not disclosed. The public notice records the applicants' statement that SpaceX's intended non-terrestrial use of this spectrum would be subject to the Commission's decision on its pending direct-to-device satellite application.

## Summary (ZH)

本条目记录知识库 2026-08-31 条目中所述 SpaceX 申请收购 Tristar 非成对 AWS-3 牌照一事的结果。据行业媒体对该命令的报道（Tesla North，2026 年 10 月 21 日；Drive Tesla Canada，2026 年 10 月 22 日），FCC 无线电信局于 2026 年 10 月 21 日通过并发布备忘录意见与命令，批准将 Tristar License Group 旗下 TLG Unpaired Holdings 的控制权转让给 SpaceX 子公司 Starlink Spectrum Holdings。命令原文未能查阅；但已直接阅读了启动该程序的 FCC 公告（DA 26-747，2026 年 7 月 17 日发布，WT Docket No. 26-187），其中的申请细节与报道一致。

三张牌照为 1695-1710 MHz 的非成对 AWS-3 授权（各 5 MHz，仅上行），覆盖三个经济区、八个州的 62 个县，约占美国人口的 4%（公告称涉及 28 个蜂窝市场区的全部或部分）。公告记载，交易完成后 SpaceX 在各相关地区将被认定持有 75 MHz 频谱，申请方同时请求牌照续期及对地面建设与服务义务的豁免或延期。媒体称 Tristar 从未在该频谱上建设网络，牌照原定 2026 年 7 月 5 日到期，因及时提交续期申请而保持有效。

据报道，该局豁免了原有基于铁塔的建设要求、牌照续期标准以及长期无服务即取消牌照的规则；作为交换，SpaceX 须满足与 EchoStar 频谱相同的卫星部署目标：自 2027 年 11 月 30 日起第 2、4、9 年的上行速率与频谱效率指标，报道称未达到最终目标将失去牌照。报道还称：无人提出否决请求（仅有一份泛泛评论 SpaceX 的意见，被认定与本交易无关）；竞争审查认为危害风险低；决定即时生效，30 天内可申请复议；价格与交割日期未披露。FCC 公告记载了申请方的说明：SpaceX 对该频谱的拟议非地面使用将取决于委员会对其待决星地直连卫星申请的决定。
