## Summary (EN)

The Wireless Broadband Alliance (WBA) announced on 7 October 2026 (London) a new report, "6G & Wi-Fi Requirements - Enabling Seamless Connectivity Across 6G and Wi-Fi". According to the release, the report sets out business and technical requirements for seamless connectivity across Wi-Fi and future cellular networks, examines lessons from previous Wi-Fi/cellular convergence efforts, states WBA's vision for a more flexible approach, and defines requirements meant to guide future architecture and standards development.

The release states WBA's position that Wi-Fi is essential to delivering the 6G vision and should be considered in the design of the connectivity framework from the start, and that convergence should account for different network ownership and deployment models instead of relying on a single cellular-centric architecture. The stated objective is a more flexible, modular and access-agnostic model. The requirements are tied to five outcome groups listed in the release: lower network costs (simplification, consolidation, reuse of existing infrastructure); better customer experience (more seamless mobility, improved indoor coverage, consistent QoS); greater automation and lower operational complexity (AI-enabled network selection, resource management and cross-network visibility); new commercial opportunities (traffic offload, federated access, partnerships, service-level-based services across mobile, fixed and Wi-Fi networks); and new services (industrial automation, smart cities, connected vehicles, intelligent transport, mission-critical communications and sensing).

On the technical side the release says the requirements are intended to reduce the cost, complexity and fragmented responsibilities that limited commercial adoption of earlier convergence approaches, and to support flexible deployment models including architectures where Wi-Fi traffic is anchored inside or outside the cellular core. Example technical areas named are multi-vendor interoperability, authentication and authorization, simpler signaling and enhanced security, AI-enabled network recommendations, QoS-aware traffic steering and switching, user-centric mobility, energy efficiency and sensing. The release carries supporting quotes from the WBA CEO, BT (Mobile Networks), a Cisco Fellow and Turk Telekom, the last describing its role on the WBA Board and in leading the 6G and Wi-Fi requirements and convergence work. The report body is a separate download; this summary follows the press release.

## Summary (ZH)

无线宽带联盟（WBA）于 2026 年 10 月 7 日（伦敦）发布新报告《6G & Wi-Fi Requirements - Enabling Seamless Connectivity Across 6G and Wi-Fi》。据新闻稿，该报告给出 Wi-Fi 与未来蜂窝网络之间实现无缝连接所需的商业与技术需求，回顾此前 Wi-Fi/蜂窝融合尝试的经验教训，阐述 WBA 对更灵活方案的设想，并定义用于指导未来架构与标准制定的需求。

新闻稿表述了 WBA 的立场：Wi-Fi 是实现 6G 愿景的关键，应在连接框架设计之初就纳入考虑；融合应兼顾不同的网络所有权与部署模式，而不是依赖单一的以蜂窝为中心的架构；目标是更灵活、模块化、与接入方式无关的模型。需求对应新闻稿列出的五类成果：降低网络成本（简化、整合、复用现有基础设施）；改善用户体验（更无缝的移动性、更好的室内覆盖、一致的 QoS）；更高自动化与更低运维复杂度（AI 驱动的网络选择、资源管理与跨网络可视化）；新的商业机会（流量分流、联合接入、合作、跨移动/固网/Wi-Fi 的基于服务等级的服务）；以及新服务（工业自动化、智慧城市、车联网、智能交通、关键任务通信与感知）。

技术方面，新闻稿称这些需求旨在降低此前融合方案因成本、复杂度和责任分散而难以商用的障碍，并支持灵活的部署模型，包括 Wi-Fi 流量锚定在蜂窝核心网内或外的架构。列举的技术领域有：多厂商互操作、认证与授权、更简化的信令与增强的安全、AI 驱动的网络推荐、QoS 感知的流量引导与切换、以用户为中心的移动性、能效与感知。新闻稿附有 WBA CEO、BT（移动网络）、思科 Fellow 与 Turk Telekom 的引语，其中 Turk Telekom 提到其在 WBA 董事会中的角色以及对 6G 与 Wi-Fi 需求和融合工作的牵头。报告正文需单独下载；本摘要依据新闻稿。
