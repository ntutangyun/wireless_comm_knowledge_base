## Summary (EN)

At its 6G Leadership Day (San Diego, 2026-08-26), Qualcomm and a coalition of operators and vendors publicly committed to a concrete 6G commercialization trajectory: spec-compliant pre-commercial 6G devices and networks demonstrated in 2028, and initial global, interoperable commercial 6G systems starting from 2029. Attendee summaries add that Qualcomm expects to ship a 6G modem in the second half of 2029, and the company framed its X105 5G Modem-RF — billed as the first 3GPP Release-19-ready modem — as the last major step before 6G. The positioning is explicitly AI-native: 6G as the radio for "context-aware AI everywhere", with AI both using and running the network. The KB already carries Qualcomm's MWC-2026 Wi-Fi 8 chip announcement; this is the cellular-side counterpart, turning the vague "6G around 2030" consensus into dated, multi-party commitments.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Dated milestones: 2028 spec-compliant pre-commercial devices/network demos; 2029 initial commercial, interoperable 6G systems; Qualcomm 6G modem expected 2H2029.
- X105 5G Modem-RF positioned as the first 3GPP Release-19-ready modem — the final 5G-Advanced step before 6G silicon.
- 3GPP timeline context: Release 20 (6G study) and Release 21 (first 6G specs) are the standards vehicles behind the 2028/2029 dates.
- AI-native framing: network-side AI (AI-run RAN) and device-side AI (6G as the data pipe for on-device agents) presented as the core 6G use case rather than raw throughput.
- Multi-party commitment (operators + vendors), not a unilateral Qualcomm roadmap.
