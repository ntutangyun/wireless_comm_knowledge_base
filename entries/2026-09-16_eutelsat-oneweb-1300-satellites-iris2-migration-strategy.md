## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-15, Eutelsat Group CEO Jean-Francois Fallacher outlined a target of keeping roughly 1,300 OneWeb satellites active in orbit by 2030, ahead of migrating traffic to the EU's IRIS² constellation in the early 2030s. SatNews reports that the 2030 fleet structure is supported by multiple Airbus Defence and Space procurement agreements already tracked in this KB: 440 Gen-1 replacement spacecraft from two contracts awarded between late 2025 and early 2026, supplemented by an order for 229 Gen-1.5 satellites (covered in this KB's 2026-09-11 entry) — 669 replacement/Gen-1.5 units across the recent Airbus contracts. The Gen-1.5 units are described as engineered with upgraded software-defined capabilities and hosted-payload accommodations, including dedicated defence payloads procured for the French Ministry of Armed Forces.

The strategically new element in this announcement is Eutelsat's stated migration plan: rather than developing a proprietary "Gen-2" OneWeb architecture, the company says it will align its LEO roadmap with IRIS² and migrate commercial, civil, and military traffic onto the unified multi-orbit IRIS² network as that infrastructure becomes operational in the early 2030s, drawing on both IRIS²'s commercial and government-reserved capacity.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **2030 fleet target:** ~1,300 OneWeb satellites in orbit, built from 440 Gen-1 replacement satellites (two Airbus contracts, late 2025-early 2026) plus 229 Gen-1.5 satellites (already covered via this KB's 2026-09-11 entry) — 669 replacement/Gen-1.5 units combined.
- **Gen-1.5 detail (new):** described as carrying upgraded software-defined capabilities and hosted-payload accommodations, including dedicated defence payloads procured for the French Ministry of Armed Forces.
- **Migration strategy (new):** Eutelsat states it will not build a proprietary OneWeb "Gen-2" architecture; instead it will migrate commercial, civil, and military traffic to IRIS² as that network becomes operational in the early 2030s.
- **Distinct from prior KB coverage:** the 2026-07-03 entry described a 340-satellite Gen-1-replacement order with "IRIS²-ready" payloads and digital channelizers, and the 2026-08-28 entry described IRIS²'s own 330-satellite LEO segment as a separate SpaceRISE-consortium build; this announcement clarifies that OneWeb's replenishment fleet is intended as a bridge to IRIS², not a parallel long-term architecture.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Prior KB entries tracked OneWeb's replenishment order (2026-07-03, 2026-09-11) and IRIS²'s own constellation build (2026-08-28) as separate threads, with IRIS²-compatibility described only as a payload feature of the replenishment satellites. This announcement is the first in this KB to state Eutelsat's overall roadmap explicitly: OneWeb's Gen-1/Gen-1.5 fleet is intended to be maintained through 2030 and then migrated onto IRIS² in the early 2030s, rather than replaced by an independently-developed OneWeb "Gen-2."
