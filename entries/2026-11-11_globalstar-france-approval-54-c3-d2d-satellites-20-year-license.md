## Summary (EN)

SatNews (10 Nov 2026) reports that the French government, through its Ministry for Digital and Ministry for Space, has authorized Globalstar's C-3 network, filed with the ITU as AST-NG-C-3, for 20 years with access to global Mobile Satellite Service (MSS) allocations. The article cites SpaceNews as reporting the decision.

Per the article the system comprises 54 satellites (48 active LEO satellites plus 6 on-orbit spares) built by MDA Space, at 1,414 km altitude with active-beamforming S-band payloads. Operations are controlled from Globalstar's Satellite Operations Control Center in Toulouse with a primary facility in Covington, Louisiana, and are authorized across the 13-year design life under the French space decree. First launches are targeted for 2027. The article notes the FCC previously authorized C-3 for direct cellular service to smartphones and has approved Amazon's $11.5 billion acquisition of Globalstar; it lists no specific conditions of the French license.
