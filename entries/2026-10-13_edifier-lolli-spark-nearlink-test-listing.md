## Summary (EN)

According to an IT之家 report of 2026-10-10 (read here through its republication on 同花顺), an Edifier (漫步者) earphone named **Lolli Spark**, model number **EDF200259**, has passed test certification listed on the OpenHarmony website. The listing shows a HiSilicon **Hi3372** chip running the lightweight **OpenHarmony 5.1.0 Release** system. The SparkLink Alliance website, per the report, also lists the model as having passed test listing (测试列名, reported 2026-10-09), with test sets that include high-definition audio protocol conformance testing. A separate IT之家 report of 2026-10-09 (https://www.ithome.com/1/011/151.htm) states that the SparkLink Alliance listing names Beijing Aidefa Technology Co., Ltd. (北京爱德发科技有限公司), whose parent is Shenzhen Edifier Technology Co., Ltd.

The report suggests the earphone may launch soon and become the second third-party-brand earphone to support NearLink audio. It recalls that Edifier listed the Comfo Clip Q2 earclip on JD.com in early September (OpenHarmony, HiSilicon chip, 12 mm dynamic driver, 399 yuan), described as the first third-party-brand NearLink earphone. No launch date, price or specifications beyond the certification entries have been published by the vendor. The original OpenHarmony and Alliance listing pages were not independently checked for this entry.

## Summary (ZH)

据 IT之家 2026 年 10 月 10 日报道（本条通过同花顺转载页阅读），漫步者一款名为 **Lolli Spark**、型号为 **EDF200259** 的耳机已通过 OpenHarmony 官网列出的测试认证，芯片为海思 **Hi3372**，运行 **OpenHarmony 5.1.0 Release** 轻量系统。报道称，星闪联盟官网（10 月 9 日报道）亦已将该型号列入测试列名，测试集包括高清音频协议一致性测试等。IT之家 2026 年 10 月 9 日另一篇报道（https://www.ithome.com/1/011/151.htm）称，星闪联盟列名中的生产方为北京爱德发科技有限公司，其母公司为深圳市漫步者科技股份有限公司。

报道认为该耳机有望近期上市，成为第二款支持星闪音频的三方品牌耳机，并回顾漫步者 Comfo Clip Q2 耳夹式耳机于 9 月初在京东上架（OpenHarmony、海思芯片、12mm 动圈、399 元），被视为首款三方品牌星闪耳机。厂商尚未公布上市时间、价格或认证条目以外的规格。本条未独立核对 OpenHarmony 与联盟的原始列名页面。
