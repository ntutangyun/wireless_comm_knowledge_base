## Summary (EN)

Fifteen organizations spanning the European connectivity ecosystem — including Wi-Fi Alliance, Wireless Broadband Alliance, Dynamic Spectrum Alliance, FTTH Council Europe, NXP Semiconductors, LANCOM Systems, FRITZ! (AVM), Open Fiber, Deutsche Glasfaser and Italy's AIIP — published an open letter (Brussels, June 24, 2026) arguing that the European Commission's proposed **Digital Networks Act (DNA)** does not sufficiently recognize Wi-Fi's role in European connectivity.

The letter's core argument: fibre, mobile, satellite and Wi-Fi are complementary, interdependent layers, and Wi-Fi is the critical *final link* — it carries **over 90% of indoor internet traffic** and is, for most Europeans, the primary gateway to the internet as full-fibre transition completes. Without prioritizing Wi-Fi (including its spectrum needs, citing Plum Consulting's 2024 Wi-Fi spectrum-requirements study), the 2030 Digital Decade targets — gigabit for all households, full 5G coverage, 90% SME digital intensity — become significantly harder to reach.

横跨欧洲连接生态的 15 家组织——包括 Wi-Fi 联盟、无线宽带联盟（WBA）、动态频谱联盟（DSA）、欧洲 FTTH 理事会、恩智浦（NXP）、LANCOM Systems、FRITZ!（AVM）、Open Fiber、Deutsche Glasfaser 及意大利 AIIP——发表公开信（布鲁塞尔，2026 年 6 月 24 日），指出欧盟委员会拟议的**《数字网络法案》（DNA）**未充分承认 Wi-Fi 在欧洲连接中的作用。

公开信核心论点：光纤、移动、卫星与 Wi-Fi 是互补且相互依存的层级，Wi-Fi 是关键的*最后一环*——承载**超过 90% 的室内互联网流量**，并且随着全光纤迁移完成，是大多数欧洲人接入互联网的首要入口。若不优先考虑 Wi-Fi（含其频谱需求，援引 Plum Consulting 2024 年 Wi-Fi 频谱需求研究），2030"数字十年"目标——千兆入户、5G 全覆盖、90% 中小企业数字化——将显著更难达成。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the policy counter-move to the RSPG's upper-6-GHz recommendation already tracked in the KB (2026-05-04_eu-rspg-upper-6ghz-decision, 2026-05-28_wifinow-uk-eu-upper-6ghz-regulation): the Wi-Fi side is now contesting the *legislative* framing (DNA), not just the spectrum-group recommendation. The signatory mix is notable — fibre operators and FTTH bodies siding with Wi-Fi (their access-layer complement) against a mobile-centric framing. Watch whether the DNA text that emerges from Parliament/Council negotiations adds Wi-Fi/unlicensed-spectrum language; that would shape the EU's upper 6 GHz endgame more durably than the RSPG opinion.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

这是对 KB 已跟踪的 RSPG 高段 6 GHz 建议（2026-05-04_eu-rspg-upper-6ghz-decision、2026-05-28_wifinow-uk-eu-upper-6ghz-regulation）的政策反制：Wi-Fi 阵营现在争夺的是*立法*层面的定位（DNA），而不只是频谱工作组建议。签署方构成值得注意——光纤运营商与 FTTH 组织站在 Wi-Fi（其接入层互补技术）一边，反对以移动为中心的叙事。关注欧洲议会/理事会谈判产出的 DNA 文本是否加入 Wi-Fi/非授权频谱条款；那将比 RSPG 意见更持久地塑造欧盟高段 6 GHz 的最终格局。
