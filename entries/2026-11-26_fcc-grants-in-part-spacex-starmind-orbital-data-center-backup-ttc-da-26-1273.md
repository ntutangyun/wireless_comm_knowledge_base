## Summary (EN)

On 25 November 2026 the FCC Space Bureau (Chief Courtney Reinhard) released a Memorandum Opinion and Order and Authorization, DA 26-1273, acting on the application by Space Exploration Holdings LLC (SpaceX) for the "SpaceX Orbital Data Center System" (Starmind), ICFS file SAT-LOA-20260108-00016, call sign S00798. The grant is marked "granted-in-part / deferred-in-part, with conditions". It covers a non-geostationary constellation of 1,000,000 satellites operating as data centres in space, in 50 km-wide shells between 500 and 1,002 km altitude at 30-degree and sun-synchronous inclinations (the nominal operating bands listed include 550-568 km at 26-32 degrees and 967-1002 km at 99.4 degrees).

The authority granted is narrow: radiofrequency communications for backup and emergency telemetry, tracking and command (TT&C) only, in 18.8-19.3 GHz (space-to-Earth) and 28.6-29.1 GHz (Earth-to-space). The order records that Starmind will rely on optical links for communications and that SpaceX says it does not plan to use the Ka-band except for limited backup and emergency operations, then through gateway earth stations already authorised for its Gen1 and Gen2 systems. The Bureau waives the U.S. Table of Frequency Allocations on its own motion for this standalone TT&C on an unprotected, non-interference basis, and denies the petitions to deny filed by Viasat, Kuiper Systems LLC (Amazon Leo) and the American Astronomical Society.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 11 月 25 日，FCC 空间局（局长 Courtney Reinhard）发布备忘录意见与命令及授权文件 DA 26-1273，处理 Space Exploration Holdings LLC（SpaceX）提交的“SpaceX 轨道数据中心系统”（Starmind）申请，ICFS 文件号 SAT-LOA-20260108-00016，呼号 S00798。授权被标注为“部分批准／部分延后，附带条件”。授权对象为由 100 万颗卫星组成、作为太空数据中心运行的非静止轨道星座，轨道壳层各宽 50 km，高度 500 至 1002 km，倾角为 30 度和太阳同步（文件列出的标称运行高度带包括 26–32 度倾角下的 550–568 km 以及 99.4 度倾角下的 967–1002 km）。

授权范围很窄：仅限备份和应急遥测、跟踪与指令（TT&C）的射频通信，频段为 18.8–19.3 GHz（空对地）和 28.6–29.1 GHz（地对空）。文件记载 Starmind 将依靠光链路通信，SpaceX 表示除有限的备份和应急运行外不计划使用 Ka 频段，且届时将通过已为其 Gen1 和 Gen2 系统授权的信关站通信。空间局主动豁免美国频率划分表，允许在非保护、非干扰基础上进行这种独立 TT&C，并驳回了 Viasat、Kuiper Systems LLC（亚马逊 Leo）和美国天文学会提出的否决请求。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Frequency and priority conditions: Ka-band use is unprotected and non-interference; SpaceX must cease operations causing documented harmful interference. The 28.6-29.1 GHz uplink must not cause harmful interference to current or future U.S. Federal systems with primary allocations, and SpaceX must coordinate with Federal agencies (including those in national security or safety-of-life service) before operating in the 18.8-19.3 GHz downlink, which is primary Federal; it must notify the FCC within 30 days after coordination and give a 24/7 interference contact two weeks before operating. SpaceX must obtain earth-station authorisations before communicating with gateways.
- Processing-round waiver: the waiver of the NGSO processing-round rules (sections 25.156 and 25.157) is granted, with no processing-round priority for SpaceX; it must protect operators authorised in earlier rounds and coordinate with all existing and future users of these bands.
- Milestones and bond: waivers of the surety-bond rule (section 25.165) and of the old milestone rule are granted in favour of the new milestones adopted in the July 2026 Space Modernization order. The authorisation becomes null and void unless SpaceX deploys and operates one satellite by 25 November 2033, 10% of the maximum number by 25 November 2035, 50% by 25 November 2038, and all remaining satellites by 25 November 2040. The licence term is 15 years from SpaceX's certification that the first Starmind satellite is in orbit and conforming.
- Debris: the plan for satellites operating below 600 km is found compliant. The waiver request to allow disposal above 600 km into Earth disposal orbits or heliocentric orbits (instead of re-entry within five years) is deferred. SpaceX must file a modification application with its final debris-mitigation plan before deploying any satellite above 600 km, and must file one (with any waiver justification) before deploying any satellite that will not be disposed of by atmospheric re-entry. SpaceX committed to deploy at lower altitudes first, steer re-entries over the ocean and full demise of each satellite on re-entry.
- Reporting: semi-annual reports (1 January and 1 July) on conjunction events and resulting manoeuvres, satellites removed from operation or screened from deployment, re-entries, total satellites deployed with orbital parameters, and the number of satellites that communicated with earth stations in the authorised Ka-band frequencies. SpaceX must also deploy consistently with its ITU filings. SpaceX has 30 days from release to decline the grant as conditioned.
- Issues the order treats as outside FCC authority: the order declines further NEPA environmental review citing the Commission's October 2026 NEPA order, states that rocket-launch emissions fall outside its purview and describes re-entry atmospheric concerns as speculative and unsupported in the record; on optical astronomy it finds SpaceX's commitment to coordinate with the National Science Foundation sufficient and declines additional conditions, and it treats infrared waste-heat concerns raised by the American Astronomical Society as outside the scope of the RF operations. Comments on feasibility, economics and thermal management of orbital data centres are likewise described as outside the FCC's radiocommunications mandate.
- Interference reasoning: the Bureau agrees with SpaceX that an optical-system outage large enough to require Ka-band use on all one million satellites at once is unlikely, and it is unpersuaded by comments that the constellation would reflect radio waves and cause interference.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- 频率与优先级条件：Ka 频段使用为非保护、非干扰；一旦出现有文件记录的有害干扰，SpaceX 必须停止相关运行。28.6–29.1 GHz 上行不得对拥有主要划分的现有或未来美国联邦系统造成有害干扰；在 18.8–19.3 GHz 下行（联邦主要划分）运行前，SpaceX 须与联邦机构（包括国家安全或生命安全类业务）完成协调，并在协调结束后 30 天内通知 FCC，运行前两周提供 7×24 小时干扰联络人。与信关站通信前须取得地球站授权。
- 处理轮次豁免：NGSO 处理轮次规则（第 25.156 与 25.157 条）的豁免获批，但 SpaceX 不享有处理轮次优先地位，必须保护此前轮次已授权的运营商，并与这些频段的所有现有和未来用户协调。
- 里程碑与保证金：保证金规则（第 25.165 条）及旧里程碑规则的豁免获批，改为适用 2026 年 7 月空间现代化命令中采纳的新里程碑。若 SpaceX 未能在 2033 年 11 月 25 日前部署并运行 1 颗卫星、2035 年 11 月 25 日前达到最大数量的 10%、2038 年 11 月 25 日前达到 50%、2040 年 11 月 25 日前完成其余全部卫星，则授权作废。许可期限为 15 年，自 SpaceX 证明首颗 Starmind 卫星已入轨并符合授权之日起算。
- 轨道碎片：600 km 以下运行卫星的碎片减缓计划被认定合规。允许 600 km 以上卫星处置到地球处置轨道或日心轨道（而非五年内再入）的豁免请求被延后。在部署任何 600 km 以上的卫星之前，SpaceX 须提交含最终碎片减缓计划的修改申请；在部署任何不通过大气再入处置的卫星之前，也须提交修改申请（并说明所需豁免理由）。SpaceX 承诺先在较低高度部署、引导卫星在海洋上空再入，并保证每颗卫星再入时完全消融。
- 报告：每年 1 月 1 日和 7 月 1 日提交半年度报告，内容包括交会事件及相应机动、退出运行或被剔除部署的卫星数、再入数、已部署卫星总数及其轨道参数，以及在授权 Ka 频段与地球站通信的卫星数。SpaceX 还须与其 ITU 申报保持一致。SpaceX 自发布起有 30 天时间可拒绝带条件的授权。
- 命令认定不属于 FCC 权限的事项：命令援引委员会 2026 年 10 月的 NEPA 命令，拒绝进一步的环境审查；指出火箭发射排放不在其职责范围，并称再入造成的大气影响属推测性且缺乏记录支持；对于光学天文，认为 SpaceX 承诺与美国国家科学基金会协调已足够，不增加额外条件；对美国天文学会提出的红外废热问题，认为超出射频运行的范围。关于轨道数据中心的可行性、经济性和热管理的意见，也被认为不属于 FCC 的无线电通信职责。
- 干扰论证：空间局同意 SpaceX 的观点，即光系统故障大到需要 100 万颗卫星同时使用 Ka 频段的情形不太可能发生，并且不认同“星座会反射无线电波并造成干扰”的评论。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the grant outcome for the application that the KB covered at filing and naming stage (2026-08-27 entry), and the primary source shows what was actually authorised: RF authority limited to unprotected backup TT&C for a constellation whose primary communications are optical, with the request to dispose of satellites above 600 km into disposal or heliocentric orbits deferred rather than resolved. The grant is effective on release; SpaceX has 30 days to decline it as conditioned, and silence counts as acceptance. The order's findings are the Bureau's own characterisation of the record, including its decisions on which concerns lie outside its radiocommunications mandate.
