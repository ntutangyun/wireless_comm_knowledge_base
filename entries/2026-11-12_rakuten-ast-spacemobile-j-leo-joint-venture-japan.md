## Summary (EN)

SatNews (11 Nov 2026) reports that Rakuten Group and AST SpaceMobile agreed on 10 Nov 2026 to form a joint venture, Rakuten AST, to deploy a sovereign low-Earth-orbit network over Japan that delivers cellular broadband directly to standard smartphones. The venture is funded by up to US$1 billion over three years from Japan's J-LEO initiative, administered by the Communications and Information Network Association of Japan (CIAJ). Japan's Ministry of Internal Affairs and Communications (MIC) selected a joint proposal from Rakuten Mobile and a Rakuten preparatory company under the publicly solicited J-LEO project.

AST SpaceMobile will manufacture and supply the large-aperture satellites; the article gives no satellite count. The network is to use commercial mid-band spectrum (no specific bands named) and base-station equipment integrated into domestic Japanese infrastructure, so local operators control orbital traffic routing. Rakuten Mobile plans commercial-environment testing within 2026, with full domestic service targeted for the end of March 2029.
