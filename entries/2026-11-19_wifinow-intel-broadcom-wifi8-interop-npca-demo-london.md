## Summary (EN)
Wi-Fi NOW (Claus Hetting, 27 Oct 2026) reports that Intel and Broadcom ran a live Wi-Fi 8 interoperability demonstration the week before, in London, at the Wi-Fi Alliance's most recent members meeting co-located with the Dynamic Spectrum Alliance Global Summit, with regulators as the audience. Per Intel (as relayed by the article), it was the first public interoperability demo between Wi-Fi 8 devices from the two companies. A Broadcom-based Wi-Fi 8 access point interoperated with an Intel Wi-Fi 8 client; Wi-Fi 7 equipment was included for comparison.

The feature shown was Non-Primary Channel Access (NPCA), which lets a device use an available non-primary part of a channel while another network is transmitting elsewhere in the wider channel. The sequence was: a Wi-Fi 7 baseline with no added interference; a second access point switched on in the same channel, with throughput dropping as the networks competed; then NPCA enabled. Intel's Carlos Cordeiro gave the example of a 160 MHz AP with an 80 MHz-only client, leaving part of the spectrum idle during a transmission. Intel reports roughly 30% higher aggregate throughput and up to 50% lower latency versus the baseline in which the APs effectively take turns.

An intermediate-MCS demo was prepared by Intel but not shown live for lack of time. The article notes that actual performance depends on network conditions, client capabilities and signal quality. It gives no absolute throughput/latency values, channel configurations beyond the example, test duration or run counts. Intel's Wi-Fi 8 client products are not yet commercially available; Intel intends to finish industry interoperability testing before launch, with a final plugfest expected around 3Q2027 and no launch date confirmed.

## Summary (ZH)
Wi-Fi NOW（Claus Hetting，2026 年 10 月 27 日）报道，Intel 与 Broadcom 于前一周在伦敦的 Wi-Fi 联盟最近一次会员会议（与动态频谱联盟全球峰会同地举行）上进行了 Wi-Fi 8 互操作现场演示，观众为监管机构。据 Intel 所述（由文章转述），这是两家公司 Wi-Fi 8 设备之间的首次公开互操作演示。基于 Broadcom 的 Wi-Fi 8 接入点与 Intel Wi-Fi 8 客户端互通，并加入 Wi-Fi 7 设备作对比。

演示的功能是非主信道接入（NPCA），即设备在更宽信道内他网正在传输时，利用空闲的非主信道部分。演示流程为：无额外干扰的 Wi-Fi 7 基线；在同一信道开启第二个接入点，网络相互竞争使吞吐下降；随后启用 NPCA。Intel 的 Carlos Cordeiro 举例：160 MHz 的 AP 搭配仅支持 80 MHz 的客户端，传输期间部分频谱处于闲置。Intel 报告相对 AP 基本轮流占用信道的基线，聚合吞吐提升约 30%、时延最多降低 50%。

Intel 还准备了中间 MCS 演示，但因时间所限未现场展示。文章指出，实际性能取决于网络条件、客户端能力与信号质量。文章未给出绝对吞吐/时延数值、示例之外的信道配置、测试时长或运行次数。Intel 的 Wi-Fi 8 客户端产品尚未商用；Intel 计划在上市前完成行业互操作测试，最终互操作测试会（plugfest）预计在 2027 年第三季度前后，未确认上市日期。
