## Summary (EN)

SpaceX published a product page for the Starlink Mini 2 (starlink.com/mini-2), a portable Starlink user terminal; the Hacker News thread linking it (2026-11-10) drew about 230 points and 240+ comments within a day (as of 2026-11-11). The page headline is "Built-in battery. Built-in Wi-Fi.": the terminal runs on a swappable battery for up to 4.5 hours, can stay online while the battery charges, and the page says it gets online in about 60 seconds without a power outlet. Preorders are open by invitation for select US customers, with more customers and countries "coming soon"; no price is shown on the page.

The page's own comparison table against Mini 1 lists two differences: Mini 2 has a battery (Mini 1 has none) and is powered by battery or USB-C, where Mini 1 uses a DC power cable. Both are listed as supporting mobile use.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Battery: 71 Wh, 490 g, swappable (spare sold separately); up to 4.5 hours; the battery also offers USB-C power output up to 60 W.
- Charging: USB-C 60 W; in the box a 60 W USB-C charger and a 3 m USB-C to USB-C cable.
- Wi-Fi: built-in Wi-Fi 6, coverage stated as up to 1,800 ft² (167 m²).
- Mechanical: 263 × 314 × 23.1 mm; 1.6 kg with battery and kickstand; IP67; 110° field of view; standard 1/4-20 camera thread for tripod mounting; quick-release battery, power button and battery indicator.
- Not stated on the page: throughput, power draw, antenna element count, uplink performance, price. Pre-launch reports from July 2026 described a battery and a smaller antenna; the dimensions now published come from SpaceX itself.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The Mini line is Starlink's portable terminal; Mini 2 makes it self-powered, so a dish, Wi-Fi access point and battery now ship as one IP67 unit that can run without any external power for up to 4.5 hours. The figures above are SpaceX's own specifications; no independent measurements of Mini 2 throughput or battery life were found. The HN discussion's high engagement is a community signal, not a technical source.
