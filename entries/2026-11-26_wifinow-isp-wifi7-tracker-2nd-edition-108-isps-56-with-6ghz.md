## Summary (EN)
Wi-Fi NOW (article by Claus Hetting, CEO and Chairman, published 25 November 2026) reports the second edition of its Global ISP Wi-Fi 7 Tracker. The number of residential broadband providers it identifies as offering Wi-Fi 7 rose 27% since September to 108 (85 in the first edition), and the number confirmed to support 6 GHz rose 30%, from 43 to 56. For another 31 operators Wi-Fi NOW has not yet established whether the Wi-Fi 7 equipment supports 6 GHz, so it says the actual share could be substantially higher than the 52% currently confirmed. The article attributes the increase to both new commercial launches and previously unidentified deployments.

Regional detail given in the article: Europe and North America each account for 19 operators confirmed with 6 GHz-capable Wi-Fi 7 equipment, about 66% and 63% of tracked operators in those regions; in Asia-Pacific exactly half of the 26 operators identified are confirmed to offer 6 GHz equipment, and the confirmed figures for Latin America and the Middle East & Africa are considerably lower. The article cautions that national spectrum rules, equipment availability and unconfirmed specifications affect any regional comparison. It also reports Wi-Fi 7 moving into lower service tiers: Bell offers its Giga Hub 2.0 Wi-Fi 7 gateway on fibre packages starting at 150 Mbps, and UK operator Connect Fibre offers Wi-Fi 7 from 250 Mbps; YouFibre and Community Fibre offer both dual-band and tri-band Wi-Fi 7 equipment, with 6 GHz on higher-tier services. Third-party platforms from Amazon eero, TP-Link, Nokia, Linksys, Calix and others are named as making Wi-Fi 7 accessible to smaller and regional operators.

The updated tracker adds a forward-looking section on announced future Wi-Fi 7 and Wi-Fi 8 ISP deployments. The article states that US operator gFiber and Vietnamese operator VNPT have publicly announced AI-native Wi-Fi 8 gateway projects targeting commercialisation ahead of formal Wi-Fi 8 certification. The full tracker (regional tables covering 108 providers, gateway models, 6 GHz support, access technologies and announced deployments) is in Wi-Fi NOW's Resource Centre and free only to its members, so this entry is based on the public news article, not the tracker itself.

文章给出的区域细节：欧洲与北美各有 19 家运营商确认提供支持 6 GHz 的 Wi-Fi 7 设备，约占两地所跟踪运营商的 66% 与 63%；亚太地区所识别的 26 家运营商中恰有一半确认提供 6 GHz 设备，拉美与中东及非洲的确认比例明显更低。文章提醒，各国频谱规则、设备供应情况和尚未确认的规格都会影响区域比较。文章还报告 Wi-Fi 7 正进入较低档位的套餐：加拿大 Bell 在起价 150 Mbps 的光纤套餐上提供其 Giga Hub 2.0 Wi-Fi 7 网关，英国运营商 Connect Fibre 从 250 Mbps 起提供 Wi-Fi 7；YouFibre 与 Community Fibre 同时提供双频与三频 Wi-Fi 7 设备，6 GHz 用于较高档位。Amazon eero、TP-Link、Nokia、Linksys、Calix 等的第三方平台被点名为使中小及区域运营商也能提供 Wi-Fi 7。

更新后的 Tracker 新增了一部分前瞻内容，涵盖已公布的未来 Wi-Fi 7 与 Wi-Fi 8 ISP 部署。文章称，美国运营商 gFiber 与越南运营商 VNPT 已公开宣布 AI 原生 Wi-Fi 8 网关项目，目标是在正式 Wi-Fi 8 认证之前商用。完整 Tracker（涵盖 108 家运营商、网关型号、6 GHz 支持情况、接入技术与已公布部署的区域表格）位于 Wi-Fi NOW 资源中心，仅对其会员免费，因此本条基于公开新闻文章而非 Tracker 本身。
