## Summary (EN)

SatNews (31 October 2026) reports that SatNews (31 October) reports the FCC voted on 30 October (the 29 October SatNews article already covers the item; not confirmed by an FCC document) to advance an NPRM proposing to auction 25 MHz of mid-band spectrum: 1675-1695 MHz (20 MHz) and 2020-2025 MHz (5 MHz). It is a proposal rather than a final rule. A related 29 October SatNews article describes the same auction guidelines as a parallel proceeding to the direct-to-device NPRM, for terrestrial, orbital or hybrid networks.

The proposed structure gives terrestrial fixed and mobile non-federal services co-primary rights in 1675-1695 MHz, subject to an ongoing NTIA review, and adds secondary Mobile Satellite Service allocations for domestic Supplemental Coverage from Space (SCS) in both ranges, with shared technical limits intended to prevent uplink interference between satellites and ground stations.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The item pairs a spectrum auction with secondary satellite allocations in the same bands, which SatNews calls a dual-allocation model. Because the allocations are secondary and partly conditional on NTIA review, the satellite side's usable rights remain unsettled until the NPRM text and comments are available.

该事项把频谱拍卖与同频段的次要卫星划分结合起来，SatNews 称之为“双重划分模式”。由于划分为次要性质且部分取决于 NTIA 审查，在 NPRM 文本和评议公布前，卫星一侧可用权利仍不确定。
