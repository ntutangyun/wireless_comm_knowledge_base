## Summary (EN)

A user post on the Huawei Fans Club (花粉俱乐部) Smart Router / Home Storage circle, dated 2026-11-03 (about 1,370 reads and 20 comments when read on 2026-11-05), reports that the poster's Huawei Router X1 Pro updated to HarmonyOS 7.0, firmware version 7.0.0.10 (V7R2). The poster says the update notes mention only "enhanced system security and stability", but after the router restarted and reconnected, a notification offered to connect to the router over NearLink. From use, the poster concludes the update adds the NearLink dedicated-link feature users had been waiting for: the router's connection status appears in the phone's NearLink/Bluetooth settings, and on the router's management page the online-device view changes from 2.4G and 5G access to 5G and NearLink access. The poster also reports faster and more stable throughput, and says the vendor had told them a beta would be pushed in December, so this came early.

Comments in the thread are mixed: one reader asks whether it should read 2.4G, 5G, NearLink; several say they have not received the update; another reply says the formal release is planned for December; one commenter says the BE series appears to have been left out. All of this is user testimony: Huawei's update notes as quoted do not mention NearLink, and no Huawei announcement of the rollout was found when the entry was written.
