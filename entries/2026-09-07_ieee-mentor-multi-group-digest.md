## Summary (EN)

Between the 2026-09-04 watermark (DCN 11-26-1613) and 2026-09-05, three IEEE 802.11 groups uploaded new-DCN contributions (revision bumps to already-tracked DCNs, e.g. 1336r13, the REVmf D3.0 MDR report, are not listed): TGbp posted a new AMP PDT ("PDT Transmitter Block Diagram"), TGm/REVmf posted two D3.0 maintenance items, and TGbt posted its first comment set against the TGbt D1.0 letter ballot (LB297).

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The TGbt row is a landing confirmation: the 2026-07-10 KB entry (2026-07-10_ieee-mentor-tgbt-plenary-eve-digest) recorded that TGbt's "initial WG letter ballot is predicted for July 2026" ahead of Huawei's last pre-ballot PQC mechanism proposals. DCN 1629's LB297 comment spreadsheet against D1.0 confirms that ballot did take place, with comment resolution now underway roughly two months after the original prediction — the document itself does not state a reason for the gap, so none is asserted here. The TGbp row (1541) surfaced only because its Uploaded-ET date (05-Sep) fell after the last run's watermark even though its DCN number sits below the numeric watermark (1613) — the same low-DCN/late-upload blind spot this KB's Mentor tracking has flagged repeatedly since 2026-08-25; it is a new DCN, not a revision of an already-tracked one. The two REVmf rows are routine D3.0 maintenance comment-resolution and scheduling items.
