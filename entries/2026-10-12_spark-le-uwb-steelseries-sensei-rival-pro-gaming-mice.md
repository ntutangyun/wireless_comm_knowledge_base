## Summary (EN)

On 1 October 2026, SPARK Microsystems announced that SteelSeries has selected SPARK's Low Energy Ultra-Wideband (LE-UWB) wireless technology for its newly launched Sensei Pro and Rival Pro gaming mouse series (and their Mini variants), first unveiled by SteelSeries on 29 September 2026 as part of the company's 25th-anniversary lineup. SteelSeries' release claims true 8K (8,000 Hz) wireless polling; SPARK's release adds that this corresponds to a 0.125 ms polling interval, "with up to 9,000 Hz over-polling for even more frequent wireless updates." SteelSeries' release also describes a hot-swappable "Infinite Power System" battery (up to 120 hours at 1K polling, 32 hours at 8K polling, 60/16 hours per battery respectively), weighing 53 g standard / 49 g Mini. SteelSeries' own release does not name a chip supplier; SPARK's 1 October release is the primary source confirming SPARK's LE-UWB as the wireless layer behind these mice. SPARK says LE-UWB maintains "reliable operation in congested 2.4 GHz wireless environments" and cites roughly 20x the bandwidth of standard 2.4 GHz; SteelSeries specifies operation in the 6.4-9.056 GHz spectrum. SPARK CEO Fares Mubarak is quoted: "Conventional wireless connectivity compromises the performance metrics that matter most to competitive gamers. LE-UWB is the next evolution." The four mice (Sensei Pro, Sensei Pro Mini, Rival Pro, Rival Pro Mini) are priced at $179.99 / EUR199.99 / GBP169.99 each, with a $24.99 wireless travel dongle sold separately; worldwide preorders opened 29 September 2026 and retail availability is scheduled for 13 October 2026.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Products: SteelSeries Sensei Pro, Sensei Pro Mini, Rival Pro, Rival Pro Mini gaming mice, part of SteelSeries' 25th-anniversary lineup.
- Wireless layer: SPARK Microsystems' LE-UWB (Low Energy Ultra-Wideband), confirmed by SPARK's own 1 October 2026 press release (not named in SteelSeries' own 29 September release).
- Polling: claimed true 8,000 Hz (0.125 ms) wireless polling; up to 9,000 Hz over-polling (SPARK).
- Claimed LE-UWB advantage: roughly 20x the bandwidth of standard 2.4 GHz, with reliable operation in congested 2.4 GHz environments (SPARK's framing); SteelSeries specifies 6.4-9.056 GHz operation.
- Battery: hot-swappable "Infinite Power System," up to 120 hours at 1K polling / 32 hours at 8K polling (60/16 hours per individual battery).
- Weight: 53 g (standard), 49 g (Mini variants).
- Pricing: $179.99 / EUR199.99 / GBP169.99 per mouse; $24.99 separate wireless travel dongle.
- Timeline: preorders opened 29 September 2026; retail availability 13 October 2026.
