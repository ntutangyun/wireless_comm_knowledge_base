## Summary (EN)

Via Satellite (19 October 2026) reports that the FCC has granted Globalstar authority to operate its third-generation satellite constellation, referred to as C-3. The constellation will operate in mobile satellite service (MSS) spectrum and provide direct-to-device services to smartphones. Globalstar announced the approval on Monday 19 October.

According to the article, the system comprises 48 low-Earth-orbit satellites, six on-orbit spares and 90 ground-station antennas. Globalstar filed for the constellation in February 2025 as a next-generation system operating at higher power after Apple invested to fund it, and MDA Space is building the satellites.

The article adds that Globalstar is going through a deal to be acquired by Amazon, which plans to operate both the C-3 constellation and a new Amazon direct-to-device constellation of up to 5,105 satellites. Globalstar CEO Paul Jacobs is quoted calling the authorization a critical step for enhancing the capabilities of its LEO constellations, delivering increased capacity, improved performance and greater flexibility.

The article does not give launch dates, frequency assignments, power levels or service rates.
