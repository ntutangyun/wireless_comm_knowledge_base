## Summary (EN)

A 28 June 2026 industry article surveys the digital car-key Tier-1 supplier landscape across competing technologies — UWB, Bluetooth Channel Sounding (CS), and SparkLink (NearLink) SLE — and places SparkLink's January-April 2026 shipment volume in that wider context for the first time in this knowledge base: **157,900 vehicles** delivered with SparkLink digital keys, against **3.5178 million** total digital-key vehicle deliveries across all technologies in the same period — SparkLink's YoY growth rate over the period is reported as **+1,077.76%**.

The article profiles four named Tier-1 suppliers and their technology mixes. Hubei Liancheng (湖北联乘) ships BLE and UWB digital-key chips — over 5 million units in 2025 — with domestically-produced Channel Sounding and domestically-produced BLE+UWB entering mass production in Q3 2026, and a domestic solution adding UWB-radar functions (CPD 活体检测, tailgate foot-kick 尾门脚踢, sentry mode 哨兵) expected to reach mass production in Q1 2027. Yuanfeng Technology (远峰科技) integrates UWB, Bluetooth CS, SparkLink SLE and NFC in its key solution and is described by the article as "全球首个发布星闪数字车钥匙的Tier 1企业" (the world's first Tier-1 enterprise to release a SparkLink digital car key) that has "已经率先完成了规模化量产" (already achieved mass-production scale first). Joint Electronics (联合电子) offers a BLE+UWB+SparkLink multi-mode solution alongside mature Bluetooth 6.0 production. Desay Systems (德赛系统) launched an intelligent-entry digital-key solution last year supporting BLE RSSI, BLE 6.0 Channel Sounding, UWB, SparkLink and NFC, forming what the article calls a full-scenario intelligent-entry loop of 主动迎宾-无感解闭锁-无网寻车 (active greeting, seamless unlock/lock, offline vehicle finding); by reusing the vehicle-side UWB digital-key hardware, the same solution extends to CPD (活体检测), foot-kick sensing and sentry mode. The article names the automaker brands adopting SparkLink keys as AITO (问界), Luxeed (智界), Stelato (享界) and Avatr (阿维塔). Looking ahead, it cites an industry forecast of 80%+ digital-key attachment rate by 2030, implying an annual delivery scale of roughly 19 million vehicles across all digital-key technologies.

## Summary (ZH)

一篇 2026 年 6 月 28 日的行业文章梳理了数字车钥匙 Tier 1 供应商在多种竞争技术——UWB、蓝牙信道探测（Bluetooth CS）与星闪（NearLink）SLE——上的格局，并首次在本知识库中把星闪 2026 年 1-4 月的出货量放入这一更大背景中比较：星闪数字车钥匙交付 **15.79 万辆**，而同期全部技术合计的数字钥匙车辆交付总量为 **351.78 万辆**——星闪同期同比增速为 **+1077.76%**。

文章介绍了四家具名 Tier 1 供应商及其技术组合。湖北联乘出货 BLE 与 UWB 数字钥匙芯片——2025 年超过 500 万颗——将在 2026 年三季度陆续实现国产化 CS 的量产与国产化 BLE+UWB 的量产；随后推出带 CPD、尾门脚踢、哨兵等 UWB 雷达功能的国产化方案，预计 2027 年一季度可达到量产状态。远峰科技的钥匙方案集成了 UWB、蓝牙 CS、星闪 SLE 与 NFC 技术，文章将其描述为"**全球首个发布星闪数字车钥匙的 Tier 1 企业**"，且"**已经率先完成了规模化量产**"。联合电子提供 BLE+UWB+星闪多模方案，同时蓝牙 6.0 产品已具备成熟量产能力。德赛系统去年推出的数字钥匙智能进入解决方案可支持 BLE RSSI、BLE 6.0 Channel Sounding、UWB、星闪及 NFC，构建了"主动迎宾-无感解闭锁-无网寻车"的全场景智能进入体验闭环；同时该方案通过复用 UWB 数字钥匙车端硬件，拓展了活体检测（CPD）、脚踢感应、哨兵模式等应用场景。文章列举采用星闪钥匙的车企品牌为问界、智界、享界与阿维塔。展望方面，文章引用行业预测称到 2030 年数字钥匙渗透率将超过 80%，对应全部数字钥匙技术合计年交付规模约 1900 万辆。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This knowledge base already holds SparkLink car-key ecosystem numbers at two other altitudes: device-side OTA reach (`2026-05-24_sparklink-car-key-device-rollout-2-0`, ~50M Huawei phones eligible) and alliance-wide aggregate deployment (`2026-08-05_sparklink-car-key-forum-china-auto-2026`, 800k+ vehicles keyed in the trailing year to July 2026, 1M+ projected for full-year 2026). This article adds a third altitude that neither of those supplies: SparkLink's **share of the competing digital-key technology landscape** in a specific quarter, and a **named multi-supplier Tier-1 field** (Hubei Liancheng, Yuanfeng, Joint Electronics, Desay) building UWB/BLE-CS/SparkLink multi-mode solutions rather than single-technology parts. Read against the 3.5178M-vehicle total digital-key market for the same four months, SparkLink's 157,900 units is a small slice — useful context against the alliance's own more triumphant framing in the July forum entry, since this is the first time this knowledge base has a same-period denominator to measure SparkLink car-key volume against its BLE/UWB competitors rather than against itself. The "first Tier-1 to release SparkLink car key, first to mass-produce" claim about Yuanfeng Technology is carried here as the article's own attributed statement, not verified independently — it does not appear in any prior KB entry and refines rather than contradicts the existing device- and alliance-level figures.
