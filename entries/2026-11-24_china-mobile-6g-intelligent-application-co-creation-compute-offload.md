## Summary (EN)

At the 2026 Mobile Network Conference (2026移动网络大会) in Guangzhou on 6 November 2026, China Mobile chief engineer Wang Xiaoyun announced the "China Mobile 6G Intelligent Application Co-creation Action", reported by Xinhua. The action aims to work with industry partners to identify high-value 6G scenarios and foster new 6G products and business models. Its plan, called "1-10-100-1000", consists of one open test facility, ten focus domains (examples named: networked robots, AI terminals, immersive communication, air-space-ground-sea integration and smart energy), 100 collaborative tasks and 1,000 partners; five "DRIVE" measures cover an open test facility, R&D funding, cross-disciplinary experts, an industry fund and shared ecosystem resources. Wang noted that fewer than 30 months remain before the 6G standard is frozen.

On the 6G capability side, the report lists stronger connectivity (higher uplink speeds, more consistent low latency, rapid group networking for multiple intelligent agents), ubiquitous low-latency compute services, open data services based on base-station sensing, and trusted authentication and security. The compute-related statement, the first of three "beyond-connectivity" capabilities, is ubiquitous low-latency compute service: "6G将就近提供智算资源，支持终端的推理任务按需实时卸载到网络，让终端更加轻量化、长续航、低成本" (6G will provide intelligent-computing resources nearby and let terminals offload inference tasks to the network in real time on demand, making terminals lighter, longer-lasting and cheaper).

Link: cellular (6G), as a stated direction; the report gives no numbers, architecture or measurements for the offloading capability.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 11 月 6 日，在广州举行的 2026 移动网络大会上，中国移动总工程师王晓云宣布"中国移动 6G 智能应用共创行动"，新华网予以报道。该行动旨在与产业伙伴共同确定高价值 6G 场景，培育 6G 新产品与新商业模式。其"一十百千"计划包括一个开放测试设施、十大重点领域（报道所举例子：网联机器人、AI 终端、沉浸式通信、空天地海一体和智慧能源）、100 项协同任务和 1,000 家伙伴；五项"DRIVE"举措涵盖开放测试设施、研发资金、跨学科专家、产业基金和共享生态资源。王晓云指出，距离 6G 标准冻结已不足 30 个月。

在 6G 能力方面，报道列出更强的连接能力（更高的上行速率、更稳定的低时延、多智能体快速组网）、泛在的低时延算力服务、基于基站感知的开放数据服务，以及可信认证与安全。与计算相关的表述是三项"超越连接"能力中的第一项，即泛在的低时延算力服务："6G将就近提供智算资源，支持终端的推理任务按需实时卸载到网络，让终端更加轻量化、长续航、低成本"。

所经链路：蜂窝（6G），为宣示的方向；报道未给出该卸载能力的数字、架构或测量。
