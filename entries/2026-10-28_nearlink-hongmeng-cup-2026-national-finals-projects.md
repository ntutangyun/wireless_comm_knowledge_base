## Summary (EN)

The alliance reports that the national finals of the third "SparkLink-HarmonyOS Cup" innovation-application challenge took place on 22 October 2026 at the Shenzhen Convention and Exhibition Center (Futian), inside the SparkLink ecosystem expo of CEIC2026. It was co-organised by the International SparkLink Wireless Short-range Communication Alliance (iSLA) and the Global Intelligent IoT Alliance (GIIC), and was presented as a joint showcase of SparkLink and OpenHarmony. Under the theme "SparkLink as the torch, HarmonyOS building the dream" (星闪成炬・鸿蒙筑梦), the contest focused on "SparkLink + HarmonyOS + AI", with five tracks: smart vehicles, smart terminals, smart home, smart manufacturing and smart innovation. Open to enrolled students (vocational, undergraduate, postgraduate) and maker teams, the contest started in June with training camps and team selection; hundreds of teams registered nationwide and 15 reached the finals (list published 18 October). Eleven industry experts formed the on-site jury, judging innovation, technical implementation, completeness and deployment value.

Award list (as published): special prize, "Tree-networking SparkLink power data-transmission terminal" (Shenzhen Technology University); first prize, "SparkLink-HarmonyOS gas-pipeline inspection and repair system" (Nanchang Hangkong University); second prize, a SparkLink-based physics-experiment teaching aid system (Beijing Institute of Technology). Third prizes (one per track) include an OpenHarmony-plus-SparkLink Mesh street-lamp system, a desktop AI educational robot (CoCube, Fudan University), a SparkLink system based on RF anti-interference technology, a dynamic elastic surround-view system based on SparkLink SLB (Sun Yat-sen University), and a sheep face-recognition and health-management system. Three e-paper-track third prizes went to a conference system on OpenHarmony, an Ascend-based production-line visual inspection system and a distributed highland-barley (qingke) cold-stress breeding control platform.

## Summary (ZH)

联盟报道，第三届“星闪鸿蒙杯”创新应用挑战赛全国总决赛于2026年10月22日在深圳会展中心（福田）举行，位于CEIC2026星闪生态创新展现场。赛事由国际星闪无线短距通信联盟（iSLA）与全球智慧物联网联盟（GIIC）共同主办，定位为星闪与OpenHarmony跨生态融合成果的联合展示。主题为“星闪成炬・鸿蒙筑梦”，聚焦“星闪+鸿蒙+AI”，设智能汽车、智能终端、智能家居、智能制造、智能创新五大赛道。赛事面向在读学生（专科、本科、研究生）及社会创客团队，6月启动，经训练营与队内选拔，全国数百支队伍报名，10月18日公布15支晋级队伍。现场评审团由11位产业专家组成，从创新性、技术实现、完整度与产业落地价值评审。

获奖名单（据原文）：特等奖为深圳技术大学《基于星闪树形组网的电力数传终端》；一等奖为南昌航空大学《星闪鸿蒙驱动的燃气管道智能巡检修复系统》；二等奖为北京理工大学《基于星闪通信的中小学物理实验智能教具系统》。三等奖（每赛道一队）包括《开源鸿蒙+星闪 Mesh 智慧路灯系统》、复旦大学《CoCube 桌面级 AI 教育机器人》、《基于射频抗干扰技术的星闪系统》、中山大学《基于星闪 SLB 的动态弹性智能化环视系统》及《羊脸识别与羊只健康管理系统》。电子纸专项三等奖包括基于开源鸿蒙的智慧会议系统、基于昇腾平台的产线视觉质检系统和分布式青稞低温胁迫育种管控平台。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Technical features the organiser says the entries exercised: low latency, interference resistance, a new frequency-hopping algorithm, and integrated communication-positioning-sensing.
- Winning designs use SparkLink tree networking (power data terminal), Mesh (street lamps) and SLB (a surround-view system), showing which topologies and modes student developers are reaching for.
- Many winning projects are built on HarmonyOS/OpenHarmony, which the organiser presents as a "SparkLink + HarmonyOS" dual-ecosystem showcase.
- No benchmarks, latency figures or code links are published; results are judged by demonstration.
