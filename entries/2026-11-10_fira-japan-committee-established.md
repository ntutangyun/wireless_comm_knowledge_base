## Summary (EN)

The FiRa Consortium announced on 5 November 2026 (Beaverton, OR) that it has established the FiRa Japan Committee, a permanent regional committee to engage stakeholders in Japan and support adoption of FiRa technology. The committee is also meant to bring Japanese requirements and perspectives to FiRa use case and specification development, and to give members and other stakeholders a forum to exchange information, identify adoption challenges and pass feedback to the FiRa Working Groups. Japanese-language materials and communications are part of the plan.

More than 90 people attended the committee's inaugural meeting, which discussed priorities for the Japanese market. Yo Tabayashi of Sony chairs the committee; FiRa president Sunil Jogi is quoted on the value of local insight for adoption and for FiRa's global work.

The release lists four focus areas: promoting awareness and adoption through Japanese-language materials, outreach and local events; engaging public transit agencies, financial institutions, standardization bodies, industry associations and others about market needs and adoption challenges; providing Japanese market requirements, feedback and recommendations to the relevant FiRa Working Groups; and building relationships with organizations and standardization bodies that can help advance FiRa technology in Japan. The next meeting is planned for December 2026.
