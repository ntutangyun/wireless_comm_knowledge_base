## Summary (EN)

SatNews (23 Nov 2026) reports that the National Telecommunications and Information Administration (NTIA) has submitted all 20 of the United States' agenda-item positions for the 2027 World Radiocommunication Conference (WRC-27) to the Inter-American Telecommunication Commission (CITEL). The conference is scheduled for 18 October to 12 November 2027 in Shanghai. Arielle Roth, assistant secretary of commerce for communications and information and NTIA administrator, is the official quoted. A separate outlet, Advanced Television, also carried the story the same day.

For Agenda Item 1, the article says the U.S. completed positions covering all 19 studies under that item, with topics including satellite direct-to-device (D2D) connectivity, competition among NGSO constellations, lunar communications protections and 6G mobile broadband. For Agenda Items 9 and 10, the U.S. proposed modernised GSO and NGSO coordination rules modelled on the FCC's updated equivalent power flux density (EPFD) framework, seeking interim adoption across "several key frequency bands" alongside longer-term reforms; the article does not name the bands.

## Summary (ZH)

SatNews（2026 年 11 月 23 日）报道，美国国家电信和信息管理局（NTIA）已向美洲国家间电信委员会（CITEL）提交美国针对 2027 年世界无线电通信大会（WRC-27）的全部 20 项议题立场。大会定于 2027 年 10 月 18 日至 11 月 12 日在上海举行。文中引述的官员是商务部负责通信和信息的助理部长、NTIA 局长 Arielle Roth。另一家媒体 Advanced Television 于同日也刊发了该消息。

关于议题 1，文章称美国已完成该议题下全部 19 项研究的立场，涉及的主题包括卫星直连设备（D2D）连接、NGSO 星座之间的竞争、月球通信保护以及 6G 移动宽带。关于议题 9 和 10，美国提出了参照 FCC 更新的等效功率通量密度（EPFD）框架的现代化 GSO 与 NGSO 协调规则，寻求在"若干关键频段"临时采纳，同时推进长期改革；文章未点明具体频段。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Submission route: positions go to CITEL, the regional body, ahead of its 48th meeting of Permanent Consultative Committee II (PCC.II) in Bogotá, Colombia, "later this month"; the article says bilateral consultations are planned there.
- Agenda Item 1: positions on all 19 studies; named topics are satellite D2D, NGSO competition, lunar communications protections and 6G mobile broadband.
- Agenda Items 9 and 10: modernised GSO/NGSO coordination rules modelled on the FCC's updated EPFD framework; interim adoption sought for several key bands plus longer-term reforms.
- Not in the article: the full list of the 20 items, the individual U.S. positions beyond those above, and the names of the bands for items 9 and 10. The article does not mention agenda items 1.13 or 1.10.
