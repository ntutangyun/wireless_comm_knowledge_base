## Summary (EN)

At the Critical Communications World (CCW) 2026 conference, Jukka Vialén (3GPP SA WG6 Vice Chair, Airbus) presented the state of Mission-Critical (MC) standardization — 3GPP's own news post (June 16, 2026) marks the moment MC services formally hook into the 6G program. A new SA6 study, **FS_MCX_MC**, is underway on MC architecture evolution and 6G capabilities for MC services. It will analyse the 6G use cases of TR 22.870 — the first approved 6G study in 3GPP — and the service requirements of TS 22.270 for their impact on the MC application layer. This is the application-layer counterpart to the radio-side 6G studies the KB has tracked (Rel-21 timeline approval; first 6G RAN study).

The same presentation lists the active Rel-20 MCX work: ambient-listening pause/resume, ProSe multi-hop relay (sidelink relays extending MC coverage), FRMCS (future railway mobile communication system) enhancements, MC UE temporary disable/re-enable, logging and recording, and discreet monitoring (slated Rel-21). Together they show a decade-old feature family (MCPTT began in Rel-13) still accreting operational features while its 6G re-architecture starts in parallel.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The KB's 6G thread so far is radio- and timeline-centric (2026-06-12_3gpp-rel21-6g-timeline-approved; 2026-05-30_3gpp-release20-6g-study-status; 2026-07-07_ericsson-6g-ran-standardization-decisions). FS_MCX_MC is the first *vertical application layer* formally starting its 6G evolution — public safety and railway (FRMCS) requirements historically drive hard reliability/latency floors into the core spec, so what this study asks for in TR 22.870 terms will foreshadow 6G's guaranteed-service machinery. ProSe multi-hop relay is also worth watching as the MC-driven push for D2D mesh coverage.
