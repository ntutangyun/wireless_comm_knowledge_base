## Summary (EN)

The International SparkLink Alliance's (iSLA) Testing and Certification Group held its 13th meeting in Beijing on 2026-09-20, drawing over 30 expert representatives from more than 20 member organizations. The meeting was chaired by Wang Zhiwei (王智玮), deputy director of the IoT Technology Department at the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology's Tele Terminal Lab, who serves as the group's lead; Zhao Xiangyang (赵向阳) of the China Electronics Standardization Research Institute's IoT Research Center serves as vice lead.

Participants heard and discussed two test-system documents — the "SparkLink Technology Variant-Model Testing Explanatory Document" and the "SparkLink Product Categories and Directory Standard" — and approved them subject to the edits agreed at the meeting. The group also classified and discussed cases arising in alliance product testing, laying groundwork for refining the variant-model testing rules, and heard progress reports on three separate workstreams: SparkLink high-concurrency testing, GT-TE testing tools, and a SparkLink embodied-intelligence technology requirements blue book. Lianfa Bodong Technology (Beijing) Co., Ltd. (联发博动科技（北京）有限公司) is credited with substantial support for the meeting.

No further technical detail is given for any of the three progress-report items — the source names them but does not describe their content, scope, or expected completion date. The embodied-intelligence blue-book item is a named, in-progress alliance work item for embodied-AI/robotics; the source gives no detail on it.
