## Summary (EN)

A Hacker News thread (31 points, 2026-10-25) links a Chicago Tribune copy of an Associated Press report from 2026-10-24. The report says Ukrainian President Zelenskyy announced that Ukraine's Starlink and Starshield links had been restored to their earlier speeds after restrictions that disrupted front-line operations for more than a week, with restoration starting overnight and sufficient by Saturday morning. Elon Musk then wrote on X that SpaceX had throttled the Starlink service in Ukraine, stating that use of the civilian commercial service for offensive drone strikes was never allowed and that "that loophole was closed last week". According to the report, he maintained that the US government controls any changes made to Starshield, the military-grade variant.

The report adds that commanders had reported lost drones and disrupted missions during the restrictions on Starshield, and that US officials gave differing accounts of any administration involvement.
