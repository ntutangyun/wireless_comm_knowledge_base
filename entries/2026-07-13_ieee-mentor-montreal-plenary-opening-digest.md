## Summary (EN)
The IEEE 802 July 2026 plenary opened in Montreal (July 12–17), and the 802.11 Mentor stream uploaded 11 new documents (DCN 1418–1433) across six groups on the opening weekend. The theme is plenary logistics — agendas, snapshots, and minutes for UCM TIG, TGbr, TGbt, and TGbp — plus four substantive technical contributions: a ZTE proposed draft text (PDT) modifying the post-quantum cryptography (PQC) parameter element in TGbt, Intel considerations for the Integrated mmWave short training field (IMMW-STF) in TGbq, an NYUAD contribution on Sionna-based ray tracing for unified channel modeling in UCM TIG, and a ZTE follow-up on AI-offload use cases for agents in the AI Offload SG.

This is the front edge of the plenary wave: TGbn D1.50 recirculation ballot, TGbi D5.0 final WG approval, and the TGbt initial letter ballot are all predicted for this session per the 802.11 Timelines page (dated 2026-07-08), and the WNG post-Wi-Fi-8 study group is expected to form this week. Substantive comment-resolution and ballot-result documents should land through July 17.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The Montreal plenary (July 12–17) is the most consequential 802.11 session of 2026 so far: TGbn's D1.50 recirculation, TGbi's final WG approval, TGbt's first letter ballot, and the expected formation of the post-Wi-Fi-8 (Wi-Fi 9) study group are all on this week's agenda. The opening-weekend uploads are mostly procedural, but ZTE's PQC parameter-element PDT (1419) continues the TGbt normative-text push seen in the pre-plenary Huawei trio (see 2026-07-10_tgbt-plenary-eve-digest), and Intel's IMMW-STF contribution (1418) is a concrete PHY building block for 802.11bq after the January architectural split over mmWave independence. Expect ballot results and comment-resolution documents in the coming days.
