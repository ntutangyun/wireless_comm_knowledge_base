## Summary (EN)

The second to fourth parts of the alliance's recap of the CEIC2026 SparkLink ecosystem expo (released 25 October 2026) cover the ecosystem zone, organised as standards, testing and certification, industry applications, and internationalisation, followed by a partner showcase.

Standards: the alliance publicly set out, for the first time (the article's wording), its SparkLink 3.0 technical layout for the AI era, released a SLE 3.0 standard blue book and an audio-industry white paper, and the secretary-general said in a live tour that the national market regulator and standardisation committee adopted the SparkLink series as recommended national standards on 28 September 2026, effective 1 April 2027; the system is stated as 15 national, 6 industry and 91 group standards.

Testing and certification: exhibitors showed a comprehensive tester supporting the latest protocol (T6000), a spatial-channel emulator and a GNSS emulator (Chuangyuan Xinke, 创远信科), and a protocol-conformance platform for the access layer (Yonghe). The alliance's OpenLab showed a car-key ranging dongle and an audio dongle; the article says products listed on the alliance website were certified with this toolset. There are two ranging-dongle variants, based on HiSilicon Hi2821A and on a Maxscend (卓胜微) chip, and an OpenLab-built test-assistant app on the Huawei app store supports phone-to-phone, phone-to-Maxscend-board and phone-to-Chuangyao-board ranging. Four OpenLabs (Shenzhen, Hubei, Wuhan, Shanghai) are named.

Applications: the digital car key uses phase difference plus time-of-flight ranging, with a stated 10 m welcome, 3 m auto-unlock and 8 m auto-lock; the combination of Yinji (银基) and the GAC Toyota Bozhi 7 (铂智7) is described as marking entry into front-fit mass production. Other shown items included a smart bed with six-zone firmness adjustment, a smart blackboard developed with Beijing Institute of Technology (teaching module with a SparkLink module, data returned through a TV-side dongle), a power-distribution scheme (Xuji gateway with State Grid breakers), a coal-mine solution, an e-paper medical display, a "one-to-hundred" high-concurrency multi-hop SparkLink Mesh, lighting/DTU solutions and industrial gateways. For internationalisation, a Japanese partner showed a modified Mazda sports car with sensors sending data over SparkLink to an AI analysis computer, and a CE-certified module was shown.

Partner showcase: HiSilicon (SLE chips, soundbar); Maxscend (automotive-grade SLE 2.0 + BLE 6.x dual-mode ranging chip MXD2680, stated accuracy under 0.3 m and range beyond 130 m; also a UWB chip MXD2710); Zhongke Jingshang (stated plan to launch its 2.0 high-bandwidth SLB chip commercially in December 2026, and that a mandatory national standard for commercial-vehicle surround-view will be the main SLB chip application); Weilian (module vendor, co-author of the published GB/T 48336.301-2026); and others. The article closes with a stated cumulative chip shipment of more than 380 million since Q4 2023 and 1,264 member units.

## Summary (ZH)

联盟对CEIC2026星闪生态创新展的回顾稿（2026年10月25日发布）的第二至第四部分介绍生态展区：按标准、测试认证、产业应用、国际化四个板块展开，随后是合作伙伴展示。

标准：联盟首次公开星闪3.0面向AI时代的核心技术布局（原文表述），发布星闪SLE3.0标准蓝皮书与音频产业发展白皮书；秘书长在探展直播中表示，2026年9月28日国家市场监督管理总局、国家标准化管理委员会已将星闪系列标准采纳为推荐性国家标准，2027年4月1日实施；标准体系现含15项国家标准、6项行业标准、91项团体标准。

测试认证：创远信科展示支持最新协议的T6000综测仪、空域信道模拟器和导航（GNSS）模拟器，永谐展示面向接入层的协议一致性测试平台；联盟OpenLab展示车钥匙测距Dongle和音频Dongle，文章称联盟官网列名产品均基于该套工具完成测试认证。测距Dongle有两款，分别基于海思Hi2821A与卓胜微芯片；OpenLab开发的星闪测试助手已上架华为应用市场，支持手机与手机、手机与卓胜微开发板、手机与创耀开发板测距。文中列出深圳、湖北、武汉、上海四个OpenLab。

产业应用：数字车钥匙采用相位差+TOF测距，文中指标为10米迎宾、3米自动解锁、8米自动上锁；银基与广汽丰田铂智7的搭载组合被描述为标志着星闪进入前装量产深水区。其他展品包括六分区调节软硬的智慧床、与北京理工大学联合打造的智慧黑板（教具模块内置星闪模组，经电视端Dongle回传数据）、电力配网方案（许继网关与国网断路器）、煤矿方案、电子纸医疗显示、“一对百”高并发多跳星闪Mesh、智慧照明/DTU以及工业网关。国际化方面，日本合作企业展示了改装马自达跑车，车上传感器经星闪回传至AI分析电脑，另展出CE认证模组。

合作伙伴展示：海思（SLE芯片、Soundbar）；卓胜微（车规级SLE 2.0与BLE 6.x双模测距芯片MXD2680，文中称测距精度小于0.3米、拉距超过130米；另有UWB芯片MXD2710）；中科晶上（称2.0高带宽SLB芯片预计2026年12月首发商用，并称商用车全景环视强制性国家标准将成为SLB芯片的主要应用）；纬联（模组商，参编已发布的GB/T 48336.301-2026）等。文末称自2023年第四季度以来星闪芯片累计出货超过3.8亿颗（原文为3.8亿），联盟成员单位1264家。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Test tooling named: T6000 comprehensive tester, spatial-channel emulator, GNSS emulator, access-layer conformance platform, car-key ranging dongle (two silicon variants: HiSilicon Hi2821A and Maxscend), audio dongle, phone-based test assistant.
- Digital car key ranging: phase difference plus time of flight; stated distances 10 m / 3 m / 8 m for welcome / unlock / lock.
- MXD2680 (Maxscend): automotive-grade SLE 2.0 and BLE 6.x dual-mode ranging chip; stated accuracy under 0.3 m, range above 130 m (exhibitor figures).
- Zhongke Jingshang: 2.0 high-bandwidth SLB chip planned for first commercial release in December 2026 (stated plan).
- Standards inventory as stated: 15 national, 6 industry, 91 group standards; national adoption date 28 Sep 2026, effective 1 Apr 2027.
- The article does not describe protocol-level details of the SLE 3.0 blue book.
