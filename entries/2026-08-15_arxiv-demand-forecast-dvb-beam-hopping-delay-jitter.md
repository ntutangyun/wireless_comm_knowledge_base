## Summary (EN)
Demirci, Mantelet, Martel, Frigon and Karabulut Kurt (Polytechnique Montréal Poly-Grames + MDA Space Satellite Systems; submitted 8 Aug 2026, accepted to the IEEE MILCOM 2026 main track) study a cross-layer question that beam-hopping (BH) LEO systems face in practice: because a BH illumination plan must be committed ahead of time, the planner works from a *forecast* of per-cell demand — so how much does forecast quality actually matter to the user-visible metrics, delay and jitter? Forecasting models are usually judged on standalone accuracy; this paper closes the loop through a **full-stack, DVB-S2X-compliant LEO simulator** with a regenerative forward-link payload, one active beam hopping over three Earth-fixed cells, 1 ms dwell time and 15–45 ms planning periods, and self-similar traffic generated by fractional Brownian motion (fBm), the motivating scenario being tactical military links with bursty, unpredictable demand.

Two forecasters are compared inside the BH plan generator against a static-allocation baseline: **FARIMA** (fractional ARIMA, which captures self-similarity) and the **Norros predictor** (the closed-form optimal predictor of Gripenberg–Norros for fBm-driven processes). Findings: forecast-based dynamic planning **reduces per-beam delay by 10–40%** relative to static allocation, depending on load and planning periodicity — but although Norros beats FARIMA by **14–16% in NMSE**, that accuracy gap yields **less than 1% delay reduction** and essentially identical jitter. The authors' conclusion is a design guideline rather than a new algorithm: once a forecaster captures the traffic's self-similar structure, further accuracy is nearly worthless to the network; effort should go to scalability and computational cost of the forecasting/planning chain across many beams and cells.

## Summary (ZH)
Demirci、Mantelet、Martel、Frigon 与 Karabulut Kurt（蒙特利尔综合理工 Poly-Grames + MDA Space 卫星系统部；2026 年 8 月 8 日提交，已被 IEEE MILCOM 2026 主会接收）研究波束跳变（BH）低轨系统在实践中面临的一个跨层问题：由于 BH 照射计划必须提前提交，规划器只能依据各小区需求的*预测*工作——那么预测质量对用户可感知的时延与抖动到底影响多大？预测模型通常只按独立精度评价；本文通过一个**符合 DVB-S2X 的全栈低轨仿真器**闭合了这一回路：再生式前向链路载荷、单个活动波束在三个地固小区间跳变、1 ms 驻留、15–45 ms 规划周期、分数布朗运动（fBm）生成的自相似流量，动机场景为突发且难以预测的战术军用链路。

在 BH 计划生成器中比较了两种预测器与静态分配基线：**FARIMA**（分数 ARIMA，可刻画自相似性）与 **Norros 预测器**（Gripenberg–Norros 针对 fBm 驱动过程的闭式最优预测器）。结论：基于预测的动态规划相对静态分配**将各波束时延降低 10–40%**（取决于负载与规划周期）——但尽管 Norros 的 NMSE 比 FARIMA 好 **14–16%**，这一精度差距只换来**不到 1% 的时延降低**，抖动几乎相同。作者给出的是设计准则而非新算法：一旦预测器捕捉到流量的自相似结构，再提精度对网络几乎无益；应把精力放在跨多波束多小区的预测/规划链路的可扩展性与计算开销上。

## Key technical points (EN)
- **Setup:** full-stack DVB-S2X LEO simulator, regenerative forward-link payload; 1 active beam over 3 Earth-fixed cells; 1 ms dwell, 15–45 ms planning periods; fBm self-similar traffic; military/tactical framing.
- **Forecasters:** FARIMA vs Norros closed-form fBm predictor, both feeding BH plan generation; static allocation baseline.
- **Delay:** forecast-driven dynamic BH plans cut delay 10–40% across beams vs static, depending on load and planning periodicity.
- **Accuracy vs benefit:** Norros 14–16% better NMSE than FARIMA → <1% delay improvement, near-identical jitter.
- **Guideline:** prioritise scalability/compute of forecasting+planning over marginal accuracy gains.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The KB's beam-hopping thread (2026-06-15_arxiv-aidos-beam-hopping-ngso, 2026-07-07_arxiv-bridge-beam-hopping-leo-drl, plus the June robust-RB-allocation and the 2026-08-14 digital-twin entries) is about *scheduling algorithms* that assume the demand input is known. This paper is the first to test the **upstream forecasting step end-to-end against network KPIs** on a standards-compliant DVB-S2X stack, and its negative result is the useful part: forecast-vs-static is worth 10–40% delay, forecaster-vs-forecaster is worth ~1% — a saturation that argues against heavier learned predictors for BH planning and, indirectly, tempers the "digital twin + AI forecasting" narrative unless the twin delivers something beyond point-forecast accuracy. It is also a rare operator-industry co-authored study (MDA Space, the Telesat Lightspeed prime — see 2026-08-05_telesat-lightspeed-225-sats-mda-expansion) with a MILCOM/military framing, consistent with the Mil-Ka Lightspeed expansion. Caveat: single-beam, three-cell simulation; the paper's own scalability argument suggests multi-beam results as the natural follow-up.

知识库的波束跳变线索（2026-06-15 Aidos、2026-07-07 BRIDGE，以及 6 月鲁棒 RB 分配与 2026-08-14 数字孪生条目）关注的是假设需求输入已知的*调度算法*。本文首次在符合标准的 DVB-S2X 协议栈上**端到端地用网络 KPI 检验上游预测环节**，其"负结果"恰是有用之处：预测 vs 静态值 10–40% 时延，预测器 vs 预测器仅值约 1%——这一饱和现象不支持为 BH 规划引入更重的学习型预测器，也间接为"数字孪生 + AI 预测"叙事降温，除非孪生能提供点预测精度之外的价值。它还是少见的运营商产业界合著研究（MDA Space，Telesat Lightspeed 主承包商——见 2026-08-05 条目），采用 MILCOM/军用框架，与 Lightspeed 军用 Ka 扩容相呼应。局限：单波束、三小区仿真；按论文自身的可扩展性论点，多波束结果是自然的后续。
