## Summary (EN)

According to SatNews (28 October 2026), the FCC issued its decision approving Amazon's acquisition of Globalstar on Tuesday 27 October 2026. The deal was announced in April at roughly 11.5 billion USD (SatNews cites a Via Satellite estimate of 10.8 billion USD covering the satellite fleet, ground infrastructure and licensed spectrum). The article says the FCC reasoned that the combination would increase competition in direct-to-device (D2D) service, where SpaceX already has a strong position through Starlink Mobile.

Globalstar is to become wholly owned by Amazon, keep its brand and operate as an affiliate of Kuiper Systems LLC (Amazon Leo), connecting its mobile satellite services with Amazon's LEO network. The article restates that the FCC Space Bureau authorised Globalstar's C-3 D2D system on 19 October: 54 spacecraft (48 active LEO plus six spares) built by MDA Space, with launches from 2027. Amazon expects closing in 2027 pending remaining regulatory and interagency approvals. The page gives no FCC order number, approval conditions or named bands. This is a single-source report: a separate web search found no second outlet confirming the approval, so the order text should be checked.
