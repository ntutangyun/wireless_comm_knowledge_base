## Summary (EN)

A January 8, 2026 article, reporting from the "SparkLink Smart Union Innovation Forum" (星闪智联创新论坛), covers a SparkLink (NearLink)-based intelligent switchgear terminal fielded by Xu Ji Electric Research Institute (许继电气科学研究院), a China Electrical Equipment Group (中国电气装备) subsidiary — described in the vendor's own announcement as the world's first switchgear terminal based on this technology, and as SparkLink's first large-scale application in the power industry. The terminal uses **SLB+SLE mode** (SparkLink Basic for higher-throughput control/data traffic, SparkLink Low Energy for low-power sensing) and relies on **SLP (SparkLink Positioning)** for high-precision locating, reporting a **12 Mbps transmission peak** and **sub-1-millisecond latency**. The article is a vendor-contributed release (通讯员 许继电气) republished by a news portal, not independent reporting.

The stated target problem is the "last 200 metres" (最后200米) wireless-access gap in power communications: connecting substations and distribution networks where fixed RS-485 serial-bus wiring is impractical to install or maintain. The article frames the deployment's significance around **supply-chain independence** — both the SparkLink standard and the chipsets involved are described as fully domestically developed — positioning this as the first large-scale application of the technology inside the power/energy sector specifically (as opposed to the smart-home, wearable, or automotive verticals this KB has covered so far for SparkLink).

## Key technical points (EN)
