## Summary (EN)

Every Day Economic News (每日经济新闻) published a report on 2026-10-24, written at CEIC2026 in Shenzhen, on what SparkLink needs in order to become a mainstream short-range standard alongside Bluetooth and Wi-Fi. The reporter's framing is that the technology gained visibility mainly through Huawei phones, earbuds and PCs (the alliance booth also showed Huawei keyboards, mice, styluses, watches and some fridges and TVs using it) and now needs other phone makers, chip vendors and industries. Almost all of the forward-looking figures come from one source, alliance secretary-general Zeng Guosong (曾国松), and are reported rather than independently verified.

The article follows two lines. First, "from Huawei to more phone makers": Android flagships run on Qualcomm and MediaTek platforms, so wider adoption needs chip integration and cost reduction; the alliance points to unified standards and certification for cross-brand interoperability, and says Shanghai HiSilicon remains the main supplier while other chip firms join. Second, "new growth in automotive and robotics": digital car keys are named a priority market, AI glasses and humanoid robots are emerging areas, and a smart-energy promotion group is planned for next year.

## Summary (ZH)

《每日经济新闻》于2026年10月24日刊发了在深圳CEIC2026现场撰写的报道，讨论星闪要成为与蓝牙、Wi-Fi并列的主流短距通信标准还需要什么。报道的基本判断是：星闪主要依靠华为手机、耳机、电脑等产品带动进入大众视野（联盟展台上还有华为键盘、鼠标、手写笔、手表及部分冰箱、电视），接下来需要其他手机厂商、芯片企业和更多行业的加入。文中几乎所有前瞻性数据都来自联盟秘书长曾国松，属转述，并未独立核实。

文章分两条线展开。其一是“从华为走向更多手机厂商”：安卓旗舰使用高通、联发科平台，想扩大适配需要完成芯片集成并降低成本；联盟强调通过统一标准与认证实现跨品牌互通，并称上海海思仍是主要供应商，同时有更多芯片企业加入。其二是“在汽车与机器人中寻找新增长”：数字车钥匙被列为重点市场，AI眼镜、人形机器人为新兴方向，明年计划成立智慧能源推广组。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Shipments (per Zeng)**: about 114.3 million SparkLink chips in 2025 (stated as actual); 150-200 million expected for 2026 (forecast).
- **Digital car key (per Zeng)**: about 1.5 million vehicles expected in 2026 and about 3 million as next year's goal. For some automotive supply-chain schemes he compares a UWB key at roughly 2,000 yuan with a SparkLink key at roughly 1,000 yuan; no bill-of-materials breakdown is given.
- **Chip timelines (company plans relayed by Zeng, not confirmed by the companies)**: Zhuosheng Micro on car-key work, Telink on an IoT/audio chip in 2027, and Zhongke Jingshang on commercial SLB 2.0 next year.
- **Non-Huawei phones**: several domestic and international phone makers are said to be exploring integration, none named as committed; Zeng says Qualcomm senior executives visited the alliance, that MediaTek is being encouraged, and that the timing of mass adoption outside Huawei is uncertain.
- **Appliances and robots**: Midea, Haier, Hisense and TCL are cited as advancing SparkLink applications. Robot-maker proof-of-concept work (including replacing some internal wiring) is under way, but Zeng states that true commercialisation has not yet happened.
- **Unnamed luxury automaker**: An unnamed international luxury brand has, per Zeng, settled on a 2030 SparkLink model and hopes STMicroelectronics or NXP will supply Bluetooth + SparkLink multimode chips. See the separate 2026-11-02 entry on the BMW report.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **出货量（曾国松口径）**：2025年星闪芯片出货约1.143亿颗（作为实际数据陈述）；2026年预计1.5-2亿颗（预测）。
- **数字车钥匙（曾国松口径）**：2026年预计约150万台，明年目标约300万台。他称在部分车载供应链方案中，UWB钥匙约2000元、星闪钥匙约1000元；文中未给出成本构成。
- **芯片时间表（经曾国松转述的企业计划，未经企业确认）**：卓胜微做车钥匙方向，泰凌微2027年推出IoT/音频芯片，中科晶上明年推出商用SLB 2.0。
- **非华为手机**：称国内外多家手机厂商正在探索集成，均未被点名为已确定；曾国松表示高通高层曾到访联盟，联盟也在鼓励联发科，但华为生态之外的规模化时间尚不确定。
- **家电与机器人**：文中称美的、海尔、海信、TCL等家电企业也在推进星闪技术应用；机器人厂商的验证项目（含替代部分内部线缆）正在进行，但曾国松明确表示真正的商用化尚未出现。
- **未具名豪华车企**：据曾国松，某国际豪华品牌已确定计划2030年推出搭载星闪的车型，目前希望由意法半导体或恩智浦提供蓝牙+星闪多模芯片。另见2026-11-02关于宝马报道的条目。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The adoption barriers this interview describes (Android platform integration, cost, cross-brand certification) are stated by the alliance itself, which also says it is a "late arrival and a catcher-up". The figures are alliance statements relayed by one outlet and should be read as targets and self-reported numbers, not audited totals.
