## Summary (EN)

The FCC's tentative agenda for its 29 October 2026 Open Commission Meeting, announced on 9 October 2026 (as relayed by the Benton Institute), includes Item 2, "Modernizing the Supplemental Coverage from Space Rules": a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking that would modernize the Commission's rules for direct-to-device (D2D) services in licensed spectrum.

SpacePolicyOnline's event listing for the same meeting describes the proposals, if adopted, as freeing up an additional 482 MHz for Supplemental Coverage from Space (SCS), removing burdensome SCS requirements, and considering measures to modernize and harmonize the SCS and Mobile Satellite Service (MSS) rules. It lists the dockets as GN 26-281 and GN 23-65 and notes that, as of 10 October, this was the one space-related item on the agenda. The Benton page itself gives only the item title and one-sentence description; it does not state the 482 MHz figure or dockets.

The same agenda carries a Further Notice of Proposed Rulemaking on 25 MHz of mid-band spectrum for advanced commercial services and a Report and Order lifting the restriction on drone operations in the 800 MHz Cellular band. The agenda is tentative and an NPRM only seeks comment; the Commission's own text was not retrievable for this entry.

## Summary (ZH)

美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）2026 年 10 月 29 日公开委员会会议的暂定议程于 2026 年 10 月 9 日公布（由 Benton Institute 转载），其中第 2 项为“修订太空补充覆盖（SCS）规则”：这是一项立法建议通知（NPRM），拟修订委员会针对授权频谱内星地直连（D2D）业务的规则。

SpacePolicyOnline 对同一会议的活动列表称，若获通过，相关提案将为太空补充覆盖（SCS）额外释放 482 MHz 频谱、取消繁琐的 SCS 要求，并考虑采取措施使 SCS 与移动卫星业务（MSS）规则现代化并相互协调。该列表给出的档案号为 GN 26-281 和 GN 23-65，并注明截至 10 月 10 日该项是议程中唯一与航天相关的议题。Benton 页面本身仅给出议题标题和一句话描述，未提及 482 MHz 数字或档案号。

同一议程还包括关于 25 MHz 中频段频谱用于先进商用服务的进一步立法建议通知，以及解除 800 MHz 蜂窝频段无人机操作限制的报告与命令。议程为暂定，NPRM 仅征求意见；本条目未能取得委员会原文。
