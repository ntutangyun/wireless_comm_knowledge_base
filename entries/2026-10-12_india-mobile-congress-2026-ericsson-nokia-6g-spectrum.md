## Summary (EN)
India Mobile Congress (IMC) 2026 ran October 7-10 in New Delhi under the theme "Scale Without Boundaries," jointly organized by India's Department of Telecommunications (DoT) and the Cellular Operators Association of India (COAI); this was the event's 10th edition. Reporting from the opening day (telecomlead.com, 8 October 2026) highlights two concrete telecom-infrastructure announcements. Ericsson said it has manufactured more than 15,000 antennas and 25 million components in India since July 2025, and is now exporting antennas from its Indian manufacturing base to Europe and other international markets; the company frames these antennas as targeting "improved network coverage and capacity, spectrum efficiency, uplink performance and energy efficiency" as operators move toward 5G Standalone, AI-driven networks and future architectures. Separately, Ulrich Dropmann, head of Standardization and Industry Environment, Strategy and Technology at Nokia Standards, stated at the event that "spectrum must be assigned at the right time and in adequate quantities to allow future 6G technologies to operate efficiently," positioning spectrum-planning timing as a critical policy requirement alongside standardization and infrastructure investment for India's eventual transition to 6G. Other IMC 2026 announcements reported the same day include an approximately INR 350 crore investment by Indian fiber-infrastructure provider Microscan in a submarine cable landing station at Alibaug (roughly 95 km from Mumbai) intended to serve international cable operators, carriers, hyperscalers, and data-center operators, and Starlink reporting completion of roughly 20 gateway locations with hundreds of antennas in India to support satellite broadband, pending further government clearances for commercial launch.
