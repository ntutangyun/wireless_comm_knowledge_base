## Summary (EN)

The Bluetooth SIG's public specifications page now lists a dedicated draft, "High-Resolution/Lossless Codec" (HLC), at version 0.9r05, assigned to the Audio, Telephony, and Automotive Working Group (feedback routed to ata-main@bluetooth.org). The page describes HLC as a Bluetooth audio codec designed for "High-Resolution audio-streaming and Lossless audio-streaming use cases." The SIG's specifications-in-development page lists "high-resolution and lossless audio" as one of several undated projects under "LE Audio enhancements," alongside spatial audio, surround sound, voice-assistant integration, sound-exposure reporting, and Auracast coverage/capacity/discovery improvements. The Bluetooth SIG has not dated the page, so when this draft first appeared cannot be determined from the source.

The draft page itself does not carry a visible publication date, revision history, or target-adoption date; it is a live specification artifact rather than a dated announcement. No press release, blog post or vendor coverage of this draft version was found.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The KB's prior HDT entry (2026-08-05_bluetooth-sig-hdt-draft-spec-public-review) covered the throughput mechanism, whose SIG-named use cases include high-resolution and lossless audio streaming (HDT PHY rate ladder up to 7.5 Mbps). The HLC draft is a separate, versioned codec specification (0.9r05) for the same use case; neither a dated announcement nor a target adoption date accompanies it. The draft gives the hi-res/lossless sub-project its own versioned codec specification (0.9r05) rather than only a roadmap bullet point, making the codec-definition side of the hi-res/lossless audio effort a distinct, trackable document alongside the already-public HDT PHY draft.
