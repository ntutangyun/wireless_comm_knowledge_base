## Summary (EN)
Sports Video Group reported on 11 November 2026 that Hornets Sports & Entertainment (HSE) and Spectrum have installed Wi-Fi 7 at Charlotte's Spectrum Center as part of Spectrum Business's Managed WiFi solution, described in the article as Spectrum's first Wi-Fi 7 deployment in a U.S. NBA arena. The article gives about 550 Wi-Fi 7 access points and cites 320 MHz channels, 4K QAM and Multi-Link Operation, with multi-gigabit speeds, lower latency and support for more devices. Wi-Fi NOW's 16 November roundup carries the same "nearly 550 access points" figure and dates the announcement to 10 November.

Coverage runs from the Event Level through the Belk Concourse, the Novant Health Suite Level and the Uptown Concourse. Listed use cases are fan ticketing, food and beverage ordering and suite guest access, live streaming and interactive apps, real-time video feeds of up to 8K, safety and venue monitoring with faster alerts, and simultaneous connectivity for fans, artist teams and media. Fiber and bandwidth provisioning details and the AP vendor are not given. The deployment is part of the arena transformation after a two-phase renovation that reopened the venue in October 2025. HSE CIO Ronnie Bryant and Spectrum Business VP Deidre Jordan are quoted in general terms about fan experience and seamless connectivity.
