## Summary (EN)

A 2026-11-06 article on the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) news feed recaps the role of Zhongke Jingshang (中科晶上), an alliance executive-council member, at CEIC2026 and the iSLA industry summit (22-24 October 2026, Shenzhen). The article states the company took part in drafting 14 of the 15 recommended national standards in the GB/T 48336 series (approved 28 September 2026, effective 1 April 2027), released a new-generation SparkLink 2.0 SLB chip, and showed prototypes and solutions in embodied intelligence, industrial control, power, home/campus networking and commercial vehicles. Summit-level industry figures are also quoted.

## Summary (ZH)

国际星闪联盟新闻栏目2026年11月6日刊发文章，回顾联盟常务理事单位中科晶上在CEIC2026暨国际星闪联盟产业峰会（2026年10月22日至24日，深圳）上的表现。文章称，该公司参与起草GB/T 48336系列15项推荐性国家标准中的14项（2026年9月28日批准发布，2027年4月1日起实施），发布新一代星闪2.0 SLB芯片，并展示了具身智能、工业控制、电力、家庭与园区组网、商用车等场景的原型与方案，同时引述了峰会层面的产业数据。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Standards**: the company says it took part in 14 of the 15 national standards, spanning four layers named in the article: overall architecture, access layer, basic service layer and basic application layer.
- **SLB 2.0 chip**: at the 2026 SparkLink application forum its deputy general manager gave a talk titled "SparkLink SLB 2.0: building an industrial-grade reliable communication foundation for the agent era". The company already offers an SLB 1.0 communication chip and modules used in smart home, smart vehicles and industrial control; the new SLB 2.0 chip is described as upgraded in band coverage, range, reliability, interference resistance and full-stack security. No numerical specifications are given.
- **Embodied intelligence**: the article says one dexterous hand may use more than 20 SparkLink chips and one robot up to a hundred or more, and that connection-density and reliability thresholds are far higher than for headsets or keyboards. The company built a prototype that replaces the cable between finger joints and the main controller with SparkLink.
- **Industrial control**: a rapid-development platform for wireless industrial controllers on the company's own baseband chip, supporting SparkLink, 5G and satellite links.
- **Power**: SparkLink schemes are in pilot verification for distribution operations and switchgear monitoring, as a member of the power-energy smart-operations SIG.
- **Home and campus**: a broadband networking solution for SparkLink APs and routers.
- **Commercial vehicles**: a multi-channel wireless video backhaul scheme for 360-degree surround view of commercial vehicles with partners; the article says the related mandatory national standard has finished review and entered the approval-for-filing stage, and says that once that standard takes effect, surround view will be the clearest near-term volume application for SLB chips (the company's view).
- **Ecosystem**: co-built SparkLink AI development kit (AI camera, AI robot main-control board, edge/end AI-node dongles, AI interactive microphone); its Wuhan subsidiary runs the alliance-authorised Hubei Innovation Lab (OpenLab).
- **Industry figures quoted**: more than 380 million SparkLink chips shipped since Q4 2023; 1,264 member units; digital car keys expected to exceed 2 million cumulative vehicles in 2026 with more than 30 production models; home-appliance chip shipments expected above 40 million; China short-range wireless connections to exceed 8 billion by 2030. The article notes current chip shipments are still mainly low-power narrowband, with wideband (SLB) the next increment.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **标准**：公司称参与起草15项国标中的14项，覆盖文章所列四个层级：总体架构、接入层、基础服务层、基础应用层。
- **SLB 2.0芯片**：在2026星闪应用论坛上，公司副总经理以《星闪SLB 2.0：构筑智能体时代工业级可靠通信底座》为题演讲。公司已有SLB 1.0通信芯片及模组，用于智能家居、智能汽车、工业控制；新一代SLB 2.0芯片被描述为在频段覆盖、传输距离、可靠性、抗干扰与全栈安全方面全面升级，文中无具体数值。
- **具身智能**：文章称一只灵巧手可能用到20多颗星闪芯片，一台机器人芯片用量可达上百颗，连接密度和可靠性门槛远高于耳机、键鼠；公司搭建了以星闪替代手指关节与主控之间线缆的灵巧手无线连接原型。
- **工业控制**：基于自研通信基带芯片的高性能无线工业控制器快速开发平台，支持星闪、5G、卫星等多种无线连接。
- **电力**：作为电力能源智慧运维星闪SIG成员，星闪方案已进入配电运维、开关柜监测等场景的试点验证。
- **家庭与园区**：面向星闪AP、星闪路由的宽带组网方案。
- **商用车**：与合作伙伴推出面向营运车辆360°全景环视的多路视频无线回传方案；文章称相关强制性国家标准已完成审定、进入报批阶段，并认为该标准一旦落地，环视将成为SLB芯片短期内最清晰、最具规模效应的应用（公司观点）。
- **生态**：共建星闪AI开发套件（AI摄像头、AI机器人主控板、边侧与端侧AI节点适配器、AI智能交互麦克风）；全资子公司武汉中科晶上建设了联盟授权OpenLab——星闪湖北创新实验室。
- **引述的产业数据**：自2023年第四季度以来星闪芯片累计出货超3.8亿颗；联盟成员单位1264家；2026年星闪数字车钥匙预计累计上车量超200万台、量产车型超30款；星闪家电芯片预计出货量超4000万；到2030年中国短距无线连接总量将突破80亿。文章同时指出当前芯片出货仍以低功耗窄带为主，宽带（SLB）是下一阶段增量。
