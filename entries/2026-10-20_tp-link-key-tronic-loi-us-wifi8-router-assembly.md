## Summary (EN)

TP-Link Systems Inc. announced on 2026-10-19 that it has entered into a letter of intent (LOI) with Key Tronic Corp., a U.S.-based electronics manufacturer, to bring product assembly to the United States. Under the LOI, the parties intend for Key Tronic to become a primary domestic manufacturing partner for TP-Link, supporting assembly of TP-Link's networking portfolio in Corinth, Mississippi. The press release says Wi-Fi 8 products are expected to be among the first to come off the new U.S. production line and that, pending U.S. regulatory approval, TP-Link expects domestic assembly of its Wi-Fi 8 home networking line to begin as early as next year.

Android Authority's report (19 October) places the deal against the FCC's ban on foreign-made routers, implemented in March, from which TP-Link has not received an exemption, and notes that TP-Link continues to pursue a temporary exemption. It quotes TP-Link corporate affairs officer Steve Kovsky, in an interview reported by PCMag, saying there are no published guidelines on what share of a router must be U.S.-made and asking whether chipsets bought from Qualcomm and Broadcom count as a U.S. product.

## Summary (ZH)

TP-Link Systems Inc. 于 2026-10-19 宣布，已与美国电子制造商 Key Tronic Corp. 签署意向书（LOI），将产品组装引入美国。按照意向书，双方拟让 Key Tronic 成为 TP-Link 的主要美国本土制造合作伙伴，在密西西比州科林斯（Corinth）支持 TP-Link 网络产品组合的组装。新闻稿称，Wi-Fi 8 产品预计是新美国产线最早下线的产品之一；在获得美国监管批准的前提下，TP-Link 预计 Wi-Fi 8 家用网络产品线的本土组装最早于明年开始。

Android Authority 10 月 19 日的报道将这笔合作放在 FCC 3 月起实施的外国制造路由器禁令的背景下：TP-Link 尚未获得豁免，并仍在争取临时豁免。报道引述 TP-Link 企业事务官员 Steve Kovsky 接受 PCMag 采访时的话：目前没有公布的指南说明路由器需有多大比例在美国制造，并追问从高通和博通采购的芯片是否算作美国产品。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Parties and place: TP-Link Systems Inc. (Irvine, California) and Key Tronic Corp.; assembly in Corinth, Mississippi.
- Instrument and status: a letter of intent, with the press release conditioning the Wi-Fi 8 assembly start on U.S. regulatory approval and giving "as early as next year" as the timing.
- Quotes in the press release: TP-Link CEO Jeffrey Chao frames the LOI as a commitment to domestic advanced manufacturing; Key Tronic CEO Brett Larsen says Key Tronic's footprint gives TP-Link a domestic foundation and will support TP-Link's Wi-Fi 8 products.
- Android Authority reports Florida, Iowa, Montana and Nebraska filed a joint lawsuit against TP-Link earlier in October, in addition to a separate Texas suit.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is a manufacturing and market-access item rather than a technical one: it is the company's own announcement that its Wi-Fi 8 line is expected to be assembled in the United States, set against the FCC ban on foreign-made routers, from which, per Android Authority, TP-Link has not received an exemption. It is a letter of intent, not a finalised agreement, and the start of assembly is conditional on regulatory approval.
