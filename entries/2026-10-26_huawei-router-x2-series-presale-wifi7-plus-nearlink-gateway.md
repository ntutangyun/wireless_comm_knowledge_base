## Summary (EN)

Huawei opened presale of its Router X2 series on 20 October 2026, according to an IT之家 (IT Home) report republished on ifeng (20 October, 10:43). The series has two lines: the Router X2 (white) at 478 yuan, 888 yuan for a twin pack, and the Router X2 Pro (Earth Brown, Oak White, Stone Grey) at 588 yuan, 1,108 yuan for a twin pack. Huawei Router X2 posts in the 花粉俱乐部 router circle (on the "book-style router" design) were the community echo that surfaced the launch.

The report describes the series as supporting "Wi-Fi 7+", with a 100% speed gain in weak-signal areas and 80% less lag; the same wording appears on Huawei's own product page (https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/routers/x2/). It lists a NearLink gaming dedicated link (星闪电竞专链) and a NearLink/Bluetooth multi-mode gateway, said to interconnect with 3,200+ brands and 400+ device categories, and a Lingxiao 760 series chip tuned with Huawei's own devices. The Pro's magnetic shell can be swapped and supports 3D-printed custom shells.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Presale 20 October 2026; prices 478 / 888 yuan (X2, single / twin) and 588 / 1,108 yuan (X2 Pro, single / twin).
- Chip: Lingxiao 760 series, with a "4-channel signal amplification" design described in the report (the report gives no antenna count).
- Huawei's specification pages (https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/routers/x2/specs/ and https://consumer.huawei.com/cn/routers/x2-pro/specs/, identical figures): Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be) 2x2 on 2.4 GHz and 5 GHz, theoretical 688 + 2,882 = 3,570 Mbps, 256 MB RAM / 128 MB ROM, 4 internal Wi-Fi antennas plus 1 smart antenna and 1 combined Bluetooth and NearLink antenna, one 2.5GE port and three GE ports, 802.11k/v/r roaming support, WPA3-SAE option. The page states figures are lab-theoretical.
- Mesh features per the report: AI placement advice from an uploaded floor plan, a mesh LED showing signal strength, wired and wireless mesh, dual-band roaming.
- Not given in the report: shipping date, the claims' test conditions.
