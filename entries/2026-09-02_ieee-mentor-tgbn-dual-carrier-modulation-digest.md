## Summary (EN)
TGbn (Ultra High Reliability) uploaded one new contribution this run: DCN 11-26-1583r0 "Consideration on Dual Carrier Modulation for ELR for 11bn" (Lei Zhou, New H3C). The title indicates a PHY-layer robustness proposal — dual carrier modulation, a diversity technique that spreads a symbol's information across two separated carriers to improve resilience against deep frequency-selective fades — targeted at 802.11bn's Extended/Enhanced Long Range (ELR) operating mode. The document body is Mentor-gated (docx download restricted to logged-in members), so only the title and authorship are public at this time.
