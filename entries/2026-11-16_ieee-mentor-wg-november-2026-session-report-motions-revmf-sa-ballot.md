## Summary (EN)

Four documents uploaded on 12-13 November 2026 record the outcome of the IEEE 802.11 Working Group's November 2026 plenary session (8-13 November, Bangkok). The WG motions composite ([DCN 11-26-1939r2](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1939&is_year=2026), Stephen McCann, Huawei) lists nine WG motions with results. The session report ([DCN 11-26-1940r3](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1940&is_year=2026), same author) is a digest of the closing reports of all sub-groups. The REVmf report to the LMSC ([DCN 11-26-2050r3](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2050&is_year=2026), Michael Montemurro, Huawei) supports the request for conditional approval to send REVmf D4.0 to SA ballot, and the REVmf closing report ([DCN 11-26-2351r0](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2351&is_year=2026)) summarizes the TG's week.

The motions record: on Wednesday 11 November the WG approved the P802.11bu PAR (document 11-26-978r10, 134 yes / 5 no / 16 abstain) and CSD (11-26-979r11, 144 / 2 / 22) for posting to the LMSC agenda with a request for approval to submit to NesCom; the AIO SG's own votes on the same motions were 48/1/8 and 51/1/7. On Friday 13 November the WG approved establishing an external liaison relationship with the Wireless Broadband Alliance on PQC technology projects (70/1/2) and appointed Mark Hamilton as the WBA external liaison coordinator (71/0/3), approved TGbi's August 2026 minutes, and authorized two ad-hoc meetings: a TGmf mixed-mode ad hoc on 9-11 February (San Jose preferred) for SA-ballot comment resolution, and a TGbn MAC mixed-mode ad hoc on 10-12 March 2027 in Singapore (128 yes / 7 no / 14 abstain within TGbn). The motions document also carries the three matching LMSC motions (4.32, 6.11, 6.12) with their results still blank ("xx") at the time of upload.

For REVmf, the WG approved comment resolutions for the LB295 comments on D3.0, instructed the editor to prepare Draft 4.0 and approved a 15-day WG recirculation ballot asking whether REVmf D4.0 should be forwarded to SA ballot (approved by unanimous consent; TG vote 11/1/0), and approved the report to the LMSC (72/0/4). The report states that REVmf went through three WG letter ballots (LB289 on D1.0, LB294 on D2.0, LB295 on D3.0), that more than 3200 comments on drafts 0.1 to 3.0 have been resolved, that IEEE Std 802.11bh-2024, 802.11be-2024, 802.11bk-2025 and 802.11bf-2025 have been rolled in, and that 802.11bi-2026 is to be rolled in during a recirculation after the initial SA ballot. Its ballot table gives approval of 92.2% (LB289), 94.3% (LB294), 95.1% (LB295) and 98.0% after post-ballot vote changes on LB295 (502 approve / 10 disapprove), and its timeline runs from the D4.0 third recirculation (November-December 2026) through initial SA ballot (December 2026-January 2027) to submission to RevCom in January 2028.

The session report also reproduces the sub-group closing reports. TGbn held 11 sessions (4 MAC/PHY, 7 joint), discussed about 90 comment-resolution documents and technical submissions, and resolved about 800 of the 8,045 LB296 comments; its goals for January 2027 are to continue LB296 resolution and deliver D2.3, and its stated timeline is a D3.0 letter ballot in January 2027, initial SA ballot (D4.0) in May 2027 and final WG approval in March 2028. The AIO SG approved the revised P802.11bu PAR and CSD (incorporating comments from other 802 WGs and NesCom members) and plans to start task-group work in January 2027, subject to LMSC and NesCom approval. The WIN SG reported 21 presentations and progress on its draft PAR and CSD (see the separate WIN draft-PAR entry). The Coex SC summary lists ETSI BRAN's EN 303 687 (6 GHz WAS/RLAN) work, a discussion of 6 GHz LPI client-to-client operation, a DFS evaluation against solid-state weather radars, and a Wi-Fi/Bluetooth cabin coexistence study in which Narrowband Access with LBT improved coexistence. The report's editors' section includes a draft-development snapshot of the amendments (be, bk, bf, bi, mf, bn, bp, bq, br, bt) with their current draft versions.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 11 月 12-13 日上传的四份文档记录了 IEEE 802.11 工作组 2026 年 11 月全会（11 月 8-13 日，曼谷）的结果。WG 动议汇总（[DCN 11-26-1939r2](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1939&is_year=2026)，Stephen McCann，华为）列出九项 WG 动议及表决结果；会议报告（[DCN 11-26-1940r3](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1940&is_year=2026)，同一作者）汇编了各分组的闭幕报告；REVmf 提交 LMSC 的报告（[DCN 11-26-2050r3](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2050&is_year=2026)，Michael Montemurro，华为）支持"有条件批准 REVmf D4.0 进入 SA 投票"的请求；REVmf 闭幕报告（[DCN 11-26-2351r0](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2351&is_year=2026)）概述该 TG 本周工作。

动议记录：11 月 11 日（周三）WG 批准将 P802.11bu 的 PAR（文档 11-26-978r10，赞成 134 / 反对 5 / 弃权 16）和 CSD（11-26-979r11，144 / 2 / 22）提交 IEEE 802 LMSC 议程，请求批准后报送 NesCom；AIO SG 内部对同一动议的表决分别为 48/1/8 和 51/1/7。11 月 13 日（周五）WG 批准与无线宽带联盟（WBA）就 PQC 技术项目建立外部联络关系（70/1/2），并任命 Mark Hamilton 为 WBA 外部联络协调人（71/0/3），批准 TGbi 2026 年 8 月会议纪要，并授权两次 ad-hoc 会议：TGmf 于 2 月 9-11 日召开混合模式 ad-hoc（首选圣何塞）用于 SA 投票意见处理，TGbn 于 2027 年 3 月 10-12 日在新加坡召开 MAC 混合模式 ad-hoc（TGbn 内表决 128 赞成 / 7 反对 / 14 弃权）。动议文档还附有三项对应的 LMSC 动议（4.32、6.11、6.12），上传时结果栏仍为空（"xx"）。

REVmf 方面：WG 批准了对 D3.0 的 LB295 意见处理结果，指示编辑准备 Draft 4.0，并批准一次为期 15 天的 WG 再循环投票，征询"REVmf D4.0 是否应转交 SA 投票"（一致同意通过；TG 内表决 11/1/0），同时批准提交 LMSC 的报告（72/0/4）。报告称 REVmf 已经历三轮 WG Letter Ballot（D1.0 的 LB289、D2.0 的 LB294、D3.0 的 LB295），已处理草案 0.1 至 3.0 的 3200 多条意见，已并入 IEEE Std 802.11bh-2024、802.11be-2024、802.11bk-2025 与 802.11bf-2025，802.11bi-2026 将在首轮 SA 投票之后的再循环中并入。其投票表给出的赞成率为 92.2%（LB289）、94.3%（LB294）、95.1%（LB295），LB295 投票变更后为 98.0%（502 赞成 / 10 反对）；时间线从 D4.0 第三次再循环（2026 年 11-12 月）经首轮 SA 投票（2026 年 12 月至 2027 年 1 月），直至 2028 年 1 月提交 RevCom。

会议报告同时转载了各分组闭幕报告。TGbn 共开 11 个时段（4 个 MAC/PHY、7 个联合），讨论约 90 份意见处理文档与技术提案，处理了 LB296 共 8,045 条意见中的约 800 条；2027 年 1 月目标为继续处理 LB296 意见并交付 D2.3，其时间线为 2027 年 1 月 D3.0 Letter Ballot、2027 年 5 月首轮 SA 投票（D4.0）、2028 年 3 月 WG 最终批准。AIO SG 批准了修订后的 P802.11bu PAR 与 CSD（吸收了其他 802 工作组与 NesCom 成员的意见），计划 2027 年 1 月开始 TG 工作（以 LMSC 与 NesCom 批准为前提）。WIN SG 报告了 21 份报告及其 PAR/CSD 草案进展（详见单独的 WIN PAR 草案条目）。Coex SC 摘要列出 ETSI BRAN 的 EN 303 687（6 GHz WAS/RLAN）工作、6 GHz LPI 客户端到客户端（C2C）运行讨论、固态气象雷达下的 DFS 评估，以及 Wi-Fi/蓝牙机舱共存研究（窄带 LBT 接入改善了共存）。报告的编辑部分还给出各修订（be、bk、bf、bi、mf、bn、bp、bq、br、bt）当前草案版本的快照。
