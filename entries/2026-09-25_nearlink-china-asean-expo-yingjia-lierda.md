## Summary (EN)

The 23rd China-ASEAN Expo was held in Nanning, co-hosted by the trade authorities of China and the ten ASEAN member states together with the ASEAN Secretariat, and organized by the Guangxi Zhuang Autonomous Region government. The International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) exhibited there with member-company products, per an alliance article published 2026-09-18, framing the appearance as showing the ASEAN market SparkLink's commercial deployment results and deepening supply-chain matchmaking and standards-coordination exchanges in wireless short-range communication, smart automotive, and IoT.

The alliance's booth covered automotive, home, industrial, and consumer-electronics applications, highlighting low-latency, high-reliability, strong-anti-interference characteristics. Two member companies are named with specific products: Yingjia Technology (鹰驾科技) showed a SparkLink wireless 360-degree surround-view system that uses SparkLink wireless links in place of in-vehicle wiring harnesses to carry high-definition images from multiple cameras stably, pitched as a lightweight hardware option for upgrading commercial vehicles. Lierda Technology (利尔达科技), described as a core iSLA member, exhibited its EB21, UB37, and FB36 SparkLink module series plus accompanying development solutions, covering home, industrial, automotive, and security-application scenarios; Lierda's senior marketing manager Ren Jun (任俊) gave a keynote on the module product matrix and networking solutions, and shared field-deployment experience in smart-home and industrial-IoT scenarios.

The article gives no technical specifications for the surround-view system (camera count, resolution, latency) or for the three named modules (SLE/SLB mode, chipset, form factor) — it is a trade-show exhibitor recap, not a product datasheet.
