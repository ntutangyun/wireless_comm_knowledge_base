## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-09, TGbq (Integrated Millimeter Wave, IMMW) uploaded five new-DCN documents that extend the IMMW PHY preamble clause block: proposed draft text (PDT) for subclause 42.3.9.1 (IMMW Preamble Introduction, which defines the preamble's composition), 42.3.9.2 (M-STF), 42.3.9.3 (M-LTF) and 42.3.9.7 (IMMW-LTF), alongside a MAC-side PDT on beamformer-initiated early termination of a beam-establishment service period, signalled across the sub-7 GHz link. Separately, the WIN SG (WLAN Intelligent Networking Study Group, the Wi-Fi 9 precursor group) uploaded a document on uplink MU-MIMO precoding, the AI Offload SG uploaded a document on authentication/authorization/accounting (AAA) for AI services carried over the AI Offload mechanism, and the 802.11 Working Group's editors posted a revised plenary-week agenda. None of these eight documents were captured by the 2026-09-09 digest (`2026-09-09_ieee-mentor-tgbq-immw-baseline-wave-digest`), which recorded TGbq's earlier eight-document baseline-drafting wave (DCN 1608-1697): six of today's eight documents (1609-1612, 1630, 1690) have DCN numbers below that digest's 1697 watermark but were first uploaded on 2026-09-09, after the prior run's digest was produced.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **DCN 11-26-1609r0 · TGbq** — "PHY PDT for IMMW Preamble Introduction". Shengquan Hu (MediaTek), uploaded 09-Sep-2026 20:22 ET. Proposed draft text for subclause 42.3.9.1 (IMMW Preamble Introduction), defining the composition of the IMMW preamble — pre-IMMW modulated fields (M-STF, M-LTF, M-SIG, IMMW-SIG) and IMMW modulated fields — and transcribing the TGbq motions passed up to July 2026. It is itself PDT, not merely an introduction to the others. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1609&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1610r0 · TGbq** — "PHY PDT for M-STF". Shengquan Hu (MediaTek), uploaded 09-Sep-2026 20:22 ET. Proposed draft text for subclause 42.3.9.2, the M-STF (mmWave Short Training Field) — the IMMW preamble field used for signal detection and timing/frequency synchronization. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1610&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1611r0 · TGbq** — "PHY PDT for M-LTF". Shengquan Hu (MediaTek), uploaded 09-Sep-2026 20:23 ET. Proposed draft text for subclause 42.3.9.3, the M-LTF (mmWave Long Training Field), used for channel estimation. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1611&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1612r0 · TGbq** — "PHY PDT for IMMW-LTF". Shengquan Hu (MediaTek), uploaded 09-Sep-2026 20:23 ET. Proposed draft text for subclause 42.3.9.7, the IMMW-LTF — the IMMW-modulated long training field, a distinct field from the pre-IMMW-modulated M-LTF of 42.3.9.3. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1612&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1690r0 · TGbq** — "PDT Joint-Beamformer Initiated Early Termination". Kaiying Lu (MediaTek USA), uploaded 09-Sep-2026 19:14 ET. Proposed draft text creating new subclause 41.7.2.3.2 ("Beamformer-initiated early termination") under 41.7.2.3, "Cross link indication of early termination": the IMMW SP initiator transmits a frame that explicitly indicates early termination of the mmWave beam-establishment service period **via the sub-7 GHz link**, and that frame may initiate negotiation of a new service period. The cross-link aspect is the distinctive part. Submitted per TGbq Motion #101 with 29 named co-authors across roughly 18 companies. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1690&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1630r0 · WIN SG** — "Uplink MU-MIMO Precoding for WIN". Rainer Strobel (Maxlinear), uploaded 09-Sep-2026 12:19 ET. A study-group-stage document proposing uplink MU-MIMO precoding for WIN, in the WLAN Intelligent Networking Study Group that this KB previously recorded as the Wi-Fi 9 precursor group (`2026-08-27_ieee-mentor-tgbn-win-sg-digest`). The deck's argument is that uplink MU-MIMO exists since 802.11ax but client transmit antennas are still used inefficiently: the AP triggers NDPs from the STAs, computes precoder coefficients, and feeds them back for the STAs to precode their uplink data. It reports two published methods — SVD-based uplink beamforming at around 4 dB gain, and joint optimisation across all STAs at around 8 dB gain over non-precoded transmission — states the coefficient-computation method is vendor-discretionary, and notes both can reuse the existing beamforming feedback report format. Simulation setting: 160 MHz, 4 STAs × 2 TX antennas, AP with 4 RX antennas, D-NLOS channel, with protocol overhead, channel aging, quantisation and frequency offsets included. The stated motivation is that the next-generation WLAN project targets AI workloads, which need a reliable high-throughput uplink. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1630&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1701r0/r1 · AI Offload SG** — "AAA of AI Service in AI Offload". Tao Chun Lee (MediaTek), r0 uploaded 09-Sep-2026 02:55 ET, r1 09-Sep-2026 03:16 ET. A document treating an edge-hosted AI service as attribute-database access and splitting access control into three separable stages. Authentication reuses current 802.11 authentication, with PQC named as the future-proofing direction. Authorization is decoupled from network authentication: a STA authenticated to the network must still be authorized for a particular AI service, via a protected Authorization Element or Password Identifier carrying proof of knowledge of an out-of-band-configured **AI Model Identifier** — a word or designator used to group devices and apply per-service policy — while a public AI service needs no authorization. Admission is the third, separate outcome: the service provider marks each service's admission according to its own resource availability, so a STA "can be authenticated, authorized, but not admitted due to resource contention on the service provider side", and admission may include relaying to external devices. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1701&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1665r2 · 802.11 WG** — "Editors agenda - 8 September 2026". Edward Au (Huawei), r2 uploaded 09-Sep-2026 10:35 ET (r0/r1 uploaded 08-Sep). Administrative meeting agenda for the working-group editors; no technical content.

- **DCN 11-26-1610r0 · TGbq** — "M-STF PHY PDT"。Shengquan Hu（联发科），2026-09-09 20:22 ET 上传。为子条款 42.3.9.2 的 M-STF（mmWave 短训练字段）提出的拟稿文本，是 IMMW 前导码中用于信号检测与时间/频率同步的字段。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1610&is_year=2026)

- **DCN 11-26-1612r0 · TGbq** — "IMMW-LTF PHY PDT"。Shengquan Hu（联发科），2026-09-09 20:23 ET 上传。为子条款 42.3.9.7 的 IMMW-LTF 提出的拟稿文本——即 IMMW 调制的长训练字段，与 42.3.9.3 中 pre-IMMW 调制的 M-LTF 是两个不同的字段。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1612&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1690r0 · TGbq** — "波束成形方发起的提前终止 PDT"。Kaiying Lu（联发科美国），2026-09-09 19:14 ET 上传。拟稿文本新增子条款 41.7.2.3.2（“波束成形方发起的提前终止”），隶属于 41.7.2.3“提前终止的跨链指示”：IMMW SP 发起方**通过 sub-7 GHz 链路**发送一帧，显式指示毫米波波束建立服务期的提前终止，该帧可发起新服务期的协商。跨链指示是其最具特色之处。依据 TGbq Motion #101 提交，署名作者 29 人，来自约 18 家公司。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1690&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1630r0 · WIN SG** — "面向 WIN 的上行 MU-MIMO 预编码"。Rainer Strobel（Maxlinear），2026-09-09 12:19 ET 上传。一份向 WIN 提出上行 MU-MIMO 预编码的研究组阶段文档，来自 WLAN 智能网络研究组（本知识库此前在 `2026-08-27_ieee-mentor-tgbn-win-sg-digest` 中记录为 Wi-Fi 9 前身研究组）。其论点是：上行 MU-MIMO 自 802.11ax 已引入，但客户端的发射天线仍未得到高效利用；做法是由 AP 触发各 STA 发送 NDP，AP 计算预编码系数并反馈给 STA，用于对上行数据包预编码。文档引用两种已发表方法——基于 SVD 的上行波束成形约 4 dB 增益，面向全部 STA 的联合优化约 8 dB 增益（相对于不预编码传输）——并指出系数计算方法由厂商自定，两者均可复用现有的波束成形反馈报告格式。仿真设置：160 MHz，4 个 STA、每个 2 根发射天线，AP 4 根接收天线，D-NLOS 信道，已计入协议开销、信道老化、量化与频偏。其自述动机是：下一代 WLAN 项目面向 AI 负载，而 AI 负载需要可靠、高吞吐的上行链路。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1630&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1701r0/r1 · AI Offload SG** — "AI Offload 中 AI 服务的 AAA"。Tao Chun Lee（联发科），r0 于 2026-09-09 02:55 ET 上传，r1 于 2026-09-09 03:16 ET 上传。文档将边缘侧托管的 AI 服务建模为属性数据库访问，并将访问控制拆为三个可分离的阶段。认证：复用现有 802.11 认证，并点名 PQC 作为面向未来的方向。授权：与入网认证解耦——已认证入网的 STA 仍需针对具体 AI 服务获得授权，方式是由受保护的授权元素或口令标识符携带对带外配置的 **AI 模型标识符**（用于将设备分组并施加分服务策略的词或标识）的知识证明；公共 AI 服务则无需授权。准入：作为第三个独立结果，服务提供方根据自身资源可用性标记各服务的准入状态，因此一个 STA 可能“已认证、已授权，却因服务提供方侧的资源竞争而未获准入”；准入还可涵盖向外部设备的中继。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1701&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1665r2 · 802.11 WG** — "编辑团队议程 - 2026年9月8日"。Edward Au（华为），r2 于 2026-09-09 10:35 ET 上传（r0/r1 于 09-08 上传）。工作组编辑团队的行政类会议议程，无技术内容。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This continues the TGbq baseline-drafting activity the 2026-09-09 digest first recorded (DCN 1608-1697), and extends the field-level IMMW PHY preamble drafting this KB began recording on that date — the M-SIG PDT at subclause 42.3.9.4 — from the SIG container to the preamble's training fields, at 42.3.9.1, 42.3.9.2, 42.3.9.3 and 42.3.9.7. Read together with the 09-09 wave, the preamble clause block is now largely populated in proposed-draft-text form. Six of today's eight documents (1609-1612, 1630, 1690) illustrate the low-DCN/late-upload blind spot this KB's Mentor tracking has repeatedly flagged since 2026-08-25: their DCN numbers sit below the prior digest's 1697 watermark, but their upload timestamps are all 2026-09-09, after that digest was produced, so a pure DCN-number filter would have silently dropped them. DCN 1630 is the first concrete technical contribution this KB has recorded from the WIN SG — the group's only prior appearance here (`2026-08-27_ieee-mentor-tgbn-win-sg-digest`) recorded its first meeting agenda, explicitly not technical content — while the AI Offload SG document continues a thread this KB has tracked since `2026-05-12_ieee-mentor-ai-offload-sg-antwerp-digest`, `2026-07-11_ieee-mentor-ai-offload-sg-digest` and `2026-07-19_ieee-p802-11bu-draft-par-ai-offload`.
