## Summary (EN)

The International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) published the finalised agenda for the "2026 SparkLink Japan Forum" (2026星闪日本论坛) on September 4, 2026, confirming the date and venue on the alliance's own channel: **September 11, 2026, 14:00–17:30, at Room 201, International Conference Center, Makuhari Messe, Chiba, Japan** (AZAPA's own page lists the forum proper as 14:00–17:00, preceded by a 13:00–13:45 technical-exhibition tour). AZAPA K.K. — whose CEO chairs SparkLink Japan — had already announced the same date and venue on 2026-08-18, under the Japanese organiser's own name for the event, "2026 SparkLink 日本産業フォーラム" (2026 SparkLink Japan Industrial Forum) (https://azapa.co.jp/sparklink20260911/). A sponsorship prospectus for the forum had circulated since July 2026 (iSLA site id 914), but that prospectus's detail body was image-only and did not carry a date; the newly published iSLA agenda image is the primary-source document that confirms the alliance's own detailed programme.

The programme runs 14:00–17:15 across fourteen sessions. It opens (14:00–14:10) with remarks from 近藤康弘 (Kondo Yasuhiro), chairman of SparkLink Japan and CEO of AZAPA K.K., followed (14:10–14:25) by a progress-and-planning update from iSLA Secretary-General 曾国松, and (14:25–14:40) a parallel update from SparkLink Japan director 齋藤昇三 (also an advisor at the Japan Electronic Devices Industry Association). Three formal ceremonies follow in sequence: a cooperation-agreement signing between iSLA and SparkLink Japan (14:40–14:50), a separate MOU signing (14:50–15:00), and a "SparkLink Japan developer partner" release ceremony (15:00–15:15) — the forum therefore functions as the venue for a formal institutional agreement between iSLA and its Japan chapter, not merely a marketing event.

The technical/business sessions that follow span several verticals: AZAPA R&D Manager Suwisuth Ungphaiboon (15:15–15:30) on economic/manufacturing-competitiveness framing for SparkLink in Japanese manufacturing; Shinshu University associate professor 单麟 (15:30–15:45), principal investigator of a MIC (総務省) research programme on sustainable radio-spectrum utilisation, presenting on ICT+AI for resilient IoT systems; two governance-focused talks — cybersecurity assessment/compliance from DCJ K.K. CEO 島貫純 (15:45–16:00), and "SDV-era" governance strategy from PwC Consulting's 落合勇太 (16:00–16:15); an automotive session from **FICOSA** (16:15–16:30) — presented by its CEO for Asia and Asia-Pacific and Japan GM, Ignasi Claret Carlos Ramos — on SparkLink applications in vision, in-vehicle infotainment and ADAS; an industrial-IoT talk from Jiangsu Runhe Software (江苏润和软件) Vice President 刘洋 (16:30–16:45), a presenter at earlier SparkLink automotive forums; a healthcare talk pairing SparkLink with e-paper technology from Guodong Qinda (Shanghai) General Manager 王萌 (16:45–17:00); and a closing talk (17:00–17:15) from Shenzhen Xingliàn Innovation Technology Product & Solutions Manager 梁家炜 on SparkLink for low-latency, multi-connection industrial field wireless communication.

## Summary (ZH)

国际星闪无线短距通信联盟（iSLA）于 2026 年 9 月 4 日公布了"2026 星闪日本论坛"的最终议程，在联盟自身渠道上确认了日期与地点：**2026 年 9 月 11 日 14:00–17:30，日本千叶幕张展览馆国际会议中心 201 会议室**（AZAPA 官方页面将论坛正式部分列为 14:00–17:00，此前另安排 13:00–13:45 技术展参观环节）。星闪日本理事长所在企业 AZAPA 株式会社已于 2026-08-18 提前公布了相同的日期与地点，并使用了日方主办单位自己对该活动的命名——"2026 SparkLink 日本産業フォーラム"（2026 星闪日本产业论坛）（https://azapa.co.jp/sparklink20260911/）。该论坛的赞助方案早在 2026 年 7 月就已发布（iSLA 站内 id 914），但该方案详情页仅为图片、未标注日期；本次新发布的 iSLA 议程图片确认了联盟自身的详细日程安排。

议程共 14 场安排，运行时段为 14:00–17:15。开场（14:00–14:10）为星闪日本理事长、AZAPA 株式会社 CEO 近藤康弘的致辞，随后（14:10–14:25）由国际星闪联盟秘书长曾国松介绍联盟整体进展和规划，（14:25–14:40）星闪日本理事（兼一般社团法人日本电子器件产业协会理事·顾问）齋藤昇三介绍星闪日本方面的进展和规划。紧接着安排了三项正式仪式：国际星闪联盟与星闪日本的合作协议签约仪式（14:40–14:50）、另一场 MOU 签约仪式（14:50–15:00），以及"星闪日本开发者伙伴"发布仪式（15:00–15:15）——这场论坛因此不仅是一次营销活动，更是 iSLA 与其日本分支机构正式建立机构层面合作关系的场合。

后续的技术/产业议题覆盖多个垂直领域：AZAPA 研发经理 UNGPHAIBOON Suwisuth（15:15–15:30）围绕星闪如何以高效且具韧性的经济方案重塑日本制造业竞争力展开；信州大学学术研究院（综合人类科学）准教授单麟（15:30–15:45，兼总务省"面向可持续有效利用无线电波的基础技术研发项目"研究负责人）介绍 ICT 与 AI 赋能的韧性物联网系统研究；随后是两场治理类议题——DCJ 股份有限公司 CEO 岛贯纯（15:45–16:00）讲解网络安全评估与合规措施，普华永道咨询有限公司落合勇太（16:00–16:15）讲解"SDV 时代"的治理战略；**FICOSA**（16:15–16:30，由其亚洲及亚太区首席执行官兼日本区总经理 Ignasi Claret Carlos Ramos 主讲）介绍星闪在视觉、车载信息娱乐与高级驾驶辅助系统（ADAS）领域的应用；江苏润和软件股份有限公司副总裁刘洋（16:30–16:45，此前已多次在星闪汽车论坛上亮相）介绍星闪赋能工业场景落地；国东勤达（上海）智能科技有限公司总经理王萌（16:45–17:00）讲解物联网星闪 + 电子纸技术在医疗行业的应用；深圳星链创新技术有限公司产品与解决方案经理梁家炜作压轴报告（17:00–17:15），介绍星闪技术在工业现场无线通信中低时延和多连接场景的应用与实践。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This closes a multi-week watch item in this KB's own tracking: the 2026 Japan forum had been flagged since 2026-08-23 (and repeatedly re-checked through 08-27, 08-31 and 09-04) as having a sponsorship prospectus but no confirmed agenda inside this KB's own source coverage — a gap in this KB's source coverage, not a disclosure gap: AZAPA K.K., the SparkLink Japan chair company, had already published the same date, time and venue on its own site on 2026-08-18, a page this KB's source list did not surface until this run. A prior run even traced and discarded a false "8月27日" claim that turned out to describe the 2025 Nagoya forum, not this one. The iSLA agenda now published confirms that date on the alliance's own channel and, more importantly, confirms the institutional agenda (a formal iSLA–SparkLink Japan cooperation agreement and MOU, not just a marketing session) — representing a documented step toward a formal Japan-chapter governance structure, distinct from the informal member relationships already covered by this KB's Jan-2026 iSLA certification-scheme entry. FICOSA's participation is also notable in the context of the "NearLink outside China" watch item this bin has carried since the 2026-08-27 GAC-Toyota bZ7 entry (covering, per that entry, a joint-venture-brand SparkLink car key): FICOSA is a Tier-1, Spain-headquartered automotive supplier with no prior SparkLink presence in this KB, presenting specifically on ADAS/infotainment applications — a second concrete signal (after the 2026-09-03 Tier-1 landscape entry) of SparkLink reaching non-Chinese, non-Huawei automotive supply chains. As the event has not yet occurred (Sept 11 is four days after this entry's ingest date), the actual outcomes of the signing ceremonies and FICOSA's technical content remain a follow-up watch item for the post-forum run.
