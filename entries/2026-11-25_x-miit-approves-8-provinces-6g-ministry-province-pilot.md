## Summary (EN)

On 2026-11-17 the General Office of China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT) published a reply letter, 工信厅通信函〔2026〕558号, approving eight provinces and municipalities as pilots for the "6G innovation development ministry–province collaborative pilot": Beijing, Hebei, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui, Hubei and Guangdong. The letter replies to the provinces' submitted "6G Innovation Development Ministry–Province Collaborative Pilot Work Plans" and says the plans were reviewed by experts.

Per the letter, the pilot provinces are to revise their plans in line with the reviewers' comments and concentrate on three areas: cultivating 6G application industries, strengthening foundational industries and exploring new product routes, taking 6G international standards and industrial R&D practice into account. The letter attaches a management measure for the pilot special action ("6G创新发展部省协同试点专项行动管理办法").

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 11 月 17 日，工业和信息化部办公厅发布复函（工信厅通信函〔2026〕558号），同意北京市、河北省、上海市、江苏省、浙江省、安徽省、湖北省、广东省作为 6G 创新发展部省协同试点省（市）。该函是对各省（市）报送的《6G创新发展部省协同试点工作方案》的答复，并称方案经专家研究论证。

函件要求试点省（市）按评审专家和司局意见进一步修改完善工作方案，结合 6G 国际标准和产业研发实际，突出地方产业优势，重点在培育 6G 应用产业、夯实基础产业、探索新型产品路线三个方面加强攻关；函件附有《6G创新发展部省协同试点专项行动管理办法》。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Primary source (MIIT, 信息通信发展司 document release, dated and published 2026-11-17): https://www.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/txs/wjfb/art/2026/art_ab6fba9058b848d0a1fa3cd7d3e91e2c.html
- Approved pilot provinces and municipalities: Beijing, Hebei, Shanghai, Jiangsu, Zhejiang, Anhui, Hubei, Guangdong.
- Commercial use: where 6G projects involve commercial applications, the enterprises concerned must obtain the relevant telecom service licences according to regulations; commercial application scenarios may not be explored before a licence is obtained.
- Radio rules: projects that use radio frequencies, set up or use radio stations, or develop, produce, import, sell or repair radio transmitting equipment must comply with national radio-management rules and obtain the required spectrum licences, station licences and equipment type approvals.
- Publicity: provinces are told to regulate 6G-related publicity, distinguishing internal from external audiences and reviewing reports strictly to keep them objective and accurate, and, for news touching national-level or whole-industry 6G development, to consult the competent department first.
- Oversight: MIIT will, under the attached management measure, periodically evaluate the pilots and strengthen organisational support in technology trials, spectrum assurance, project layout, application of results and ecosystem cultivation.
- Pilot duration: the approval letter does not state a period; MIIT's June 2026 notice launching the pilot action (工信厅通信函〔2026〕255号) says approved plans are implemented for, in principle, three years.
- Related: the 2026 6G Development Conference programme (KB entry 2026-11-24) states that a ministry–province pilot results-release ceremony would be held at the conference; that entry does not cover this approval letter.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- 原文来源（工信部信息通信发展司文件发布，成文及发布日期 2026-11-17）：https://www.miit.gov.cn/jgsj/txs/wjfb/art/2026/art_ab6fba9058b848d0a1fa3cd7d3e91e2c.html
- 获批试点省（市）：北京、河北、上海、江苏、浙江、安徽、湖北、广东。
- 商业应用：6G 项目如涉及商业应用，相关企业应依规取得电信业务经营许可；未获许可前不得开展商业化应用场景探索。
- 无线电管理：涉及使用无线电频率、设置使用无线电台（站），研制、生产、进口、销售和维修无线电发射设备的，须遵守国家无线电管理规定，依法取得频率许可证、台站执照和发射设备型号核准证。
- 宣传要求：规范 6G 新闻宣传，报道本地区 6G 工作时坚持内外有别、严格审查，确保客观准确；涉及国家层面或产业整体 6G 发展的新闻，应按程序征求业务主管部门意见。
- 组织保障：工信部将按附件管理办法定期开展试点评价，并在技术试验、频率保障、项目布局、成果应用、生态培育等方面强化组织保障。
- 试点周期：本批复函未载明试点期限；工信部 2026 年 6 月启动该专项行动的通知（工信厅通信函〔2026〕255号）称，批复方案的实施期限原则上为 3 年。
- 相关：2026 年 6G 发展大会议程条目（KB 2026-11-24）称大会将举行部省协同试点成果发布仪式；该条目未涉及本批复函。
