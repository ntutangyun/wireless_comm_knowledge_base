## Summary (EN)
Bloomberg reported 20 August 2026 (corroborated by TradingView/Stocktwits and a follow-up search sweep) that **both SpaceX and AST SpaceMobile** have emerged as bidders for the 800 MHz spectrum portfolio Grain Management LLC acquired from T-Mobile in the ~$2.9B transaction completed the week of 11–14 August 2026 (the same portfolio the KB's 2026-08-20 entry covered as the source of AST's 30-day FCC test authority). The FCC's approval of the Grain–T-Mobile swap came with a condition: Grain must run a **competitive solicitation** for the licenses, which the FCC wants completed by **November 5, 2026**. Grain has asked interested parties for preliminary offers by the **first week of September**, and is reportedly seeking roughly **$6 billion** for the portfolio — though a lease rather than an outright sale remains possible. The licenses span **817–824 MHz (uplink) / 862–869 MHz (downlink)** across **3,224 U.S. counties**, which the FCC itself described as "a valuable source of paired, broadband-ready, low-band spectrum" that had been underutilized. A separate report cites a complete-direct-to-device-applications deadline around December 4, 2026 following the solicitation.

This turns what the 08-20 entry framed as a probable AST-Grain bilateral arrangement into an open, FCC-timed competitive process with SpaceX as a direct rival bidder for the same nationwide low-band spectrum AST is currently using for its 30-day handset test (through ~12 September, per the prior entry). For SpaceX, winning would let Starlink pair 800 MHz with its existing PCS holdings and pending mid-band (EchoStar) acquisitions to extend Direct-to-Cell voice and rural coverage; for AST, winning would convert an in-progress test authorization into a nationwide, non-carrier-brokered low-band position — distinct from its current per-operator arrangements with AT&T, Verizon and FirstNet. Neither company has issued a public statement per the available coverage; the story rests on unnamed-source reporting.
