## Summary (EN)

SatNews (5 October 2026) reports that Canadian carrier TELUS and AST SpaceMobile completed their initial end-to-end network integration test, linking TELUS's terrestrial wireless core with AST's LEO constellation and validating direct satellite-to-cellular connectivity on standard, unmodified smartphones on the TELUS network. The article says TELUS is a strategic equity investor in AST SpaceMobile, alongside AT&T, Verizon, Vodafone, Rakuten and Bell Canada.

Technical details given: payloads designed to 3GPP NTN standards; the custom AST5000 ASIC offering up to 10,000 MHz of processing bandwidth per spacecraft for RF digital beamforming, Doppler compensation and dynamic spectrum allocation; use of partner operators' terrestrial spectrum including 850 MHz low-band and mid-band; steerable spot beams with peak speeds up to 120 Mbps per cell; and a link margin the article says supports reception through moderate obstacles ("one wall in").

Commercial availability is targeted within the next 12 months, starting with text, voice and 911 routing in unserved regional areas, with broadband data and video to follow.
