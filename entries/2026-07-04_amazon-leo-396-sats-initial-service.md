## Summary (EN)

Amazon's Leo constellation (formerly Project Kuiper) crossed its initial-service threshold this week: the July 2 launch of 29 satellites on ULA's final Atlas V mission brought the fleet to 396 satellites in low Earth orbit, and Amazon says that is enough to begin "initial service" of its broadband network later in 2026. Initial commercial coverage will be a limited zone concentrated at mid-northern and mid-southern latitudes, expanding as more satellites launch — Amazon reports "hundreds of flight-ready satellites standing by" and a new vertical-integration facility feeding continued deployments.

The regulatory picture is the notable subplot. Amazon's FCC license required 50% of the 3,232-satellite licensed constellation (1,616 units) in orbit by July 30, 2026 — a deadline it has clearly missed at 396. The company sought an extension in January and received it in June 2026, though reportedly "with strings attached." The eventual system is planned at 7,700+ satellites across filings.

On the retail side, customers will choose between three antenna options spanning 100 Mbps to 1 Gbps download tiers. Strategically, the piece confirms Amazon's two-front play: fixed broadband against Starlink (~10,400 satellites today), and direct-to-device via the $11.5B+ Globalstar acquisition (April 2026, 24 satellites) — the infrastructure that currently underpins Apple's iPhone satellite features, with Leo slated to power Apple's iPhone satellite services from 2028.

## Summary (ZH)

亚马逊 Leo 星座（前身为 Kuiper 计划）本周跨过了初始服务门槛：7 月 2 日 ULA 最后一次 Atlas V 任务发射 29 颗卫星，使在轨总数达到 396 颗，亚马逊表示这已足够在 2026 年晚些时候启动宽带网络的"初始服务"。初期商业覆盖将限于中北纬和中南纬的有限区域，随后随发射扩展 — 亚马逊称有"数百颗备飞卫星待命"，并有新的垂直整合工厂支撑持续部署。

监管是值得注意的支线：亚马逊的 FCC 许可要求在 2026 年 7 月 30 日前将获许可的 3232 颗星座的 50%（1616 颗）送入轨道 — 以 396 颗的现状显然未达标。公司于 1 月申请延期，并于 2026 年 6 月获批，但据报道"附带条件"。最终系统按各项申报计划超过 7700 颗。

零售侧，用户可在三种天线选项中选择，下行速率档位从 100 Mbps 到 1 Gbps。战略上，文章确认了亚马逊的双线布局：固定宽带对抗 Starlink（目前约 10400 颗），以及通过 115 亿美元以上收购 Globalstar（2026 年 4 月，24 颗卫星）切入手机直连 — 该基础设施目前支撑苹果 iPhone 卫星功能，Leo 计划自 2028 年起为苹果 iPhone 卫星服务提供能力。

## Key technical points (EN)

- 396 satellites in LEO after the July 2 launch (29 satellites, final Atlas V flight); initial service planned for later 2026 in mid-latitude zones.
- FCC milestone missed (1,616 required by July 30, 2026); extension granted June 2026 with conditions; licensed constellation 3,232, planned system 7,700+.
- Three customer antenna tiers: 100 Mbps up to 1 Gbps download.
- Globalstar acquired for $11.5B+ (April 2026) — direct-to-device foothold and the current backbone of Apple's iPhone satellite services; Leo to power Apple satellite features from 2028.
- Competitive context: Starlink at ~10,400 satellites and moving into retail mobile (see 2026-07-03_starlink-mobile-retail-carrier-plans).
