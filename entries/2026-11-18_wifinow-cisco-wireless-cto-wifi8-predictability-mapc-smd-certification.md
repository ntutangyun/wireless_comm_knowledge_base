## Summary (EN)
In an interview published by Wi-Fi NOW on 8 November 2026 (Claus Hetting interviewing Matt MacPherson, Cisco Wireless CTO), Wi-Fi 8 is positioned as targeting predictability and reliability rather than peak speed: "We're moving into a domain where networks have to become more predictable." It builds on Wi-Fi 7's Extremely High Throughput and makes Ultra High Reliability the central goal, while the maximum channel width stays at 320 MHz and the highest modulation at 4096-QAM.

The interview describes the mechanisms in terms of what they do for large networks. Multi-AP Coordination (MAPC) standardises coordination among neighbouring APs for shared medium access and can reduce avoidable contention and interference in dense deployments, depending on which mechanisms are implemented. The Seamless Mobility Domain (SMD) prepares target links before a client transitions and shares association and security context in advance; a common logical mobility domain spans multiple AP multi-link devices, coordinated by an SMD Management Entity, without needing a dedicated physical AP-to-AP link, aiming at less interruption and packet loss while preserving multi-link benefits during movement. Enhanced Long Range (ELR), Distributed Resource Units (DRU) and Non-Primary Channel Access (NPCA) are described as improving link robustness and spectrum access, including in single-AP settings.

On deployment and certification, the interview names large managed environments with hundreds or thousands of APs, and service-provider multi-dwelling residential networks, as the likeliest beneficiaries. It also says agentic AI adds machine-to-machine traffic (between devices and campus servers and toward cloud services, often at machine speed), with robotics as the example, and cites a Cisco survey in which half of respondents see campus Wi-Fi among the areas with the largest AI-driven growth in performance and capacity demand. MacPherson notes that many advanced 802.11 features are optional or conditionally mandatory and Wi-Fi Alliance certification does not require all of them, so products of the same generation may differ; Wi-Fi 8 certification requirements are still being developed, and he and other industry leaders want Wi-Fi Alliance enterprise or carrier-class certification profiles to set a stronger interoperability baseline. The article does not discuss Cisco product plans or timelines.

## Summary (ZH)
Wi-Fi NOW 于 2026 年 11 月 8 日发布的访谈（Claus Hetting 访谈 Cisco 无线 CTO Matt MacPherson）把 Wi-Fi 8 定位为追求可预测性与可靠性而非峰值速率：“我们正在进入一个网络必须更可预测的领域。”它建立在 Wi-Fi 7 极高吞吐量的基础上，以超高可靠性（UHR）为核心目标，同时最大信道宽度仍为 320 MHz，最高调制仍为 4096-QAM。

访谈从“对大型网络有什么作用”的角度介绍各机制。多 AP 协调（MAPC）把相邻 AP 之间对共享介质接入的协调标准化，视所实现的机制而定，可在密集部署中减少可避免的竞争与干扰。无缝移动域（SMD）在客户端切换前准备目标链路并提前共享关联与安全上下文；一个共同的逻辑移动域跨越多个 AP 多链路设备，由 SMD 管理实体协调，无需专用的 AP 间物理链路，目标是减少中断与丢包并在移动期间保留多链路收益。增强长距离（ELR）、分布式资源单元（DRU）与非主信道接入（NPCA）被描述为提升链路鲁棒性与频谱接入，包括单 AP 场景。

在部署与认证方面，访谈指出拥有成百上千个 AP 的大型托管环境以及运营商运营的多住户住宅网络最可能受益。访谈还提到智能体 AI 带来机器到机器流量（设备与园区服务器之间、以及通往云服务，往往以机器速度），以机器人为例，并引用 Cisco 调查：一半受访组织认为园区 Wi-Fi 是 AI 驱动性能与容量需求增长最大的领域之一。MacPherson 指出，许多高级 802.11 功能是可选或有条件强制的，Wi-Fi 联盟认证并不要求全部实现，因此同一代产品之间可能有差异；Wi-Fi 8 的认证要求仍在制定中，他与其他业界领导者希望 Wi-Fi 联盟推出企业级或运营商级认证配置文件，以建立更强的互操作基线。文章未涉及 Cisco 的产品计划或时间表。

## Key technical points (EN)
- Positioning: Wi-Fi 8 = Ultra High Reliability on top of Wi-Fi 7 EHT; 320 MHz and 4096-QAM maxima unchanged.
- MAPC: standardised neighbouring-AP coordination for shared medium access; benefit depends on which mechanisms are implemented.
- SMD: pre-transition link preparation and advance sharing of association/security context; logical mobility domain across AP MLDs under an SMD Management Entity, no dedicated physical AP-to-AP link needed.
- ELR, DRU, NPCA: robustness and spectrum-access proposals that also apply with a single AP.
- Certification: advanced 802.11 features are optional or conditionally mandatory; Wi-Fi 8 requirements still under development; enterprise / carrier-class certification profiles are requested.
- Stated drivers: agentic AI machine-to-machine traffic and robotics; Cisco survey in which half of organisations see campus Wi-Fi among the largest AI-driven demand-growth areas.
