## Summary (EN)

DCN 11-26-2096r0 (Jerome Henry, Cisco; uploaded 28 October 2026) is the full set of minutes of the IEEE 802.11 Working Group for the September 2026 interim session (#219, 13-18 September, Waikoloa, Hawaii). It is the formal record behind several items the KB already carries as live session digests, and it adds the numbers. The Chair's opening report lists three ballots that closed in late August / early September. P802.11REVmf LB295 on D3.0 closed 31 August with 487 approve / 25 disapprove / 16 abstain (95.1% approval, 528 ballots returned, 774 comments). P802.11bn LB296 on D2.0 closed 2 September with 417 / 128 / 15 (76.5% approval, 560 ballots returned, 8,045 comments). P802.11bt LB297 on D1.0 closed 3 September with 385 / 46 / 95 (89.3% approval, 526 returned, 651 comments). All three passed. Voter membership was reported as 704 voters (described in the minutes as an all-time record), with 106 aspirants, 31 potential voters and 11 ex-officio voters; the session had 602 participants (314 in person, 288 remote).

The task-group reports in the closing plenary give the session outcomes. TGbn resolved roughly 1,850 of the 8,045 LB296 comments in ten meeting slots, approved generation of D2.1, approved making P802.11bn D2.0 available for purchase from the IEEE Store, and approved a three-day mixed-mode MAC ad hoc in San Diego on 6-8 January 2027; its stated timeline is D2.2 in November, a D3.0 letter ballot in January 2027 and initial SA ballot in May 2027. TGbp approved 30 further PDT proposals and instructed the Technical Editor to prepare P802.11bp D1.0 (including CAD 11-26/1582r3) for a 45-day WG ballot asking whether D1.0 should be forwarded to SA ballot (recorded as 24/0/3 on the motions slide). TGbt addressed 232 CIDs of about 650 LB297 comments, ran six motions, approved making D1.0 available for sale and an IRTF liaison, and began D1.1; the minutes note that its earlier expectation of D2.0 from this session was too optimistic and the timeline was readjusted. TGmf resolved about 380 LB295 comments and approved the Mandatory Draft Review report on D3.0, targeting conditional LMSC approval for initial SA ballot in November. TGbi was waiting on RevCom (scheduled for 22 September) after an unchanged recirculation drew no comments. TGbq elected a secretary and asked the editor to prepare for draft 1.1; TGbr considered comments on D0.1 with D0.2 expected by 10 October.

The study groups: the WIN SG (WLAN Intelligent Networking, created in July) heard 27 technical presentations, confirmed Ross Jian Yu as secretary and targets PAR/CSD approval in January 2027; the AIO SG heard ten contributions on use cases, scope and security/privacy and will respond to LMSC comments on the P802.11bu (AI Offload) PAR in November; the UCM TIG collected further text contributions (Sionna ray-tracing accuracy, optical channel models) and reviewed revision 5 of the TIG report. The Coexistence SC reviewed simulations of non-LBT 6G uplink in the upper 6 GHz band overlapping 802.11be, with goodput reductions of up to 78 percent in four geometries, and a 5 GHz usage discussion concluding that narrowband devices relying only on AFH present a significant risk to Wi-Fi deployments.

Other items recorded in the minutes: an IETF TSVWG liaison announcing RFC 9956 (the NQB PHB), which updates RFC 8325 guidance on mapping Diffserv to 802.11; an 802.24 liaison presenting a proposed IEEE 802 management-services model based on YANG, with the WG Chair remarking that the 802.11 MIB is ageing and that members should get educated on YANG (an 802.24 tutorial is planned for November); the status that IEEE Std 802.11-2024 remains stalled in the ISO/IEC JTC1 PSDO process because of IPR concerns (two 60-day pre-ballots cancelled); and logistics, namely the November session on 8-13 November 2026 in Bangkok (mixed mode) and proposed 2027 interim fees of US$800 early bird / US$1,000 standard / US$1,200 late.

## Summary (ZH)

DCN 11-26-2096r0（Jerome Henry，Cisco；2026 年 10 月 28 日上传）是 IEEE 802.11 工作组 2026 年 9 月临时会议（第 219 次，9 月 13-18 日，夏威夷 Waikoloa）的完整会议纪要。本知识库此前已通过当日摘要收录了其中若干内容，这份正式纪要补上了具体数字。主席开场报告列出三次在 8 月底至 9 月初截止的投票：P802.11REVmf LB295（D3.0）于 8 月 31 日截止，487 赞成 / 25 反对 / 16 弃权（赞成率 95.1%，回收 528 张选票，774 条评论）；P802.11bn LB296（D2.0）于 9 月 2 日截止，417 / 128 / 15（赞成率 76.5%，回收 560 张，8,045 条评论）；P802.11bt LB297（D1.0）于 9 月 3 日截止，385 / 46 / 95（赞成率 89.3%，回收 526 张，651 条评论）。三者均通过。投票成员数为 704（纪要称为历史新高），另有 106 名申请者、31 名潜在投票者和 11 名当然投票者；本次会议共 602 人参加（314 现场、288 远程）。

闭幕全会上的各任务组报告给出了会议成果。TGbn 在 10 个会议时段内解决了 LB296 的 8,045 条评论中的约 1,850 条，批准生成 D2.1，批准将 P802.11bn D2.0 在 IEEE Store 上开放购买，并批准 2027 年 1 月 6-8 日在圣地亚哥举行为期三天的混合模式 MAC ad hoc；其时间表为 11 月出 D2.2、2027 年 1 月 D3.0 工作组投票、2027 年 5 月初始 SA 投票。TGbp 批准了另外 30 项 PDT 提案，并指示技术编辑准备 P802.11bp D1.0（含 CAD 11-26/1582r3），进行 45 天的工作组投票，询问 D1.0 是否应转入 SA 投票（动议页记录为 24/0/3）。TGbt 在约 650 条 LB297 评论中处理了 232 个 CID，进行了 6 项动议，批准 D1.0 开放购买及一项 IRTF 联络，并开始 D1.1；纪要指出此前预期本次会议产出 D2.0 过于乐观，时间表已据此调整。TGmf 解决了约 380 条 LB295 评论并批准了针对 D3.0 的强制性草案审查报告，目标是 11 月获得 LMSC 有条件批准以进入初始 SA 投票。TGbi 在未做修改的再次征求投票未收到评论后，等待 9 月 22 日的 RevCom 审议。TGbq 选出秘书并要求编辑准备 D1.1；TGbr 审议了针对 D0.1 的评论，预计 10 月 10 日前提供 D0.2。

研究组方面：WIN SG（WLAN 智能网络，7 月成立）听取了 27 个技术报告，确认 Ross Jian Yu 为秘书，目标在 2027 年 1 月批准 PAR/CSD；AIO SG 听取了 10 份关于用例、范围和安全/隐私的提案，11 月将回应 LMSC 对 P802.11bu（AI 卸载）PAR 的意见；UCM TIG 收集了更多文本贡献（Sionna 射线追踪精度、光信道模型），并审阅了 TIG 报告第 5 版。共存 SC 审议了上 6 GHz 频段非 LBT 的 6G 上行与 802.11be 重叠部署的仿真，四种部署几何下 Wi-Fi 吞吐最多下降 78%；5 GHz 使用情况讨论的结论是，仅依赖 AFH 的窄带设备部署在 5 GHz 对现有 Wi-Fi 构成显著风险。

纪要中记录的其他事项：IETF TSVWG 联络函，宣布 RFC 9956（NQB PHB）发布，更新了 RFC 8325 中 Diffserv 到 802.11 的映射指南；802.24 联络报告，提出基于 YANG 的 IEEE 802 统一管理服务模型，WG 主席表示 802.11 的 MIB 已显陈旧，建议成员学习 YANG（11 月将有 802.24 教程）；IEEE Std 802.11-2024 因 IPR 问题仍停滞在 ISO/IEC JTC1 PSDO 流程中（两次 60 天预投票被取消）；以及后勤信息——11 月会议为 2026 年 11 月 8-13 日曼谷（混合模式），2027 年临时会议拟定费用为早鸟 800 美元 / 标准 1,000 美元 / 后期 1,200 美元。
