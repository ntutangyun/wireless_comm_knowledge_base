## Summary (EN)

TP-Link's Omada business networking division announced, on 2 September 2026 at CEDIA Expo 2026, an expansion of its Fusion Gateway product family with a new "Fusion Pro Series" that integrates Omada's Guard video-management system (VMS) directly with its Omada Network SDN controller, so both a site's Wi-Fi/switching infrastructure and its video-surveillance infrastructure can be managed from a single platform. The Fusion Gateway lineup now comprises Fusion, Fusion Pro and Fusion Max Series models; Fusion Pro is expected to become available in October 2026. Omada also gave a first look at its Wi-Fi 8 platform at the booth; the release gives no details. TP-Link frames the announcement citing growing demand for "higher performance connectivity, simpler deployment, and more intelligent security" among integrators serving the smart-home and light-commercial market that CEDIA Expo targets.

Beyond the platform announcement, Omada showcased its broader portfolio. On the networking side: Wi-Fi 7 access points, 2.5G/10G switches, and a family of wireless bridges (Sector Bridge, Flex Bridge, and a "Beam Bridge 7 Kit") for point-to-point or point-to-multipoint backhaul links. On the surveillance side: PTZ, dual-lens 180° panoramic, 4G and full-color cameras, and network video recorders. A third, Tapo-branded "smart security" tier adds video doorbells, smart intercoms, and smart locks with biometric (palm-vein and fingerprint) authentication. The press release does not disclose radio-level specifications (band/stream counts, MLO support, throughput) for the Wi-Fi 7 access points or the wireless bridges.
