## Summary (EN)
Eutelsat Group placed a bundle of firm manufacturing contracts on 2026-09-10 that converts the IRIS² and OneWeb-replenishment budget lines from the 2026-08-07 execution-phase announcement (already in this KB as 2026-08-28_eutelsat-iris2-execution-phase-330-satellites) into named contractors, signed values, and launch dates. Aerospacelab was selected as prime contractor for 264 of the 330-satellite IRIS² LEO segment — the dual Mil-Ka/Ku-band platforms — under a EUR 2.4 billion (~USD 2.8 billion) contract, the first time this KB's IRIS² coverage names the LEO bus builder. Airbus Defence and Space received an initial contract for at least 66 Ka-band IRIS² satellites (military Ka for European users; delivery targeted for 2029, built in Toulouse) and separately received authorization to proceed on 229 additional OneWeb refresh (service-continuity) satellites for roughly EUR 1 billion (~USD 1.16 billion) — the same 229-satellite / ~EUR 1B figure the 08-28 entry had only as a forward budget commitment inside the IRIS² press release. Those 229 satellites now have a manufacturing home (Toulouse) and a launch plan: two dedicated Ariane 64 launches secured for the OneWeb deployment, in 2027 and 2028. Separately, Thales Alenia Space secured an order worth around EUR 500 million (~USD 581 million) to build the payloads for all 330 IRIS² LEO satellites — 330 of the programme's 348 total, the remaining 18 MEO satellites going to SES/OHB — spanning both the Aerospacelab and Airbus platform batches — the first payload-level contract this KB has recorded for the programme.

2026 年 9 月 10 日，Eutelsat Group 签署了一批正式制造合同，将 2026 年 8 月 7 日执行阶段公告（已收录于知识库 2026-08-28_eutelsat-iris2-execution-phase-330-satellites 条目）中的 IRIS² 与 OneWeb 补网预算条目，落实为具体承包商、签约金额与发射计划。Aerospacelab 被选定为 IRIS² 330 颗低轨卫星星座中 264 颗（Mil-Ka/Ku 双频平台）的总承包商，合同金额为 24 亿欧元（约合 28 亿美元）——这是本知识库 IRIS² 相关报道中首次出现低轨平台制造商的名称。Airbus Defence and Space 另获至少 66 颗 Ka 频段 IRIS² 卫星的初始合同（面向欧洲用户的军用 Ka；计划 2029 年交付，图卢兹制造），并单独获得授权推进 229 颗 OneWeb 补网（服务连续性）卫星的建造，金额约 10 亿欧元（约合 11.6 亿美元）——这正是 8-28 条目中仅作为 IRIS² 新闻稿内预算承诺出现的"229 颗卫星、约 10 亿欧元"数字。这 229 颗卫星如今有了确定的制造地点（图卢兹）与发射计划：已为 OneWeb 部署预订两次专属 Ariane 64 发射，分别安排在 2027 年与 2028 年。另外，Thales Alenia Space 获得约 5 亿欧元（约合 5.81 亿美元）订单，为全部 330 颗 IRIS² 卫星（涵盖 Aerospacelab 与 Airbus 两批平台）制造载荷——这是本知识库为该计划记录的首份载荷级合同。

## Key technical points (EN)
- **Aerospacelab (NEW prime contractor):** 264 dual Mil-Ka/Ku-band IRIS² LEO platforms, EUR 2.4B (~USD 2.8B) — the first time this KB's IRIS² coverage names the bus builder for the majority of the constellation.
- **Airbus Defence and Space:** an initial contract for at least 66 Ka-band IRIS² satellites (military Ka for European users; delivery 2029, built in Toulouse) plus 229 additional OneWeb refresh (service-continuity) satellites (~EUR 1B / ~USD 1.16B), supplementing the 440 OneWeb refresh satellites already ordered in late 2024 and early 2026, bringing Airbus's OneWeb refresh total to 669. The 229 additional satellites feature advanced digital channelizers for onboard processing and flexibility, plus hosted payload capabilities.
- **Thales Alenia Space:** an order worth around EUR 500M (~USD 581M) for the payloads across all 330 IRIS² LEO satellites (of 348 programme total; the 18 MEO satellites go to SES/OHB) (both Aerospacelab- and Airbus-built platforms) — the first payload-level contract this KB has recorded.
- **Launch plan:** two dedicated Ariane 64 launches secured for the OneWeb deployment, in 2027 and 2028.
- **Programme context:** this firms up the figures from the 2026-08-07 IRIS² execution-phase announcement (330-satellite LEO segment, EUR 2.23B shared + EUR 1.16B commercial infrastructure, first Ka-only launch 2029, commercial Ku LEO fully operational mid-2032) with named suppliers and signed contract values.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
This is the landing event for figures the KB has carried only as budget-line commitments since 08-28: that entry stated IRIS² architecture, band plan, and a combined capital commitment, but named no satellite manufacturer beyond a generic reference to the SpaceRISE consortium, and gave no launch vehicle. This entry adds three concrete facts the prior one lacked — (1) Aerospacelab, not previously mentioned in this KB's IRIS² coverage, as the prime contractor for the bulk of the LEO segment; (2) Thales Alenia Space as the payload supplier across the whole 330-satellite fleet; (3) a firm two-launch Ariane 64 manifest for the 229-satellite OneWeb replenishment, with 2027/2028 dates. It does not change any of the 08-28 entry's headline figures (330 total IRIS² satellites, 264/66 Mil-Ka/Ku split, 229-satellite / ~EUR 1B OneWeb replenishment) — it verifies and operationalizes them.
