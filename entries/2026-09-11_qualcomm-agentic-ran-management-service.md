## Summary (EN)
Qualcomm announced (from Barcelona, 2 March 2026) an Agentic RAN Management Service as part of its Dragonwing RAN Automation Suite, adding an autonomous management layer which the release says lets operators drive performance improvements and OPEX reduction without the need for hardware refresh cycles. The service is built around specialized AI agents that, in the company's framing, collaboratively monitor network conditions, analyze issues, generate and test candidate action plans, and autonomously execute changes in a closed loop — moving beyond the more common pattern of AI-assisted recommendation toward AI-driven execution.

The Agentic RAN Management Service itself comprises an Agentic Layer of specialist RAN AI Agents plus an rApp Marketplace, Quality of Experience Insights, Digital Twin & AI Simulation and Advanced Analytics, and enables three new RAN AI features: Autonomous Demand-based RAN Adaptation (including for Fixed Wireless Access), Autonomous RAN Supervision, and RAN Energy Management. Separately, under a heading titled "RAN AI Portfolio Now Available on Commercial Platforms," three AI-driven capabilities were announced: uplink adaptation, which dynamically adjusts uplink parameters to improve cell-edge coverage, capacity in congested areas, dropped-connection rate and spectrum efficiency in the presence of bursty traffic; downlink beamforming channel prediction, which anticipates channel conditions ahead of time to enable proactive rather than reactive beamforming and improve MIMO effectiveness; and factory calibration, which applies machine learning to radio-unit tuning at manufacturing scale. The release states the underlying Dragonwing RAN Automation Suite is "already deployed by Tier 1 operators worldwide," without naming any, and discloses no quantitative performance figures. This entry is ingested as a deliberate backfill: the announcement is roughly six months old, within the KB's 12-month freshness window, and documents the vendor-product side of agentic RAN, which the KB's existing coverage — mostly academic research prototypes — had not yet captured.

## Summary (ZH)
高通于 2026 年 3 月 2 日在巴塞罗那宣布推出智能体化 RAN 管理服务，作为其 Dragonwing RAN 自动化套件的一部分，在无需更换硬件的前提下为现有商用 RAN 平台增加一层自主管理能力。该服务基于若干专用 AI 智能体构建，按照高通的表述，这些智能体协同监测网络状况、分析问题、生成并测试候选行动方案，并在闭环中自主执行变更——相较于更常见的"AI 辅助建议"模式，进一步迈向"AI 驱动执行"。

Agentic RAN 管理服务本身由一个「智能体层」（由各司其职的 RAN AI Agent 组成）以及 rApp 市场、体验质量洞察、数字孪生与 AI 仿真、高级分析等部分构成，并支撑三项新的 RAN AI 功能：基于需求的自主 RAN 自适应（含固定无线接入）、自主 RAN 监管，以及 RAN 能耗管理。另外，在「RAN AI 产品组合现已在商用平台上可用」这一独立标题下，公告了三项 AI 驱动能力：上行自适应，可动态调整上行参数，以改善小区边缘覆盖、拥塞区域容量、掉线率，以及突发业务场景下的频谱效率；下行波束成形信道预测，可提前预判信道状况，实现主动而非被动响应式的波束成形，提升 MIMO 效果；以及工厂标定，在制造环节大规模应用机器学习对射频单元进行调优。公告称其底层的 Dragonwing RAN 自动化套件「已被全球一级运营商部署」，但未点名具体运营商，也未披露任何量化性能数据。本条目作为有意的回溯补录被收录：该公告发布至今约六个月，在知识库 12 个月新鲜度窗口之内，记录的是智能体 RAN 在厂商产品侧的进展——这是知识库现有以学术研究原型为主的相关条目此前尚未覆盖到的内容。
