## Summary (EN)

On 11 November 2026 the Wi-Fi NOW Resource Centre published an "Expert Insights" article by its CEO and Chairman, Claus Hetting, on "UWB in ACTION", described as Europe's first open UWB congress, held on 29 October 2026 in Chemnitz, Germany and organised by Wi-Fi NOW and Pinpoint. The KB already holds the shorter 30 October news report of the same event (2026-11-02_uwb-in-action-2026-chemnitz-event-report), which names the participants but gives no measured figures; this later article walks through the sessions and carries the numbers the speakers presented. The article's core message is that UWB has entered an adoption phase and that the remaining barriers are business issues rather than technical ones.

By session, as reported: ABI Research (Andrew Zignani) forecast UWB shipments rising from about 597 million in 2026 to about 1.18 billion in 2030, smartphone penetration above 52% by 2030, more than 53% of new vehicles (about 57 million) with UWB by 2030, and UWB-enabled enterprise access points growing at a 35% CAGR (2025-2030). STMicroelectronics (Rias Al-kadi) said 802.15.4ab adds roughly 20-30 dB of link budget over 15.4z, with one multi-millisecond (MMS) frame alone adding about 20 dB, giving ranges of several hundred metres; ST said this lets 15.4ab cover typical enterprise AP spacing of about 40 m. Qorvo, described as now part of Skyworks (Alexis Bizalion), proposed a "trust contract" model in which applications state location-quality requirements and the system returns confidence data. NXP (Olivier Thivolet) described four standards-based modes in Wi-Fi APs: FiRa TWR, DL-TDoA, FiRa/omlox UL-TDoA (about 10 cm asset-tracking accuracy) and radar sensing. HPE (Anilash Azeez) presented the AP47 (Wi-Fi 7, BLE, UWB), reporting AP auto-placement by two-way ranging at about 0.5 m mean accuracy in more than 95% of cases, best with APs up to 25 m apart. Deutsche Bahn (Steven Karch) reported a UWB indoor-navigation proof of concept at a German station that met a sub-1 m requirement for blind and partially sighted travellers, with certification as the next step. SICK / ZIGPOS (Eric Mademann) cited a deployment of about 800 anchors, roughly 380 of them HPE APs. Flowcate (Matthias Jost, omlox Committee Chair) presented omlox plus UWB as a vendor-neutral spatial layer for physical AI, citing the SafeInLoc case at a 1,200 m2 test site with Fraunhofer IOSB-INA. Infineon (Bjorn Scharfen) demonstrated UWB radar presence sensing of an untagged person up to 4.8 m away, including when seated; in its benchmark UWB trailed 60 GHz radar but outperformed PIR sensors, and Infineon noted its chip supports channel 12 while advocating protecting channel 9.

## Summary (ZH)

2026年11月11日，Wi-Fi NOW 资源中心发布了其 CEO 兼主席 Claus Hetting 撰写的"专家洞察"文章，回顾"UWB in ACTION"——文中称其为欧洲首个开放式 UWB 大会，于2026年10月29日在德国开姆尼茨举行，由 Wi-Fi NOW 与 Pinpoint 主办。知识库已收录同一活动10月30日的较短新闻报道（2026-11-02_uwb-in-action-2026-chemnitz-event-report），该报道列出了参与方但没有实测数字；本文较晚发布，逐场回顾并带有演讲者给出的数据。文章的核心观点是：UWB 已进入采用阶段，剩余障碍是商业问题而非技术问题。

按场次（均为文章转述）：ABI Research（Andrew Zignani）预测 UWB 出货量将从2026年约5.97亿增至2030年约11.8亿，2030年智能手机渗透率超过52%，超过53%的新车（约5700万辆）配备 UWB，支持 UWB 的企业级接入点在2025-2030年的复合年增长率为35%。STMicroelectronics（Rias Al-kadi）表示 802.15.4ab 相比 15.4z 增加约20-30 dB 链路预算，仅一个多毫秒（MMS）帧就增加约20 dB，可达数百米，ST 表示足以覆盖约 40 m 的典型企业 AP 间距。Qorvo（文中称现属 Skyworks，Alexis Bizalion）提出"信任契约"模型：应用声明定位质量要求，系统返回置信度数据。NXP（Olivier Thivolet）介绍 Wi-Fi AP 中四种基于标准的模式：FiRa TWR、DL-TDoA、FiRa/omlox UL-TDoA（资产跟踪精度约10 cm）以及雷达感知。HPE（Anilash Azeez）展示 AP47（Wi-Fi 7、BLE、UWB），报告通过双向测距自动确定 AP 位置的平均精度约0.5 m、超过95%的情形达到，AP 间距不超过25 m时最佳。德国铁路（Steven Karch）报告在德国一个火车站完成 UWB 室内导航概念验证，满足面向盲人及视障旅客的1 m 以内精度要求，下一步为认证。SICK / ZIGPOS（Eric Mademann）提到一个约800个锚点的部署，其中约380个为 HPE AP。Flowcate（Matthias Jost，omlox 委员会主席）将 omlox 加 UWB 作为面向物理 AI 的厂商中立空间层，引用与 Fraunhofer IOSB-INA 在1200平方米测试场地的 SafeInLoc 案例。Infineon（Bjorn Scharfen）演示了 UWB 雷达存在感知，可检测最远4.8 m 外未佩戴标签的人员（含坐姿）；其基准中 UWB 落后于60 GHz 雷达但优于 PIR 传感器，Infineon 还指出其芯片支持信道12，同时主张保护信道9。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Market (ABI Research, as presented): ~597 M UWB shipments in 2026 to ~1.18 B in 2030; smartphone penetration >52% by 2030; >53% of new vehicles (~57 M) by 2030; UWB enterprise APs at 35% CAGR 2025-2030.
- 802.15.4ab (ST): ~20-30 dB link budget gain over 15.4z; one MMS frame ~20 dB; ranges of several hundred metres, which ST said covers typical enterprise AP spacing of ~40 m.
- Status statements in the article: the 4ab physical layer is complete with chips and dev kits available; the protocol layer (FiRa, omlox, CCC) is about halfway; full applications "another couple of years" away; the standard was noted as not yet formally released; backward compatibility with installed RTLS is called a hard question.
- NXP AP modes: FiRa TWR, DL-TDoA, FiRa/omlox UL-TDoA (~10 cm asset tracking), radar sensing.
- HPE AP47: two-way-ranging AP auto-placement ~0.5 m mean in >95% of cases, APs up to 25 m apart; HPE claims to be the only vendor compliant with both FiRa and omlox.
- Deutsche Bahn: sub-1 m requirement met in a station PoC for blind and partially sighted travellers.
- Infineon radar: untagged person detected up to 4.8 m, UWB behind 60 GHz radar but ahead of PIR; channel 12 supported, channel 9 to be protected.
- Other claims: FiRa framed as mobile-centric and omlox as infrastructure-centric, able to coexist; FCC and European transmit-power limits still differ; education was named as the biggest gap.
- Source caveat: figures are as presented by speakers; the article's publisher co-produced the event.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the second KB entry on the same Chemnitz event, and it adds what the first did not: per-session numbers and status statements. The most useful for the KB's 802.15.4ab thread are ST's link-budget figures (20-30 dB, ~20 dB from an MMS frame) and the cross-vendor description of where the standard stands (PHY complete, protocol layer about halfway, not yet formally released). On the deployment side, the HPE AP47 auto-placement result, NXP's four AP modes and the ~800-anchor mixed deployment connect the earlier enterprise-AP UWB entries to concrete operating figures. All numbers are vendor- or organiser-reported and the article's author's company co-organised the event, so they are presentation claims, not measurements published with methods.
