## Summary (EN)

In the embodied-intelligence + AI session of the 22 October CEIC2026 SparkLink summit (as reported by CITNews on 25 October 2026), alliance vice-chairman Wan Lei summarised results to date. The "SparkLink Short-Range and Embodied Intelligence Technical Requirements Blue Book", compiled by the alliance with core members of its embodied-intelligence special interest group (SIG), was released; it surveys the short-range communication needs of the industry.

The SparkLink AIEP standard and the Dora protocol stack were released together. The report describes this as the industry's first open, unified standard for wireless communication of embodied agents, with the standard and the open protocol stack appearing at the same time, and aimed at robotics, industrial automation and smart healthcare.

A SparkLink AI development kit was also released, co-built by Huawei, DORA, Inkemei (因科美), Zhongke Jingshang, Wuhan Pansheng (武汉攀升), China Mobile and Lierda. It comprises a SparkLink AI camera, an AI robot main-control board, edge-side and device-side AI node adapters/dongles, and an AI interactive microphone. Partners including China Mobile, Huawei, Midea, TD Tech, Zhongke Jingshang, Shenkaihong, the Shanghai National Engineering Research Center of Digital Television and universities plan three demonstration scenarios: a SparkLink smart robot factory, unmanned smart retail, and immersive robot teleoperation.

## Summary (ZH)

据 CITNews 2026 年 10 月 25 日报道，在 10 月 22 日 CEIC2026 星闪峰会的“星闪具身智能+AI”专题中，联盟副理事长万蕾解读了阶段性成果。由联盟联合具身智能SIG核心成员单位编制的《星闪短距与具身智能技术需求蓝皮书》正式发布，梳理了产业对短距通信的需求。

星闪 AIEP 标准与 Dora 协议栈同台发布。报道称这是业界首个面向具身智能体无线通信的开放统一标准，标准与开源协议栈同步亮相，面向机器人、工业自动化、智慧医疗等领域。

星闪AI开发套件同时发布，由华为、DORA、因科美、中科晶上、武汉攀升、中国移动、利尔达等共建，涵盖星闪AI摄像头、AI机器人主控板、边侧与端侧AI节点适配器/Dongle、AI智能交互麦克风等。中国移动、华为、美的、鼎桥、中科晶上、深开鸿、上海数字电视国家工程研究中心及高校等将围绕星闪智慧机器人工厂、无人智慧零售、机器人沉浸式遥操作三大场景打造示范应用。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Blue book on short-range communication requirements for embodied intelligence (alliance plus embodied-intelligence SIG members).
- AIEP standard released together with the Dora protocol stack; the report calls it the first open unified standard for embodied-agent wireless communication (the source's claim).
- AI kit components: AI camera, robot main-control board, edge-side AI node adapter/dongle, device-side AI node adapter/dongle, AI interactive microphone.
- Planned demonstration scenarios: smart robot factory, unmanned retail, immersive robot teleoperation.
- No protocol parameters (latency, rate, frame format) are given in the report.
