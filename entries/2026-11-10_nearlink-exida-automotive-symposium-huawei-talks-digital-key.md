## Summary (EN)

A 2026-11-08 article on the SparkLink Alliance news feed reports that two experts from Huawei, a member of the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA), gave keynote talks at the 14th exida Automotive Symposium 2026 on 27-28 October 2026 at Spitzingsee, Bavaria, Germany. The symposium is described as an annual gathering on functional safety and cybersecurity for automotive electronics. The article says the talks introduced SparkLink to the audience, covered the alliance's role in building an international standards ecosystem, and discussed automotive digital keys against current regulation and compliance requirements.
