## Summary (EN)

A September 7, 2026 announcement on the International SparkLink Alliance's own news feed (navId=33, item id 939) describes a commercial deployment of SparkLink (NearLink) e-paper technology in a hospital setting — the first SparkLink healthcare-application story this KB has recorded. The deployment is a joint effort between "国东勤达" (Guodong Qinda) and "润和软件" (HopeRun Software): 13.3-inch e-paper bedside terminals, paired with SparkLink communication base stations and a backend management platform, were installed at Hong Kong's Union Hospital (仁安医院) to replace traditional printed patient-information cards. The release describes this as the solution's first commercial deployment.

The announcement frames the system as a "complete ward information digitalisation" package: the backend can push second-level, one-click batch updates to every bedside display, removing the manual handwriting and swapping of paper cards each time a patient's status, medication, or care team changes. Claimed benefits are zero ongoing paper/ink/printing consumption, and — because the displays use backlight-free e-ink — no flicker and no blue light, intended to avoid disturbing patient rest at night the way a lit display would. The article states SparkLink's transmission speed here is "4x faster than traditional wireless solutions" (no baseline technology is named), that a single base station covers a standard ward area, and that the system can synchronise data across multiple orders/instructions simultaneously. The partners say they plan to keep iterating the technology and solution, and to work with the alliance to replicate the deployment at additional healthcare facilities. This is a vendor-contributed release republished on the alliance's own site, not independent reporting, and it does not specify which SparkLink air-interface mode (SLB vs SLE) the terminals use.

## Summary (ZH)

国际星闪联盟官方资讯（navId=33，条目 id 939）于 2026 年 9 月 7 日发布公告，介绍星闪电子纸技术在医疗场景的一次商业化落地——这是本知识库首次收录的星闪医疗应用报道。此次落地由"国东勤达"与"润和软件"联合推进：在香港仁安医院部署了 13.3 英寸星闪电子纸病床终端，并配套星闪通信基站与后台管理平台，替代传统纸质病人信息卡。官方称这是该方案首次完成商用落地。

公告将该系统定位为"病区信息数字化管理"整体方案：后台可实现秒级同步、一键批量下发更新至每张病床终端，省去每次患者状态、用药或诊疗组变更时人工手写与更换纸质卡片的流程。宣称的效益包括零纸张、零油墨、零打印耗材消耗；由于采用无背光电子墨水屏，显示"无频闪、无蓝光"，具备类纸质观感，旨在避免夜间亮屏对病人休息造成干扰。文章称星闪的传输速度"较传统无线方案快 4 倍"（未指明具体对比对象），单个基站可覆盖标准病区范围，并可同时对多条医嘱/指令数据进行同步。合作方表示将持续深化技术迭代与方案打磨，并计划与国际星闪联盟合作，在更多医疗机构复制该部署。该文为厂商供稿、经联盟官网转载，并非独立报道，且未说明终端采用的具体星闪空口模式（SLB 或 SLE）。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Deployer/partners**: 国东勤达 (Guodong Qinda) and 润和软件 (HopeRun Software), announced via the International SparkLink Alliance's news feed, September 7, 2026.
- **Site**: Hong Kong's 仁安医院 (Union Hospital), Tai Wai.
- **Integration**: the release describes deep integration with the hospital's HIS (hospital information system), and claims strong wall penetration and zero missed refreshes, attributing these to SparkLink's low latency, interference resistance, high concurrency and low power.
- **Hardware**: 13.3-inch e-paper bedside terminals + SparkLink communication base stations + a backend management platform.
- **Claimed performance**: transmission speed "4x faster than traditional wireless solutions" (baseline unnamed); one base station covers a standard ward; simultaneous multi-order data sync; second-level batch update push from the backend.
- **Claimed benefits**: eliminates manual paper-card replacement; zero paper/ink/printing consumption; backlight-free, flicker-free, blue-light-free display for undisturbed patient rest.
- **Not specified**: which SparkLink mode (SLB/SLE) the terminals use; no independent/third-party coverage of this deployment found.
- **Stated next step**: partners plan further iteration and replication at additional healthcare facilities via the alliance.
