## Summary (EN)
On 31 July 2026 NTIA announced that the Administration has cleared the study plan for the **4.4 GHz federal band (4400–4940 MHz)** for full-power, exclusive commercial licensed use and notified Congress. Under the spectrum-pipeline provisions of the 2025 reconciliation law (the "One Big Beautiful Bill", which directs NTIA to identify at least 500 MHz — up to an 800 MHz pipeline, minimum 600 MHz between 1.3–10 GHz — of federal spectrum for licensed commercial use within five years) the notification opens a **60-day review by the Commerce and Appropriations Committees**, after which OMB can release Spectrum Relocation Fund (SRF) money to the incumbent agencies that submitted relocation/transition plans (nine agencies and military departments, per earlier NTIA remarks). NTIA Administrator Arielle Roth said that with the 4.4 GHz announcement all four of NTIA's pipeline bands are now advancing simultaneously.

The four bands are: **1.6 GHz (1675–1695 MHz)**, **2.7 GHz (2700–2900 MHz; NOAA/FAA radar incumbents, relocation plans awaiting SRF funding)**, **4.4 GHz (4400–4940 MHz; multiple federal and military incumbents)** and **7 GHz (7125–7400 MHz first tranche, on a Presidential 12-month deadline with a final report due December 2026)**. This is the first time all four have been in formal study at once — described by NTIA as an unprecedented milestone for the December-2025 "Winning the 6G Race" Presidential Memorandum. Four days earlier (27 July) NTIA also launched the **"Call to Action for 6G Leadership and Security"**, affirmed by 25 governments across Europe, the Indo-Pacific and the Western Hemisphere (incl. Australia, Canada, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Italy, Japan, Republic of Korea, Sweden, UK, US), committing to 12-month-plus cooperation on 6G that is "open, interoperable, secure, resilient, AI-supported, highly innovative, and resource efficient".

## Summary (ZH)
2026 年 7 月 31 日，NTIA 宣布美国政府已批准 **4.4 GHz 联邦频段（4400–4940 MHz）** 面向全功率、独占商用许可用途的研究计划并通报国会。依据 2025 年预算调节法（"大而美法案"）的频谱管线条款——要求 NTIA 五年内识别至少 500 MHz（管线上限 800 MHz，其中 1.3–10 GHz 间至少 600 MHz）联邦频谱用于许可商用——该通报开启**商务与拨款委员会 60 天审查期**，之后 OMB 才可向已提交搬迁/过渡计划的现有部门（据 NTIA 此前说法为九个机构与军种）拨付频谱搬迁基金（SRF）。NTIA 局长 Arielle Roth："随着今天 4.4 GHz 的宣布，我们四个管线频段全部同步推进。"

四个频段为：**1.6 GHz（1675–1695 MHz）**、**2.7 GHz（2700–2900 MHz；NOAA/FAA 雷达在用，搬迁计划待 SRF 拨款）**、**4.4 GHz（4400–4940 MHz；多个联邦与军方用户）**、**7 GHz（首批 7125–7400 MHz，总统设定 12 个月期限，最终报告 2026 年 12 月到期）**。这是四个频段首次同时进入正式研究——NTIA 称之为落实 2025 年 12 月"赢得 6G 竞赛"总统备忘录的空前里程碑。四天前（7 月 27 日）NTIA 还发起 **"6G 领导力与安全行动倡议"**，获欧洲、印太与西半球 25 国联署（含澳大利亚、加拿大、捷克、丹麦、爱沙尼亚、芬兰、法国、德国、意大利、日本、韩国、瑞典、英国、美国），承诺就"开放、可互操作、安全、韧性、AI 支撑、高度创新且资源高效"的 6G 开展 12 个月以上的合作。

## Key technical points (EN)
- 4.4 GHz band = 4400–4940 MHz (540 MHz), overlapping the 3GPP n79 (4.4–5.0 GHz) range already used for 5G in China/Japan — a US clearance would harmonize a large upper-mid-band block that is a natural 6G candidate alongside 7–8.4 GHz.
- 2.7 GHz (2700–2900 MHz) is the NOAA/FAA weather and air-traffic radar band — the same radar family the Northeastern coexistence paper (2026-08-15) models as a radiolocation/radionavigation coexistence constraint.
- 7 GHz study (7125–7400 MHz) is the most advanced: December-2026 final report; industry testing coverage with Ericsson/Nokia prototypes.
- Process: NTIA/agency study plans → congressional notification → 60-day committee review → OMB releases SRF → agency relocation; the pipeline law's target is 500 MHz identified within 5 years.
- Companion policy: 25-government Call to Action (27 Jul 2026) — security, interoperability, resilience, trusted AI; milestone-based coordination over 12 months.

## Key technical points (ZH)
- 4.4 GHz 频段 = 4400–4940 MHz（540 MHz），与中国/日本已用于 5G 的 3GPP n79（4.4–5.0 GHz）重叠——美国若清频，将与 7–8.4 GHz 一起形成天然的 6G 上中频段候选。
- 2.7 GHz（2700–2900 MHz）为 NOAA/FAA 气象与空管雷达频段——正是东北大学共存论文（2026-08-15）建模为无线电定位/导航共存约束的同一雷达家族。
- 7 GHz 研究（7125–7400 MHz）进度最快：2026 年 12 月出最终报告；产业界正用爱立信/诺基亚原型测试覆盖。
- 流程：NTIA/机构研究计划 → 通报国会 → 委员会 60 天审查 → OMB 拨付 SRF → 机构搬迁；管线法目标为五年内识别 500 MHz。
- 配套政策：25 国行动倡议（2026 年 7 月 27 日）——安全、互操作、韧性、可信 AI；12 个月里程碑式协调。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The KB's US spectrum thread had the FCC side (upper C-band auction rules, 2026-08-05; AWS-3 auction) but no entry on the **NTIA federal-band pipeline** that will supply the 6G-era mid/upper-mid-band blocks. This is the moment the pipeline moved from a 7 GHz-only sprint to four bands in parallel, with 4.4 GHz being the largest single block (540 MHz) and the one most aligned with existing global n79 ecosystems. It also fixes the near-term calendar: 60-day 4.4 GHz review into early October 2026, 7 GHz report December 2026, and the December-2026/2027 auction sequencing the FCC has been staging. Read together with the same-day AMRA/QGAA coexistence entry, the technical question for the 2.7–3.7 GHz and 4.4 GHz clusters is increasingly *how* to share with radar/navigation incumbents at PRB granularity, not *whether*.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
知识库的美国频谱线索此前只有 FCC 侧（上 C 波段拍卖规则 2026-08-05；AWS-3 拍卖），缺少将供给 6G 时代中/上中频段的 **NTIA 联邦频段管线**。此刻管线从仅 7 GHz 的冲刺变为四频段并行，其中 4.4 GHz 是最大单块（540 MHz），也最贴合现有全球 n79 生态。它也确定了近期日程：4.4 GHz 60 天审查至 2026 年 10 月初、7 GHz 报告 2026 年 12 月、以及 FCC 正在编排的 2026 年 12 月/2027 年拍卖序列。与同日的 AMRA/QGAA 共存条目并读，2.7–3.7 GHz 与 4.4 GHz 频段簇的技术问题正从"是否"共享转向"如何"在 PRB 粒度上与雷达/导航在用方共享。
