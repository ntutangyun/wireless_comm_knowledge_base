## Summary (EN)

The International SparkLink Alliance's (iSLA) Requirements and Standards Working Group held its 21st meeting in Wuxi on 2026-09-16, chaired by Cai Tingxiao (蔡廷晓) of the China Electronics Standardization Institute's IoT Research Center (中国电子技术标准化研究院物联网研究中心), with welcoming remarks from Dr. Tang Zhuang (唐壮), founder and CTO of Jiangsu Maxscend Microelectronics Co., Ltd. (江苏卓胜微电子股份有限公司), which supported the meeting. Over 70 expert representatives from more than 40 member organizations across multiple regions attended in person — a smaller session than the 20th meeting in Hangzhou three months earlier (90+ representatives, 50+ organizations, per the 2026-09-17 KB entry on that meeting).

The meeting approved four new standard project-initiation proposals: "Communication Anti-interference under Strong Electromagnetic Interference — Part 1: General Principles," "SparkLink Device Environmental Applicability Guidelines," "Multi-device Collaborative Service Quality Evaluation Guide," and "SparkLink Vehicle Head-unit–Phone Interaction." It also approved one revision to the draft-for-comment of the "SparkLink Wireless Communication System — Basic Service Layer — Network Packet Format and Multi-hop Networking Protocol" standard, and nine of ten submitted standard change requests, against T/XS 10001-2025 (SLB), T/XS 10002-2025 (SLE) and T/XS 10003-2025 (data-link interface): MCS-related and editorial revisions to the SLB standard; audio-related revisions to the DLI standard; and SLE revisions to the pairing procedure, controller transmit-test commands and expected GFSK frequency-deviation results.

The source article gives titles and approval status for each work item but no further technical detail (parameter values, timelines to publication, or the content of the anti-interference general-principles document). The "SparkLink Vehicle Head-unit–Phone Interaction" project initiation is the most concrete new signal for the automotive vertical this KB tracks — a project title, not yet a published standard.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the second Requirements & Standards WG meeting in this KB, after the 20th (Hangzhou, 2026-06-09/10; 2026-09-17 entry). The 20th meeting was held about three months earlier; both meetings processed project proposals, a draft for comment and CRs against the T/XS 1000x-2025 standards. The "SparkLink Vehicle Head-unit–Phone Interaction" project initiation is new: existing automotive-vertical entries in this KB cover digital car keys and wireless BMS, not vehicle-head-unit-to-phone interaction as its own standardized work item — worth watching for a published draft.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

这是本知识库记录的第二次需求与标准工作组会议，此前为第20次（杭州，2026年6月9-10日；2026-09-17条目）。第20次会议约三个月前召开；两次会议均处理了立项建议、征求意见稿及针对T/XS 1000x-2025标准的CR。"星闪车机手机交互"立项是新信息：本知识库现有车载领域条目覆盖数字车钥匙与无线电池管理，尚未有车机与手机交互本身作为独立标准化工作项目的记录——值得关注是否会形成正式发布的草案。
