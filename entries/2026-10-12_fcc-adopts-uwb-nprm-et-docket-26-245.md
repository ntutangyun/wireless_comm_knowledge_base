## Summary (EN)

On 30 September 2026, at its Open Meeting, the US Federal Communications Commission formally adopted the Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NPRM) "Unleashing the Power of Unlicensed Ultra-Wideband Devices" (ET Docket No. 26-245) that had previously circulated as a draft on 9 September 2026 (covered in this KB as 2026-09-14_fcc-uwb-part15-rules-overhaul-nprm). Independent tech-policy coverage (Benton Foundation) confirms the adoption took place at the 30 September 2026 meeting, matching the NPRM's own tentative-agenda date. Both the FiRa Consortium and the Car Connectivity Consortium (CCC) — two of the five organizations behind the joint petition (RM-12014) that helped prompt the proceeding — issued same-day reaction statements. FiRa Consortium President Sunil Jogi is quoted: "Today's action is a significant milestone for the UWB ecosystem. Ultra-wideband is already enabling secure, precise experiences in hundreds of millions of devices worldwide," and separately, "This NPRM recognizes that UWB is a mature, secure, and spectrum-efficient technology with broad benefits for consumers, businesses, and the economy." CCC published its own statement the same day, titled "Car Connectivity Consortium Applauds the FCC's Notice of Proposed Rulemaking to Modernize Ultra-Wideband Rules." Neither reaction release restates the NPRM's detailed technical proposals (new UWB device category for factory automation/autonomous navigation/AI-enabled sensing, updated emission/measurement rules, removal of the written frequency-coordination requirement for UWB imaging systems, etc.) in more depth than the original draft; this entry treats the event as confirmation that the draft moved from circulated-for-consideration to formally adopted, not as a new substantive rule text. As of this entry, no Federal Register publication date (which starts the 30-day comment / 60-day reply-comment clocks) has been independently confirmed.
