## Summary (EN)

Fundación Innova IRV announced on 2026-09-21 (see the dated article linked above) that it will organise SparkLink Europe 2026 together with the International SparkLink Short-Range Wireless Communication Alliance (iSLA) on 26 November 2026 at the DEKRA Knowledge Center in Málaga (the event site places it in Málaga Tech Park), under the theme "SparkLink: Bridging the Way to Intelligence". The announcement names semiconductor makers, automotive suppliers and OEMs, smart-device and appliance companies, industrial automation firms and research institutions as the intended audience. The event website also refers to a previous edition, SparkLink Europe 2025.

The event site now lists a full one-day programme (09:30 to 15:20, followed by an optional DEKRA facility tour until 16:00) and an exhibition page. The programme runs as short keynote and industry talks, a panel and exhibition-area breaks; this entry reports the session titles and abstracts as published, before the event has happened.

## Summary (ZH)

Innova IRV 基金会于2026年9月21日发文（见上方带日期的链接）宣布，将与国际星闪无线短距通信联盟（iSLA）合办 SparkLink Europe 2026，时间为2026年11月26日，地点为马拉加的 DEKRA Knowledge Center（活动官网注明位于马拉加科技园），主题为 “SparkLink: Bridging the Way to Intelligence”。公告将半导体厂商、汽车供应商与整车厂、智能设备与家电企业、工业自动化企业和科研机构列为目标参与者。活动官网还提到了此前一届 SparkLink Europe 2025。

活动官网现已列出完整的一日日程（09:30 至 15:20，之后为可选的 DEKRA 设施参观，至 16:00）及展区页面。日程由若干简短的主旨与行业演讲、一场圆桌讨论和展区交流时段组成；本条目按官网发布内容记录各场次标题与摘要，活动尚未举行。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **iSLA keynote (10:00)**: "Progress and plans of the international SparkLink Alliance", by secretary-general Guosong Zeng. The abstract says it will cover SparkLink standards milestones, ecosystem evolution, "full industrial chain integration" from specifications to chips, modules and applications, and a global roadmap for smart vehicles, smart terminals and industrial IoT. These are the organisers' own programme descriptions.
- **DEKRA (10:20)**: "Foundation for Collaboration within the Sparklink EcoSystem" by Thomas Jäger (Senior Vice President, Global Connectivity and ITS Technologies); the abstract mentions assessment of global digital connected products, digital trust and SparkLink processes.
- **Ficosa (11:45)**: a talk by Ignasi Claret, CEO Asia Pacific; no title or abstract is given on the programme page.
- **ABTC (12:05)**: "From Specification to Product: An Embedded Engineer's View of SparkLink" by Javier Longares Abaiz; the abstract says ABTC is evaluating SparkLink alongside BLE, Wi-Fi and UWB and discusses the practical questions in moving from development kits to products.
- **Waldaschaff Automotive (13:55)**: "Sparklink Enabled Wireless 360 Camera Monitoring System for Commercial Vehicles". The abstract describes a surround-view system for trailers and large construction machinery that transmits four to six 1080p video streams with end-to-end latency "controlled within 200 milliseconds" and says it surpasses wired transmission; these are the presenters' claims, not measurements.
- **Academic talks**: University of Oulu (Prof. Juha Röning) on robotics and physical AI in Europe's competitiveness (11:25); University of Oviedo (Ignacio Rodríguez) on the nationally funded RARIN project, with planned SparkLink experimentation and initial results in wireless sensing (14:15).
- **Industry ecosystem and testing**: YanLink on a commercial OS layer (YanLinkOS) connecting Oniro, connectivity, edge AI and industrial deployment (14:35); Hyperconnectivity Campus (HCC) on side-by-side testing of short-range wireless for physical AI on a live shopfloor (14:55). A panel discussion (13:25) has no topic or participants listed.
- **Exhibition page**: lists a "SparkLink Ecosystem" area with companies including Triductor Technology (Suzhou), HiSilicon (Shanghai), Beijing Sylincom, Shenzhen Kaihong, V-link, Lierda, Jiangsu HopeRun, CEANIC, Iton and YanLink, plus categories for smart terminals, smart home, smart car and industry applications.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **iSLA 主旨演讲（10:00）**：“Progress and plans of the international SparkLink Alliance”，由秘书长曾国松演讲。摘要称将涵盖星闪标准里程碑、生态演进、从规范到芯片、模组、应用的“全产业链整合”，以及面向智能汽车、智能终端和工业物联网的全球路线图。以上为主办方的日程描述。
- **DEKRA（10:20）**：“Foundation for Collaboration within the Sparklink EcoSystem”，演讲者 Thomas Jäger（全球连接与 ITS 技术高级副总裁）；摘要提到全球数字互联产品评估、数字信任与星闪相关流程。
- **Ficosa（11:45）**：由亚太区 CEO Ignasi Claret 演讲；日程页未给出题目和摘要。
- **ABTC（12:05）**：“From Specification to Product: An Embedded Engineer's View of SparkLink”，演讲者 Javier Longares Abaiz；摘要称 ABTC 正在将星闪与 BLE、Wi-Fi、UWB 一并评估，并讨论从开发套件走向产品的实际工程问题。
- **Waldaschaff Automotive（13:55）**：“Sparklink Enabled Wireless 360 Camera Monitoring System for Commercial Vehicles”。摘要描述一套面向挂车和大型工程机械的环视系统，可同时传输4至6路1080P视频，端到端时延“控制在200毫秒以内”，并称在无线方式下全面超越有线传输；这些是演讲方的说法，并非实测数据。
- **高校演讲**：奥卢大学（Juha Röning 教授）讨论机器人与物理 AI 对欧洲竞争力的意义（11:25）；奥维耶多大学（Ignacio Rodríguez）介绍西班牙国家资助的 RARIN 项目，包括计划开展的星闪实验和无线感知的初步结果（14:15）。
- **产业生态与测试**：YanLink 介绍连接 Oniro、连接、边缘 AI 与工业部署的商用操作系统层 YanLinkOS（14:35）；Hyperconnectivity Campus（HCC）介绍在真实车间并行测试短距无线技术以支撑物理 AI（14:55）。13:25 的圆桌讨论未列出主题和嘉宾。
- **展区页面**：列出“SparkLink Ecosystem”展区，参展企业包括 Triductor Technology（苏州）、海思（上海）、Beijing Sylincom Technology（北京）、深圳开鸿、V-link、利尔达、江苏润和、CEANIC、Iton、YanLink 等，并设有智能终端、智能家居、智能汽车、行业应用等分类。
