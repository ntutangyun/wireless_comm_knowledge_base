## Summary (EN)

On July 22, 2026, the FCC voted to adopt the Upper C-band order (Report and Order, Order of Proposed Modification, and Order on Reconsideration — FCC 26-46; Chairman Carr and Commissioner Trusty approving, Commissioner Gomez approving in part / dissenting in part). The order reallocates 3.98-4.14 GHz for terrestrial flexible use in the contiguous United States and sets rules to auction the 160 MHz by July 2027 — the Commission's first spectrum auction in four years, and 60% more spectrum than the 100 MHz minimum Congress mandated for Upper C-band auctioning by July 2027.

The centerpiece framing is the "super band": bridging the existing Lower C-band (3.7-3.98 GHz, auctioned 2020-21) with the new Upper C-band segment creates a contiguous, harmonized 440 MHz run from 3.70 to 4.14 GHz under compatible technical rules — unified equipment ecosystems instead of separate interference-protection regimes. The order creates 3,248 new flexible-use licenses. Reported deployment milestones from pre-vote coverage: service launch in the largest markets from December 2030 and elsewhere from July 2031, reflecting the satellite-operator transition out of the band.

Aviation-safety coordination — the flashpoint of the 2021-22 Lower C-band / radio-altimeter dispute — is addressed in the order with funding provisions for altimeter retrofitting and relocation costs. The auction proceeds are expected to run to tens of billions of dollars.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 7 月 22 日，FCC 投票通过高段 C 频段命令（报告与命令、拟议修改令及复议令——FCC 26-46；主席 Carr 与委员 Trusty 赞成，委员 Gomez 部分赞成/部分反对）。该命令将 3.98-4.14 GHz 重新分配用于美国本土的地面灵活使用，并设定规则在 2027 年 7 月前拍卖这 160 MHz——这是 FCC 四年来的首次频谱拍卖，比国会要求的 2027 年 7 月前至少拍卖 100 MHz 的下限多出 60%。

核心框架是"超级频段"：将现有低段 C 频段（3.7-3.98 GHz，2020-21 年拍卖）与新的高段 C 频段衔接，在兼容技术规则下形成 3.70-4.14 GHz 连续、统一的 440 MHz 频谱——统一设备生态而非各自独立的干扰保护体制。命令创建 3,248 张新的灵活使用牌照。投票前报道的部署里程碑：最大市场自 2030 年 12 月起、其他地区自 2031 年 7 月起开通服务，反映卫星运营商迁出该频段的过渡期。

航空安全协调——2021-22 年低段 C 频段与无线电高度表争议的焦点——在命令中通过高度表改装与迁移成本的资金安排加以处理。拍卖收入预计达数百亿美元量级。

## Key technical points (EN)

- FCC 26-46 adopted July 22, 2026: Report and Order + Order of Proposed Modification + Order on Reconsideration; 2-1 partial dissent (Gomez in part).
- 160 MHz at 3.98-4.14 GHz reallocated for terrestrial flexible use (contiguous US); auction by July 2027 — first FCC spectrum auction in four years.
- 60% above the congressional 100 MHz minimum for Upper C-band.
- "Super band": contiguous 440 MHz (3.70-4.14 GHz) with harmonized technical rules across Lower and Upper C-band.
- 3,248 new flexible-use licenses; reported service milestones December 2030 (largest markets) / July 2031 (elsewhere).
- Order funds radio-altimeter retrofitting/relocation — pre-empting a rerun of the 2021-22 aviation interference dispute.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the adoption/landing of the vote that 2026-07-11_fcc-part100-space-modernization-order flagged as scheduled for July 22 (that entry centered on the Part 25 → Part 100 satellite licensing overhaul, with the Upper C-band auction as the companion item). It is also the first regulatory entry in the cellular-spectrum bin, which so far held only academic FR3 coexistence work (2026-06-13_arxiv-fr3-terrestrial-ntn-spectrum-sharing). The 440 MHz contiguous mid-band block is the largest unified 5G/6G spectrum asset in the US market; the 2030-31 service dates make this a 6G-era band in practice. Watch: auction scheduling (target July 2027), satellite transition mechanics out of 3.98-4.14 GHz, and whether the harmonized super-band rules become the template for the remaining mid-band pipeline (NTIA items).
