## Summary (EN)

In a press release dated 9 September 2026 (Beaverton, Oregon), the FiRa Consortium announced that Sunil Jogi has been appointed its President. The release describes him as a wireless and semiconductor veteran with more than 25 years of experience, who previously served on the FiRa Board of Directors and as Co-Chair of the Requirements Working Group, and who spent 19 years at FiRa Sponsor member NXP Semiconductors, including as Director of UWB Marketing and Product Management. The release states that he will guide FiRa's technical specifications, certification programs, regulatory engagement and ecosystem initiatives; in his quoted remarks Jogi names strengthening interoperability, unlocking new use cases and expanding FiRa's global adoption as aims. Rias Al-kadi, Vice Chair of the FiRa Board, is quoted praising his "leadership, strategic vision, technological expertise"; Jogi is quoted: "It is a privilege to serve as President of the FiRa Consortium at such an important time for UWB." The release gives no membership figures. Jogi was subsequently quoted in FiRa's reaction to the FCC UWB rulemaking vote (covered in this KB on 2026-10-12).
