## Summary (EN)

On 8 September 2026, the Car Connectivity Consortium (CCC) announced the opening of CCC Plugfest #19, hosted by Geely Holding Group, a four-day interoperability event scheduled for 7-10 September 2026 in Ningbo, China. Participants included Apple, the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology, COMPRION GmbH, DEKRA, Ellisys, Geely Holding Group, GosuncnWelink Technology Co. Ltd., Leapmotor Technology Co. Ltd., and Volkswagen Group (China) Technology Company Limited. Planned testing covered CCC Digital Key Version 3 and Version 4 implementations over Bluetooth LE and NFC, remote keyless entry and passive entry, Bluetooth LE sniffing, updated NFC test cases and China-specific interoperability scenarios including cross-platform key sharing via relay servers; Bluetooth LE and UWB test-equipment providers were also given the opportunity to demonstrate their tools. CCC President Alysia Johnson and CCC Certification Architect Dr. Jörg Brakensiek are both quoted on the purpose of Plugfests in validating interoperability across the member ecosystem. The release cites ResearchInChina's Automotive Digital Key Industry Trend Report 2026: 13.3 million passenger vehicles equipped with digital keys in 2025, a 58.1% installation rate in 2025, and an expectation that market penetration will exceed 60% in China during 2026; CCC's membership is cited at over 300 companies.

## Summary (ZH)

2026年9月8日，车联网联盟（CCC）宣布由吉利控股集团主办的第19届Plugfest开幕，该活动为期四天，于2026年9月7日至10日在中国宁波举行。参与方包括苹果、中国信息通信研究院、COMPRION GmbH、DEKRA、Ellisys、吉利控股集团、高新兴物联科技（GosuncnWelink Technology）、零跑汽车（Leapmotor Technology）以及大众汽车集团（中国）科技有限公司。计划测试涵盖 CCC 数字钥匙 Version 3 与 Version 4 在蓝牙低功耗与 NFC 上的实现、无钥匙进入与被动进入、蓝牙 LE 嗅探、更新的 NFC 测试用例，以及包括经中继服务器实现跨平台钥匙共享在内的中国特有互操作场景；蓝牙 LE 与 UWB 测试设备供应商亦可借此展示其工具。CCC主席Alysia Johnson与CCC认证架构师Jörg Brakensiek博士均就Plugfest在验证会员生态系统互操作性方面的作用发表了评论。新闻稿援引 ResearchInChina《2026 汽车数字钥匙产业趋势报告》的数据：2025年中国配备数字钥匙的乘用车达1,330万辆，2025年安装率为58.1%，预计2026年中国市场渗透率将超过60%；CCC会员企业数据被引用为超过300家。
