## Summary (EN)
TGbp (Ambient Power Communication / AMP IoT) uploaded two new proposed-draft-text (PDT) contributions on 16 August 2026, both from Lumin Liu (Huawei), refreshing the two core MAC-procedure clauses of the AMP draft: an "Update-1" of the AMP Transmit Procedure PDT and an "Update-2" of the AMP Receive Procedure PDT (the receive-procedure thread is the same 40.3.14 line that ran to revision 7 during the May Antwerp interim, see 2026-05-18_ieee-mentor-tgbp-antwerp-digest). The receive-procedure document was revised once within the same evening (r0 → r1). These are the only new 802.11 Mentor documents past the previous watermark (11-26-1545); TGbn, TGbi, TGbq, TGbt and the WG-level queues were silent — the between-sessions lull ahead of the September 2026 interim continues.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
TGbp is the only 802.11 task group still producing normative text between sessions, consistent with its predicted first WG letter ballot in September 2026 (Timelines page, D0.50 current). The transmit/receive procedure clauses are the backbone that the excitation, triggering, PPDU-padding (2026-08-13_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest) and group-ack PDTs (2026-08-03_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest) hang off, so a fresh Huawei pass over both is the expected pre-ballot consolidation step. Only title and author metadata are public; the document bodies are gated, so this digest records the activity, not the content.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
TGbp 是唯一在会间仍持续产出规范文本的 802.11 任务组，与其预计 2026 年 9 月首次工作组函件投票（Timelines 页面，当前 D0.50）相符。发送/接收流程条款是激励、触发、PPDU 填充（2026-08-13_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest）与组确认（2026-08-03_ieee-mentor-tgbp-digest）等 PDT 所依附的主干，华为对两者的新一轮修订是投票前预期中的整合步骤。仅标题与作者元数据公开，文档正文受限，本摘要记录的是活动而非内容。
