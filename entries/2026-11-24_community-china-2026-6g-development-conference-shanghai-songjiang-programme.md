## Summary (EN)

A transcript of the pre-conference press briefing, published on Yicai with the Shanghai Municipal Science and Technology Commission as source, describes the 2026 6G Development Conference, held 24–25 November 2026 in Songjiang District, Shanghai. It is organised by the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT) and the IMT-2030 (6G) Promotion Group under guidance from the Ministry of Industry and Information Technology, and hosted locally by Shanghai municipal bodies and the Songjiang District government. Its theme is "智启星程，携手全球协同创新". The article says the conference has been held five times (Shanghai in 2022 and 2024, Beijing in 2025).

The format is "1+2+X+1": one opening ceremony, two main forums on the afternoon of 24 November, multiple sub-forums (12 on 25 November, across six venues in the morning and six in the afternoon), and one 6G industrial R&D exhibition. The opening ceremony is to release a 6G development concept initiative and hold a ceremony releasing results of the ministry–province 6G collaborative pilots; the conference will also share phased results of the second-stage 6G technology trials and a series of 6G research reports.

This item surfaced through an X post dated 2026-11-23 about the conference opening; the programme details here come from the Yicai report, not from that post. It is a late pickup in the sense that the announcement had been public since early November.

## Summary (ZH)

第一财经刊发的会前新闻通气会文字实录（来源：上海市科学技术委员会）介绍了 2026 年 6G 发展大会：2026 年 11 月 24–25 日在上海松江区举行，由中国信通院和 IMT-2030（6G）推进组主办，工业和信息化部指导，上海市有关部门及松江区政府承办，主题为"智启星程，携手全球协同创新"。文章称大会已举办五届（2022 年、2024 年在上海，2025 年在北京）。

大会采用"1+2+X+1"结构：1 场开幕式、11 月 24 日下午 2 场主论坛、多场分论坛（11 月 25 日共 12 场，上午、下午各 6 个会场）以及 1 场 6G 产业研发成果展览。开幕式将发布 6G 发展理念倡议，并举行 6G 创新发展部省协同试点成果发布仪式；大会还将分享第二阶段 6G 技术试验阶段性成果和 6G 系列研究报告。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Main forums (24 Nov afternoon): "6G technology convergence and new terminal innovation" and "6G technology+ innovation". Sub-forum topics listed by the article include network architecture, wireless networks, satellite-terrestrial integration, network security, 5G-A, mobile intelligent-agent networks, integrated sensing and communication, communication-intelligence integration, U6G, and 6G for real-time physical AI.
- Second-stage 6G technology trials: according to the article, MIIT approved frequency use for 6 GHz-band 6G trials in May 2026; the Beijing Huairou outdoor test site is under construction and can already test some typical 6G scenarios; 15 domestic and foreign entities take part this year. Reported result areas are 6G immersive communication, wireless intelligence, integrated sensing and communication, space-air-ground integration and 6G network architecture.
- Timeline statement in the article: the first edition of the 6G international standard is expected in the first half of 2029, with commercial readiness around 2030.
- Releases announced: Shanghai University and the Shanghai Zhilian Information Technology Research Institute will jointly release a 6G AI RAN base-station prototype system, a U6G new-spectrum dataset and a multimodal channel-measurement dataset; Shanghai will also unveil a 6G proof-of-concept platform.
- Invited speakers and bodies named: academicians Wu Hequan and Zhang Wenjun (keynotes), the ITU WP5D chair, the 3GPP RAN chair and 3GPP chair, plus EU 6G-IA, Japan XGFM, Korea 6G Forum, one6G, 5G-ACIA, GSMA, and companies including Huawei, ZTE, Ericsson, Nokia and Qualcomm.
- Industrial R&D items the article lists as released or in progress: base-station prototypes at tens of Gbps, an intelligent-agent core-network prototype, non-terrestrial network communication payloads, thousand-element-class massive antenna arrays, and U6 GHz device modules and test instruments.
