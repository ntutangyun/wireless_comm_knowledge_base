## Summary (EN)

Costa Rica has approved **full-band 6 GHz Wi-Fi access across all 1,200 MHz** (5925–7125 MHz), using Automated Frequency Coordination (AFC) for standard-power operation, matching the regulatory frameworks of the United States and Canada. The decision, reported in Wi-Fi NOW's July 20, 2026 roundup, adds another country to the full-band camp in the global 6 GHz split between full-band Wi-Fi allocation (Americas-led) and lower-band-only or IMT-reserved approaches (much of Europe pending the UK's July decision).

The strategic weight is cumulative rather than individual: each additional full-band adoption strengthens the device-ecosystem case for 6 GHz Wi-Fi (economies of scale for 6 GHz radios, AFC clients, and standard-power outdoor equipment) and adds pressure on regulators still weighing IMT reservations for the upper band.

## Summary (ZH)

哥斯达黎加批准 **6 GHz 全频段（5925–7125 MHz，共 1,200 MHz）向 Wi-Fi 开放**，标准功率运行采用自动频率协调（AFC），与美国和加拿大的监管框架一致。该决定见于 Wi-Fi NOW 2026 年 7 月 20 日综述，使全球 6 GHz 格局中"全频段 Wi-Fi 分配"阵营（美洲主导）再添一国——与之相对的是欧洲多数国家仍限于下段或为 IMT 保留上段（英国 7 月的决定除外）。

其战略意义在于累积效应：每多一个全频段采纳国，就为 6 GHz Wi-Fi 设备生态（6 GHz 射频、AFC 客户端、标准功率室外设备的规模经济）增加一分筹码，并对仍在权衡上段 IMT 保留的监管机构形成压力。
