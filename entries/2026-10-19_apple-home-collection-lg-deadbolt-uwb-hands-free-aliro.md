## Summary (EN)

On 13 October 2026 Apple announced "Apple Home Collection", a smart-home accessory lineup developed with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric. The lineup includes an LG Smart Deadbolt Lock. Apple's release says: "Using Ultra Wideband technology, the lock can tell how close a user is and when they're approaching the door," and that the door then unlocks automatically "with no tapping required" for a user holding a home key on a compatible iPhone or Apple Watch. Tap-to-unlock, an integrated PIN pad and a hidden backup key slot are also listed. The lock is Matter compatible and supports Aliro, and the release states that core access functions work locally without an internet connection; "every device supports Thread". The release says the collection is available from December 2026, with select accessories from early 2027, and does not say which group the lock falls into. Wi-Fi NOW's 18 October roundup picked the item up as a sign of Apple backing UWB for secure home access.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Product: LG Smart Deadbolt Lock, part of Apple Home Collection (Apple with LG Electronics and Schneider Electric), announced 13 October 2026.
- UWB role per Apple's release: the lock determines how close a user is and when they approach the door, triggering automatic unlock with no tap.
- Other access methods listed: tap-to-unlock with iPhone or Apple Watch, integrated PIN pad, hidden backup key slot; guest access shared through the Home app.
- Standards: Matter compatible, supports Aliro (the release gives no further Aliro detail); core access functions work locally without internet; every device in the lineup supports Thread.
- Availability: collection from December 2026, select accessories from early 2027; the release lists no price for the lock.
- Not stated in the release: UWB chip, ranging accuracy, protocol/FiRa version, or whether UWB access follows Aliro's UWB profile.
