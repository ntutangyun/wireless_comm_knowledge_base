## Summary (EN)
Wi-Fi NOW's 9 Aug 2026 roundup (Claus Hetting) relays Dell'Oro Group's July 2026 Wireless LAN 5-year forecast: enterprise Wi-Fi 7 infrastructure is projected to post **triple-digit growth in 2026**, surpassing the entire lifecycle revenue of Wi-Fi 6E, with Wi-Fi 7 expansion persisting for roughly three more years while AIOps drives WLAN software revenues upward.

The forecast's most interesting supply-side argument comes from Senior Director Siân Morgan: AP vendors can redesign Wi-Fi 8 hardware to reduce dependency on expensive memory components, and that bill-of-materials relief creates a competitive-pricing incentive to move to Wi-Fi 8 sooner than the standards cadence alone would suggest. In other words, the DRAM/flash cost spike of 2025–26 may act as an *accelerant* for early Wi-Fi 8 product cycles rather than a brake on them.
