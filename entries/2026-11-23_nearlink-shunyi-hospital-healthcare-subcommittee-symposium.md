## Summary (EN)

The alliance's own news feed (posted 2026-11-21) reports that on 19 November the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) expert committee's Healthcare and Wellness sub-committee held a SparkLink application-cooperation symposium with Beijing Shunyi District Hospital. The stated aim was to discuss real medical-scenario needs and bottlenecks, SparkLink's differentiated advantages and future application scenarios, in order to convert the technology into clinical use for intra-operative response, ward monitoring and device interconnection.

Participants named in the report include the sub-committee director Hong Lei, iSLA deputy secretary-general Gao Hong (CAICT TAF terminal lab), the iSLA Smart Healthcare SIG deputy lead Zhang Guangdao (Lepu Medical), Huawei's SparkLink standards chief expert Liu Hang, adviser Liang Xingang (Yuanxiaowei Technology), and the hospital's vice-president Ma Zhaofeng with heads of its medical affairs, nursing, information, research and medical-engineering departments. Hospital staff raised concrete scenario needs; the topics discussed were in-hospital patient navigation, infusion management, mobile-device positioning and anti-loss, fall sensing, vital-sign monitoring, mobile nursing and ward device interconnection, together with data security, medical-device registration, retrofit of existing equipment and tiered rollout. Both sides agreed on the principles "demand-driven, safety first, small steps, iterative validation" and to start with scenarios that are low-risk, clinically urgent and have relatively short approval cycles, and to prepare network infrastructure and data-platform integration in step with the timeline of the hospital's new campus.

Gao Hong proposed a device-edge-cloud staged path: on the device side, first identify observation devices that are clinically urgent and quickly convertible to SparkLink; on the network side, explore infrastructure experiments such as an "all-optical SparkLink gateway" using dedicated spectrum to improve communication security and reliability; on the platform side, promote data aggregation as a basis for later AI-assisted diagnosis and hospital management. Products involving medical-device registration will follow review and approval requirements, with vendors organised for technical validation and registration preparation. Hong Lei suggested the two sides could sign a memorandum of cooperation and start pilots from a scenario/task list; the vice-president said the hospital would designate a lead department and contact persons and set up regular communication.

The report also describes the sub-committee itself: it was formally established at the 2026 iSLA Industry Summit, focuses on medical, rehabilitation and elderly care, and operates through four mechanisms (co-built laboratories, topic groups, an expert panel and a demand library). The plan stated in the report is for the first topics and joint laboratories to take effect in 2027.

## Summary (ZH)

联盟官网动态（2026年11月21日发布）报道：11月19日，国际星闪联盟专家委员会医疗康养分委会与北京市顺义区医院联合召开星闪技术应用合作座谈会，围绕各类医疗场景的实际需求与瓶颈、星闪赋能医疗的差异化优势、未来应用场景等展开研讨，旨在打通星闪技术与临床应用的转化通道，实现术中响应、病区监护、设备互联等场景的无线化升级。

报道列出的参会人员包括分委会主任洪磊、联盟副秘书长高宏（中国信通院泰尔终端实验室）、智慧医疗SIG副组长张光道（乐普医疗）、华为星闪标准首席专家刘航、联盟顾问梁欣刚（元小为科技），以及顺义区医院副院长马兆峰及医务、护理、信息、科研、医工等处室负责人。医院方提出了具体场景需求，讨论议题包括患者院内导航、输液管理、移动设备定位与防丢失、跌倒感知、生命体征监测、移动护理、病房设备互联，并就数据安全、医疗器械注册、存量设备改造、场景分级实施交换了意见。双方一致认为应坚持“需求导向、安全第一、小步快跑、迭代验证”，优先选择风险可控、临床迫切、审批周期相对较短的场景先行验证，并结合医院新院区建设节点做好网络基础设施与数据平台对接准备。

高宏提出按“端、边、云”分层分阶段推进：端侧优先鉴别临床迫切、可快速星闪化的观测设备；网络侧探索全光星闪网关等基础设施实验，利用专用频谱资源提升通信安全可靠性；平台侧推动数据汇聚，为后续AI辅助诊疗和医院管理提供基础。涉及医疗器械注册的产品将遵循审评审批要求，同步组织厂商开展技术验证和注册准备。洪磊建议双方可签署合作备忘录，从场景清单和任务清单中开展试点；马兆峰副院长表示医院将明确牵头部门与对接责任人，建立常态化沟通机制。

报道还介绍了分委会本身：其在2026国际星闪联盟产业峰会上正式成立，聚焦医疗、康复、养老三大方向，采用“共建实验室、课题组运作、专家组、需求库”四位一体运行机制；按规划，首批课题与联合实验室将于2027年落地见效。
