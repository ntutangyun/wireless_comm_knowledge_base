## Summary (EN)

On 16 November 2026 (ET, 02:51) Jiajia Huang (Huawei) uploaded DCN 15-26-0536r0, "IG FG UWB 2026 Nov Plenary Mins", the minutes of the IEEE 802.15 Interest Group Future Generation UWB meeting at the November 2026 plenary (hybrid mode). The body was downloaded and read. The IG chair, Wenzheng Li (Calterah Semiconductor), opened the meeting at 4:00 PM with more than 20 attendees and adjourned at 5:50 PM; the agenda (15-26-0468-00-fguw) passed by unanimous consent with no changes. The attendance list records 51 attendees in IMAT for the Monday 9 November PM2 session. These minutes are the group's own record; the closing report and opening deck (DCN 506, 468r1) were digested on 2026-11-11, and the minutes add the discussion and survey counts.

Scope and objectives: during discussion (slide 18 of 15-26-0468-00), a comment held that a CSD and PAR should be the scope of a study group, not an interest group, and that the IG's purpose should be to provide evidence that requirements of emerging applications cannot be met by existing standards. Motion #1, to approve the IG FG UWB scope and objectives in 15-26-0468-01 (moved Clint Chaplin, seconded Ben [Rolfe]), passed by unanimous consent.

Contributions: DCN 15-26-0464-00, "Enhanced UWB One-Way Ranging Advertisement (EOWRA)": a question asked why existing one-to-many solutions in 4ab cannot solve the problem and what the novelty points are; the answer recorded is that existing solutions result in longer latency while the proposed solution does not need to establish a link and is therefore faster. A suggestion was made to check existing publications on vehicle accident prevention. A question on slide 8 about how higher RF penetration can be achieved was answered "one possible way is to increase the power of the ADV, or by other technologies". An informal survey recorded 2 in the room and 1 online in favour; the follow-up item calls for more discussion in follow-up contributions on motivation, gaps and scenarios. DCN 15-26-0463-00, "UWB evolution directions beyond 4ab": no discussion was heard; an informal survey recorded 6 in the room and 2 online in favour of the topics.

PAR and CSD: the chair presented the initial draft PAR (15-26-0465-00) and CSD (15-26-0466-00). Comments recorded: "a good point to start with", and that PAR and CSD are more relevant to a study group, with an IG's scope being to show the issues, what needs improving and theoretical proof of the gaps; the reply recorded is that the two documents were prepared as a starting point to iterate before study-group formation. Motion #2, to approve the draft PAR and draft CSD, is recorded as "Motion not considered". The activity summary (15-26-0467-00) was confirmed by unanimous consent, and the chair's proposed working method (teleconferences, reflector, contribution process, interim work, timeline) met no objection. The chair called for more contributions.

范围与目标：讨论中（15-26-0468-00 第 18 页）有意见认为 CSD 与 PAR 应属于研究组（study group）而非兴趣组的范围，兴趣组的目的应是证明新兴应用的需求无法由现有标准满足。动议 #1，批准 15-26-0468-01 中的 IG FG UWB 范围与目标（Clint Chaplin 提出，Ben [Rolfe] 附议），以一致同意通过。

贡献：DCN 15-26-0464-00《Enhanced UWB One-Way Ranging Advertisement (EOWRA)》：有人提问 4ab 中现有的一对多方案为何不能解决该问题、创新点是什么；记录的回答是现有方案时延更长，而所提方案无需建立链路，因此更快。有人建议查阅关于车辆事故预防的已有文献。关于第 8 页如何获得更高的射频穿透能力，回答为“一种可能方式是提高 ADV 的功率，或采用其他技术”。非正式调查记录为现场 2 人、线上 1 人支持；后续事项要求在后续贡献中进一步说明动机、差距与场景。DCN 15-26-0463-00《UWB evolution directions beyond 4ab》：无人讨论；非正式调查记录为现场 6 人、线上 2 人支持这些主题。

PAR 与 CSD：主席介绍了 PAR 初稿（15-26-0465-00）与 CSD 初稿（15-26-0466-00）。记录的意见有：“是一个好的起点”，以及 PAR 与 CSD 与研究组更相关，兴趣组的范围是说明存在哪些问题、哪些需要改进并从理论上证明差距；记录的答复是这两份文档是作为起点，在研究组成立前反复迭代。动议 #2（批准 PAR 初稿与 CSD 初稿）记录为“Motion not considered”（未予审议）。活动摘要（15-26-0467-00）获一致同意确认；主席提出的工作方式（电话会议、邮件列表、贡献流程、会间工作、时间线）无人反对。主席呼吁提交更多贡献。
