## Summary (EN)

The Federal Register published the synopsis of the FCC's Notice of Proposed Rulemaking "Unleashing the Power of Unlicensed Ultra-Wideband Devices" (47 CFR Part 15; ET Docket No. 26-245; RM-11844; RM-12014; FCC 26-66) on Monday 2 November 2026 (91 FR 68912, document 2026-22337, pages 68912-68925). The notice states that comments are due on or before 2 December 2026 and reply comments on or before 4 January 2027; it also gives 4 January 2027 as the deadline for Paperwork Reduction Act comments. The Commission adopted the NPRM on 30 September 2026 and released it on 1 October 2026; the full text is at docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-26-66A1.pdf. Earlier KB entries covered the 9 September circulated draft and the 30 September adoption, and noted that the Federal Register date, which sets the comment clocks, had not been confirmed. This notice supplies it. The proceeding is treated as "permit-but-disclose" for ex parte purposes, and filings go through the FCC's ECFS under the docket number.

The published synopsis summarises the Commission's rationale: the 2002 UWB framework has not kept pace with technology, producing a growing number of waiver requests, and the NPRM seeks comment on modernising the rules while protecting federal and non-federal incumbents. It recounts the Bosch petition (filed 18 June 2019) and the joint petition filed on 18 December 2025 by the Alliance for Automotive Innovation, the Car Connectivity Consortium, the FiRa Consortium, PI/omlox and the Ultra-Wideband Alliance.

The synopsis lists the proposals in these groups. (1) The UWB transmitter definition: a tentative conclusion to remove the "at any point in time" wording, so that frequency-hopping, stepped, swept and other time-varying waveforms that momentarily fall outside the instantaneous bandwidth threshold are not excluded, with a request for coexistence studies, including on whether instantaneous bandwidths below 500 MHz could raise instantaneous power spectral density and affect services such as ARNS, RNSS/GNSS, MSS, radar altimeters and weather radar. (2) Measurement procedures: updating sections 15.31(c) and 15.521(d) so that swept, stepped and pulse-gated devices can be measured in normal operating mode, a rule the Commission says the 2004 MBOA-SIG waiver set up but was never codified. (3) A new UWB device category for compact devices, mobile or fixed, indoor or outdoor, aimed at the repeated waiver requests for outdoor keyless entry and door locks, vehicle and rail safety ranging and other precision tracking; proposed safeguards include height limits, mounting restrictions, downward-directed emissions, transmission only during event-driven sessions, no wide-area networks and duty cycles under one percent in 24 hours, with comment sought on the duty-cycle averaging window, emission limits, PRF and dwell-time limits. (4) Comment on whether to create a further class for AI-enabled UWB sensing applications. (5) A review of all existing categories (including ground-penetrating radar, wall and through-wall imaging, and indoor devices) for consolidation, simplification or removal of redundant provisions. The notice also asks how the updated definition aligns with standards-body work such as the IEEE's.

## Summary (ZH)

联邦公报于 2026 年 11 月 2 日（周一）刊登了 FCC 拟制规则通知（NPRM）“Unleashing the Power of Unlicensed Ultra-Wideband Devices”的摘要（47 CFR Part 15；ET Docket No. 26-245；RM-11844；RM-12014；FCC 26-66），引用为 91 FR 68912，文件号 2026-22337，第 68912-68925 页。通知载明：意见须在 2026 年 12 月 2 日或之前提交，回复意见须在 2027 年 1 月 4 日或之前提交；《文书削减法》相关意见的截止日同为 2027 年 1 月 4 日。该 NPRM 由委员会于 2026 年 9 月 30 日通过、10 月 1 日发布；全文见 docs.fcc.gov/public/attachments/FCC-26-66A1.pdf。KB 此前的条目涵盖了 9 月 9 日的传阅草案和 9 月 30 日的通过，并注明决定意见期起点的联邦公报日期尚未确认；本通知补上了这一信息。该程序在 ex parte 规则下按“允许但须披露”（permit-but-disclose）处理，意见通过 FCC 的 ECFS 系统按该档案号提交。

已刊登的摘要概述了委员会的理由：2002 年的 UWB 框架未能跟上技术发展，导致豁免申请越来越多；NPRM 就在保护联邦与非联邦在位用户的同时现代化规则征求意见。摘要回顾了博世（Bosch）2019 年 6 月 18 日提交的请愿，以及 2025 年 12 月 18 日由 Alliance for Automotive Innovation、Car Connectivity Consortium、FiRa Consortium、PI/omlox 和 Ultra-Wideband Alliance 共同提交的联合请愿。

摘要将提议分为几组。（1）UWB 发射机定义：初步结论是删去“at any point in time”字样，使跳频、步进、扫频及其他时变波形不会因瞬时带宽短暂低于门限而被排除，并要求提供共存研究，包括瞬时带宽低于 500 MHz 是否会抬高瞬时功率谱密度并影响 ARNS、RNSS/GNSS、MSS、雷达高度计和气象雷达等业务。（2）测量程序：更新 15.31(c) 与 15.521(d)，使扫频、步进和脉冲门控设备可在正常工作模式下测量；委员会称这一做法源自 2004 年 MBOA-SIG 豁免，但从未被写入规则。（3）新增 UWB 设备类别：面向紧凑型、可移动或固定、室内或室外设备，针对户外无钥匙进入和门锁、车辆与轨道安全测距及其他精确跟踪等反复出现的豁免申请；拟议的保护措施包括高度限制、安装限制、向下发射、仅在事件驱动的会话期间发射、不得组成广域网，以及 24 小时内占空比低于 1%，并就占空比平均窗口、发射限值、PRF 和驻留时间限制征求意见。（4）就是否为 AI 赋能的 UWB 感知应用另设类别征求意见。（5）审查所有现有类别（包括探地雷达、墙体与穿墙成像、室内设备），考虑合并、简化或删除冗余条款。通知还询问更新后的定义与 IEEE 等标准组织的工作如何衔接。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Federal Register: 91 FR 68912, 2 November 2026, proposed rule, document 2026-22337; comments due 2 December 2026, replies due 4 January 2027; adopted 30 September, released 1 October 2026 (FCC 26-66).
- Transmitter definition: tentative conclusion to remove "at any point in time"; coexistence studies requested on instantaneous bandwidths below 500 MHz and incumbent services (ARNS, RNSS/GNSS, MSS, radar altimeters, weather radar).
- Measurement: sections 15.31(c) and 15.521(d) to be updated so swept, stepped and pulse-gated devices can be measured in normal operating mode.
- New device category: compact mobile or fixed indoor/outdoor devices with height and mounting limits, downward emissions, event-driven transmission only, no wide-area networks and duty cycle under 1% per 24 hours; comment sought on duty-cycle window, emission limits, PRF and dwell time.
- Open questions: a separate class for AI-enabled UWB sensing, and a review of the existing GPR, wall-imaging, through-wall and indoor categories.
- Status: a proposal only; the notice does not change the rules, and existing Part 15 Subpart F rules remain in force during the comment period.
