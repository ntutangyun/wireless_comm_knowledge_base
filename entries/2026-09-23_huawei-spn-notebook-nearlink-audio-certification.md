## Summary (EN)

Huawei's official NearLink compatibility test/certification listing now shows two new, unreleased notebook models — codenamed **SPN-W52** and **SPN-KB01** — that have passed **NearLink Audio** certification. The finding was reported by Huawei Central / HC Newsroom (huaweicentral.com; X post by @HCNewsroom, 2026-09-22), an independent outlet not affiliated with Huawei that tracks NearLink certificates (it has previously reported NearLink certificates on Huawei tablets and other HarmonyOS PCs, e.g. the 'SLM'-codenamed PC line). No other specifications for the SPN series are disclosed by the certification listing; the outlet suggests the notebooks could launch in Q4 2026, potentially alongside the Mate 90 series, though neither date is confirmed by Huawei.

NearLink Audio (NearLink E2.0, on the SLE access layer) is Huawei's low-latency wireless audio link, already shipping on devices such as the Huawei FreeBuds 7 (this KB's 2026-08-27 entry) and third-party hardware like the Edifier Comfo Clip Q2 (2026-08-18 entry). Per Huawei Central, the certificate indicates compatibility with Huawei's FreeBuds series. A notebook clearing NearLink Audio certification extends the audio-source side of NearLink beyond phones and the MatePad Pro Max tablet.
