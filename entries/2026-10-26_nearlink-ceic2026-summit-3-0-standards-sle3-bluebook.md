## Summary (EN)

CITNews reported on 25 October 2026 on the CEIC2026 / International SparkLink Alliance Industry Summit held on 22 October at the Shenzhen Convention & Exhibition Center (Futian) under the theme "SparkLink AI, Connecting Everything". According to the report, the summit released the SparkLink 3.0 standards, 15 recommended national standards, and a blue book on short-range connectivity requirements for embodied intelligence.

Alliance chairman Shi Jinglin said that SparkLink 1.0 defined the communication architecture and 2.0 established end-to-end integrated communication, positioning, sensing and networking; 3.0 targets all-scenario intelligent IoT, with continued innovation in AI, passive and millimetre-wave directions. Since the 3.0 effort started in August 2025, three standards, including lossless audio, have been formally published and two, including drone operation identification, are about to be submitted for review, with many more in preparation. Shanghai HiSilicon COO Zhi Hao said SLE 3.0 will be upgraded in rate, coverage and interference resistance; a "SparkLink SLE 3.0 Technology Standard Evolution Blue Book", compiled and published by more than 60 organisations from industry, academia and research, accompanies this.

The report also describes a ceremony witnessing the release of 15 recommended national standards for SparkLink. In a talk on the use of SparkLink in the 360-degree surround-view mandatory national standard, a researcher from the Research Institute of Highway (Ministry of Transport) said that wireless SLB links can replace wiring harnesses for multi-camera backhaul on semi-trailers, tractors and trailer combinations; that standard has completed expert review, is in the approval stage, and is expected to be published at the end of this year.

## Summary (ZH)

CITNews 于 2026 年 10 月 25 日报道，10 月 22 日以“星闪AI·智合万物”为主题的 CEIC2026 暨国际星闪联盟产业峰会在深圳会展中心（福田）举行。报道称，会上集中发布了星闪3.0标准、15项推荐性国家标准以及《星闪短距与具身智能技术需求蓝皮书》。

联盟理事长石晶林介绍，星闪1.0定义了通信架构，2.0打通了通信、定位、感知、网络一体化的端到端能力，3.0面向全场景智能物联，在AI、无源、毫米波等方向持续创新。3.0标准自2025年8月启动以来，已正式发布无损音频等三项标准，无人机运行识别等两项标准即将送审，另有大量标准在制定中。上海海思COO支浩表示，星闪SLE 3.0将在速率、覆盖、抗干扰等方面升级；由产学研60余家单位共同编制并发布的《星闪SLE 3.0技术标准演进蓝皮书》将推动3.0标准走深走实。

报道还描述了15项星闪推荐性国家标准的发布见证仪式。在介绍星闪技术用于360全景环视强制性国家标准时，交通运输部公路科学研究院的研究员表示，借助星闪宽带（SLB）可替代半挂车、牵引车及列车多路摄像头的线束回传；该强制性国家标准已完成审定、进入报批阶段，预计年底发布。
