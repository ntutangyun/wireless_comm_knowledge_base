## Summary (EN)

In a press release dated 20 October 2026 (Taipei), ASUS announced that the ZenWiFi BN12, which it describes as "the world's first WiFi 8 mesh system" and which was first shown at Computex, is now shipping. The release lists the hardware as tri-band with speeds of up to 19 Gbps (no per-band breakdown is given), two 10G Ethernet ports and three 2.5G Ethernet ports, and coverage of up to 7,000 sq ft for the two-pack configuration.

The Wi-Fi 8 (IEEE 802.11bn) features the release names are new Modulation and Coding Scheme (MCS) rates, Unequal Modulation (UEQM), Non-Primary Channel Access (NPCA) and Multi-Access Point Coordination. ASUS states that Wi-Fi 8 "emphasizes ultra-high reliability" rather than peak speed alone. On the software side the release lists Smart AiMesh (mesh coordination and backhaul optimisation), Adaptive QoE, which ASUS says adjusts resource priorities dynamically from real-time traffic and application demand rather than from "predefined classifications and static priorities", WiFi Insight (full-spectrum interference detection, network visualisation, channel switching and a 24-hour history log), and network protection listed as commercial-grade AiProtection, security scanning, Safe Browsing, Triple DNS Guard, advanced parental controls and VPN support.

The release gives no regional availability, pricing or SKU details and no per-band speed breakdown; it refers readers to the product page. The "world's first" wording is ASUS's own claim and is relayed here as such.
