## Summary (EN)

HPE announced it will return as a Worldwide Supplier for the 2027 Ryder Cup, held 17-19 September 2027 at Adare Manor in County Limerick, Ireland -- the event's 100th anniversary. The announcement states HPE will deploy an "HPE Self-Driving Network" using Wi-Fi 7 across the 230-acre venue, spanning the golf course, grandstands, hospitality venues, media compounds, and operations centers, to keep fans, volunteers, media, staff, systems, and devices connected during tournament week; the network is described as continuously monitoring, adapting, and optimizing user experience in real time. HPE will also expand its Connected Intelligence Center -- first introduced at the 2025 Ryder Cup -- powered by HPE Private Cloud AI and developed by HPE Services, bringing together data, AI models, and tools from across the tournament's operational systems into a single operations view. The announcement frames the relationship as continuing one that began at the 2018 Ryder Cup in Paris, and notes that over 250,000 fans are expected on site and over 650 million viewers worldwide will be able to follow the event.
