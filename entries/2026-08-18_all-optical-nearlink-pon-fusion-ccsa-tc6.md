## Summary (EN)

At the 107th meeting of the CCSA TC6 (transport and access network) WG2 (access network and home network) working group, held July 21-23, 2026 in Tengchong, Yunnan, a brainstorming session on "optical access networks converging with embodied intelligence and home agents" brought together CAICT, China Telecom / Mobile / Unicom, Huawei, ZTE, FiberHome, Qualcomm and Nokia Shanghai Bell. Huawei expert Zheng Jianyu presented "The all-optical NearLink (PON x NearLink) innovation architecture enabling new smart-home and smart-enterprise scenarios" (report published by the International SparkLink Alliance on August 11). The pitch: agentic-AI workloads with device-edge-cloud collaboration impose extreme determinism and security demands that neither Wi-Fi-over-PON nor wired-only access satisfies. "All-optical NearLink" fuses NearLink deeply with the PON access layer through a **unified clock synchronisation** across the optical and wireless segments and **global cross-domain bandwidth scheduling**, producing an optical+wireless "TDM hard pipe" that combines wired determinism with wireless convenience.

Capabilities claimed for the architecture: millisecond-class deterministic access, lossless service roaming between NearLink cells, frame/packet-level flexible scheduling, end-to-end security, and network-wide integrated communication-sensing-positioning ("通感定一体化") — the properties Huawei says let the network self-organise around AI workloads. Target domains listed are government/enterprise offices, smart grid, intelligent manufacturing and smart home, via high-security dedicated-line, NearLink-vision linkage and device-edge-cloud collaborative network offerings. A live "all-optical NearLink + embodied intelligence" demo was shown, and the participating experts called on standards bodies to coordinate on an all-optical NearLink architecture and related technical standards.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Architecture: NearLink radio (SLB-class high-throughput/low-latency mode implied by the TDM framing) integrated with the PON access layer; the wireless hop is scheduled as an extension of the optical TDM domain rather than as an independent contention-based cell.
- Two enabling mechanisms named: (1) unified clock synchronisation across PON and NearLink segments; (2) global, cross-domain bandwidth scheduling — together forming an optical+wireless "TDM hard pipe".
- Claimed properties: millisecond-class deterministic access; lossless roaming across NearLink cells; frame/packet-granular scheduling; end-to-end security; network-wide integrated communication + sensing + positioning.
- Motivation: agentic AI / embodied intelligence with device-edge-cloud collaboration; determinism and security as the binding constraints.
- Application targets: government/enterprise office, smart grid, intelligent manufacturing, smart home; solution forms: high-security dedicated line, NearLink-vision linkage, device-edge-cloud collaborative network.
- Venue: CCSA TC6 WG2 #107, Tengchong, 2026-07-21..23; multi-operator/multi-vendor audience incl. Qualcomm and Nokia; demo shown; explicit call for standards work.
- No numeric latency/jitter/throughput figures disclosed in the write-up.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the first appearance in this KB of NearLink as an *access-network* element rather than a device-to-device or device-to-phone link: Huawei is proposing that the NearLink air interface be time-synchronised to, and scheduled together with, the PON TDM domain — effectively treating the wireless last metre as one more slot-scheduled segment of the fibre network. That is a different architectural bet from the Wi-Fi-over-PON (FTTR) model that CCSA TC6 WG2 has standardised for home networks, and from the Bluetooth/UWB roles NearLink competes with elsewhere in this KB. The venue matters: WG2 is the body behind China's FTTR and home-network standards, and the audience included Qualcomm and Nokia, so this is a bid to bring NearLink into operator-grade access standardisation, not just the iSLA/HarmonyOS device ecosystem. It also gives NearLink's SLB mode — which has had far fewer public applications than SLE (car keys, audio, HID) — a concrete role, and connects the "通感定一体" (comms-sensing-positioning) capability from the GB/T 46803 standard entries in this KB to a network-level architecture. Caveats: this is a single-vendor keynote write-up with no performance figures, no reference to a study item or draft, and no operator commitment; the standards call is aspirational. Watch items: a CCSA TC6 study item or an iSLA "all-optical NearLink" specification, and any FTTR product that exposes a NearLink port alongside Wi-Fi 7.

这是星闪首次在本知识库中以**接入网要素**而非设备间/设备-手机链路的身份出现：华为提议让星闪空口与 PON 的 TDM 域时钟同步并统一调度——实际上把无线"最后一米"当作光纤网络的又一段时隙调度链路。这与 CCSA TC6 WG2 已标准化的家庭网络 Wi-Fi-over-PON（FTTR）模型是不同的架构路线，也不同于星闪在本库其他条目中与蓝牙/UWB 竞争的角色。会议场合很关键：WG2 是中国 FTTR 与家庭网络标准的归口工作组，且听众包括高通与诺基亚，因此这是把星闪引入运营商级接入网标准化的尝试，而不只是 iSLA/鸿蒙设备生态。它同时为星闪 SLB 模式——公开应用远少于 SLE（车钥匙、音频、HID）——提供了具体角色，并把本库 GB/T 46803 标准条目中的"通感定一体"能力与网络级架构关联起来。注意事项：单一厂商演讲纪要，无性能数据，未提及研究课题或草案，无运营商承诺；标准化呼吁尚属愿景。关注点：CCSA TC6 研究课题或 iSLA "全光星闪"规范立项；是否出现在 Wi-Fi 7 之外暴露星闪接口的 FTTR 产品。
