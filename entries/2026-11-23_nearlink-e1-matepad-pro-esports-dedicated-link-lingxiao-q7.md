## Summary (EN)

A report dated 2026-11-13 (IT之家, carried on ifeng's Dafeng Hao self-media platform on 2026-11-14) says Huawei MatePad Pro 13.2 (2023) and other tablets have received HarmonyOS 7.0.0.111 SP16, which adds NearLink's "e-sports dedicated link" (星闪电竞专链). With a compatible Huawei router the feature sets up a dedicated NearLink connection that the article says improves game smoothness and stability. The router named is the Huawei Lingxiao parent-child router Q7 (wired edition, 凌霄子母路由 Q7 电线版), which the article says was launched on 2026-06-01 at the nova 16 series event; the router's NearLink e-sports link is described as supporting direct connection from Huawei phones and tablets and as lowering the game network stutter rate compared with traditional Wi-Fi (a vendor claim without figures in the report).

The article notes that the NearLink connection depends on Wi-Fi: a NearLink-capable phone cannot connect to a NearLink router unless it is also connected to that router's Wi-Fi network. Huawei's site lists the MatePad Pro 13.2 (2023) as supporting NearLink E1.0, and the article concludes from this that more first-generation NearLink devices will also support the feature.
