## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-08, TGbp (Ambient Power Communication, AMP) uploaded three PHY-layer proposed-draft-text (PDT) documents continuing its baseline drafting: a parameter definition for the TRIGVECTOR/PHYCONFIG_VECTOR, a document defining AMP frame elements, and a document specifying PHY TXVECTOR/RXVECTOR fields. Separately, TGbn (802.11bn, Ultra High Reliability) uploaded an Editor's Report on its D2.0 draft, an administrative status document rather than new technical content.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

TGbp's three PHY documents extend the PDU/frame-format drafting thread this KB has tracked since `2026-08-29_ieee-mentor-tgbp-preamble-pdt-digest` (preamble PDT) and `2026-09-07_ieee-mentor-multi-group-digest` (transmitter block diagram, DCN 1541): TRIGVECTOR/PHYCONFIG_VECTOR parameters, frame elements, and TXVECTOR/RXVECTOR fields are the PHY-MAC interface primitives that the group's earlier block-diagram and padding work (DCN 1537, 1541) depend on. DCN 1543 was first uploaded on 07-Sep-2026 (r0, 13:24 ET) and revised to r1 on 08-Sep-2026. TGbn's Editor's Report is a process document tied to the D2.0 letter-ballot recirculation (LB296, 77% per `2026-09-04_ieee-tgbn-d20-lb296-recirc-77pct`); it carries no new numeric checkpoint beyond what the 2026-09-08 Timelines re-check already confirmed.
