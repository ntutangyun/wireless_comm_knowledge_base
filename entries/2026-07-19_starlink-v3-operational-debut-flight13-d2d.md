## Summary (EN)

SpaceX scheduled Starship Flight 13 to lift off from Starbase (Boca Chica, Texas) as early as July 16, 2026 — the **operational debut** of the next-generation Starlink V3 satellite infrastructure, following Flight 12's test deployment in early July. The manifest carries 20 prototype V3 satellites, six of them fitted with external camera housings to collect thermal-tracking data during atmospheric reentry.

The V3 numbers define the new capacity baseline: **1 Tbps downlink per satellite** (a tenfold improvement over V2 Mini), **160 Gbps uplink per node**, at roughly 1,500 kg per fully integrated unit — a satellite sized for Starship's fairing and cadence. Architecturally, V3 combines a dense mesh of high-capacity optical laser inter-satellite links (real-time routing independent of regional ground stations) with on-board edge computing, aimed squarely at satellite-to-phone connectivity for unmodified handsets. SatNews cites market projections that SpaceX could capture more than 50% of the emerging direct-to-phone connectivity market by 2028.

The same news cycle saw Starlink's direct-to-cell service announced for Italy with Fastweb and Vodafone — standard phones connecting to LEO satellites where terrestrial coverage ends — extending the carrier-partnership model already tracked in the KB (2026-07-03_starlink-mobile-retail-carrier-plans).

## Summary (ZH)

SpaceX 计划星舰 Flight 13 最早于 2026 年 7 月 16 日从德州 Starbase（Boca Chica）升空——这是下一代星链 V3 卫星基础设施的**运营首秀**，紧随 7 月初 Flight 12 的测试部署。本次载荷为 20 颗 V3 原型卫星，其中 6 颗配备外部相机舱，用于收集再入大气层时的热追踪数据。

V3 的参数定义了新的容量基线：**单星下行 1 Tbps**（较 V2 Mini 提升十倍）、**单节点上行 160 Gbps**，整星质量约 1,500 千克——是为星舰整流罩与发射节奏量身定制的卫星。架构上，V3 将高容量激光星间链路（ISL）密集网状组网（实时路由，不依赖区域地面站）与星上边缘计算结合，目标直指普通手机的卫星直连。SatNews 引用市场预测称，SpaceX 到 2028 年可能占据新兴直连手机市场 50% 以上份额。

同一新闻周期内，星链宣布与 Fastweb、Vodafone 合作在意大利推出直连手机服务——普通手机在地面网络覆盖不到之处接入 LEO 卫星——延续了 KB 已追踪的运营商合作模式（2026-07-03_starlink-mobile-retail-carrier-plans）。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Flight 13 (NET July 16, 2026, Starbase): operational debut of Starlink V3; 20 prototype V3 satellites, 6 with reentry thermal-tracking camera housings.
- V3 per-satellite capacity: 1 Tbps downlink (10× V2 Mini), 160 Gbps uplink per node; ~1,500 kg fully integrated.
- Dense optical laser ISL mesh enables real-time routing independent of regional ground stations; on-board edge computing.
- Direct-to-device: satellite-to-phone on unmodified handsets is the design target; >50% direct-to-phone market share projected by 2028.
- Starlink direct-to-cell lands in Italy via Fastweb and Vodafone partnerships.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The KB's 2026-07-05_starship-flight12-starlink-v3-test-deploy entry recorded V3's *test* deployment; Flight 13 moves V3 to *operational* status and, more importantly, frames V3 as the hardware substrate of a direct-to-device constellation matrix — laser-ISL routing plus edge compute as the enabler of phone-direct service at scale, not just backhaul capacity growth. Combined with the Italy carrier launch, the direct-to-cell business is visibly transitioning from per-country experiments to constellation-level architecture. Watch: whether Flight 13 actually flew on July 16 and deployment success, V3 ramp cadence on subsequent Starship flights, and how Amazon Leo's Globalstar-derived Band 53/n53 direct-to-device play answers.
