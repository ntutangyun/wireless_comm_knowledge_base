## Summary (EN)

According to C114 (12 Nov 2026), China Mobile formally released a self-developed CIS-RAN "communication-intelligence integrated" small-cell prototype at the 2026 Mobile Network Conference, citing information disclosed by China Mobile Research Institute. The prototype is described as a three-layer (hardware, software, operations) architecture for the AI era. On the hardware side, a communication-intelligence integrated base station works together with an "intelligent-compute extension unit": the base-station BBU integrates domestically produced AI compute chips, and the extension unit supports on-demand compute expansion and sharing across several stations. Two directions are named: AI4RAN (AI enhancing wireless performance and sensing) and RAN4AI (the network carrying AI services with large capacity, low latency and deterministic delivery), plus a wireless operations agent driven by natural language for self-provisioning, self-operation and self-optimisation.

C114 also reports that an earlier CIS-RAN "wireless brain" system, built around a communication-intelligence integrated base station and developed with China Mobile Zijin (Jiangsu) Innovation Institute, won a bid for the compute network and "bodyless" data-collection facility of the Suzhou Embodied-Intelligence Robot Innovation Center, covering wireless collection, edge inference and multi-robot verification. Link computed over: cellular base-station access network (the related forum talk frames the work as 6G-oriented); this is a prototype announcement relayed by trade press, not a measured result.

## Summary (ZH)

据 C114（2026 年 11 月 12 日）报道，中国移动在"2026 移动网络大会"上正式发布自研的 CIS-RAN 通智一体小站样机，信息来自中国移动研究院披露。样机面向 AI 时代网络演进，采用硬件、软件、运维三层通智融合架构。硬件上，通智一体基站与"智算扩展单元"协同：基站 BBU 集成国产智算芯片，扩展单元支持算力按需扩容并可在多站之间共享。报道点出两个方向：AI4RAN（AI 增强无线性能与感知能力）与 RAN4AI（网络以大容量、低时延、确定性方式承载 AI 业务），以及支持自然语言驱动自开通、自运维、自优化的无线运维智能体。

C114 还提到，此前以通智一体基站为核心、由中国移动研究院与紫金（江苏）创新研究院联合研发的 CIS-RAN 无线智脑系统已中标苏州市具身智能机器人创新中心的算力网络及"无本体"数据采集设施项目，贯通无线采集、边缘推理与多机验证。所经链路：蜂窝基站接入网（相关论坛报告将其定位为面向 6G）；这是经行业媒体转述的样机发布，不是实测结果。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Compute sits in the base station itself: BBU-integrated AI chips, with a separate extension unit that can be scaled and shared between sites.
- RAN4AI is framed as turning the network from a transmission pipe into a communication-compute-intelligence service; AI4RAN as moving the RAN from rule-driven to data-driven.
- C114 notes the direction is broadly consistent with the international "AI-RAN" idea, and that China Mobile's next step is to use the small cell as a testbed to move from architecture exploration to engineering practice.
- At a semiconductor wireless-network-chip forum, a China Mobile Research Institute expert said the industry has no consensus between "ASIC + GPU + CPU heterogeneous" and "GPU-native" compute routes; preliminary experiments with a GPU hosting the physical-layer stack of a 4TR small cell reportedly cut power and cost to levels close to a traditional architecture (a speaker statement relayed by C114, no figures given).
- No performance numbers for the prototype were disclosed in the article.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

It is an operator-side example of compute being built into the radio access node and shared between sites, with the same box meant to serve both RAN tasks and AI services. The evidence is a trade-press report of a prototype launch with no measurements, so it records direction and architecture rather than demonstrated performance. Infrastructure-level AI-RAN work also overlaps with the cellular scope.
