## Summary (EN)

On the closing day of the July 2026 Montreal plenary (Session 218, July 12–17), Robert Stacey (Intel) uploaded **DCN 11-26/1477r0, "Draft P802.11bu PAR"** — the first Mentor document to carry the **802.11bu** amendment designation. This confirms that the AI Offload Study Group's project has been assigned its project number and that the SG has progressed from use-case collection to a concrete Project Authorization Request draft.

The lineage is now complete across three sessions: the AI Offload SG was approved at the March 2026 plenary (scope: standards enabling APs to serve as edge AI compute nodes for sub-20 ms latency-sensitive applications), began PAR/CSD skeleton discussions at the Antwerp May interim (DCN 11-26/978 and /979, Gaurang Naik, Qualcomm), absorbed use-case contributions from InterDigital, OPPO, and ZTE through May–July, and has now produced a named draft PAR at the Montreal plenary. The PAR document body itself is account-gated on Mentor, so the draft scope text is not yet publicly readable; the title, author, and timing are the public signal.

The post-plenary Mentor tail was otherwise quiet: the only other new uploads past the plenary-closing watermark were DCN 11-26/1475 (TGbq July–September teleconference agenda, Edward Au, Huawei) — administrative. The **Session 218 report** (11-26-1140-02) also appeared on the 802.11 Reports page, the first plenary report since Antwerp's Session 217.

## Key technical points (EN)

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This is the moment the "AP as edge AI compute node" effort stops being a study-group exploration and becomes a numbered amendment project. The KB has tracked the full arc — 2026-05-01_ieee-march-2026-readout (SG approval), 2026-05-12_ieee-mentor-ai-offload-sg-antwerp-digest (initial PAR/CSD discussions), and the July digests (2026-07-11, 2026-07-13 ZTE agent use-case follow-ups) — but 802.11bu as a designation appears here for the first time. Watch next: the PAR's WG approval vote (September interim or by motion), the CSD companion, and whether the WNG post-UHR study group (also slated to form in July) surfaces its own parallel designation. Once the PAR text becomes publicly readable, the exact scope wording — which functions of AI offload (compute negotiation, model transfer, result delivery) get MAC-layer treatment — will be the thing to read closely.
