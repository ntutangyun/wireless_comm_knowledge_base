## Summary (EN)

A 27-author consortium paper (first author Filip Lemic; submitted 2026-07-16) proposes a unified evaluation methodology for AI-native Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) systems — the 6G design pattern where radio sensing, data transmission, and control actions form a single closed loop with AI-driven policy adaptation. The problem it targets: ISAC proposals are currently evaluated with incompatible, per-paper metrics and setups, making sensing-vs-communication trade-offs and AI-policy contributions impossible to compare across works.

The methodology covers evaluation pipelines, validation procedures, and a common performance-metric set spanning both the sensing and communication axes plus the closed-loop control layer. Two instantiations demonstrate the framework's breadth: a UAV-based outdoor coverage-extension scenario and a RIS-enabled indoor coverage-extension scenario. The author-count and structure suggest an EU-project consensus document — the kind of methodology baseline that later feeds standardization discussions.
