## Summary (EN)

Wi-Fi NOW published an interview (July 4, 2026) with Bill Anderson, founder and CEO of AmpThink — the integrator behind more high-density stadium Wi-Fi projects in North America than any other firm (100+ venues since its 2011 Lucas Oil Stadium debut with Cisco). Anderson's headline claim: stadium Wi-Fi is undergoing a structural shift from *proximity networking* to *hyper-directional networking*, and directional overhead designs are now "essential to all our network designs."

The conventional ultra-high-density playbook placed hundreds to thousands of low-power APs physically close to fans — under seats, in handrails, in floor boxes. It works, but carries millions of dollars in hidden cost: coring concrete, weatherproofing, cabling runs, and the operational burden of maintaining APs embedded in seating infrastructure. The hyper-directional model inverts this: far fewer AP units, each carrying multiple radios behind software-configurable, highly directional antenna arrays, mounted on catwalks 200+ feet above the crowd. Each beam illuminates a specific, non-overlapping seating section, with side-lobe filtering keeping co-channel interference manageable at scale.

The enabling hardware in AmpThink's current designs is Cisco's Catalyst 9104 stadium antenna platform, paired with AmpThink's own custom turnable/tiltable catwalk mounting kits that let installers aim each array precisely at its assigned section. The interview positions this not as a niche experiment but as the emerging default: the approach has moved from Allegiant Stadium's first commercial install to a standard element of new stadium builds and refreshes.

## Summary (ZH)

Wi-Fi NOW 于 2026 年 7 月 4 日发表了对 AmpThink 创始人兼 CEO Bill Anderson 的专访。AmpThink 是北美完成高密度体育场 Wi-Fi 项目最多的集成商（自 2011 年与 Cisco 合作的 Lucas Oil 体育场首秀以来已交付 100 多个场馆）。Anderson 的核心论断是：体育场 Wi-Fi 正在经历从"近距离组网"到"超定向组网"的结构性转变，定向高空设计如今"已是我们所有网络设计的必备要素"。

传统的超高密度方案是把数百到数千台低功率 AP 布置在离观众极近的位置——座椅下、扶手内、地面盒中。这种方式有效，但隐性成本高达数百万美元：混凝土开孔、防水处理、布线工程，以及维护嵌入座椅设施的 AP 的运维负担。超定向模式将其颠倒过来：AP 数量大幅减少，每台设备在软件可配置的高定向天线阵列后集成多个射频单元，安装在观众上方 200 多英尺的检修通道上。每个波束照射一个特定且互不重叠的座位区，通过旁瓣滤除将大规模同频干扰控制在可接受范围。

AmpThink 当前设计的核心硬件是 Cisco Catalyst 9104 体育场天线平台，配合 AmpThink 自研的可旋转/可俯仰检修通道安装套件，使安装人员能将每个阵列精确对准指定座位区。专访将这一方案定位为正在形成的默认设计而非小众实验：它已从 Allegiant 体育场的首个商用部署走向新建和翻新场馆的标准配置。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Architecture shift: proximity networking (hundreds–thousands of under-seat/handrail APs) → hyper-directional overhead networking (fewer AP units, more radios per unit, catwalk-mounted 200+ ft above clients).
- Beams are software-configurable and highly directional, with side-lobe filtering in the radiation pattern; each beam serves a non-overlapping seating section.
- Hardware: Cisco Catalyst 9104 stadium antenna AP plus AmpThink custom turnable/tiltable mounting kits for precise catwalk aiming.
- Economic driver: proximity deployments carry millions in hidden installation cost (concrete coring, weatherproofing, cabling) and heavy operational overhead; overhead directional designs cut both.
- Adoption signal: Cisco's 9104 shipments grew from hundreds (FY2021) to 4,000+ (FY2022) to 6,000+ (FY2023); AmpThink now treats hyper-directional as essential in all designs.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

First stadium / ultra-high-density venue entry in the KB — the deployment bin so far tracks enterprise campus, hospitality/MDU (2026-07-04_worldvue-openwifi-3500-device-scale), and outdoor industrial (2026-07-03_cisco-9177-outdoor-wifi7-urwb-ap) verticals. The technology itself is not new (the Catalyst 9104 dates to 2021), but the article marks the trend's maturation point: the most experienced stadium integrator now calls hyper-directional the default rather than the experiment. Worth watching how this RF architecture interacts with Wi-Fi 7/8 features designed for dense cells — notably MLO traffic steering and 802.11bn multi-AP coordination, whose coordinated spatial reuse assumptions differ when cells are sharply sectorized from overhead.
