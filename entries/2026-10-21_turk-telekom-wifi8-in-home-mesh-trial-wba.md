## Summary (EN)

A Wireless Broadband Alliance member release dated 2026-10-12 describes in-home Wi-Fi 8 (IEEE 802.11bn) testing by Türk Telekom. The release says Türk Telekom "has become the first operator in Europe to test" Wi-Fi 8 in a multi-device mesh architecture through real-world use cases; this is the operator's own claim, relayed here and not verified by this KB. It also describes the operator as one of the few worldwide conducting such real-world testing, and says it ran comparable home-condition tests for Wi-Fi 7.

The release names three test conditions: areas with weak signal coverage due to distance from the modem and obstacles, high-density environments with many devices connected at once, and high-interference settings created by dozens of overlapping wireless networks across multiple locations. The reported results are qualitative and no numbers are given: Wi-Fi 8 is said to deliver stronger connectivity and higher data transmission performance than Wi-Fi 7 in weak-signal areas, to use network resources more efficiently in dense venues such as event venues, cafés and libraries, and to show lower packet loss than previous generations under high interference.

The release also reports a real-world test of Unequal Modulation (UEQM), described as one of Wi-Fi 8's new capabilities: the tests "showed that Wi-Fi 8 preserves the high performance of data streams with strong signal levels while maintaining connection continuity for streams operating under weaker signal conditions" and improved data transmission speeds relative to Wi-Fi 7. Quotes in the release come from Zafer Orhan (Chief Network Officer, Türk Telekom) and Tiago Rodrigues (CEO, WBA), who welcomes the "early lab testing and practical exploration of emerging capabilities" and refers to WBA's Wi-Fi 8 program and the continuing evolution of IEEE 802.11bn.

## Summary (ZH)

无线宽带联盟（WBA）2026-10-12 发布的一则会员新闻稿介绍了 Türk Telekom 对 Wi-Fi 8（IEEE 802.11bn）的家庭环境测试。新闻稿称 Türk Telekom“已成为欧洲首家”在多设备 Mesh 架构中通过真实用例测试 Wi-Fi 8 的运营商；这是运营商自身的说法，本知识库仅转述、未经核实。稿件还称其为全球少数开展此类实地测试的运营商之一，并表示此前对 Wi-Fi 7 做过类似的家庭条件测试。

稿件列出三类测试条件：因距离调制解调器和障碍物造成信号弱的区域、大量设备同时连接的高密度环境，以及多个地点中数十个无线网络同时工作形成的高干扰环境。报告的结果为定性描述，未给出具体数字：弱信号区域中 Wi-Fi 8 的连接和数据传输性能强于 Wi-Fi 7；在活动场馆、咖啡馆、图书馆等高密度场景中网络资源利用更高效；在高干扰条件下丢包率低于前几代。

稿件还报告了对不等调制（UEQM，称为 Wi-Fi 8 的新能力之一）的实地测试：测试“表明 Wi-Fi 8 在保持强信号数据流高性能的同时，维持了较弱信号数据流的连接连续性”，且数据传输速度相比 Wi-Fi 7 有所提升。稿中引述了 Türk Telekom 首席网络官 Zafer Orhan 和 WBA CEO Tiago Rodrigues 的话；后者对“早期实验室测试和对新兴能力的实际探索”表示欢迎，并提到 WBA 的 Wi-Fi 8 项目及 IEEE 802.11bn 的持续演进。
