## Summary (EN)

Between 2026-09-07 and 2026-09-08, TGbq (Integrated Millimeter Wave, IMMW) uploaded eight new-DCN contributions spanning both the MAC and PHY clauses of its draft: MAC-side proposed draft text (PDT) for service-period operation, P2P operation and power save; PHY-side PDT for the M-SIG field and IMMW-MCS parameters; a general beam-establishment document; a broadcast service-period document for beam measurement; and a discussion document on the SIG field for the IMMW null data packet (NDP). The submitting companies are four (Intel, Samsung, Huawei, ZTE), although two of the documents are cross-industry consensus texts with long co-author lists (51 named authors across some 19 affiliations on the beam-establishment PDT, 27 across 15 on the power-save PDT). All eight populate the TGbq D0.1 specification skeleton (11-26-1310) and cite adopted motions by number. Two of the eight (DCN 11-26-1608 and 11-26-1635) were uploaded on 2026-09-07 and the other six on 2026-09-08; none were covered by the 2026-09-08 digest (`2026-09-08_ieee-mentor-tgbp-tgbq-digest`), which recorded TGbq's first two baseline MAC/PHY clause contributions.

## Summary (ZH)

在 2026-09-07 至 2026-09-08 期间，TGbq（集成毫米波，IMMW）上传了八份新编号贡献，覆盖其草案的 MAC 与 PHY 两个条款：MAC 侧的服务周期操作、P2P 操作与省电模式拟稿文本（PDT）；PHY 侧的 M-SIG 字段与 IMMW-MCS 参数 PDT；一份通用波束建立文档；一份用于波束测量的广播服务周期文档；以及一份关于 IMMW 空数据包（NDP）SIG 字段的讨论文档。提交单位共四家（英特尔、三星、华为、中兴），但其中两份文档是跨公司共识文本、署名很长（波束建立 PDT 有 51 位署名作者、涉及约 19 家单位；省电 PDT 27 位作者、15 家单位）。八份文档均填入 TGbq D0.1 规范骨架（11-26-1310），并按编号引用已通过的动议。其中两份（DCN 11-26-1608 与 11-26-1635）于 2026-09-07 上传，其余六份于 2026-09-08 上传；2026-09-08 的摘要（`2026-09-08_ieee-mentor-tgbp-tgbq-digest`，记录了 TGbq 最早的两份基线 MAC/PHY 条款贡献）均未涵盖这八份。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **DCN 11-26-1608r0 · TGbq** — "mac-pdt-immw-sp-operation". Dmitry Akhmetov (Intel), uploaded 07-Sep-2026 14:08 ET. Proposed draft text for a new clause 41.1 "IMMW SP Operation" plus three definitions: BESP (a service period used only for beam establishment), SPI IMMW STA (the STA allowed to perform channel access and initiate frame exchanges in an SP) and SPR IMMW STA (the intended receiver). Adopted rules transcribed with their motion numbers: SPs are negotiated between two IMMW MLDs via their sub-7 GHz affiliated STAs and may be periodic, non-periodic or on-demand; an IMMW STA "shall not transmit frames to a peer IMMW STA outside an SP negotiated between them"; every non-beam-establishment frame exchange is initiated with an ICF/ICR exchange, and a responder that cannot participate says so explicitly in the ICR (scope of that feedback TBD); the channel access function is suspended at SP end and restarted at the next SP; a single channel access function serves all TIDs and a single NAV timer applies. More than a dozen items are marked TBD, including the establishment mechanism for periodic, non-periodic and on-demand SPs. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1608&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1635r0 · TGbq** — "PDT General beam establishment". Cheng Chen (Intel) with 50 co-authors across 19 affiliations, uploaded 07-Sep-2026 22:51 ET. Proposed draft text for subclause 41.7.2.1: beam establishment runs as a negotiation phase (a sub-7 GHz frame exchange between the same two IMMW MLDs, frame TBD), a training phase and a feedback phase. The negotiation covers the sequence of training modules, the target start time on the IMMW link, the number of transmit and receive sectors per module (M, N >= 1, M + N > 2), training direction and whether quasi-omni transmit/receive is used; training for one STA may span multiple SPs; the result may be reported by the sub-7 GHz affiliated STA. "After the successful completion of beam establishment, an IMMW link is established." Thirteen TBDs remain, including the negotiation frame itself. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1635&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1652r0 · TGbq** — "PDT MAC--IMMW P2P Operation". Rubayet Shafin and Jonghoe Koo (Samsung Electronics), uploaded 08-Sep-2026 16:53 ET. A two-sentence clause 41.6 stating that two IMMW MLDs may perform peer-to-peer operation on the mmWave band, with frame exchanges following the IMMW SP operation rules (Motion #102); nothing further is specified in this revision. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1652&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1657r0 · TGbq** — "PDT PHY: M-SIG". Juan Fang (Intel) with 13 co-authors, uploaded 08-Sep-2026 11:30 ET. Proposed draft text for subclause 42.3.9.4 defining the M-SIG container, structured after U-SIG (Motion #57): two OFDM symbols (M-SIG-1, M-SIG-2) of 26 data bits each, BCC rate 1/2 with a 4-bit CRC and 6-bit tail, BPSK, 200 ns guard interval, version-independent and version-dependent field groups, and identical content in every 320 MHz subchannel of a 640 MHz PPDU. The actual field list is not yet written. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1657&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1658r0 · TGbq** — "PDT MAC for IMMW Power Save". Kaidong Wang (Huawei) with 26 co-authors, uploaded 08-Sep-2026 04:00 ET. A clause-heading placeholder (41.5 "IMMW power save") with one introductory sentence; no mechanism, fields or motions are specified in this revision. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1658&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1689r0 · TGbq** — "PDT Broadcast SP for beam measurement". Jinho Choi (Samsung Electronics), uploaded 08-Sep-2026 19:43 ET. Proposed draft text for subclause 41.7.2.2 (Motion #87): an IMMW AP may schedule a broadcast SP used exclusively for downlink beam measurement by one or more non-AP STAs, which need not be associated and do not transmit in it; participation is optional and does not by itself complete beam-pair-link establishment. Other details are TBD. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1689&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1694r0 · TGbq** — "Thoughts on SIG field for IMMW NDP". Jiyuan Yang (ZTE, with Sanechips co-authors), uploaded 08-Sep-2026 22:27 ET. Discussion slides, not draft text: proposes that the SIG field of the IMMW NDP carry different parameter sets depending on the NDP's purpose (for beam establishment or tracking: training mode, antenna configuration, beam/sector ID, a down-counter of remaining NDPs, timestamp; for channel sounding: GI and LTF size, number of LTF symbols, number of spatial streams, a beamformed flag). The deck ends with a straw poll and names as its next step deciding which parameters ride in the SIG field and which are negotiated over the sub-7 GHz link. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1694&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1697r0 · TGbq** — "PDT PHY: Parameters for IMMW-MCSs". Yulin Huang (Huawei) with 4 co-authors, uploaded 08-Sep-2026 23:19 ET. Proposed draft text with the first IMMW rate tables: only 320 MHz and 640 MHz bandwidth modes, an EHT-style non-OFDMA tone plan (234 / 468 data tones), 1.25 MHz tone spacing and 800 ns symbol duration, guard intervals of 50 ns and 100 ns only, optional 2-spatial-stream SU-MIMO, and an MCS set of MCS0 to MCS7 plus three additional entries (QPSK rate 2/3, 16-QAM rate 2/3, 16-QAM rate 5/6); index numbering is TBD. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1697&is_year=2026)

- **DCN 11-26-1608r0 · TGbq** — "mac-pdt-immw-sp-operation"。Dmitry Akhmetov（英特尔），2026-09-07 14:08 ET 上传。为新条款 41.1"IMMW SP 操作"提出拟稿文本，并新增三个定义：BESP（仅用于波束建立的服务周期）、SPI IMMW STA（在 SP 内获准进行信道接入并发起帧交换的 STA）与 SPR IMMW STA（预期接收方）。已通过的规则按动议编号转录：SP 由两个 IMMW MLD 经其 sub-7 GHz 从属 STA 协商，可为周期性、非周期性或按需；IMMW STA"不得在双方协商的 SP 之外向对端 IMMW STA 发送帧"；所有非波束建立的帧交换均以 ICF/ICR 交换发起，无法参与的响应方须在 ICR 中明确反馈（反馈范围待定）；信道接入功能在 SP 结束时挂起、在下一个 SP 开始时恢复；所有 TID 共用单一信道接入功能，并采用单一 NAV 计时器。十余处待定项，包括周期性、非周期性与按需 SP 的建立机制。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1608&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1635r0 · TGbq** — "PDT 通用波束建立"。Cheng Chen（英特尔）及 50 位共同作者（涉及 19 家单位），2026-09-07 22:51 ET 上传。为子条款 41.7.2.1 提出拟稿文本：波束建立分为协商阶段（同一对 IMMW MLD 之间的 sub-7 GHz 帧交换，帧格式待定）、训练阶段与反馈阶段。协商内容包括训练模块序列、IMMW 链路上的目标起始时间、每个模块的发射与接收扇区数（M、N ≥ 1 且 M + N > 2）、训练方向以及是否使用准全向收发；单个 STA 的训练可跨多个 SP；结果可由 sub-7 GHz 从属 STA 上报。"波束建立成功完成后，IMMW 链路即告建立。"仍有 13 处待定，包括协商帧本身。[文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1635&is_year=2026)

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This KB's prior TGbq entries tracked link-management behaviour (`2026-08-13_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest`: link switching, P2P operation, cross-link Co-TWT, state transitions), a channel-access simulation revision (`2026-09-01_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest`), and the group's first two baseline MAC/PHY clause contributions (`2026-09-08_ieee-mentor-tgbp-tgbq-digest`: a BSS-operation MAC clause and a beamforming introduction subclause). Today's eight documents extend that baseline drafting across a wider set of clauses in a two-day window — MAC-side service-period operation, P2P operation and a power-save placeholder; PHY-side M-SIG and MCS parameters; plus beam-establishment and beam-measurement text — from four submitting companies. The substantive new draft text is concentrated in three of them: the SP-operation clause (the no-transmission-outside-an-SP rule, mandatory ICF/ICR initiation, SP-bounded channel access), the beam-establishment procedure (negotiated over the sub-7 GHz link, with per-module sector counts) and the first IMMW rate tables (320/640 MHz, 50/100 ns guard intervals, MCS0-7 plus three extra entries). These PDTs populate a D0.1 specification skeleton (11-26-1310) that is not yet a letter-ballot draft, so this remains the group's earliest drafting activity rather than comment resolution against a balloted draft. Several clause numbers still collide across documents (two texts both claim 41.5; SP operation is 41.1 in one and cross-referenced as 41.4 in another), which is normal for an unreconciled skeleton.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

本知识库此前记录的 TGbq 条目涉及链路管理行为（`2026-08-13_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest`：链路切换、P2P 操作、跨链路 Co-TWT、状态转换）、一份信道接入仿真修订（`2026-09-01_ieee-mentor-tgbq-digest`），以及该组前两份基线 MAC/PHY 条款贡献（`2026-09-08_ieee-mentor-tgbp-tgbq-digest`：一份 BSS 操作 MAC 条款与一份波束成形引言子条款）。今天的八份文档在两天内将该基线起草工作扩展到更广泛的条款——MAC 侧的服务周期操作、P2P 操作与省电占位条款；PHY 侧的 M-SIG 与 MCS 参数；再加上波束建立与波束测量文本——来自四家提交单位。实质性的新拟稿文本集中在其中三份：SP 操作条款（SP 之外不得发送的规则、强制的 ICF/ICR 发起、以 SP 为界的信道接入）、波束建立流程（经 sub-7 GHz 链路协商，含每模块扇区数）以及首份 IMMW 速率表（320/640 MHz、50/100 ns 保护间隔、MCS0-7 外加三档）。这些 PDT 填入的是尚未进入函审的 D0.1 规范骨架（11-26-1310），因此仍属于该组最早期的起草活动，而非针对已投票草案的评论解决。各文档之间的条款编号仍有冲突（两份文本都占用 41.5；SP 操作在一份中为 41.1、在另一份中被引用为 41.4），这在未合并的骨架阶段属常见现象。
