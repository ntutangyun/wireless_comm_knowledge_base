## Summary (EN)

SatNews (18 October 2026) reports on the conditions India is attaching to foreign satellite operators, naming the Ministry of Home Affairs, the Department of Telecommunications (DoT), TRAI and the WPC wing, and operators including SpaceX/Starlink, Reliance Jio, Bharti Airtel, SES and Eutelsat OneWeb. The article says all voice, messaging and broadband data crossing Indian airspace must terminate at certified Satellite Earth Station Gateways (SESGs) physically located in India, with real-time lawful interception, decryption access and full visibility into packet routing, and that operational licences stay withheld until these prerequisites are met.

It also says DoT rules bar operators from using inter-satellite optical crosslinks to bypass Indian teleports, so user traffic must land in India. Starlink is reported to have run preliminary technical tests and leased gateway facilities in Mumbai and other regional centres; full clearance would require an independent audit and hardware-level isolation. On spectrum, terrestrial operators warn that direct-to-cell services on shared cellular frequencies could cause harmful interference with existing towers, with WPC overseeing boundary coordination. The article says the government has provided for administrative (non-auction) allocation under the Telecommunications Act, Jio has lobbied TRAI for open bidding for satellite spectrum used in retail broadband and direct-to-device, COAI has challenged satellite spectrum charges, and TRAI is finalising pricing recommendations.

Communications Minister Jyotiraditya Scindia is quoted as saying "India does not allow monopolies" and that compliance must be done by each operator individually before commercial operations. The article gives no figures for fees, licence costs or timelines and cites no official policy documents, so its regulatory details rest on the reporting itself.

## Summary (ZH)

SatNews（2026 年 10 月 18 日）报道了印度对外国卫星运营商附加的条件，涉及内政部、电信部（DoT）、TRAI 和 WPC，以及 SpaceX/Starlink、Reliance Jio、Bharti Airtel、SES 和 Eutelsat OneWeb 等运营商。文章称，所有穿越印度领空的语音、消息和宽带数据必须在位于印度境内的认证卫星地球站网关（SESG）终结，并提供实时合法监听、解密访问和对分组路由的完全可见性；在满足这些前提之前，运营许可将被暂缓。

文章还称，DoT 规则禁止运营商利用星间光链路绕过印度的信关站，因此用户流量必须在印度落地。据报道 Starlink 已进行初步技术测试，并在孟买及其他地区中心租用了网关设施；完全放行需要独立审计和硬件级隔离。频谱方面，地面运营商警告称，共享蜂窝频率上的直连手机服务可能对现有基站造成有害干扰，边界协调由 WPC 负责。文章称政府已依据《电信法》规定行政（非拍卖）分配方式，Jio 游说 TRAI 对用于零售宽带和直连设备的卫星频谱实行公开竞标，COAI 对卫星频谱费用提出质疑，TRAI 正在最终确定定价建议。

通信部长 Jyotiraditya Scindia 被引述称“印度不允许垄断”，并称每个运营商须单独完成合规后才能开始商业运营。文章未给出费用、许可成本或时间表，也未引用官方政策文件，其监管细节仅基于报道本身。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Gateways**: all traffic to terminate at certified SESGs in India, with real-time lawful interception and routing visibility.
- **ISLs**: optical crosslinks may not be used to bypass Indian teleports.
- **Starlink status**: preliminary tests and leased Mumbai-area gateways; full clearance needs audit and hardware-level isolation.
- **D2C**: interference concerns on shared cellular frequencies; WPC to coordinate boundaries.
- **Spectrum**: administrative allocation provided for; auction vs. administrative pricing dispute; TRAI recommendations pending.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **网关**：所有流量须在印度境内认证 SESG 终结，并支持实时合法监听和路由可见性。
- **星间链路**：不得利用光链路绕过印度信关站。
- **Starlink 现状**：初步测试及租用孟买一带网关；完全放行需审计和硬件级隔离。
- **直连手机**：共享蜂窝频率的干扰担忧；WPC 负责边界协调。
- **频谱**：规定行政分配；拍卖与行政定价之争；TRAI 建议待定。
