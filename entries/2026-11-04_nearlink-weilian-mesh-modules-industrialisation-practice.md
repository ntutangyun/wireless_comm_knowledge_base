## Summary (EN)

A 2026-10-30 article on the SparkLink Alliance news feed gives the view of Shenzhen Weilian Technology (深圳市纬联技术), a low-power wireless module maker founded in 2015 that states cumulative shipments above 200 million units, and which describes itself as an initial member of the alliance and a drafting unit for the standard. The article describes the SparkLink standard system as covering two technical directions, SLE (low-power access) and SLB (low-latency broadband), and says national-standard drafting is progressing, with several basic-service-layer parts at a key stage. Weilian says it took part in drafting Basic Service Layer: Device Discovery and Transmission Control (part 301).

The company names Mesh networking as its core focus, alongside image transmission, low latency and ranging, and lists its module line-up and shipping applications.

国际星闪联盟新闻栏目2026年10月30日刊发的文章，介绍了深圳市纬联技术有限公司的看法。该公司成立于2015年，是低功耗无线连接模组厂商，自称累计出货量超过2亿，是国际星闪联盟初始会员和标准起草单位。文章称星闪标准体系覆盖低功耗接入（SLE）与低时延宽带（SLB）两个技术方向，国家标准编写稳步推进，多项基础服务层标准进入关键阶段；纬联称参与了《基础服务层·设备发现和传输控制》（第301部分）的编写。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Standards**: Weilian says it is a drafting unit of the SparkLink Basic Service Layer: Device Discovery and Transmission Control (part 301); the article also mentions closer coordination between iSLA and GIIC on smart-home integration.
- **Modules**: H981, H980, H963, H963T, H375U, H375S, H373U, H383S, H385, H353, stated as mass-produced and certified by the alliance's test programme, with USB and SDIO interfaces; a SparkLink 2.0 module is said to be coming.
- **Mesh figures (stated by the vendor)**: automatic multi-node networking and node discovery without manual configuration; SLE link budget 115 dB and receive sensitivity -110 dBm; one gateway can host 20 devices; SLE supports 256 concurrent users; second-level rerouting when a node fails; SLE 250 us two-way interaction and microsecond-level multi-device synchronisation.
- **Shipments (stated)**: SparkLink image transmission in baby monitors, modules in hotel-automation applications, Mesh projects with several customers in commercial stage; transmission, ranging and low-latency applications under joint commercial development.
- **Application mix**: the article says use is moving from keyboards, mice and remote controls toward image transmission, smart home, Mesh and ranging.
