## Summary (EN)

On the morning of 23 October 2026 a SparkLink industry capital matchmaking session was held at the Futian Convention and Exhibition Center, Shenzhen (held at the same venue during the CEIC2026 week), under the theme "High-quality wireless connectivity - the key leap for agents". It was hosted by the Pingshan District industry bureau, co-organised by the International SparkLink Alliance, and supported by Zhongke Jingshang and CAS Advanced Fund. A district official presented local industrial policy; an alliance expert-committee deputy director said SparkLink has moved from standard-making through chip mass production to large-scale commercial use, with members spanning consumer electronics, automotive, robotics and smart home.

A roundtable covered three chapters: technology and positioning, scenarios and applications, opportunities and outlook. Panellists came from Kunpeng Capital's AI fund, the alliance's industry advisory group (Beijing Yuanxiaowei), Zhongke Jingshang, NXP (system architecture R&D) and Robotera (robot-fleet use). They agreed on low latency, high reliability, precise positioning and high concurrency as differentiators and framed SparkLink as the connectivity base for agents. Zhongke Jingshang shared chip and standard-making progress, NXP discussed opportunities and challenges for SparkLink going global, Robotera showed exploration of SparkLink in robot bodies and robot-swarm cooperation, and the investor discussed where SparkLink and AI meet. Six companies pitched - Xiaxin Microelectronics, Shanghai Lushi Information, Xunlian Core Technology, Shengyan Zhixin, Hongxin Zhihu and Shengfeite - covering chips, modules, audio and industry solutions; several reached preliminary intent with investors.
