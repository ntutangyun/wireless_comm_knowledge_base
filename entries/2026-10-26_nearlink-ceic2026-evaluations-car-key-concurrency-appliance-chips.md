## Summary (EN)

The CITNews report of 25 October 2026 on the 22 October CEIC2026 SparkLink summit also lists ecosystem results announced on stage.

Evaluations: the head of the IoT technology department at the CAICT Telecommunication Terminal Laboratory (泰尔终端实验室) reported high-concurrency results measured repeatedly in office buildings, outdoor sites and underground car parks, stating that SparkLink reaches concurrency on the order of a hundred, 256 and even 500 nodes and is overall better than the industry average on concurrency, latency, rate and packet loss. The Shanghai Motor Vehicle Inspection & Certification Technology Research Center reported a SparkLink digital car key 2.0 evaluation: cumulative shipments over 1.6 million units, more than 30 mass-production models, a measured lock/unlock success rate of 100%, and stable behaviour in anti-ping-pong false-trigger, interference and low-battery scenarios.

Products and chips: the China Household Electrical Appliances Research Institute, with Midea, Haier, Hisense, TCL and Konka, announced 2026 SparkLink appliance chip shipments above 40 million. Maxscend (卓胜微), Chuangyao (创耀), Zhongke Jingshang (中科晶上) and Zhilian'an (智联安) jointly released a new generation of SparkLink chips, described as higher performance, lower power and lower cost. Two OpenLabs (chip IP in Beijing, smart appliance in Shanghai) were accredited. Huawei said its "Hongtu" programme has attracted 80+ partners and that one-stop SparkLink + HarmonyOS test certification has started, with three test labs accredited. Power-grid companies presented SparkLink application scenarios, and new SIGs for smart healthcare and sensors were founded.

## Summary (ZH)

CITNews 2026 年 10 月 25 日对 10 月 22 日 CEIC2026 星闪峰会的报道还列出了现场发布的生态成果。

评测方面：中国信通院泰尔终端实验室物联技术部主任在办公楼、户外、地下车库等场地反复实测，称星闪已具备百级、256乃至500节点量级的高并发能力，并发数、时延、速率、丢包率等核心指标整体优于行业平均水平。上海机动车检测认证技术研究中心发布星闪数字车钥匙2.0评测：累计出货超160万台、量产车型超30款，实测解闭锁成功率100%，在防乒乓误触、抗干扰、低电量场景表现稳定。

产品与芯片：中国家用电器研究院联合美的、海尔、海信、TCL、康佳公布2026年星闪家电芯片总出货量4000万+。卓胜微、创耀、中科晶上、智联安联合发布新一代星闪芯片，称性能更高、功耗更低、成本更优。联盟向星闪芯片IP（北京）与智能家电（上海）两家OpenLab授牌。华为称“鸿图计划”已有80+伙伴加入，星闪+鸿蒙一站式测试认证启动，并向三家测试机构授牌。电网企业发布星闪电力应用场景，并成立星闪智慧医疗SIG与传感器SIG。
