## Summary (EN)

Ericsson and Korean operator KT completed a lab trial of mid-band **FDD** Massive MIMO using Ericsson's commercialised AIR 3284 radio (announced February 26, 2026; ingested now as backfill — the cellular-massive-mimo bin had no FDD Massive MIMO coverage). Headline numbers: throughput uplift up to +95% downlink and +127% uplink, with coverage gains up to +60% DL (at 50 Mbps) and +64% UL (at 15 Mbps) versus conventional mid-band FDD radios.

The uplink emphasis is the strategic part: both companies frame UL capacity and consistency as the binding constraint for "AI-ready" networks — device-originated AI traffic (uploads to cloud inference, XR capture, agent telemetry) stresses the uplink in ways consumer 5G never did. FDD Massive MIMO also matters because most Korean (and global) mid-band FDD holdings sit in legacy paired spectrum that TDD-centric Massive MIMO roadmaps have bypassed; making paired spectrum beamforming-capable extends the Massive MIMO story to bands operators already own. The trial is positioned as groundwork for 5G Advanced and the 6G transition.
