## Summary (EN)

Motorola announced the Signature 27 flagship smartphone on 2026-11-05 (pre-orders from 2026-11-19, sales from 2026-11-26). Its press release lists "Wi-Fi 8 compatibility" as a feature, claims up to 2x faster Wi-Fi than the previous-generation Signature, and describes a new 6-antenna design for faster 5G downloads. The phone uses the Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 platform, which Motorola describes as among the first smartphones to carry that chipset.

The press release does not name the Wi-Fi chip, the Wi-Fi 8 feature set or channel width, and makes no claim of being the first phone with Wi-Fi 8. Trade coverage links the platform to Qualcomm's FastConnect 8800 connectivity system; this is unconfirmed for this phone.
