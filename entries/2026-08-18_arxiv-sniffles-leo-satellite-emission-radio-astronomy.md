## Summary (EN)
Indermuehle and Lourenço (CSIRO Space & Astronomy; MNRAS 550, 1–20, advance access 17 June 2026; arxiv 13 Aug 2026) present the first results of **SNIFFLES**, an ongoing programme that measures what large NGSO constellations actually radiate into radio-astronomy receiver bands. Using the 22 m Mopra telescope (NSW, Australia) with follow-up on a single ATCA dish and its new BIGCAT GPU correlator backend, they conducted **4,629 tracked observations totalling 375.9 h** of satellites from four constellations — Starlink (original, V2-Mini, V2-Mini Ku-optimized, V2-Mini direct-to-cell), OneWeb, Amazon Leo and Guowang (Hulianwang-Digui) — across L, S, C, X and K bands (1–26 GHz), attributing each detection to a specific spacecraft by matching the observed Doppler track against predicted ephemerides. The paper deliberately adopts ITU-R Radio Regulations vocabulary: **intended emission** (the licensed carrier), **unwanted emission** (out-of-band and spurious, RR 1.146, incl. harmonics) and **unintended radiation** (leakage from platform electronics such as power converters, computers and networking gear, which the RRs address only indirectly via RR 15.12; earlier work called it UEMR).

They log **2,345 detections at 300+ unique frequencies** from three of the four systems — nothing from Amazon Leo, consistent with it not yet being operational. By band the split is L 209 / S 2,027 / C 46 / X 41 / K 22; by system OneWeb 79, original Starlink 9, Starlink V2-Mini DTC 1,787, V2-Mini Ku 235, V2-Mini 40, Guowang 204. Headline findings: (1) Starlink DTC in Australia (2620–2630 MHz, 5 MHz wide at the time, then the only active DTC transmission in the country) is accompanied by its **second harmonic at 5240–5260 MHz, third at 7860–7890 MHz and fourth at 10480–10520 MHz** — a licensed S-band downlink leaking into C/X bands. (2) **Unintended radiation at 2700 MHz**, inside the RR 5.340 primary radio-astronomy allocation, appears in **76.9 % of observations of Starlink V2-Mini Ku-optimized satellites** (and 95.6 % of standard V2-Mini), plus detections at 2690.76 MHz and 5000 MHz; a OneWeb detection at **1613.19 MHz** sits inside the 1610.6–1613.8 MHz band protecting the 1612.231 MHz OH maser line. (3) OneWeb's most persistent unintended line is **8599 MHz (24/24 observations)** and it accounts for all nine K-band lines (17.8–19.3 GHz, its FSS downlink). (4) Guowang satellites show dense combs of narrow lines around 1.45, 1.77, 2.38–2.42, 2.54 and 2.70–2.74 GHz. (5) ATCA/BIGCAT calibrated follow-up puts flux densities in the **kJy–MJy range, up to eleven orders of magnitude brighter than typical astronomical sources**, enough to drive receiver LNAs non-linear and saturate samplers when the satellite couples into the telescope main beam — a geometry the authors' earlier modelling says will occur every few minutes once Starlink Gen 2, OneWeb phase 2 and Guowang filings are populated, implying ~15 % data loss in affected bands.

The regulatory section notes ~1.8 million satellite filings received at ITU-R by Nov 2025 and SpaceX's January 2026 FCC filing for a 1-million-satellite orbital-data-centre concept, and argues the RRs already provide hooks (RR 15.12, Res. 739, RA.769) for addressing unwanted emission and unintended radiation. The detection database is being withheld pending responsible-disclosure consultation with operators; follow-up papers will add ATCA/BIGCAT high-dynamic-range measurements, sub-GHz work with ASKAP and Murriyang (Parkes), and time-resolved tracking of mitigation by satellite generation.

## Summary (ZH)
Indermuehle 与 Lourenço（CSIRO 空间与天文；MNRAS 550, 1–20，2026 年 6 月 17 日在线，arxiv 2026 年 8 月 13 日）报告 **SNIFFLES** 的首批结果——一项持续测量大型非静止轨道星座实际向射电天文接收频段辐射了什么的观测计划。他们使用澳大利亚新南威尔士 22 m Mopra 望远镜，并以 ATCA 单天线及其新 BIGCAT GPU 相关器后端做补充观测，对四个星座——Starlink（初代、V2-Mini、V2-Mini Ku 优化、V2-Mini 手机直连）、OneWeb、Amazon Leo 与国网（互联网低轨）——在 L、S、C、X、K 频段（1–26 GHz）完成 **4,629 次跟踪观测、共 375.9 小时**，通过将观测到的多普勒轨迹与预测星历比对，把每次检出归属到具体卫星。论文刻意采用 ITU-R《无线电规则》术语：**有意发射**（许可载波）、**无用发射**（带外与杂散，RR 1.146，含谐波）与 **无意辐射**（电源变换器、计算机、网络设备等平台电子器件的泄漏，《规则》仅经 RR 15.12 间接涉及；早期文献称 UEMR）。

共记录 **2,345 次检出、300 余个唯一频率**，来自四个系统中的三个——Amazon Leo 无任何检出，与其尚未运营一致。按频段：L 209 / S 2,027 / C 46 / X 41 / K 22；按系统：OneWeb 79、初代 Starlink 9、Starlink V2-Mini 直连 1,787、V2-Mini Ku 235、V2-Mini 40、国网 204。要点：(1) 澳大利亚的 Starlink 手机直连（2620–2630 MHz，当时带宽 5 MHz，为该国唯一在用直连发射）伴随 **5240–5260 MHz 二次谐波、7860–7890 MHz 三次谐波、10480–10520 MHz 四次谐波**——许可的 S 频段下行泄漏进 C/X 频段。(2) 落入 RR 5.340 射电天文主要划分的 **2700 MHz 无意辐射**出现在 **76.9% 的 Starlink V2-Mini Ku 优化卫星观测**中（标准 V2-Mini 为 95.6%），另有 2690.76 MHz 与 5000 MHz 检出；OneWeb 在 **1613.19 MHz** 的检出位于保护 1612.231 MHz OH 脉泽谱线的 1610.6–1613.8 MHz 频段内。(3) OneWeb 最持久的无意谱线为 **8599 MHz（24/24 次观测）**，且 K 频段全部 9 条谱线（17.8–19.3 GHz，其 FSS 下行）均属 OneWeb。(4) 国网卫星在约 1.45、1.77、2.38–2.42、2.54、2.70–2.74 GHz 呈密集窄线梳。(5) ATCA/BIGCAT 定标补充测量给出 **kJy–MJy 量级、比典型天体源亮多达 11 个数量级**的流量密度，足以在卫星耦合进望远镜主瓣时使接收机低噪放非线性并令采样器饱和——作者早先的建模显示，一旦 Starlink Gen 2、OneWeb 二期与国网申报得以部署，此类几何每隔几分钟即出现一次，受影响频段数据损失约 15%。

监管部分指出，截至 2025 年 11 月 ITU-R 已收到约 180 万份卫星申报，SpaceX 于 2026 年 1 月向 FCC 提交了 100 万颗在轨数据中心卫星的概念申报，并主张《规则》已有处理无用发射与无意辐射的抓手（RR 15.12、第 739 号决议、RA.769）。检出数据库因待与运营商进行负责任披露协商而暂不公开；后续论文将增加 ATCA/BIGCAT 高动态范围测量、ASKAP 与 Murriyang（Parkes）的 1 GHz 以下观测，以及按卫星代际的时间分辨减缓跟踪。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
The KB's ngso-regulation bin has so far tracked NGSO spectrum from the *sharing-among-communications-services* side — the FCC's Part 100 licensing overhaul (2026-08-05), the GSO/NGSO EPFD and degraded-throughput sharing rewrite, the unlicensed 200 MHz D2D proceeding (2026-08-07), Amazon Leo's 5,105-satellite D2D filing (2026-08-04) — while the sat-direct-device thread has covered Starlink direct-to-cell purely as a service (2026-07-03, 2026-08-17). SNIFFLES is the first entry that puts **measured emission data** on the other side of the ledger: it is a multi-constellation, per-satellite-version, 1–26 GHz characterization of what Starlink, OneWeb and Guowang radiate into passive-service bands, using ITU-R's own three-way vocabulary. Three points are new for the KB. First, the direct-to-cell downlink itself is shown to leak **harmonics up to the fourth order (10.5 GHz)** — a concrete out-of-band-emission consequence of the D2D architecture that the licensing entries do not mention. Second, unintended radiation at 2700 MHz appears in three-quarters or more of passes of specific Starlink V2-Mini variants, i.e. it is a repeatable platform signature, not a rare glitch, and platform electronics are exactly the part of a satellite that current NGSO licensing does not regulate. Third, the per-version breakdown (original vs V2-Mini vs Ku-optimized vs DTC; Guowang's line combs; OneWeb's 8.6 GHz and K-band lines) gives regulators and operators a baseline against which mitigation across satellite generations can be judged — which is what the follow-on SNIFFLES papers promise. Caveats: the paper is a survey from one southern-hemisphere site, the detection database is not yet public, and Amazon Leo was not yet operational during the campaign, so its footprint remains uncharacterized.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
知识库的 ngso-regulation 板块此前从*通信业务间共享*一侧跟踪 NGSO 频谱——FCC Part 100 许可改革（2026-08-05）、GSO/NGSO EPFD 与劣化吞吐量共享规则改写、200 MHz 免许可 D2D 议程（2026-08-07）、Amazon Leo 5,105 颗 D2D 申报（2026-08-04）——而 sat-direct-device 脉络仅把 Starlink 手机直连当作一项服务来记录（2026-07-03、2026-08-17）。SNIFFLES 是首个把 **实测发射数据** 放到天平另一侧的条目：对 Starlink、OneWeb 与国网在 1–26 GHz 向无源业务频段辐射了什么，按星座、按卫星版本、以 ITU-R 自身的三分术语进行刻画。对知识库而言有三点新意。其一，手机直连下行本身被证明泄漏 **至四次谐波（10.5 GHz）**——这是 D2D 架构一个具体的带外发射后果，许可类条目未曾提及。其二，2700 MHz 无意辐射出现于特定 Starlink V2-Mini 变体四分之三以上的过境中，即它是可重复的平台特征而非偶发故障，而平台电子器件恰是当前 NGSO 许可不予规制的部分。其三，按版本的拆分（初代 vs V2-Mini vs Ku 优化 vs 直连；国网线梳；OneWeb 8.6 GHz 与 K 频段谱线）为监管者与运营商提供了评判各代卫星减缓效果的基线——这正是后续 SNIFFLES 论文所承诺的。注意事项：论文是来自南半球单一站点的普查，检出数据库尚未公开，且 Amazon Leo 在观测期间尚未运营，其特征仍未刻画。
