## Summary (EN)

On July 7, 2026 (Cape Town), rain South Africa and Huawei announced the world's first multi-thousand-site commercial deployment of Massive MIMO in sub-1 GHz FDD spectrum. Large-scale rollout has already been completed in South Africa's major cities, with a multi-thousand-site national footprint as the target. Commercial results reported: uplink coverage improved by 5 dB, downlink coverage by 3 dB, and network capacity up to 3x versus conventional 4T4R low-band equipment.

Massive MIMO has historically been a mid-band (TDD, 2.6/3.5 GHz) and mmWave story — at sub-1 GHz, antenna elements are physically large and FDD operation removes the channel-reciprocity shortcut that TDD massive MIMO relies on for beamforming, which is why low-band has stayed on 2T2R/4T4R for a decade. Pushing a large FDD array into sub-1 GHz combines low-band's propagation and deep-indoor penetration with beamforming capacity gains previously exclusive to higher bands.

The launch extends a partnership that began with South Africa's first commercial 5G network in 2019 (rain CEO Conrad Leigh), and Huawei VP Fang Xiang framed it as "more than a network upgrade... a global technology milestone." It follows the April 2026 MTN Nigeria / Huawei deployment of the world's first commercial sub-1 GHz massive MIMO *site* — this is the first *network-scale* commercialization of the same technology direction.

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 7 月 7 日（开普敦），rain 南非与华为宣布全球首个数千站点规模的 Sub-1 GHz FDD 频段大规模 MIMO 商用部署。南非主要城市已完成大规模部署，目标是数千站点的全国覆盖。商用结果显示：上行覆盖提升 5 dB，下行覆盖提升 3 dB，网络容量相比传统 4T4R 低频设备最高提升 3 倍。

大规模 MIMO 历来是中频段（TDD，2.6/3.5 GHz）和毫米波的故事——在 Sub-1 GHz，天线阵子物理尺寸大，且 FDD 模式失去了 TDD 大规模 MIMO 赖以做波束赋形的信道互易性捷径，这正是低频段十年来停留在 2T2R/4T4R 的原因。将大规模 FDD 阵列推入 Sub-1 GHz，把低频段的传播优势与深度室内穿透，与此前只属于更高频段的波束赋形容量增益结合了起来。

此次发布延续了始于 2019 年南非首个商用 5G 网络的合作（rain CEO Conrad Leigh），华为副总裁方向称其为"不止是网络升级……是一个全球技术里程碑"。此前 2026 年 4 月 MTN 尼日利亚与华为部署了全球首个商用 Sub-1 GHz 大规模 MIMO 单站点——本次则是同一技术方向的首次网络级规模商用。

## Key technical points (EN)

- World-first *multi-thousand-site* commercial sub-1 GHz massive MIMO 5G network; major-city rollout already complete in South Africa.
- FDD low-band massive MIMO: +5 dB uplink coverage, +3 dB downlink coverage vs conventional 4T4R.
- Up to 3x network capacity versus 4T4R low-band equipment — beamforming-class capacity gains at coverage-band frequencies.
- Combines wide-area coverage and deep indoor penetration (sub-1 GHz propagation) with spatial multiplexing previously confined to mid-band TDD.
- Follows the April 2026 MTN Nigeria single-site first; escalation from site-level proof to network-scale commercialization within a quarter.
- Continues Huawei's FDD massive MIMO product line push (tri-band FDD massive MIMO went global in 2025).

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The KB's cellular-massive-mimo bin is currently academic-heavy (formal verification of DL power control, StemGNN CBF under backhaul delay) with one industry trial entry (2026-07-11_ericsson-kt-midband-fdd-massive-mimo — mid-band FDD trial on the road to 5G-Advanced). Watch whether Ericsson/Nokia answer with sub-1 GHz array products, and whether uplink-coverage-driven deployments (the +5 dB UL figure is the headline for rural/deep-indoor economics) become the standard justification for low-band mMIMO.
