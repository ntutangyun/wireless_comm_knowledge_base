## Summary (EN)

An announcement posted on the website of MediaTek subsidiary Airoha Technology, dated 6 October 2026, reports that MediaTek and Airoha are jointly showcasing throughput and power results for MediaTek's Filogic 8800 Wi-Fi 8 (draft 802.11bn) platform at Network X 2026. The announcement attributes the gains to two named mechanisms: an enhanced form of Dynamic Sub-channel Operation, referred to as "DSO+," which the company states improves overall system throughput by up to nearly 100% in mixed-generation environments where legacy (Wi-Fi 5/6/7) clients coexist with Wi-Fi 8 clients; and an enhanced form of Non-Primary Channel Access, referred to as "NPCA+," which the company states delivers up to about 80% throughput improvement in congested, overlapping-network conditions. Beyond raw throughput, the announcement also describes AI-assisted power-saving that it says reduces consumption by 33% under typical workloads (rising to about 50% total power reduction when combined with disabling unused bands), and AI-based smart network diagnostics that it says cuts mean time to repair (MTTR) from 2-4 hours down to under one minute, alongside a 20% reduction in field-service truck rolls. The Filogic 8800 platform was shortlisted for a Network X Awards 2026 "Best Indoor Connectivity Solution" award.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Platform**: MediaTek Filogic 8800 (draft 802.11bn / Wi-Fi 8), presented by MediaTek jointly with subsidiary Airoha Technology at Network X 2026.
- **DSO+ (enhanced Dynamic Sub-channel Operation)**: up to nearly 100% system throughput improvement in mixed-generation (Wi-Fi 5/6/7/8 coexisting) environments.
- **NPCA+ (enhanced Non-Primary Channel Access)**: up to about 80% throughput improvement in congested, overlapping-network scenarios.
- **AI power-saving**: 33% consumption reduction under typical workloads; up to ~50% total reduction combined with disabling unused bands.
- **AI diagnostics**: MTTR reduced from 2-4 hours to under 1 minute; a 20% reduction in field-service truck rolls.
- **Recognition**: shortlisted for Network X Awards 2026, "Best Indoor Connectivity Solution."
