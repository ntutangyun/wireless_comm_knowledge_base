## Summary (EN)

On 25 November 2026 (ET, 19:32) TG4ab uploaded two revisions of DCN 15-26-0277, "P802.15.4ab Comment Resolution Motions" (r14 and r15, Benjamin Rolfe, BCA et al.). Nothing else new appeared in either group's listing: IG FG UWB has no new rows (latest 15-26-0536 r0). The two revisions are an .xlsx workbook with a motion list ("SA Ballot" sheet) and vote-counting sheets; the comparison below is against r13 (12 November) and, for r15, against r14.

Compared with r13, the motion list changes in three places. Motion #52 (move to approve resolutions to R1-209 and R1-42 in document 15-26-0456-02; mover Xiliang Luo, seconder Pooria Pakrooh; no unanimous consent) now carries a recorded result: 25 yes / 2 no / 3 abstain, 92.6% yes, PASS, note "PM1". Motion #42 (R1-282 in 15-26-0448-00; mover Rojan Chitrakar), whose note read "Deferred to next call, still work in progress" in r13/r14, reads "Superseded by Motion #51" in r15; motion #51 (R1-282 in 15-26-0448-01) is noted "Comment withdrawn". Motion #58 is new in r15 (r14 has no text for it): "Move to reject all remaining technical and general comments without approved resolutions to Standards Association Ballot recirculation #1 in document 15-26-0380-17 with disposition detail that the group could not reach consensus on changes to the draft to resolve the comment", mover Alex Krebs, seconder Mickael Maman, unanimous consent "No", recorded as 10 yes / 5 no / 1 abstain, 66.7% yes, result FAIL. The sheet's vote-counting tab for this motion carries a date cell that corresponds to 24 November 2026 and a per-member CRG yes/no/abstain record that tallies to the same 10/5/1; the tab carries the note "For assisting the chair, for final results see the meeting minutes".

Other motions on the sheet keep the results already seen in earlier revisions (for example #55 20/13/1 FAIL, #49 24/17/7 FAIL, #38 14/23/0 FAIL, and #56 and #57 PASS by unanimous consent). The 25 November uploads also come after the 23 November agenda workbook (540r0) and CRG meeting-slide deck (539r0) that list the interim CRG Webex calls.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The earlier digests recorded motion #52 from the closing report and motion #55 and #38 failures; this revision adds the group's recorded vote on a motion, moved by Alex Krebs, that would have rejected, with a "no consensus" disposition, every remaining technical and general comment lacking an approved resolution in recirculation #1 — it did not pass, 10/5/1 as tallied on the sheet. The sheet itself says final results are in the meeting minutes, so the tallies here are the chair-assist workbook's. This is a task-group record of a ballot-resolution process on the P802.15.4ab draft, not a ratified standard.
