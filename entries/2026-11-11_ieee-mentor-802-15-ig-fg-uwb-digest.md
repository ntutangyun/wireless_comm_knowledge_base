## Summary (EN)

Two IG FG UWB (Future Generation UWB Interest Group) documents were uploaded on 10 November 2026 (ET), both by Wenzheng Li (Calterah Semiconductor), the IG chair, and both were read from the downloaded bodies. They record what happened at the group's first session, held Monday 9 November 2026, 16:00-18:00 Bangkok local time (UTC+7), in Thai Boromphimarn 3 with Webex 1. The secretary was Jiajia Huang (Huawei). They report the group's own record of the session and are not 802.15 Working Group decisions.

DCN 15-26-0468r1, "IG FG UWB Opening Report and Meeting Slides, November 2026" (uploaded 10 November 05:42 ET), is the revision of the 4 November opening deck (r0, covered in the 5 November digest) that now carries the decision records. Its agenda was approved by unanimous consent (agenda DCN 15-26-0445-01-fguw). Its background slide states that WNG contribution 15-26-0015-02 (January 2026) presented ideas for UWB evolution beyond IEEE P802.15.4ab, that WNG contribution 15-26-0302-00 (July 2026) expanded the discussion to Mobility Safety applications and initial gaps relative to 4ab, and that November 2026 was the first meeting of the IG. The motion to adopt the IG scope and objectives (slide 18 of the deck), moved by Clint Chaplin and seconded by Ben Rolfe, carried by unanimous consent. The scope reads: explore UWB evolution beyond IEEE P802.15.4ab, including, but not limited to, 4ab enhancements, emerging application needs, and operating and deployment conditions. The objectives are to consolidate requirements, identify gaps relative to 4ab and assess feasibility and the need for further standardization; to develop input to draft CSD/PAR with broad agreement on core project elements and record remaining issues; and to prepare evidence and inputs for SG formation. The deck records the follow-up on two technical contributions: DCN 15-26-0464-00 "Enhanced UWB One-Way Ranging Advertisement (EOWRA)" (presenter Zhongxing Yu, Calterah) with 3 participants in favour and a note that it needs further clarification on the targeted use cases, how one-way ranging (OWR) benefits them, and the OWR enhancements compared with the current solution; and DCN 15-26-0463-00 "UWB evolution directions beyond 4ab" (presenter Xiaolong Zhang, Calterah) with 8 participants in favour. The motion to adopt the draft PAR (15-26-0465-00) and CSD (15-26-0466-00) "as working drafts for further IG development" is recorded as "Motion cancelled". The Activity Summary (15-26-0467-00) is described as prepared as an input for future SG formation and to be continuously updated. The deck's proposed working method is to use Mentor for contributions and successive draft revisions, the WG reflector for requests and review comments, and teleconferences as needed, with the chair consolidating comments before each review; no recurring schedule is proposed. The session adjourned at 5:50 p.m. Bangkok time.

DCN 15-26-0506r0, "IG FG UWB Closing Report, November 2026" (uploaded 10 November 05:59 ET), summarises the outcomes in five slides. It lists: adopted the IG Scope and Objectives by unanimous consent; heard two technical contributions; reviewed and discussed the initial draft PAR and CSD; reviewed the Activity Summary; discussed the IG working method, interim work and teleconferences, timeline and action items. Its proposed next steps are to continue collecting technical contributions, evidence and comments on the draft PAR/CSD; to refine requirements, gaps and input to the draft PAR/CSD while recording remaining issues; and to target broad agreement on the draft CSD and PAR in January 2027, "subject to actual progress". Teleconferences are to be considered as needed, and SG formation is stated to depend on readiness, actual progress and WG/LMSC approvals. Its summary slide lists a call for submissions on use cases, technical directions, gap analysis and inputs to the draft PAR/CSD. (The more detailed call for contributions is on slide 29 of the opening deck 0468r1 and covers: enhancements building on 4ab (ranging, sensing, data communication and additional proposals); applications and requirements, with Mobility Safety as an initial example; operating and deployment conditions, with spectrum and regulatory impacts as initial examples; further evolution directions with supporting requirements, gap analyses and feasibility evidence; and comments and proposed text for the draft PAR and CSD.)

## Summary (ZH)

2026 年 11 月 10 日（美东时间），IG FG UWB（Future Generation UWB 兴趣组）上传了 2 份文档，均由 IG 主席 Wenzheng Li（Calterah Semiconductor）提交，均已下载并阅读正文。文档记录了该组首次会议的情况：2026 年 11 月 9 日（周一）曼谷当地时间 16:00-18:00（UTC+7），地点 Thai Boromphimarn 3 及 Webex 1，秘书为 Jiajia Huang（华为）。它们是该 IG 自身的会议记录，并非 802.15 工作组的决定。

DCN 15-26-0468r1《IG FG UWB Opening Report and Meeting Slides, November 2026》（11 月 10 日 05:42 ET 上传）是 11 月 4 日开幕幻灯片（r0，已在 11 月 5 日摘要中介绍）的修订版，现已加入决议记录。议程以全体一致通过获批（议程 DCN 15-26-0445-01-fguw）。背景页指出：WNG 贡献 15-26-0015-02（2026 年 1 月）提出了 IEEE P802.15.4ab 之后的 UWB 演进设想，WNG 贡献 15-26-0302-00（2026 年 7 月）将讨论扩展到 Mobility Safety 应用及相对 4ab 的初步差距，2026 年 11 月为该 IG 首次会议。采纳 IG 范围与目标（幻灯片第 18 页）的动议由 Clint Chaplin 提出、Ben Rolfe 附议，全体一致通过。范围为：探索 IEEE P802.15.4ab 之后的 UWB 演进，包括但不限于 4ab 增强、新兴应用需求以及运行与部署条件。目标为：整合需求、识别相对 4ab 的差距并评估进一步标准化的可行性与必要性；形成 CSD/PAR 草案的输入，就核心项目要素达成广泛共识并记录遗留问题；为成立 SG 准备证据与输入。文档记录了两份技术贡献的后续：DCN 15-26-0464-00《Enhanced UWB One-Way Ranging Advertisement (EOWRA)》（报告人 Zhongxing Yu，Calterah），3 人表示赞成，并注明需进一步澄清目标用例、单向测距（OWR）对这些用例的好处以及相对现有方案的 OWR 增强；DCN 15-26-0463-00《UWB evolution directions beyond 4ab》（报告人 Xiaolong Zhang，Calterah），8 人表示赞成。将 PAR 初稿（15-26-0465-00）与 CSD 初稿（15-26-0466-00）“作为进一步 IG 工作的工作草案”采纳的动议记录为“Motion cancelled”（动议取消）。活动摘要（15-26-0467-00）被描述为为将来成立 SG 准备的输入，并将持续更新。文档建议的工作方式为：用 Mentor 提交贡献和草案修订，用 WG 邮件列表发送征集、通知与评审意见，按需召开电话会议，由主席在每次评审前汇总意见；暂不设固定周期。会议于曼谷时间下午 5:50 休会。

DCN 15-26-0506r0《IG FG UWB Closing Report, November 2026》（11 月 10 日 05:59 ET 上传）用 5 页幻灯片总结了结果：以全体一致通过采纳 IG 范围与目标；听取两份技术贡献；审阅并讨论 PAR 与 CSD 初稿；审阅活动摘要；讨论 IG 工作方式、休会期工作与电话会议、时间表及行动项。建议的后续步骤为：继续收集技术贡献、证据以及对 PAR/CSD 草案的意见；细化需求、差距及 PAR/CSD 输入并记录遗留问题；目标为 2027 年 1 月就 CSD 与 PAR 草案达成广泛共识（“取决于实际进展”）。电话会议视需要考虑，成立 SG 取决于准备程度、实际进展及 WG/LMSC 的批准。其总结页列出征集内容：用例、技术方向、差距分析及 PAR/CSD 草案输入。（更详细的贡献征集见开幕幻灯片 0468r1 第 29 页，包括：基于 4ab 的增强（测距、感知、数据通信及其他提案）；应用与需求（Mobility Safety 为初始示例）；运行与部署条件（频谱与监管影响为初始示例）；更多演进方向及配套的需求、差距分析和可行性证据；对 PAR 与 CSD 草案的意见和建议文字。）

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The earlier digest (5 November) covered the IG's pre-session documents; these two uploads record how the first session actually went: the scope and objectives were adopted, the draft PAR/CSD were discussed but their adoption motion was cancelled, and the stated plan is to seek broad agreement on the draft CSD and PAR in January 2027. They are the IG's own reports; no Study Group has been formed according to these documents.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)
