## Summary (EN)
China's National Public Service Platform for Standards Information (std.samr.gov.cn) now lists a multi-part family of recommended national-standard plans titled **《星闪无线通信系统》 ("SparkLink Wireless Communication System")** — and, as of 2026-08-23, at least two of the parts show the **批准 (approval)** stage highlighted on the platform's project progress bar, i.e. they have passed drafting, public comment and review and are awaiting formal publication as GB/T standards. Verified directly on the platform: **Part 101 "架构和通用要求" (Architecture and general requirements, plan no. 20260704-T-469, status 正在批准)** and **Part 202 "接入层 同步低时延宽带（SLB）设备技术要求和测试方法" (Access layer — SLB device technical requirements and test methods, plan no. 20260705-T-469, status 正在批准)**. A third part, **Part 201 "接入层 同步低时延宽带（SLB）空中接口技术要求" (Access layer — SLB air-interface technical requirements, plan no. 20260073-T-469)**, belongs to an earlier 2026 plan batch. The platform's index additionally surfaces **Part 102 (媒体接入层标识 / MAC-layer identifiers), Part 203 (接入层 SLE 设备技术要求和测试方法 / SLE device requirements and test methods) and Part 301 (基础服务层 设备发现和传输控制 / basic service layer — device discovery and transport control)**, indicating a systematically numbered family: 1xx = architecture/general, 2xx = access layer, 3xx = basic service layer.

Two institutional details stand out. First, the naming: unlike GB/T 46803.1/.2-2025 (published 2025-12, in force 2026-07-01), which standardised the SLE PHY + data-link layer under the generic description "low-power wireless communication network based on polar codes", this family carries the **brand name 星闪 (SparkLink) in the official GB title** — the technology is now institutionally identified by name in China's national-standard system. Second, the scope: this is the **first appearance of the SLB (high-throughput, low-latency) mode and of the basic service layer on the national-standard track**, plus dedicated *device technical-requirements and test-methods* parts (202/203) — exactly the normative test anchors that a certification scheme cites. Drafting is led by CESI (中国电子技术标准化研究院) with the International SparkLink Alliance, Huawei, ZTE, CAICT, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Tsinghua and BUPT among roughly 35 drafting organisations, under TC28 (executed by its data-communication subcommittee SC6). Project cycle is 6 months, with implementation 6 months after publication.

Caveats: the platform pages do not display plan-issue or public-notice dates for these items, so the exact batch dates could not be pinned; the approval-stage status is as observed on 2026-08-23; and the titles of Parts 102/201/203/301 are taken from the platform's search index rather than fully fetched detail pages. No press coverage of this family exists at all — it was found only via the primary source.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)
This is the watch item set by 2026-06-05_nearlink-sle-gbt-46803-national-standard firing: that entry noted GB/T 46803 covered only the SLE side and said to watch "for the parallel SLB national-standard parts and any conformance/certification scheme that references these GB/T numbers". Both halves are now visible: the SLB air-interface and device-test parts are in the approval pipeline, and the device-test parts (202/203) are precisely the kind of normative reference the iSLA certification scheme (2026-08-17_nearlink-isla-certification-scheme: RF + protocol + interoperability test scope, dual-track alliance listing + MIIT approval) can graduate onto once GB/T numbers exist. The brand-named titles also mark an institutional upgrade over the 46803 generic naming — SparkLink is standardised as itself, not as an anonymised technology description. Watch next: formal publication with GB/T numbers and dates (each part is a 6-month-cycle project already at the approval stage, so publication plausibly lands within months), whether the published SLB air-interface text matches iSLA SparkLink 2.0, and whether the certification scheme re-anchors from alliance T/XS specs to the GB/T family.
