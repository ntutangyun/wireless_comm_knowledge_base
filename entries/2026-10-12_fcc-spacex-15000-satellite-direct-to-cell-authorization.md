## Summary (EN)

The FCC adopted and released Order DA 26-1078 on 2026-10-06 (per the FCC's order document, fcc.gov/document/spacex-approved-ngso-mss-system-direct-device-services), authorizing SpaceX to deploy a constellation of 15,000 non-geostationary-orbit (NGSO) satellites in Very Low Earth Orbit (VLEO), across nine orbital shells between 326 km and 335 km altitude, to provide supplemental coverage from space (SCS) and mobile-satellite service (MSS) within the United States, MSS and direct-to-cell service outside the United States, and fixed-satellite-service (FSS) backhaul plus telemetry, tracking and command (TT&C). The order also clears SpaceX to use the EchoStar-sourced 1695-1710 MHz band for SCS uplink, and waives the rule that would otherwise have required a spectrum-leasing agreement with a ground-based mobile operator before SpaceX could use this spectrum for direct-to-cell service. SpaceX's authority in the EchoStar-acquired spectrum is conditioned on Step Two of the SpaceX-EchoStar transaction, expected on or about 2027-11-30; in the meantime SpaceX may deploy satellites and perform communications payload testing with EchoStar's permission. SpaceX has until October 2032 to deploy half of the 15,000-satellite constellation and until October 2035 to complete deployment (Light Reading reports the specific dates as 2032-10-07 and 2035-10-07). The FCC deferred SpaceX's separate request for the 2020-2025 MHz band, and also deferred any action regarding the 20.2-21.2 GHz and 30.0-31.0 GHz bands, citing heavy federal use. Light Reading reports the new constellation ("V2") is projected to deliver up to 150 Mbps per user, up from roughly 4 Mbps on the current direct-to-device constellation of more than 650 satellites.

## Summary (ZH)

FCC 于 2026 年 10 月 6 日通过并发布了 DA 26-1078 号命令（据 FCC 命令文件 fcc.gov/document/spacex-approved-ngso-mss-system-direct-device-services），批准 SpaceX 部署一个由 15,000 颗非地球静止轨道（NGSO）卫星组成的星座，运行于极低地球轨道（VLEO）、分布在海拔 326 公里至 335 公里之间的九个轨道层，用于在美国境内提供"空间补充覆盖"（SCS）与移动卫星业务（MSS），在美国境外提供 MSS 与直连手机（direct-to-cell）服务，以及提供固定卫星业务（FSS）回传与遥测、跟踪与指令（TT&C）功能。该命令同时放行 SpaceX 使用源自 EchoStar 的 1695-1710 MHz 频段用于 SCS 上行，并豁免了原本要求 SpaceX 必须先与地面移动运营商签订频谱租赁协议、方可将该频谱用于直连手机服务的规则。SpaceX 对 EchoStar 所得频谱的使用权以 SpaceX-EchoStar 交易第二阶段完成为条件（预计于 2027 年 11 月 30 日前后完成）；在此之前，经 EchoStar 许可，SpaceX 可部署卫星并进行通信载荷测试。SpaceX 需在 2032 年 10 月前完成该 15,000 颗卫星星座一半的部署，并在 2035 年 10 月前完成全部部署（Light Reading 给出的具体日期为 2032-10-07 与 2035-10-07）。FCC 对 SpaceX 另外提出的 2020-2025 MHz 频段申请予以暂缓处理，并因联邦用户使用密集，暂缓处理 20.2-21.2 GHz 与 30.0-31.0 GHz 频段。据 Light Reading 报道，这一新星座（"V2"）预计可为每位用户提供最高约 150 Mbps 的速率，高于当前由逾 650 颗卫星组成的直连设备星座约 4 Mbps 的速率。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Order**: FCC DA 26-1078, adopted and released 2026-10-06 (per the FCC's order document).
- **Constellation**: 15,000 NGSO satellites, VLEO, nine shells at 326-335 km altitude.
- **Authorized services**: SCS + MSS (US domestic); MSS + direct-to-cell (outside US); FSS backhaul; TT&C.
- **Spectrum cleared**: EchoStar-sourced 1695-1710 MHz for SCS uplink (per the FCC order); other EchoStar-sourced band figures reported elsewhere were not confirmed against the cited source and are not repeated here.
- **Waiver**: spectrum-leasing-agreement requirement with a ground mobile operator waived for this use.
- **EchoStar transaction gating**: SpaceX's use of the EchoStar-acquired spectrum is conditioned on Step Two of the SpaceX-EchoStar transaction (expected on or about 2027-11-30); until then, SpaceX may deploy satellites and perform communications payload testing with EchoStar's permission.
- **Deployment deadlines**: 50% by October 2032; 100% by October 2035 (Light Reading: 2032-10-07 / 2035-10-07).
- **Deferred**: the 2020-2025 MHz band request, and the 20.2-21.2 GHz and 30.0-31.0 GHz bands, citing heavy federal use.
- **Speed projection** (Light Reading): up to ~150 Mbps/user ("V2") vs. ~4 Mbps on the current direct-to-device constellation of more than 650 satellites.
