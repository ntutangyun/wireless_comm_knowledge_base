## Summary (EN)

2026-09-17 was the closing day of the 2026-09-13/18 Waikoloa interim session. TGbp's own closing report (DCN 11-26-1898r0, Bo Sun/Sanechips) states that the task group held 10 meetings during the week, discussed and approved 30 more Proposed Draft Text (PDT) proposals, and then passed a single motion instructing the technical editor to prepare IEEE P802.11bp D1.0 (including the coexistence-assessment document CAD 11-26/1582r3) and approving a 45-day Working Group Technical Letter Ballot on "Should P802.11bp D1.0 be forwarded to SA Ballot?" -- 22+2 Yes / 0 No / 3 Abstain. This is TGbp's first Working Group Letter Ballot, following the D0.1 -> D0.5 pre-ballot drafting phase this KB has tracked via its PDT-wave digests since 2026-08-21. The closing report's own timeline slide places the next milestones at D2.0 WG recirculation (Mar 2027), SA ballot pool formation (Mar 2027), initial SA ballot on D4.0 (Aug 2027), and final 802.11 WG approval (Jan 2028).

Separately, a new WIN SG (the Wi-Fi 9 / WLAN Intelligent Networking study group) contribution from Nokia (DCN 11-26-1890r0/r2, Kerstin Johnsson et al.) analyzes the overhead of AP-triggered polling for low-latency uplink traffic. The document reports that EDCA gives no bound on UL access delay -- measured P95 UL latency is about 9 ms, and the AC_VO access category does not improve on this because, per the document, the delay is caused by channel blocking rather than contention -- and quantifies polling overhead directly: at a 3 ms polling interval, polling 5/10/20 stations consumes 4.7% / 5.7% / 7.2% of airtime at roughly 12 Mbps and 6.8% / 9.2% / 13.7% at roughly 6 Mbps (an analytical floor; the document notes measured exchanges run 2-2.5x higher). The document's stated conclusion is that trigger-based operation needs both timely AP channel access and a lower-cost way to discover new uplink traffic at idle stations, since existing buffer-status-report (BSR) mechanisms either require an existing UL opportunity to piggyback on or, if run as explicit BSR polling, consume airtime on every poll regardless of whether the station has new data.

A third document, a TGbp contribution from Infineon titled "Security Concerns Analysis" (DCN 11-26-1891r0, Hui Luo and Rakesh Taori), opens with four worries the authors say have been raised about the AMP authentication-frame design under discussion -- that Anonce/Snonce values might be reused with no guarantee they are generated only once; that it may be unsafe to carry uplink data in Authentication Frame 2; that the CCMP-128 authentication tag in Authentication Frame 3 may not authenticate the AP to the station; and that an additional encrypted challenge from the station in Frame 2 with a response from the AP in Frame 3 may therefore be needed -- and then analyzes each in turn. Its conclusions are reassuring rather than alarming. The online nonce-reuse attack is bounded to 250 attempts/second by the 802.11bp PHY, so with a 64-bit Snonce and a good random-number generator a full day of attempts produces a duplicate with probability 1.17x10^-12 (the deck's alternative phrasing: 2.3 billion years to guarantee one). The offline attacks on Frame 2's encrypted uplink data are, per the deck, equivalent to claiming AES-128-CMAC or CCMP-128 itself is unsafe (the best classical attack, Biclique, needs 2^126.1 years); the only hedge offered is that random bytes could be added to the uplink data if quantum known-plaintext attacks were a concern. A CCMP-128 tag, as an AEAD construction, already proves both sides hold the same temporal key, so it authenticates in both directions. And the proposed extra encrypted challenge/response would, depending on whether the optional MIC is present, amount to lengthening the authentication tag plus a response assurance -- leaving, in the deck's words, the AP-to-STA direction stronger than the STA-to-AP direction. Two smaller items uploaded the same day are not detailed individually: DCN 11-26-1892r0 (a routine UCM TIG closing report listing prior contributions and a next-meeting plan) and DCN 11-26-1897r0 (a single-CID editorial terminology clarification for REVmf D3.0). A fourth document, DCN 11-26-1889 (an "Updates" revision of the UL-channel-access-for-AMP-STA draft text, now co-authored with the original 1885 author), restates and expands the same Clause 39.4 draft text already summarised in this KB's 2026-09-17 digest and is not detailed as a separate item.

## Summary (ZH)

2026-09-17 是 2026-09-13 至 18 日 Waikoloa 临时会议的闭幕日。TGbp 自身的闭幕报告（DCN 11-26-1898r0，Bo Sun / Sanechips）指出，该任务组本周共召开 10 次会议，讨论并批准了另外 30 份建议草案文本（PDT）提案，随后通过一项动议：指示技术编辑筹备 IEEE P802.11bp D1.0（纳入共存评估文档 CAD 11-26/1582r3），并批准启动为期 45 天的工作组技术信函投票，投票议题为"P802.11bp D1.0 是否应提交 SA 投票？"——表决结果为 22+2 赞成 / 0 反对 / 3 弃权。这是 TGbp 首次工作组信函投票，此前经历了本知识库自 2026-08-21 起通过 PDT 浪潮系列摘要持续记录的 D0.1 → D0.5 预投票起草阶段。闭幕报告自带的时间表显示，后续里程碑依次为：D2.0 工作组重新投票（2027 年 3 月）、组建 SA 投票池（2027 年 3 月）、基于 D4.0 的首轮 SA 投票（2027 年 8 月），以及 802.11 工作组最终批准（2028 年 1 月）。

另外，WIN SG（即 Wi-Fi 9 / WLAN 智能组网研究组）新收到一份来自诺基亚的贡献（DCN 11-26-1890r0/r2，Kerstin Johnsson 等），分析了针对低时延上行流量的 AP 触发式轮询所带来的开销。文档指出，EDCA 无法为上行接入时延提供确定性上界——实测 P95 上行时延约为 9 毫秒，且 AC_VO 接入类别对此并无改善，文档解释这是因为该时延源于信道阻塞而非竞争——并直接量化了轮询开销：以 3 毫秒轮询间隔为例，轮询 5/10/20 个站点在约 12 Mbps 时分别消耗 4.7%／5.7%／7.2% 的空口时间，在约 6 Mbps 时分别消耗 6.8%／9.2%／13.7%（这是一个分析下限，文档指出实测交互开销约为其 2–2.5 倍）。文档给出的结论是：触发式接入既需要 AP 及时获得信道接入机会，也需要一种更低成本的方式来发现空闲站点产生的新上行流量，因为现有的缓冲区状态报告（BSR）机制要么依赖已有的上行传输机会来搭载，要么在采用显式 BSR 轮询时，无论站点是否有新数据都会在每次轮询中消耗空口时间。

第三份文档是英飞凌提交的 TGbp 文稿《安全问题分析》（Security Concerns Analysis，DCN 11-26-1891r0，Hui Luo、Rakesh Taori）。文稿开篇列出作者称已被提出的四项担忧——Anonce/Snonce 值可能被重复使用且无法保证仅生成一次；在认证帧 2 中携带上行数据可能不安全；认证帧 3 中的 CCMP-128 认证标签可能无法实现 AP 对站点的认证；因而可能需要由站点在认证帧 2 中发送额外的加密挑战值、AP 在认证帧 3 中作出响应——随后逐一分析。其结论偏向“无需过虑”而非“存在漏洞”：在线 nonce 重用攻击受 802.11bp PHY 限制，每秒最多 250 次，若 Snonce 为 64 位且站点具备良好的随机数发生器，攻击者连续尝试一天产生重复值的概率为 1.17x10^-12（文稿的另一种表述：需持续 23 亿年才能保证出现一次重复）；针对认证帧 2 加密上行数据的离线攻击，按文稿的说法等价于宣称 AES-128-CMAC 或 CCMP-128 本身不安全（已知最佳经典攻击 Biclique 需 2^126.1 年），唯一的保留意见是若担心量子已知明文攻击，可在上行数据中加入随机字节；CCMP-128 标签作为 AEAD 构造，本身即可证明双方持有相同的临时密钥，因而是双向认证；而所提议的额外加密挑战/响应，视可选 MIC 是否存在，等价于加长认证标签并附加一次响应保证——用文稿原话说，结果是“不对称的”：AP 对站点方向的认证强度高于站点对 AP 方向。同日上传但未逐条详述的另有两份较小文档：DCN 11-26-1892r0（一份例行的 UCM TIG 闭幕报告，列出既有贡献并给出下次会议计划）与 DCN 11-26-1897r0（针对 REVmf D3.0 的单一 CID 编辑性术语澄清）。此外，DCN 11-26-1889（"更新版"AMP 站点上行信道接入草案文本，现由 1885 号文档原作者共同署名）重述并扩展了本知识库 2026-09-17 摘要中已总结过的第 39.4 条草案文本，故不再作为独立条目详述。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Approval to generate D1.0 and the first WG Letter Ballot mark TGbp's transition from pre-ballot PDT drafting (D0.1 through D0.5, tracked in this KB's digests since 2026-08-21) into formal balloting -- a concrete standards-track milestone for the `amp-iot` bin. The Nokia WIN SG contribution is the first document this KB has recorded that quantifies AP-triggered polling overhead with specific airtime percentages for the emerging Wi-Fi 9 low-latency use case. The Infineon security review is a direct follow-on to the AMP-security PDT series this KB has tracked from the same authors (Hui Luo/Infineon) since 2026-08-21, here shifting from proposing security mechanisms to analyzing the attack scenarios raised against them -- and concluding that each stays within the standard security assumptions of AES-128.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

批准生成 D1.0 与首轮工作组信函投票的启动，标志着 TGbp 从预投票阶段的 PDT 起草工作（D0.1 至 D0.5，本知识库自 2026-08-21 起通过摘要持续记录）正式进入信函投票阶段——这是 `amp-iot` 分类下一个具体的标准化进展节点。诺基亚提交的 WIN SG 文档是本知识库首次记录的、针对新兴 Wi-Fi 9 低时延应用场景、以具体空口时间占比量化 AP 触发式轮询开销的文档。英飞凌的安全审查则是本知识库自 2026-08-21 起持续跟踪的、同一作者（Hui Luo / 英飞凌）AMP 安全 PDT 系列的直接后续——本次内容从提出安全机制转向分析针对这些机制被提出的攻击场景，并得出结论：这些攻击均未超出 AES-128 的标准安全假设。
