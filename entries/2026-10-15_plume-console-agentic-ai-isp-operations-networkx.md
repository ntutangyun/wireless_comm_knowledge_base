## Summary (EN)

Wi-Fi NOW (Claus Hetting, article dated 13 October 2026) reports that Plume launched Plume Console at Network X in Vienna, described as an agentic AI operations platform for internet service providers (ISPs). Portuguese ISP NOS is reported, according to the company, to be one of the first adopters. Per Plume, Console gives ISP teams one place to investigate subscriber issues, create and deploy agents and direct actions, and it works across Plume WiFi, Plume Security, Plume Care and existing business systems. It includes ready-made agents for network operations, security analysts, care teams and marketers, and engineering teams can build custom agents.

The article says Console uses a hierarchy of specialised, deterministic agents that monitor and act on outages, device disconnections, thermal events, application-performance degradation and security issues, and presents findings through a prioritised action board, dynamic insight tiles and detailed reports. Users can move from a network-wide view down to a single device or application session. The article says Plume has access to connectivity data from more than 60 million homes and hundreds of millions of connected client devices and CPEs; Plume says its existing platform already resolves the vast majority of customer-experience issues automatically, with Console targeting the remainder.

The article describes four examples from demonstrations shown to Wi-Fi NOW. In an outage case, Console identified 55 offline homes at an unnamed Austrian ISP, determined a likely upstream cause and deployed a monitoring agent for the affected subscribers. In a thermal case, repeated reboots of Wi-Fi extenders were traced to placement on dusty floors, suppressed fan operation, temperature and firmware behaviour. In a streaming case, Console drilled from a network-wide 92% positive streaming-experience rate to one household, device and Netflix session and suggested repositioning an extender. In a care case, Console detected an IoT device with frequent disconnections and sent the subscriber step-by-step recovery instructions on a smartphone without a support call. The article does not present independent verification of any performance results.

## Summary (ZH)

Wi-Fi NOW（Claus Hetting，文章日期 2026 年 10 月 13 日）报道，Plume 在维也纳的 Network X 上发布了 Plume Console，称其为面向互联网服务提供商（ISP）的智能体 AI 运营平台。据公司称，葡萄牙运营商 NOS 是首批采用者之一。按 Plume 的说法，Console 让 ISP 团队在同一处排查用户问题、创建并部署智能体、指挥处置动作，并覆盖 Plume WiFi、Plume Security、Plume Care 以及现有业务系统。平台带有面向网络运营、安全分析、客户服务和营销团队的现成智能体，工程团队也可构建自定义智能体。

文章称，Console 采用一组专用的确定性智能体层级，监控并处理断网、设备掉线、过热事件、应用性能劣化和安全问题，并通过优先级行动看板、动态洞察卡片和详细报告呈现结果。用户可以从全网视图一直下钻到单个设备或应用会话。文章称 Plume 可获取超过 6,000 万户家庭及数亿台联网终端与 CPE 的连接数据；Plume 表示其现有平台已能自动解决绝大多数客户体验问题，Console 针对的是其余部分。

Wi-Fi NOW 观看了四个演示。断网案例中，Console 识别出某奥地利运营商（未具名）的 55 个离线家庭，判断可能的上游原因，并为受影响用户部署了监控智能体。过热案例中，Wi-Fi 扩展器反复重启被追溯到放置在多尘地面、风扇运转受抑制、温度以及固件行为。流媒体案例中，Console 从全网 92% 的正面流媒体体验率下钻到某个家庭、设备和 Netflix 会话，并建议调整扩展器位置。客户服务案例中，Console 检测到一台频繁掉线的物联网设备，并在用户手机上发送分步恢复指引，无需拨打支持电话。文章未提供对任何性能结果的独立验证。
