## Summary (EN)

On 30 September 2026, Samsung announced the Galaxy SmartTag3, its new item tracker succeeding the SmartTag2, with regional editions published the same day (e.g., Global, US, Australia). Samsung says Bluetooth 6.0 Channel Sounding lets Compass View give distance and direction guidance from up to 60 m (about three times the previous generation), and that as users get closer UWB "delivers even greater precision, creating a more continuous finding experience from long range to close range." A third layer, the crowdsourced Galaxy Find Network (described as drawing on more than 900 million participating Galaxy devices, a figure Samsung bases on Galaxy smartphones sold), supplies encrypted location updates when the tag is out of direct Bluetooth/UWB range. The SmartTag3 is also the first in the Galaxy SmartTag lineup with cross-OS support (Galaxy and iOS; Compass View, Ring Phone and Left Behind Alert remain Galaxy-only), letting both Galaxy and iOS devices use SmartThings Find's core location and sharing features. Physically, the device is reported as roughly 35% smaller by volume and 33% lighter than the SmartTag2, carries an IP67 dust/water rating, and is rated (per the US release) for up to 550 days of battery life in standard use (790 days in Power Saving Mode). Pricing (US release) is $29.99 for a single unit or $99.99 for a four-pack; SmartTag3 launches first in Korea on 7 October 2026, followed by the US (by early November 2026), with additional markets to follow in 2027.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Positioning: long-range Channel Sounding + close-range UWB + Galaxy Find Network beyond direct range. Samsung says Channel Sounding gives Compass View distance/direction guidance up to 60 m (~3x prior generation), and that UWB adds precision as users get closer.
- Range claim: Channel Sounding's 60 m is reported as roughly 3x the SmartTag2-generation Compass View directional-finding range.
- New cross-platform support: first in the Galaxy SmartTag lineup to let both Galaxy and iOS devices use SmartThings Find's core location/sharing features (Compass View, Ring Phone, Left Behind Alert remain Galaxy-only).
- Physical changes vs SmartTag2: ~35% smaller by volume, ~33% lighter; IP67 dust/water resistance; aluminum-accented button.
- Battery (per the US release): up to 550 days standard use, 790 days in Power Saving Mode.
- Pricing/availability (US release): $29.99 single / $99.99 four-pack; Korea launch 7 October 2026, then US (by early November 2026), with additional markets to follow in 2027.
- Per Samsung's footnote: Bluetooth Channel Sounding currently works only with Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8, S26 Ultra and S26+; the 60 m range is measured under line-of-sight conditions; Compass View is not available on iOS.
- Galaxy Find Network relies on 900M+ participating devices (a figure Samsung bases on Galaxy smartphones sold).
