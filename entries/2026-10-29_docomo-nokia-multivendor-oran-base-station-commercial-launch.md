## Summary (EN)

NTT DOCOMO announced on 11 September 2026 that it began commercial operation, on 9 September 2026, of Nokia's latest base station equipment, which complies with O-RAN ALLIANCE specifications and forms part of Nokia's AirScale platform. The equipment is built from two baseband cards, "Levante" for data processing and "Ponente" for control processing, and supports both LTE and 5G. DOCOMO states that, according to its own research as of 9 September 2026, it is the world's first commercial operator to deploy this equipment in a multi-vendor configuration, in which the Nokia baseband connects to O-RAN-compliant radio units from several suppliers. The release does not name the radio-unit vendors, and the "first" claim is DOCOMO's own and was not independently verified here.

The release says the new equipment is expected to accommodate more radio units while consuming less power than Nokia's existing base station equipment. High-power radio units are meant for wide-area coverage and compact units for urban sites with limited space and high-demand venues. DOCOMO planned a first field use on 12 September 2026 in a mobile base station vehicle at the 59th Joso Kinugawa Fireworks Festival, using Nokia's Dual Boost Massive MIMO optimisation technology, and then nationwide deployment starting with urban areas, stadiums and event venues. A memorandum of understanding signed on 12 August 2026 covers AI-era network architectures, virtualised/cloud RAN, SON and RIC automation across multi-vendor equipment, and autonomous networks.

## Key technical points (EN)

- Equipment: Nokia AirScale baseband built from Levante (data processing) and Ponente (control processing) cards; supports LTE and 5G; O-RAN ALLIANCE-compliant.
- Multi-vendor: the baseband connects to O-RAN-compliant radio units from multiple vendors; the vendors and the open-fronthaul profile used are not stated in the release.
- Efficiency claim: more radio units per baseband and lower power than Nokia's earlier base station equipment; no measured figures are given.
- Radio-unit variety: high-power units for wide-area coverage, compact units for dense urban sites and venues; frequency bands are not specified.
- Rollout: commercial operation from 9 September 2026; mobile base station vehicle with Dual Boost Massive MIMO on 12 September; nationwide deployment starting with high-traffic locations.
- Roadmap context: a 12 August 2026 MoU on AI-era architectures, vRAN/Cloud RAN, multi-vendor SON/RIC automation and autonomous networks.
