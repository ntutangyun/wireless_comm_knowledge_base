## Summary (EN)

On 18 May 2026 Qorvo announced that standards-based Ultra-Wideband real-time location has been integrated into **enterprise Wi-Fi access points (EAPs)** from unnamed enterprise Wi-Fi OEMs, so that the existing Wi-Fi estate — its PoE power, backhaul and management plane — doubles as the anchor infrastructure for a UWB RTLS instead of a separately cabled "shadow network" of dedicated anchors. Qorvo claims three industry firsts from this programme: FiRa-compliant RTLS deployments running on EAPs in healthcare and logistics, omlox-compliant RTLS deployments on EAPs in automotive manufacturing (mining is also cited), and combined FiRa + omlox support within a single enterprise Wi-Fi infrastructure. Alexis Bizalion (Director of Product Marketing & Enablement) frames the goal as "making real-time location a native capability of the network."

The tag-side companion is the new **QPK3000** hardware module for tags and end devices: a fully integrated UWB module that interoperates with EAP-hosted anchors, dedicated anchors and other UWB infrastructure, and is compatible with both FiRa- and omlox-based systems, so a product vendor can add RTLS-tag capability "through a simple integration process" without RF design. In a follow-up Microwaves & RF article (18 Jun 2026) Bizalion positions the approach for industrial IoT: 10–30 cm positioning in cluttered factories versus metre-scale BLE/Wi-Fi RSSI, sub-100 ms update cycles for hundreds to thousands of tags, FiRa secure ranging for zone-based access and machine interlocks that require verified physical proximity, and omlox as the multi-vendor "location fabric" that lets UWB, RFID and BLE coexist under one location API. Neither piece names the Wi-Fi OEMs, the UWB IC inside the access points, or channel/range specifications.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The KB's UWB RTLS coverage so far is anchor-centric: NXP's omlox Starter Kit (2026-08-15) and Murata's factory tracking system (2026-08-05) both assume dedicated UWB anchors, and the Apple/Google "indoor GPS" piece (2026-07-28) is phone-centric. This is the first entry where the *Wi-Fi access point itself* becomes the UWB anchor and where FiRa (secure ranging, consumer heritage) and omlox (industrial location API) run on the same enterprise infrastructure — the convergence that omlox and FiRa promised when they aligned in March 2025 (referenced in the omlox Starter Kit entry). For enterprise Wi-Fi it also adds a concrete UWB-in-AP data point alongside the Wi-Fi 7 deployment forecasts (2026-08-13 Dell'Oro entry).
