## Summary (EN)

The official IEEE 802.11 Working Group project-timelines page (page stamp 2026-10-27) now lists IEEE Std P802.11bi-2026, "Enhanced Privacy Protection" (task group TGbi), in its Published section with a Published date of 2026-10-26. The row shows the draft at D6.00 and the incorporated baselines 802.11-2024, 802.11bh-2024, 802.11be-2024, 802.11bk-2025 and 802.11bf-2025.

The same row records the path to that point: WG letter ballots on D1.0 (2025-02-27, 89%), D2.0 (2025-09-02, 94%), D3.0 (2025-12-12, 96%) and D4.0 (2026-02-05, 98%), followed by IEEE SA ballots on D4.0 (2026-03-13, 92%), D5.0 (2026-06-10, 92%), D6.0 (2026-08-04, 94%) and D6.0 (2026-08-22, 94%). The row's later milestone columns carry the dates 2026-07-01 (twice), 2026-09-01 and 2026-10-26; the last is the Published column.

The "Recent standards approvals" section of the 802.11 Working Group home page states that IEEE 802.11bi-2026 "was published on October 23, 2026" (that item carries no separate date stamp). The Timelines page date (2026-10-26) therefore differs by three days from the home page; this entry uses 23 October as date_published, and the IEEE Standards Association page itself was not located in this scan.

## Summary (ZH)

IEEE 802.11 工作组官方项目时间线页面（页面标注日期 2026-10-27）已将 IEEE Std P802.11bi-2026《Enhanced Privacy Protection（增强隐私保护）》（TGbi）列入 Published（已发布）栏，发布日期为 2026-10-26。该行显示草案版本 D6.00，所并入的基线为 802.11-2024、802.11bh-2024、802.11be-2024、802.11bk-2025 与 802.11bf-2025。

同一行还记录了此前的流程：WG Letter Ballot 对 D1.0（2025-02-27，89%）、D2.0（2025-09-02，94%）、D3.0（2025-12-12，96%）与 D4.0（2026-02-05，98%）的投票，随后是 IEEE SA 对 D4.0（2026-03-13，92%）、D5.0（2026-06-10，92%）、D6.0（2026-08-04，94%）与 D6.0（2026-08-22，94%）的投票。该行后续里程碑列中的日期为 2026-07-01（两处）、2026-09-01 与 2026-10-26，最后一个为 Published 列。

802.11 工作组主页"Recent standards approvals"栏明确写道 IEEE 802.11bi-2026"was published on October 23, 2026"（该条目没有单独的日期标注）。因此 Timelines 页面的日期（2026-10-26）与主页相差三天；本条目以 10 月 23 日作为 date_published，本次扫描未找到 IEEE SA 自身的标准页面。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Official status line:** IEEE Std P802.11bi-2026, "Enhanced Privacy Protection", TGbi, draft D6.00, listed under Published with date 2026-10-26 on the Timelines page stamped 2026-10-27.
- **Ballot trail on the page:** four WG letter ballots (89%, 94%, 96%, 98%) and four IEEE SA ballots (92%, 92%, 94%, 94%).
- **Baselines incorporated:** 802.11-2024, 802.11bh-2024, 802.11be-2024, 802.11bk-2025, 802.11bf-2025.
- **Date discrepancy:** the WG home page gives 23 October 2026; the Timelines page gives 2026-10-26.
- **Downstream:** the November 2026 WG session report already in this KB states that 802.11bi-2026 is to be rolled into REVmf during a recirculation after the initial SA ballot (see 2026-11-16_ieee-mentor-wg-november-2026-session-report-motions-revmf-sa-ballot).
