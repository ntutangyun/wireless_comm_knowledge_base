## Summary (EN)

A Bluetooth SIG blog post by Jason Marcel (19 October 2026) discusses Bluetooth in healthcare. Citing ABI Research, it says annual Bluetooth healthcare shipments are projected to rise 51% by 2030, from 110 million to 166 million devices; patient monitoring is the largest and fastest-growing category (80 million to 128 million annually), and home monitoring is forecast to rise 25% (28 million to 35 million) over five years.

The post lists challenges in three groups: patient experience (setup complexity, charging, accessibility, reliance on personal devices), clinician workflow (patient-generated data lacking context, provenance and quality indicators) and operational scale (multi-device deployments made costly by fragmented diagnostics, lifecycle services, security evidence and integration patterns). Technical areas discussed are common methods for discovery, connection, troubleshooting and reconnection; interoperability through the Bluetooth Generic Health Sensor (GHS) specification, which the post says can reduce custom profiles and proprietary integrations (the only profile it names); ultra-low-power operation and mechanisms to preserve and synchronise data across connectivity interruptions; and information on when, where and how data was collected. Growth areas named are home remote patient monitoring, continuous-sensing wearables, clinical workflow integration and ambient/multimodal sensing. SIG Medical Devices Working Group participation is open to Contributing Adopter and Associate members.

## Summary (ZH)

蓝牙技术联盟博客文章（作者 Jason Marcel，2026 年 10 月 19 日）讨论蓝牙在医疗健康领域的作用。文章引用 ABI Research 称，蓝牙医疗设备年出货量预计到 2030 年增长 51%，由 1.1 亿台增至 1.66 亿台；患者监护是最大且增长最快的类别（年出货量由 8000 万增至 1.28 亿台），家庭监测预计五年内增长 25%（由 2800 万增至 3500 万台）。

文章将挑战分为三类：患者体验（设置复杂、充电、无障碍、依赖个人设备）、临床工作流（患者生成的数据缺少上下文、来源与质量指示）以及规模化运营（诊断、生命周期服务、安全证据和集成模式碎片化导致多设备部署成本高）。文中涉及的技术方向包括：发现、连接、排障与重连的通用方法；通过蓝牙 Generic Health Sensor（GHS）规范实现互操作（文章称其可减少自定义 profile 与专有集成，也是文中唯一点名的 profile）；超低功耗，以及在连接中断时保存和同步数据的机制；关于数据何时、何地、如何采集的信息。点名的增长领域为家庭远程患者监护、连续感知可穿戴设备、临床工作流集成以及环境/多模态感知。SIG 医疗设备工作组向 Contributing Adopter 与 Associate 会员开放参与。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Forecasts (ABI Research, per the post)**: healthcare shipments 110M to 166M by 2030 (+51%); patient monitoring 80M to 128M; home monitoring 28M to 35M (+25%).
- **Interoperability**: Generic Health Sensor (GHS) specification, said to reduce custom profiles and proprietary integrations.
- **Data continuity**: ultra-low power and mechanisms to preserve and synchronise data when connectivity is interrupted.
- **Data validity**: when, where and how data was collected, for security, privacy and clinical confidence.
- **Challenges**: setup complexity, charging, accessibility; missing context/provenance; fragmented multi-device integration.
