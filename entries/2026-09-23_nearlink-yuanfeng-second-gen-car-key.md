## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-21, IT Times Net (IT时代网), via Tencent News, reported that Yuanfeng Technology (远峰科技) — already covered in this KB as the first Tier-1 supplier to mass-produce a SparkLink digital car key (`2026-09-03_nearlink-car-key-tier1-landscape-q1-2026`) — has released a **second-generation SparkLink digital car-key scheme**, upgrading precision, bandwidth and communication range over its first-generation product, and extending the use case "from basic lock/unlock toward spatial awareness and contactless mobility scenarios," per the article's framing.

The article describes a vehicle-home-parking coordination scenario: as the car approaches a private parking spot, SparkLink's high-precision ranging triggers an automatic smart ground-lock to sense the vehicle and lower for unlocking, then automatically opens as the car nears the garage and closes again once parked — without the driver opening a phone app. The second generation's higher communication bandwidth is said to let the system connect additional endpoint types such as smart EV charging guns and in-vehicle robots for coordinated device operation. In a welcome scenario, the scheme can detect the key signal from 10 meters away, triggering welcome lights, pop-out flush door handles, and unfolding side mirrors.

On compatibility, the scheme supports phones and wearables from Huawei, Apple, Xiaomi, OPPO, vivo and Honor, and is compatible with Bluetooth 6.0 Channel Sounding, allowing a vehicle to switch between the two ranging technologies without hardware changes. On performance, the scheme is said to be optimized for underground-garage environments with complex signal conditions and pillar obstruction, supporting remote vehicle control, find-my-car light/horn activation, and one-touch remote parking; a single vehicle supports up to 4 concurrent key-ranging sessions, enabling independent sensing and zone-based control when multiple people travel together. On security, the scheme is stated to comply with the **SESIP Level 3** security certification standard.

The source article carries a disclaimer that it contains AI-assisted content, which this entry notes as a caveat on the specific figures (10 m detection range, 4-key concurrency, SESIP L3) — they should be treated as the vendor/outlet's own reported specifications pending independent confirmation, not as independently verified test results.
