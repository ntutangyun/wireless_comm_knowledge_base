## Summary (EN)

At its 2026-09-30 Open Meeting the FCC took two separate actions. By a 2-1 vote it adopted an order codifying that satellite licensing — spacecraft manufacturing, launch authorizations, orbital deployment, on-orbit operations, spectrum authorizations and ground-station earth terminals — is categorically excluded from NEPA environmental review; that order takes effect 30 days after Federal Register publication. In a separate action at the same meeting it voted to open more than 1,000 MHz of spectrum for commercial satellite communication services — the adoption of the Satellite Spectrum Abundance item this KB tracked as a draft on 2026-09-15.
