## Summary (EN)

The IEEE 802.11 Working Group's official Timelines page now records that TGbt (Post-Quantum Cryptography) completed its D1.0 Working Group Letter Ballot on 2026-09-03 with 89% approval. This is the first concrete ballot-result checkpoint this KB has recorded for TGbt: the most recent prior status this KB carried (`2026-09-04_ieee-tgbn-d20-lb296-recirc-77pct`) listed TGbt as D1.00 with "no ballots yet, WG ballot predicted." The Timelines page corroborates the ballot's existence with IEEE Mentor activity already in this KB: DCN 11-26-1629, "IEEE 802.11bt LB297 comments on D1.0" (Jay Yang, ZTE, uploaded 2026-09-04), recorded in `2026-09-07_ieee-mentor-multi-group-digest` as a comment spreadsheet against this same ballot — that entry could confirm the ballot had taken place but not carry a numeric approval figure, which the Timelines page now supplies. TGbt's Sponsor Ballot has not yet been initiated. The page's other task-group figures are unchanged from the 2026-09-04 checkpoint: TGbn D2.0 Working Group Letter Ballot at 77% (2026-09-02), TGbi D6.0 Standards Association ballot at 94% (2026-08-22), and TGmf D3.0 Working Group Letter Ballot at 95% (2026-08-31).
