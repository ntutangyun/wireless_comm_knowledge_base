## Summary (EN)

Zyxel Networks announced (via Wi-Fi NOW, July 8, 2026) three outdoor products shipping in Q3 2026, extending its cloud-managed portfolio into industrial and large-public-venue (LPV) outdoor coverage: the flagship WBE665S ruggedised Wi-Fi 7 AP, the NWA55AX PRO outdoor Wi-Fi 6 AP, and the NWA55AX point-to-point wireless bridge.

The WBE665S is the technically interesting one: a 22 Gbps aggregate, 12-spatial-stream, triple-radio 802.11be AP with an internal 120-degree wide-angle "smart antenna", IP67-rated against dust and water with an operating range of -40°C to +70°C — aimed at industrial sites, warehouses, stadiums, outdoor campuses and municipal Wi-Fi. All three products are managed through Zyxel's Nebula cloud platform. Zyxel positions itself as a cost-effective alternative to the large enterprise vendors and was among the first enterprise vendors to ship Wi-Fi 7 APs; the outdoor push is framed as portfolio completion (Wi-Fi 7 + switching + security + cloud management + outdoor) rather than a point product.
