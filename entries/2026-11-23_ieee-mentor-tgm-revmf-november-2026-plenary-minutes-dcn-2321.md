## Summary (EN)

DCN 11-26-2321r3, "Minutes for REVmf 2026 November Plenary - Bangkok" (Jon Rosdahl, Qualcomm; document date 2026-11-12; r3 uploaded 20 November 2026), records the REVmf task group's sessions on Monday to Thursday of the November 2026 plenary under TG chair Michael Montemurro (Huawei). In the editor's report (Po-Kai Huang, Intel), Draft P802.11REVmf D3.1 was available in the members' area and the comment database, as of 3 November 2026, showed 531 resolutions approved, 35 ready for motion and 208 other; the stated plan is to incorporate motioned documents after the November meeting to produce D4.0 no later than 30 November 2026. The editor also recommends using the placeholder `<ANA>` for the bit location of any reserved bit a document needs, rather than assigning one, and describes how errors found in motioned resolutions are handled.

The minutes record comment-resolution reviews by document (for example 11-26-1978 by Mark Rison (Samsung), 1772 by Binita Gupta (Cisco), 1973/1974/1986/2298/2299 by Brian Hart (Cisco), 2198 by Jouni Malinen (Qualcomm) on PTK key derivation, 2297 by Morse Micro on S1G regulatory text, 2324 on 2.4 GHz channelization by Guido Hiertz (Ericsson)), with each resolution marked ready for motion or more work required. In a straw poll on CID 2624 about "may" or "should" for sending a channel switch announcement, the result was 12 for "may" and 5 for "should".

The Thursday PM2 session lists the motions with results. #84 approved the November session CIDs in the Motion Oct B, Oct C, Nov A and Nov B tabs (unanimous consent). #85 incorporated 11-26-2297 (S1G regulatory text, CID 1214 again) (unanimous). #86 instructed the editor to move the "NDP Preamble Clarified" field of the Extended Capabilities element to bit 113 (stated as approved by the ANA manager; one no vote). #87 incorporated the PTK key-derivation cleanup of 11-26-2198 (unanimous). #88 on CID 2726 (regulatory topic, added in a late revision) failed 3-9-6 and the CID returned to More Work Required. #89 (CID 2294, vendor-specific inheritance) passed with one abstention. #90 (CIDs 2439 and 2440, a recorded vote) failed 5-4-4 and the CIDs returned to More Work Required. #91 (CID 2297, rejected because the commenter withdrew it) passed with one abstention. #92 (CIDs 2573 and 2575), #93 (CIDs 2578 and 2514) and #94 (CID 2614) passed with one abstention each. #95 rejected the remaining CIDs in the More Work Required tab (one abstention) and #96 those in the Submission Required tab (unanimous), with stated exceptions. The closing motions approved a 15-day WG recirculation ballot asking "Should REVmf D4.0 be forwarded to SA Ballot?" (11 yes, 1 no, 0 abstain), the conditional SA-ballot report for the LMSC (11-1-0), and a mixed-mode ad hoc on 9-11 February 2027 (preferred venue San Jose, CA) for SA-ballot comment resolution. The TGmf timeline in the minutes lists D4.0 initial SA ballot in December 2026, TGbi roll-in in January-February 2027, D5.0 recirculation SA ballot in July 2027, a request for LMSC approval to go to RevCom in November 2027 and RevCom/SASB approval in February 2028.

## Summary (ZH)

DCN 11-26-2321r3《Minutes for REVmf 2026 November Plenary - Bangkok》（Qualcomm 的 Jon Rosdahl；文档日期 2026-11-12；r3 于 2026 年 11 月 20 日上传）记录了 REVmf 任务组在 2026 年 11 月全会周一至周四的会议，TG 主席为华为的 Michael Montemurro。编辑报告（Intel 的 Po-Kai Huang）称 Draft P802.11REVmf D3.1 已在成员区提供；截至 2026 年 11 月 3 日，意见数据库显示 531 条解决方案已批准、35 条待动议、208 条其他；计划在 11 月会议后并入已动议文档，最迟于 2026 年 11 月 30 日产出 D4.0。编辑还建议，文档需要占用保留比特时，比特位置写作占位符 `<ANA>`，而不要自行指定，并说明了对已动议解决方案中发现错误的处理方式。

纪要按文档记录了意见处理评审（如 Samsung 的 Mark Rison 的 11-26-1978、Cisco 的 Binita Gupta 的 1772、Cisco 的 Brian Hart 的 1973/1974/1986/2298/2299、Qualcomm 的 Jouni Malinen 关于 PTK 密钥派生的 2198、Morse Micro 关于 S1G 监管文本的 2297、Ericsson 的 Guido Hiertz 关于 2.4 GHz 信道划分的 2324），每项解决方案被标记为待动议或需继续工作。在关于 CID 2624 发送信道切换通告用“may”还是“should”的 Straw Poll 中，结果为 12 票“may”、5 票“should”。

周四 PM2 时段列出了各动议及结果。#84 批准 Motion Oct B、Oct C、Nov A、Nov B 各页签中的 11 月会议 CID（无异议通过）。#85 并入 11-26-2297（S1G 监管文本，CID 1214 再次处理）（无异议）。#86 指示编辑将 Extended Capabilities 元素中的“NDP Preamble Clarified”字段移至比特 113（称已获 ANA 管理者认可；一票反对）。#87 并入 11-26-2198 的 PTK 密钥派生清理（无异议）。#88 关于 CID 2726（监管议题，在较晚的修订中加入）以 3-9-6 未通过，该 CID 回到“需继续工作”。#89（CID 2294，厂商特定继承）一票弃权通过。#90（CID 2439 与 2440，记名投票）以 5-4-4 未通过，两条 CID 回到“需继续工作”。#91（CID 2297，因评论人撤回而拒绝）在一票弃权下通过。#92（CID 2573 与 2575）、#93（CID 2578 与 2514）与 #94（CID 2614）均在一票弃权下通过。#95 以拒绝方式处理“需继续工作”页签中其余 CID（一票弃权），#96 处理“需提交文稿”页签中的 CID（无异议），均列有例外。收尾动议批准了一次为期 15 天、询问“REVmf D4.0 是否应转交 SA 投票”的 WG 再循环投票（11 赞成、1 反对、0 弃权）、提交 LMSC 的有条件 SA 投票报告（11-1-0），以及 2027 年 2 月 9-11 日用于 SA 投票意见处理的混合模式 ad hoc 会议（首选地点美国加州圣何塞）。纪要中的 TGmf 时间线列出：2026 年 12 月 D4.0 首轮 SA 投票，2027 年 1-2 月并入 TGbi，2027 年 7 月 D5.0 SA 再循环，2027 年 11 月申请 LMSC 批准提交 RevCom，2028 年 2 月 RevCom/SASB 批准。
