## Summary (EN)

WorldVue — a managed-services provider for hospitality and multi-dwelling-unit (MDU) networks holding roughly a 25% share of North America's hospitality managed Wi-Fi market — reported that the Telecom Infra Project's OpenWiFi platform has "progressed well beyond the proof-of-concept stage" and now serves as a proven alternative to proprietary enterprise Wi-Fi platforms. The company operates more than 3,500 OpenWiFi access points and switches in production hotel and residential networks.

The reliability numbers WorldVue cites are the story: only 10 device-related support cases over six months across the fleet, and a hardware issue rate below 0.02%. WorldVue attributes the operational win to the disaggregated model's consistent software framework across hardware from multiple suppliers (Edgecore, CyberTAN among them), which reduces staff training requirements, support costs, and vendor lock-in while preserving hardware choice.

For the broader Wi-Fi ecosystem, this is one of the first public at-scale production testimonials for OpenWiFi in the hospitality/MDU vertical — a segment historically dominated by proprietary controller-based platforms. It signals that open, disaggregated Wi-Fi stacks are crossing from trials into commercially defensible operations.
