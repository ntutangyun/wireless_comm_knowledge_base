## Summary (EN)
TGbr (Enhanced Light Communications Task Group) has two uploads on 24 November 2026 (ET), both by Nikola Serafimovski (FTH). DCN 11-26-2033r2, "D0.1 Implementation Comments" (an .xlsx workbook; the workbook was read in full), was uploaded at 09:21 ET. DCN 11-26-2392r0, "November 2026 TGbr telecon agenda" (a .pptx), was uploaded at 02:00 ET. The agenda's last slide lists "Consider comments against D0.1", "Doc. 11-26/2032r0", "Doc. 11-26/2033r0" and AOB. The same slide set names the group's officers as Nikola Serafimovski (Chair), Stefan Videv and Mohamed Islim (Vice Chairs), Volker Jungnickel (Technical Editor) and Tuncer Baykas (Secretary).

The workbook has three sheets: "TGbr D0.1 Comments", "Discussion Guide" and "Table 41-1 values". Its Discussion Guide states that the basis is the deck 11-26/2032r0 dated 19 October 2026, that it carries 26 proposed comments across seven retained topics, that comment numbers are local rather than ballot CIDs, that locations use the printed page/line anchors of TGbr D0.1, and that cross-references were checked against TGmf D3.1. The seven topics are: (1) ELC capability exchange (comments 1-4); (2) band baselines and LC compatibility (5-7); (3) channel mapping (8-11); (4) conversion and timing (12-14); (5) transmit performance and emission control (15-17); (6) receiver measurements (18-22); and (7) management and conformance (23-26). The comments sheet itself lists 27 rows, all submitted by Nikola Serafimovski (FTH): 26 are in category Technical and comment 26 (architecture heading and cross-reference cleanup) is Editorial; comment 27, a shorter version of the per-band common-baseline text, is filed under topic 2.

A "Disposition Status" column has entries for seven of the comments at this revision. Comments 5, 6 and 7 are marked "revise" with revised text in the Disposition Detail column; comments 8, 9, 10 and 11 are marked "TBD". For 8, 9 and 10 the detail reads "Waiting for contribution to define text and new tables for 6 GHz mapping in ELC. Assigned to Mohamed." For comment 11 the detail gives text to append to 41.2.3.4 and states that the second part is TBD. The remaining comments have no Disposition Status entered; comment 4 carries only a Disposition Detail note ("After 41.1.2 insert 41.1.3, TBD.").

## Key technical points (EN)
- **DCN 11-26-2033r2 · TGbr** — [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2033&is_year=2026) D0.1 Implementation Comments. Nikola Serafimovski (FTH). Seven topics (the Discussion Guide counts 26 comments; the comments sheet lists 27 rows); uploaded 24 November 2026 09:21 ET.
- **DCN 11-26-2392r0 · TGbr** — [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2392&is_year=2026) November 2026 TGbr telecon agenda. Nikola Serafimovski (FTH). Agenda slide: consider comments against D0.1 (11-26/2032r0, 11-26/2033r0), AOB.
- Capability exchange (comments 1-4): comment 1 proposes a new subclause 9.4.2.[ELC], "ELC Capabilities element", added to Table 9-162 with Element ID 255, an ANA-allocated Element ID Extension, Extensible No and Fragmentable No; the stated reason is that a local MIB Boolean cannot identify optical path compatibility. The proposed text says MCS, spatial-stream and channel-width capabilities are indicated with the existing PHY elements and that advertised capabilities shall be supported by the complete implementation, including its frequency converters and optical front ends. Comment 3 proposes a new 41.1.3, "ELC capability exchange", with frame-presence rules.
- Band baselines (comments 5-7): the revised text for comment 5 inserts Table 41-2, "ELC baseline profiles", with fields Profile Identifier, Optical class, PHY, MCS, Bandwidth, number of spatial streams, Guard Interval, IF mapping and common optical band, and reserves one row each for 0 - VIS - 400-600 nm, 1 - NIR - 800-1000 nm and 2 - SWIR - 1200-1600 nm. Comment 6 revised text: an ELC STA shall support the baseline profile for each advertised optical band, and baseline discovery and association shall not require prior negotiation of multiple optical streams or multi-link operation. Comment 7 revised text: an ELC PHY with NIR support conforms to the LC PHY requirements of Clause 33; the VIS and SWIR lines are written with "may conform".
- Channel mapping (comments 8-11): comment 8 proposes the IF center-frequency equations f_IF = -154 + 5 x n_c for the 5 GHz mapping and f_IF = 21 + 5 x n_c for the 6 GHz mapping. Comment 9 states that Table 41-1 shows an IF center of 36 MHz for channel 5 where the equation and the 21 MHz starting frequency give 46 MHz, and points to the "Table 41-1 values" worksheet (59 permitted 20 MHz centers plus the 40, 80, 160 and 320 MHz center-index sets). Comment 10 proposes replacing the draft's 320 MHz row labelled "134a" with an amendment of the existing global operating class 137 (center-frequency indices 31, 63, 95, 127, 159 and 191).
- Conversion, timing and transmit (comments 12-17): comment 13 proposes that conversion delay, optical front-end delay, transmit/receive switching and power-state recovery be included in the timing budget so that immediate-response requirements are met without asking the peer to extend SIFS or response timeouts, verified through ACK, CTS, Block Ack and any advertised trigger-based response. Comment 17 proposes that optical emission limits be enforced locally, that peer information not authorize an increase beyond them, and that non-laser LED sources within the scope of IEC 62471 be assessed accordingly.
- Receiver and management (comments 18-26): comments 18-22 deal with the receiver reference plane, per-band baseline tests, a 1000-PPDU sample count with wavelength coverage, environmental conditions and an optional note on optical power and RF RSSI (the Discussion Guide marks several of these as conditional or questioned). Comment 23 notes the draft's ELC management object reuses subidentifier 226, already assigned to dot11LCOptionImplemented in TGmf D3.1, and proposes dot11ELCOptionImplemented as a separate member. Comment 26 (Editorial) proposes changing the heading "4.4 Components of the IEEE Std 802.11 architecture" to 4.3.
- The Discussion Guide's decision list (D1-D7) names what the comments leave open, including the PHY/PPDU, rates and IF/channel rule for the VIS, NIR and SWIR baselines (D1), the element encoding and allocation (D2), receiver optical-power and modulation-depth conditions (D3), the sample count and wavelength coverage (D4) and the OID and PICS allocation (D7).

## Key technical points (ZH)
- **DCN 11-26-2033r2 · TGbr** — [文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2033&is_year=2026) D0.1 Implementation Comments。Nikola Serafimovski（FTH）。七个主题（Discussion Guide 计 26 条意见，意见表列有 27 行）；2026 年 11 月 24 日 09:21 ET 上传。
- **DCN 11-26-2392r0 · TGbr** — [文档链接](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=2392&is_year=2026) November 2026 TGbr telecon agenda。Nikola Serafimovski（FTH）。议程页：审议针对 D0.1 的意见（11-26/2032r0、11-26/2033r0）及 AOB。
- 能力交换（意见 1-4）：意见 1 提议新增子条款 9.4.2.[ELC]“ELC Capabilities element”，加入 Table 9-162，Element ID 为 255，Element ID Extension 由 ANA 分配，Extensible 为 No，Fragmentable 为 No；所述理由是本地 MIB 布尔量无法标识光路兼容性。文字提议 MCS、空间流和信道带宽能力仍由现有 PHY 元素指示，且所宣告的能力须由包含频率转换器与光前端在内的完整实现所支持。意见 3 提议新增 41.1.3“ELC capability exchange”，含帧出现规则。
- 频段基线（意见 5-7）：意见 5 的修订文字插入 Table 41-2“ELC baseline profiles”，字段包括 Profile Identifier、Optical class、PHY、MCS、Bandwidth、空间流数、Guard Interval、IF mapping 与 common optical band，并预留三行：0 - VIS - 400-600 nm，1 - NIR - 800-1000 nm，2 - SWIR - 1200-1600 nm。意见 6 修订文字：ELC STA 须支持其所宣告的每个光学频段的基线配置，基线发现与关联不得要求事先协商多光流或多链路操作。意见 7 修订文字：支持 NIR 的 ELC PHY 符合第 33 条对 LC PHY 的要求；VIS 与 SWIR 两行写作“may conform”。
- 信道映射（意见 8-11）：意见 8 提议 IF 中心频率公式，5 GHz 映射为 f_IF = -154 + 5 × n_c，6 GHz 映射为 f_IF = 21 + 5 × n_c。意见 9 指出 Table 41-1 中信道 5 的 IF 中心为 36 MHz，而公式与 21 MHz 起始频率给出的是 46 MHz，并指向“Table 41-1 values”工作表（59 个允许的 20 MHz 中心，以及 40、80、160、320 MHz 的中心索引集合）。意见 10 提议用对现有全球操作类 137（中心频率索引 31、63、95、127、159、191）的修订取代草案中标为“134a”的 320 MHz 行。
- 转换、时序与发射（意见 12-17）：意见 13 提议将转换时延、光前端时延、收发切换及省电状态恢复纳入时序预算，使立即响应要求无需对端延长 SIFS 或响应超时即可满足，并通过 ACK、CTS、Block Ack 及所宣告的基于触发的响应进行验证。意见 17 提议光发射限值须在本地强制执行，对端信息不得授权超过这些限值，并按 IEC 62471 范围内的非激光 LED 光源进行评估。
- 接收机与管理（意见 18-26）：意见 18-22 涉及接收机参考平面、各频段基线测试、1000 个 PPDU 的样本数与波长覆盖、环境条件，以及关于光功率与 RF RSSI 的可选注释（Discussion Guide 将其中若干项标注为有条件或存疑）。意见 23 指出草案中的 ELC 管理对象复用了子标识符 226，而该标识符在 TGmf D3.1 中已分配给 dot11LCOptionImplemented，并提议另设成员 dot11ELCOptionImplemented。意见 26（Editorial）提议将标题“4.4 Components of the IEEE Std 802.11 architecture”改为 4.3。
- Discussion Guide 的决策清单（D1-D7）列出了这些意见留待决定的事项，包括 VIS、NIR、SWIR 基线的 PHY/PPDU、速率与 IF/信道规则（D1）、元素编码与分配（D2）、接收机光功率与调制深度条件（D3）、样本数与波长覆盖（D4），以及 OID 与 PICS 分配（D7）。
