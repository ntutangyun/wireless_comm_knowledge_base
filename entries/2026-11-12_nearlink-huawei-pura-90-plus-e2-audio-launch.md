## Summary (EN)

An article published on 2026-10-29 (IT之家 reporting, carried by ifeng Tech) says Huawei unveiled the Pura 90 Plus at its Double Eleven refresh-season product event held the day before (2026-10-28). The phone, built on the Kirin 9025 chip, is described as using the NearLink E2.0 solution, with NearLink audio working from the day it goes on sale. Per Huawei, the NearLink audio physical-layer transfer rate reaches 16 Mbps, which the article says is enough to carry 48 kHz / 24-bit Hi-Fi audio without compression loss. The headphones named as compatible are FreeClip 2 Collector's Edition, FreeClip 2 and FreeBuds Pro 5.
