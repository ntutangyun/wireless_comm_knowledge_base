## Summary (EN)
Wi-Fi NOW reported on 11 November 2026 that Extreme Networks' second-generation agentic AI platform is producing early results, with the figures company-reported. Customers collectively saved more than 1,000 troubleshooting work hours in the first month; the other figures are company-reported with no period stated: ambient AI drew roughly 10x the engagement of the first-generation conversational AI; about one third of Extreme Platform ONE customers have migrated (voluntary, with backward-compatible subscriptions); and customers using Agent ONE Coworker mode engage the technical assistance centre from inside the platform over 40 times more often, with 58% of issues resolved without human support.

Agent ONE Coworker is described as an ambient, context-aware assistant working alongside administrators in Extreme Platform ONE for onboarding, configuration, troubleshooting and escalation, with a "nudge" feature that flags issues proactively, explains them and suggests remediation. It supports third-party Cisco and HPE devices via SNMP initially, with broader API integrations planned. Stephen Spellicy (VP Product Marketing and Solutions) says the results match what the company wanted and expects the numbers to improve as customers adopt the new workflows.

The stated roadmap is: Agent ONE Coworker mode now; an autonomous "Operator mode" with customer-, partner- and alliance-built skills in Q1 2027, supported by Extreme Exchange (the agentic AI marketplace launched in May 2026); and, in the first half of 2027, on-premises "Extreme Exchange Zones" that would pool unused compute in access points and switches through containerisation to run AI locally, with data sovereignty, privacy, resilience and cost cited as motivations. The article adds that upcoming Wi-Fi 8 silicon with dedicated NPUs could further support edge AI inferencing.
