## Summary (EN)

The ACM SIGCOMM 2026 accepted-papers list (110 papers) includes three satellite-networking papers. **"Planet-Scale IoT Connectivity via LEO Satellites"** (Hong Kong PolyU with SJTU/HKUST — the Xia/Zheng LoRa-satellite lineage) targets global IoT reachability through LEO constellations, the research thread commercialized by Skylo/EchoStar-style NTN IoT services. **"CommSAR"** (SJTU + Aerospace Information Research Institute, CAS) enables **bidirectional communication inside SAR imaging satellites via a shared waveform** — reusing the radar waveform for data transport, an ISAC-flavored design at satellite scale. **"Dissecting the StarLink: Characterizing Queuing and Flow Dynamics"** (TUM/TU Delft, Cech–Mohan–Ott) is the camera-ready of the Starlink queuing-configuration work already covered in this KB (arxiv 2605.27717).

Only titles/authors are public; camera-ready PDFs arrive around the September 2026 conference. Planet-Scale IoT and CommSAR are candidates for dedicated entries when preprints post.
