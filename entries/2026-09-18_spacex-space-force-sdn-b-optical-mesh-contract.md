## Summary (EN)

SatNews reported on 2026-09-17 that U.S. Space Force officials, speaking at an industry defense conference, confirmed the government is letting SpaceX define the satellite count and constellation architecture for the $2.29 billion Space Data Network Backbone (SDN-B) contract, rather than the government prescribing fixed spacecraft quantities. Col. Ryan Frazier, acting Portfolio Acquisition Executive for Space-Based Sensing and Targeting, explained that the government set only high-level performance specifications — orbital coverage, network capacity, concurrent user capacity, and data throughput — leaving vehicle design and orbital-plane distribution to SpaceX.

The SDN-B stems from an Other Transaction Authority (OTA) delivery order that Space Systems Command awarded to SpaceX on 2026-05-26, evolved from earlier national-security space studies and joint-program concepts previously referred to as MILNET. The system is designed as an optically interconnected LEO data-transport layer for the Department of War, operating alongside the Space Development Agency's own Transport Layer to form a combined global, high-throughput, low-latency mesh network that routes tactical targeting data, environmental intelligence, and command communications between military sensors and operational forces. The network uses laser communication terminals so that data packets hop across orbital planes without intermediate ground relays. SpaceX's implementation draws on commercial satellite-manufacturing techniques from its Starlink and government-hardened Starshield platforms. Per the May 2026 OTA terms, SpaceX must deliver a fully operational prototype capability by the end of 2027, with initial flight integration and optical-crosslink verification testing planned as prototype spacecraft reach orbit.

## Summary (ZH)

据 SatNews 2026 年 9 月 17 日报道，美国太空军官员在一场国防行业会议上确认，政府允许 SpaceX 自行确定其 22.9 亿美元「太空数据网络骨干」（Space Data Network Backbone，SDN-B）合同下的卫星数量与星座架构，而非由政府规定固定的航天器数量。太空基传感与打击项目采办执行官（代理）Ryan Frazier 上校解释称，政府仅设定了高层级性能指标——轨道覆盖范围、网络容量、并发用户容量与数据吞吐量——将航天器设计与轨道面分布决策交由 SpaceX 负责。

SDN-B 源自太空系统司令部（Space Systems Command）于 2026 年 5 月 26 日授予 SpaceX 的一份「其他交易授权」（OTA）交付订单，脱胎于此前被称为 MILNET 的国家安全太空研究与联合项目构想。该系统被设计为面向「战争部」（Department of War）的光学互联低地球轨道（LEO）数据传输层，与太空发展局（Space Development Agency）自身的传输层协同运行，共同构成一个全球性、高吞吐、低时延的太空网状网络，用于在军事传感器与作战部队之间传输战术目标数据、环境情报与指挥通信。该网络采用激光通信终端，使数据包可在轨道面之间跳转而无需经由地面中继。SpaceX 的实现方案借鉴了其 Starlink 商用卫星制造技术以及经过军用加固的 Starshield 平台架构。根据 2026 年 5 月的 OTA 合同条款，SpaceX 须在 2027 年底前交付一套完全可运行的原型系统能力，并计划在原型航天器入轨后开展初步的飞行集成与光学星间链路验证测试。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Contract**: $2.29B firm-fixed-price OTA delivery order, Space Systems Command to SpaceX, awarded 2026-05-26; evolved from earlier MILNET concept studies.
- **Performance-based acquisition model**: government specifies orbital coverage, network capacity, concurrent-user capacity, and data throughput only; SpaceX determines satellite count, bus design, and orbital-plane distribution.
- **Network architecture**: optically interconnected LEO mesh using laser communication terminals; packets hop across orbital planes without intermediate ground relays.
- **Program relationship**: operates alongside (not replacing) the Space Development Agency's own Transport Layer, forming a combined global mesh for tactical targeting, environmental intelligence, and command-communications traffic.
- **Platform basis**: SpaceX implementation leverages Starlink commercial manufacturing techniques plus the government-hardened Starshield platform architecture.
- **Delivery schedule**: fully operational prototype capability required by end of 2027; initial flight integration and optical-crosslink verification testing planned as prototype spacecraft enter orbit.

- **项目关系**：与太空发展局自身的传输层并行运行（而非取代），共同构成用于战术目标数据、环境情报与指挥通信流量的全球网状网络。
- **平台基础**：SpaceX 的实现方案借鉴 Starlink 商用卫星制造技术，并采用经军用加固的 Starshield 平台架构。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This KB has not previously tracked a named U.S. military LEO optical-mesh transport-layer contract at this scale or with this acquisition model. The performance-based approach — where the government specifies only system-level capability targets and delegates satellite count, bus design, and orbital-plane architecture entirely to the contractor — is a distinct procurement model from the detailed government-specified satellite counts seen in this KB's other constellation-authorization entries (e.g., the FCC's Amazon Leo authorizations), and it sits alongside rather than inside the Space Development Agency's existing Transport Layer program.
