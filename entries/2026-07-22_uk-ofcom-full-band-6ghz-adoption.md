## Summary (EN)

UK regulator Ofcom has formally adopted the regulation that opens the **entire 6 GHz band (5925–7125 MHz) to Wi-Fi**, making the UK the first country in Europe to enable full-band 6 GHz Wi-Fi operation. The July 2026 decision concludes the process that began with the February 2025 consultation and the January 2026 statement-plus-further-consultation which defined the split-priority concept. In the **Lower 6 GHz** (5925–6425 MHz), higher-power Wi-Fi is now authorised both indoors and outdoors on a licence-exempt basis, provided devices operate under an Automated Frequency Coordination (AFC) system — mirroring the US/Canada standard-power model.

The **Upper 6 GHz** is divided at 6585 MHz into two priority zones. The Wi-Fi priority portion (6425–6585 MHz, 160 MHz) is available on the same licence-exempt basis as the lower band: low-power indoor and very-low-power use without coordination, outdoor/higher-power use under AFC. The mobile priority portion (6585–7125 MHz, 540 MHz) is reserved for future licensed mobile (expected post-2030, focused on dense urban areas), but licence-exempt Wi-Fi may also access it — exclusively through AFC-controlled devices that would vacate when mobile deploys.

Wi-Fi NOW's Claus Hetting characterises the framework as "an evolution of the FCC's rules on 6 GHz spectrum sharing with AFC": rather than the EU RSPG's approach of reserving upper 6 GHz for IMT, Ofcom's priority concept gives Wi-Fi immediate usable access to all 1,200 MHz while preserving a future mobile option without exclusive allocation. Low-power Wi-Fi use of the upper band is proposed to begin by the end of 2026.

## Summary (ZH)

英国监管机构 Ofcom 正式通过法规，将**整个 6 GHz 频段（5925–7125 MHz）向 Wi-Fi 开放**，使英国成为欧洲首个实现全频段 6 GHz Wi-Fi 运行的国家。2026 年 7 月的这一决定为始于 2025 年 2 月咨询、经 2026 年 1 月"声明+进一步咨询"（提出优先级分割概念）的整个进程画上句号。在**下 6 GHz**（5925–6425 MHz），高功率 Wi-Fi 现已获准在室内外以免许可方式运行，前提是设备受自动频率协调（AFC）系统控制——与美国/加拿大的标准功率模式一致。

**上 6 GHz** 以 6585 MHz 为界分为两个优先级区。Wi-Fi 优先段（6425–6585 MHz，160 MHz）与下段同样免许可：低功率室内与极低功率使用无需协调，室外/更高功率需 AFC 控制。移动优先段（6585–7125 MHz，540 MHz）保留给未来的许可移动业务（预计 2030 年后、聚焦密集城区），但免许可 Wi-Fi 也可接入——仅限受 AFC 控制、在移动网络部署时让出频谱的设备。

Wi-Fi NOW 的 Claus Hetting 将该框架描述为"FCC 6 GHz AFC 频谱共享规则的演进"：与欧盟 RSPG 将上 6 GHz 保留给 IMT 的路线不同，Ofcom 的优先级概念让 Wi-Fi 立即获得全部 1,200 MHz 的可用接入，同时在不做排他分配的情况下保留未来移动选项。上段的低功率 Wi-Fi 使用拟于 2026 年底前开放。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Full 1,200 MHz of 6 GHz (5925–7125 MHz) now open to Wi-Fi in the UK — first in Europe.
- Lower 6 GHz (5925–6425 MHz): licence-exempt standard-power/outdoor Wi-Fi authorised under AFC control, matching the US/Canada model.
- Upper 6 GHz split at 6585 MHz: Wi-Fi priority 6425–6585 MHz (160 MHz); mobile priority 6585–7125 MHz (540 MHz).
- Wi-Fi priority zone: LPI + VLP without coordination; outdoor/higher power via AFC.
- Mobile priority zone: Wi-Fi access allowed only via AFC-controlled devices; licensed mobile expected after 2030 in high-demand urban areas.
- Low-power Wi-Fi access to upper 6 GHz proposed by end of 2026; AFC system extension to the upper band proposed.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This entry closes the arc that 2026-05-28_wifinow-uk-eu-upper-6ghz-regulation opened: what was then Ofcom's AFC-shared *proposal* (contrasted with the EU RSPG's IMT reservation) is now adopted regulation. It is also the third AFC-based national 6 GHz framework logged this month (after 2026-07-10_south-africa-standard-power-6ghz-afc and Costa Rica's full-band adoption), reinforcing AFC as the de facto global mechanism for standard-power and shared 6 GHz Wi-Fi. The priority-zone construct — Wi-Fi as AFC-controlled secondary user inside a mobile-priority allocation — is a genuinely new regulatory shape to watch for device-side implications (AFC clients that must handle zone-dependent rules and future vacate conditions).
