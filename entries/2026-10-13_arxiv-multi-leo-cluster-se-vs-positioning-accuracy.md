## Summary (EN)

Kapoglis, Saleh, Wymeersch and Sallouha (arXiv 2610.13526, submitted 2026-10-09; accepted at the 2026 IEEE 1st ISAC Conference) study the correlation between spectral efficiency (SE) and positioning accuracy in cooperative multi-LEO satellite clusters, for an uplink direct-to-device scenario where several satellites jointly process user signals. They derive time-of-arrival (TOA)-based and Doppler-based position error bounds (PEB), quantify communication performance through achievable SE under joint processing, and evaluate both with realistic LEO constellation simulations. For clusters of seven satellites, SE shows a strong negative correlation with the Doppler-based PEB (coefficient -0.72), and a weaker one with the TOA-based PEB (between -0.55 and -0.37). The authors conclude that communication-oriented LEO clusters are largely compatible with Doppler-based positioning but not necessarily with TOA-based positioning.

## Summary (ZH)

Kapoglis、Saleh、Wymeersch 与 Sallouha（arXiv 2610.13526，2026 年 10 月 9 日提交；已被 2026 IEEE 首届 ISAC 会议接收）研究了协作多 LEO 卫星簇中频谱效率（SE）与定位精度的相关性，场景为多颗卫星联合处理用户信号的上行直连设备链路。论文推导了基于到达时间（TOA）和基于多普勒的位置误差界（PEB），以联合处理下的可达 SE 衡量通信性能，并通过真实 LEO 星座仿真对两者进行评估。对于七颗卫星组成的簇，SE 与基于多普勒的 PEB 呈较强负相关（相关系数 -0.72），与基于 TOA 的 PEB 相关性较弱（-0.55 至 -0.37）。作者结论为：面向通信优化的 LEO 卫星簇与基于多普勒的定位基本兼容，但与基于 TOA 的定位不一定兼容。
