## Summary (EN)

Sina Tech (新浪科技) republished on 2026-10-28 an IT之家 item, which in turn cites Lanjing News (蓝鲸新闻) and unnamed informed sources, saying that BMW is advancing a SparkLink (NearLink) digital key scheme for keyless unlocking. According to the report, BMW intends to source multimode chips that support both Bluetooth and SparkLink from STMicroelectronics and NXP, and the scheme is expected to reach vehicles in 2030. The article describes the chip sourcing as an intention rather than a signed supply deal. None of BMW, STMicroelectronics or NXP has confirmed it.

The article places the report next to figures released at CEIC2026: the Shanghai Motor Vehicle Inspection Certification & Tech Innovation Center evaluation of SparkLink digital car key 2.0 reported more than 1.6 million units shipped across more than 30 production models and a 100 percent lock/unlock success rate in its tests, and the alliance's April 2026 (Beijing auto show) forecast of more than 1 million vehicles with a SparkLink key in 2026. It also notes BMW's reported aim of building more than 95 percent of its China-sold cars locally by 2030.

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Claim**: BMW is advancing a SparkLink digital key; target year 2030; purpose is hands-free (no phone out of the pocket) unlocking.
- **Chips**: Bluetooth + SparkLink multimode parts from STMicroelectronics and NXP are described as the intended sourcing; the report does not name part numbers.
- **Status**: single-chain report from unnamed sources, no official confirmation. Separately, per alliance secretary-general Zeng at CEIC2026, an unnamed international luxury brand has settled on a 2030 SparkLink model and hopes STMicroelectronics or NXP will supply Bluetooth + SparkLink multimode chips (see the 2026-11-02 interview entry); that statement does not name BMW.
- **Ranging basis (background given in the same article)**: phase-difference plus time-of-flight positioning to decimetre level, and relay-attack detection; these are alliance/vendor descriptions.
- **Context figures (CEIC2026, evaluation centre)**: 1.6M+ units shipped, 30+ models, 100% lock/unlock success in the test set.
