## Summary (EN)

On 2026-09-29, the International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) held an acceptance-review ceremony at the Yangtze River Delta AI+ Joint Innovation Center (长三角AI+联合创新中心) for an alliance-authorized OpenLab: the **"SparkLink Smart Appliance (Shanghai) Innovation Lab"** (星闪智能家电（上海）创新实验室). After an on-site expert-panel review, the lab passed with a high score and was formally plaqued as **iSLA's 4th officially authorized OpenLab** — described by the source as a key step in building out SparkLink's smart-appliance ecosystem. Attendees included Qingpu District Economic Commission party secretary/director Lu Beilei (陆蓓蕾), iSLA vice-chairman and Huawei Fellow Wan Lei (万蕾), Shanghai Qingpu New Town Development (Group) general manager Xu Xiaofeng (徐晓峰), and Shanghai HiSilicon standards & ecosystem development head Wu Jun (吴军); the meeting was chaired by iSLA secretary-general Zeng Guosong (曾国松).

The lab is built and operated by the "Shanghai Qingpu Xinglian SparkLink Promotion Center" (上海青浦星联星闪促进中心), jointly established by **9 units**: Midea Group, Shanghai HiSilicon, Hangzhou Yongxie (杭州永谐), the Shanghai National Engineering Research Center for Digital Television, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, Hefei Hengxinji (合肥恒新基), Shanghai Yangtze Delta Baoye (上海长三角宝业), Shanghai Bilu Electronics (上海比路电子), and the Yangtze River Delta AI+ Joint Innovation Center — spanning a "chip–module–finished device–university research–local industry platform" operating structure. iSLA frames it as the alliance's first open innovation platform dedicated to smart appliances, with four functions: technical R&D, interoperability testing, ecosystem incubation, and application demonstration.

The review panel was led by iSLA vice-chairman/Huawei Fellow Wan Lei, with members including iSLA OpenLab management-office director Wang Xiao (王孝), alliance board-member unit Chuangyao Technology (创耀科技) chairman Tan Yaolong (谭耀龙), iSLA testing & certification group leader / CAICT TAF terminal lab IoT-technology deputy director Wang Zhiwei (王智玮), iSLA requirements & standards group leader / CESI IoT research center data-communication-lab director Cai Tingxiao (蔡廷晓), Tongji University professor Wang Rui (王睿), and Createrra Information & Technology (上海) CEO Chen Xiangmin (陈向民). Reviewers toured a 400+ m² lab/exhibition area, viewing self-developed items including a SparkLink protocol test instrument, a SparkLink air-interface protocol analyzer, and a SparkLink spatial-sensing ranging and dynamic obstacle-avoidance system.

The source states iSLA's OpenLab network now has a layered structure: Shenzhen hosts the alliance's own OpenLab Innovation Service Center (at iSLA's secretariat headquarters), with alliance-authorized OpenLabs subsequently established in Hubei (general-purpose), Wuhan (smart PC), and Beijing (chip IP) — Qingpu's smart-appliance lab is the 4th in this authorized tier. The source frames Qingpu's selection on three grounds: industry (concentration of Midea, HiSilicon, SJTU, the national digital-TV engineering research center and other upstream/downstream resources in Shanghai/Yangtze-Delta); location (Qingpu New Town as a cross-regional integration hub); and policy (continued industrial-policy support from the Joint Innovation Center).

## Summary (ZH)

2026年9月29日，国际星闪联盟授权OpenLab——**星闪智能家电（上海）创新实验室**验收评审会在长三角AI+联合创新中心举行。经专家组现场综合评审，实验室以高分通过验收并获正式授牌，成为**国际星闪联盟第四家官方授权OpenLab**——信源称此举标志着星闪智能家电生态建设迈出关键一步。出席嘉宾包括上海市青浦区经济委员会党组书记、主任陆蓓蕾，国际星闪联盟副理事长、华为Fellow万蕾，上海青浦新城发展（集团）有限公司总经理徐晓峰，上海海思标准与生态发展部部长吴军；会议由国际星闪联盟秘书长曾国松主持。

该实验室由"上海青浦星联星闪促进中心"承建运营，由**9家单位**联合发起组建：美的集团、上海海思、杭州永谐、上海数字电视国家工程研究中心、上海交通大学、合肥恒新基、上海长三角宝业、上海比路电子、长三角AI+联合创新中心——形成覆盖"芯片—模组—整机—高校科研—地方产业平台"的运营体系。联盟将其定位为首个面向智能家电领域的开放创新平台，兼具技术研发、测试互通、生态孵化、应用示范四大职能。

评审专家组由国际星闪联盟副理事长、华为Fellow万蕾博士担任组长，成员包括国际星闪联盟OpenLab管理办公室主任王孝、联盟理事单位创耀科技董事长谭耀龙、国际星闪联盟测试与认证组组长/中国信通院泰尔终端实验室物联技术部副主任王智玮、国际星闪联盟需求与标准组组长/中国电子技术标准化研究院物联网研究中心数据通信研究室主任蔡廷晓、同济大学教授王睿，以及创远信科（上海）CEO陈向民。评审专家实地观摩了占地400余平方米的实验室及展厅，包括星闪协议测试仪、星闪空口协议分析仪、星闪空间感知测距及动态避障系统等自研成果。

信源称，国际星闪联盟OpenLab生态目前已形成层级布局：深圳作为联盟秘书处总部，设星闪OpenLab创新服务中心；在此基础上，联盟此前已先后在湖北（综合性）、武汉（智能PC）、北京（芯片IP）建成授权OpenLab——青浦智能家电实验室为该授权层级的第4家。信源将青浦的选址归因于三方面优势：产业层面（美的、海思、上海交大、数字电视国家工程研究中心等上下游资源在沪及长三角集聚）；区位层面（青浦新城作为跨区域协同的枢纽）；政策层面（联创中心持续的产业政策支持）。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

This KB's prior OpenLab coverage (2026-08-17_nearlink-isla-certification-scheme) documented only the Wuhan interoperability-testing lab and its 44-vendor/57-product tally; this entry adds the Hubei (general-purpose) and Beijing (chip IP) OpenLabs as previously-undocumented nodes in the network, and establishes Qingpu/smart-appliances as the 4th authorized node and the first one explicitly scoped to home appliances — a vertical this KB has tracked mainly through the cross-brand OneConnect smart-home interconnect standard (2026-08-28) rather than through shared test/incubation infrastructure.
