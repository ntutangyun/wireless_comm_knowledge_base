## Summary (EN)

Xinhua reported on 2026-11-26 that China's Ministry of Industry and Information Technology (MIIT), the Cyberspace Administration of China and the National Development and Reform Commission have jointly issued the "New-Generation Communication Network Construction Implementation Plan" (《新一代通信网建设实施方案》), with State Council approval. The plan coordinates the build-out of four kinds of network: basic networks, space networks, international networks and converged networks.

The plan's 2030 goals are a new-generation communication network with complete coverage and leading performance (建成覆盖完善、性能领先的新一代通信网), 6G network construction carried out "at an appropriate time" (适时开展6G网络建设), the low-orbit satellite internet system largely built, continued optimisation of international submarine and terrestrial cables, scale development of converged networks such as the industrial internet and connected vehicles, and a more complete industry ecosystem. Actively advancing 5G-A network coverage at scale is listed among the plan's tasks.

## Summary (ZH)

新华社 2026 年 11 月 26 日报道，工业和信息化部、国家网信办、国家发展改革委联合印发《新一代通信网建设实施方案》，文件经国务院同意。方案统筹推进基础网络、空间网络、国际网络和融合网络四类网络建设。

方案提出，到 2030 年建成覆盖完善、性能领先的新一代通信网，适时开展 6G 网络建设，低轨卫星互联网系统基本建成，国际海陆缆布局持续优化，工业互联网、车联网等融合网络实现规模化发展，产业生态更加完善。积极推进 5G-A 网络规模覆盖列在方案的任务之中。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Primary reporting (Xinhua, 2026-11-26): https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/20261126/9fe827a2d98741acb60f62d36ba89cf7/c.html. MIIT's official interpretation (工信微报, republished by news.cn 2026-11-26 08:37): https://www.news.cn/politics/20261126/cbd5e443729f42dd9a54884a5065eee0/c.html. Xinhua says the plan was issued "recently" (日前) and gives no issuance date.
- Four network categories. Basic networks: scale coverage of 5G-A (5G-Advanced), 6G network construction at an appropriate time, and ten-gigabit optical-network city pilots. Space networks: coordinated development of high-, medium- and low-orbit satellite systems, including low-orbit satellite internet and high-throughput GEO satellites. International networks: international information channels, submarine and terrestrial cable layout, and overseas data-centre and cloud nodes. Converged networks: mobile IoT, industrial networks, connected vehicles, low-altitude communication networks and digital-transformation pilots.
- Standards: the Xinhua report says the plan calls for accelerating the development of standards for 6G, satellite internet and similar technologies.
- Engineering projects: MIIT's interpretation names four, one per category: basic-network quality upgrade, space-network coordinated breakthrough, international-network diversified layout, and converged-network foundation strengthening.
- Resource guarantees: the plan strengthens guarantees for land, forest, grassland, sea use and spectrum (加大用地、用林、用草、用海和频谱等要素保障力度).
- No numeric targets for 5G-A coverage, optical networks, satellites or 6G, and no 6G date, appear on the pages read; the document number is also not stated there.
