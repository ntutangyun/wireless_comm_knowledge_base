## Summary (EN)

Indermuehle, Winkel, Di Vruno, Jozsa and Lourenco (CSIRO, MPIfR, SKAO, Rhodes University) measure unwanted emissions from Starlink direct-to-device (DTD) satellites, which carry mobile base stations transmitting in terrestrial mobile bands. They use autocorrelations of single Australia Telescope Compact Array (ATCA) antennas on tracked passes of DTD and other satellites, with the 16 cm and 4 cm receivers, targeting the harmonics of the 2620-2630 MHz two-carrier downlink and the intermodulation products around them, and searching for emission next to the carriers. Results are compared with the ITU-R unwanted-emission limits and radio astronomy protection criteria. The authors describe the harmonic measurements as the first calibrated ones of H2 to H4 recorded simultaneously in Australia.

Harmonic ratios and receiver tests are used to argue that the harmonics are generated on the satellite rather than in the telescope receiver. An aggregate equivalent power flux density (epfd) simulation of a constellation is run for Effelsberg and the ATCA.

The authors conclude that harmonics are received far outside the serving beams, that their radiated level must be limited directly rather than through power at the antenna port, and that compliance with per-satellite ITU-R limits does not protect radio astronomy from the aggregate of a constellation.

## Summary (ZH)

Indermuehle、Winkel、Di Vruno、Jozsa 和 Lourenco（CSIRO、MPIfR、SKAO、罗德斯大学）测量了 Starlink 直连设备（DTD）卫星的非期望发射。这类卫星搭载移动基站，在地面移动频段发射。作者利用澳大利亚望远镜紧凑阵（ATCA）单天线的自相关，在对 DTD 及其他卫星的跟踪过境中，使用 16 cm 和 4 cm 接收机，测量 2620-2630 MHz 双载波下行的谐波及其周围的互调产物，并搜索载波旁的发射，再与 ITU-R 非期望发射限值和射电天文保护准则比较。作者称这是在澳大利亚同时记录 H2 至 H4 的首批校准测量。

作者通过谐波比值和接收机测试，论证谐波产生于卫星而非望远镜接收机；并针对 Effelsberg 和 ATCA 对星座的聚合等效功率通量密度（epfd）进行了仿真。

结论：谐波在服务波束之外很远处仍可被接收；其辐射电平必须直接限制，而非通过天线端口功率间接限制；满足单星 ITU-R 限值并不能保护射电天文免受星座聚合发射的影响。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **Harmonics**: H2 to H4 at 5240, 7860 and 10480 MHz fall by about 16 dB per order, show no trend with elevation, and originate on the satellite. H2 is also detected in 32% of integrations more than 10 degrees from any DTD satellite.
- **Intermodulation**: inside the harmonic zones the spectra follow the n-fold convolution of the carriers; outside them, products of order n+2 and higher lie on average 19 dB below the zone and switch between -23 and -15 dB within seconds.
- **Next to carriers**: no satellite emission detected down to 33 dB below the carriers.
- **Limits**: H2 (at or above -38.9 dBW in 4 kHz, isotropic-equivalent) is at or above the SM.329 space-station limit; H3 and H4 meet it.
- **Aggregate**: for 8640 satellites the aggregate exceeds the protection criteria by 65 dB (second harmonic) and 25 dB (fifth harmonic), and by more than 100 dB for out-of-band emission at the mask level in 2690-2700 MHz.
- **Context**: DTD is on the WRC-27 agenda (item 1.13); the paper notes Optus holds 2670-2690 MHz in Australia and plans DTD services there.

## Key technical points (ZH)

- **谐波**：H2 至 H4 位于 5240、7860 和 10480 MHz，每升一阶约下降 16 dB，与仰角无明显趋势，源自卫星。在距任何 DTD 卫星 10 度以外的积分中，也有 32% 检测到 H2。
- **互调**：谐波区内频谱遵循载波的 n 重卷积；区外 n+2 阶及更高阶产物平均比谐波区低 19 dB，并在数秒内在 -23 与 -15 dB 之间切换。
- **载波旁**：低至载波以下 33 dB 未检测到卫星发射。
- **限值**：H2（4 kHz 内各向同性等效不低于 -38.9 dBW）达到或超过 SM.329 空间站限值；H3、H4 满足限值。
- **聚合影响**：对 8640 颗卫星，聚合发射超出保护准则：二次谐波 65 dB，五次谐波 25 dB，2690-2700 MHz 掩模电平带外发射超出 100 dB 以上。
- **背景**：DTD 在 WRC-27 议程中（议题 1.13）；文中提到 Optus 在澳大利亚持有 2670-2690 MHz 并计划用于 DTD 业务。

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Gives measured harmonic and intermodulation levels of an operating DTD downlink against ITU-R limits and radio astronomy criteria, and a simulated aggregate for a large fleet. It complements regulatory entries on DTD spectrum by quantifying unwanted-emission behaviour.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

给出了在运行 DTD 下行链路的谐波与互调实测电平，并与 ITU-R 限值及射电天文准则对照，同时给出大规模星座的聚合仿真结果。它以量化方式补充了有关 DTD 频谱的监管类条目。
