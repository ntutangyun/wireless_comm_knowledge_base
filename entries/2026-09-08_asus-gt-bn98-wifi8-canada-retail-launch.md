## Summary (EN)

ASUS's own press release confirms that the ROG Rapture GT-BN98 Pro (BN30000) went on retail sale in Canada on 2026-09-04, ahead of the broader worldwide rollout the company has pencilled in for October. This is a landing/availability event distinct from the two prior KB entries on this router family, which covered announcements only: the Computex reveal of the GT-BN98 Pro (`2026-06-08_asus-rog-gt-bn98-pro-wifi8-router`) and the Gamescom showing of the lower-tier GT-BN98 (`2026-08-31_asus-rog-gt-bn98-quad-band-wifi8-gamescom`), neither of which disclosed pricing or a purchase date. ASUS's release states it is now "the world's only quad-band WiFi 8 router currently available to buy from retail stores." Press coverage (Notebookcheck) puts the Canadian retail price at roughly US$940-equivalent, and ASUS's own Canadian eShop lists it at CA$1,299.99, for a device built on the still-unratified 802.11bn draft (ASUS's release itself uses only the "WiFi 8 architectural framework" wording and names UEQM and NPCA as WiFi 8 features).

## Key technical points (EN)

- Event: retail on-sale date, not an announcement — ASUS's press release dated 2026-09-04 states the "ROG Rapture GT-BN98 series is available now in Canada", ahead of an October worldwide release; per its footnote and trade-press coverage (Notebookcheck, routerarena) the model on Canadian shelves is the Pro (BN30000).
- Model: the release announces the series as a whole and names both the Pro (BN30000) and the GT-BN98 (BN25000); its footnote marks the Pro as "available in Canada only", which trade-press coverage reads as the Pro being the sole model on Canadian shelves.
- Price: reported by press coverage at roughly US$940-equivalent / CA$1,299.99 (ASUS's own release does not state a price).
- Spec recap (per this press release; 4T4R/4096-QAM per band per Notebookcheck): quad-band, 4T4R per band, 4096-QAM, up to 30,000 Mbps total wireless throughput (ASUS footnotes this figure as referring to the Pro/BN30000 only), 2.6 GHz quad-core CPU, 2 GB RAM, two 10 Gbps ports plus four 2.5 Gbps LAN ports, a 16-antenna architecture combined into eight external antennas.
- Standard status (KB background, not a release statement): 802.11bn (Wi-Fi 8) is still a draft, not a ratified spec; the release itself says only "built on the WiFi 8 architectural framework".
- 802.11bn features named in the release: "WiFi 8 features such as UEQM (Unequal Modulation) and NPCA (Non-Primary Channel Access)", and "2x LDPC wall-penetration technology supports coverage of up to 7,000 square feet" — draft-standard features already covered by earlier KB entries, listed here because the June and August entries on this router did not name them.
- Claim: ASUS calls it "the world's only quad-band WiFi 8 router currently available to buy from retail stores" — ASUS's own claim, relayed as such; the KB has not independently verified that no other Wi-Fi 8 router is on sale elsewhere.
