## Summary (EN)

The alliance's own news feed (posted 2026-11-05) reports that the Yangtze River Delta AI+ Joint Innovation Center (the "Joint Innovation Center") attended the CEIC2026 / International SparkLink Alliance (iSLA) Industry Summit in Shenzhen on 22 October as an ecosystem partner and took part in the plaquing ceremony of the SparkLink Smart Appliance (Shanghai) Innovation Lab. At the summit's ecosystem expo it presented what it describes as its built SparkLink testing-and-certification capability and scenario solutions, supported by two SparkLink facilities it says are already operating: the Shanghai SparkLink Open Lab and the SparkLink Smart Appliance (Shanghai) Innovation Lab.

According to the report, the Shanghai SparkLink Open Lab was unveiled on 31 December 2025 at the Midea Global Innovation Park in Shanghai, with a core space of 1,600 square metres. It is led by Midea Group together with the Qingpu District government, iSLA, Shanghai HiSilicon and Shanghai Jiao Tong University, and runs a "headquarters + branch" model: the headquarters sits at the Joint Innovation Center, which carries public-service and ecosystem-linking functions, while the branch at the Midea park focuses on frontier technology work and scenario integration. At this stage it concentrates on two directions, SparkLink Mesh breakthroughs and SparkLink radar-sensing innovation. The report adds that the Smart Appliance (Shanghai) Innovation Lab passed an expert-panel on-site acceptance on 29 September 2026 and was formally authorised by iSLA, described as the first smart-appliance OpenLab in the SparkLink system, with three stated core functions: standards formulation, test validation and transfer of results. The Joint Innovation Center is named as the operator that handled the whole process from preparation and review to opening.

The report also describes the Joint Innovation Center itself: it is co-built by Shanghai's Qingpu District and Huawei and is organised as a "6+1" set of functional sections (an open-source HarmonyOS application innovation centre, a Kunpeng-Ascend innovation centre, an AI application innovation centre, an embodied-intelligence innovation centre, an IT-application-innovation (信创) collaborative centre, the SparkLink Open Lab, and a public computing-power base). It was unveiled on 27 July at the Yangtze River Delta Integration Demonstration Zone developer conference; its first batch of resident companies signed on 29 September; and the first phase of the public computing base, 105 P of intelligent computing, is stated as about to enter use. For resident companies' technical problems, the report outlines a process of engineer visits, task breakdown and feasibility assessment, with in-house "6+1" capability used where available and joint work with Huawei, technology partners and universities where there is a gap. The stated future plan is to run protocol migration and test certification as chargeable industry services and to deliver scenario solutions directly to enterprises.

## Summary (ZH)

联盟官网动态（2026年11月5日发布）报道：10月22日，长三角AI+联合创新中心（下称“联创中心”）作为生态合作伙伴受邀参加在深圳举行的CEIC2026暨国际星闪联盟（iSLA）产业峰会，并出席星闪智能家电（上海）创新实验室授牌仪式。在峰会同期的星闪生态创新展上，联创中心集中展示其已建成的星闪测试认证能力与场景化解决方案，其依托的是两个据称已实体化运行的星闪载体：上海星闪开放实验室与星闪智能家电（上海）创新实验室。

据报道，上海星闪开放实验室于2025年12月31日在上海美的全球创新园区揭牌成立，核心空间1600平方米，由美的集团主导，联合青浦区政府、国际星闪联盟、上海海思、上海交通大学共同建设，采用“总部+分部”协同运营模式：总部设于联创中心，承担公共服务与生态链接功能；分部位于美的园区，聚焦前沿技术攻关与场景融合创新，现阶段主攻星闪Mesh技术突破和星闪雷达感知创新两个方向。报道还称，星闪智能家电（上海）创新实验室于2026年9月29日以高分通过专家组现场验收，正式获国际星闪联盟授牌，系星闪体系中首个智能家电领域OpenLab，三大核心职能为标准制定、测试验证与成果转化；联创中心全流程承接了从筹建、评审到挂牌启用的工作，是实验室的核心运营方。

报道同时介绍了联创中心本身：由上海市青浦区与华为共建，设“6+1”功能板块（开源鸿蒙应用创新中心、鲲鹏昇腾创新中心、AI应用创新中心、具身智能创新中心、信创产业协同创新中心、星闪开放实验室，以及公共算力服务底座）；7月27日在长三角一体化示范区开发者大会上揭牌启用，9月29日完成首批企业签约入驻，公共算力底座首期105P智算能力表述为即将建成投用。针对入驻企业的技术难题，报道描述了工程师调研、拆解技术任务、评估可行性后立项或联合攻关的流程。其规划是把协议迁移、测试认证打造为可结算的产业服务，并把场景解决方案直送企业一线。
