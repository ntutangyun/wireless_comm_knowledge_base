## Summary (EN)

TGbq (Integrated Millimeter Wave, IMMW) posted its largest single-window baseline-PHY drafting wave this KB has recorded — 20 new DCNs, against 8 in each of the 09-09 and 09-11 waves — spanning 2026-09-11 to 2026-09-13 ahead of the Waikoloa interim. NXP alone submitted five proposed draft text (PDT) contributions in one pass, covering five PHY subclauses spread across Clause 42.3: PPDU formats, the IMMW-STF field, the transmit procedure, modulation and coding schemes, and the mathematical description of signals. Alongside it, Intel proposed an M-STF design for the 640 MHz mode that extends the existing 320 MHz M-STF, Huawei submitted MAC-layer draft text for MIMO operation, Qualcomm paired a beam-tracking framework presentation with a companion MAC-layer PDT, ZTE proposed a PHY service interface, Samsung proposed PHY timing-related parameters, and Ofinno proposed coordination between two overlapping-BSS IMMW AP MLDs over the sub-7 GHz link. Several existing PDTs and discussion papers (LG Electronics' service-period/feedback documents, Xiaomi's cross-channel beamforming paper, OPPO's Tx-power indication paper, Ofinno's BFT feedback paper) were also revised, and TGbq's September-interim agenda was updated twice. A further eight new-DCN TGbq uploads in the same window are named but not covered individually below.

## Summary (ZH)

TGbq（集成毫米波，IMMW）在 2026-09-11 至 2026-09-13 期间、Waikoloa 临时会议召开前，发布了本知识库记录以来单一窗口内规模最大的基线 PHY 起草浪潮——共 20 份新 DCN，而 09-09 与 09-11 两次浪潮各为 8 份。仅 NXP 一家便一次性提交了五份拟稿文本（PDT），覆盖分布在第 42.3 条下的五个 PHY 子条款：PPDU 格式、IMMW-STF 字段、发送流程、调制编码方案，以及信号的数学描述。与此同时，英特尔提出了面向 640 MHz 模式的 M-STF 设计（在既有 320 MHz M-STF 基础上扩展），华为提交了面向 MIMO 操作的 MAC 层拟稿文本，高通把一份波束跟踪框架演示文稿与一份配套的 MAC 层 PDT 一并提交，中兴提出了 PHY 服务接口方案，三星提出了 PHY 时序相关参数方案，Ofinno 则提出由两个重叠 BSS（OBSS）的 IMMW AP MLD 经 7 GHz 以下链路进行协调。此外，若干既有 PDT 与讨论文档（LG 电子的服务周期/反馈文档、小米的跨信道波束成形论文、OPPO 的发射功率指示论文、Ofinno 的 BFT 反馈论文）也进行了修订，TGbq 的九月临时会议议程也更新了两次。同一窗口内另有八份 TGbq 新 DCN，下文仅列名而未逐份详述。

## Key technical points (EN)

- **DCN 11-26-1732r0, 1733r0, 1735r0, 1736r0, 1737r0 · TGbq** — Rui Cao (NXP), all uploaded 11-Sep-2026: PDTs for Subclause 42.3.3 (IMMW PPDU formats), 42.3.9.6 (IMMW-STF), 42.3.16 (IMMW Transmit Procedure), 42.3.6 (modulation and coding schemes), and 42.3.8 (mathematical description of signals) — five PHY subclauses spread across Clause 42.3, submitted together. They are not a contiguous block: 42.3.4/.5/.7 and 42.3.10 through .15 are not among them. [1732](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1732&is_year=2026) / [1733](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1733&is_year=2026) / [1735](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1735&is_year=2026) / [1736](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1736&is_year=2026) / [1737](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1737&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1715r0 · TGbq** — "640 MHz M-STF Design Proposal". Ratnesh Kumbhkar (Intel), uploaded 13-Sep-2026. An M-STF design for the 640 MHz mode that extends the existing 320 MHz M-STF rather than defining a new sequence: a complete 320 MHz M-STF is preserved within each 320 MHz segment, so a 320 MHz receiver can reuse its existing auto-correlation and cross-correlation detection unchanged, with a global phase rotation applied between the two segments. The deck reports M-STF PAPR of 5.25 dB for a 180-degree rotation, against 7.35 dB with no rotation and 7.3 dB median PAPR for M-SIG. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1715&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1778r0 · TGbq** — "PDT MAC for IMMW MIMO Operation". Wee Kiat New (Huawei) with a large multi-company author list, uploaded 11-Sep-2026. Short proposed draft text for subclause 41.8, stated as relating to motions 56 and 81: IMMW STAs may optionally support SU-MIMO with two spatial streams, which may be transmitted using two different polarizations. The KB's 09-09 entry already recorded optional 2-spatial-stream SU-MIMO from the PHY side (DCN 1697, IMMW-MCS parameters); this is the first **MAC-layer** draft text for it in this KB's TGbq coverage. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1778&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1721r0 / 1799r0 · TGbq** — "IMMW Beam Tracking Framework" and "MAC PDT IMMW Beam Tracking". Giovanni Chisci (Qualcomm), both uploaded 13-Sep-2026. 1721 is a discussion deck whose content is MAC-level: a three-step framework of evaluations, setup and training, where evaluation is an ongoing process based on received PPDUs and measurements and can use the ICF/ICR exchange at the beginning of a service period or other PPDUs during the SP; evaluation outcomes map to actions (no degradation → no training; degraded link → beam tracking training for refinement; beam failure → establish a new beam pair), and the deck gives setup-signalling examples for Rx-, Tx- and combined Tx+Rx refinement. 1799 is the companion MAC PDT. [1721](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1721&is_year=2026) / [1799](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1799&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1788r0 · TGbq** — "PDT IMMW PHY service interface". Yanchun Li (ZTE), uploaded 11-Sep-2026. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1788&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1815r0 · TGbq** — "PDT PHY Timing-Related Parameters". Sean Chiu (Samsung), uploaded 12-Sep-2026. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1815&is_year=2026)
- **DCN 11-26-1825r0 · TGbq** — "OBSS Interference Coordination on the IMMW Link". Uploaded 13-Sep-2026 by Javier Perez-Ramirez (Ofinno); the document names Ziru Chen (Ofinno) as author. The deck's stated novelty is narrow and explicit: OBSS interference on the mmWave link "has been discussed in [2]-[7]" for detection, reporting and channel switching, but all of that "stays inside one BSS, and one AP acts alone" — "the SFD does not define coordination between two OBSS IMMW AP MLDs". It proposes that two OBSS IMMW AP MLDs negotiate over the sub-7 GHz link, with two options: coordinated protection of an SP (the requested AP MLD does not transmit on the mmWave channel during the protected SPs, or transmits only on a non-overlapping beam) and a coordinated channel switch (the two links move to non-overlapping mmWave channels for the overlapping SPs). It cites this KB's previously recorded cross-link Co-TWT document (11-26/1534r0) among its references. [document](https://mentor.ieee.org/802.11/documents?is_dcn=1825&is_year=2026)
- Also revised (not new mechanisms): DCN 919r1/920r4/1359r2 (LG Electronics, IMMW feedback and service-period discussion papers), 1322r1 (Xiaomi, cross-channel beamforming), 1330r1 (OPPO, Tx power indication), 859r3 (Ofinno, BFT feedback for IMMW), and 1476r3/r4 (Huawei, September-interim agenda, revised twice).
- **Also uploaded in this window, not covered individually here and not yet recorded anywhere in this KB**: DCN 1606r0 (OPPO, Jiqing Ni — "On TRN Field Design for IMMW"), 1607r0 (OPPO, Jiqing Ni — "Two Layer Beam Maintenance for IMMW"), 1394r0 (OPPO, Liuming Lu — "AP PUO for IMMW STAs"), 1666r0 (Nokia, Mario Costa — "Temporary Suspension of On-Demand SP"), 1591r0 (OPPO, Yapu Li — "Data field of IMMW PPDU"), 1660r0 (LG Electronics, Dongju Cha — "Further Details on ODSP Negotiation"), 1639r0 (LG Electronics, Hongwon Lee — "MAX IMMW session idle period"), and 1640r0 (LG Electronics, Hongwon Lee — "beam establishment early termination followup"). This digest does not inventory the whole wave.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

Prior TGbq waves this KB tracked (09-09, 09-11) filled in the IMMW preamble field-by-field (M-STF, M-LTF, IMMW-LTF, IMMW-SIG, Data field) and opened single MAC procedures (early termination, ACK recovery). This wave is larger — 20 new DCNs against 8 in each of those two — and differs in character: NXP's five-PDT submission lands baseline PHY text across a spread of Clause 42.3 subclauses in one pass, while Huawei's 41.8 text and Ofinno's OBSS proposal move two existing topics up a layer. Huawei's contribution is the first MAC-layer draft text for IMMW MIMO in this KB's coverage (the PHY-side MCS tables in DCN 1697 already carried optional 2-stream SU-MIMO), and Ofinno's proposal takes OBSS interference — already discussed for detection, reporting and single-AP channel switching, including the cross-link Co-TWT work this KB recorded on 2026-08-13 — into negotiated coordination between two OBSS IMMW AP MLDs, which the SFD does not currently define. Combined with Qualcomm's paired beam-tracking framework and MAC PDT and Intel's 640 MHz M-STF extension, the group's baseline PHY/MAC clause set is visibly consolidating ahead of the September interim. Note that this digest covers the submissions listed above; eight further new TGbq DCNs in the same window are named but not analysed.

## Why it matters / what's new (ZH)

本知识库此前跟踪的 TGbq 浪潮（09-09、09-11）是逐字段填补 IMMW 前导码（M-STF、M-LTF、IMMW-LTF、IMMW-SIG、Data 字段），并开启单项 MAC 流程（提前终止、ACK 恢复）。本次浪潮规模更大——20 份新 DCN，而前两次各为 8 份——性质也有所不同：NXP 一次性提交的五份 PDT 把基线 PHY 文本铺到第 42.3 条下分散的若干子条款上，而华为的 41.8 条文本与 Ofinno 的 OBSS 提案则把两个既有议题向上推了一层。华为的贡献是本知识库报道中首份针对 IMMW MIMO 的 MAC 层拟稿文本（PHY 侧的 MCS 表格 DCN 1697 已经包含可选 2 空间流 SU-MIMO）；Ofinno 的提案则把 OBSS 干扰——此前已在检测、上报与单 AP 信道切换层面被讨论过，也包括本知识库 2026-08-13 记录的跨链路 Co-TWT 工作——推进到两个 OBSS IMMW AP MLD 之间的协商式协调，而 SFD 目前尚未定义这一点。结合高通成对提交的波束跟踪框架与 MAC PDT，以及英特尔对 640 MHz M-STF 的扩展设计，该组的基线 PHY/MAC 条款集正在九月临时会议前明显走向整合。需要说明的是，本摘要仅覆盖上文列出的提交；同一窗口内另有八份 TGbq 新 DCN 仅列名而未作分析。
