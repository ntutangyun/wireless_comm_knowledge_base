## Summary (EN)

The International SparkLink Alliance's (iSLA) Requirements and Standards Working Group held its 20th meeting in Hangzhou on 2026-06-09 and 06-10, chaired by Cai Tingxiao (蔡廷晓) of the China Electronics Standardization Institute's IoT Research Center (电子标准院物联网研究中心), whom the source identifies as the group's 组长 (group lead). Over 90 expert representatives from more than 50 alliance member organizations attended in person. The meeting received 5 new project-initiation proposals, 1 standard draft for public comment, 8 standard change-request (CR) proposals, and 4 progress reports/topic presentations, and it went on to approve all of the submitted proposals and drafts in those first three categories. China Mobile (Hangzhou) Information Technology Co., Ltd. (中移（杭州）信息技术有限公司) provided logistical support for the meeting, per the source article.

The 5 newly approved project-initiation proposals are: "SparkLink Synchronous Low-Power Air Interface 3.0" (星闪同步低功耗空口 3.0); "Data Connection Interface Enhancement" (数据连接接口增强); "SparkLink Wireless Communication System Testing — Foundational Service Layer 5G Convergence Test Specification" (星闪无线通信系统 测试 基础服务层 5G融合测试规范); "SparkLink Wireless Communication System Testing — Scenario-Based SLE Performance Requirements and Test Specification" (星闪无线通信系统 测试 基于场景的SLE性能要求和测试规范); and "SparkLink Post-Quantum Cryptography Protocol" (星闪抗量子密码协议). The single approved standard draft opened for public comment is "SparkLink Wireless Communication System — Foundational Application Layer — Civil Unmanned Aircraft Operation Identification Application Configuration and Test Specification" (星闪无线通信系统 基础应用层 民用无人驾驶航空器运行识别应用配置和测试规范). The 8 approved CRs span three existing group standards: T_XS 10001-2025 (Access layer — Synchronous Low-Latency Broadband Air Interface (SLB), technical requirements and test methods), T_XS 10002-2025 (Access layer — Synchronous Low-Power Air Interface (SLE), technical requirements and test methods), and T_XS 10003-2025 (Access layer — Data Connection Interface, technical requirements and test methods).

The source article, hosted by the Shenzhen Comentropy Industry and Standards Innovation Service Center (深圳市共熵产业与标准创新服务中心) and attributed to a re-post from the WeChat official account of the International SparkLink Alliance itself, does not give further technical detail (parameters, timelines, or draft text) for any of the newly approved items beyond their titles and standard numbers.
