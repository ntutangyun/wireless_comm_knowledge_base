## Summary (EN)

Amati et al. address real-time multi-hop relay selection for connected vehicles in dense urban NR-V2X, where sparse road-side units and frequent NLoS conditions break single-hop connectivity and exact optimization is too slow for topologies that change every second. Each network snapshot is a directed attributed graph — nodes are CAVs and RSUs, edges are candidate links carrying radio-aware features (SNR, capacity, LoS/NLoS) — and relay selection is learned rather than solved.

The learning-to-optimize pipeline trains an edge-aware Graph Isomorphism Network (GINE) under supervision from an offline MILP oracle, with lightweight runtime post-processing enforcing acyclicity and single-outgoing-link constraints. Training data is substantial: 286,602 snapshots generated by an OpenStreetMap–SUMO–GEMV2 pipeline across three Rome districts (Porta Pia, EUR, Trastevere) spanning suburban to urban-stress densities.

GINE recovers 11.3% connectivity gain against the MILP oracle's 12.3% bound while running 15–20× faster in urban-medium regimes and ≥100× in the worst case — near-constant inference latency versus MILP's exponential blow-up. The domain-shift result matters most for deployment: training on a single district loses 23.3% when transferred, but multi-area training cuts that penalty to 2.6%.

## Summary (ZH)

Amati 等针对密集城市 NR-V2X 中网联车辆的实时多跳中继选择：路侧单元稀疏、频繁的非视距条件破坏了单跳连通性，而精确优化对秒级变化的拓扑太慢。将每个网络快照建模为有向属性图——节点为网联车与路侧单元，边为携带射频感知特征（SNR、容量、视距/非视距）的候选链路——中继选择由学习而非求解完成。

学习优化（L2O）流水线在离线 MILP 预言机的监督下训练边感知图同构网络（GINE），运行时以轻量后处理强制无环与单出边约束。训练数据规模可观：由 OpenStreetMap–SUMO–GEMV2 流水线在罗马三个城区（Porta Pia、EUR、Trastevere）生成 286,602 个快照，覆盖郊区到城市高压密度。

GINE 相对 MILP 预言机 12.3% 的连通性增益上界恢复了 11.3%，在城市中等密度下推理快 15–20 倍，最坏情况快 100 倍以上——推理时延近乎恒定，而 MILP 呈指数爆炸。对部署最有意义的是域偏移结果：仅在单一城区训练迁移时损失 23.3%，多城区联合训练将损失压至 2.6%。

## Key technical points (EN)

- Directed attributed graph model; edge features = SNR, capacity, LoS/NLoS per candidate link.
- Edge-aware GIN (GINE) trained by learning-to-optimize from a MILP oracle; runtime constraint enforcement keeps outputs feasible.
- 286,602 snapshots, OpenStreetMap–SUMO–GEMV2, three Rome districts, three density regimes.
- 11.3% connectivity gain vs 12.3% oracle bound; 15–20× (typical) to ≥100× (worst-case) speedup.
- Multi-area training shrinks cross-district domain-shift loss from 23.3% to 2.6%.

## Why it matters / what's new (EN)

The 5g-nr bin has V2X entries on congestion control (2607.18495 collective-perception DCC was passed over) and AoI scheduling, but no learning-to-optimize entry for sidelink relay topology. The pattern — offline exact solver as training oracle, GNN as runtime approximator, feasibility enforced by post-processing — is a template applicable to any NR scheduling problem with graph structure. The 2.6% multi-area domain-shift result is a useful empirical anchor for the perennial "will it generalize outside the training city?" objection to learned RRM.
