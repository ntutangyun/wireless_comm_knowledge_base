## Current state (EN)

This bin tracks the **FiRa Consortium** — the application-layer and certification ecosystem built on IEEE 802.15.4z (and, going forward, 802.15.4ab) that makes UWB services interoperable across vendors: digital key (with CCC), hands-free access, point-and-trigger, and indoor positioning profiles. Where the `uwb-ranging` bin follows the PHY/MAC standards and silicon, this bin follows who certifies against them and which application families converge onto the consortium.

KB coverage today: the bin's first industry entry, SPARK Microsystems joining FiRa as an associate member (`2026-07-03_spark-microsystems-joins-fira`) — notable because SPARK is a low-latency UWB *data-communications* vendor (LE-UWB) entering a ranging-first consortium, a concrete signal of the "one UWB radio for both ranging and data" convergence that 802.15.4ab also encodes. Adjacent context: STMicroelectronics joined the FiRa board with an automotive digital-key focus, and FiRa has been searching for a host for its first Plugfest in Asia — both signs of the certification ecosystem widening in membership and geography.

Watch for: the first Asia Plugfest (host call closed June 15, 2026); FiRa releases aligning with IEEE 802.15.4ab as it stabilizes; whether data-comms profiles appear in FiRa's release scope; and automotive digital-key volume milestones flowing through FiRa/CCC certification.

## Current state (ZH)

本桶追踪 **FiRa 联盟**——构建于 IEEE 802.15.4z（以及未来的 802.15.4ab）之上的应用层与认证生态，使 UWB 服务实现跨厂商互操作：数字钥匙（与 CCC 协作）、免操作门禁、指向触发、室内定位等应用规范。`uwb-ranging` 桶跟踪 PHY/MAC 标准与芯片，本桶则跟踪谁在对标认证、哪些应用族在向联盟汇聚。

当前 KB 覆盖：本桶首条产业条目——SPARK Microsystems 以准会员身份加入 FiRa（`2026-07-03_spark-microsystems-joins-fira`）。其意义在于 SPARK 是低时延 UWB *数据通信*厂商（LE-UWB），进入以测距为先的联盟，是"一颗 UWB 射频同时承载测距与数据"汇合趋势（802.15.4ab 同样编码了该方向）的具体信号。相邻背景：意法半导体已加入 FiRa 董事会（聚焦汽车数字钥匙），FiRa 正在为首次亚洲 Plugfest 征集主办方——认证生态在成员与地域两个维度扩张。

待关注：首次亚洲 Plugfest（主办方征集 2026 年 6 月 15 日截止）；FiRa 版本与逐步稳定的 IEEE 802.15.4ab 的对齐；数据通信规范是否进入 FiRa 发布范围；经由 FiRa/CCC 认证的汽车数字钥匙出货里程碑。
