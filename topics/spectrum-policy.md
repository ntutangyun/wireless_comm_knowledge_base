## Current state (EN)

This bin covers the **regulatory layer** of Wi-Fi: 6 GHz unlicensed rollouts, the upper-6 GHz contention with mobile operators, AFC (Automated Frequency Coordination) deployments, and coexistence with other radio access technologies (NR-U, and now Bluetooth). Spectrum policy decisions are slow but irreversibly shape what Wi-Fi can do where — once a regulator carves a band for mobile-only or shared use, the radio side has to live with that for a generation.

2026 has been an active regulatory year. In Europe, the **EU RSPG decision** on the upper 6 GHz band (`2026-05-04_eu-rspg-upper-6ghz-decision`) allocated 540 MHz to mobile and froze 160 MHz — a defeat for the "all upper-6 to Wi-Fi" position — while **Ofcom's "Better Together" plan** (`2026-05-04_ofcom-better-together-6ghz-uk`, `2026-05-28_wifinow-uk-eu-upper-6ghz-regulation`) takes a sharing approach. The Wi-Fi side has since escalated to the *legislative* arena: a 15-organization coalition open letter demands stronger Wi-Fi recognition in the EU **Digital Networks Act** (`2026-07-10_wifi-community-eu-dna-open-letter`). In the US, the **FCC expanded 6 GHz rules** (`2026-05-21_fcc-6ghz-rule-changes`). The regulatory map now extends beyond the usual markets: **South Africa's ICASA is finalizing lower-6 GHz standard power under a government-run (CSIR), OpenAFC-based coordination system** (`2026-07-10_south-africa-standard-power-6ghz-afc`) — the first African standard-power datapoint and the first reported government-operated OpenAFC. A third technology has also entered the band's coexistence conversation: **Wi-Fi Alliance and the Bluetooth SIG launched formal joint 6 GHz coexistence work** (`2026-07-10_wfa-bluetooth-sig-6ghz-coexistence`), anticipating Bluetooth's higher-band expansion. On the technical-coexistence side, the Notre Dame LPI/SP study (`2026-06-15_arxiv-wifi-6ghz-lpi-sp-self-coexistence`) quantified Wi-Fi-on-Wi-Fi power-class asymmetry (up to 18 dB, with OBSS-PD remedies backfiring), joining the earlier NR-U coexistence mechanism papers (`2026-05-04_arxiv-policy-driven-drl-nru-wifi-coexistence`, `2026-05-01_arxiv-llm-6ghz-wifi-nru-coexistence`).

Watch for: the EU DNA text emerging from Parliament/Council (does Wi-Fi/unlicensed language survive?); ICASA's end-2026 signature and whether other African/developing-market regulators copy the CSIR OpenAFC model; Bluetooth SIG higher-band PHY parameters (the trigger that makes Wi-Fi/BT 6 GHz coexistence concrete); technical specifications of the Ofcom sharing rules; Asia-Pacific 6 GHz decisions.

## Current state (ZH)

本桶覆盖 Wi-Fi 的**监管层**：6 GHz 免许可推行、Upper-6 GHz 与移动运营商的争用、AFC（自动频率协调）部署，以及与其他无线接入技术（NR-U，如今还有蓝牙）的共存。频谱政策决策缓慢但不可逆地塑造 Wi-Fi 能在何处做什么——监管者一旦把某个频段划为仅移动或共享使用，射频侧就要承受一代人的时间。

2026 年是监管活跃年。欧洲方面，**欧盟 RSPG 关于 upper 6 GHz 的决定**（`2026-05-04_eu-rspg-upper-6ghz-decision`）将 540 MHz 分给移动业务、冻结 160 MHz——是"upper-6 全归 Wi-Fi"立场的失利；**Ofcom 的 "Better Together" 计划**（`2026-05-04_ofcom-better-together-6ghz-uk`、`2026-05-28_wifinow-uk-eu-upper-6ghz-regulation`）采取共享路线。Wi-Fi 阵营已将战线升级到*立法*层面：15 家组织联署公开信要求欧盟**《数字网络法案》**更充分承认 Wi-Fi（`2026-07-10_wifi-community-eu-dna-open-letter`）。美国方面，**FCC 扩展了 6 GHz 规则**（`2026-05-21_fcc-6ghz-rule-changes`）。监管地图正扩展到传统市场之外：**南非 ICASA 正敲定低 6 GHz 标准功率，由政府机构（CSIR）基于 OpenAFC 运营协调系统**（`2026-07-10_south-africa-standard-power-6ghz-afc`）——非洲首个标准功率数据点，也是首例政府运营的 OpenAFC。第三种技术也加入该频段的共存讨论：**Wi-Fi 联盟与蓝牙 SIG 启动正式的 6 GHz 共存联合工作**（`2026-07-10_wfa-bluetooth-sig-6ghz-coexistence`），为蓝牙向高频段扩展做准备。技术共存侧，圣母大学 LPI/SP 研究（`2026-06-15_arxiv-wifi-6ghz-lpi-sp-self-coexistence`）量化了 Wi-Fi 自身的功率等级不对称（最高 18 dB，且 OBSS-PD 补救适得其反），与此前的 NR-U 共存机制论文（`2026-05-04_arxiv-policy-driven-drl-nru-wifi-coexistence`、`2026-05-01_arxiv-llm-6ghz-wifi-nru-coexistence`）并列。

待关注：欧洲议会/理事会谈判产出的 DNA 文本（Wi-Fi/免许可条款是否保留）；ICASA 2026 年底签署及其他非洲/发展中市场监管者是否复制 CSIR OpenAFC 模式；蓝牙 SIG 高频段 PHY 参数（使 Wi-Fi/蓝牙 6 GHz 共存具体化的触发点）；Ofcom 共享规则的技术规范；亚太地区的 6 GHz 决定。
