## Current state (EN)

This bin tracks **direct-to-device (D2D) satellite connectivity** — connecting unmodified smartphones straight to satellites: Starlink direct-to-cell, AST SpaceMobile, and the Apple/Globalstar emergency-SOS lineage. The regulatory and strategic picture moved sharply in 2026. The year opened with the FCC authorizing 7,500 more Gen2 Starlink satellites while *denying* SpaceX's new direct-to-cell spectrum bid (`2026-05-30_fcc-starlink-gen2-authorization-spectrum`) — a posture of "coverage supplement, not carrier." By mid-year that posture had inverted: the FCC approved SpaceX's $17B acquisition of EchoStar's 65 MHz of mid-band spectrum (AWS-4 / AWS-3 / H-Block), and at the June IPO roadshow SpaceX signaled retail Starlink Mobile carrier ambitions with a possible terrestrial network of its own (`2026-07-03_starlink-mobile-retail-carrier-plans`).

The competing architecture is AST SpaceMobile's large-aperture approach: the Block 2 BlueBird launches (satellites 8/9/10, 2,400 sq ft arrays — the largest commercial LEO satellites) and an FCC commercial SpaceMobile US authorization, with 989 Mbps peak D2D measured and a 45–60 satellite year-end target (`2026-06-18_ast-spacemobile-bluebird-block2-launch`, `2026-05-24_ast-spacemobile-q1-2026-45-bluebirds`).

Watch for: completion of the EchoStar spectrum transfer (~Nov 2027) and whether "Starlink Mobile" ships as retail MNO service or reverts to wholesale; whether the >100× Gen2 D2D capacity claim survives contact with deployment; AST's year-end constellation count vs. its 45–60 target; and 3GPP NTN handset-side support broadening beyond emergency messaging into mainstream broadband expectations.

## Current state (ZH)

本桶追踪**卫星直连手机（D2D）**——让未改装智能手机直接连接卫星：Starlink 直连蜂窝、AST SpaceMobile，以及 Apple/Globalstar 紧急求救一脉。2026 年监管与战略格局急剧变化。年初 FCC 在批准 7,500 颗 Gen2 Starlink 卫星的同时*否决*了 SpaceX 新的直连频谱申请（`2026-05-30_fcc-starlink-gen2-authorization-spectrum`）——当时的态势是"覆盖补充，而非运营商"。到年中态势逆转：FCC 批准 SpaceX 以 170 亿美元收购 EchoStar 的 65 MHz 中频段频谱（AWS-4 / AWS-3 / H-Block），SpaceX 在 6 月 IPO 路演上释放 Starlink Mobile 零售运营商野心，并可能自建地面网络（`2026-07-03_starlink-mobile-retail-carrier-plans`）。

与之竞争的是 AST SpaceMobile 的大口径架构：Block 2 BlueBird 发射（8/9/10 号星，2,400 平方英尺阵列——最大的商业 LEO 卫星）与 FCC 商用 SpaceMobile 美国授权，实测 989 Mbps 峰值 D2D，年底目标 45–60 颗（`2026-06-18_ast-spacemobile-bluebird-block2-launch`、`2026-05-24_ast-spacemobile-q1-2026-45-bluebirds`）。

待关注：EchoStar 频谱转让完成（约 2027 年 11 月）以及"Starlink Mobile"最终以零售 MNO 形态落地还是回归批发；Gen2 D2D 容量提升 100 倍以上的宣称能否经受部署检验；AST 年底星座规模与 45–60 颗目标的差距；3GPP NTN 终端侧支持从紧急短消息向主流宽带预期的扩展。
